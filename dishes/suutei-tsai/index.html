Suutei tsai (Mongolian salty milk tea)

Suutei tsai is tea simmered with milk and a pinch of salt. It tastes mild, creamy and slightly salty. It is drunk through the day and offered to every guest.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp green or brick tea
  • 500 ml water
  • 500 ml milk
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Boil the water with the tea for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the milk and bring to a gentle boil.
  3. Simmer 5 minutes, scooping and pouring to cool a little.
  4. Add salt and strain.
  5. Serve hot in bowls.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat milk.
  • Reduce the salt.
  • Serve with fruit instead of fried biscuits.

History

It is a daily drink in Mongolian households and a sign of hospitality. It is often served with boortsog, curd or bread.
