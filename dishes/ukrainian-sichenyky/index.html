Sichenyky (Ukrainian minced cutlets)

Origin: Ukraine
420kcal

Sichenyky are tender oval cutlets made from finely chopped meat or fish mixed with onion and soaked bread. They are rolled in breadcrumbs and fried until golden outside and juicy inside. Families serve them with mashed potatoes, buckwheat or a fresh salad.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g minced pork
  • 300 g minced beef
  • 2 onions
  • 80 g white bread
  • 100 ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 60 g breadcrumbs
  • oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Soak the bread in milk and squeeze it lightly.
  2. Grate or finely chop the onions.
  3. Mix the meat, onion, bread, egg, salt and pepper and knead well.
  4. Shape oval cutlets with wet hands and roll them in breadcrumbs.
  5. Fry in a little oil for 4 minutes per side.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat for 8 more minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use chicken or turkey mince for fewer calories.
  • Bake the cutlets at 200 °C instead of frying.
  • Add grated zucchini to the mince for extra moisture.

History

The name comes from the Ukrainian verb for chopping, since the meat was traditionally chopped by hand with a cleaver. Versions with river fish are common near the Dnipro.
