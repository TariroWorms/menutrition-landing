Brunost (Norwegian brown cheese)

Origin: Norway

Brunost is a brown, fudge-like cheese made by slowly boiling down whey, milk and cream until the milk sugar caramelizes. It is sliced very thin with a cheese slicer and eaten on bread, flatbread or waffles. The taste is sweet and caramelly with a slight tang, nothing like regular cheese. It is a daily breakfast staple in Norway.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 2 l whey
  • 1 l milk
  • 200 ml cream
  • Optional: 1 tbsp sugar

Method

  1. Pour whey, milk and cream into a wide pot.
  2. Simmer slowly for several hours, stirring occasionally, until reduced and thick.
  3. As it thickens, stir constantly and watch for burning.
  4. When caramel-brown and fudgy, pour into a mould.
  5. Chill until firm and slice thinly.

How to make it lighter

  • Use thin slices, as the sugar content is high.
  • Pair with whole-grain bread and fruit.
  • Choose goat-milk versions for a tangier flavour.

History

Brunost was developed in the nineteenth century, when dairy farmers began adding milk and cream to the whey they had long boiled down. Versions based on goat's milk and cow's milk exist, and it is now seen as a symbol of Norwegian food.
