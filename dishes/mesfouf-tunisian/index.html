Mesfouf (sweet couscous with dates)

Origin: Tunisia

Mesfouf is steamed fine couscous tossed with butter, sugar, raisins, dates and nuts. It is fluffy, lightly sweet and aromatic with a touch of rose or orange blossom water. It is eaten warm or cold as a breakfast, a snack or a light dessert.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g fine couscous
  • 50 g butter
  • 80 g dates, chopped
  • 50 g raisins
  • 50 g almonds
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp orange blossom water
  • 250 ml hot water

Method

  1. Moisten the couscous with a little salted water and rub to separate the grains.
  2. Steam for 15 minutes over simmering water.
  3. Sprinkle with water and rub again, then steam 10 more minutes.
  4. Toss with butter while hot.
  5. Add dates, raisins, sugar and orange blossom water.
  6. Top with toasted almonds and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use whole-wheat couscous for more fiber.
  • Reduce butter and sugar.
  • Add fresh fruit for natural sweetness.

History

Mesfouf is made throughout Tunisia and in eastern Algeria, often at Ramadan to break the fast or for celebrations. Variations include versions with pomegranate seeds or pistachios.
