Discada (mixed meats on a plow disc)

Discada is a party dish of several meats cooked in layers on a disco, a wide, shallow steel pan originally made from a worn-out plow disc set over a fire. Bacon and chorizo go in first to render their fat, followed by sausages, ham, pork and beef, then onion, peppers and tomato. The meats are chopped small and eaten in tortillas with salsa straight from the disc.

Recipe

10 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g bacon
  • 300 g Mexican chorizo
  • 300 g frankfurters, sliced
  • 500 g pork, diced
  • 700 g beef, diced
  • 2 onions (300 g)
  • 2 bell peppers (300 g)
  • 3 tomatoes (400 g), salt, pepper

Method

  1. Heat the disc or a very large heavy pan and cook the bacon until crisp.
  2. Add the chorizo and fry until it breaks up, then the sliced sausages.
  3. Add the pork and cook until browned, then the beef.
  4. Stir in the onion, peppers and tomato and cook until soft.
  5. Season and serve from the pan with corn tortillas and salsa.

How to make it lighter

  • Double the beef and halve the bacon, chorizo and sausages.
  • Spoon off surplus fat before adding the vegetables.
  • Add extra peppers and onion and eat with a big salad.

History

The dish comes from the ranching and farming regions of northern Mexico, where farmers reused old plow discs as cooking pans. It is a fixture of weekend gatherings in Chihuahua, Durango, Coahuila and Nuevo León.
