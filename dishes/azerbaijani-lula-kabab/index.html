Lula kabab (Azerbaijani minced lamb kebab)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Lula kabab is minced lamb mixed with onion, salt and pepper, kneaded until sticky and pressed by hand onto wide flat skewers. It is grilled quickly over hot coals so the outside chars while the inside stays juicy. The kebabs are slid onto lavash and eaten with sliced onion, sumac, herbs and grilled tomatoes.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g minced lamb (with some fat)
  • 2 onions (300 g)
  • 4 sheets lavash
  • 1 red onion with 1 tsp sumac
  • fresh herbs, grilled tomatoes
  • salt, black pepper

Method

  1. Grate the onions, squeeze out the juice and mix with the lamb, salt and pepper.
  2. Knead hard for 5 minutes, then chill 30 minutes.
  3. With wet hands press the meat into long sausages around flat skewers.
  4. Grill over hot coals for 8–10 minutes, turning, until browned.
  5. Serve on lavash with sumac onions, herbs and tomatoes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use leaner meat and add a little grated zucchini for moisture.
  • Fill up on grilled vegetables and herbs rather than bread.
  • Mix in some minced chicken to lower the fat.

History

Lula kabab is one of the most popular grilled dishes of the Caucasus. In Azerbaijan the meat is traditionally chopped by hand and mixed with tail fat, which helps it cling to the skewer.
