Tas kebab is a Turkish braise of beef cubes slowly cooked with onion, garlic, tomato and green peppers until the meat is fork-tender and the sauce thick and glossy. Potatoes are often added to the pot, making it a complete one-dish meal. Despite the name it is not grilled — 'tas' refers to the bowl it is cooked and served in, usually alongside rice pilaf or bread.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef, cubed
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 green bell peppers, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, grated, and 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 potatoes, cubed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, black pepper, paprika
  • 400 ml hot water

Method

  1. Brown the beef in the oil in a heavy pot.
  2. Add onion, garlic and peppers and cook until soft.
  3. Stir in tomato paste, grated tomatoes and spices, then add the hot water.
  4. Cover and simmer gently for 1–1.5 hours until the beef is tender.
  5. Add the potatoes for the last 25 minutes and cook until soft.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the beef before browning.
  • Serve with a salad instead of rice to keep the meal lighter.
  • Measure the oil: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Tas kebab belongs to the stew tradition of Ottoman palace and home cooking, where 'kebab' covered many meat dishes, not only grilled ones. It remains a standard dish in Turkish home kitchens and esnaf lokantası, the simple tradesmen's restaurants.
