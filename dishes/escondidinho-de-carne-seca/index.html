Escondidinho de carne seca (jerked beef and cassava bake)

Origin: Brazil · Northeast

Escondidinho means "little hidden one": a layer of savoury shredded jerked beef sautéed with onion and garlic is hidden under a smooth purée of cassava, butter and milk. The top is covered with cheese and baked until golden. The contrast of salty meat and soft, slightly sweet cassava makes it real comfort food. It is served straight from the dish, often with a green salad.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g carne seca, desalted
  • 1 kg cassava, peeled
  • 200 ml milk
  • 40 g butter
  • 150 g mozzarella or queijo coalho, grated
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves, tomato, parsley

Method

  1. Boil the desalted meat until tender, shred it and sauté with onion, garlic and tomato.
  2. Boil the cassava until very soft, remove the core and mash with butter and warm milk.
  3. Spread the meat in a baking dish and cover evenly with the cassava purée.
  4. Top with grated cheese.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 20–25 minutes until golden and bubbling.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk and less butter in the purée.
  • Mix the cassava with mashed pumpkin to lower calories and carbs.
  • Use a light layer of cheese only on top.

History

The dish comes from the Northeast, where carne seca and cassava are everyday staples, and it has spread across Brazil as a popular restaurant and home dish. Versions with chicken or shrimp are also common.
