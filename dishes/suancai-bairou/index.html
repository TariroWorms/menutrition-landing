Suancai bairou (pork belly with sour cabbage)

Origin: China · Dongbei
402kcal

Suancai bairou is a simple pot of boiled pork belly slices and shredded fermented napa cabbage in a clear, tangy broth. Glass noodles are often added to absorb the sourness. It is a typical winter meal eaten with dipping sauces such as garlic and chili.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g pork belly
  • 300 g fermented napa cabbage
  • 80 g glass noodles
  • 1 piece of ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 600 ml water

Method

  1. Boil the pork belly whole for 25 minutes, then cool and slice thinly.
  2. Rinse and shred the fermented cabbage.
  3. Place the cabbage in a pot with the ginger and water and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the pork slices and soaked noodles and cook for 10 minutes.
  5. Serve with crushed garlic and chili for dipping.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose a leaner cut and skim off the fat.
  • Taste the broth before adding any salt, as the fermented cabbage is already salty.
  • Add tofu or vegetables for balance.

History

Fermenting cabbage in large crocks is a long-standing way to keep vegetables through the northeast's long winter. Suancai bairou is among the most common dishes made with it.
