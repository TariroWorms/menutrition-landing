Xiaoguo mixian (small-pot rice noodles)

Origin: China · Yunnan · Kunming

Xiaoguo mixian is made by simmering rice noodles in a small pot with a savory pork sauce, pickled greens and fresh chives. The broth is thick, lightly spicy and tangy. It is eaten hot, straight from the pot, with chili oil on the side.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g fresh rice noodles
  • 100 g minced pork
  • 2 tbsp pickled mustard greens
  • 1 handful of chives
  • 400 ml pork or chicken broth
  • 1 tbsp chili oil

Method

  1. Fry the minced pork in a little oil until browned.
  2. Add the chopped pickles and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the broth and bring to a simmer.
  4. Add the rice noodles and cook for 3-4 minutes until tender.
  5. Stir in the chives, season with salt and top with chili oil.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork and skim the fat from the broth.
  • Add leafy greens for more fiber.
  • Go easy on the pickles and soy to limit salt.

History

Mixian are a staple of Yunnan, and several regional versions exist. The small-pot style is closely associated with Kunming and street-side noodle shops.
