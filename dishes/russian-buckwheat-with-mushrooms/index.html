Buckwheat with mushrooms (grechka s gribami)

Origin: Russia

Grechka s gribami is buckwheat groats simmered until fluffy and mixed with mushrooms and onion fried in butter. The roasted, nutty grain pairs naturally with the earthy mushrooms, giving a warming and satisfying dish. It is eaten as a main course in Lent or on meatless days, or as a side to meat.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g buckwheat groats
  • 500 ml water
  • 300 g mushrooms
  • 1 large onion
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • salt, pepper

Method

  1. Rinse the buckwheat and toast it in a dry pan for 2–3 minutes until fragrant.
  2. Add the water and salt, bring to a boil, cover and simmer on low heat for 15–18 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  3. Meanwhile fry the chopped onion in oil until soft.
  4. Add the sliced mushrooms and cook until browned and the liquid has evaporated; season.
  5. Fold the mushrooms and butter into the buckwheat.
  6. Cover and let it rest 5 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace butter with a little vegetable oil to make the dish vegan.
  • Add more mushrooms and less buckwheat for a lower-calorie portion.
  • Serve with a salad of fresh vegetables or sauerkraut for extra fibre and vitamin C.

History

Buckwheat porridge has been one of the most important everyday foods in Russia for centuries. Combining it with forest mushrooms is a long-standing tradition, especially during the Orthodox fasts.
