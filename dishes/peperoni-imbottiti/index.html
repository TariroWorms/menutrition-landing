🫑 Peperoni imbottiti (Italian stuffed peppers)

Origin: Italy · Southern Italy

Peperoni imbottiti are Italian stuffed peppers: whole bell peppers filled with a mixture of rice, ground beef and tomato, topped with grated parmesan and baked until tender. The peppers turn sweet and soft while the filling stays savoury and juicy, and the cheese forms a golden crust. They are served hot as a main course, often with a spoonful of the baking juices.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 4 large bell peppers
  • 150 g rice, parboiled
  • 300 g ground beef
  • 400 g tomato passata
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 40 g parmesan, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper and parsley

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190 °C. Cut the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds.
  2. Brown the onion and beef in 1 tbsp oil, add half the passata and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Mix in the parboiled rice and parsley and season.
  4. Fill the peppers, stand them in a baking dish and pour the remaining passata around them.
  5. Top with parmesan, drizzle with the remaining oil and bake for 40–45 minutes until the peppers are tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean ground beef (5% fat) to cut fat noticeably.
  • Swap part of the rice for diced vegetables such as zucchini or mushroom.
  • One stuffed pepper with a large green salad is a satisfying, measured portion.

History

Stuffed peppers are cooked all over Italy, and recipes change from region to region and family to family — some use bread, others rice, meat or vegetables. There is no single documented origin; today they are a typical summer home dish, especially in the south.
