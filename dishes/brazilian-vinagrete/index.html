Vinagrete (Brazilian vinaigrette salsa)

Origin: Brazil

Vinagrete is a chopped salsa of tomato, onion and green pepper dressed with vinegar, oil, salt and parsley. It is bright, juicy and sharp, cutting through the richness of grilled meat. At a churrasco it is spooned over sliced picanha or sausages, and it is also eaten with farofa, rice and beans. It is best made shortly before serving.

What it is made of

tomatoonionbell peppervinegarolive oilparsley

Recipe

6 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 green bell pepper
  • 3 tbsp white vinegar
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt

Method

  1. Seed the tomatoes and dice them finely.
  2. Dice the onion and pepper to the same small size.
  3. Mix the vegetables in a bowl.
  4. Add vinegar, oil, salt and parsley and stir.
  5. Rest 10 minutes so the flavours meld, then serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the oil to one tablespoon; the tomato juices carry the dressing.
  • Soak the chopped onion in cold water for a milder taste.
  • Use it as a topping for grilled fish or chicken instead of heavier sauces.

History

The name simply means vinaigrette in Portuguese, but in Brazil it refers to this vegetable salsa rather than a plain dressing. It is a standard companion of churrasco, the barbecue tradition associated especially with the southern gaúcho culture.
