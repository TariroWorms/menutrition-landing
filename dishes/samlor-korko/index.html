Samlor korko (Cambodian stirred vegetable stew)

Origin: Cambodia

Samlor korko is a rustic, thick stew of many vegetables such as eggplant, pumpkin, green papaya and beans, with pork or fish. It is flavoured with kroeung paste, thickened with ground toasted rice and finished with fresh herbs. It tastes earthy, savoury and mildly bitter.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g pork, cubed
  • 1 eggplant
  • 150 g pumpkin
  • 100 g green beans
  • 2 tbsp kroeung paste
  • 2 tbsp toasted ground rice
  • 1 l water and 2 tbsp fish sauce

Method

  1. Fry the kroeung in a little oil until fragrant.
  2. Add the pork and sear lightly.
  3. Pour in water and fish sauce and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Add the vegetables in order of hardness and cook until tender.
  5. Stir in the toasted rice to thicken and finish with herbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork or fish.
  • Increase the vegetable proportion.
  • Use less fish sauce for lower sodium.

History

The name roughly means stirred soup, and it is a very traditional Khmer home dish. It is often cooked for family meals and festivals, and its toasted-rice thickener is typical of old Khmer cooking.
