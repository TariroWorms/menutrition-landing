🍅 Ezme (Turkish Spicy Tomato Salad)

Origin: Turkey · Southeast Anatolia

Ezme is a Turkish relish of very finely chopped tomato, onion, peppers and parsley, dressed with pomegranate molasses and a little olive oil. It is fresh, tangy, slightly sweet and pleasantly spicy. It is served as a meze or alongside grilled kebabs.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe tomatoes, seeded
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 green pepper and 1 hot chili
  • 1 small bunch parsley
  • 1 tbsp pomegranate molasses
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • Salt

Method

  1. Chop the tomatoes, onion, peppers and parsley as finely as possible, or pulse briefly in a food processor.
  2. Drain off excess tomato juice.
  3. Mix in the pomegranate molasses, olive oil, chili flakes and salt.
  4. Rest for 10 minutes and serve cool.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only 1 tsp olive oil — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Pair it with grilled meat or vegetables for a light plate.
  • Ezme is naturally light, so it works as a big portion of vegetables.

History

Ezme, meaning 'crushed' in Turkish, is a fixture of kebab houses across Turkey and is especially associated with the southeastern kebab tradition. It is usually prepared fresh to order by hand-chopping with a large knife.
