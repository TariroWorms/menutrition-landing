🍗 Spanish arroz con pollo

Spanish arroz con pollo is a homely one-pan dish of chicken and short-grain rice cooked in a saffron-tinted tomato base. Strips of bell pepper, green peas and a handful of olives make it colourful and slightly salty, and the rice soaks up all the chicken juices. It is softer and saucier than paella and is served straight from the pan.

Recipe

4 servings55 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  • 300 g short-grain rice
  • A pinch of saffron in 2 tbsp warm water
  • 1 red and 1 green bell pepper, cut into strips
  • 2 tomatoes, grated
  • 150 g peas
  • 60 g green olives
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp sweet paprika, salt
  • 900 ml hot water or chicken stock

Method

  1. Season the chicken and brown it in the olive oil in a wide pan, then set aside.
  2. Fry the onion, garlic and peppers for 5 minutes, add the grated tomato and paprika and cook until thick.
  3. Return the chicken, add the hot water or stock and saffron, and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Stir in the rice, season and cook uncovered on medium heat for 10 minutes, then add the peas and olives and cook on low heat for 8–10 minutes more.
  5. Cover with a cloth and rest for 5 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken thighs to keep the flavour and lose much of the fat.
  • Measure the olive oil: 1 tablespoon instead of 2 saves about 120 kcal for the pan.
  • Add extra peppers and peas and serve a little less rice per plate.

History

Chicken with rice is an everyday dish in Spanish home cooking, closely related to the rice dishes of Valencia and the Mediterranean coast. Versions of it spread across Latin America, where it is a staple from the Caribbean to Peru.
