Vínarterta (Icelandic prune layer cake)

Origin: Iceland

Vínarterta is a tall cake of thin, firm cardamom shortbread layers sandwiched with spiced prune jam. It is left for a day or two so the layers soften. The flavour is sweet, fruity and gently spiced, and it is served in thin slices with coffee.

What it is made of

flourbuttersugareggprunescardamom

Recipe

12 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 200 g butter
  • 200 g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 300 g pitted prunes
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Cream the butter and sugar, then add eggs and vanilla.
  2. Mix in flour and cardamom to form a dough and chill for 1 hour.
  3. Roll and bake 6 thin layers at 190 °C for 8 minutes each.
  4. Simmer the prunes with water until soft and mash to a jam.
  5. Stack the layers with prune jam between them and rest 24 hours.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the sugar in the dough; the prunes are sweet.
  • Cut very thin slices.
  • Replace some butter with yoghurt for lighter layers.

History

Its name means Vienna cake, and it is linked to Danish baking brought to Iceland. It is a standard at Christmas and special family gatherings, and it was also carried to Icelandic communities in Canada.
