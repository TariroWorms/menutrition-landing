🍫 Regag with chocolate

Origin: United Arab Emirates · Dubai

Regag with chocolate is a sweet version of the Emirati paper-thin bread: a hot, crisp regag sheet is brushed with butter, spread with chocolate-hazelnut spread and rolled or folded so the chocolate melts inside. It is crunchy outside and soft and sweet within, a favourite snack at Dubai cafeterias and food stalls.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain flour, 200 ml water and a pinch of salt for the batter (or 2 ready regag sheets)
  • 60 g chocolate-hazelnut spread
  • 10 g butter

Method

  1. Make thin regag sheets on a very hot griddle.
  2. While a sheet is still warm on the griddle, brush it lightly with butter.
  3. Spread with chocolate-hazelnut spread.
  4. Roll or fold the regag and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp of spread per sheet and spread it thinly.
  • Leave out the butter — the spread is rich enough.
  • Add sliced banana or strawberries to make a smaller amount of spread feel like more.

History

Sweet regag fillings such as chocolate spread are a modern addition to the traditional bread and are now sold alongside the egg and cheese versions in cafeterias and at festivals across the UAE.
