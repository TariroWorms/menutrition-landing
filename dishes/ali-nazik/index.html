🍆 Ali nazik

Origin: Turkey · Gaziantep

Ali nazik is a Turkish dish of smoky charred eggplant mashed with thick garlic yogurt, topped with sautéed minced lamb. A drizzle of melted butter with red pepper finishes the plate. The contrast is the point: cool, creamy and tangy underneath, warm and savoury on top. It is served as a main course, often with rice on the side.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggplants
  • 500 g ground lamb
  • 400 g thick plain yogurt
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Char the eggplants over a gas flame or under a hot grill for 15–20 minutes, turning, until black outside and soft inside. Cool, peel and chop.
  2. Mix the eggplant with the yogurt, garlic and a pinch of salt.
  3. Fry the lamb in the oil over high heat for 8–10 minutes until browned, season with salt and pepper.
  4. Spread the eggplant yogurt on a plate and spoon the lamb on top.
  5. Melt the butter with the red pepper flakes and drizzle it over the dish.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean ground lamb and pour off the fat after browning.
  • Skip the butter drizzle and sprinkle the pepper flakes straight on top — 1 tbsp butter is about 100 kcal.
  • Use more eggplant and less meat: it adds volume for very few calories.

History

Ali nazik is a speciality of Gaziantep in south-eastern Turkey, a city known for its kebabs and grilled dishes. Several stories explain the name, often linking it to a guest or an Ottoman ruler praising the dish, but none is documented. Today it is a staple of kebab houses across Turkey.
