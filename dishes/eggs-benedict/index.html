🍳 Eggs Benedict

Origin: United States · New York

Eggs Benedict is a brunch classic: a toasted English muffin half topped with a slice of ham, a soft poached egg and a spoonful of warm, buttery hollandaise sauce. Cutting into it releases the runny yolk into the sauce. It is usually served as two halves per plate, often with a little salad or fruit.

What it is made of

english muffinhamegghollandaise sauce

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 English muffins, split
  • 4 slices ham
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 egg yolks for the sauce
  • 80 g butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp white vinegar for poaching
  • Salt and cayenne pepper

Method

  1. Whisk the yolks with lemon juice over a pan of barely simmering water, then slowly whisk in the melted butter until thick; season.
  2. Bring a pan of water with the vinegar to a gentle simmer and poach the eggs for 3 minutes.
  3. Toast the muffins and warm the ham in a dry pan.
  4. Top each muffin half with ham, a poached egg and a spoonful of hollandaise.
  5. Dust with cayenne and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Order or make a single half rather than two to halve the portion.
  • Use just 1 tbsp hollandaise per egg; the sauce is the most energy-dense part.
  • Serve with a big side of salad or tomatoes instead of potatoes.

History

The dish is usually credited to New York in the late 19th century, with competing stories naming Delmonico's restaurant or a guest at the Waldorf Hotel; none is certain.
