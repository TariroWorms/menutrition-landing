A fresh apple eaten whole with the skin on is one of the simplest snacks there is: crisp, juicy and sweet to tart depending on the variety. A medium apple weighs around 180 g and is mostly water and natural sugars, with fibre concentrated in and just under the skin. It travels well and needs no preparation beyond a rinse.

What it is made of

apple

Recipe

1 servings2 min

Ingredients

  • 1 medium apple (about 180 g)
  • Optional: a pinch of ground cinnamon
  • Optional: a squeeze of lemon juice

Method

  1. Rinse the apple well under running water and dry it.
  2. Eat it whole, or core it and cut it into wedges. Sprinkle with cinnamon if you like, and add a little lemon juice if the slices will sit for a while, to stop them browning.

How to make it lighter

  • A medium 180 g apple is about 95 kcal — an easy snack to fit into almost any calorie budget.
  • Keep the skin on: it holds much of the fibre, so the apple is more filling for the same calories.
  • A whole apple is more filling than the same amount as juice, which drinks quickly and has no chewing to slow you down.

History

The cultivated apple descends mainly from a wild apple of the Tian Shan mountains in Central Asia, around present-day Kazakhstan. From there it spread along trade routes to Persia, the Mediterranean and Europe, and later to the Americas. Today apples are grown in temperate regions around the world in thousands of varieties.
