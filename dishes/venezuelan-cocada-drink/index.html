Cocada (Venezuelan coconut shake)

Origin: Venezuela · coast

In Venezuela cocada is not a candy but a frosty drink made by blending fresh coconut flesh with its water, milk, condensed milk and plenty of ice. It is finished with a dusting of cinnamon. The texture is thick and slightly grainy from the coconut, and the taste is sweet and refreshing. It is sold at beaches and roadside stands along the coast.

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • flesh of 1 fresh coconut (250 g)
  • 300 ml coconut water
  • 300 ml cold milk
  • 100 g sweetened condensed milk
  • ice and ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Open the coconut, reserve its water and scoop out the flesh.
  2. Cut the flesh into small pieces.
  3. Blend flesh, coconut water, milk and condensed milk until fairly smooth.
  4. Add ice and blend again until frosty.
  5. Serve with a sprinkle of cinnamon.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the condensed milk; fresh coconut is already sweet.
  • Use more coconut water and less milk.
  • Treat it as a dessert-sized drink.

History

Coconut palms line much of the Venezuelan Caribbean coast, and vendors there have long blended the fresh fruit into cold drinks. Cocada is especially associated with beach outings on the central coast and Margarita.
