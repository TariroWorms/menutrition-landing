Fuzhou fish balls

Origin: China · Fujian · Fuzhou
225kcal

Fuzhou fish balls are balls of fish paste beaten until springy, wrapped around a spoonful of minced pork and poached. Biting in releases a hot, savoury filling inside a firm, sweet shell. They are served in a light broth with scallions and white pepper.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g white fish fillet
  • 80 g ground pork
  • 2 tbsp sweet potato starch
  • 1 egg white
  • 2 scallions
  • 800 ml light broth
  • white pepper, salt

Method

  1. Chop the fish into a paste and beat in starch, egg white and salt until sticky.
  2. Season the pork with salt, pepper and a little scallion.
  3. Flatten a spoon of fish paste in your hand, add pork and close into a ball.
  4. Slip the balls into barely simmering water and poach until they float.
  5. Heat the broth, add the balls and scallion and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • A very light, high-protein dish; keep the broth low in salt.
  • Use only lean pork for the filling.
  • Add greens or mushrooms for fibre.

History

Fuzhou fish balls are a long-standing local snack and a common New Year dish, as the word for fish sounds like abundance. They are sold in soup shops across Fujian.
