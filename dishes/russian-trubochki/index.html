Trubochki (wafer rolls with condensed milk)

Origin: Russia

Trubochki are delicate wafer tubes baked in a special waffle iron and rolled while still hot. Once cool they are filled with boiled condensed milk, sometimes whipped with butter. The shell shatters at the first bite and gives way to a rich, toffee-like cream. They are a classic treat with tea at home and at children's parties.

What it is made of

floureggsugarbutterboiled condensed milk

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 120 g sugar
  • 120 g melted butter
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 380 g boiled condensed milk
  • 50 g soft butter for the cream

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with sugar until pale.
  2. Stir in melted butter, salt and flour to a smooth batter.
  3. Heat the waffle iron and bake a tablespoon of batter until golden.
  4. Immediately roll each hot wafer around a cone or stick and let it cool.
  5. Beat the condensed milk with soft butter until smooth.
  6. Pipe the cream into the cooled tubes just before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Fill only part of each tube or use a thinner cream to cut sugar.
  • Serve two small rolls instead of a big plate — they are very energy-dense.
  • Mix the condensed milk with curd cheese for a lighter, tangier filling.

History

Electric waffle irons for thin wafers became common in Soviet kitchens in the second half of the 20th century, and these rolls became a favourite home-made sweet. Boiled condensed milk was one of the most beloved fillings of the era.
