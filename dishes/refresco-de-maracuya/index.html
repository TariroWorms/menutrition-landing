Refresco de maracuyá (passion fruit juice)

Origin: Peru

Refresco de maracuyá is one of the everyday drinks served with lunch across Peru. Yellow passion fruit pulp is briefly blended with cold water, then strained to remove the black seeds and sweetened to taste. The result is bright yellow, very aromatic and pleasantly sour, and it cuts through rich dishes such as ceviche or fried food. Many restaurants include it with the set-lunch menu.

What it is made of

passion fruitsugar

Recipe

6 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 6 passion fruits (about 200 g pulp)
  • 1.5 litres cold water
  • 100 g sugar
  • ice

Method

  1. Cut the passion fruits in half and scoop out the pulp.
  2. Pulse the pulp with half the water just a few seconds so the seeds stay whole.
  3. Strain through a sieve, pressing the pulp.
  4. Add the remaining water and sugar and stir to dissolve.
  5. Serve very cold over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the sugar or use a little stevia.
  • Dilute it more for a lighter, very low-calorie drink.
  • Drink it with meals instead of soft drinks.

History

Passion fruit grows widely in Peru's warm coastal and jungle valleys. Fresh fruit drinks, or refrescos, are a standard part of the Peruvian menú, and maracuyá is among the most popular together with chicha morada.
