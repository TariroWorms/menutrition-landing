Fiskesuppe (Norwegian fish soup)

Origin: Norway · Bergen
298kcal

Norwegian fish soup is a creamy soup with chunks of white fish and salmon, carrots, leek and potato. The broth is lightly thickened by the potatoes and finished with cream and fresh herbs. It tastes mild and comforting, with a sweet note from the vegetables. It is served hot with bread, often as a main course on cold days.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g white fish fillet
  • 200 g salmon fillet
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 leek
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 l fish stock
  • 100 ml cream, plus chives or dill to finish

Method

  1. Cut the vegetables into small cubes.
  2. Simmer them in the fish stock for 10 minutes until the potatoes are nearly tender.
  3. Cut the fish into chunks and add them to the pot.
  4. Simmer gently for 4 minutes, stirring as little as possible.
  5. Stir in the cream, season and finish with chives or dill.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace part of the cream with milk for a lighter soup.
  • Add more vegetables to bulk up the portion.
  • Add the fish at the end so it stays tender.

History

Fish soup is closely tied to the western coast of Norway, with Bergen known for its fish market and seafood cooking. Recipes vary from house to house, and the creamy style is the one most people know today.
