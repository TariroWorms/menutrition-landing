Bhindi masala

Origin: India · North India

Bhindi masala is an Indian dry curry of okra cooked with onion, tomato and warming spices — turmeric, cumin and coriander. The okra is fried until its edges turn crisp and golden, which keeps it from going slimy, then tossed with the softened onion-tomato masala. It is served as a side dish with chapati or rice and dal as part of an everyday Indian meal.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh okra
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves and 2 cm ginger, minced
  • 1/2 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder, 1/2 tsp dried mango powder (amchur), salt
  • Fresh coriander to serve

Method

  1. Wash the okra and dry it completely, then trim the ends and cut into 2 cm pieces.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide pan and fry the okra in a single layer over medium-high heat for 8–10 minutes, stirring now and then, until crisp at the edges. Set aside.
  3. Heat the remaining oil, add the cumin seeds and, when they sizzle, the onion. Fry until golden.
  4. Add the garlic, ginger, turmeric, ground coriander and chilli and cook for 1 minute, then add the tomatoes and salt and cook until soft.
  5. Return the okra, sprinkle with amchur, toss gently for 2–3 minutes and finish with fresh coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Okra itself is very low in calories; most of the energy comes from the oil, so measure it — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Roast the okra in the oven at 220 °C on a lined tray with 1 tsp oil instead of pan-frying.
  • Serve with one chapati or a small portion of rice and add a bowl of dal for a more filling meal.

History

Bhindi masala is a home-style dish eaten all over India, especially in the north, and it is a staple of Indian restaurant menus around the world.
