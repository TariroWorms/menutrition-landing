Indio viejo (Nicaraguan shredded beef stew)

Origin: Nicaragua

Indio viejo is a thick Nicaraguan stew of pulled beef simmered with vegetables and bound with soaked corn masa. The flavor is savory and gently tart from sour orange, with a fresh note of mint. It is served in deep bowls, usually with fried plantain or white rice on the side.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef (brisket or flank)
  • 100 g corn masa
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 sweet pepper
  • 100 ml sour orange juice
  • 1 handful of mint
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Boil the beef in water until very tender, about 1.5 hours, then shred it.
  2. Keep the broth and soak the masa in a cup of it until smooth.
  3. Fry chopped onion, pepper, tomato and garlic in oil for 8 minutes.
  4. Add the shredded beef, sour orange juice and the masa mixture.
  5. Simmer 20 minutes, stirring, until thick; add mint and salt and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef and skim the fat from the broth.
  • Add extra vegetables to stretch the meat.
  • Serve with rice or baked plantain instead of fried.

History

The dish is a classic of Nicaraguan home cooking and is linked to the country's corn-based food traditions. Cooks in different towns prepare it with slight variations, and it is common at family meals and festive tables.
