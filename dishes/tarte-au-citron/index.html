🍋 Lemon meringue tart

Tarte au citron is a French tart of crisp, buttery shortcrust filled with a smooth, tangy lemon curd made from lemon juice, eggs, sugar and butter. In the meringue version it is topped with swirls of meringue that are lightly browned. It is sharp and sweet at once and is served cool, in slices.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour, 100 g cold butter, 1 egg yolk, 2 tbsp cold water
  • Juice and zest of 3 lemons
  • 3 eggs and 2 egg yolks
  • 150 g sugar for the curd
  • 60 g butter for the curd
  • 2 egg whites and 100 g sugar for the meringue

Method

  1. Rub the flour and butter together, add the yolk and water, form a dough and chill for 30 minutes.
  2. Line a 22 cm tin, blind-bake at 180 °C for 20 minutes and cool.
  3. Whisk the lemon juice, zest, eggs, yolks and sugar over low heat until thick, then stir in the butter.
  4. Pour the curd into the case and let it set.
  5. Whisk the egg whites with sugar to stiff peaks, pipe over the curd and brown briefly under a hot grill.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the tart into 10 slices instead of 8.
  • Skip the meringue for a slightly lighter slice with the same lemon flavour.
  • Serve with fresh berries rather than cream.

History

Lemon tarts are a staple of French pâtisserie, and the curd-filled version became a classic of bakery windows in the 20th century. The meringue-topped variant is shared with British baking, where lemon meringue pie is equally familiar. Today it is found in pastry shops across France.
