🍆 Moussaka

Moussaka is a baked casserole of sliced eggplant and potato layered with a lamb and tomato sauce and topped with a thick layer of béchamel. Baked until golden, it slices into rich, creamy layers with a warmly spiced meat filling. It is served in squares, warm rather than piping hot, often with a simple salad.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants, sliced 1 cm thick
  • 3 potatoes, sliced
  • 500 g ground lamb
  • 1 onion, chopped, and 2 garlic cloves
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp cinnamon, 1/2 tsp allspice
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper
  • 600 ml thick béchamel sauce

Method

  1. Brush the eggplant and potato slices with oil and roast at 200 °C for 20–25 minutes until soft.
  2. Fry the onion and garlic, add the lamb and brown it, then add tomatoes, spices, salt and pepper and simmer for 25 minutes until thick.
  3. Layer the potatoes in a baking dish, then half the eggplant, the meat sauce and the remaining eggplant.
  4. Spread the béchamel evenly over the top.
  5. Bake at 180 °C for 45 minutes until golden, then rest for 20 minutes before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the eggplant instead of frying it — eggplant soaks up a lot of oil, and 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use a thinner layer of béchamel and lean lamb, draining off the fat after browning.
  • Serve a modest square with a large green salad.

History

Dishes called moussaka exist across the Balkans and the eastern Mediterranean, but the Greek version with a béchamel topping is usually credited to the chef Nikolaos Tselementes, who popularised it in the 1920s. Today it is one of the best-known dishes of Greek cuisine.
