Galchi jorim (braised hairtail with radish)

Origin: South Korea · Jeju
300kcal

Galchi jorim features slices of silvery hairtail simmered over thick wedges of radish in a spicy soy-based sauce. The radish soaks up the sauce and turns sweet and tender while the fish stays soft and delicate. It is a classic side dish for rice, with the sauce spooned over the grains.

Recipe

3 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g hairtail, cut into chunks
  • 300 g radish, sliced thickly
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce (45 ml)
  • 2 tbsp gochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
  • 1 tbsp sugar (12 g)
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 scallions (30 g)

Method

  1. Layer the radish at the bottom of a wide pot with 250 ml water.
  2. Mix soy sauce, chili flakes, sugar and garlic into a sauce.
  3. Place the fish over the radish and pour on half the sauce.
  4. Simmer covered for 15 minutes, then add the rest of the sauce.
  5. Add scallions, spoon sauce over the fish and cook 5 minutes more.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar and soy sauce to lower sodium and sweetness.
  • Add extra radish for volume with very few calories.
  • Remove the skin if you want to trim fat.

History

Hairtail is a prized fish on Jeju island, where it is caught in summer and autumn. Braising it with radish is a traditional home method that stretches a small amount of fish across a family meal.
