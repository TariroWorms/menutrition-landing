Suya (spiced beef skewers)

Origin: Nigeria · Hausa

Suya is thinly sliced beef coated in yaji, a dry rub of ground roasted peanuts, chili, ginger and other spices, then grilled over charcoal. It is smoky, nutty and hot. Vendors serve it in paper with raw onion, tomato and extra yaji, usually in the evening.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef sirloin
  • 80 g roasted peanuts, ground
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp ground chili
  • 1 tsp ginger powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion, 2 tomatoes to serve

Method

  1. Freeze the beef briefly and slice it into very thin strips.
  2. Mix peanuts, paprika, chili, ginger, garlic powder and salt into yaji.
  3. Toss the meat with oil and most of the yaji.
  4. Thread onto skewers and marinate for an hour.
  5. Grill over high heat, turning, until charred at the edges.
  6. Sprinkle with remaining yaji and serve with onion and tomato.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean cuts and trim visible fat.
  • Use less oil and skip charring for a gentler version.
  • Serve with a big onion and tomato salad instead of bread.

History

Suya is associated with the Hausa people of northern Nigeria and is now a national street food. It is sold at roadside stands, where the meat is cut to order and grilled on open fires.
