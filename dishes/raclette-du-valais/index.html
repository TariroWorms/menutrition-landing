🧀 Raclette du Valais

Raclette du Valais is the traditional raclette of its home canton, made with the protected Valais raw-milk cheese, which melts into a pungent, gooey layer. A half wheel is heated and scraped portion by portion onto potatoes boiled in their skins. It is served with cornichons, pickled onions and thin slices of Valais air-dried meat.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g Raclette du Valais cheese
  • 1 kg small potatoes
  • 150 g cornichons
  • 150 g pickled onions
  • 120 g Valais air-dried beef, thinly sliced
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins in salted water until tender; keep warm.
  2. Heat the cheese under a raclette heater, or melt slices in a tabletop grill.
  3. Scrape each melted portion onto a plate beside the potatoes.
  4. Season with black pepper.
  5. Serve with cornichons, pickled onions and the dried meat.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep to about 150 g cheese per person.
  • Let the lean dried beef and pickles take more of the plate.
  • Add a crisp green salad to balance the cheese.

History

Raclette du Valais has held Swiss protected designation of origin since 2007, and only cheese made in the canton may carry the name. Melting cheese by the fire has long been part of herding life in the Valais Alps.
