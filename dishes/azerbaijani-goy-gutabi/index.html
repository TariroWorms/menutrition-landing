Goy gutabi (herb gutab)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Goy gutabi are thin half-moon flatbreads filled with a generous mix of chopped greens: spinach, cilantro, dill, green onion and sometimes wild herbs or sorrel. The filling wilts in the heat of the griddle, staying bright and fresh. They are brushed with a little butter and dusted with sumac. Stacks of them are eaten warm with yogurt or ayran.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 160 ml water
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 300 g spinach
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 1 bunch dill
  • 4 green onions
  • 20 g butter, sumac

Method

  1. Make a firm dough from the flour, water and salt and rest it 30 minutes.
  2. Chop the greens finely, season with salt and squeeze lightly.
  3. Roll 10 very thin circles of dough.
  4. Put greens on one half of each circle, fold and seal the edges.
  5. Cook on a hot dry pan about 1 minute per side.
  6. Brush lightly with butter and sprinkle with sumac.

How to make it lighter

  • Add crumbled feta-type cheese for protein, or leave it out for fewer calories.
  • Use a very thin film of butter or none.
  • Serve with plain yogurt for extra protein.

History

'Göy' means greens, and herb gutab are perhaps the most common kind, especially in spring when fresh and wild herbs appear at markets.
