Nadughi with mint

Origin: Georgia
200kcal

Nadughi is a soft, fluffy fresh cheese made from the whey left after cheesemaking, similar to ricotta. Mixed with chopped mint and a pinch of salt, it becomes a light, fragrant spread. Often it is wrapped in a thin sheet of sulguni and sliced into spirals. It is a cool appetizer for the Georgian table.

What it is made of

nadughi (whey cheese)mintsulguni cheesecilantro

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g nadughi or ricotta
  • 1 bunch fresh mint, finely chopped
  • a few sprigs of cilantro
  • 300 g fresh sulguni (optional, for rolls)
  • salt

Method

  1. Drain the nadughi well if it is wet.
  2. Mix with mint, a little cilantro and salt.
  3. For rolls, soften sulguni in hot water and flatten into a thin sheet.
  4. Spread the nadughi mixture over it and roll up tightly.
  5. Chill for 15 minutes and slice into rounds.
  6. Serve with fresh vegetables or bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sulguni and serve the minty nadughi alone for a lighter dish.
  • Use as a spread instead of butter.
  • Add cucumber or radish slices on the side.

History

Nadughi is part of the traditional dairy craft of western Georgia, where nothing from the milk goes to waste. Rolls of sulguni with nadughi are a popular feature of supra feasts.
