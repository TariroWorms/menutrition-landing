Siri paye (Lahori head and trotters stew)

Origin: Pakistan · Lahore

Siri paye is the classic Lahori weekend breakfast: goat head and trotters simmered for hours in a thick, spiced broth until everything is tender and gelatinous. The flavour is deep and meaty with ginger, black pepper and garam masala. It is served very hot in a bowl with fresh naan, green chillies, lemon and ginger, and eaten by tearing bread into the broth.

Recipe

6 servings420 min

Ingredients

  • 1 goat head, halved and cleaned (1.2 kg)
  • 500 g goat trotters
  • 2 onions (300 g)
  • 50 g ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 60 ml oil, salt, 3 l water

Method

  1. Blanch the head and trotters for 10 minutes and rinse well.
  2. Fry sliced onions in oil until browned and add ginger-garlic paste and spices.
  3. Add the meat, fry briefly, then pour in the water.
  4. Cover and simmer on very low heat for 7-8 hours, topping up water if needed.
  5. Skim the fat, garnish with ginger, chilli and coriander and serve with naan.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim the surface fat well before serving.
  • Enjoy it as an occasional treat in a small bowl.
  • Add lemon and fresh ginger rather than extra salt.

History

Siri paye is a famous morning dish in the old city of Lahore, where specialist shops open at dawn. It is made as a slow, communal dish that sustains people for a long day.
