🍕 Pizza Bufalina

Pizza bufalina is a Neapolitan-style pizza topped with tomato, creamy buffalo mozzarella and fresh basil, baked in a wood-fired oven. The cheese is softer and milkier than regular mozzarella, so the pizza is moist and fresh with a charred, puffy rim. It is served whole, one per person, straight from the oven.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g pizza dough
  • 200 g crushed tomatoes
  • 250 g buffalo mozzarella, drained and torn
  • 10 basil leaves
  • 15 ml olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven as hot as possible with a stone or tray inside.
  2. Stretch the dough into two thin rounds with a thicker edge.
  3. Spread the salted tomatoes and bake 4–5 minutes.
  4. Add the buffalo mozzarella and bake 3–4 minutes more until the rim is charred.
  5. Top with basil and a drizzle of olive oil.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 150 g mozzarella for two pizzas; the flavour is strong.
  • Eat half a pizza with a large salad.
  • Skip the extra oil drizzle; 1 tbsp olive oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Buffalo mozzarella (mozzarella di bufala Campana) is made in Campania from the milk of water buffalo, and this pizza is a Margherita-style variation that showcases it. It is a standard choice on menus in Naples and in Neapolitan pizzerias abroad.
