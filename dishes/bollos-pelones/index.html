Bollos pelones (stuffed corn dumplings in tomato sauce)

Origin: Venezuela

Bollos pelones are small balls of corn dough with a filling of minced beef cooked with onion, pepper and garlic. They are poached in salted water or broth and then simmered in a light tomato sauce. Each one is soft and comforting, with a savoury heart. They are served hot in a bowl with sauce and grated white cheese on top.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g precooked corn flour
  • 320 ml warm water or broth
  • 300 g lean minced beef
  • 1 onion (150 g)
  • 1 bell pepper (150 g)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 400 g tomato passata
  • 40 g grated white cheese

Method

  1. Brown the beef with half the onion, pepper and garlic until dry; season and cool.
  2. Knead corn flour, water and salt into a soft dough and divide into 16 balls.
  3. Hollow each ball, add a spoon of filling and close it smoothly.
  4. Cook the rest of the onion, pepper and garlic, add passata and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Poach the bollos in simmering salted water for 10 minutes, then transfer to the sauce for 10 minutes.
  6. Serve with the sauce and grated cheese.

How to make it lighter

  • Use extra-lean beef or minced chicken.
  • Add grated carrot or zucchini to the filling.
  • Serve with a salad and skip extra cheese.

History

The name means roughly bare buns, because unlike hallacas or bollos wrapped in leaves they are cooked without any wrapping. They are a home-style dish found across Venezuela, especially in the central and Andean regions.
