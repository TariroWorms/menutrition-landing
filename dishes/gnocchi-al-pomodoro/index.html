🍅 Gnocchi al Pomodoro

Gnocchi al pomodoro are potato dumplings served in a simple tomato sauce with fresh basil and a shower of grated parmesan. The gnocchi are soft and pillowy, the sauce bright, slightly sweet and lightly acidic. It is one of the most everyday ways to serve gnocchi in Italian homes, eaten hot as a first course or a light main.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g floury potatoes
  • 200 g plain flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes or passata
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 handful fresh basil
  • 30 g parmesan, grated

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins until tender, peel while warm and press through a ricer.
  2. Mix in the flour and salt to a soft dough, roll into ropes and cut into 2 cm pieces.
  3. Warm the garlic in the olive oil, add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt and simmer 15 minutes; tear in half the basil.
  4. Boil the gnocchi in salted water until they float and lift them into the sauce.
  5. Toss gently, then serve topped with parmesan and the remaining basil.

How to make it lighter

  • This is one of the lightest gnocchi dishes — keep it that way by limiting the parmesan to 1 tbsp per plate.
  • Add extra vegetables to the sauce, such as courgette or peppers, for a bigger bowl.
  • Weigh the gnocchi: about 200 g cooked gnocchi with sauce is a satisfying portion.

History

Potato gnocchi became common in Italy after potatoes spread there, and dressing them with tomato sauce and basil is a home-cooking standard throughout the country.
