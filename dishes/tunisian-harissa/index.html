Tunisian harissa (chili paste)

Origin: Tunisia

Tunisian harissa is a thick red paste of dried chilies pounded with garlic, caraway, coriander and olive oil. It is hot, smoky and aromatic. A spoonful is stirred into stews and couscous, or served in a small dish with bread and olive oil as a starter.

What it is made of

chili peppergarlicolive oilcarawaycoriandersalt

Recipe

10 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g dried red chilies
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp ground caraway
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 100 ml olive oil

Method

  1. Soak the chilies in hot water for 20 minutes and drain.
  2. Remove the stems and seeds for a milder paste.
  3. Blend with garlic, spices and salt.
  4. Add the oil gradually to make a thick paste.
  5. Store in a jar under a thin layer of oil in the fridge.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only a small spoonful.
  • Deseed the chilies for a mild version.
  • Check the salt if you have high blood pressure.

History

Chilies reached North Africa from the Americas in the 16th century and harissa has since become the national condiment of Tunisia. Each family has its own balance of spices.
