Vinegret (beet salad)

Origin: Belarus

Vinegret is a ruby-red salad of boiled beets, potatoes and carrots mixed with crunchy pickles and sauerkraut. Unrefined sunflower oil gives it a nutty aroma, while the pickles bring sourness. It is a winter staple, eaten as a starter or alongside herring and bread.

Recipe

6 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g beets
  • 300 g potatoes
  • 200 g carrots
  • 150 g pickled cucumbers
  • 150 g sauerkraut
  • 60 g onion
  • 40 ml sunflower oil

Method

  1. Boil the beets, potatoes and carrots in their skins until tender, then cool.
  2. Peel and cut everything into small even cubes.
  3. Dice the pickles and onion; squeeze the sauerkraut.
  4. Toss the beets with a little oil first so they don't stain everything.
  5. Combine all vegetables, dress with the remaining oil and salt to taste.
  6. Chill for 30 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the beets instead of boiling for deeper flavor without extra fat.
  • Use less oil and add a splash of pickle brine for tang.
  • Add boiled beans for more protein and fiber.

History

The salad is shared across the former Soviet space and its name comes from the French word for a vinegar dressing. In Belarus it became a fixture of holiday and everyday tables thanks to cheap root vegetables and home ferments.
