Serbian moussaka is a hearty baked dish of thinly sliced potatoes layered with seasoned ground beef. Instead of a flour-based sauce, it is finished with a custard of eggs and yogurt that sets into a golden top. Parsley freshens the meat, and it is served in squares as a filling family meal.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes, peeled and thinly sliced
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 20 g parsley, chopped
  • 3 eggs
  • 300 g plain yogurt
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Brown the onion and beef in the oil with paprika, salt and pepper; stir in the parsley.
  2. Oil a baking dish and layer potatoes, then meat, ending with potatoes.
  3. Add 150 ml water, cover with foil and bake at 200 °C for 40 minutes.
  4. Whisk the eggs with the yogurt and a pinch of salt, pour over and bake uncovered 20 minutes until golden.
  5. Rest 10 minutes before cutting.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean ground beef (5–10% fat) and drain the fat after browning.
  • Low-fat yogurt in the topping trims calories without changing the texture much.
  • Serve one square with a big green salad and skip extra sides.

History

Moussaka came to the Balkans through Ottoman cooking, and in Serbia the potato version became a classic home dish served at family tables.
