Hoe deopbap (raw fish rice bowl)

Origin: South Korea

Hoe deopbap is a rice bowl topped with slices of raw fish, shredded lettuce, cucumber and other fresh vegetables. A sweet, sour and spicy chogochujang sauce is stirred through everything before eating. It is clean, cool and savory and is a common lunch in coastal cities.

Recipe

1 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g cooked rice
  • 120 g sushi-grade white fish or salmon, sliced
  • 50 g lettuce, shredded
  • 50 g cucumber, julienned
  • 2 tbsp gochujang (40 g)
  • 1 tbsp vinegar (15 ml)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil and 1 tsp sugar

Method

  1. Mix gochujang, vinegar, sugar and sesame oil into the sauce.
  2. Place warm rice in a wide bowl.
  3. Arrange lettuce and cucumber over the rice.
  4. Lay the sashimi slices on top.
  5. Add sauce and mix everything just before eating.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only very fresh, properly handled fish.
  • Use less sauce to cut sugar and sodium.
  • Add more vegetables and a little less rice.

History

Raw fish, called hoe, is a long-standing part of Korea's seafood tradition, especially on the coasts. Serving it over rice with vegetables and sauce is a practical, filling way to enjoy it.
