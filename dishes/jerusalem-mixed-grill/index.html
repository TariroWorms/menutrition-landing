Jerusalem mixed grill

Origin: Israel · Jerusalem

Jerusalem mixed grill is a sizzling mix of chicken hearts, livers and sometimes spleen or lamb, cooked on a hot flat griddle with onion, cumin, turmeric and paprika. The meat is rich, salty and slightly smoky. It is stuffed into a pita or served on a plate with salads and tahini.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g chicken hearts
  • 300 g chicken livers
  • 300 g chicken breast, cut small
  • 2 onions (300 g)
  • 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp paprika
  • 3 tbsp oil (45 ml), 4 pitas

Method

  1. Trim and cut the hearts and livers into bite-size pieces.
  2. Heat the oil on a large griddle or pan until very hot.
  3. Cook the hearts and chicken first for 8 minutes with the onion.
  4. Add the livers and spices and cook 5 more minutes.
  5. Season with salt and stir until browned.
  6. Serve in pita with salad, tahini and pickles.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and more onion.
  • Serve with salad instead of fries.
  • Choose more breast meat and fewer livers if you limit cholesterol.

History

The dish is a street-food specialty of Jerusalem, associated with Mahane Yehuda market and its grill houses. Its roots lie in the offal grills of Middle Eastern cities and the Jewish communities there.
