Pumpkin Risotto (Risotto alla Zucca)

Origin: Italy · Lombardy & Veneto

Risotto alla zucca is a northern Italian autumn dish of Arborio rice slowly cooked with pumpkin until the squash melts into the grains. Butter and Parmesan stirred in at the end give a velvety, gently sweet result, and crisp sage leaves add an earthy note. It is served hot in shallow bowls, often with extra grated cheese.

What it is made of

arborio ricepumpkinbutterparmesansage

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g Arborio rice
  • 500 g pumpkin, diced
  • 40 g butter
  • 60 g Parmesan, grated
  • 8 sage leaves
  • 1.2 l hot water
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Melt half the butter, add the pumpkin and cook for 8 minutes until soft, mashing some of it.
  2. Add the rice and stir for 2 minutes to toast.
  3. Add hot water a ladle at a time, stirring, for about 18 minutes.
  4. Fry the sage leaves in the remaining butter until crisp.
  5. Off the heat, stir in the Parmesan, season and top with the sage.

How to make it lighter

  • 1 tbsp butter ≈ 100 kcal: halving the butter saves around 150 kcal per pan.
  • Use more pumpkin and less rice — the squash adds volume for far fewer calories.
  • Grate the Parmesan finely so a smaller amount covers more.

History

Pumpkin has long been grown in the Po valley, and risotto alla zucca is a seasonal classic around Mantua and the Veneto. It is cooked in homes and restaurants across northern Italy every autumn.
