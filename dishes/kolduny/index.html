🥟 Kolduny

Origin: Belarus · Lithuania

Kolduny are Belarusian dumplings made from grated potato dough wrapped around a filling of ground pork and onion. They are boiled until the potato casing turns soft and pillowy while the meat inside stays juicy. Kolduny are served hot with a spoonful of sour cream.

What it is made of

potato doughground porkonionsour cream

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg potatoes, finely grated and squeezed
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 300 g ground pork
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 100 g sour cream to serve

Method

  1. Mix the squeezed potato with the flour and salt into a firm dough.
  2. Season the pork with onion, salt and pepper.
  3. Flatten a spoonful of potato dough in your palm, add a little filling and seal into a patty.
  4. Boil in salted water for 15–20 minutes until they float and are cooked through.
  5. Serve with sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean ground pork to lower the fat.
  • Serve a small spoon of sour cream, or dilute it with a little water — 1 tbsp sour cream is about 30 kcal.
  • Pair with a cucumber and dill salad instead of a second helping.

History

Kolduny are part of the shared cuisine of Belarus, Lithuania and Poland, where the name is used for several kinds of filled dumplings; the potato-dough version is especially associated with Belarus.
