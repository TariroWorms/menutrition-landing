🍫 Chocolate Fondant

Fondant au chocolat is a small, rich chocolate cake made from dark chocolate, butter, eggs, sugar and a little flour, baked just long enough to set the outside while the centre stays molten. Cut open, it releases a warm flow of liquid chocolate. It is served warm straight from the ramekin, often with fresh berries.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 120 g dark chocolate
  • 100 g butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 80 g sugar
  • 30 g flour

Method

  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together over a gentle heat.
  2. Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale, then fold in the chocolate.
  3. Sift in the flour and fold until just combined.
  4. Divide between 4 buttered ramekins.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 8–10 minutes until the edges are set but the centre wobbles.
  6. Turn out or serve directly in the ramekins while warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake in 6 smaller ramekins instead of 4 for lighter portions.
  • Serve with fresh berries instead of extra sauce.
  • Share one fondant between two at a restaurant.

History

Molten chocolate cakes became a restaurant favourite in France and the United States in the 1980s and 1990s, with several chefs credited with popularising them. The French fondant is now a standard of bistro dessert menus.
