Chả cá Lã Vọng (Hanoi turmeric fish with dill)

Origin: Vietnam · Hanoi

Chả cá Lã Vọng is a Hanoi classic of firm fish marinated in turmeric and galangal, pan-fried and finished at the table with huge handfuls of dill and spring onion. It is served with rice vermicelli, peanuts, herbs and a pungent fermented shrimp dip.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g firm white fish fillet
  • 1 tbsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp galangal, grated
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 100 g dill and spring onion
  • 200 g cooked rice vermicelli
  • 30 g peanuts
  • herbs, fish dipping sauce

Method

  1. Marinate fish with turmeric, galangal and a little salt for 30 minutes.
  2. Grill or pan-fry the fish until just cooked.
  3. Heat oil in a pan and add the fish.
  4. Add dill and spring onion and toss until wilted.
  5. Serve with vermicelli, peanuts, herbs and dip.

How to make it lighter

  • Pan-fry in less oil or grill the fish.
  • Add more raw herbs and lettuce.
  • Use a lighter dipping sauce to cut salt.

History

The dish is named after a famous restaurant street in Hanoi's Old Quarter. The dill-and-turmeric pairing is unusual in Vietnamese cooking and is strongly tied to the capital.
