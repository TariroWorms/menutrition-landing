🦑 Black Paella (Arròs Negre)

Origin: Spain · Valencia & Catalonia

Paella negra, known in Valencian and Catalan as arròs negre, is a striking black rice dish coloured with squid ink. Short-grain rice is cooked in fish stock with squid and garlic until it is glossy, deep black and full of briny sea flavour. It is served with a spoonful of aioli, the garlic-and-oil emulsion that softens the intensity of the ink.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g short-grain rice
  • 400 g squid, cleaned and diced
  • 8 g squid ink (2 sachets)
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 tomato, grated
  • 1 l fish stock
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, salt
  • For the aioli: 1 egg yolk, 1 clove garlic, 100 ml olive oil, salt

Method

  1. Make the aioli: crush the garlic with salt, whisk in the yolk, then add the oil drop by drop until thick.
  2. Heat the oil in a paella pan and sauté the squid for 3 minutes; add the garlic and grated tomato and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Dissolve the squid ink in the hot fish stock and pour it into the pan; season with salt.
  4. Bring to a boil, scatter in the rice and simmer for 18 minutes without stirring.
  5. Let it rest for 5 minutes and serve with the aioli on the side.

How to make it lighter

  • Aioli is mostly oil: 1 tbsp ≈ 100 kcal, so use a teaspoon per serving.
  • Squid is lean protein; adding more squid and less rice lightens the plate.
  • Pair with a tomato or green salad to round out the meal.

History

Arròs negre is a traditional coastal rice of the Valencian and Catalan Mediterranean, where fishermen used cuttlefish or squid and their ink. It is eaten today in seafood restaurants along the eastern Spanish coast.
