🍋 Lemon Tagliatelle

Origin: Italy · Amalfi Coast

Lemon tagliatelle are ribbon pasta tossed in a quick sauce of butter, cream, parmesan and plenty of lemon zest and juice. The citrus cuts through the richness, so the dish tastes bright and fresh while still creamy. It cooks in the time it takes to boil the pasta and is served as a light first course, often in spring and summer.

What it is made of

tagliatellelemonbutterparmesancream

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 160 g dried tagliatelle
  • 1 unwaxed lemon, zest and juice
  • 20 g butter
  • 80 ml cream
  • 30 g parmesan, grated
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the tagliatelle in salted water until al dente; keep a cup of cooking water.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan, add the cream and lemon zest and warm gently.
  3. Add the pasta, parmesan, half the lemon juice and a splash of cooking water; toss until creamy.
  4. Taste, add more juice and black pepper, and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Lean on lemon zest and pepper for flavour and use less butter and cream.
  • Measure the parmesan: 30 g for two plates is enough to season the sauce.
  • Serve with a large side of grilled vegetables to bulk up a smaller pasta portion.

History

Pasta with lemon is associated with southern Italy, particularly the Amalfi Coast and Sorrento, which are known for their large fragrant lemons. It is now a popular simple pasta both in Italy and abroad.
