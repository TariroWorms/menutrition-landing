Ceviche de concha (black clam ceviche)

Origin: Ecuador · Esmeraldas
210kcal

Ceviche de concha is made from conchas negras, small black clams that live in the mangrove mud of northern Ecuador. The clams are opened raw and their dark, briny juice becomes part of the marinade with lime and onion. It tastes intensely of the sea, sharp and a little metallic. People eat it with chifles or toasted corn and often a cold drink.

What it is made of

Black clamslimered oniontomatocilantrochili pepper

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 50 black clams, very fresh
  • 1 red onion, finely chopped
  • 120 ml lime juice
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1 small chili, minced
  • cilantro, salt

Method

  1. Scrub the clams well under cold running water.
  2. Open them with a small knife over a bowl, saving all the dark juice.
  3. Rinse the onion in salted water and drain.
  4. Combine clams, their juice, lime juice, onion, tomato and chili; season with salt.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes, add cilantro and serve at once with toasted corn.

How to make it lighter

  • Buy clams only from trusted sellers and eat them the same day.
  • Pregnant women and people with weak immunity should avoid raw shellfish.
  • Pair with toasted corn rather than fried plantain to keep it light.

History

Black clams have long been gathered by hand in the mangroves of Esmeraldas and El Oro. Many Ecuadorians consider this ceviche a restorative and a reputed aphrodisiac. Because the clams are eaten raw, freshness is essential.
