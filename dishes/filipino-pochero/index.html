Pochero (Filipino beef and plantain stew)

Origin: Philippines

Pochero is a hearty Filipino stew of tender beef in a light tomato broth, with saba bananas, chickpeas, potatoes and cabbage. The plantains lend gentle sweetness against the savory meat. It is ladled over rice, sometimes with a sauce of mashed eggplant on the side.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g beef shank, cubed
  • 2 saba bananas, sliced
  • 150 g cooked chickpeas
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 200 g tomato sauce
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1.2 l water
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Simmer the beef in water for 60 minutes until tender.
  2. Sauté garlic, onion and tomato sauce, then add to the pot.
  3. Add potatoes and chickpeas and cook 10 minutes.
  4. Add bananas and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Add cabbage, season and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim the fat from the broth.
  • Use lean beef cuts.
  • Add extra cabbage and green beans to boost fiber.

History

Pochero descends from the Spanish cocido and olla podrida, adapted in the Philippines with local bananas and tomato. It remains a classic dish for family Sundays and celebrations.
