Lomi (Batangas thick noodle soup)

Origin: Philippines · Batangas

Lomi is a thick, almost stew-like noodle soup made with thick egg noodles, pork and liver. The broth is thickened with cornstarch and swirled egg until silky. It is savory and rich with a hint of soy and garlic. Diners add calamansi and soy sauce at the table.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g fresh thick egg noodles
  • 200 g pork, sliced
  • 100 g pork liver
  • 1.5 litres pork broth
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch in water
  • 2 eggs, garlic, onion, soy sauce

Method

  1. Sauté garlic and onion, then the pork and liver slices.
  2. Pour in the broth and simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Add the noodles and cook 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the cornstarch slurry until thick.
  5. Drizzle beaten eggs into the soup and stir; season with soy sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork and more cabbage or carrots.
  • Dilute the broth slightly to cut sodium.
  • Use whole-wheat noodles for more fibre.

History

Lomi comes from the Chinese lo mein tradition, brought by Hokkien-speaking settlers, and is especially associated with Batangas province. It is a popular dish in the cool season and on rainy days. Variants add shrimp, quail eggs or chicharrón.
