Kafteji (Tunisian fried vegetable hash)

Origin: Tunisia

Kafteji is a Tunisian street dish of fried potatoes, peppers, zucchini and pumpkin chopped together on a board and mixed with a fried egg and harissa. It is hot, savory and pleasantly oily. It is often served in a sandwich or on a plate with a squeeze of lemon.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 potatoes
  • 2 zucchini
  • 2 green peppers
  • 200 g pumpkin
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp harissa
  • oil for frying
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • lemon

Method

  1. Cut the vegetables and fry them until golden.
  2. Drain well on paper towels.
  3. Fry the eggs lightly.
  4. Chop the vegetables with the eggs into small pieces.
  5. Mix with harissa, cumin and lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the vegetables instead of frying.
  • Use less oil and more peppers.
  • Serve in a small portion with salad.

History

It is typical street food in Tunis, cooked to order by chopping the hot vegetables with a cleaver. It shows how vegetables are the base of much Tunisian cooking.
