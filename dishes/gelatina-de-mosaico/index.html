Gelatina de mosaico (mosaic jelly)

Origin: Mexico

Gelatina de mosaico is a party dessert of colored jelly cubes suspended in a white gelatin made with milk and condensed milk. Each slice shows a confetti of red, green, orange and purple. It is cool, sweet and wobbly, and turns up at birthdays, school fairs and family gatherings.

Recipe

12 servings360 min

Ingredients

  • 3 packets flavored jelly (85 g each, different colors)
  • 750 ml hot water for the colored jellies
  • 30 g unflavored gelatin
  • 120 ml cold water
  • 400 g sweetened condensed milk
  • 500 ml milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Dissolve each jelly packet in 250 ml hot water, pour into separate dishes and chill until firm.
  2. Cut the set jellies into 1–2 cm cubes and mix them in an oiled ring mould.
  3. Bloom the unflavored gelatin in cold water, then melt it gently.
  4. Whisk the condensed milk, milk and vanilla, then stir in the melted gelatin.
  5. Pour the cooled milk mixture over the cubes and chill for at least 4 hours before unmoulding.

How to make it lighter

  • Use sugar-free jelly packets to cut the sugar sharply.
  • Replace half the condensed milk with evaporated milk.
  • Serve thin slices; it is light in texture but still sweet.

History

Gelatin desserts became hugely popular in Mexico in the twentieth century, when packaged jelly powders reached every home. Street vendors and home cooks turned them into an art, and mosaic jelly is one of the most loved styles.
