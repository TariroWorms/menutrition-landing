🍝 Gnocchi al Ragù

Gnocchi al ragù are potato dumplings served with a slow-simmered beef and tomato ragù and finished with grated parmesan. The soft gnocchi soak up the thick, meaty sauce, making a hearty and comforting plate. It is a popular Sunday and family dish in Italy, served hot as a first course or a full meal.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g floury potatoes
  • 200 g plain flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 400 g minced beef
  • 1 onion and 1 carrot, finely chopped
  • 400 g passata
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 40 g parmesan, grated

Method

  1. Soften the onion and carrot in the oil for 8 minutes, add the beef and brown it well.
  2. Add the passata, a pinch of salt and 200 ml water, cover and simmer gently for 1–1½ hours.
  3. Meanwhile boil the potatoes, peel, rice and mix with the flour and salt; shape into ropes and cut into 2 cm pieces.
  4. Boil the gnocchi in salted water until they float, toss with the ragù and serve with parmesan.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced beef and skim any fat from the top of the ragù.
  • Add extra finely chopped carrot and onion to the ragù for more volume with less meat per plate.
  • Serve about 200 g with a side salad instead of bread.

History

Ragù, a meat sauce simmered for hours, is made in many regional styles across Italy, and serving it over potato gnocchi is a traditional Sunday pairing in many Italian homes.
