Hortobágyi palacsinta (meat-filled pancakes)

Origin: Hungary · Hortobágy

Hortobágyi palacsinta is a savoury pancake filled with finely cut stewed meat and rolled, then topped with paprika cream sauce. The pancake is soft, the filling rich and the sauce lightly tangy. It is served warm as a main course.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 300 ml milk
  • 400 g cooked veal or chicken, minced
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 150 g sour cream

Method

  1. Whisk flour, eggs, milk and a pinch of salt into a batter.
  2. Fry thin pancakes in a lightly oiled pan.
  3. Cook the onion, add paprika and the meat with a splash of water.
  4. Fill each pancake and roll up.
  5. Mix sour cream with a spoon of paprika sauce and pour over.
  6. Bake at 180 °C for 10 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and little oil for the pancakes.
  • Choose lean chicken or veal for the filling.
  • Swap sour cream for light yoghurt.

History

The dish is named after the Hortobágy, the great grassland of eastern Hungary known for its herdsmen and cattle. It is a classic of restaurant menus, often a way to use leftover stewed meat.
