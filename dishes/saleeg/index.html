🍚 Chicken saleeg

Saleeg is a Hejazi rice dish in which short-grain rice is simmered in chicken broth and then milk until soft and creamy, like a savoury risotto. It is flavoured with cardamom, enriched with butter, and served topped with pieces of chicken that are often browned before serving. It is mild, comforting and very soft.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken (about 1.2 kg), cut into pieces
  • 400 g short-grain rice, rinsed
  • 750 ml milk
  • 1.2 l water
  • 4 cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick, 1 bay leaf
  • 1 onion, halved
  • 30 g butter
  • Salt

Method

  1. Boil the chicken with the onion, cardamom, cinnamon, bay leaf and salt for about 40 minutes; strain and keep the broth.
  2. Cook the rice in about 1 l of the broth, stirring often, until it absorbs most of it.
  3. Add the milk gradually and keep stirring over low heat until thick and creamy.
  4. Brown the chicken pieces briefly in a hot oven or pan.
  5. Stir the butter into the rice and serve with the chicken on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken and remove the fat from the broth.
  • Halve the butter: 10 g butter ≈ 72 kcal.
  • Serve with a large Arabic salad to keep the rice portion smaller.

History

Saleeg is associated with the Hejaz region of western Saudi Arabia, especially Taif, Mecca and Jeddah, where it is a popular family dish, often served in winter and at gatherings.
