Rogan josh is a Kashmiri curry of lamb braised slowly with onion, garlic, ginger and yogurt in a deep red sauce coloured by mild Kashmiri chili. The sauce is aromatic rather than fiery, with warm spices and a gentle tang from the yogurt. The lamb becomes meltingly tender, and the curry is served with rice or flatbread and fresh coriander.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 2 onions, finely sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves and 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri chili powder
  • 200 g plain yogurt, whisked
  • 1 tsp ground fennel, 4 cardamom pods, 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, salt
  • Fresh coriander to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oil, add the whole spices and fry the onions until deep golden.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger and lamb and brown for 8–10 minutes.
  3. Stir in the chili powder and fennel, then add the yogurt a spoonful at a time, stirring so it does not split.
  4. Add 300 ml water and salt, cover and simmer for 75–90 minutes until the lamb is tender.
  5. Reduce the sauce if needed and finish with coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean leg of lamb and trim the fat before cooking.
  • Cut the oil to 1 tbsp and use low-fat yogurt.
  • Serve with a small portion of rice and a side of salad or vegetables.

History

Rogan josh is a signature dish of Kashmiri cooking and is often linked to Persian influence that arrived with the Mughals; the name is often explained as rogan, 'oil' or 'fat', and josh, 'heat' or 'intensity'. It later became a staple of Indian restaurant menus worldwide.
