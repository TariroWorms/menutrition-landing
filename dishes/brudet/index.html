Brudet (Dalmatian fish stew)

Origin: Croatia · Dalmatia

Brudet is a fish stew from the Adriatic coast, cooked slowly with onion, garlic, tomato and a splash of white wine. The sauce is rich and gently tangy, and the fish stays tender. It is traditionally ladled over soft polenta or served with bread.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g mixed white fish, in pieces
  • 2 onions
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 150 ml white wine
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • parsley
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Soften the sliced onions in olive oil.
  2. Add garlic and cook for one minute.
  3. Add tomatoes and wine and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Lay the fish in the sauce without stirring.
  5. Cook gently for 15 minutes, shaking the pot.
  6. Finish with parsley and serve with polenta.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean fish and less oil.
  • Serve with vegetables instead of polenta.
  • Skip the wine for a lighter sauce.

History

It grew out of the fishermen's kitchens of the Dalmatian coast, where the day's catch was cooked in a single pot. Similar stews appear across the Mediterranean under different names.
