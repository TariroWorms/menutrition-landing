This version of pad kra pao uses beef: thin slices or mince stir-fried at high heat with Thai basil, garlic and plenty of chilli, seasoned with fish sauce and a little sugar. The meat comes out savoury and spicy, fragrant with basil, and is served straight over a plate of steamed jasmine rice for a quick, filling one-dish meal.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g beef, thinly sliced or minced
  • 1 large handful of Thai basil leaves
  • 4 garlic cloves and 3–5 small chillies, pounded
  • 1.5 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300 g cooked jasmine rice

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a wok and fry the garlic and chilli for 20 seconds.
  2. Add the beef and stir-fry over high heat for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Season with fish sauce, sugar and a splash of water.
  4. Toss in the basil until just wilted and serve over rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean beef such as sirloin or 5% fat mince.
  • Use 1 tbsp of oil or less — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Add sliced green beans or onion to the stir-fry and serve less rice.

History

Beef is one of the standard protein choices for kra pao at Thai street stalls, alongside pork and chicken. The dish is eaten across Thailand as an everyday rice plate.
