Lazy golubtsy (lazy cabbage rolls)

Origin: Russia
435kcal
per serving · 300 g

Lazy golubtsy have all the flavours of stuffed cabbage without the rolling: finely shredded cabbage is mixed straight into minced meat and rice and shaped into soft patties. They are browned lightly and stewed in a tomato and sour cream sauce until tender. They are served hot, often with extra sauce and bread or mashed potatoes.

Recipe

5 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef
  • 400 g white cabbage
  • 80 g rice
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 egg
  • 250 ml tomato sauce
  • 120 g sour cream
  • 200 ml water
  • salt, pepper

Method

  1. Shred the cabbage finely, blanch for 2 minutes and squeeze dry.
  2. Parboil the rice for 7 minutes.
  3. Mix mince, cabbage, rice, half the onion, egg, salt and pepper; shape oval patties.
  4. Brown the patties briefly on both sides.
  5. Fry the rest of the onion with grated carrot, add tomato sauce, sour cream and water, pour over the patties and stew covered for 35 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Increase the cabbage share for fewer calories.
  • Bake instead of frying before stewing.
  • Use low-fat sour cream or yogurt in the sauce.

History

The 'lazy' version became a popular weeknight shortcut in Soviet home cooking and in kindergartens and canteens. Some families bake them in the oven instead of stewing them on the hob.
