Mango kunafa is a summer twist on the classic Arab dessert: kataifi strands are toasted in ghee until crisp and golden, then layered with whipped cream and fresh mango. A little sugar sweetens the cream, and the fruit brings a juicy, tropical freshness that contrasts with the crunchy pastry. It is served chilled, in a dish or in individual cups.

Recipe

6 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g kataifi (shredded kunafa pastry)
  • 60 g ghee, melted
  • 300 ml cream
  • 40 g sugar
  • 2 ripe mangoes, diced

Method

  1. Pull the kataifi apart, toss with the ghee and toast in a pan over medium heat, stirring, for 8–10 minutes until golden and crisp. Stir in half the sugar at the end and cool.
  2. Whip the cream with the remaining sugar to soft peaks.
  3. Layer the toasted kataifi, cream and mango in a dish or cups, finishing with mango on top.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes and serve cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more mango and less cream — the fruit gives volume for fewer calories.
  • Toast the kataifi with half the ghee in a non-stick pan.
  • Serve in small cups to keep portions in check.

History

Mango kunafa is a modern variation on traditional kunafa that became popular in Gulf cafés and home kitchens. It is especially common in the summer mango season in the UAE and Saudi Arabia.
