Muraveinik (anthill cake)

Origin: Russia

Muraveinik, or anthill cake, is made by baking a simple shortbread dough, breaking it into small pieces and mixing them with a cream of boiled condensed milk and butter plus chopped walnuts. The mass is heaped into a mound and sprinkled with poppy seeds or grated chocolate to look like an anthill. It is crunchy, caramel-sweet and rich.

Recipe

10 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 200 g cold butter
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 80 g sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 380 g boiled condensed milk
  • 100 g soft butter for the cream
  • 100 g walnuts

Method

  1. Rub the cold butter into flour, sugar and baking powder, add sour cream and knead quickly.
  2. Chill, then grate or break the dough onto a tray.
  3. Bake at 190 °C for 15–20 minutes until golden and cool.
  4. Beat the boiled condensed milk with the soft butter and mix in the crumbs and chopped walnuts.
  5. Heap into a mound, sprinkle with grated chocolate and chill for 3 hours.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve small portions; it is very energy-dense.
  • Reduce butter in the cream.
  • Use oat cookies for part of the base.

History

Muraveinik became a popular home cake in the late Soviet period because it needed only basic ingredients and no special skills. It is still a common choice for birthdays.
