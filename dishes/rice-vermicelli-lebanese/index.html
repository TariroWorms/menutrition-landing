🍚 Lebanese Rice with Vermicelli

Origin: Lebanon · Levant

Lebanese rice with vermicelli is the everyday Levantine side dish: short pieces of vermicelli are toasted golden in butter, then cooked with rice and salted water until fluffy. The toasted noodles add a nutty flavour and flecks of brown to the white grains. It is served alongside stews, grilled meats and vegetable dishes cooked in tomato sauce.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 30 g butter
  • 60 g vermicelli, broken into short pieces
  • 300 g long-grain or Egyptian rice, rinsed and soaked 15 minutes
  • 600 ml boiling water
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a pot over medium heat and fry the vermicelli, stirring, until deep golden.
  2. Add the drained rice and stir for a minute to coat.
  3. Pour in the boiling water and salt, bring to a boil, then cover and cook on low 15–18 minutes.
  4. Turn off the heat, rest covered 5 minutes and fluff with a fork.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the butter; 1 tbsp of butter is about 100 kcal.
  • Use less vermicelli and more rice, or the reverse—both are similar in calories, so watch the portion instead.
  • Serve one scoop alongside a large portion of vegetable stew.

History

Rice with toasted vermicelli is cooked in homes across Lebanon, Syria, Palestine and Jordan, and is equally common in Egypt and Turkey. It is the default rice served with most Levantine stews today.
