Sirene po shopski (baked cheese in a clay pot)

Origin: Bulgaria · Shopluk

Sirene po shopski is white brined cheese baked in a small clay dish with tomatoes, peppers and an egg until bubbling and golden. It is salty, soft and rich, with juicy vegetables. It is served hot straight from the pot with fresh bread.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g white brined cheese, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp dried savory

Method

  1. Butter the clay dishes.
  2. Layer slices of cheese, tomato and pepper.
  3. Sprinkle with paprika and savory.
  4. Crack an egg over each dish.
  5. Bake at 200 C for 12-15 minutes until the cheese softens and the egg sets.
  6. Serve hot with bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Pick a lower-fat cheese and soak it in water to reduce salt.
  • Add extra vegetables such as zucchini.
  • Skip extra butter.

History

The dish is named after the Shopi, people of the western Bulgarian region Shopluk, and reflects the use of sheep or cow cheese in local cooking. It is a typical meal in Bulgarian taverns.
