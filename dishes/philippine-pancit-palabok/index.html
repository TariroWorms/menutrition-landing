Pancit palabok (rice noodles in shrimp sauce)

Origin: Philippines

Pancit palabok is a platter of rice noodles covered with a thick, orange sauce of shrimp broth and annatto. It is topped with shrimp, ground pork, boiled egg, scallions and crushed chicharrón for crunch. The taste is savory and briny, with a smoky note from flaked smoked fish. Calamansi is squeezed over just before eating.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g rice noodles
  • 200 g shrimp, in the shell
  • 150 g ground pork
  • 3 tbsp annatto water or powder, 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 boiled eggs, 30 g chicharrón
  • scallions, garlic, fish sauce, calamansi and smoked fish flakes

Method

  1. Cook the noodles, drain and keep warm.
  2. Boil the shrimp briefly, peel them and simmer the shells in the cooking water for stock; strain.
  3. Cook garlic and pork, add flour, annatto and stock until thick.
  4. Season with fish sauce.
  5. Pour the sauce over the noodles and top with shrimp, egg, scallions, fish flakes and chicharrón; serve with calamansi.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more shrimp and less pork and chicharrón.
  • Serve with a side of vegetables.
  • Season with calamansi rather than more fish sauce.

History

Palabok is associated with the town of Malabon, where a famous version is made with generous seafood toppings. It descends from pancit dishes introduced by Chinese traders and was adapted with local annatto and shrimp. It is a staple of birthdays and merienda tables.
