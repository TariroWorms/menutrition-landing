🍲 Teleća čorba (veal soup)

Teleća čorba is a Balkan veal soup with tender cubes of meat, carrot, potato and celery in a clear broth that is finished with a beaten egg and lemon juice. The egg and lemon give the soup a silky body and a gentle tang without making it heavy. It is served hot as a starter, often at weddings and celebrations.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g veal, cubed
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 1 celery stalk or 100 g celeriac, diced
  • 1 egg
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1.8 l water, salt, black pepper, parsley

Method

  1. Brown the veal lightly in the oil, cover with water and simmer for 1 hour, skimming.
  2. Add the carrots, celery and potatoes and cook for 20–25 minutes until tender.
  3. Beat the egg with the lemon juice and whisk in a ladle of hot broth.
  4. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the egg mixture; do not boil again.
  5. Season and serve with parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • The soup is light on its own — enjoy a full bowl as a starter.
  • Choose lean veal and skim the fat off the broth.
  • Add more vegetables and less potato for a lighter bowl.

History

Čorba comes from the Turkish çorba, 'soup', a legacy of Ottoman influence in the Balkans. Veal čorba is a standard dish of Serbian kafana taverns and festive meals across the former Yugoslavia.
