🌯 Chicken tikka roll

A chicken tikka roll is flaky paratha, cooked with a layer of egg on one side, rolled around pieces of grilled chicken tikka, raw onion, a squeeze of lemon and green mint chutney. It is warm, juicy and tangy, with a crisp, buttery-tasting wrap. It is a favourite street snack in India and Pakistan, eaten on the go from paper wrappers.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g chicken tikka (yogurt-marinated and grilled), in strips
  • 4 parathas
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp mint chutney
  • 1 lemon, salt, chaat masala
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Warm a paratha in a lightly oiled pan.
  2. Pour a beaten egg over the top, flip so the egg side cooks and sticks to the bread, about 1 minute.
  3. Lay the chicken tikka, onion, chutney, a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of chaat masala along the egg side.
  4. Roll tightly, wrap the bottom in paper and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a thinner paratha or use half an egg per roll.
  • Load the roll with extra onion and cucumber and less chicken-and-bread.
  • One roll with a fresh salad keeps the meal moderate.

History

Egg-coated paratha rolls are associated with Kolkata, where the kathi roll is usually credited to a city restaurant in the early 20th century. Chicken tikka fillings are now among the most popular versions across South Asia and beyond.
