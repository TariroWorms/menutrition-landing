Chicken cacciatore (pollo alla cacciatora)

Origin: Italy · Central Italy

Pollo alla cacciatora, "hunter's style" chicken, is an Italian braise of chicken pieces simmered with tomatoes, bell peppers, olives, capers and white wine. The chicken becomes tender and falls from the bone, and the sauce is thick, rustic and savoury with a briny edge from the olives and capers. It is served hot as a main course.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg chicken pieces, skin on
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 150 ml dry white wine
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 60 g black olives and 1 tbsp capers
  • Salt, pepper and a sprig of rosemary

Method

  1. Season the chicken and brown it in the oil on all sides; set aside.
  2. Soften the onion, garlic and peppers in the same pan for 8 minutes.
  3. Add the wine and let it bubble for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the tomatoes, olives, capers and rosemary and return the chicken.
  5. Cover and simmer 35–40 minutes until tender; season and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin before cooking to cut much of the fat.
  • Use 1 tbsp oil instead of 2: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Add more peppers and tomato to the sauce for a bigger plate with few extra calories.

History

Dishes "alla cacciatora" are named after hunters, and the style is found in many Italian regions with local differences, from versions with tomato and peppers to ones with vinegar and rosemary. Today chicken cacciatore is a classic of Italian home cooking and of Italian restaurants abroad.
