Ikan woku (Manado fish in yellow herb sauce)

Origin: Indonesia · Manado

Ikan woku is fish cooked in a bright yellow sauce of turmeric, chili, shallot and ginger with a generous handful of basil, lemongrass, lime leaf and tomato. The broth is thin, fragrant and sour-spicy rather than creamy. It is eaten with rice.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g fish steaks or fillets
  • 6 shallots, 3 garlic cloves, 3 cm ginger, 3 cm turmeric, 4 chilies, ground
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised
  • 4 lime leaves
  • 2 tomatoes, wedged
  • 2 handfuls basil
  • 300 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt and lime juice

Method

  1. Rub the fish with salt and lime juice.
  2. Fry the ground paste with lemongrass and lime leaves until fragrant.
  3. Add the water and bring to a boil.
  4. Add the fish and tomatoes and simmer 12 minutes.
  5. Add the basil and stir gently.
  6. Serve hot with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Almost no added fat, so it is a light fish dish.
  • Choose oily fish for omega-3.
  • Reduce chilies for a milder version.

History

Woku is a cooking style of the Minahasan people of North Sulawesi, where herbs are used in abundance. It is also made with chicken.
