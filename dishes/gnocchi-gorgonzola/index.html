🧀 Gnocchi with Gorgonzola and Walnuts

Origin: Italy · Lombardy & Piedmont

Gnocchi with gorgonzola are soft potato dumplings tossed in a creamy sauce of melted gorgonzola and cream, scattered with chopped walnuts. The blue cheese gives a pungent, tangy richness that contrasts with the mild, pillowy gnocchi and the crunch of the nuts. It is served hot, straight from the pan, as a rich first course.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potato gnocchi
  • 150 g gorgonzola, diced
  • 150 ml cream
  • 40 g walnuts, roughly chopped
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 2–3 minutes and set aside.
  2. Melt the gorgonzola with the cream in a wide pan over low heat, stirring until smooth; season with pepper.
  3. Boil the gnocchi in salted water until they float, about 2 minutes, and lift them into the sauce.
  4. Toss gently, scatter with the walnuts and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the cream and loosen the sauce with gnocchi cooking water instead.
  • Use 15 g walnuts per portion at most — nuts are calorie-dense.
  • Serve a 200 g portion with a bitter-leaf salad such as rocket or radicchio.

History

Gorgonzola is a blue cheese from Lombardy and Piedmont, and gnocchi in gorgonzola sauce is a popular dish across northern Italy; walnuts are a common addition.
