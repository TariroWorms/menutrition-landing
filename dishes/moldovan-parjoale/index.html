Pârjoale moldovenești (Moldovan meat patties)

Origin: Moldova

Pârjoale are large, flat patties of minced pork seasoned with onion, garlic and fresh herbs, rolled in flour or crumbs and fried until crisp outside and juicy within. They are a homely main course with mashed potatoes, pickles or mămăligă. Cold leftovers go well in a sandwich.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced pork
  • 1 onion, 100 g
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 egg
  • 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 40 g flour for coating
  • 60 ml oil for frying

Method

  1. Grate the onion and mix with the pork, garlic, egg, crumbs and herbs.
  2. Season with salt and pepper and knead well for 3 minutes.
  3. Rest the mixture 15 minutes.
  4. Shape into flat patties of about 120 g each and dust with flour.
  5. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat.
  6. Fry the patties 5 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
  7. Drain on paper and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake them at 200 C instead of frying to cut the fat.
  • Use lean pork or half turkey mince.
  • Serve with a large salad rather than extra potatoes.

History

Patties and meatballs appear across Romanian and Moldovan home cooking under many names, and pârjoale are among the most common. They are an everyday family dish rather than a restaurant speciality.
