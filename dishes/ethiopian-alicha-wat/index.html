Alicha wat (Ethiopian mild turmeric stew)

Origin: Ethiopia

Alicha wat is the gentle counterpart of the red wats: onion, garlic, ginger and turmeric build a golden sauce without chilli. The lamb becomes tender and the flavor is warm and mellow. It is a good choice for children and for anyone avoiding heat, and is eaten with injera.

What it is made of

lambonionturmericgingergarlic

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb
  • 400 g onion
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 400 ml water
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Cook chopped onion in a dry pot for 10 minutes, then add oil.
  2. Stir in garlic, ginger and turmeric for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the lamb pieces and turn to coat.
  4. Pour in water, cover and simmer 50 minutes until tender.
  5. Season with salt and serve with injera.

How to make it lighter

  • Swap lamb for potatoes and carrots for a lighter vegetarian version.
  • Trim visible fat from the lamb.
  • Add greens near the end for extra fiber.

History

Alicha means mild in Amharic, and the dish is typically served beside spicier wats on a shared platter. It can be made with meat, or with vegetables on fasting days.
