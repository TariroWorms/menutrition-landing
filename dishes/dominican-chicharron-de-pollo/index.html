Chicharrón de pollo (Dominican fried chicken bites)

Origin: Dominican Republic

Chicharrón de pollo is made of small pieces of chicken, often on the bone, marinated in lime juice, garlic and oregano and fried in a light flour coating. They come out crunchy outside and juicy inside. They are served hot with tostones, fried yuca or a squeeze of lime.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thighs, cut in pieces
  • 3 limes
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 100 g flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with lime juice, garlic, oregano and salt and marinate for at least 1 hour.
  2. Drain and toss the pieces lightly in flour.
  3. Heat oil to 175 C.
  4. Fry in batches for 8-10 minutes until deep golden and cooked through.
  5. Drain on paper towels.
  6. Serve hot with lime wedges and tostones.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the pieces instead of deep frying.
  • Use skinless chicken to cut the fat.
  • Serve with a big salad instead of fried sides.

History

Despite the name that usually refers to pork cracklings, in the Dominican Republic chicharrón de pollo is a popular fried chicken dish found at colmados and street stands. It is typically accompanied by fried green plantains.
