🌯 Enchiladas suizas

Origin: Mexico · Mexico City

Enchiladas suizas are corn tortillas rolled around chicken and baked under a creamy green sauce of tomatillos and cream, topped with melted cheese until golden. The sauce is tangy and smooth, the cheese gives a rich, bubbling crust. It is one of the most comforting enchilada styles, usually served with rice or a simple salad.

Recipe

4 servings55 min

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 400 g chicken breast
  • 500 g tomatillos, husked
  • 1 green chili
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 150 ml cream
  • 120 g melting cheese, grated
  • Salt, fresh coriander

Method

  1. Poach the chicken in salted water for 15 minutes and shred it.
  2. Boil the tomatillos and chili for 8 minutes, then blend with garlic, coriander and the cream.
  3. Warm the tortillas, fill with chicken and roll them into a baking dish.
  4. Pour the sauce over, top with cheese and bake at 190 °C for 20 minutes until bubbling.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream and more tomatillo: the sauce stays tangy with fewer calories.
  • Use a thinner layer of cheese: it browns better, and 30 g less cheese saves about 100 kcal per dish.
  • Pair with a salad rather than rice to keep the plate lighter.

History

The name means “Swiss enchiladas”, a nod to the dairy-rich sauce and melted cheese associated with Switzerland. The dish is usually credited to the Sanborns restaurant in Mexico City around the middle of the 20th century, though accounts differ.
