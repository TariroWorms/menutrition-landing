Majadito (Santa Cruz rice with dried beef)

Origin: Bolivia · Santa Cruz

Majadito is a one-pot rice dish colored golden with annatto and flavored with shredded dried beef, onion and tomato. It is traditionally served with a fried egg and slices of fried plantain. It is a daily home-style meal in the lowlands of Bolivia.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g rice
  • 150 g dried or shredded beef
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 tsp annatto oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 ripe plantains
  • salt

Method

  1. Soak and shred the dried beef.
  2. Sauté onion and tomato with annatto oil.
  3. Add the beef and rice and stir for 2 minutes.
  4. Pour in 500 ml water, salt and cook covered for 18 minutes.
  5. Fry the eggs and plantain slices.
  6. Serve the rice topped with egg and plantain.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil when frying the egg and plantain.
  • Rinse the dried beef to cut sodium.
  • Add peas or carrots for vegetables.

History

It belongs to the cooking of the eastern lowlands around Santa Cruz, where dried beef known as charque is common. The dish is a practical way to turn preserved meat and rice into a filling meal.
