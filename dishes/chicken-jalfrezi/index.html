🌶️ Chicken jalfrezi

Chicken jalfrezi is a quick, dry-ish stir-fry of chicken with chunky bell peppers, onion, tomato and fresh green chilies, seasoned with garam masala. The vegetables stay slightly crisp, and the sauce is thick and clinging rather than soupy. It is one of the hotter dishes on a typical curry-house menu and is served with rice or flatbread.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless chicken, in strips
  • 2 bell peppers, in chunks
  • 2 onions, one sliced and one in chunks
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 green chilies, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves and 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric, 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a wide pan and fry the sliced onion until soft and golden.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger, turmeric and cumin, then the chicken, and stir-fry over high heat for 6–8 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook until they soften into a thick sauce.
  4. Add the peppers, onion chunks and green chilies and stir-fry for 3–4 minutes so they keep some bite.
  5. Season, sprinkle in the garam masala and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Load up on peppers and onion — they bulk out the plate for very few calories.
  • Use a non-stick pan and 1 tbsp oil; 1 tbsp is about 120 kcal.
  • Serve with cauliflower rice or a modest portion of basmati.

History

Jalfrezi is often said to come from colonial-era Bengal, where leftover roast meat was re-fried with onions and chilies; the name is often explained as coming from words meaning hot or spicy frying. The modern chicken version became a fixture of British curry houses in the late twentieth century.
