Kanelsnegl (Danish cinnamon swirl)

Origin: Denmark

Kanelsnegl means cinnamon snail, named for its spiral shape. The dough is rolled with butter, cinnamon and sugar, then baked until the edges are crisp and the center is soft. A thin sugar glaze on top makes it a favorite bakery treat with coffee.

Recipe

8 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g wheat flour
  • 150 ml milk
  • 1 egg
  • 80 g butter
  • 60 g sugar
  • 8 g cinnamon
  • 10 g yeast
  • icing sugar

Method

  1. Make a yeast dough from flour, milk, egg, sugar and some butter and let it rise for an hour.
  2. Roll it into a rectangle and spread with butter mixed with sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Roll it up tightly from the long side and cut into thick slices.
  4. Place the slices on a tray and let them rise for 30 minutes.
  5. Bake at 200 C for 12 to 15 minutes until golden.
  6. Cool slightly and brush with icing.

How to make it lighter

  • Make smaller rolls to cut the portion.
  • Reduce the sugar in the filling and add more cinnamon.
  • Use a thin glaze or skip it.

History

It belongs to the Danish bakery tradition of sweet pastries, which grew in the nineteenth century. Bakery versions are often made with flaky laminated dough, while the home version, as here, uses a rich, buttery yeast dough.
