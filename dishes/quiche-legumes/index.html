Quiche aux légumes is a flaky pastry case filled with a creamy egg custard and a mix of vegetables such as courgette, peppers, tomato and spinach. The vegetables add colour and moisture to a soft, savoury filling. It is served in wedges, warm or cold, often with salad for lunch.

Recipe

6 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1 pastry sheet (about 230 g)
  • 1 courgette, diced
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 100 g spinach
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 3 eggs
  • 200 ml cream
  • Salt, pepper, thyme

Method

  1. Line a 24 cm tin with the pastry and blind-bake at 190 °C for 12 minutes.
  2. Sauté courgette and pepper in the oil 6–8 minutes, add spinach until wilted; season with thyme, salt and pepper.
  3. Whisk eggs and cream with salt and pepper.
  4. Spread the vegetables in the shell and pour over the custard.
  5. Bake 30–35 minutes until set; rest before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Pack in more vegetables and less custard for a lighter slice.
  • Replace part of the cream with water.
  • Keep slices to one-sixth of the tart and add a side salad.

History

Quiche grew out of the custard tarts of Lorraine and became common across France. Vegetable versions are now everyday fare in French bakeries and lunch counters.
