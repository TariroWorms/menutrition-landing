🫑 Chiles rellenos

Origin: Mexico · Puebla

Chiles rellenos are roasted poblano peppers stuffed with melting cheese, dipped in a light, fluffy egg batter and deep-fried until golden. They are served swimming in a thin tomato sauce, so each bite is crisp, soft and saucy with a gentle chilli heat. In Mexico they are eaten as a main dish with rice and beans.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 4 poblano peppers
  • 200 g melting cheese (e.g. Oaxaca), in sticks
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 3 tbsp flour for dusting
  • 400 g tomatoes, ¼ onion, 1 garlic clove
  • Salt
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Method

  1. Roast, sweat and peel the poblanos, then slit them and remove the seeds.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, onion, garlic and salt and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Fill each pepper with cheese and dust with flour.
  4. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then fold in the yolks.
  5. Dip the peppers in the batter and fry in hot oil until golden on both sides. Drain on paper.
  6. Serve in a pool of the tomato sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the stuffed peppers with the cheese instead of battering and frying them.
  • Use a lighter cheese and a little less of it.
  • Drain well on kitchen paper and add extra tomato sauce rather than sides fried in oil.

History

Stuffed chillies have a long history in Mexican cooking, and the battered, cheese-filled poblano is often associated with the state of Puebla. The dish is now a fixture of Mexican home cooking and Mexican restaurants abroad.
