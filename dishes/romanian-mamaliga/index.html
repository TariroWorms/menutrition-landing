Mămăligă (Romanian polenta)

Origin: Romania

Mămăligă is cornmeal boiled in salted water until thick enough to slice. It tastes mild and sweet and is served with sour cream, salty cheese and sometimes fried pork. It replaces bread at many Romanian tables.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g coarse cornmeal
  • 800 ml water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 100 g salty cheese
  • 20 g butter

Method

  1. Boil salted water.
  2. Pour in cornmeal in a thin stream while stirring.
  3. Cook on low heat 25 minutes, stirring often.
  4. Stir in butter.
  5. Turn out onto a board and rest 5 minutes.
  6. Slice and serve with cheese and sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light sour cream.
  • Add vegetables on the side.
  • Use a little olive oil instead of butter and keep the cheese portion small.

History

It was long a peasant staple across Romania once maize arrived in the region. Traditionally it is cut with a thread rather than a knife.
