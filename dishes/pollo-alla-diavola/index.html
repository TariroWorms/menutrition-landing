🌶️ Pollo alla diavola (devil's chicken)

Origin: Italy · Tuscany

Pollo alla diavola is a whole chicken split and flattened, then grilled with chili, garlic, lemon, olive oil and rosemary. Pressing it flat lets the skin crisp evenly over the heat while the meat stays juicy, and the chili gives the "devilish" heat the name refers to. It is served hot with lemon wedges.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (about 1.4 kg), spatchcocked
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon, plus lemon wedges to serve
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1–2 tsp chili flakes
  • 2 sprigs rosemary, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the oil, lemon juice, garlic, chili, rosemary, salt and pepper.
  2. Rub it all over the flattened chicken and leave 30 minutes, or overnight in the fridge.
  3. Grill skin-side down under a heavy weight over medium heat for 20 minutes.
  4. Turn and cook another 15–20 minutes until the juices run clear.
  5. Rest 5 minutes, cut into pieces and serve with lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Eat the breast meat and leave the skin to cut the fat significantly.
  • Use 1 tbsp oil in the marinade; the lemon and chili carry the flavour.
  • Serve with grilled vegetables or a big salad.

History

The dish is usually associated with Tuscany, and the name is often explained by the fiery heat of the chili or the hot coals it is cooked over; the exact origin is not documented. Today pollo alla diavola is found in trattorias across Italy.
