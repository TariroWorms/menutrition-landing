Paccheri alla Norma

Origin: Italy · Catania, Sicily

Paccheri alla Norma is the Sicilian classic pasta alla Norma made with wide paccheri tubes. Fried cubes of aubergine are folded into a simple tomato and basil sauce, and the dish is finished with grated ricotta salata — a firm, salty aged ricotta. The result is soft, sweet and savoury, with silky aubergine against the bite of the pasta.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g paccheri
  • 2 medium aubergines, cubed
  • 500 g tomato passata or chopped tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 60 g ricotta salata, grated
  • A handful of basil leaves, salt

Method

  1. Salt the aubergine cubes, rest them for 20 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Fry the aubergine in 3 tbsp oil until golden and soft, then drain.
  3. Cook the garlic in the remaining oil, add the tomatoes and simmer for 15 minutes; stir in half the aubergine and the basil.
  4. Cook the paccheri until al dente, toss with the sauce and top with the rest of the aubergine and the ricotta salata.

How to make it lighter

  • Aubergine soaks up oil — roast it in the oven brushed with oil instead of frying.
  • Use half the ricotta salata; it is salty and strong, so a little goes far.
  • Every tablespoon of olive oil is about 120 kcal, so measure it rather than pouring.

History

Pasta alla Norma comes from Catania in Sicily. The story goes that it was named after Vincenzo Bellini's opera Norma — Bellini was born in Catania — as a way of praising the dish as a masterpiece.
