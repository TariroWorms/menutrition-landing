🍕 Pizza ortolana (vegetable pizza)

Pizza ortolana — “the greengrocer’s pizza” — is topped with tomato, mozzarella and grilled or roasted vegetables: zucchini, eggplant and sweet peppers. The vegetables bring smoky sweetness and colour, while the mozzarella melts between them. It is a popular vegetarian choice in Italian pizzerias and is eaten whole or in slices.

Recipe

2 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g ready pizza dough (or homemade)
  • 150 g crushed tomatoes
  • 125 g mozzarella, torn
  • 1 small zucchini, sliced
  • 1 small eggplant, sliced
  • 1 red pepper, cut into strips
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, salt

Method

  1. Brush the vegetables with 1 tbsp oil, salt them and grill or roast at 220 °C for 15 minutes.
  2. Preheat the oven with a stone or tray inside to the maximum.
  3. Stretch the dough by hand into a 30 cm round, leaving a slightly thicker rim.
  4. Spread the tomato, add the mozzarella and arrange the vegetables on top.
  5. Bake on the hottest setting of the oven (250–280 °C) on a preheated stone or tray for 7–10 minutes until the crust is blistered.
  6. Drizzle with the remaining oil and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Grill the vegetables dry in a pan instead of frying — eggplant soaks up a lot of oil.
  • Use less mozzarella and spread it thinly — cheese is the most calorie-dense topping.
  • Pile on extra vegetables: they add volume for few calories.

History

Ortolana comes from ortolano, a market gardener, and the name is used across Italy for dishes built on seasonal vegetables. The pizza has no single recorded origin and is found in pizzerias throughout the country.
