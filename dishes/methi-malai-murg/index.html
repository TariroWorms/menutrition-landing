Methi malai murg is a North Indian chicken curry in a mild, creamy sauce flavoured with fenugreek leaves. Chicken is cooked with garlic and ginger, then simmered in cream thickened with a smooth cashew paste. The fenugreek adds a pleasant bitter, herbal note that balances the richness. It is served with rice or flatbread.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken, cut into pieces
  • 40 g cashews, soaked and blended to a paste with water
  • 150 ml cream
  • 3 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) or a handful of fresh ones
  • 4 garlic cloves and 3 cm ginger, grated
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 green chili, slit
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • ½ tsp garam masala, salt

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the oil until soft, then add the garlic, ginger and chili for 1 minute.
  2. Add the chicken and cook for 6–8 minutes until it turns white on all sides.
  3. Stir in the cashew paste and 150 ml water and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Crush the fenugreek leaves between your palms and add with the cream and garam masala.
  5. Simmer for 5 more minutes until the chicken is cooked and the sauce thick; season and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream and add more water to the cashew paste.
  • Halve the cashews — about 20 g is enough to thicken the sauce.
  • Serve with a measured portion of rice or one flatbread and a side of salad.

History

Methi malai murg belongs to the creamy 'malai' style of North Indian and Mughlai restaurant cooking, where cream and nut pastes enrich mild curries. No reliable record of its origin exists; it is popular today in restaurants and homes across North India.
