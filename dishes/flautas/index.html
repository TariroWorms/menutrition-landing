🌯 Chicken flautas (Flautas)

Flautas are corn tortillas rolled tightly around shredded chicken and fried until crisp. They are served with a topping of cream, crumbled cheese, shredded lettuce and salsa. The name means flutes, after their long thin shape.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 small onion and 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tomatoes and 1 chipotle or jalapeño
  • Oil for frying (about 150 ml)
  • 100 ml Mexican crema or thick yogurt
  • 100 g queso fresco, crumbled
  • Shredded lettuce, salsa, salt

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken with half the onion, garlic and salt for 20 minutes, then shred it.
  2. Blend the tomatoes with the chile and the rest of the onion, cook the sauce 5 minutes and mix a few spoonfuls into the chicken.
  3. Warm the tortillas so they do not crack, fill each with chicken and roll tightly; secure with a toothpick.
  4. Fry in hot oil for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and crisp, then drain on paper.
  5. Top with lettuce, cream, cheese and salsa and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the rolled flautas at 220 °C for 15 minutes with a light brush of oil instead of deep frying, to cut a lot of fat.
  • Use thick yogurt in place of cream and less cheese; keep the chicken filling generous for protein.
  • Serve two or three flautas with plenty of lettuce and salsa, not a heaped plate.

History

Rolled and fried tortillas filled with shredded meat are a well-loved part of Mexican street food and home cooking. Flautas are closely related to taquitos, and in some regions the two names are used for similar things. They are common at family gatherings, taquerías and market stalls.
