Ohn no khao swe (Burmese coconut chicken noodle soup)

Origin: Myanmar

Ohn no khao swe is a rich, golden soup of chicken in coconut milk thickened with chickpea flour, poured over wheat noodles. It is creamy and mild with a gentle spice. At the table it is topped with a boiled egg, crispy fried noodles, scallions, lime and chili flakes.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g chicken thigh
  • 300 g egg noodles, cooked
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp chickpea flour, toasted
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • lime, scallions and chili flakes to serve

Method

  1. Fry onion, garlic and turmeric in a little oil.
  2. Add chicken and brown briefly.
  3. Pour in 600 ml water and simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Add coconut milk and chickpea flour mixed with water and simmer until thick.
  5. Serve over noodles with egg, scallions, lime and chili.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk and chicken breast to cut fat.
  • Skip the fried noodle topping.
  • Add more fresh vegetables and lime.

History

The dish is a Burmese favourite and is thought to be the ancestor of khao soi and similar coconut noodle soups in the region. It is eaten mostly for lunch or dinner.
