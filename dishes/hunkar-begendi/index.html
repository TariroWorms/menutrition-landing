🍆 Hünkar beğendi

Hünkar beğendi, “the sultan liked it”, is a stew of tender braised lamb served on a smooth purée of smoky, flame-roasted eggplant. The purée is enriched with milk, butter and cheese until it is creamy like a béchamel. The savoury lamb in tomato sauce and the silky smoked eggplant are served together as a main course.

Recipe

4 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, grated, 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 large eggplants
  • 300 ml milk
  • 30 g butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 60 g grated cheese
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in the oil, add the onion, then the tomatoes, tomato paste, salt and a cup of water. Cover and simmer for about 1 hour until tender.
  2. Roast the eggplants directly over a flame or under a hot grill until charred and soft. Peel and chop the flesh finely.
  3. Melt the butter, stir in the flour for 1 minute, then whisk in the milk and cook until thick.
  4. Stir the eggplant and cheese into the sauce and season.
  5. Spread the eggplant purée on plates and spoon the lamb and its sauce on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb leg and skim the fat from the sauce.
  • Halve the butter and cheese in the purée; the smoky eggplant keeps it flavourful.
  • Serve with a green salad rather than rice or bread.

History

The name means roughly “the sovereign liked it”. It is a classic of Ottoman palace-style cooking, and a popular story links it to the visit of the French empress Eugénie to Istanbul in 1869, though this cannot be confirmed. Today it is a fixture of traditional Turkish restaurants.
