🍛 Green curry chicken

Green curry chicken is the classic Thai pairing of tender chicken with green curry paste and coconut milk, simmered with eggplant and finished with Thai basil and a splash of fish sauce. The sauce is creamy, herbaceous and hot, balanced with a little sugar and salt from the fish sauce. It is eaten with steamed jasmine rice.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast or thighs, sliced
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp green curry paste
  • 2 Thai eggplants or 1 small eggplant, cubed
  • 1 handful Thai basil leaves
  • 1–2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, 1 tsp sugar

Method

  1. Fry the curry paste in the oil with a few spoons of coconut cream for 2–3 minutes.
  2. Add the chicken and stir until coated.
  3. Pour in the remaining coconut milk with 100 ml water and simmer gently.
  4. Add the eggplant and cook for 10 minutes until soft.
  5. Season with fish sauce and sugar, stir in the basil and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Light coconut milk cuts the fat noticeably without losing the flavour.
  • Chicken breast keeps it leaner than thighs.
  • Serve with a small portion of rice and extra vegetables in the curry.

History

Green curry comes from central Thailand and is usually dated to the early 20th century, when it began to appear in Thai cookbooks. Chicken is the most common version today, served everywhere from Bangkok street stalls to Thai restaurants abroad.
