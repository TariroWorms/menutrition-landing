🍝 Cavatelli with tomato sauce

Cavatelli al pomodoro are small hand-rolled pasta shells from southern Italy tossed in a simple sauce of tomato, garlic, olive oil and fresh basil. The pasta is chewy with a hollow centre that holds the sauce, and the tomato is cooked only briefly so it stays fresh and light. It is a quick, everyday dish served hot as a first course or main.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g cavatelli
  • 800 g ripe tomatoes or canned peeled tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • A handful of fresh basil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Warm the oil and garlic gently until fragrant, then add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt.
  2. Simmer for 15 minutes, crushing the tomatoes, until slightly thickened.
  3. Boil the cavatelli in salted water until al dente, about 10–12 minutes for dried.
  4. Toss the pasta with the sauce and torn basil, adding a splash of pasta water if needed.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp of oil in the sauce instead of 3 — each tablespoon saved is about 120 kcal.
  • Weigh the pasta: 80 g dry per person is a standard portion.
  • Bulk out the plate with extra tomato sauce or a side of grilled vegetables.

History

Cavatelli are a traditional durum-wheat pasta of southern Italy, made in Puglia, Molise, Basilicata and Campania by pressing small pieces of dough with a finger or knife. Pairing them with a plain tomato and basil sauce is one of the most common home preparations in the region today.
