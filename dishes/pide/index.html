🥖 Turkish Pide with Lamb

Pide is a Turkish boat-shaped flatbread baked in a very hot oven with a filling running down its open center. This version is topped with spiced ground lamb cooked with tomato and bell pepper, and finished with an egg cracked over the top near the end of baking. The crust is crisp at the rim and soft underneath; it is cut into strips and eaten by hand.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 7 g dried yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 250 ml warm water
  • 300 g ground lamb
  • 2 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp paprika, salt, pepper
  • 4 eggs

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and warm water into a soft dough, knead 8 minutes and let rise for 1 hour.
  2. Prepare the topping: sauté the lamb until browned, drain the fat, add tomato, pepper and spices and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Divide the dough into 4, roll each into a long oval and spread the topping down the middle.
  4. Fold the edges over the filling and pinch the ends into a boat shape.
  5. Bake at 250 °C for 10–12 minutes until golden, cracking an egg onto each pide for the last 3–4 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb mince and drain the fat after browning.
  • Roll the dough thinner and make smaller pides.
  • Eat it with a big salad of tomato, cucumber and parsley and eat one or two strips less.

History

Pide is Turkey's boat-shaped baked flatbread, closely tied to the Black Sea region, where cities such as Samsun, Trabzon and Kastamonu each claim their own style. Today pide salons serving many toppings are found in every Turkish city.
