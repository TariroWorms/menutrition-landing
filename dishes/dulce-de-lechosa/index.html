Dulce de lechosa (candied green papaya)

Origin: Venezuela

Dulce de lechosa is made by cutting unripe papaya into thin strips and cooking them slowly in a syrup of papelón with cinnamon and cloves. The fruit turns translucent, amber and pleasantly chewy, while the syrup thickens and darkens. It tastes of caramel and spice. It is served in small bowls, often with a piece of white cheese, during the Christmas season.

What it is made of

green papayapapelón (raw cane sugar)cinnamonclove

Recipe

10 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg green papaya, peeled and seeded
  • 500 g papelón or dark brown sugar
  • 500 ml water
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 tsp baking soda (for soaking)

Method

  1. Cut the papaya into thin strips and soak them in water with baking soda for 1 hour; rinse well.
  2. Dissolve the papelón in the water with cinnamon and cloves.
  3. Add the papaya and bring to a gentle boil.
  4. Simmer uncovered for 60–80 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the strips are translucent and the syrup is thick.
  5. Cool and keep in jars in the fridge.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve two or three strips as a small dessert portion.
  • Pair with fresh white cheese instead of extra syrup.
  • Cook with less papelón and a little more spice.

History

Lechosa is the Venezuelan word for papaya, and candying it is a traditional way to use unripe fruit. The sweet is part of the classic Venezuelan Christmas table.
