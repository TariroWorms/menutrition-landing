Sindhi biryani

Origin: Pakistan · Sindh

Sindhi biryani is a bold, tangy version of the layered rice dish, made with meat, potatoes, tomatoes, dried plums, yogurt and plenty of green chilli and fresh herbs. The rice is parboiled and layered with the masala, then steamed on low heat so every grain takes colour and aroma. It tastes spicy, sour and herby, and is eaten with raita and salad.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb, in pieces
  • 400 g basmati rice
  • 300 g potatoes
  • 300 g tomatoes
  • 150 g yogurt
  • 3 green chillies, a handful of mint and coriander
  • 5 dried plums, 2 onions, 80 ml oil, spices, salt

Method

  1. Fry onions in oil until brown, add the meat, spices, tomatoes and yogurt and cook until tender.
  2. Add potatoes, plums and green chillies and simmer until the potatoes are done.
  3. Parboil the rice with salt and whole spices until 70 percent cooked.
  4. Layer rice and masala in a heavy pot with herbs.
  5. Seal the lid and steam on low heat for 25-30 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more potatoes and less oil in the masala.
  • Serve with a large cucumber-yogurt raita.
  • Use chicken breast for a leaner dish.

History

Sindhi biryani is a signature of the Sindh province and its cooks, who are known for generous spice and the use of dried plums for sourness. It is a staple of weddings and festive meals.
