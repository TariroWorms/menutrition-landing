A red velvet slice is a single portion of the classic American layer cake: a crimson cocoa sponge with a soft, fine crumb and a light tang from buttermilk, layered with sweet, tangy cream cheese frosting. It is a staple of bakeries and coffee shops, usually eaten as a dessert or with coffee.

Recipe

12 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain flour
  • 25 g cocoa powder
  • 300 g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 150 g butter, softened
  • 240 ml buttermilk and 1 tsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp baking soda, 1–2 tsp red food colouring
  • 400 g cream cheese and 100 g icing sugar for the frosting

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C and line two 20 cm tins.
  2. Beat 100 g butter with the sugar, then beat in the eggs.
  3. Mix in the flour, cocoa and baking soda alternately with the buttermilk, vinegar and food colouring.
  4. Bake for 25–30 minutes and cool completely.
  5. Beat the cream cheese with the remaining butter and the icing sugar, layer the cakes with it and cut into slices.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut thinner slices — a 60 g slice instead of 100 g makes a real difference.
  • Spread the frosting thinly between layers and skip the frosting on the sides.
  • Share a slice or pair a small piece with fresh berries.

History

Red velvet cake is widely associated with the American South, though its exact origin is disputed. Cocoa "velvet" cakes appear in American cookbooks around the turn of the 20th century, and the bright red colour and cream cheese frosting became the familiar standard later in the century.
