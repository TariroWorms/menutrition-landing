Estamboli polo (tomato and potato rice)

Origin: Iran

Estamboli polo is a Persian mixed rice colored red-orange by tomato paste and layered with fried potato pieces and small beef pieces. It tastes savory and mildly tangy, with soft potatoes through the rice. A crust forms at the bottom of the pot.

Recipe

5 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g basmati rice
  • 300 g beef, diced
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 3 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Brown onion and beef with turmeric, add tomato paste and a little water.
  2. Simmer until the meat is tender.
  3. Parboil the rice and fry the potato pieces.
  4. Layer rice, meat sauce and potatoes in an oiled pot.
  5. Steam on low heat for 40 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the potatoes rather than frying them.
  • Use lean beef.
  • Add a cucumber-yogurt side.

History

Despite the name, which means Istanbul-style, it is a thoroughly Iranian home dish found at family tables and picnics.
