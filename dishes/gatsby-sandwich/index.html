Gatsby (Cape Town chip sandwich)

Origin: South Africa · Cape Town

The Gatsby is a very long bread roll piled with hot chips, sliced meat or fish, salad and sauce. It is made to be cut in portions and shared. It is salty, saucy and very filling.

Recipe

3 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 long roll (about 30 cm)
  • 400 g thick chips
  • 200 g steak strips
  • 2 lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato
  • 3 tbsp sauce
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Fry or bake the chips until golden and salt them.
  2. Sear the steak strips in a hot pan.
  3. Split the roll lengthwise.
  4. Spread sauce inside and add lettuce and tomato.
  5. Pile on the steak and hot chips.
  6. Press closed and cut into portions.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the chips instead of frying them.
  • Share one among several people.
  • Add more salad and less sauce.

History

It is said to have been invented in Cape Town in the 1970s, and the name is a local legend about a customer's reaction. It became a favorite of workers and students. Fillings vary from steak to masala to fish.
