🧆 Hummus with chicken shawarma

Hummus shawarma is a plate of creamy hummus topped with warm strips of spiced chicken shawarma, toasted pine nuts and a drizzle of olive oil. The cool, tangy purée contrasts with the savoury, slightly charred meat. It is a filling main dish in Levantine restaurants.

Recipe

3 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g cooked chickpeas
  • 70 g tahini
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 400 g boneless chicken thighs
  • 2 tsp shawarma spice mix, 1 garlic clove
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 20 g pine nuts
  • Salt, parsley

Method

  1. Slice the chicken thinly and toss with the spices, crushed garlic, half the lemon juice, 1 tbsp olive oil and salt. Leave for 20 minutes.
  2. Blend the chickpeas with the tahini, remaining lemon juice, salt and a little water until smooth.
  3. Sear the chicken in a hot pan until browned and cooked through.
  4. Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan until golden.
  5. Spread the hummus on plates, pile the chicken in the centre and finish with pine nuts, parsley and the remaining oil.

How to make it lighter

  • Pine nuts are dense in calories: 20 g is about 2 tablespoons, so measure rather than scatter.
  • Cook the chicken in a non-stick pan with a spoon of oil; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use chicken breast instead of thigh for a leaner topping.

History

Topping hummus with meat is a long-standing Levantine habit, and serving it with shawarma is a popular modern restaurant version found across Lebanon, Jordan, the Gulf and beyond.
