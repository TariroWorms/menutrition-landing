🐟 Thai fish cakes

Origin: Thailand

These Thai fish cakes are made from white fish blended with red curry paste and fish sauce, mixed with chopped green beans and shredded makrut lime leaves. Deep-fried, they come out crispy outside and bouncy inside, with a fragrant, gently spicy flavour. They are a popular starter, served hot with a sweet-sour cucumber and chili dip.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g white fish fillet, skinless
  • 2 tbsp red curry paste
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 100 g green beans, thinly sliced
  • 5 makrut lime leaves, finely shredded
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Cucumber, chili and vinegar for the dip

Method

  1. Blend the fish, curry paste, fish sauce and sugar until smooth and sticky.
  2. Fold in the green beans and lime leaves.
  3. Shape into small flat patties with oiled hands.
  4. Deep-fry at 175 °C for 2–3 minutes until golden, then drain.
  5. Serve hot with diced cucumber and chili in sweetened vinegar.

How to make it lighter

  • Use an air fryer or bake at 200 °C to skip most of the frying oil.
  • Drain on paper towels before serving.
  • Fill half the plate with salad or steamed greens.

History

Fish cakes are a classic Thai appetizer sold at markets and restaurants throughout Thailand, typically made from firm white fish pounded with curry paste. They are usually served with a sweet chili and cucumber dipping sauce.
