🦐 Seafood Pastilla

Seafood pastilla is a savoury, non-sweet take on the Moroccan pie: crisp warqa pastry filled with shrimp and white fish, thin vermicelli and egg, all seasoned with garlic, herbs, paprika and cumin. It is baked until golden and cut into wedges, often appearing as a starter at weddings and family gatherings.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g white fish fillet, cubed
  • 300 g peeled shrimp
  • 100 g thin vermicelli
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 bunch parsley and coriander, chopped
  • 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 10 sheets warqa pastry
  • 3 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Cook the vermicelli in boiling water, drain well.
  2. Sauté garlic in 1 tbsp oil, add fish, shrimp, paprika and cumin and cook for 4–5 minutes; drain off liquid.
  3. Mix the seafood with vermicelli, herbs and lemon juice, then stir in most of the beaten egg.
  4. Line an oiled tin with warqa sheets, add the filling, fold in and top with more sheets.
  5. Brush with the remaining egg and oil and bake at 190 °C for 25–30 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more seafood and less vermicelli for a higher-protein filling.
  • Drain the seafood mixture well so less oil is needed to crisp the pastry.
  • Serve one wedge with a large salad of tomato, cucumber and herbs.

History

Seafood pastilla is generally regarded as a more recent variation on the traditional pigeon pastilla and is especially popular in Morocco's coastal cities. Today it appears regularly on celebration menus and in restaurants across the country.
