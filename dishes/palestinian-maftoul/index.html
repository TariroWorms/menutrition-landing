Maftoul (Palestinian hand-rolled couscous)

Origin: Palestine

Maftoul is a Palestinian dish of hand-rolled bulgur and flour pearls, larger than regular couscous, steamed over a rich stew of chicken, chickpeas and onions. The pearls are chewy and nutty and soak up the olive oil and spiced broth. It is often called Palestinian couscous and is served at family gatherings.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g maftoul
  • 600 g chicken pieces
  • 2 onions
  • 150 g cooked chickpeas
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp allspice
  • 1.2 l water or stock

Method

  1. Brown the chicken with onions in oil and add spices.
  2. Add water and simmer until the chicken is tender.
  3. Steam the maftoul over the broth for 20 minutes.
  4. Mix in chickpeas and a few ladles of broth.
  5. Serve on a platter topped with the chicken.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken and less oil.
  • Choose whole wheat maftoul for fibre.
  • Add zucchini and carrots to the broth.

History

Maftoul is closely associated with rural Palestine, where women gather to roll it by hand in the early summer. The coarse pearls are dried and stored for use through the year.
