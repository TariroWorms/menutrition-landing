Yarpaq dolmasi (Azerbaijani grape-leaf dolma)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Yarpaq dolmasi are small, neat parcels of grape leaves filled with minced lamb, a little rice, onion and plenty of mint, dill and coriander. They are packed tightly in a pot and simmered gently in a little water and butter. Served warm with garlicky yogurt, they are tender, tangy from the leaves and fragrant with herbs.

Recipe

6 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 50 grape leaves (fresh or brined)
  • 500 g minced lamb
  • 80 g short-grain rice
  • 1 onion (150 g)
  • 30 g mint, dill and coriander
  • 30 g butter
  • 300 g yogurt with 2 garlic cloves

Method

  1. Blanch fresh leaves for a minute or rinse brined ones well.
  2. Mix lamb, grated onion, rinsed rice, chopped herbs, salt and pepper.
  3. Place a teaspoon of filling on each leaf and roll into a tight small parcel.
  4. Pack seam-side down in a pot, add butter and water to half their height, weigh down with a plate.
  5. Simmer covered for 50–60 minutes and serve with garlic yogurt.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb or a lamb-beef mix.
  • Increase the herbs and rice, reduce the meat.
  • Choose thick strained yogurt with less fat.

History

Dolma is one of the best-known dishes of Azerbaijani cuisine, and the tradition of making and sharing it was added to UNESCO's intangible heritage list in 2017. Grape-leaf dolma is the most common everyday kind.
