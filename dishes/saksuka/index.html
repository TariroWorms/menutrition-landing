Şakşuka is a Turkish meze of fried cubes of eggplant, zucchini and peppers tossed in a garlicky tomato sauce. The vegetables are soft and silky, the sauce sweet and tangy, and the dish is served at room temperature as a cold starter or side, often with other mezes.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 eggplants, cubed
  • 2 zucchini, cubed
  • 2 green peppers, chopped
  • Olive oil for frying
  • 300 ml tomato sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • Salt

Method

  1. Salt the eggplant cubes for 20 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Fry the eggplant, zucchini and peppers in batches until golden; drain on paper.
  3. Warm the tomato sauce with the garlic and a pinch of salt for 5 minutes.
  4. Toss the vegetables with the sauce and let cool before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the vegetables in the oven with 2 tbsp oil instead of deep-frying them.
  • Drain fried vegetables well on paper to remove excess oil.
  • Serve it as a side to grilled meat or chicken to keep the meal balanced.

History

Şakşuka is a common dish on the Turkish meze table; it is different from North African shakshuka, despite the similar name.
