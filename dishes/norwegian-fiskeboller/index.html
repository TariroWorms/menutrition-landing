Fiskeboller (Norwegian fish balls in white sauce)

Origin: Norway

Fiskeboller are airy balls made from finely ground white fish, milk and a little flour. They are gently poached and served in a pale, creamy sauce, often with boiled potatoes and carrots. The flavour is mild and slightly sweet, with a very soft texture. In Norway they are a classic weekday dinner, and many people also know them from cans.

What it is made of

white fishmilkflourbuttercreamnutmeg

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g white fish fillet (cod or haddock)
  • 2 dl milk
  • 2 tbsp potato starch
  • 40 g butter
  • 30 g flour
  • Salt, nutmeg and white pepper

Method

  1. Grind the fish with salt in a food processor until smooth.
  2. Mix in the starch and slowly add cold milk to make a light mass.
  3. Shape into balls with two wet spoons and slide them into barely simmering salted water.
  4. Poach for 8 minutes until firm, then lift out.
  5. Melt butter, stir in flour and add poaching liquid and milk to make a white sauce, season with nutmeg and add the balls.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk and less butter in the sauce.
  • Serve with plenty of carrots and broccoli.
  • Keep the water just below boiling so the balls stay soft.

History

Fish balls became a household staple in Norway as a way to use lean white fish from the coastal catch. Factory-made canned versions from the twentieth century made them an everyday comfort food.
