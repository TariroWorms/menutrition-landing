Sopa de tortilla, also called tortilla soup, is a Mexican soup of chicken simmered in a tomato and chipotle broth, served over crisp strips of corn tortilla. Each bowl is topped with diced avocado, crumbled cheese and a swirl of cream. It is smoky and lightly spicy, with crunchy strips that slowly soften in the hot broth.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g chicken breast
  • 4 tomatoes (about 500 g)
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves
  • 1–2 chipotle chillies
  • 1 l water
  • 100 g tortilla strips (baked or fried)
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • 60 g crumbled fresh cheese
  • 4 tbsp cream

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in the water with half the onion and salt for 20 minutes, then remove and shred it.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, remaining onion, garlic and chipotle until smooth.
  3. Cook the tomato purée in a pot for 5 minutes, then add the chicken broth and simmer 10 minutes more.
  4. Return the shredded chicken to the soup and season.
  5. Put tortilla strips in bowls, ladle the soup over and top with avocado, cheese and cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the tortilla strips instead of frying them.
  • Use a small spoon of cream and a little cheese, or pick just one of the two.
  • Use half an avocado for four bowls and add extra chicken for a more filling soup.

History

Tortilla soup is closely associated with central Mexico and is often linked to Mexico City, where it is also known as sopa azteca. It is a common starter in Mexican homes and restaurants today.
