🍅 Tomates Farcies (French Stuffed Tomatoes)

Origin: France · Provence

Tomates farcies are large tomatoes hollowed out and filled with seasoned ground beef, bread soaked in a little water, garlic, parsley and egg, then baked until the tomatoes slump and the filling browns. They are juicy, savoury and homely, a summer family dish in France usually served with rice or a green salad.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 8 large tomatoes
  • 400 g lean ground beef
  • 60 g stale bread
  • 1 egg
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190 °C. Cut the tops off the tomatoes, scoop out the pulp and salt the insides.
  2. Soak the bread in a little water, squeeze it and mix with the beef, egg, garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
  3. Fill the tomatoes, replace the tops and set them in an oiled dish with the chopped pulp around them.
  4. Bake for 45–50 minutes until the filling is cooked through.
  5. Serve warm with the pan juices.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean beef (5% fat) to cut fat noticeably.
  • Use 1 tsp oil for the dish instead of drizzling more; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve with a large salad rather than a big portion of rice.

History

Stuffed vegetables are a long-standing tradition of French home cooking, especially in Provence and around Nice, where tomatoes, courgettes and peppers are filled with leftover meat. Tomates farcies remain a classic of family meals and school canteens across France.
