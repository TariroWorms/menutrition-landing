🧀 Mac and cheese balls

Mac and cheese balls are bite-sized rounds of cold, set macaroni and cheese that are coated in egg and breadcrumbs and deep-fried. The outside turns crunchy and golden while the inside stays soft, cheesy and slightly stretchy. They are a popular bar snack, party appetizer and street-food treat, usually served hot with a dipping sauce.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g elbow macaroni, cooked
  • 200 g cheddar, grated
  • 2 eggs
  • 80 g breadcrumbs
  • Oil for deep-frying
  • Salt, black pepper and paprika

Method

  1. Mix the hot cooked macaroni with the cheddar, salt, pepper and paprika until the cheese melts and binds it.
  2. Spread in a dish and chill for at least 1 hour until firm.
  3. Shape into balls of about 40 g.
  4. Dip each ball in beaten egg, then roll in breadcrumbs.
  5. Deep-fry at 175 °C for 3–4 minutes until golden and drain on paper towels.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the balls lightly brushed with oil instead of deep-frying.
  • Count them: three or four balls are a snack, a full plate is a meal.
  • Pair them with a crisp salad or raw vegetables instead of fries.

History

Mac and cheese balls are a modern American snack that turns leftover macaroni and cheese into a fried bite, in the same spirit as Italian arancini. They are found today in restaurants, food trucks and fairs across the United States and beyond.
