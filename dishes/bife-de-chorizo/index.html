Bife de chorizo (strip loin steak)

Origin: Argentina

Despite its name, bife de chorizo has nothing to do with sausage: it is a boneless strip loin steak with a band of fat along one side. Argentine parrillas cut it very thick, often 4 cm or more, and grill it to a deep crust. The meat is firm yet tender and full of beef flavour. It is the steak most visitors order on their first night in Buenos Aires.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 strip loin steaks, 450 g each, 4 cm thick
  • Coarse salt
  • Chimichurri to serve

Method

  1. Let the steaks reach room temperature.
  2. Prepare medium-hot embers.
  3. Salt and grill 7–8 minutes on the first side.
  4. Turn, grill 6–7 minutes more, then sear the fat edge upright.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the fat strip after cooking to save around 50 kcal per steak.
  • Order one bife for two with extra salad.
  • Avoid adding butter or creamy sauces; salt and chimichurri are enough.

History

The origin of the name is debated, and no single explanation is accepted. What is certain is that it became the emblematic steak of Argentine steakhouses.
