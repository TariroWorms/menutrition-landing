Fricassé (Tunisian fried sandwich)

Origin: Tunisia
405kcal

Fricassé is a small Tunisian bun made of yeasted dough, deep-fried until golden and then split and filled. The filling is usually tuna, hard-boiled egg, olives, potato and harissa. It is crisp outside, soft inside, and a favourite snack.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 250 ml warm water
  • 1 can tuna
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • 1 boiled potato
  • 8 olives
  • 1 tbsp harissa
  • oil for frying

Method

  1. Make a soft dough with flour, yeast, water and salt, and let it rise.
  2. Shape small buns and let them rest.
  3. Deep-fry until puffed and golden.
  4. Split the buns and spread with harissa.
  5. Fill with tuna, egg, potato and olives.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the buns instead of frying.
  • Choose tuna in water.
  • Add a lot of salad and have just one bun.

History

Despite its French-sounding name, it is a Tunisian street-food specialty found in bakeries and by the seaside. It is popular as a quick lunch or at beach outings.
