🥒 Zucchini parmigiana

Parmigiana di zucchine is a baked Italian dish of thinly sliced zucchini layered with tomato, mozzarella, grated parmesan and fresh basil. It bakes into soft, juicy layers with a golden, bubbling top. Lighter than the eggplant version, it is served warm or at room temperature as a side or a vegetarian main.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g zucchini, sliced lengthwise 5 mm thick
  • 400 g tomato passata
  • 200 g mozzarella, sliced
  • 50 g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Fresh basil, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Grill or roast the zucchini slices until lightly golden.
  2. Simmer the passata with the oil, garlic and salt for 10 minutes.
  3. Layer sauce, zucchini, mozzarella, parmesan and basil in a dish, repeating 3 times.
  4. Bake at 200 °C for 25 minutes and rest 10 minutes before cutting.

How to make it lighter

  • Grill the zucchini instead of frying it so the slices absorb very little oil.
  • Use light mozzarella and keep parmesan to a thin layer on top.
  • Serve with a large green salad as a main course.

History

It is a summer variation of the classic southern Italian parmigiana, which is traditionally made with eggplant. Home cooks across Italy make it with zucchini when they are in season, and it is a common dish in Campania and Sicily today.
