Gulbishniki (potato and curd bake)

Origin: Belarus

Gulbishniki are a homely Belarusian dish of mashed potatoes kneaded with tvorog (farmer cheese) and egg, shaped and baked until golden. Inside they are soft and slightly tangy from the cheese, with a thin crust on top. They are eaten hot with sour cream or fried onion as a meatless main.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes
  • 250 g farmer cheese (tvorog)
  • 2 eggs
  • 30 g butter
  • 20 g flour
  • salt
  • 150 g sour cream for serving

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes, mash them with the butter and let cool slightly.
  2. Push the farmer cheese through a sieve and add to the potatoes.
  3. Beat in one egg, the flour and salt.
  4. Shape small oval patties and place on a greased tray.
  5. Brush with the second beaten egg.
  6. Bake at 200 °C for 20-25 minutes until golden; serve with sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat farmer cheese for more protein and less fat.
  • Serve with yogurt instead of sour cream.
  • Pair with a big green salad to balance the starch.

History

The name comes from 'bulba', the Belarusian word for potato, the country's most beloved staple. The dish is a typical way of using leftover mashed potatoes in village cooking.
