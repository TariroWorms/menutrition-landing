🌯 Saj shawarma

Saj shawarma rolls chicken shawarma tightly in paper-thin saj bread together with garlic sauce, pickles and a few fries, then toasts the roll until crisp. It is cut into pieces and eaten hot, crunchy outside and juicy and garlicky inside — a favourite fast meal at shawarma counters across the Gulf.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken thighs
  • Juice of 1 lemon, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tsp shawarma spice mix, salt
  • 4 large saj breads
  • Garlic sauce, about 4 tbsp
  • 300 g potatoes, cut into thin fries, and 1 tbsp oil
  • Pickled cucumbers

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in lemon, garlic, oil, spices and salt for at least 2 hours, then roast at 220 °C for 25 minutes and slice thinly.
  2. Toss the fries with oil and salt and bake at 220 °C for 25–30 minutes until crisp.
  3. Spread a little garlic sauce on each saj bread, add chicken, pickles and a few fries and roll tightly.
  4. Toast the rolls in a dry pan or on a griddle until crisp, then cut into pieces.

How to make it lighter

  • Spread garlic sauce thinly — it is mostly oil.
  • Leave the fries out of the roll and add extra pickles instead.
  • One roll plus a salad is a full meal; avoid ordering a second.

History

Saj bread is baked on a domed griddle in the Levant, and Gulf shawarma shops adopted it for a thin, crisp roll packed with fries. Today saj shawarma is a common order across the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait.
