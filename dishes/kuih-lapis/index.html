Kuih lapis (steamed layer cake)

Origin: Malaysia

Kuih lapis is a chewy, jelly-like cake made of thin alternating layers of white and coloured batter. The batter of rice flour, tapioca starch and coconut milk is steamed one layer at a time, often with pandan or red colouring. It is peeled apart layer by layer, which is half the fun.

What it is made of

rice flourtapioca starchcoconut milksugarpandan

Recipe

12 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g rice flour
  • 100 g tapioca starch
  • 600 ml coconut milk
  • 200 g sugar
  • 200 ml water
  • pandan juice or food colouring
  • a pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix both flours, sugar, coconut milk, water and salt into a thin batter.
  2. Divide the batter in two and colour one half with pandan.
  3. Oil a pan and steam a thin first layer for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the alternate colour layer by layer, steaming each until set.
  5. Cool completely before cutting into squares.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk and less sugar.
  • Cut small pieces; it is dense and filling.
  • Colour with natural pandan rather than dyes.

History

The cake belongs to the family of Malay and Nyonya kuih, the steamed sweets eaten at tea time and festivals. Making the layers takes patience, so it is often prepared for celebrations.
