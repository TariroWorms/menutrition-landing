🧀 Grilled Halloumi

Grilled halloumi is simply thick slices of the firm Cypriot brined cheese cooked on a hot grill or pan until striped and golden. Because halloumi has a high melting point, it holds its shape, turning crisp outside and soft and squeaky inside with a salty, milky taste. It is served hot at breakfast, as a meze or alongside vegetables.

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g halloumi
  • Fresh mint or oregano
  • Black pepper
  • Lemon wedges to serve

Method

  1. Pat the halloumi dry and cut into 1 cm slices.
  2. Heat a dry grill pan or non-stick pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook the slices 1–2 minutes per side until golden with grill marks.
  4. Sprinkle with herbs and pepper and serve immediately with lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Grill without oil — halloumi browns well in a dry pan.
  • Weigh your portion: two or three slices go a long way.
  • Serve with tomato, cucumber or watermelon to bulk up the plate.

History

Halloumi has been made in Cyprus for centuries, traditionally from sheep's and goat's milk, and has protected-origin status in the EU. Grilling or frying it is the most common way to eat it in Cyprus and across the eastern Mediterranean today.
