Saucisson vaudois is a smoked pork sausage from the Swiss canton of Vaud, traditionally poached rather than grilled. It is classically served with papet vaudois, a soft stew of leeks and potatoes, and here also with cabbage. The sausage is sliced thickly over the vegetables, giving a smoky, simple and warming winter plate.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 1 saucisson vaudois (about 400 g)
  • 600 g leeks, sliced
  • 500 g potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 300 g white cabbage, shredded
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300 ml water, 1 tbsp vinegar, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Soften the onion in the oil, add the leeks and cabbage and cook for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the potatoes, water, vinegar, salt and pepper and bring to a simmer.
  3. Lay the sausage on top, cover and cook gently for 40 minutes without letting it boil, so the casing does not burst.
  4. Lift out the sausage, slice it thickly and serve on top of the vegetables.

How to make it lighter

  • Fill most of the plate with the leek, cabbage and potato and keep the sausage to a few slices.
  • Skim off fat that rises to the surface of the vegetables during cooking.
  • Use just one tablespoon of oil to soften the vegetables — about 120 kcal for the whole pot.

History

Saucisson vaudois carries a Swiss protected geographical indication (IGP), and papet vaudois with the sausage is regarded as the signature dish of the canton of Vaud.
