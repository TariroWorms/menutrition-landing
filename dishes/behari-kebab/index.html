Behari kebab (papaya-tenderized beef skewers)

Origin: Pakistan · Karachi

Behari kebab is made of thin strips of beef marinated with raw papaya paste, yogurt, fried onion, garlic and a sharp spice mix, then threaded onto skewers and grilled over charcoal. The papaya makes the meat extremely soft, while the mustard oil and chilli add a pungent kick. It is eaten with paratha or naan and sliced onion.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef, thinly sliced
  • 60 g raw papaya paste
  • 100 g yogurt
  • 60 g fried onion, crushed
  • 30 g ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil (30 ml)
  • 2 tsp chilli, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, salt

Method

  1. Pound the beef thin and mix with papaya paste; rest 30 minutes.
  2. Add yogurt, onion, paste, oil and spices and marinate 6 hours.
  3. Thread onto flat skewers.
  4. Grill over hot coals for 8-10 minutes, turning and basting.
  5. Serve with sliced onion, lemon and paratha.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef and brush with little oil.
  • Serve with raw vegetables.
  • Do not marinate longer than 8 hours so the meat does not turn mushy.

History

Behari kebab is associated with the Bihari community and became a Karachi barbecue staple after migration to the city. It is also popular at street stalls across Pakistan.
