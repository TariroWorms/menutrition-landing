🍎 Waldorf Salad

Origin: United States · New York

Waldorf salad combines crisp diced apple, sliced celery and walnuts in a creamy mayonnaise dressing, served on a bed of lettuce leaves. It balances sweet, crunchy and creamy textures in one bowl. It is served chilled as a starter, a light lunch or a side for roast poultry.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 crisp apples, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, sliced
  • 60 g walnuts, roughly chopped
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 small head lettuce
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Toss the diced apple with lemon juice so it does not brown.
  2. Add the celery and most of the walnuts.
  3. Fold in the mayonnaise and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Arrange lettuce leaves on plates and spoon the salad on top.
  5. Scatter the remaining walnuts and serve chilled.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the mayonnaise — 1 tbsp mayonnaise ≈ 90–100 kcal — and loosen it with lemon juice.
  • Use more celery and lettuce and fewer walnuts for volume with fewer calories.
  • Keep the walnuts to about 10 g per serving as a crunchy topping.

History

The salad is usually credited to the Waldorf Hotel in New York in the 1890s, where it was first made with apples, celery and mayonnaise. Walnuts became a standard addition in later versions, and the dish spread through American cookbooks.
