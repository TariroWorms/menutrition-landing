Matar paneer is a North Indian curry of soft paneer cubes and sweet green peas simmered in a tomato and onion gravy spiced with garam masala. A splash of cream rounds the sauce into something mellow and comforting. It is served with rice or flatbreads as an everyday vegetarian main.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cubed
  • 250 g green peas
  • 3 tomatoes, puréed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 60 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil and fry the onion until golden.
  2. Add garam masala and stir for 30 seconds, then the tomato purée; cook 8–10 minutes until thick.
  3. Add peas and 150 ml water and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Add paneer and simmer gently for 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream and salt and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace half the cream with water or skip it for a lighter gravy.
  • Use more peas and a little less paneer per portion.
  • Pair with a small portion of rice and a large salad.

History

Matar paneer is a staple of home cooking and restaurants across North India, particularly Punjab, and is widely eaten in Indian restaurants around the world today.
