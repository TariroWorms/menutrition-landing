Suanmeitang (sour plum drink)

Origin: China · Beijing
90kcal

Suanmeitang is a deep brown drink made by simmering smoked plums with dried hawthorn, licorice root and osmanthus, then sweetening it with sugar. It tastes tangy and slightly smoky with a floral finish. It is served well chilled as a summer refresher.

What it is made of

smoked plum (wumei)hawthorn berrieslicorice rootsugardried osmanthus flowers

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 40 g smoked plums
  • 20 g dried hawthorn
  • 5 g licorice root
  • 1 tsp dried osmanthus
  • 80 g rock sugar
  • 1.5 litres water

Method

  1. Rinse plums, hawthorn and licorice and soak 30 minutes.
  2. Bring to a boil in the soaking water.
  3. Simmer 30 minutes on low heat.
  4. Add osmanthus and sugar and cook 5 minutes more.
  5. Strain and chill well before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by half for a lighter drink.
  • Serve over ice with a lemon slice.
  • Swap it for soda: even sweetened, it has far fewer calories.

History

The drink has a long history in Beijing, where it is linked with street vendors and old summer tradition. It is also valued in Chinese food culture as a refreshing, digestion-friendly drink.
