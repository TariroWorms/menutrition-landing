Tort Joffre (Romanian chocolate layer cake)

Origin: Romania · Bucharest

Tort Joffre is a tall cake of thin cocoa sponge layers sandwiched with chocolate buttercream and wrapped in a smooth dark glaze. Each slice is rich and dense, with a soft crumb and a slightly bitter edge from the cocoa. It is served at birthdays and in old Bucharest cafés with strong coffee.

Recipe

12 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 180 g flour
  • 6 g eggs
  • 200 g sugar
  • 60 g cocoa powder
  • 250 g butter
  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 150 ml cream
  • 200 ml milk

Method

  1. Whisk eggs with 120 g sugar until pale, fold in flour and 40 g cocoa, and bake in three thin layers at 180°C for 10 minutes each.
  2. Cook milk, 80 g sugar and 20 g cocoa until thick, cool, then beat with 200 g butter into a cream.
  3. Soak the layers with a little warm sweetened water.
  4. Stack the layers with the cream between them and chill 2 hours.
  5. Heat cream and pour over chopped chocolate with 50 g butter to make a ganache, glaze the cake and chill before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve thin slices; the cake is energy-dense.
  • Replace part of the butter in the cream with cooked milk custard.
  • Use dark chocolate with at least 60 percent cocoa so you can reduce sugar.

History

The cake takes its name from Marshal Joseph Joffre, the French commander of the First World War who was celebrated in Romania when it joined the Allies. It is said to have been created in Bucharest around that period. Various pastry shops keep their own versions.
