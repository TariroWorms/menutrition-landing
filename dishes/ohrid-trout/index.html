Ohrid trout (Ohridska pastrmka)

Origin: North Macedonia · Ohrid

Ohrid trout is a prized lake fish baked or grilled whole with olive oil, lemon and herbs. The flesh is fine, pale and delicately flavoured, so the seasoning is kept simple. It is served at lakeside restaurants with boiled potatoes and a tomato salad.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 trout, 350 g each, cleaned
  • 3 tbsp olive oil (40 ml)
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 cloves garlic (12 g)
  • 1 handful parsley (20 g)
  • salt, pepper

Method

  1. Season the fish inside and out with salt and pepper.
  2. Fill the cavity with lemon slices, garlic and parsley.
  3. Place in a baking dish and drizzle with olive oil.
  4. Bake at 200 C for 20–25 minutes until the flesh flakes.
  5. Serve with lemon and a fresh salad.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only a small amount of oil, the fish is already rich.
  • Serve with salad and legumes for balance.
  • Choose a sustainably sourced trout.

History

Lake Ohrid is one of Europe’s oldest lakes and holds its own endemic trout. Because the fish is protected and prized, it is traditionally cooked simply to show off its taste.
