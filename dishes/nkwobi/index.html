Nkwobi (spicy cow foot in palm oil sauce)

Origin: Nigeria · Igbo

Nkwobi is a bar and party dish of tender cow foot pieces tossed in a thick orange sauce of palm oil, potash, chili and ground seeds. It is rich, hot and gelatinous with a faint bitter note from utazi leaves. It is eaten warm with a cold drink and shared among friends.

What it is made of

cow footred palm oiledible potashonionchili pepperutazi leaves

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg cow foot, cut in pieces
  • 100 ml palm oil
  • 1 tsp edible potash (akanwu), dissolved
  • 2 tbsp ground ehuru (calabash nutmeg)
  • 2 chili peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 10 utazi leaves
  • 1 stock cube, salt

Method

  1. Boil the cow foot with onion and salt for 2-3 hours until tender.
  2. Dissolve potash in a little warm water, let it settle and pour off the clear liquid.
  3. Stir palm oil with the potash liquid until it thickens into a yellow paste.
  4. Add ground ehuru, chili and stock cube.
  5. Fold in the hot cow foot with a little of its stock.
  6. Garnish with sliced onion and utazi and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat and skin from the stock.
  • Use less palm oil for a lighter sauce.
  • Serve with fresh vegetables rather than extra starch.

History

Nkwobi is a classic of Igbo cuisine in south-eastern Nigeria and is usually served in a wooden bowl. It is a popular dish at social gatherings and local bars.
