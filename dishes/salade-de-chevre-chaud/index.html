🥗 Warm Goat Cheese Salad

Salade de chèvre chaud is a French bistro salad of rounds of goat cheese set on slices of bread and baked until warm and lightly golden, then served on mixed greens with walnuts, a drizzle of honey and a sharp vinaigrette. The tangy, soft cheese contrasts with crisp toast, crunchy nuts and fresh leaves.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 small goat cheese rounds (about 120 g), halved
  • 4 slices baguette
  • 100 g mixed salad greens
  • 30 g walnuts
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp vinaigrette (olive oil, vinegar, salt, pepper)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  2. Place a piece of goat cheese on each slice of bread and bake 6–8 minutes until soft and golden.
  3. Toss the greens with the vinaigrette and divide between plates.
  4. Top with the warm toasts, scatter walnuts and drizzle with honey.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp vinaigrette per plate; the dressing adds up quickly.
  • Halve the honey or leave it out.
  • Use two toasts per person, not three or four.

History

It is a fixture of French café and bistro menus, popular across France since the late twentieth century; no single inventor or date is reliably recorded.
