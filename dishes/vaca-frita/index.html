Vaca frita (Cuban crispy shredded beef)

Origin: Cuba

Vaca frita is shredded beef that is first simmered tender, then tossed with lime juice and garlic and seared in a hot pan with onions. The result is juicy inside, crisp and golden in places, and bright with citrus. It is normally served with white rice, black beans and sweet plantains.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings140 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g flank steak
  • Juice of 3 limes (90 ml)
  • 6 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 onions, sliced (240 g)
  • 2 tbsp oil (30 ml)
  • 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp pepper

Method

  1. Simmer the steak with salt for 90 minutes until tender.
  2. Cool and shred into thin strands.
  3. Toss with lime juice, garlic, salt and pepper and rest 30 minutes.
  4. Fry the onions in oil until golden and set aside.
  5. Sear the beef in a very hot pan in batches, pressing until crisp.
  6. Mix with the onions and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • A non-stick pan lets you use less oil.
  • Squeeze extra lime and skip added salt if you watch sodium.
  • Serve with salad and beans instead of fried sides.

History

It is a close relative of ropa vieja, using the same boiled flank but finished by frying instead of stewing. The dish is a staple of Cuban home cooking and restaurants and is a popular way to use leftover beef.
