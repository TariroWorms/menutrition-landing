🍔 Wet Burger (Islak Burger)

Origin: Turkey · Istanbul

Islak burger, Istanbul's famous 'wet burger', is a small beef patty in a soft bun soaked in a garlicky tomato sauce and then left to steam in a glass cabinet until the bread turns soft and moist. It is warm, saucy and strongly garlicky, and so soft it almost melts. It is sold cheaply from street stalls and eaten on the spot, often two at a time.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 6 small soft burger buns
  • 400 g tomato sauce or passata
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt, pepper and cumin

Method

  1. Mix the beef with onion, salt, pepper and cumin and shape into 6 thin patties.
  2. Fry or grill the patties until cooked through.
  3. Simmer the tomato sauce with garlic, oil and salt for 10 minutes.
  4. Dip the cut sides of each bun in the sauce, add a patty and spoon over more sauce.
  5. Pack the burgers tightly in a covered dish and steam over simmering water or in a low oven for 20 minutes so the buns soften.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced beef (5–10% fat).
  • One wet burger is a snack; have one with a salad instead of two.
  • Cut the oil in the sauce; the garlic provides plenty of flavour.

History

The wet burger is closely associated with Taksim Square in Istanbul, where stalls have sold it for decades, especially late at night. It is now found in other Turkish cities too.
