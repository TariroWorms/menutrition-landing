A lobster roll is a soft, split-top bun filled with chunks of cooked lobster meat. In the classic Maine style the meat is cold and lightly dressed with mayonnaise, a little diced celery and a squeeze of lemon. The result is sweet, tender seafood against a toasted, slightly crisp roll, usually served with a lemon wedge and a side of chips or coleslaw.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g cooked lobster meat, cut into chunks
  • 4 split-top buns
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 celery stalk, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice and lemon wedges
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 tbsp oil for toasting, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Mix the lobster with mayonnaise, celery, lemon juice, chives, salt and pepper; chill for 10 minutes.
  2. Brush the sides of the buns lightly with oil and toast them in a hot pan until golden.
  3. Open the buns and fill generously with the lobster mixture.
  4. Serve at once with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Use just enough mayonnaise to coat the meat — each tablespoon adds about 90 kcal.
  • Toast the bun in a dry pan instead of brushing it with oil.
  • Serve with a green salad instead of fries or chips.

History

The lobster roll is a New England creation; a restaurant in Milford, Connecticut, is often credited with serving the first ones in the 1920s, though the claim is hard to verify. Today it is a summer staple of seaside shacks from Maine to Massachusetts and has spread to seafood restaurants worldwide.
