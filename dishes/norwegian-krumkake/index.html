Krumkake (Norwegian rolled cookie)

Origin: Norway

Krumkake is a paper-thin, patterned cookie baked between hot irons and rolled into a cone while still warm. It tastes of butter and a little cardamom and shatters with a light crunch. It is often filled with whipped cream and berries just before serving.

Recipe

6 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 125 g flour
  • 100 g sugar
  • 100 g butter, melted
  • 2 eggs
  • 100 ml milk
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 200 ml whipped cream

Method

  1. Whisk eggs and sugar until pale.
  2. Stir in the melted butter, milk and cardamom.
  3. Fold in the flour to a smooth, pourable batter.
  4. Heat the krumkake iron and add a spoonful of batter.
  5. Bake until golden, about 40 seconds per side.
  6. Roll quickly around a cone while still hot and let it set.
  7. Fill with whipped cream just before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve plain or with fresh berries instead of heavy cream.
  • Use a little less sugar; the cardamom adds sweetness of aroma.
  • Fill only at the last moment so the cones stay crisp.

History

It is a classic Christmas bake in Norwegian homes, made with a special decorated iron. Each family tends to have its own favourite batter and its own way of shaping the cones.
