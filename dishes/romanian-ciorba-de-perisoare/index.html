Ciorbă de perișoare (Romanian meatball soup)

Origin: Romania

This is a sour soup of small rice-and-pork meatballs with carrots, celery and onion. It is soured with borș or lemon and finished with fresh parsley and lovage. Sour cream is often stirred in at the table.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g minced pork
  • 50 g rice
  • 1 egg
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 1.5 l water or stock
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • parsley

Method

  1. Mix mince, rice, egg, parsley and salt.
  2. Form small balls.
  3. Simmer diced onion and carrots in water.
  4. Drop in the meatballs and cook 30 minutes.
  5. Stir in lemon juice or borș.
  6. Add parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean mince or turkey.
  • Replace sour cream with yogurt.
  • Add more vegetables.

History

Sour ciorbă soups are the heart of Romanian home cooking. The meatball version is a common weekend family dish.
