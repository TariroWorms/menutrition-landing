Banana frita com canela (fried banana with cinnamon)

Origin: Brazil

Banana frita com canela is ripe banana sliced lengthwise or into rounds, fried until golden and caramelized, then tossed in cinnamon sugar. The outside is lightly crisp and sticky while the inside turns soft and creamy. It is a quick home dessert and also a common side to rice, beans and meat at lunch.

What it is made of

bananasugarcinnamonoil

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe but firm bananas
  • 30 ml vegetable oil
  • 40 g sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Mix the sugar and cinnamon on a plate.
  2. Peel the bananas and cut lengthwise or into thick rounds.
  3. Heat the oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  4. Fry the bananas 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Drain briefly and roll in cinnamon sugar while hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and just a teaspoon of oil.
  • Very ripe bananas need little or no sugar; cinnamon alone works.
  • Serve with plain yogurt for a more balanced dessert.

History

Bananas are among the most eaten fruits in Brazil and grow across the whole country. Frying them is an everyday home technique, and the cinnamon-sugar version is a familiar dessert in family kitchens.
