Pittu (steamed rice-flour and coconut cylinders)

Origin: Sri Lanka
255kcal

Pittu is made by layering roasted rice flour and grated coconut in a cylindrical steamer so that it cooks into crumbly, soft cylinders. It is mild, tender and slightly sweet from the coconut. It is eaten with coconut milk, curry or sambol for breakfast and dinner.

What it is made of

rice flourcoconutsalt

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g roasted rice flour
  • 100 g grated coconut
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 120 ml warm water

Method

  1. Mix rice flour, salt and water to damp crumbs.
  2. Layer coconut and flour crumbs in a pittu steamer.
  3. Steam for 8-10 minutes.
  4. Push out the cylinders gently.
  5. Serve warm with curry or coconut milk.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less coconut.
  • Choose wholegrain flour.
  • Serve with lentils or vegetables.

History

Pittu is a traditional food of both Sri Lanka and southern India, widely eaten in the north and east of the island. It is also a staple on journeys and at festivals.
