Toyug chighirtmasi (chicken with eggs)

Origin: Azerbaijan
450kcal

Toyug chighirtmasi is chicken cooked slowly with a large amount of onion in butter, then with tomatoes, until the sauce is thick and sweet. Just before serving, eggs are poured over and left to set around the meat. Saffron and black pepper are typical seasonings. It is served hot from the pan with bread and greens.

What it is made of

chickenoniontomatoeggbuttersaffron

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 4 onions
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 4 eggs
  • 40 g butter
  • a pinch of saffron
  • salt, black pepper, cilantro

Method

  1. Brown the chicken pieces in half of the butter.
  2. Add the sliced onions and the rest of the butter, cover and cook gently 20 minutes.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and simmer 20 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  4. Season with salt, pepper and the saffron steeped in a spoonful of hot water.
  5. Beat the eggs, pour them over the chicken, cover and cook until set.
  6. Sprinkle with herbs and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken breast or thigh to reduce fat.
  • Replace part of the butter with a little olive oil.
  • Add bell peppers or spinach for extra vegetables.

History

Chighirtma is one of the classic home dishes of Azerbaijan, and the chicken version is the best known. It is often made for guests because it is quick to prepare in one pan.
