🍗 Steamed chicken feet (phoenix claws)

Origin: China · Guangdong

Steamed chicken feet, known in dim sum as phoenix claws, are first cooked until puffy, then steamed in black bean sauce with chili, garlic and star anise. The skin becomes soft and gelatinous and soaks up the savory, slightly spicy sauce. They are served in small bamboo baskets and eaten by nibbling the skin off the small bones.

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken feet, claws trimmed
  • 3 tbsp black bean sauce
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1–2 red chilies, sliced
  • 2 star anise
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Vegetable oil for frying
  • Water

Method

  1. Blanch the feet for 5 minutes, drain and dry very well.
  2. Deep-fry until golden, then soak in cold water for 1 hour so the skin puffs.
  3. Simmer the feet with star anise in water for 30 minutes and drain.
  4. Fry garlic and chili in a little oil, add black bean sauce and sugar, then toss the feet in it.
  5. Steam in small dishes for 20 minutes and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the deep-frying step and simply simmer then steam; the skin will be less puffy but less oily.
  • Use less black bean sauce, which is salty.
  • Count roughly 4–5 pieces as one dim sum portion.

History

The dish is a classic of Cantonese dim sum served in teahouses in Guangdong and Hong Kong, and it travelled with Cantonese communities to Chinatowns worldwide.
