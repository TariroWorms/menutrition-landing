Rigó Jancsi (chocolate mousse cake)

Origin: Hungary

Rigó Jancsi is a square slice of cocoa sponge with a thick layer of chocolate whipped cream and a shiny dark glaze. The texture is soft and airy while the flavour is deeply chocolatey with a touch of apricot. It is cut into neat cubes and served with coffee.

Recipe

12 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 120 g dark chocolate
  • 400 ml whipping cream
  • 4 eggs
  • 100 g sugar
  • 60 g flour
  • 30 g cocoa
  • 50 g apricot jam

Method

  1. Whisk eggs with sugar, fold in flour and cocoa and bake the sponge at 180 C for 15 minutes.
  2. Melt half the chocolate and let it cool.
  3. Whip the cream and fold in the melted chocolate to make the mousse.
  4. Brush the sponge with jam and spread the mousse on top, then chill for 3 hours.
  5. Cover with the remaining melted chocolate and cut into squares.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve small pieces; it is rich.
  • Use 70 percent chocolate for a deeper flavour with less sugar.
  • Fold a little yoghurt into the cream to lighten it.

History

It is a classic of Hungarian pastry shops named after a Romani violinist, Jancsi Rigó. Its exact origins are told in several versions, but the cake has been popular across Hungary for over a century.
