Semolina porridge (mannaya kasha)

Origin: Russia

Mannaya kasha is a soft, silky porridge made by whisking fine wheat semolina into simmering milk. It tastes mild and gently sweet and is usually finished with butter, jam, honey or berries. It is a classic breakfast for children across Russia.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 60 g semolina
  • 1-2 tbsp sugar
  • a pinch of salt
  • 15 g butter

Method

  1. Bring the milk to a gentle boil with the salt and sugar.
  2. Pour the semolina in a thin stream while whisking constantly.
  3. Lower the heat and cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring, until thickened.
  4. Take off the heat, cover and leave for 2 minutes.
  5. Serve with a piece of butter and berries or jam.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk or half milk and half water.
  • Skip the sugar and sweeten with fresh fruit.
  • Leave out the butter and top with a few nuts or seeds instead.

History

Semolina porridge was a fixture of Soviet kindergarten and school breakfasts, which is why many Russians associate it with childhood. Lumps in the porridge are a well-known joke, so cooks take care to whisk constantly.
