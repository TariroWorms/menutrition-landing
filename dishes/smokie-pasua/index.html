Smokie pasua (Nairobi street sausage)

Origin: Kenya

Smokie pasua is a smoked sausage boiled or fried, split open and stuffed with kachumbari, a fresh mix of tomato, onion and chili. It is salty, juicy and tangy. It is a cheap street snack found at roadside stalls in Nairobi.

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 smoked sausages (400 g)
  • 2 tomatoes (200 g)
  • 1 red onion (80 g)
  • 1 green chili
  • cilantro
  • lime juice
  • salt

Method

  1. Boil or grill the sausages until hot.
  2. Dice the tomatoes and onion and mince the chili.
  3. Mix with cilantro, lime and salt to make kachumbari.
  4. Split each sausage lengthwise.
  5. Fill with kachumbari and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose chicken sausage for less fat.
  • Add extra kachumbari for fibre.
  • Pair with a salad instead of fries.

History

The name mixes English "smokie" with Swahili "pasua", meaning "split". It became a popular street food in Nairobi in recent decades.
