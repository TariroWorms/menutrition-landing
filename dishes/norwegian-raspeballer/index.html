Raspeballer (Norwegian potato dumplings)

Origin: Norway

Raspeballer are large, firm dumplings made from raw grated potato and flour, with a piece of salted pork in the centre in some regions. They are boiled in broth and have a dense, slightly grey interior. They are served with salted meat, sausage, rutabaga mash and melted butter.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg raw potatoes
  • 150 g flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 200 g salted pork, cubed
  • 500 g rutabaga
  • butter

Method

  1. Grate the potatoes finely and drain off the liquid.
  2. Mix with flour and salt to a thick dough.
  3. Shape into balls, pressing a piece of pork into each.
  4. Boil gently in salted water or broth for about 60 minutes.
  5. Serve with mashed rutabaga and melted butter.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a leaner cut of pork.
  • Serve with plenty of rutabaga and carrots.
  • Use barley flour for part of the flour.

History

The dish has many regional names such as komle or kumle, and is eaten across the western and central parts of Norway. It was a practical, filling meal based on cheap staples.
