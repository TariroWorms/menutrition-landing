🍃 Etli Yaprak Sarma (Stuffed Grape Leaves with Meat)

Origin: Turkey

Etli yaprak sarma are grape leaves rolled tightly around a filling of minced beef, rice, onion, tomato and spices, then simmered gently until tender. Unlike the cold olive-oil version, the meat version is served warm. The leaves are tangy, the filling is soft and savoury.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 40 grape leaves (jarred, rinsed)
  • 250 g minced beef
  • 80 g short-grain rice, rinsed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, grated
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp each dried mint, paprika and black pepper
  • Salt
  • 400 ml hot water

Method

  1. Mix the beef, rice, onion, tomato, half the tomato paste, spices and salt.
  2. Place a teaspoon of filling at the base of each leaf, fold in the sides and roll tightly.
  3. Pack the rolls seam-side down in a pot and set a small plate on top.
  4. Mix the hot water with the remaining tomato paste and pour over.
  5. Simmer covered on low heat for 45–50 minutes until the rice is cooked; serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean minced beef to lower the fat.
  • They are small, so count them: a portion of 8–10 rolls is usually enough.
  • Serve with a large green salad on the side.

History

Stuffed vine leaves are found throughout the former Ottoman lands, from the Balkans to the Levant, and the Turkish word sarma simply means 'wrapped'. In Turkey the meat-filled version is eaten hot as a main dish, while the rice-only version is a cold meze.
