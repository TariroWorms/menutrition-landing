Pölsa (Swedish offal hash)

Origin: Sweden · Norrland

Pölsa is a traditional hash of minced offal such as heart, liver and tongue, cooked with onion and pearl barley until thick and spiced with allspice and marjoram. It is earthy, hearty and savory with a soft, spoonable texture. It is served with boiled potatoes, pickled beetroot and often a fried egg.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g minced pork liver and heart
  • 100 g pearl barley
  • 1 pc onion
  • 500 ml meat broth
  • 2 g allspice
  • 1 g dried marjoram
  • 600 g potatoes
  • 100 g sliced pickled beetroot
  • salt – to taste

Method

  1. Mince or finely chop the offal and onion.
  2. Simmer with broth, pearl barley, allspice and marjoram for about 1.5 hours.
  3. Stir occasionally and add more broth if needed until thick.
  4. Season with salt.
  5. Serve with boiled potatoes and pickled beetroot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less liver and more barley for fibre.
  • Serve with plenty of vegetables.
  • Skip added salt, as broth and beetroot add plenty.

History

Pölsa is a traditional dish of northern Sweden, born from the need to use every part of the animal. It is an old-fashioned comfort food that remains a regional favourite.
