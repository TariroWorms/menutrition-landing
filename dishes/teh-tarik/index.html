Teh tarik (Malaysian pulled milk tea)

Origin: Malaysia

Teh tarik is a hot, sweet milk tea whose foam comes from pouring it between two vessels in a long arc. It tastes malty, caramel-like and smooth. It is drunk at mamak stalls with roti canai or nasi lemak.

What it is made of

black teacondensed milkmilk

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp black tea leaves (12 g)
  • 500 ml water
  • 80 ml condensed milk
  • 100 ml evaporated or whole milk
  • pinch of salt

Method

  1. Boil the water and steep the tea leaves for 5 minutes until very dark.
  2. Strain the tea into a jug.
  3. Stir condensed milk and milk into the tea.
  4. Pour the tea from one jug into another from a height, several times, until frothy.
  5. Serve at once in warm glasses.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the condensed milk or an unsweetened version to cut sugar.
  • Swap whole milk for skimmed or oat milk.
  • Ask for 'kurang manis' (less sweet) when ordering.

History

The drink is associated with the Indian-Muslim (mamak) communities of Malaysia, whose stalls made it a national favourite. The pulling cools the tea and gives it a light, airy body.
