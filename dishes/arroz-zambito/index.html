Arroz zambito (Peruvian rice pudding with chancaca)

Origin: Peru

Arroz zambito is the brown cousin of Peru's classic arroz con leche. Instead of white sugar it is sweetened with chancaca, unrefined cane sugar, which gives the rice a caramel colour and a deep molasses taste. Grated coconut, raisins, chopped walnuts, cinnamon and cloves are cooked in, making it thick, chewy and fragrant. It is eaten warm or at room temperature, by itself or next to mazamorra morada.

What it is made of

ricepanela (chancaca)milkcoconutraisinswalnutscinnamoncloves

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g rice
  • 250 g chancaca or panela
  • 500 ml milk, 500 ml water
  • 60 g grated coconut
  • 50 g raisins, 40 g walnuts
  • 2 cinnamon sticks, 4 cloves

Method

  1. Melt the chancaca with 200 ml water, cinnamon and cloves into a syrup; strain.
  2. Cook the rice in the remaining water until almost tender.
  3. Pour in the syrup and the milk and simmer, stirring, for 20 minutes.
  4. Add coconut, raisins and walnuts and cook until thick and creamy.
  5. Rest a few minutes and serve warm or at room temperature.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk and two-thirds of the chancaca.
  • Swap walnuts for a few toasted seeds if you avoid nuts.
  • Serve in small bowls; it is rich and filling.

History

The dessert is linked to Lima's Afro-Peruvian community, and its name refers to the dark colour of the rice. It is a traditional sweet of Lima homes and of street vendors in neighbourhoods such as Barrios Altos.
