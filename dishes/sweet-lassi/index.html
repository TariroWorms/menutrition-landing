🥛 Sweet Lassi

Origin: India · Punjab

Sweet lassi is a thick, chilled yogurt drink from North India, blended with sugar and a little water until frothy. It is often scented with ground cardamom or a few drops of rose water and served in tall glasses or clay cups. Creamy, cool and lightly tangy, it is a classic partner for spicy food and a favourite on hot summer days.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g plain yogurt
  • 150 ml cold water
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • ¼ tsp ground cardamom
  • ½ tsp rose water
  • ice cubes

Method

  1. Put yogurt, water, sugar and cardamom in a blender.
  2. Blend for about 30 seconds until smooth and frothy.
  3. Stir in the rose water and taste for sweetness.
  4. Pour over ice and serve right away.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt for a lighter glass.
  • Reduce the sugar to 1 tbsp — each teaspoon of sugar is about 16 kcal.
  • Choose a smaller glass of 150–200 ml and log it as part of the meal.

History

Lassi is closely associated with Punjab, where yogurt-based drinks have long been part of everyday farm life. Today it is sold across India and Pakistan at street stalls and restaurants, in both sweet and salted versions.
