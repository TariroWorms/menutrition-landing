Lazy varenyky (cottage cheese dumplings)

Origin: Ukraine

Lazy varenyky take the cheese filling and turn it into the dough itself: cottage cheese is mixed with egg, a little sugar and just enough flour to hold together. The dough is rolled into a log, cut into small pieces and boiled, so nothing needs to be wrapped. They are soft, milky and gently sweet, served with butter, sour cream or jam, often for breakfast.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g cottage cheese (tvorog)
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp sugar (25 g)
  • 120 g flour plus extra for dusting
  • 20 g butter
  • 100 g sour cream, pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mash or sieve the cottage cheese until smooth.
  2. Mix in the egg, sugar and salt, then add flour to make a soft dough.
  3. On a floured board roll it into ropes about 2 cm thick.
  4. Cut into pieces 2 cm long, pressing them lightly with a fork if you like.
  5. Boil in salted water for 2–3 minutes after they rise.
  6. Serve with melted butter and sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat cottage cheese and skip the butter.
  • Top with fresh berries instead of jam.
  • Cut the sugar in half; the dough tastes fine with less.

History

The dish got its name because it saves the work of rolling and filling dough circles. It is a well-loved home and nursery dish in Ukraine and neighbouring countries.
