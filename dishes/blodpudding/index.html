Black pudding (Blodpudding)

Origin: Sweden

Blodpudding is a dense baked pudding made from pig blood, rye flour, milk and syrup, spiced with allspice. It is rich, earthy and slightly sweet with a firm, sliceable texture. It is cut in slices, fried in butter and served with lingonberry jam and bacon or pork.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml pig blood
  • 200 g rye flour
  • 300 ml milk
  • 40 g dark syrup
  • 1 pc onion
  • 2 g allspice
  • salt – to taste
  • 30 g butter
  • 100 g lingonberry jam

Method

  1. Mix the blood, milk, syrup and spices.
  2. Stir in the rye flour and salt to a smooth batter.
  3. Pour into a greased loaf tin and bake at 175°C for about 1 hour.
  4. Cool, then slice.
  5. Fry slices in butter until crisp and serve with lingonberry jam.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less syrup for less sugar.
  • Fry with minimal butter or bake the slices.
  • Serve with plenty of lingonberries and greens.

History

Blood pudding is an old Swedish way to make use of the whole animal and was traditionally made in the autumn at slaughter time. It remains a regional favourite, often served on weekdays.
