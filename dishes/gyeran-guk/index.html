Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 800 ml light beef or anchovy broth
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • pinch of salt and pepper

Method

  1. Bring the broth to a boil with garlic and soy sauce.
  2. Beat the eggs lightly with a pinch of salt.
  3. Lower the heat to a gentle simmer.
  4. Pour in the eggs in a thin stream while stirring slowly so ribbons form.
  5. Add scallions, cook 30 seconds, then finish with sesame oil and pepper.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a homemade low-sodium broth and less soy sauce.
  • Add tofu or mushrooms for more bulk and protein.
  • Use 2 whole eggs plus 2 whites for fewer calories.

History

A simple home soup that is made in minutes from pantry ingredients. It is a typical soothing dish served alongside rice and banchan.
