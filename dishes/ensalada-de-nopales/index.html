Nopales salad (ensalada de nopales)

Origin: Mexico

Ensalada de nopales is a cool salad of boiled cactus paddles cut into strips and mixed with tomato, white onion, cilantro and green chile. The nopales have a pleasant bite and a lemony, green-bean-like flavour. It is eaten as a side with grilled meat, in tacos or simply with tortillas.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g cleaned nopales, in strips
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 white onion
  • 1 handful cilantro
  • 1 serrano or jalapeño
  • 50 g queso fresco
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Method

  1. Boil the nopales in salted water with a slice of onion for 10–15 minutes.
  2. Drain and rinse well to remove the slime, then cool.
  3. Chop the tomato, onion, cilantro and chile.
  4. Mix everything with the nopales, lime juice, oregano and salt.
  5. Top with crumbled cheese and chill before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • This salad is naturally low in calories; keep the cheese light.
  • Add avocado for healthy fats if it is a main meal.
  • Grilling the nopales instead of boiling avoids most of the slime.

History

The prickly pear cactus has been eaten in Mexico since pre-Hispanic times and appears on the national coat of arms. Nopales are sold fresh and cleaned in markets all over the country.
