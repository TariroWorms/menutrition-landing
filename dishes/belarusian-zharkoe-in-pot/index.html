Zharkoe (pork and potatoes in a pot)

Origin: Belarus

Zharkoe u garshku is a Belarusian pot roast in which browned pork, potatoes, mushrooms and onion are layered in clay pots and baked slowly. The vegetables soak up the meat juices and the mushrooms add an earthy depth. It is served right in the pot, often topped with dill and sour cream.

Recipe

4 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork shoulder
  • 800 g potatoes
  • 250 g mushrooms
  • 1 onion (80 g)
  • 1 carrot (75 g)
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 300 ml stock
  • 1 bay leaf, salt, pepper, dill

Method

  1. Cut the pork into cubes and brown quickly in a pan; season.
  2. Fry the onion, carrot and sliced mushrooms until soft.
  3. Layer meat, vegetables and cubed potatoes in 4 clay pots or one casserole.
  4. Mix the stock with sour cream, pour over, add the bay leaf and cover.
  5. Bake at 180 °C for about 1 hour; sprinkle with dill before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork loin and trim the fat.
  • Replace part of the potatoes with more mushrooms and carrots.
  • Use light sour cream or skip it and add more herbs.

History

Cooking in clay pots in the traditional wood-fired stove was common in Belarusian villages, where food was left to slowly stew for hours. Forest mushrooms, gathered widely in Belarus, are a typical addition.
