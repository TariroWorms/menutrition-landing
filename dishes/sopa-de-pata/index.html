Sopa de pata (Salvadoran cow-foot and tripe soup)

Origin: El Salvador
300kcal

Sopa de pata is a hearty soup in which cow's feet and tripe are simmered for hours until tender and the broth turns silky. Corn on the cob, cassava, plantain and cabbage are added at the end. It is served with lime, chopped cilantro and tortillas.

Recipe

6 servings240 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg cow's feet, cut
  • 500 g tripe, cleaned
  • 2 corn cobs, cut in pieces
  • 300 g cassava
  • 2 green plantains
  • 1/4 cabbage
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • Cilantro, lime, salt

Method

  1. Boil feet and tripe with onion and garlic for 3 hours until tender.
  2. Skim the broth.
  3. Add cassava and corn and cook 20 minutes.
  4. Add plantain and cabbage and cook 15 minutes.
  5. Season with salt.
  6. Serve with cilantro and lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim fat from the broth for a lighter soup.
  • Add more vegetables for balance.
  • Serve smaller portions as it is rich.

History

It is a traditional weekend and celebration soup in El Salvador, often eaten at family gatherings. The long simmering is typical of Central American peasant cooking that makes good use of every part of the animal.
