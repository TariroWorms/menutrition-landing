🥛 Doogh

Doogh is a cold, savoury yogurt drink from Iran made by whisking yogurt with water, salt and dried mint, often topped up with sparkling water for a light fizz. It is tangy, salty and refreshing, thinner than ayran when diluted well. It is traditionally served alongside grilled kebabs and rice, especially in hot weather.

Recipe

4 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g plain yogurt
  • 400 ml cold water
  • 400 ml cold sparkling water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp dried mint
  • Ice cubes

Method

  1. Whisk the yogurt with the still water and salt until completely smooth.
  2. Rub the dried mint between your palms into the jug and stir.
  3. Just before serving, stir in the sparkling water gently and pour over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt for an even lighter glass.
  • Dilute it more with sparkling water for a refreshing drink with fewer calories per glass.
  • Watch the salt if you drink several glasses a day.

History

Yogurt drinks like doogh have been made for centuries across Persia and the neighbouring regions; the name comes from a Persian word related to milking. Today doogh is drunk all over Iran and also in Afghanistan, Azerbaijan and Kurdish areas, both homemade and bottled.
