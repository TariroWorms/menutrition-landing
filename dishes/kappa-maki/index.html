🥒 Kappa maki (cucumber roll)

Origin: Japan

Kappa maki is a thin Japanese sushi roll of vinegared rice and a single strip of crisp cucumber wrapped in nori seaweed. It is fresh, crunchy and mild, with a light tang from the rice. Usually cut into six pieces, it is a common choice for a light meal, a palate cleanser between richer sushi, or a plant-based option.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g sushi rice
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp sugar, ½ tsp salt
  • 2 nori sheets, halved
  • 1 cucumber, cut into long thin strips
  • Wasabi (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse and cook the rice with 220 ml of water.
  2. Dissolve the sugar and salt in the vinegar, fold into the hot rice and let it cool.
  3. Lay a half sheet of nori on a bamboo mat and spread a thin layer of rice over it, leaving 1 cm free at the top edge.
  4. Place a strip of cucumber along the middle and roll tightly using the mat.
  5. Cut each roll into six pieces with a wet knife.

How to make it lighter

  • Kappa maki is one of the lightest sushi choices; pair it with sashimi for protein.
  • Keep the rice layer thin to lower the calories per piece.
  • Go easy on salty dipping sauces.

History

The roll is named after the kappa, a water creature in Japanese folklore said to love cucumbers. Kappa maki is a standard item at sushi restaurants in Japan and worldwide.
