🍣 Temaki (hand roll)

Origin: Japan

Temaki is a cone-shaped sushi hand roll: a sheet of crisp nori wrapped around vinegared rice, raw salmon, creamy avocado and fresh cucumber. It is eaten with the hands, ideally straight after rolling while the nori still crackles. Temaki is popular at home sushi parties, where everyone rolls their own.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g cooked sushi rice
  • 4 nori sheets, halved
  • 200 g sashimi-grade salmon, cut into strips
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 cucumber, cut into sticks
  • Rice vinegar, sugar and salt for the rice

Method

  1. Season the warm rice with vinegar, sugar and salt and let it cool.
  2. Hold a half nori sheet shiny side down and spread a little rice on the left third.
  3. Lay salmon, avocado and cucumber diagonally on the rice.
  4. Fold the bottom-left corner over the filling and roll into a cone.
  5. Eat immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a thinner layer of rice and more cucumber.
  • Use a quarter of an avocado per roll — avocado is calorie-dense.
  • Two hand rolls with a salad make a balanced meal.

History

Temaki literally means 'hand roll' in Japanese. It is a casual form of sushi made at home and at sushi bars; salmon and avocado fillings became popular in the late 20th century, influenced by sushi abroad.
