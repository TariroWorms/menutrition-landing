Caldillo de congrio is a Chilean soup of conger eel simmered with potato and onion in a light broth and finished with a splash of cream. It is warming, delicately savoury and rich without being heavy. It is served hot in a bowl with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g conger eel (or firm white fish) in thick steaks
  • 3 potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 litre of water or fish stock
  • 100 ml cream
  • 1 bay leaf, salt and pepper
  • Fresh coriander or parsley, and lemon wedges

Method

  1. Soften the onion, garlic and carrot in the oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the potatoes, bay leaf and the water or stock and simmer for 12 minutes.
  3. Season the fish with salt and pepper and lay it in the pot.
  4. Simmer gently for 8–10 minutes until the fish is just cooked through.
  5. Stir in the cream and warm for 2 minutes without boiling.
  6. Scatter the herbs and serve hot with lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream, or swap it for a few spoonfuls of milk, to lower the fat.
  • Add more carrot and onion for extra vegetables: the soup stays light at about 75 kcal per 100 g.
  • Eat it with a slice of wholemeal bread instead of a roll with butter.

History

Congrio, the conger eel, is a prized fish of the Chilean coast, and this soup is one of the best-loved dishes of the sea there. The poet Pablo Neruda wrote a famous ode to it, which helped make the dish known well beyond Chile. It is a staple of seaside restaurants and markets, and a favourite at home on cool days.
