Begun bhaja (fried eggplant slices)

Origin: Bangladesh
173kcal

Begun bhaja is made of thick eggplant rounds rubbed with turmeric, chilli powder and salt, then shallow-fried in mustard oil until crisp outside and creamy inside. The mustard oil adds a sharp, nutty edge. It is a classic side with dal and rice, and often with khichuri on rainy days.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants (600 g)
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 4 tbsp mustard oil (60 ml)
  • salt

Method

  1. Cut the eggplant into 1 cm rounds.
  2. Rub with turmeric, chilli powder and salt and rest 10 minutes.
  3. Heat the mustard oil until it smokes lightly.
  4. Fry the slices on both sides until golden and tender.
  5. Drain on paper and serve hot with rice and dal.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush slices with oil and bake instead of frying.
  • Salt the slices first to draw out water and reduce oil absorption.
  • Serve with a lentil soup for protein.

History

Bhaja, meaning fried, is a large category of Bengali side dishes. Eggplant, called begun, is among the most popular vegetables for it and appears both in Bangladesh and in West Bengal.
