Uzbek dolma (stuffed peppers)

Origin: Uzbekistan

Uzbek dolma is a summer dish of sweet peppers filled with minced lamb or beef, rice and onion. The peppers are packed tightly in a pot and gently stewed in a light tomato and carrot broth until tender. It is served hot with the cooking juices and a spoonful of yogurt or sour cream.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 8 bell peppers
  • 400 g minced lamb or beef
  • 100 g rice
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 600 ml water
  • salt, pepper, cumin

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and mix it with the meat, chopped onion and spices.
  2. Cut the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds.
  3. Fill the peppers loosely, leaving room for the rice to swell.
  4. Grate the carrot and tomatoes into the pot, add water and salt.
  5. Stand the peppers upright, cover and simmer for 50 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef or turkey to cut the fat.
  • Replace part of the rice with chopped vegetables in the filling.
  • Serve with plain yogurt instead of sour cream.

History

Stuffed vegetables are common across the former Ottoman and Persian worlds, and Uzbekistan has its own versions using peppers, tomatoes, quince and vine leaves. In Uzbek homes dolma is mainly made in late summer when peppers are cheap and plentiful.
