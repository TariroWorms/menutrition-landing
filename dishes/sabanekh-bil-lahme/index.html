🥬 Spinach Stew with Meat (Sabanekh bil Lahme)

Sabanekh bil lahme is a Levantine stew of spinach cooked with chunks of lamb, onion and plenty of garlic and fresh cilantro, finished with a generous squeeze of lemon juice. The spinach wilts into a soft, leafy sauce around the tender meat, with a bright, tangy finish. It is a comforting home dish, usually served over rice.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb, cubed
  • 700 g spinach, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 5 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 500 ml water, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in the oil, add the onion and cook until soft.
  2. Add the water, salt and pepper, cover and simmer for 45 minutes.
  3. Fry the garlic and cilantro briefly in a dry pan or with a few drops of oil until fragrant.
  4. Add the spinach to the meat and cook for 10 minutes until wilted.
  5. Stir in the garlic-cilantro mix and the lemon juice and simmer for 5 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean lamb and trim visible fat.
  • Make the stew the centre of the plate and keep the rice to a small side.
  • Double the spinach for a larger, more filling bowl.

History

Spinach stews with meat are everyday home cooking in Lebanon, Syria, Palestine and Jordan, part of the wider family of yakhneh vegetable stews. The garlic-and-cilantro base, often called taqliyeh, is typical of the region's cooking.
