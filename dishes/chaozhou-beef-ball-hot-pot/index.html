Chaozhou beef ball hot pot

Origin: China · Guangdong

Chaoshan hot pot is built around beef cut by cut, from hand-beaten beef balls to paper-thin slices of different muscles. The broth is clear and lightly beefy, and each piece is dipped for seconds only. Diners finish with a sand-ginger or satay dip and a bowl of the enriched broth.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g sliced beef (brisket and shoulder)
  • 300 g beef balls
  • 1.5 l clear beef broth
  • 200 g napa cabbage
  • 2 shallots
  • 20 g ginger
  • Sand-ginger or satay dip to serve

Method

  1. Bring the beef broth to a gentle simmer with ginger and shallots.
  2. Add the beef balls and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until they float and bounce.
  3. Add the napa cabbage and let it soften.
  4. Dip the thin beef slices in the broth for 10 to 15 seconds only.
  5. Lift out and dip into the sauce.
  6. Finish by sipping the broth.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean cuts and trim visible fat.
  • Load up on napa cabbage and greens to make the meal lighter.
  • Use a small dip portion to keep salt and fat down.

History

The dish comes from the Chaoshan region of eastern Guangdong, where beef hot pot became a local specialty. Its hallmark is the hand-pounded beef ball, which is prized for its bounce.
