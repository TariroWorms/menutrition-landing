A pork chop is a thick cut of pork loin, usually on the bone, seasoned with garlic and herbs and grilled or pan-seared until browned outside and juicy within. Brushed with olive oil and finished with a short rest, it has a savoury, slightly sweet flavour. It is served as a main course with vegetables, potatoes or a salad.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 bone-in pork chops (about 250 g each)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 sprigs thyme
  • 1 sprig rosemary
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Take the chops out of the fridge 20 minutes ahead and pat them dry; season with salt and pepper.
  2. Rub them with the olive oil, garlic and chopped herbs.
  3. Grill or sear over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes per side, depending on thickness.
  4. Check that the centre reaches 63 °C, then rest the chops for 5 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose a loin chop and trim the visible fat rim before cooking.
  • Brush the oil on the meat rather than pouring it into the pan; 1 tbsp olive oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Pair it with grilled vegetables or a big salad to keep the plate filling.

History

Pork chops are eaten across Europe and the Americas as an everyday cut of meat, cooked on the grill, in a pan or under the broiler.
