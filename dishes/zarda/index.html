🍚 Zarda

Zarda is a sweet rice dish from South Asia: basmati rice cooked with sugar, ghee, saffron and cardamom until glossy and golden-yellow, then studded with raisins and almonds. The grains stay separate, sweet and fragrant. It is served at weddings, festivals and family celebrations, often after a biryani or pulao.

Recipe

6 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g basmati rice
  • 200 g sugar
  • 3 tbsp ghee
  • Pinch of saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm water
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 50 g raisins
  • 40 g almonds, slivered

Method

  1. Soak the rice for 30 minutes, then boil until three-quarters cooked and drain.
  2. Warm the ghee with the cardamom and lightly toast the almonds and raisins.
  3. Add the rice, sugar and saffron water and fold gently.
  4. Cover tightly and cook on very low heat for 15 minutes until the syrup is absorbed and the rice is tender.
  5. Fluff gently and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Zarda is dense in sugar and ghee, so a few spoonfuls go a long way as dessert.
  • Reduce the ghee to 1–2 tbsp; 1 tbsp ghee ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Cut the sugar by a quarter and let the raisins add sweetness.

History

The name comes from the Persian word zard, 'yellow', a nod to the saffron colour, and the dish is often associated with Mughal-era cooking in the Indian subcontinent. It remains a celebratory dessert in Pakistan, India and Bangladesh.
