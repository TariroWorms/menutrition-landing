Botvinya (cold kvass soup with greens and fish)

Origin: Russia

Botvinya is a chilled soup made from boiled and puréed beet greens, sorrel or spinach, mixed with fresh cucumber, herbs and cold kvass. It is tart, green and very refreshing. Traditionally it is served alongside pieces of cold poached fish such as salmon or sturgeon.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g young beet greens
  • 200 g sorrel or spinach
  • 1 l cold kvass
  • 2 cucumbers
  • spring onion and dill
  • 1 tsp grated horseradish
  • 400 g salmon or white fish fillet
  • ice cubes

Method

  1. Boil the beet greens and sorrel for 5 minutes, drain and chop finely.
  2. Poach the fish in salted water for 8-10 minutes and cool.
  3. Dice the cucumbers and chop the spring onion and dill.
  4. Mix the greens, cucumber, herbs and horseradish.
  5. Pour in cold kvass and chill.
  6. Serve with the fish pieces and a few ice cubes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean white fish rather than oily fish.
  • Pick a less sweet kvass or dilute it with water.
  • Add extra cucumber for more volume.

History

Botvinya is one of the classic dishes of old Russian cuisine and was popular on noble and merchant tables in the 19th century. The name comes from botva, the leafy tops of root vegetables such as beets.
