Tika kabab (Azerbaijani lamb shashlik)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Tika kabab is the classic Azerbaijani skewer of lamb pieces. The meat, often from the leg or loin, is marinated for a few hours with grated onion, salt and pepper, then threaded on skewers, sometimes with small pieces of tail fat between them. It is grilled over embers and served with raw onion, sumac, herbs and grilled tomatoes and peppers.

What it is made of

lambonionsumacblack peppertomato

Recipe

4 servings240 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg lamb leg, cut in 4 cm chunks
  • 2 onions (300 g)
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • 4 tomatoes and 4 peppers for grilling
  • herbs, lavash
  • salt, black pepper

Method

  1. Grate one onion and rub it with salt and pepper into the lamb.
  2. Marinate in the fridge for 3–4 hours.
  3. Thread the meat onto skewers, not too tightly.
  4. Grill over glowing coals for 12–15 minutes, turning often, with the vegetables alongside.
  5. Serve with sliced raw onion, sumac and herbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean leg and skip the tail fat.
  • Fill half the plate with grilled vegetables.
  • Add a squeeze of lemon instead of fatty sauces.

History

Tika means piece or chunk, distinguishing it from minced lula kabab. Charcoal-grilled lamb is a fixture of Azerbaijani gatherings and roadside restaurants.
