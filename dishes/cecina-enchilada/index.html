Cecina enchilada (Oaxacan chile-rubbed pork)

Origin: Mexico · Oaxaca

Cecina enchilada is pork leg or loin cut into thin sheets and coated with a brick-red paste of guajillo and other dried chiles, garlic, vinegar and spices. It rests for several hours and is then grilled over charcoal, where the chile paste toasts and the fat crisps at the edges. Served with tortillas, salsa and beans, it is a favourite of Oaxacan market grills alongside tasajo.

What it is made of

porkdried chilesgarlicvinegaronion

Recipe

4 servings300 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g pork leg or loin
  • 5 guajillo chiles
  • 2 ancho chiles
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • salt

Method

  1. Slice the pork into thin sheets about 4 mm thick.
  2. Toast and soak the chiles, then blend with garlic, vinegar, oregano, cumin and salt to a thick paste.
  3. Coat the pork evenly and refrigerate at least 4 hours.
  4. Grill over hot charcoal 2-3 minutes per side.
  5. Serve with tortillas, grilled onions, beans and salsa.

How to make it lighter

  • Use pork loin and trim the fat cap.
  • Serve with lots of grilled vegetables and lime.
  • Reduce the ancho for less heat if needed.

History

In Oaxacan markets three grilled meats are usually offered side by side: tasajo (beef), cecina enchilada (chile pork) and chorizo. The chile coating once also helped preserve the meat.
