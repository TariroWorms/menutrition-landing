🫘 Loubieh bi zeit

Loubieh bi zeit means “green beans in oil”: flat green beans stewed slowly with olive oil, onion, garlic and tomato until very tender and coated in a thick, sweet sauce. It is one of the classic Levantine dishes cooked in olive oil and is usually eaten at room temperature, served on its own or alongside rice as a light main or side.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g flat green beans, trimmed and halved
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 6 garlic cloves, halved
  • 4 ripe tomatoes, chopped, or 400 g canned tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 5 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt, black pepper, ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • 200 ml water

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil and soften the onion for 5 minutes, then add the garlic.
  2. Add the green beans and stir for 5 minutes until they turn bright green.
  3. Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, spices and water.
  4. Cover and simmer on low heat for 40 minutes until the beans are very tender and the sauce is thick.
  5. Cool to room temperature and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Olive oil is the main source of calories here — 2–3 tbsp still gives good flavour, each about 120 kcal.
  • Enjoy it on its own or with a small portion of rice.
  • Add extra tomato and beans to make the pot go further.

History

Loubieh bi zeit belongs to the family of vegetable dishes cooked in olive oil (bi zeit) that are typical of Lebanon, Syria and Palestine and are traditionally eaten during Lent and on meatless days. It remains a common home dish and mezze item across the Levant.
