🍆 Karnıyarık (stuffed eggplant)

Origin: Turkey

Karnıyarık is a Turkish dish of whole eggplants, fried or roasted until soft, split down the middle and filled with minced beef cooked with onion, tomato and green pepper. They are topped with a slice of tomato and pepper and baked in a little tomato sauce. The eggplant becomes silky and the filling juicy; it is usually served with rice pilaf.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 4 medium eggplants
  • 300 g minced beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green peppers, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes (2 chopped, 1 sliced)
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp oil, salt, black pepper, chopped parsley

Method

  1. Peel the eggplants in stripes, brush with 2 tbsp oil and roast at 200 °C for 25 minutes until soft.
  2. Fry the onion in the remaining oil, add the beef and cook until browned.
  3. Add the peppers, chopped tomatoes and seasoning and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Split each eggplant lengthwise, open the pocket and fill with the meat.
  5. Top with tomato slices, pour over the tomato paste mixed with 150 ml water and bake for 20 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the eggplants instead of deep-frying them — they absorb far less oil.
  • Use lean minced beef and drain off the fat after browning.
  • Serve with salad instead of rice pilaf for a lighter plate.

History

The name means 'split belly' in Turkish, describing how the eggplant is opened for the filling. It is a classic of Ottoman-influenced home cooking and of lokanta canteens across Turkey.
