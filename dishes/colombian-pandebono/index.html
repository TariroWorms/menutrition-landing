Pandebono (Colombian cheese bread)

Origin: Colombia · Valle del Cauca
330kcal

Pandebono are small round or ring-shaped breads made from cassava starch, pre-cooked corn flour, fresh salty cheese and egg. Baked hot, they puff up with a thin golden crust and a stretchy, cheesy interior. They are best eaten straight from the oven with coffee or hot chocolate for breakfast or an afternoon snack.

Recipe

12 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g sour or sweet cassava starch
  • 50 g pre-cooked corn flour (masarepa)
  • 250 g fresh salty white cheese, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2-4 tbsp milk if needed
  • A pinch of baking powder

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C and line a tray with baking paper.
  2. Mix the cassava starch, corn flour, sugar and baking powder.
  3. Work in the cheese and egg by hand, adding milk until the dough is smooth and soft.
  4. Shape into 12 balls or small rings and place on the tray.
  5. Bake for 15-20 minutes until puffed and golden.
  6. Serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a lower-fat fresh cheese or part-skim mozzarella for some of the cheese.
  • Make them small and pair one with fruit for a balanced breakfast.
  • Naturally gluten-free: check that the corn flour and starch are certified if you need strict avoidance.

History

Pandebono is closely associated with the Valle del Cauca region around Cali, where bakeries sell it warm all day. Like other cassava-starch breads in South America, it is naturally gluten-free.
