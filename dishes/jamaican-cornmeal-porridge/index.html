Jamaican cornmeal porridge

Origin: Jamaica

Jamaican cornmeal porridge is a warm, smooth breakfast of cornmeal slowly cooked in water and coconut milk with cinnamon, nutmeg and vanilla. It is creamy and mildly sweet, thick enough to hold a spoon. It is popular for children and as a comforting morning meal.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 120 g fine cornmeal
  • 750 ml water
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg and 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • pinch of salt

Method

  1. Whisk the cornmeal with 250 ml cold water to a smooth slurry.
  2. Boil the remaining water with the cinnamon stick and salt.
  3. Stir in the slurry and cook on low heat 10-15 minutes, stirring.
  4. Add coconut milk, nutmeg, vanilla and sugar and cook 5 more minutes.
  5. Remove the cinnamon and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk to cut fat.
  • Reduce sugar and add fruit instead.
  • Stir constantly to avoid lumps and keep the porridge smooth.

History

Cornmeal porridge is a Jamaican breakfast staple, a close relative of porridges made from maize across the Americas. Its coconut milk and spices reflect local cooking.
