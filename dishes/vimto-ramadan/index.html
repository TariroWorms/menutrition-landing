🍇 Vimto (Ramadan Berry Cordial Drink)

Origin: Gulf · Arabian Peninsula

This is the purple berry-flavoured cordial diluted with cold water and poured over ice, a fixture on Gulf iftar tables during Ramadan. It is sweet and fruity, with grape, raspberry and blackcurrant notes, and is drunk by the jug after breaking the fast with dates and water. Families often mix it a little stronger or lighter to taste.

What it is made of

vimto cordialwaterice

Recipe

4 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 200 ml berry cordial concentrate
  • 800 ml cold water
  • Ice cubes
  • Fresh mint leaves (optional)

Method

  1. Pour the cordial into a large jug.
  2. Add the cold water and stir well.
  3. Serve over ice, garnished with mint if you like.

How to make it lighter

  • Dilute more: 1 part cordial to 6–7 parts water still tastes fruity with less sugar.
  • Drink it alongside plain water at iftar rather than as the only drink.
  • Use a smaller glass of about 200 ml per serving.

History

The cordial was first made in England in the early twentieth century; it became so linked with Ramadan in Saudi Arabia, the Gulf states and Yemen that a large share of its yearly sales there is said to happen during the holy month.
