🐟 Steamed grouper

Steamed grouper is a prized seafood dish: a whole grouper or thick fillets steamed with ginger, then finished with scallion, fresh cilantro, soy sauce and hot oil with sesame. Grouper has firm, white, flaky flesh with a mild sweet taste that suits this gentle method. It is often chosen for banquets and served with rice.

Recipe

3 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g grouper (whole, cleaned, or thick fillets)
  • 25 g fresh ginger, sliced and shredded
  • 3 scallions, shredded
  • 1 small bunch cilantro
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Method

  1. Place the fish on ginger slices on a heatproof plate.
  2. Steam for 10–14 minutes depending on thickness, until the flesh is opaque.
  3. Pour off the liquid and top with shredded ginger, scallion and cilantro.
  4. Pour the soy sauce mixed with water around the fish.
  5. Heat the oils until smoking, pour over the herbs and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only a teaspoon of sesame oil: 1 tsp ≈ 40 kcal.
  • Use a light hand with the soy sauce to keep salt down.
  • Serve with steamed greens and a small portion of plain rice.

History

Grouper is one of the most valued fish in Cantonese, Hong Kong and Southeast Asian seafood restaurants, where it is typically chosen live and steamed to order.
