🥒 Punjene Tikvice (Stuffed Zucchini)

Origin: Balkans · Southeast Europe

Punjene tikvice are zucchini hollowed out and stuffed with ground beef, rice and parsley, then baked in a light tomato sauce. The zucchini becomes soft and mild, soaking up the tomato, while the filling stays savoury and herby. The dish is a summer favourite in Balkan home cooking, served hot with its sauce.

Recipe

4 servings80 min

Ingredients

  • 4 medium zucchini
  • 400 g ground beef
  • 80 g rice, rinsed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Halve the zucchini lengthways and scoop out the centres, chopping the flesh.
  2. Mix the beef, rice, onion, parsley, salt and pepper.
  3. Fill the zucchini halves and place them in a baking dish.
  4. Mix the tomatoes with the chopped zucchini flesh, oil and 200 ml water and pour around.
  5. Cover and bake at 180 °C for 50–60 minutes until the rice is tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean ground beef to cut the fat.
  • Add more parsley and zucchini flesh to the filling and less rice.
  • Serve with a cucumber or tomato salad on the side.

History

Stuffed zucchini belongs to the wide family of stuffed vegetables shared across the Balkans, Turkey and the Middle East. It is a common seasonal dish in Bosnian, Serbian and Croatian homes when zucchini is plentiful.
