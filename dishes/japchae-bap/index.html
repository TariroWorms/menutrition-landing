Japchae-bap (japchae over rice)

Japchae-bap is the classic glass noodle stir-fry placed over a bowl of rice. The sweet potato noodles are chewy and glossy, seasoned with soy sauce and sesame oil. It is a filling one-bowl meal.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g sweet potato noodles
  • 300 g cooked rice (2 bowls)
  • 100 g beef strips
  • 1 carrot
  • 100 g spinach
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce and 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil and sesame seeds

Method

  1. Boil the noodles until soft, drain and cut.
  2. Stir-fry carrot and beef separately.
  3. Blanch the spinach.
  4. Toss everything with soy sauce, sugar and sesame oil.
  5. Spoon over hot rice and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and sugar.
  • Add mushrooms, onion and bell pepper to raise the vegetable share.
  • Choose brown rice for more fiber.

History

Japchae is a Korean festive dish, and serving it over rice turns it into an everyday lunch. It is popular in casual restaurants and at home.
