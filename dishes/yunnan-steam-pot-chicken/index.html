Yunnan steam pot chicken (qiguo ji)

Origin: China · Yunnan

Pieces of chicken are packed in a clay pot with a central chimney, which sits over boiling water. The steam rises through the chimney and condenses, giving a clear, rich broth without any added water. The meat is very tender and mild, flavoured with ginger and herbs.

What it is made of

chickengingerscallionsshiitake mushroomsGoji berriessalt

Recipe

3 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken, in pieces
  • 20 g ginger
  • 4 dried shiitake
  • 10 g goji berries
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Blanch the chicken pieces in boiling water for 2 minutes and rinse.
  2. Place chicken, ginger, mushrooms and salt in the pot.
  3. Set the pot over a larger pan of boiling water.
  4. Steam for 2.5–3 hours, topping up the water below.
  5. Add goji and scallion for the last 10 minutes.
  6. Serve the chicken in its broth.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim the fat from the broth before serving.
  • Remove the skin for a leaner dish.
  • Add more mushrooms for fibre.

History

The qiguo pot is made in Jianshui in Yunnan and the dish dates from the Qing dynasty according to local tradition. It is often cooked with medicinal herbs such as sanqi (notoginseng).
