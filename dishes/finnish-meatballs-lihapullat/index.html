Lihapullat (Finnish meatballs)

Origin: Finland

Lihapullat are small meatballs of beef and pork mixed with onion, milk-soaked crumbs and a little allspice. They are browned and finished in a cream gravy. Served with potatoes and lingonberry jam, they are a classic Finnish home meal.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g ground beef
  • 250 g ground pork
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 50 g breadcrumbs in 100 ml milk
  • 200 ml cream
  • 600 g potatoes

Method

  1. Mix both meats with onion, soaked crumbs, salt, pepper and a pinch of allspice.
  2. Shape into walnut-sized balls.
  3. Brown them in a pan with a little butter for about 8 minutes.
  4. Pour in the cream and simmer gently for 8 minutes.
  5. Serve with boiled potatoes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef only and replace part of the cream with milk.
  • Bake the meatballs instead of frying them.
  • Add a cucumber or green salad on the side.

History

Meatballs spread across the Nordic countries over many generations, and each country has its own version. In Finland they are a staple of school lunches and family dinners.
