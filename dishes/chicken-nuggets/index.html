Chicken nuggets are bite-sized pieces of chicken coated in seasoned flour and breadcrumbs and deep-fried until golden. The coating is crisp and the inside soft and juicy, which makes them easy to eat with the fingers. They are a children's favourite and a fast-food staple, usually served with fries and a dipping sauce.

What it is made of

chickenbreadcrumbsflourspices

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 60 g flour
  • 80 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • Salt and pepper
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Mix the flour with the spices and whisk half of it with about 80 ml water into a thin batter.
  3. Dust the chicken in the dry flour, dip it in the batter, then roll it in the breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in hot oil at 175 °C for 4–5 minutes until golden and cooked through, then drain on paper.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake at 220 °C for 15–18 minutes or air-fry instead of deep-frying.
  • Count the pieces: a portion of 6 is very different from 20.
  • Choose a lighter dip such as salsa instead of creamy sauces.

History

The chicken nugget is usually credited to Robert C. Baker, a food scientist at Cornell University, who developed a method for forming and breading chicken pieces in the mid-twentieth century. Fast-food chains made nuggets a mass-market product from the early 1980s.
