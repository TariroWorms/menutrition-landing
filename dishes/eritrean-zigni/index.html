Zigni (Eritrean spicy beef stew)

Origin: Eritrea

Zigni is the Eritrean beef stew, made with plenty of onion cooked down with berbere, tomato paste and garlic. The sauce turns dark red and thick, with a heat that builds slowly. It is served on a large flatbread called injera or taita, often with a hard-boiled egg.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef
  • 400 g onion
  • 3 tbsp berbere spice mix
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 pcs garlic
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300 ml water
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Cook the finely chopped onion in a dry pot for 15 minutes.
  2. Add oil, garlic and berbere and cook 8 minutes.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste.
  4. Add the beef and water, cover and simmer 60 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and serve over injera.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef and skim the oil from the surface.
  • Balance with a side of greens.
  • Reduce berbere for a milder version.

History

Zigni is a staple of festive meals in Eritrea and is closely related to Ethiopian key wat. It is commonly served with a flatbread of fermented dough.
