Chanakhi (lamb and vegetables in clay pots)

Origin: Georgia

Chanakhi is a thick stew of lamb pieces, eggplant, potatoes, tomatoes, onion and peppers layered in individual clay pots with garlic and plenty of fresh herbs. Baked slowly, the vegetables melt into the meat juices and the lamb becomes very tender. It is served straight from the pot, often with bread to dip into the rich, tomato-scented broth.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g lamb shoulder
  • 2 eggplants (500 g)
  • 4 potatoes (500 g)
  • 5 tomatoes (600 g)
  • 2 onions (200 g)
  • 2 bell peppers (250 g)
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • a bunch of cilantro, basil and parsley (60 g)

Method

  1. Cut the lamb into chunks, season and place a few pieces in each clay pot with a little lamb fat or oil.
  2. Layer sliced onion, cubed potatoes and eggplant on top.
  3. Add chopped tomatoes, peppers, garlic and half the herbs, seasoning each layer.
  4. Pour in a little water, cover and bake at 180 °C for about 2 hours.
  5. Stir in the remaining herbs and serve in the pots.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the lamb before cooking.
  • Add extra vegetables and use less meat per pot.
  • Serve with a salad instead of a second piece of bread.

History

Chanakhi is especially associated with eastern Georgia, where clay pots called kotani are widely used for slow cooking. Similar lamb and vegetable pot stews exist across the Caucasus.
