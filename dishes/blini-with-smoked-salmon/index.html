🥞 Blini with smoked salmon

Blini with smoked salmon pairs small, soft buckwheat pancakes with silky slices of smoked salmon, a dollop of sour cream, fresh dill and a squeeze of lemon. The result is smoky, tangy and fresh at once. It is a popular party canapé and brunch dish, served warm or at room temperature.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 16 small buckwheat blini (made with milk, eggs and butter)
  • 200 g smoked salmon, sliced
  • 120 g sour cream
  • 1 lemon
  • A small bunch of dill, black pepper

Method

  1. Warm the blini briefly in a low oven or a dry pan.
  2. Mix the sour cream with a little chopped dill, a squeeze of lemon and black pepper.
  3. Spread a teaspoon of the sour cream on each blin.
  4. Top with a folded strip of smoked salmon.
  5. Finish with a dill sprig and a small lemon wedge and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light sour cream, or mix it half and half with lemon juice and herbs to stretch it.
  • Smoked salmon is protein-rich but salty — one strip per blin is plenty.
  • Fill out the plate with cucumber slices or a green salad rather than extra blini.

History

Blini are a centuries-old Russian dish, traditionally eaten with fish, roe and sour cream. Topping them with smoked salmon is a widespread modern take, now found on brunch menus and party tables far beyond Russia.
