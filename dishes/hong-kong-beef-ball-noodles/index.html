Hong Kong beef ball noodles

Origin: Hong Kong

Beef balls with a bouncy, almost springy bite float in a clear beef broth over thin egg or rice noodles. The balls are seasoned with white pepper and often served with sliced scallions. It is a light, quick bowl found at noodle shops all over the city.

What it is made of

beefegg noodlesbrothscallionswhite pepper

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g egg noodles
  • 8 beef balls
  • 600 ml beef broth
  • 2 scallions
  • white pepper, salt

Method

  1. Bring the broth to a simmer.
  2. Boil the noodles for 2 minutes and drain.
  3. Warm the beef balls in the broth for 4 minutes.
  4. Place the noodles in bowls and add balls and broth.
  5. Top with scallions and pepper.

How to make it lighter

  • Drink only half of the broth to cut sodium.
  • Add leafy greens.
  • Choose lean beef balls with little starch.

History

Beef balls are made by pounding lean beef into a paste until it turns elastic, a Cantonese technique also used for fish balls. The bowl is a regular at Hong Kong noodle shops.
