Balandėliai (Lithuanian cabbage rolls)

Origin: Lithuania

Balandėliai, meaning little doves, are cabbage leaves wrapped around a filling of minced pork, rice and onion and braised in a light tomato sauce. The leaves turn silky and sweet and the filling stays juicy. They are served hot with boiled potatoes and a spoon of sour cream.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 head of cabbage (1 kg)
  • 400 g minced pork
  • 100 g rice, cooked
  • 1 onion (120 g), chopped
  • 1 egg (60 g)
  • 300 ml tomato sauce
  • 100 g sour cream, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Blanch the cabbage and separate the leaves, trimming thick ribs.
  2. Mix the meat with rice, onion, egg, salt and pepper.
  3. Place a spoon of filling on each leaf and roll tightly.
  4. Pack seam side down in a pot, cover with tomato sauce and a little water.
  5. Simmer covered for 50-60 minutes and serve with sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean turkey or chicken for the filling.
  • Use more rice and cabbage than meat for fewer calories.
  • Serve with yogurt instead of sour cream.

History

Stuffed cabbage is eaten across Eastern Europe, and the Lithuanian name refers to the small dove-like shape of the rolls. It is typical home cooking, often made in large batches for the family.
