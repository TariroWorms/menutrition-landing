Ikan pari bakar (grilled stingray with sambal)

Origin: Malaysia

Ikan pari bakar is a wing of stingray smeared with spicy sambal, wrapped in banana leaf and grilled over charcoal. The flesh is tender and sweet, the sambal hot and tangy, and the leaf adds a smoky aroma. It is a hawker-centre favourite served with rice and lime.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g stingray wing
  • 4 tbsp sambal (chili paste)
  • 1 banana leaf
  • 1 tsp turmeric, salt
  • 2 limes

Method

  1. Rub the fish with salt and turmeric.
  2. Spread sambal on both sides.
  3. Wrap in softened banana leaf.
  4. Grill over medium heat 6-8 minutes per side.
  5. Serve with lime wedges and rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a ready-made or home-made sambal with little oil rather than frying it in extra oil.
  • Squeeze lime generously instead of adding sugar.
  • Pair with cucumber and herbs.

History

Grilled stingray is popular on the Malaysian and Singaporean coasts, where it is sold at night markets and seafood stalls. Banana leaf wrapping keeps the delicate fish moist.
