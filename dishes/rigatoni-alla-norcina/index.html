🍝 Rigatoni alla Norcina

Origin: Italy · Umbria (Norcia)

Rigatoni alla norcina is a rich Umbrian pasta of crumbled sausage simmered in cream, finished with grated pecorino and, in its most festive form, shaved or grated black truffle. The ridged tubes trap the creamy, earthy sauce, making every bite savoury and fragrant. It is served hot, straight from the pan, as a filling first course.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g rigatoni
  • 250 g sausage, casing removed
  • 150 ml cooking cream
  • 15 g black truffle, grated (or a little truffle paste)
  • 40 g pecorino, grated
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Boil the rigatoni in well-salted water until al dente.
  2. Meanwhile, crumble the sausage into a pan with the oil and brown it for 6–8 minutes.
  3. Pour in the cream, add half the truffle and simmer for 3 minutes.
  4. Drain the pasta, keeping a little cooking water, and toss it in the sauce with the pecorino, loosening with water if needed.
  5. Serve topped with the remaining truffle and black pepper.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 100 ml cream and a splash of pasta water instead of more cream; the sauce stays silky with less fat.
  • Halve the sausage and keep the full truffle aroma — the flavour comes mostly from the truffle.
  • Serve a 250 g portion with a green salad to make a more balanced plate.

History

The dish is named after Norcia, a town in Umbria famous for its butchers and its black truffles. Versions vary across the region, and some cooks leave out the cream or the truffle, but sausage, cheese and truffle remain the signature trio.
