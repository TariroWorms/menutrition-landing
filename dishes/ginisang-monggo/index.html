Ginisang monggo (sautéed mung bean stew)

Origin: Philippines

Ginisang monggo is a thick, comforting stew of split mung beans cooked with garlic, onion and tomato, finished with sautéed pork and greens such as spinach or malunggay. It tastes earthy and savory, with a fish sauce kick. It is eaten with rice, traditionally on Fridays.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g mung beans
  • 150 g pork, diced
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 100 g spinach
  • 1 l water

Method

  1. Boil the beans in water 30 minutes until soft.
  2. Sauté the pork until browned, then add the garlic, onion and tomato and cook until soft.
  3. Add to beans with fish sauce and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Stir in spinach and cook 2 minutes.
  5. Season and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the pork and swap the fish sauce for soy sauce for a vegan version.
  • Use less fish sauce to cut salt.
  • Add extra greens for fiber.

History

Mung beans are a long-established legume in Southeast Asia and an everyday protein in Filipino homes. The dish is cheap, filling and often made meatless on days of abstinence.
