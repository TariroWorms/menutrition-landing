Paçe koke (Albanian lamb head soup)

Origin: Albania

Paçe koke is a traditional soup made by long-simmering a lamb head until the meat and gelatin soften into a rich broth. It is served hot with a sharp mix of crushed garlic and vinegar stirred in at the table. The taste is deep and meaty, balanced by the sour garlic.

What it is made of

lamb headgarlicvinegaronionbroth

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lamb head, cleaned and split
  • 1 onion
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 6 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 l water
  • salt

Method

  1. Soak the head in cold water for 2 hours.
  2. Cover with fresh water, bring to a boil and skim.
  3. Add onion, bay and salt and simmer 2.5 hours.
  4. Lift out the head and pick the meat from the bones.
  5. Return the meat to the strained broth and reheat.
  6. Mix garlic with vinegar and stir in a spoonful per bowl; dust with paprika.

How to make it lighter

  • Chill the broth and remove the fat from the top.
  • Pick only lean meat and discard fatty skin.
  • Add the garlic-vinegar mix at the table to control salt and acidity.

History

Offal and head soups are a long Balkan tradition, built on using the whole animal. In Albania paçe is eaten in the early morning or after a night out and is a remedy for a tired body.
