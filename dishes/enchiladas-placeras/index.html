Enchiladas placeras (Michoacán plaza enchiladas)

Origin: Mexico · Michoacán

Enchiladas placeras are the classic evening dish of the plazas of Michoacán. Corn tortillas are dipped in a guajillo chile sauce, briefly fried and folded with cheese and onion, then served beside pieces of chicken and diced potato and carrot browned in the same red fat. Shredded cabbage, pickled chiles and crumbled cheese finish the plate.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 4 chicken pieces, poached
  • 3 potatoes, diced and boiled
  • 3 carrots, diced and boiled
  • 5 guajillo chiles
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 100 g queso fresco, plus 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1/4 cabbage, shredded
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Pickled chiles

Method

  1. Soak and blend the guajillos with garlic, salt and a little water; strain.
  2. Dip each tortilla in the sauce and fry briefly on both sides in a little oil.
  3. Fill with cheese and onion and fold in half.
  4. In the same pan, brown the chicken, potatoes and carrots with a spoonful of sauce.
  5. Serve the enchiladas with the chicken and vegetables, cabbage, cheese and pickled chiles.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan with a teaspoon of oil per batch.
  • Choose skinless chicken breast.
  • Double the cabbage and carrots for more fibre.

History

The name comes from the "plaza", the town square where cooks set up stalls at night, especially in Morelia and Pátzcuaro. The chicken is often called pollo placero, and the whole plate is a fixture of the region's street food.
