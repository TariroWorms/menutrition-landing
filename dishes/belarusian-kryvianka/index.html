Kryvianka (Belarusian blood sausage)

Origin: Belarus
420kcal

Kryvianka is a dark, soft sausage made from fresh pig blood mixed with cooked buckwheat, fried back fat and onion. It is baked until set, with a slightly crisp skin and a rich, earthy flavour. It is eaten hot, sliced and fried, with sauerkraut or lingonberry sauce.

What it is made of

pork bloodBuckwheatPork back fatonionblack pepper

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1 litre fresh pork blood
  • 300 g buckwheat groats
  • 250 g pork back fat
  • 2 onions (160 g)
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried marjoram
  • salt
  • 2 m pork casings

Method

  1. Cook the buckwheat until almost tender and cool.
  2. Dice the fat and fry it with chopped onion until golden.
  3. Strain the blood and mix with buckwheat, fat, onion, pepper, marjoram and salt.
  4. Fill the casings loosely, tie the ends and prick in a few places.
  5. Bake on a greased tray at 170 °C for about 50–60 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Blood is rich in iron; a small portion goes a long way.
  • Use less back fat and more onion for a lighter version.
  • Serve with sauerkraut or a fresh salad.

History

Blood sausage was made in Belarusian villages on the day of the pig slaughter so that nothing went to waste. Buckwheat, a grain grown widely in Belarus, gives the local version its typical texture.
