Pork stewed with vermicelli (zhurou dun fentiao)

Origin: China · Dongbei
455kcal

This stew simmers pork belly with napa cabbage and chewy sweet potato noodles in a soy-based broth. The noodles soak up the pork fat and become silky and savory. It is a warming winter dish eaten with rice or steamed buns.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g pork belly
  • 300 g napa cabbage
  • 100 g dried sweet potato noodles
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 piece of ginger and 2 scallions
  • 600 ml water

Method

  1. Soak the noodles in warm water for 30 minutes.
  2. Cut the pork into slices and sear in a dry pot until the fat renders.
  3. Add ginger, scallion and soy sauce and stir for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the water, add the cabbage and simmer for 25 minutes.
  5. Add the drained noodles and cook for 8 minutes until soft.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose a leaner cut of pork and skim off the fat.
  • Add more cabbage and less noodles.
  • Use less soy sauce to reduce the salt.

History

Cabbage and sweet potato starch noodles are winter staples in northeast China, where fresh vegetables were once scarce. Stewing everything in one pot was a practical way to feed a family on cold days.
