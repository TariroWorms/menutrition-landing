Kilishi (Nigerian spiced beef jerky)

Origin: Nigeria · North

Kilishi is a thin, dry, chewy jerky made from beef that is sun-dried, brushed with a spicy peanut paste and roasted again. It is intensely savoury and hot, with a nutty coating. People eat it as a protein-rich snack, tear it into pieces for a meal, or carry it on long trips.

Recipe

6 servings240 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lean beef
  • 100 g roasted peanuts, ground
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ginger powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp water or oil

Method

  1. Slice the beef very thin along the grain and trim the fat.
  2. Dry the slices in a low oven at 70 C or in the sun until firm.
  3. Mix peanuts, chili, ginger, garlic, paprika and salt with water into a paste.
  4. Brush both sides of the dried meat with the paste.
  5. Dry again for an hour, then roast briefly over charcoal or under a grill.
  6. Cool and store in a dry container.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim all fat before drying to keep it lean and long-lasting.
  • Use less salt for a lighter-sodium snack.
  • Eat small portions as a high-protein snack.

History

Kilishi comes from the Hausa of northern Nigeria and neighbouring Sahel areas, where drying meat was a practical way to keep it. It is now sold across the country in packs and is a popular gift.
