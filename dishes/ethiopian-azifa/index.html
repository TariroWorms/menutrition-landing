Azifa (Ethiopian lentil salad)

Azifa is a refreshing salad of cooked brown or green lentils tossed with red onion, chilli, mustard and lemon juice. It is sharp, tangy and slightly spicy, and served cold. It is a common companion to the injera platter on fasting days.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g brown lentils
  • 1 pcs onion
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 3 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 pcs jalapeño
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 pinch salt

Method

  1. Boil the lentils in water for 20-25 minutes until tender but whole.
  2. Drain and cool completely.
  3. Finely chop the onion and jalapeño.
  4. Whisk the mustard with lemon juice, oil and salt.
  5. Toss everything together and chill before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil for a lighter salad.
  • Add chopped tomato and herbs for more vegetables.
  • Great as a high-fiber protein source.

History

This salad is typical of fasting-day meals, when legumes replace meat. Its sharpness balances the richness of stews.
