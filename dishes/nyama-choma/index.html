Nyama choma (Kenyan grilled meat)

Origin: Kenya

Nyama choma means "burnt meat" in Swahili: chunks of goat or beef grilled slowly over charcoal with only salt. The outside is smoky and browned while the inside stays juicy. It is cut up on a board and eaten with ugali and kachumbari.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg goat or beef ribs and shoulder
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 lemon
  • kachumbari to serve

Method

  1. Cut the meat into large pieces and rub with salt and oil.
  2. Let it rest 30 minutes.
  3. Light the charcoal and let it burn to glowing embers.
  4. Grill slowly, turning often, for 40-50 minutes.
  5. Rest, chop and serve with lemon and kachumbari.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean cuts and trim the visible fat.
  • Serve with plenty of salad.
  • Do not char heavily to limit burnt crust.

History

Grilled meat is the centrepiece of social gatherings in Kenya, with roadside and neighbourhood spots serving it all day. It is the national favourite for celebrations and weekends.
