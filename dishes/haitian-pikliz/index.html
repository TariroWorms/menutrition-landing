Pikliz (Haitian pickled slaw)

Origin: Haiti

Pikliz is the condiment that sits on almost every Haitian table. Shredded cabbage, carrot, onion and scotch bonnet peppers are packed in a jar with vinegar and lime juice and left to mature for a few days. It is sharp, crunchy and very hot, and is spooned over fried meat, fish and plantains to cut through richness.

What it is made of

cabbagecarrotscotch bonnet peppervinegarlimeonion

Recipe

8 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g white cabbage, shredded
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 scotch bonnet peppers, sliced
  • 250 ml white vinegar
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Mix the cabbage, carrot, onion and peppers in a bowl.
  2. Add the salt and press lightly with your hands.
  3. Pack the vegetables into a clean jar with the cloves.
  4. Pour over the vinegar and lime juice until everything is covered.
  5. Seal and refrigerate for at least 2 days.
  6. Serve cold as a condiment.

How to make it lighter

  • It adds almost no calories, so use it freely instead of heavy sauces.
  • Deseed the peppers for milder heat.
  • Use a little less salt if you watch your sodium.

History

Pikliz is a staple of Haitian street food and home cooking, served beside griot, fried fish and fritters. Every family has its own balance of heat and acidity.
