Unash (Turkmen noodle and bean soup)

Origin: Turkmenistan

Unash is a hearty Turkmen soup of homemade flour noodles cooked with beans in a light tomato and onion base. Its name comes from the word for flour. It is often served with a spoon of sour milk or yogurt stirred in, giving it a pleasant tang, and it is especially popular in cold weather.

Recipe

4 servings80 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 100 ml water
  • 150 g dried beans, soaked
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, grated
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1.5 l water
  • yogurt or sour milk to serve
  • salt, pepper, dried mint

Method

  1. Boil the soaked beans in water until tender, about 1 hour.
  2. Fry onion in oil, add tomato and cook 5 minutes, then add to the beans.
  3. Knead flour, water and salt into a firm dough, roll thin and cut into thin strips.
  4. Add the noodles to the soup and cook 8 minutes.
  5. Season and serve with a spoon of yogurt and a pinch of dried mint.

How to make it lighter

  • Use wholemeal flour for the noodles.
  • Choose low-fat yogurt for the topping.
  • Add spinach or carrot for extra vegetables.

History

Noodle soups like unash are part of a wider Central Asian tradition of hand-made pasta. In Turkmenistan it is a thrifty home dish that can be made with or without meat.
