🍰 Tarte Tropézienne

Origin: France · Saint-Tropez

The Tarte Tropézienne is a large round of soft brioche, topped with pearl sugar, split and filled with a thick, creamy filling based on pastry cream and scented with orange blossom water. It is fluffy, sweet and fragrant, and is served chilled in wedges.

Recipe

8 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 round brioche, about 500 g
  • 500 ml milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 100 g sugar
  • 40 g cornflour
  • 50 g butter
  • 1–2 tbsp orange blossom water
  • Pearl sugar to decorate

Method

  1. Heat the milk; whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour together.
  2. Pour the hot milk over the yolks, return to the pan and stir until thick.
  3. Off the heat, beat in the butter and orange blossom water; cover and chill.
  4. Slice the brioche horizontally and spread the chilled cream over the bottom half.
  5. Replace the top, sprinkle with pearl sugar and chill for 1 hour before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Make the cream with semi-skimmed milk and halve the butter.
  • Spread a thinner layer of cream.
  • Cut 10 wedges instead of 8.

History

The cake was created in Saint-Tropez in the 1950s by a pastry chef of Polish origin, and the story goes that its name was suggested by actress Brigitte Bardot during a film shoot in the town. It has since become an emblem of the Côte d’Azur, sold in bakeries across the south of France.
