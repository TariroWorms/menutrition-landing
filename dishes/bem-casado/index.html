Bem-casado (Brazilian wedding cookie)

Origin: Brazil

Bem-casado consists of two small, very soft sponge rounds sandwiched with doce de leite and coated with a thin sugar glaze. Each one is wrapped in paper or fabric and tied with ribbon. Guests receive them at weddings as a wish for a sweet, happy marriage.

What it is made of

floureggsugardoce de leitecornstarch

Recipe

20 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 120 g sugar
  • 100 g flour
  • 40 g cornstarch
  • 300 g doce de leite
  • Glaze: 200 g icing sugar, 40 ml water

Method

  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, then beat in the sugar and yolks.
  2. Fold in sifted flour and cornstarch.
  3. Pipe small discs onto lined trays and bake at 180 °C for 8-10 minutes.
  4. Cool and sandwich pairs with doce de leite.
  5. Dip in the sugar glaze and let dry.
  6. Wrap each one individually.

How to make it lighter

  • One bem-casado is a portion; they are small but sugary.
  • Skip the glaze and dust lightly with icing sugar.
  • Use a thin layer of doce de leite.

History

The name means 'well married', since two halves are joined into one. The sweet is linked to Portuguese confectionery traditions and became a customary wedding favour in Brazil, now also sold year-round.
