🍗 Chicken bulgogi (dak bulgogi)

Origin: South Korea

Dak bulgogi is chicken thigh marinated in soy sauce, garlic, ginger, a little sugar and sesame oil, then grilled or pan-seared until caramelised. It is sweet-savoury and juicy, with charred edges and fresh scallion on top. It is served with rice and side dishes, or wrapped in lettuce leaves.

Recipe

3 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken thighs, sliced
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 scallions, sliced

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, sugar, garlic, ginger and sesame oil.
  2. Toss the chicken and onion in the marinade and leave for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Cook in a very hot pan or on a grill for 8–10 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Scatter with scallions and serve with rice or lettuce leaves.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken breast instead of thigh for a leaner version.
  • Halve the sugar; the onion adds natural sweetness.
  • Wrap it in lettuce leaves instead of eating it with a large bowl of rice.

History

Bulgogi, meaning “fire meat”, traditionally refers to marinated beef grilled over fire in Korea. Chicken versions using a similar marinade are now common in Korean homes and restaurants.
