🥨 Trdelník

Origin: Czech Republic · Slovakia

Trdelník is a sweet pastry made by wrapping strips of yeast dough around a thick spit, rolling it in sugar, walnuts and cinnamon, and grilling it over an open heat until caramelised. It comes off the spit as a hollow, crisp-shelled tube with a soft inside. It is sold hot from street stalls in Central European old towns.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 7 g dried yeast
  • 200 ml warm water
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 50 g sugar for the dough
  • pinch of salt
  • 80 g sugar for coating
  • 60 g chopped walnuts
  • 2 tsp cinnamon

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water and oil into a smooth dough; let it rise 1 hour.
  2. Roll into long strips about 2 cm wide and wind them around foil-wrapped rolling pins or metal tubes brushed with oil.
  3. Mix the coating sugar, walnuts and cinnamon and roll the wrapped dough in it.
  4. Bake at 200 °C for 15–20 minutes, turning, until golden and caramelised.
  5. Slide off the tubes and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Share one trdelník: a full tube is a large portion.
  • Use half the coating sugar; cinnamon and walnuts keep the flavour.
  • Eat it plain rather than filled with extra sweet fillings.

History

Similar spit cakes are known in several Central European countries, and trdelník is traditionally linked to the Slovak town of Skalica. It became a famous street snack for visitors in Prague and other Czech cities in the 21st century.
