Nan-e nokhodchi (Persian chickpea cookies)

Origin: Iran

Nan-e nokhodchi are small, delicate cookies made from roasted chickpea flour, sugar and oil, often shaped like clovers or flowers. They are fragrant with cardamom and crumble gently in the mouth. They are a classic treat for Nowruz, the Persian new year.

Recipe

20 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g chickpea flour
  • 100 g powdered sugar
  • 100 ml oil or melted butter
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • chopped pistachios for topping

Method

  1. Lightly toast the chickpea flour in a dry pan for a few minutes.
  2. Mix with sugar and cardamom.
  3. Add the oil and knead to a soft dough.
  4. Press into small shapes and place on a tray.
  5. Bake at 150 C for 20 minutes, cool, then top with pistachios.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar and keep cookies small.
  • Choose heart-healthy oil.
  • Eat one or two with tea instead of more sweets.

History

Chickpea flour is an old Iranian pastry ingredient, and these cookies are among the typical sweets prepared for Nowruz. They are naturally free of wheat.
