Curtido (Salvadoran pickled cabbage slaw)

Origin: El Salvador

Curtido is a crunchy, tangy cabbage relish seasoned with oregano and sometimes a little chili. It is lightly soured with vinegar or a short ferment, giving it a bright acidity. It is the standard side for pupusas, cutting through their richness.

What it is made of

cabbagecarrotonionoregano

Recipe

6 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 head cabbage, shredded (400 g)
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1/2 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 200 ml hot water
  • 100 ml white vinegar
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Pour hot water over the cabbage, carrot and onion and let stand 5 minutes, then drain.
  2. Mix vinegar, oregano and salt.
  3. Toss the vegetables with the dressing.
  4. Pack into a jar and press down.
  5. Rest at room temperature for several hours or refrigerate overnight.
  6. Serve cold with pupusas.

How to make it lighter

  • Naturally low in calories, it is a great topping for heavy dishes.
  • Reduce the salt if you are watching sodium.
  • Add a pinch of chili for a spicier version.

History

Curtido is a staple of Salvadoran tables and comes with almost every pupusa order. Similar pickled slaws appear across Central America under local names.
