🍄 Veal Escalope with Mushroom Cream Sauce

This is a quick French classic: thin veal escalopes are seared, then the pan is deglazed with white wine and turned into a silky sauce with sliced mushrooms and cream. The veal stays tender, while the sauce is rich, savoury and slightly tangy. It is usually served with rice, potatoes or green vegetables.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 veal escalopes (about 150 g each)
  • 200 g button mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 shallot or small onion, finely chopped
  • 80 ml dry white wine
  • 100 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Season the veal and sear it in hot oil for 1–2 minutes per side; set aside on a warm plate.
  2. In the same pan, fry the onion and mushrooms for 5–6 minutes until golden and dry.
  3. Pour in the wine and let it bubble for 2 minutes, scraping up the browned bits.
  4. Add the cream and simmer for 3–4 minutes until the sauce coats a spoon.
  5. Return the veal and its juices to the pan, warm through for 1 minute, sprinkle with parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream and add a splash of water — the sauce is still glossy with fewer calories.
  • Double the mushrooms: they add volume for very few calories.
  • Serve with steamed vegetables instead of buttery mash or fries.

History

Veal in a cream and mushroom sauce belongs to the tradition of French sauce cooking, where pan juices are deglazed with wine and enriched with cream. Similar dishes appear across France and Switzerland under several regional names.
