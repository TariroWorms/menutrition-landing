Tavë dheu (Tirana clay pot bake)

Origin: Albania · Tirana

Tavë dheu is a baked dish from Tirana made in an earthenware pot with pieces of lamb and liver, onions and tomatoes, finished with a yogurt and cheese topping. It is rich, savory and slightly tangy. It comes to the table bubbling in its pot with fresh bread.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g lamb, cubed
  • 200 g lamb liver, cubed
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 150 g yogurt
  • 1 egg
  • 80 g grated cheese
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • salt, pepper, oregano

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in butter, then add the onions and cook until soft.
  2. Add the liver and tomatoes, season and cook 10 minutes.
  3. Transfer to a clay pot or baking dish and bake at 190 °C for 30 minutes.
  4. Whisk yogurt with the egg and half the cheese.
  5. Pour it over the meat and sprinkle the rest of the cheese.
  6. Bake another 20 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less cheese and low-fat yogurt for the topping.
  • Serve with a large green salad instead of bread.
  • Trim visible fat from the lamb before cooking.

History

Baking in clay pots is an old method in Albanian homes and cheap inns. The name simply means clay dish, and the Tirana version with an egg and yogurt crust is the best known.
