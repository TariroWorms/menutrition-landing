Găluște cu prune (Moldovan plum dumplings)

Origin: Moldova

Găluște cu prune are soft potato-dough dumplings wrapped around a whole plum and sometimes a sugar cube, boiled and then rolled in breadcrumbs toasted in butter and sugar. The outside is tender and mildly salty, while the inside is warm, juicy and sweet-sour. They are eaten as a dessert or a filling main in late summer when plums are in season.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g boiled potatoes, mashed
  • 150 g flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 12 small plums, pitted
  • 12 sugar cubes or 2 tbsp sugar
  • 80 g breadcrumbs
  • 50 g butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar

Method

  1. Mix the warm mashed potato with flour, egg and salt into a soft dough.
  2. Divide into 12 pieces and flatten each.
  3. Replace the plum stones with a sugar cube and wrap the dough around each plum.
  4. Boil in salted water for 10 minutes after they float.
  5. Toast the breadcrumbs in the butter with the sugar.
  6. Roll the drained dumplings in the crumbs.
  7. Serve warm, with a little cinnamon if you like.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter and sugar in the crumb coating.
  • Use ripe plums so no extra sugar is needed inside.
  • Eat 2 or 3 as a dessert rather than a full plate.

History

Plum dumplings are known throughout Central and Eastern Europe and the Balkans, wherever potatoes and plum orchards meet. In Moldova they belong to late-summer home cooking.
