Firfir (Ethiopian shredded injera)

Origin: Ethiopia

Firfir is a satisfying dish that turns leftover injera into a spicy hash. Torn pieces soak up a sauce of onion, berbere and niter kibbeh until soft and flavorful. It is a popular breakfast or brunch dish, often with a fried egg on the side.

Recipe

3 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g injera
  • 1 pcs onion
  • 2 tbsp berbere spice mix
  • 2 tbsp niter kibbeh (spiced butter)
  • 150 ml water
  • 1 pcs tomato

Method

  1. Tear the injera into bite-size pieces.
  2. Cook chopped onion in a dry pan, then add niter kibbeh.
  3. Stir in berbere and the diced tomato.
  4. Add water and simmer 5 minutes to make a sauce.
  5. Fold in the injera until coated and warm, 3 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less spiced butter for a lighter dish.
  • Add a boiled egg for protein.
  • Make it with day-old injera for the best texture.

History

Firfir comes from the habit of not wasting injera, which hardens within a day. It exists in many regional versions, with or without meat, and has a fasting variety made with oil.
