🥛 Mint laban with cucumber

Origin: Middle East

Mint laban is a chilled salted yogurt drink blended with fresh mint and cucumber until smooth and pale green. The mint gives a cool, fresh aroma and the cucumber makes it lighter and more refreshing. It is drunk very cold with grilled meats, sandwiches and rice dishes, or on its own on a hot day.

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g plain yogurt
  • 200 ml cold water
  • ½ cucumber, peeled and chopped
  • A small handful of fresh mint leaves
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • Ice cubes

Method

  1. Put the yogurt, water, cucumber, mint and salt in a blender.
  2. Blend until completely smooth.
  3. Pour over ice and garnish with a mint leaf.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt and more cucumber for a lighter drink.
  • Add extra water and ice to stretch one glass further.
  • It works as a light snack between meals instead of a sweet drink.

History

Yogurt drinks flavoured with mint are common across the Middle East, Turkey and Iran, and ready-made mint laban is sold widely in Gulf supermarkets.
