Hernekeitto (pea soup with pancake)

Origin: Finland

Hernekeitto is a thick soup of dried yellow peas simmered with pork, onion and carrot. It tastes earthy and smoky and is eaten with a little mustard. The meal is finished with a sweet oven pancake with jam.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g dried yellow peas, soaked overnight
  • 300 g smoked pork or ham hock
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1.5 l water
  • Mustard to serve

Method

  1. Drain the soaked peas and put them in a pot with the water and pork.
  2. Bring to a boil, skim, and simmer for 30 minutes.
  3. Add chopped onion and carrot.
  4. Cook for another 40 minutes until the peas are soft.
  5. Remove the pork, shred it, return it to the soup and season with salt.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a lean smoked cut and trim visible fat to lower the calories.
  • Blend part of the soup for a creamier texture without cream.
  • Serve fruit instead of the sweet pancake for a lighter finish.

History

Pea soup is traditionally served on Thursdays in Finland, a habit that was long common in army kitchens and schools. The Thursday custom is still widely followed.
