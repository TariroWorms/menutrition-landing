Chenjeh kebab (Persian lamb skewers)

Origin: Iran

Chenjeh is the Iranian name for skewered lamb cubes, marinated overnight in grated onion, yogurt and saffron. Over hot charcoal the outside chars lightly while the inside stays tender and juicy. It is served with saffron rice, grilled tomatoes and a sprinkle of sumac.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb leg or shoulder, cubed
  • 1 large onion, grated (about 200 g)
  • 100 g plain yogurt
  • 1 g saffron, dissolved in 3 tbsp hot water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and black pepper
  • 2 tomatoes, to grill

Method

  1. Mix the grated onion, yogurt, saffron water, oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Coat the lamb cubes in the marinade and refrigerate for at least 8 hours.
  3. Thread the meat onto flat skewers, leaving little gaps.
  4. Grill over hot coals for 10-12 minutes, turning often.
  5. Grill the tomatoes alongside and rest the skewers for 3 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean cuts and trim visible fat to lower the calories.
  • Serve with plenty of grilled vegetables and a salad instead of a large portion of rice.
  • Use less salt and rely on saffron and sumac for flavor.

History

Skewered grilled meat is among the oldest cooking methods in the Persian-speaking world, and chenjeh is a staple of Iranian kebab houses. The onion marinade is the traditional way to tenderize the meat.
