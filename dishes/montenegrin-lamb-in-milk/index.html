Lamb in milk (Jagnjetina u mlijeku)

Origin: Montenegro
420kcal

Jagnjetina u mlijeku is a Montenegrin mountain dish of lamb slowly cooked in milk with potatoes and onion. The milk reduces to a light creamy sauce and tenderizes the meat. The taste is mild, soft and rich. It is served hot from the pot, often with fresh bread.

What it is made of

lambmilkpotatoonionbutterbay leaf

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g lamb on the bone
  • 500 ml milk
  • 500 g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • bay leaf, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown the lamb with the onion in butter.
  2. Add the milk, bay leaf and seasoning.
  3. Simmer covered for 60 minutes on low heat.
  4. Add the potatoes in chunks.
  5. Cook 30 more minutes until the sauce thickens and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean cuts and remove visible fat.
  • Use low-fat milk.
  • Serve with salad or steamed vegetables.

History

Dishes cooked in milk belong to the pastoral tradition of Montenegro's highlands, where sheep and dairy are central to daily life. The method keeps the meat gentle and the cooking simple.
