Fruit chaat (spiced fruit salad)

Origin: Pakistan

Fruit chaat is a bowl of chopped seasonal fruit such as apple, banana, orange, guava, pomegranate and grapes tossed with lemon juice and chaat masala. It is sweet, sour and salty at once, with a faint heat from black pepper. It is a refreshing snack on hot days and a fixture of the iftar table during Ramadan.

What it is made of

appleorangepomegranatelemonsugar

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 apples (300 g)
  • 2 oranges (300 g)
  • 1 banana (120 g)
  • 100 g pomegranate seeds
  • 100 g grapes
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice (30 ml)
  • 1 tsp chaat masala, pinch of black pepper and salt

Method

  1. Wash and dice all the fruit into bite-size pieces.
  2. Place in a large bowl with the pomegranate seeds.
  3. Add lemon juice, chaat masala, pepper and a pinch of salt.
  4. Toss gently and chill 10 minutes.
  5. Serve cold, tossing once more before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip sugar; the fruit is already sweet.
  • Add guava or cucumber for more fibre.
  • Serve right after mixing to keep the fruit crisp.

History

Chaat means a savoury, tangy snack and is found all over South Asia. Fruit chaat is a street-side and home favourite in Pakistan, especially in Ramadan, when fruit is used to break the fast.
