🍎 Apple Pita (Pita sa Jabukama)

Origin: Bosnia and Herzegovina · Balkans

Pita sa jabukama is a Balkan apple pie made by layering or rolling thin phyllo sheets around grated apples mixed with sugar, cinnamon and chopped walnuts. Baked until crisp and golden, it has flaky outer layers and a soft, juicy, spiced filling. It is cut into squares or slices and served warm or cold, often dusted with sugar.

What it is made of

phyllo pastryapplecinnamonsugarwalnuts

Recipe

8 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g phyllo pastry
  • 1 kg apples, grated
  • 100 g sugar
  • 80 g walnuts, chopped
  • 2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 45 ml sunflower oil

Method

  1. Mix the grated apples (lightly squeezed) with sugar, cinnamon and walnuts.
  2. Brush a baking dish with oil and lay in half the phyllo sheets, brushing a little oil between them.
  3. Spread the apple filling evenly, then cover with the remaining sheets, oiling lightly.
  4. Score into portions and bake at 180 °C for 35–40 minutes until golden.
  5. Cool slightly before cutting; serve warm or cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush the sheets very lightly or use spray oil; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Cut the sugar to 60 g; sweet apples carry the flavour.
  • Halve the walnuts and scatter them only on top.

History

Pita sa jabukama is a familiar home-baked dessert in Bosnia and Herzegovina and across the former Yugoslav countries, part of a wider family of phyllo pies (pita) eaten throughout the Balkans.
