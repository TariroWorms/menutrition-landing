La bandera dominicana (rice, beans and stewed meat)

Origin: Dominican Republic

La bandera, "the flag", is the classic Dominican lunch of white rice, red bean stew and stewed meat, usually chicken or beef. Each part is simple but well seasoned with sofrito, garlic and oregano. A cabbage salad and fried green plantain slices often complete the plate.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g long-grain white rice
  • 250 g cooked red kidney beans
  • 600 g chicken thighs
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 4 cloves garlic, 1 lime
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 200 g shredded cabbage

Method

  1. Season the chicken with garlic, oregano, salt and lime and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Brown the chicken in oil, add onion and tomato paste, then a cup of water, and stew covered for 30 minutes.
  3. Simmer the cooked beans with a ladle of the chicken's cooking sauce and a little water until the broth thickens.
  4. Cook the rice in salted water with a spoon of oil until fluffy.
  5. Dress the cabbage with lime, oil and salt.
  6. Serve rice with beans and meat on the side, with the salad.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken and less oil to lighten the stew.
  • Replace part of the white rice with brown rice for more fibre.
  • Fill half the plate with salad.

History

The name refers to the three colours on the plate, echoing the national flag. The combination reflects the Spanish, African and Taíno influences found in Dominican home cooking, and it is eaten almost daily across the country.
