Arishta ash (noodle and bean soup)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Arishta ash is a hearty soup of thin homemade egg noodles, red beans and small pieces of lamb. The broth is lightly tinted with tomato and seasoned with dried mint just before serving. Some cooks add a spoon of yogurt or vinegar to each bowl. It is a filling winter dish that easily feeds a whole family.

Recipe

4 servings110 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g homemade or dried egg noodles
  • 200 g red beans, soaked overnight
  • 300 g lamb, diced
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried mint, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Boil the beans in fresh water for about an hour until nearly soft.
  2. Brown the lamb and chopped onion in a pot.
  3. Stir in the tomato paste, then add the beans and 1.5 litres of their water or fresh water.
  4. Simmer 40 minutes until the meat is tender.
  5. Add the noodles and cook 8–10 minutes.
  6. Season and serve with dried mint.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more beans and less meat for extra fibre.
  • Wholewheat noodles keep you full longer.
  • Add spinach in the last minutes for extra greens.

History

'Əriştə' is the Azerbaijani word for hand-cut noodles, a staple shared with Iranian and Turkish cooking. Noodle and bean soups of this kind are traditional in the colder months.
