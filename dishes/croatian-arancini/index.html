Arancini (Croatian candied orange peel)

Origin: Croatia · Dalmatia

Croatian arancini are not rice balls but long strips of orange peel candied in sugar syrup. They are chewy, sweet and bitter-citrusy, and are sometimes dipped in dark chocolate. They are served as a sweet with coffee or used in cakes.

What it is made of

orangesugarlemon

Recipe

12 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 4 oranges
  • 300 g sugar
  • 300 ml water
  • 1 lemon
  • sugar for coating

Method

  1. Peel the oranges in thick strips and cut into thin sticks.
  2. Boil the peel in water and drain; repeat twice to remove bitterness.
  3. Heat sugar, water and lemon juice to make a syrup.
  4. Simmer the peel in the syrup for about an hour until translucent.
  5. Drain and let dry on a rack.
  6. Roll in sugar and store in a jar.

How to make it lighter

  • Eat only a few pieces; they are very sweet.
  • Cut the sugar in the coating and keep portions small; a few pieces are plenty.
  • Use dark chocolate sparingly.

History

The orange peel sweets are made in Dalmatia, where bitter orange trees grow, especially around Dubrovnik and on Pelješac. They are a traditional holiday sweet and often given as a gift.
