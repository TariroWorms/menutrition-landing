Torshi liteh (Persian chopped eggplant pickle)

Origin: Iran

Torshi liteh is a soft, tangy relish of steamed or roasted eggplant mixed with herbs, garlic and vinegar. It tastes sour, garlicky and herbal. It is served with rice, kebabs and bread as a side.

What it is made of

eggplantherbsgarlicolive oilvinegar

Recipe

8 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg eggplants
  • 1 bunch each parsley, mint and coriander
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 150 ml white vinegar
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Roast or steam the eggplants until very soft.
  2. Peel and chop finely.
  3. Mix with finely chopped herbs and crushed garlic.
  4. Add the vinegar, oil and salt.
  5. Pack into a jar and rest in the fridge for 2 days.

How to make it lighter

  • Use little oil.
  • Salt lightly for low-sodium diets.
  • Serve in small spoonfuls as a relish.

History

Torshi, pickles, are a staple of Persian tables, and each region has its own versions. Liteh, meaning mashed or chopped, is a typical northern preparation.
