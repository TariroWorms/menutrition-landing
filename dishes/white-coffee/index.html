Despite its name, Lebanese white coffee contains no coffee at all: it is hot water perfumed with a few drops of orange blossom water, sometimes lightly sweetened. It is caffeine-free, delicate and floral. It is served in small cups after meals, especially in the evening.

What it is made of

waterorange blossom watersugar

Recipe

2 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 400 ml water
  • 2 tsp orange blossom water
  • 1–2 tsp sugar (optional)

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil.
  2. Pour into small cups.
  3. Stir in the orange blossom water and sugar if using.
  4. Serve hot after a meal.

How to make it lighter

  • Drink it unsweetened — it is almost calorie-free.
  • 1 tsp sugar adds about 16 kcal per cup.
  • Offer it instead of a dessert drink after dinner.

History

White coffee (ahweh baida) is a Lebanese custom, often offered after dinner as a soothing alternative to coffee. Orange blossom water itself has long been distilled across the Levant and North Africa for sweets and drinks.
