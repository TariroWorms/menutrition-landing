Recipe

3 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g wheat flour (for noodles)
  • 350 g lamb, sliced
  • 2 peppers
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • cumin, salt

Method

  1. Knead flour, water and salt into a firm dough and rest it 30 minutes.
  2. Roll and stretch into long strands and boil 3–4 minutes.
  3. Fry the lamb in hot oil with onion until browned.
  4. Add garlic, peppers, tomatoes and cumin and stir-fry 8 minutes.
  5. Season with soy and vinegar.
  6. Top the noodles with the sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb and less oil.
  • Add extra vegetables to the sauce.
  • Make half the noodles and fill the bowl with more sauce.

History

Laghman is part of the shared noodle tradition of the Silk Road, and the Uyghur version is known as laghmen or lagman. Its pulled noodle technique is related to the Chinese lamian.
