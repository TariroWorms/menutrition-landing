Bhutanese red rice

Origin: Bhutan

Bhutanese red rice is a short, plump, semi-milled grain grown in the mountain valleys. Cooked, it turns soft and slightly sticky with a nutty, earthy flavour and a pinkish colour. It is the base of almost every Bhutanese meal.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g red rice
  • 600 ml water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp butter (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse the rice well.
  2. Put rice and water in a pot with the salt.
  3. Bring to the boil, then cover and cook on low heat.
  4. Simmer about 25 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  5. Rest covered for 10 minutes.
  6. Fluff with a fork and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Soak the rice for 30 minutes to shorten cooking.
  • Serve with vegetables and legumes for balance.
  • Skip the butter to keep it vegan.

History

The rice grows in high-altitude terraces fed by glacial water, and it has been the staple for centuries. Its bran gives it a distinctive colour and extra fibre.
