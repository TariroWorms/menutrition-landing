🧀 Cheese dakgalbi

Cheese dakgalbi is spicy stir-fried chicken with gochujang, cabbage, chewy rice cakes and scallions, finished with a thick layer of melted mozzarella. The fiery, sweet sauce contrasts with the stretchy cheese. It is cooked on a large hot plate at the table and shared straight from the pan.

Recipe

3 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  • 3 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 300 g cabbage, roughly chopped
  • 150 g Korean rice cakes (tteok)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 scallions, cut into lengths
  • 150 g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

  1. Mix the gochujang, sugar and garlic, coat the chicken and leave for 20 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan, add the chicken and stir-fry for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the cabbage, onion and rice cakes and cook, stirring, for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is done and the rice cakes are soft.
  4. Add the scallions, push everything to the edges, put the mozzarella in the middle and cover until melted.
  5. Serve straight from the pan.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the mozzarella and scatter it over the top instead of making a thick pool.
  • Use more cabbage and fewer rice cakes.
  • Use skinless chicken breast for a leaner dish.

History

Dakgalbi is closely associated with Chuncheon in Gangwon Province, where it is said to have begun as an inexpensive chicken dish in the 1960s. The cheese-topped version became popular in Korean restaurants in the 2010s and also caught on in Japan.
