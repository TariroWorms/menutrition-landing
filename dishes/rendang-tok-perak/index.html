Rendang tok (Perak dry beef rendang)

Origin: Malaysia

Rendang tok is beef simmered in coconut milk and spices until the liquid has cooked away and the meat is dark, dry and coated in a fragrant paste. Toasted grated coconut adds nuttiness. It is rich, spicy and long-keeping, served with rice or lemang.

Recipe

6 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef chuck, cubed
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 100 g toasted grated coconut (kerisik)
  • spice paste: 10 dried chilies, 6 shallots, 4 garlic, 3 cm galangal, 2 lemongrass
  • 2 turmeric leaves or lime leaves, salt

Method

  1. Blend the spice paste ingredients.
  2. Simmer paste, beef and coconut milk, stirring often.
  3. Cook 1.5 hours until the sauce thickens and the oil separates.
  4. Stir in kerisik and leaves.
  5. Cook until dry and dark, then rest and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean cuts and skim the surface oil.
  • Use light coconut milk.
  • Serve a small portion with plenty of vegetables.

History

The dish belongs to the Malay rendang tradition and is especially linked to Perak, where it is made for festive occasions. Slow cooking both preserves and concentrates the flavours.
