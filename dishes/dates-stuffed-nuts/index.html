🌰 Nut-stuffed dates

Stuffed dates are soft pitted dates filled with a whole almond, a piece of walnut or a few pistachios. The chewy, honey-sweet fruit contrasts with the crunchy, slightly bitter nut. They are arranged on trays for guests, served with Arabic coffee, and are especially common during Ramadan, Eid and weddings.

Recipe

6 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 18 soft dates (such as Medjool or Sukkari)
  • 6 blanched almonds
  • 6 walnut halves
  • 2 tbsp shelled pistachios

Method

  1. Make a slit along each date and remove the pit.
  2. Press an almond, a walnut half or a few pistachios into each date.
  3. Close the dates gently around the filling.
  4. Arrange on a plate and serve with Arabic coffee.

How to make it lighter

  • Each stuffed date is rich: two or three make a satisfying portion.
  • Choose smaller dates to keep each piece lighter.
  • Pair them with unsweetened coffee or tea rather than sweet drinks.

History

Filling dates with nuts is a long-standing way of presenting them to guests across the Arab world, and stuffed dates are a standard item on Gulf hospitality trays today.
