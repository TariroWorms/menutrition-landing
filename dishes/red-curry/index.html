🍛 Thai red curry with chicken (gaeng phet gai)

Thai red curry with chicken is a creamy, aromatic curry made by frying red curry paste — dried red chillies, lemongrass, galangal, garlic, shallots and shrimp paste — in coconut milk, then simmering sliced chicken with bamboo shoots and bell pepper. It is rich and moderately spicy, finished with fresh basil and served with jasmine rice.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast or thigh, thinly sliced
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste
  • 200 g bamboo shoots, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 kaffir lime leaves, torn
  • 1 tsp sugar, salt to taste
  • A handful of basil leaves

Method

  1. Heat a third of the coconut milk until it bubbles and the oil starts to separate.
  2. Fry the curry paste in it for 2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken and stir until coated, then pour in the rest of the coconut milk and the lime leaves.
  4. Add the bamboo shoots and bell pepper and simmer for 8–10 minutes until the chicken is cooked.
  5. Season with sugar and salt, stir in the basil and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk, or half coconut milk and half water.
  • Add extra bamboo shoots and peppers to stretch each portion.
  • Keep the rice portion modest — the curry sauce is the richest part of the meal.

History

Gaeng phet, literally "spicy curry", is one of the core coconut-milk curries of central Thai cooking, built on a pounded chilli paste. Red curry is now one of the most widely known Thai dishes abroad.
