🥔 Kibbeh Batata (Potato Kibbeh)

Origin: Levant · Lebanon

Kibbeh batata is a tray-baked kibbeh in which mashed potato replaces most of the meat in the shell. The soft potato-bulgur dough, seasoned with onion and spices, is spread in two layers around a filling of spiced ground lamb and onion, then baked with a drizzle of oil until golden on top. It is soft, mild and comforting, served in squares with salad or pickles.

Recipe

6 servings80 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g potatoes
  • 120 g fine bulgur
  • 250 g ground lamb
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tsp seven-spice (baharat)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Boil potatoes until tender, peel and mash.
  2. Soak bulgur 10 minutes, squeeze dry and knead into the mash with 1 grated onion, half the spices and salt.
  3. Fry the lamb with the second chopped onion and remaining spices.
  4. Press half the potato dough into an oiled tray, spread the meat, then cover with the rest of the dough.
  5. Score into squares, drizzle with the remaining oil and bake at 200 °C for 30–35 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp oil instead of 3 — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Brown the lamb in its own fat and drain before layering.
  • Add finely chopped vegetables to the filling to stretch the meat.

History

Potato kibbeh is a home-style variation from Lebanon and Syria, often made as an economical everyday version of the classic dish.
