Herring with potatoes (seliadzets z bulbai)

Origin: Belarus

Fillets of salted herring are laid on a plate with thin rings of onion and drizzled with fragrant unrefined sunflower oil. Next to them come steaming potatoes, boiled in their skins or peeled, with butter and dill. The contrast of cold salty fish and hot mild potato is the whole point, and it is a fixture of family dinners and holiday tables.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 salted herrings (500 g)
  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 1 red onion
  • 3 tbsp unrefined sunflower oil
  • 20 g butter
  • Dill

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 20-25 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile skin and fillet the herring and remove the bones.
  3. Cut the fillets into 2 cm pieces and lay them on a platter.
  4. Top with thin onion rings and drizzle with sunflower oil.
  5. Drain the potatoes, toss with butter and chopped dill.
  6. Serve the hot potatoes next to the cold herring.

How to make it lighter

  • Soak very salty herring in milk or water for an hour to lower sodium.
  • Skip the butter on the potatoes and rely on dill and oil.
  • Add a beet or cabbage salad for fibre.

History

Salted herring became a cheap, widely available staple across Eastern Europe in the 19th and 20th centuries. In Belarus, the land of potatoes, the two naturally ended up on the same plate and remain a beloved everyday pairing.
