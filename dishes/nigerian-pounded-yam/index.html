Pounded yam (iyán)

Origin: Nigeria

Pounded yam is a pillowy, stretchy swallow of freshly boiled white yam. The tubers are pounded in a mortar until every lump disappears and the dough turns smooth and glossy. It tastes mildly sweet and creamy. It is rolled into balls and eaten with egusi, okra, efo riro or other soups.

What it is made of

yam

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg white yam
  • about 300 ml water, plus a pinch of salt

Method

  1. Peel the yam and cut into chunks, rinsing off the starch.
  2. Boil in lightly salted water for about 20 minutes until very soft.
  3. Drain, keeping a little cooking water.
  4. Pound or process hot, adding drops of water, until completely smooth and stretchy.
  5. Shape into balls and serve immediately with soup.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep the swallow portion modest and let the vegetable soup fill the plate.
  • Pair with a broth-based soup rather than a very oily one.
  • Eat slowly with plenty of leafy greens and lean protein.

History

Yam is a major food crop in Nigeria, which produces more of it than any other country. Pounded yam is traditionally a festive meal, prepared by hand with a mortar and pestle.
