🍗 Crispy chicken strips

Crispy chicken strips, also called chicken tenders or fingers, are strips of chicken breast dipped in egg, coated in seasoned breadcrumbs and deep-fried until crunchy and golden. Inside, the meat stays juicy and tender. They are served with dipping sauces and fries or salad, and are a staple of fast-food menus and kids' meals.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast, cut into strips
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 120 g breadcrumbs
  • 50 g plain flour
  • 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt and pepper
  • Oil for deep-frying

Method

  1. Season the chicken strips with salt, pepper, paprika and garlic powder.
  2. Set out three bowls: flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs.
  3. Coat each strip in flour, dip in egg, then press into the breadcrumbs.
  4. Deep-fry in oil at 175 °C for 4–5 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  5. Drain on paper towels and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake at 220 °C on a rack with a light spray of oil, or use an air fryer, instead of deep-frying.
  • A lot of the energy is in the coating: thin breading on thicker strips means more chicken per bite.
  • Pair with salad or vegetable sticks rather than fries, and choose a tomato-based dip.

History

Breaded and fried chicken pieces have a long history in Southern US cooking, and boneless strips became a fast-food menu item from the 1970s and 1980s. Several restaurants claim to have introduced them first, and none of the claims is settled. Today they are eaten across the world.
