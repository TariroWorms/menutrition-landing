🍋 Veal Piccata (Piccata Milanese)

Piccata milanese is a quick Italian veal dish: thin veal cutlets pan-fried in butter until lightly golden, then finished with a pan sauce of white wine, fresh lemon juice and capers. The meat stays tender and the sauce is glossy, bright and tangy. It is typically served with a vegetable side, mashed potatoes or a simple salad.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g veal cutlets, pounded thin
  • 40 g butter
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 100 ml dry white wine
  • Juice of 1 lemon plus slices
  • 2 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • Salt, pepper, chopped parsley

Method

  1. Season the veal with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil and half the butter and fry the cutlets 1–2 minutes per side; keep warm.
  3. Deglaze the pan with the white wine and let it reduce by half.
  4. Add the lemon juice, capers and remaining butter and swirl into a sauce.
  5. Return the veal to coat it, sprinkle with parsley and serve with lemon slices.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and halve the butter — 1 tbsp butter ≈ 100 kcal.
  • Serve with steamed green vegetables instead of buttery mash.
  • Spoon just a little sauce over each cutlet.

History

Piccata is a dish of northern Italian cooking associated with Milan, where veal scaloppine are a classic of the trattoria repertoire. The lemon-and-caper version later became widely known abroad through Italian-American restaurants, where chicken piccata is now common.
