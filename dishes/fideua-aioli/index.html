🥘 Fideuà with seafood and aioli

Fideuà is the Valencian seafood noodle dish: thin noodles cooked paella-style in a wide pan with saffron-scented fish stock, shrimp, mussels and squid. The noodles soak up the briny stock and the ends curl up and turn crunchy on top. It is served from the pan with a spoonful of garlicky aioli on the side, which is stirred in at the table.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g thin noodles (fideos)
  • 250 g shrimp
  • 400 g mussels, cleaned
  • 200 g squid, cut into rings
  • 1 litre fish stock, hot
  • a pinch of saffron
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tomato, grated
  • 4 tbsp aioli, to serve

Method

  1. Steam the mussels open in a covered pan with a splash of water; strain and add their liquid to the stock.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan, sear the shrimp and squid, then add the garlic and tomato.
  3. Add the noodles and toast them for 2 minutes in the pan.
  4. Pour in the hot stock with the saffron and cook without stirring for 10 minutes.
  5. Add the mussels and shrimp on top, let the noodles crisp for a few minutes and serve with aioli.

How to make it lighter

  • Aioli is the richest part of the plate: take one teaspoon instead of a big spoonful.
  • Add more mussels and squid and fewer noodles for a lighter, seafood-heavy portion.
  • Squeeze lemon over the noodles for flavour instead of extra oil; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Fideuà comes from the Valencian coast and is usually credited to the fishing town of Gandia, where the story goes that a ship's cook swapped rice for short noodles in a paella-style dish in the early 20th century. Gandia still hosts an annual fideuà competition. Today it is served in seafood restaurants all along Spain's Mediterranean coast.
