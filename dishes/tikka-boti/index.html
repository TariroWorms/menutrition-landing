Tikka boti (charcoal-grilled meat cubes)

Origin: Pakistan · Lahore

Tikka boti is a skewer of boneless meat cubes marinated in yogurt, lemon, chilli and ginger-garlic, then grilled over charcoal until charred at the edges and juicy inside. It tastes smoky, tangy and spicy. It is served hot with naan, mint chutney, sliced onion and lemon.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless mutton or beef, cubed
  • 150 g yogurt
  • 30 g ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice (30 ml)
  • 2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp garam masala
  • 30 ml oil, salt
  • onion and lemon, to serve

Method

  1. Mix the marinade ingredients and coat the meat; rest at least 4 hours.
  2. Thread the meat onto skewers.
  3. Grill over hot charcoal for 12-15 minutes, turning and brushing with oil.
  4. Rest for a few minutes.
  5. Serve with naan, chutney, onion and lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean cuts and trim fat.
  • Add vegetables to the skewers.
  • Serve with salad rather than extra naan.

History

Skewered grilled meats are a staple of Lahore and Punjab street food. Tikka boti is a favourite at barbecue stalls in the evening.
