Russian rice milk porridge (risovaya kasha)

Origin: Russia

Risovaya kasha is a soft, creamy porridge of short-grain rice simmered in milk until the grains swell and turn tender. It is mildly sweet and is finished with butter, and sometimes with raisins, pumpkin or jam. Russians eat it warm for breakfast, especially children.

Recipe

3 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g round rice
  • 300 ml water
  • 700 ml milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • a pinch of salt
  • 20 g butter

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and boil it in the water for 8-10 minutes until the water is almost absorbed.
  2. Pour in the milk, add salt and sugar and bring back to a gentle boil.
  3. Cook on low heat for 20-25 minutes, stirring often so it does not stick.
  4. When creamy, take off the heat, cover and leave for 5 minutes.
  5. Serve with butter, plus raisins or berries if you like.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk or more water and less milk.
  • Cut the sugar and sweeten with grated apple or berries.
  • Add cubes of pumpkin during cooking for extra fibre and volume.

History

Milk porridges were everyday breakfast food in Russian homes and became standard in Soviet kindergartens and canteens. Rice porridge is one of the most familiar of them, alongside semolina and oat porridge.
