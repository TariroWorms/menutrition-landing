Kunna (Chiniot clay-pot mutton)

Origin: Pakistan · Chiniot

Kunna is a mutton dish from Chiniot in Punjab, cooked sealed in a clay pot for hours with yogurt, onion, ginger and whole spices. The meat becomes very tender in a thick, oily gravy. It is eaten with naan or tandoori roti.

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g mutton on the bone
  • 200 g yogurt
  • 2 onions, sliced (300 g)
  • 50 g ginger and garlic paste
  • 60 g ghee
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chili powder

Method

  1. Mix the mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste and spices.
  2. Heat ghee in the pot and fry the onions until golden.
  3. Add the meat mixture.
  4. Seal the pot lid with dough.
  5. Cook on very low heat for 2.5 hours.
  6. Open and stir, then serve with naan.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat and use less ghee.
  • Serve with salad and a smaller bread portion.
  • Chill and skim excess oil before reheating.

History

The dish is named after the sealed clay pot, called kunna, in which it is cooked. It is a speciality of Chiniot and neighbouring Punjab towns.
