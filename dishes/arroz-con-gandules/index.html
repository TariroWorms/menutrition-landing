Arroz con gandules (Puerto Rican rice with pigeon peas)

Origin: Puerto Rico

Arroz con gandules is a one-pot rice colored golden by annatto and tomato, studded with pigeon peas, bits of pork and green olives. It is savory, aromatic with sofrito and oregano, and the rice stays fluffy with a crisp layer at the bottom called pegao. It is the traditional rice of Puerto Rican holiday tables.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g medium-grain rice
  • 250 g cooked pigeon peas
  • 100 g diced pork or ham
  • 100 g sofrito
  • 2 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 8 green olives
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 2 tbsp annatto oil
  • 600 ml water or broth
  • salt

Method

  1. Heat the annatto oil and brown the pork.
  2. Add the sofrito, tomato sauce, olives and oregano and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the rice to coat with the sauce.
  4. Add the pigeon peas, liquid and salt and bring to a boil.
  5. Cook until the liquid reaches the rice level, then stir once, cover and cook on low for 20 minutes.
  6. Rest 5 minutes and fluff, scraping up the crisp bottom layer.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less pork and more peas for fiber.
  • Choose brown rice and extend the cooking time.
  • Make your own sofrito to control the salt.

History

This dish is considered the national dish of Puerto Rico and is eaten especially at Christmas. Pigeon peas arrived through the Atlantic trade and became central to island cooking.
