Fërgesë

Origin: Albania · Tirana

Fërgesë is a baked Albanian dish of peppers and tomatoes cooked down with cottage cheese or feta until thick and creamy. It is savoury, slightly tangy and rich, and is served bubbling hot from the pan. Some versions add liver or other meat.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g peppers
  • 300 g ripe tomatoes
  • 200 g cottage cheese
  • 100 g feta
  • 1 onion (120 g)
  • 3 tbsp oil (40 ml), salt, oregano

Method

  1. Fry the onion and chopped peppers in oil until soft.
  2. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook 15 minutes.
  3. Stir in the cottage cheese and crumbled feta.
  4. Season and transfer to a clay dish.
  5. Bake at 190 C for 15 minutes until bubbling and lightly golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light cottage cheese and less oil.
  • Serve with whole grain bread.
  • Add extra vegetables to increase volume.

History

Fërgesë belongs to the traditional home cooking of Tirana and central Albania. Its roots lie in simple farm food that combines the vegetables and cheese of the household.
