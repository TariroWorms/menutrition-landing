Sheer khurma (Eid vermicelli milk pudding)

Origin: Pakistan

Sheer khurma is a warm, creamy pudding of fine vermicelli cooked in milk with dates, nuts and cardamom. It is lightly sweet and fragrant, often with a little saffron. Families serve it on the morning of Eid.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 l whole milk
  • 80 g vermicelli
  • 100 g chopped dates
  • 40 g almonds
  • 30 g pistachios
  • 50 g sugar
  • 20 g ghee
  • 3 cardamom pods

Method

  1. Toast the vermicelli in ghee until golden.
  2. Add milk and cardamom and bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Cook for 15 minutes until the milk reduces slightly.
  4. Add the dates and sugar.
  5. Simmer another 10 minutes.
  6. Top with nuts and serve warm or chilled.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat milk and less sugar, as the dates are already sweet.
  • Add more nuts for protein.
  • Measure portions in small bowls.

History

The name means milk with dates in Persian. It is linked to Eid celebrations across South Asia and Afghanistan, with each home using its own mix of nuts.
