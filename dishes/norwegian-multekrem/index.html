Multekrem (cloudberry cream)

Origin: Norway

Multekrem is whipped cream folded with sweetened cloudberries. It is airy and soft, with a gentle tart, honey-like fruit flavour. It is served in small glasses, often with a crisp biscuit.

What it is made of

creamberriessugarvanilla

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 300 ml whipping cream
  • 150 g cloudberries
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla sugar

Method

  1. Mix cloudberries with 2 tbsp sugar and let stand 10 minutes.
  2. Whip the cream with the remaining sugar and vanilla to soft peaks.
  3. Fold in most of the berries gently.
  4. Spoon into small glasses.
  5. Top with the remaining berries.
  6. Chill for 30 minutes.
  7. Serve with a thin biscuit.

How to make it lighter

  • Use small glasses; a few spoonfuls are satisfying.
  • Substitute half the cream with thick yogurt.
  • Reduce sugar when the berries are ripe.

History

Cloudberries grow wild in Norwegian bogs and mountains and are treasured as a seasonal delicacy. This simple cream is a classic Christmas dessert.
