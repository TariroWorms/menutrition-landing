Pastel de feira (Brazilian fried pastry)

Origin: Brazil

Pastel is a large rectangle of very thin dough folded over a filling and deep-fried until it puffs into a blistered, crackly shell. Popular fillings are seasoned ground beef, melted cheese, chicken, or hearts of palm. It is sold hot at street markets (feiras), traditionally with a glass of sugarcane juice. One pastel is a quick, filling snack.

Recipe

8 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 250 ml warm water
  • 400 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 200 g mozzarella, grated
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix flour, salt, oil and water into a smooth dough; rest 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion and beef until browned, season and let cool.
  3. Roll the dough very thin and cut into 15 x 10 cm rectangles.
  4. Put a spoonful of beef or cheese on one half, fold and seal with a fork.
  5. Deep-fry at 180 °C for 2-3 minutes until puffed and golden.
  6. Drain on paper and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose vegetable fillings like hearts of palm or greens.
  • Fry in very hot oil so the dough seals quickly and absorbs less fat.
  • Share one pastel and add a salad or fruit.

History

The pastel is closely associated with Japanese and Chinese immigrant families who ran market stalls in São Paulo in the twentieth century. The pairing of pastel and caldo de cana became a classic of the city's street markets.
