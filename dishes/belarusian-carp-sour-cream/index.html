Carp baked in sour cream

Origin: Belarus

Carp in sour cream is a simple Belarusian fish dish: pieces of carp are lightly floured, browned, then baked with onion under a layer of sour cream. The cream keeps the fish moist and forms a mild, slightly tangy sauce. It is sprinkled with dill and served with boiled potatoes.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 1 carp, about 1.2 kg, cleaned
  • 2 onions (160 g)
  • 250 g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • salt, pepper
  • fresh dill

Method

  1. Cut the carp into portions, season with salt and pepper.
  2. Dust in flour and brown briefly in oil on both sides.
  3. Lay the fish in a baking dish on a bed of sliced onion.
  4. Thin the sour cream with a little water, season and pour over the fish.
  5. Bake at 190 °C for 25–30 minutes and sprinkle with dill.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the frying and bake the fish directly to save fat.
  • Use light sour cream or a mix with yogurt.
  • Serve with steamed vegetables instead of a large portion of potatoes.

History

Carp is one of the most common fish in Belarusian ponds and fish farms, and it is widely eaten at home. Baking fish with sour cream is a typical method in Belarusian and wider East European cooking.
