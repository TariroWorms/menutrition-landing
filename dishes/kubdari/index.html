Kubdari (Svan meat pie)

Origin: Georgia · Svaneti
675kcal

Kubdari is a hearty meat-filled bread from the high mountains of Svaneti. Hand-chopped beef or pork is mixed with onion, garlic and Svan salt, a blend of salt with fenugreek, coriander and chili. Wrapped in dough and baked, the filling stays juicy and warmly spiced. It is cut into wedges and is a meal in itself.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 300 ml warm water
  • 300 g beef, finely chopped
  • 200 g pork, finely chopped
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp Svan salt
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Knead a soft yeast dough with flour, yeast, water and salt; let it rise 1 hour.
  2. Chop the meat by knife into small pieces.
  3. Mix meat with onion, crushed garlic and Svan salt.
  4. Divide dough into four, flatten and place a quarter of the filling in each.
  5. Seal the edges and roll out carefully into 1 cm rounds; prick a small hole on top.
  6. Bake at 220 °C for 25–30 minutes.
  7. Rest 5 minutes and cut into wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef only and skip the pork to cut fat.
  • Add finely chopped greens to the filling for more volume.
  • Eat a quarter with a large salad instead of half a pie.

History

Kubdari is the signature bread of Svaneti, a remote highland region of north-western Georgia. Svan salt, which gives it its character, is another product strongly associated with the area.
