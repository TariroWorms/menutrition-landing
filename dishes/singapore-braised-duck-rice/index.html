Braised duck rice (lor ark png)

Origin: Singapore

Lor ark png is duck simmered in a dark master stock of soy sauce, galangal, cinnamon and star anise until the meat is tender, then sliced over plain rice with a spoon of the sticky gravy. It tastes sweet, salty and deeply spiced. Sides include braised egg, tofu and cucumber.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1 duck (1.8 kg)
  • 600 ml master stock made with dark soy sauce
  • 2 galangal slices, 2 star anise, 1 cinnamon stick
  • 300 g cooked rice
  • cucumber, braised egg, chili dip

Method

  1. Blanch the duck and drain.
  2. Simmer in the spiced stock for 1.5 hours.
  3. Rest and cut into pieces.
  4. Reduce part of the stock to a gravy.
  5. Serve over rice with cucumber and chili.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin and fat.
  • Use less gravy.
  • Add vegetables and a low-sodium stock.

History

The dish is from the Teochew community, whose braised-goose and duck traditions came to Singapore with migrants. The master stock is reused and deepened over time.
