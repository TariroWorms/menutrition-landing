Sikhye (sweet rice punch)

Origin: South Korea

Sikhye is a light, sweet, clear drink made by steeping cooked rice with barley malt until the starch turns to sugar. It is served very cold with a few soft rice grains floating in it, and sometimes a pine nut or slice of fruit. The flavor is mildly malty and refreshing, popular after a large meal.

Recipe

6 servings360 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice (150 g)
  • 100 g barley malt powder
  • 2 l water
  • 100 g sugar
  • pine nuts for serving
  • pinch of salt

Method

  1. Soak the barley malt in the water for 1 hour and strain it, keeping the liquid.
  2. Mix the malt liquid with the cooked rice and keep warm at 55-60 C for 4-5 hours until the rice grains float.
  3. Strain the rice grains out and rinse them in cold water.
  4. Boil the liquid with the sugar for 10 minutes and skim.
  5. Chill and serve with the rice grains and pine nuts.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar in the final boil for a lighter drink.
  • Serve small portions after meals.
  • Rinse the rice grains well to cut starch.

History

Sikhye is a traditional Korean drink and dessert, enjoyed at holidays and often served after meals to help digestion. It is also made at home during New Year and Chuseok.
