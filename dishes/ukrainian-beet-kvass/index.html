Buriakovyi kvas (beet kvass)

Origin: Ukraine

Beet kvass is a non-alcoholic fermented drink made from sliced raw beets left to sour in water, sometimes with a crust of rye bread to start the fermentation. It tastes earthy, salty-sour and refreshing. Besides being drunk chilled, it is the traditional souring base for borscht and dishes like shpundra.

What it is made of

beetsbreadsugarwater

Recipe

8 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg raw beets
  • 2 litres boiled, cooled water
  • 1 slice rye bread
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Peel the beets and cut them into thin slices.
  2. Place them in a large clean jar with the bread.
  3. Dissolve sugar and salt in the water and pour over the beets.
  4. Cover with gauze and leave at room temperature for 3–5 days.
  5. Skim any foam daily until the liquid tastes pleasantly sour.
  6. Strain, bottle and keep chilled; drink within a week.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sugar and bread for a lighter, gluten-free version.
  • Add a slice of ginger or garlic for flavour.
  • Drink a small glass before meals rather than large amounts.

History

Fermenting beets was a long-standing way to preserve them in Ukrainian homes, and the liquid was prized as a souring agent long before vinegar became common. Today it is also sold as a probiotic drink.
