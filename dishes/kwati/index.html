Kwati (Nepali sprouted bean soup)

Origin: Nepal · Newar

Kwati is a warm, thick soup made from a mix of sprouted beans and legumes cooked with ginger, garlic, cumin and a little ghee. It is earthy, tangy and deeply satisfying. Many families eat it with rice or beaten rice.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g mixed beans (black-eyed peas, chickpeas, soybeans, lentils, green gram)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tbsp ghee or oil
  • salt, coriander, 1.2 l water

Method

  1. Soak the beans overnight and leave to sprout for 1-2 days, rinsing twice a day.
  2. Heat the ghee and fry cumin, onion, ginger and garlic.
  3. Add turmeric and the sprouted beans.
  4. Pour in the water and simmer for 40-50 minutes until soft.
  5. Season with salt and finish with coriander.
  6. Serve hot with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only a teaspoon of ghee to keep it light.
  • Pressure cook to save time.
  • Add greens for extra vitamins.

History

Kwati is traditionally prepared for the Janai Purnima festival in late summer. The mix of nine beans is considered nourishing, and the sprouting is said to make it easier to digest.
