🍄 Veal scaloppine with mushrooms

Scaloppine ai funghi are thin slices of veal seared quickly in a hot pan, then finished in a sauce of sautéed mushrooms, white wine and cream. The veal stays tender and the sauce is earthy and silky. It is served straight from the pan, often with mashed potatoes, polenta or green vegetables.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g veal escalopes, pounded thin
  • 300 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 100 ml dry white wine
  • 100 ml cooking cream
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, chopped
  • Salt, pepper and chopped parsley

Method

  1. Season the veal with salt and pepper.
  2. Sear the veal in 1 tbsp oil over high heat for 1 minute per side and set aside.
  3. Add the remaining oil, the garlic and mushrooms and cook until golden.
  4. Pour in the wine and let it bubble until reduced by half.
  5. Stir in the cream, return the veal to the pan and heat through for 2 minutes.
  6. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream and add a splash of water to keep the sauce loose.
  • Double the mushrooms and serve with steamed vegetables instead of mash.
  • Sear in a non-stick pan so 1 tbsp oil is enough — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Scaloppine — thin escalopes of veal pounded flat and cooked quickly in a pan — are a staple of Italian home and trattoria cooking. They are especially associated with northern and central Italy, though the dish is now served throughout the country and in Italian restaurants abroad.
