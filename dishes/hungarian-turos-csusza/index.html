Túrós csusza (noodles with curd cheese)

Túrós csusza is a simple dish of broad noodles mixed with soft curd cheese, sour cream and crisp bacon pieces. It is salty, tangy and creamy at the same time. It is eaten hot as a main course and is a favourite of Hungarian home cooking.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g broad egg noodles or square pasta
  • 250 g curd or cottage cheese
  • 150 g sour cream
  • 100 g smoked bacon, diced
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water and drain.
  2. Fry the bacon until crisp and golden.
  3. Toss the pasta with a spoon of bacon fat.
  4. Fold in the cheese and half the sour cream.
  5. Top with the bacon pieces.
  6. Serve with the remaining sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat curd cheese and less bacon.
  • Choose whole-grain pasta.
  • Add steamed greens on the side.

History

The dish is an everyday classic of the Hungarian countryside, built from dairy, flour and a bit of smoked pork. Variations without bacon are common during fasting days.
