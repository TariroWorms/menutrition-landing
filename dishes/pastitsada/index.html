Pastitsada is a Corfiot Sunday dish of rooster braised slowly in a tomato sauce perfumed with cinnamon, cloves and allspice, then served over thick pasta. The long cooking makes the meat tender and the sauce rich and gently sweet-spiced. It is ladled generously over bucatini or similar pasta.

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg rooster or chicken, jointed
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 700 g tomato passata
  • 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cloves, 4 allspice berries
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 500 g bucatini or thick pasta
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown the rooster pieces in olive oil and set aside.
  2. Soften the onion and garlic in the same pot.
  3. Add passata, spices, salt, pepper and enough water to almost cover the meat; return the meat.
  4. Cover and braise gently for 2–2½ hours (1 hour for chicken) until very tender.
  5. Cook the pasta, toss with some of the sauce and serve topped with meat and more sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin before braising to reduce fat.
  • Serve a smaller pasta portion (about 70–80 g dry) with more meat and sauce.
  • Add a green salad or steamed greens to make the plate more filling.

History

Pastitsada comes from Corfu, where centuries of Venetian rule left a strong Italian influence on the island's cooking; its name is thought to relate to the Venetian 'pastissada'. It remains a traditional festive and Sunday meal on the island.
