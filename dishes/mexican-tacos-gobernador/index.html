Tacos gobernador (shrimp and cheese tacos)

Origin: Mexico · Sinaloa

Tacos gobernador are a Sinaloan seafood taco: chopped shrimp are sautéed with onion, tomato and poblano or bell pepper, then folded into corn tortillas with plenty of melting cheese. The tacos are pressed on a griddle until the cheese oozes and the outside turns crisp. They are eaten hot with avocado salsa and lime.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g peeled shrimp, chopped
  • 1 onion and 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 poblano or green pepper, diced
  • 150 g Chihuahua or mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • avocado salsa and lime to serve

Method

  1. Melt the butter and soften the onion and pepper for 4 minutes.
  2. Add the tomato and cook until saucy.
  3. Add the shrimp and cook just until pink; season.
  4. Fill each tortilla with shrimp and a handful of cheese and fold.
  5. Griddle the tacos on both sides until crisp and the cheese melts.
  6. Serve with avocado salsa and lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cheese and more peppers for a lighter taco.
  • Cook on a dry non-stick pan instead of oiling the tortillas.
  • Pair with a shredded cabbage slaw dressed with lime.

History

The taco is usually traced to Mazatlán in the 1980s, and the name means “governor’s tacos”, linked in popular telling to a state governor who enjoyed them. Since then they have spread to seafood stands across northern Mexico.
