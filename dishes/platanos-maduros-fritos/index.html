Plátanos maduros fritos (fried sweet plantains)

Origin: Cuba
240kcal

Plátanos maduros are very ripe, black-skinned plantains cut into slices and fried until the edges caramelize. They are soft, sweet and slightly sticky, with a deep banana flavor. They balance salty rice, beans and roast meats on a Cuban plate.

What it is made of

plantainoilsalt

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe plantains (about 600 g)
  • 4 tbsp oil (60 ml)
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Peel the ripe plantains and slice on a long diagonal, 1 cm thick.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan over medium heat.
  3. Fry the slices 2-3 minutes per side until deep golden.
  4. Do not crowd the pan so they brown rather than steam.
  5. Drain on paper towels.
  6. Sprinkle with a pinch of salt and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake on a lined tray at 220 °C with a brush of oil for less fat.
  • Use a non-stick pan and a thin film of oil.
  • Balance the sweetness with beans and vegetables.

History

Plantains were brought to the Caribbean in the early colonial period and quickly became a staple. Cooks learned that the riper the fruit, the sweeter the fry. Maduros are a daily side dish across Cuban households.
