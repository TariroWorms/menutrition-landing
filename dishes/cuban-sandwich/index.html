Cuban sandwich (sándwich cubano)

Origin: Cuba

The Cuban sandwich layers sliced roast pork, ham, Swiss cheese and dill pickles with yellow mustard in a loaf of soft Cuban bread. It is buttered and pressed on a hot plancha until the crust is crisp and the cheese melts. Each bite is salty, tangy and rich.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf soft Cuban or French bread, about 250 g
  • 120 g roast pork, sliced
  • 100 g ham, sliced
  • 80 g Swiss cheese
  • 4 dill pickle slices (40 g)
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp butter (15 g)

Method

  1. Halve the bread lengthwise and spread mustard on both sides.
  2. Layer ham, pork, cheese and pickles.
  3. Close and brush the top with butter.
  4. Press in a hot grill pan or sandwich press with a weight.
  5. Cook 3-4 minutes per side until crisp and the cheese melts.
  6. Cut diagonally and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork and ham and half the cheese to cut fat.
  • Choose wholegrain bread for more fiber.
  • Add extra pickles and mustard for flavor instead of butter.

History

The sandwich is tied to Cuban workers and cigar-factory communities in Florida, notably Tampa and Miami, as well as to Havana. Its exact birthplace is debated between those cities. It remains a staple of cafés and lunch counters.
