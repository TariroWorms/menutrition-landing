Dates with gahwa is the classic hospitality pair of the Gulf: three dates served beside a small cup of light Arabic coffee brewed with cardamom. The coffee is pale gold, thin and slightly bitter, balanced by the honey-sweet dates. It is offered to guests on arrival, at majlis gatherings, at weddings and to break the fast in Ramadan.

What it is made of

datesarabic coffeecardamom

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml water
  • 2 tbsp lightly roasted Arabic coffee, ground
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • A few saffron threads (optional)
  • 12 dates

Method

  1. Bring the water to the boil, add the coffee and simmer on low heat for 10 minutes.
  2. Add the cardamom and saffron and simmer 2–3 minutes more.
  3. Let it settle, then pour into a dallah or thermos, leaving the grounds behind.
  4. Serve in small cups with three dates per person.

How to make it lighter

  • The coffee itself has almost no calories; the dates carry the energy.
  • Stick to three dates per round rather than refilling the plate.
  • Do not add sugar to the gahwa; the dates provide the sweetness.

History

Serving Arabic coffee with dates is a central custom of hospitality in the Arabian Peninsula; in 2015 Arabic coffee was inscribed on UNESCO’s list of intangible cultural heritage as a symbol of generosity.
