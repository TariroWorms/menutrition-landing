Klepe (Bosnian dumplings)

Origin: Bosnia and Herzegovina
570kcal

Klepe are small square dumplings filled with spiced minced beef and onion, boiled and served with garlic yoghurt and melted butter. They are soft, savory and cool with a tangy sauce. A pinch of paprika is often sprinkled on top.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 150 g ground beef
  • 1 onion
  • 300 g yoghurt
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 50 g butter
  • paprika, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Knead flour, eggs, salt and a little water into a firm dough.
  2. Mix the meat with grated onion and pepper.
  3. Roll out the dough thin and cut into small squares.
  4. Place a little filling on each and pinch the corners.
  5. Boil in salted water until they float, 8 minutes.
  6. Serve with garlic yoghurt and melted butter.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean meat and low-fat yoghurt.
  • Use less butter.
  • Add a side of vegetables.

History

They are related to the manti dumplings found across the Balkans, Turkey and Central Asia, and have long been a part of Bosnian home cooking. They are often prepared for family gatherings and weekends.
