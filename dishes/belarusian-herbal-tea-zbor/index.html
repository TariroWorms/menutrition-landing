Herbal tea (zyolki)

Origin: Belarus

Zyolki (literally 'little herbs') is the Belarusian name for home blends of dried herbs brewed like tea. Common mixes combine wild thyme, mint, linden blossom, St John's wort and berry leaves, giving a floral, herby cup. It is drunk hot, plain or with a spoon of honey, especially in the evening.

Recipe

4 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 l water
  • 2 tbsp dried wild thyme
  • 1 tbsp dried mint
  • 1 tbsp linden flowers
  • honey to taste

Method

  1. Warm a teapot with hot water.
  2. Put the herbs in the pot.
  3. Pour in water just off the boil.
  4. Cover and steep for 7-10 minutes.
  5. Strain and serve, adding honey if you like.

How to make it lighter

  • Drink it plain to keep it virtually calorie-free.
  • Check with a doctor about St John's wort if you take medication.
  • Serve iced in summer with lemon.

History

Gathering herbs in summer, especially around the Kupalle midsummer festival, is an old Belarusian village custom. The dried bunches were kept for winter teas.
