Oatmeal with milk is a simple porridge of rolled oats simmered in milk and water until thick and creamy. It is soft, mild and warming, and is usually eaten for breakfast, topped with fruit, cinnamon or a little sugar. The ratio of milk to water changes how rich and creamy it tastes.

What it is made of

Recipe

1 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 50 g rolled oats
  • 150 ml milk
  • 100 ml water
  • A pinch of salt
  • Cinnamon or fresh fruit to serve

Method

  1. Put the oats, milk, water and salt in a small pan.
  2. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, stirring.
  3. Cook for 4–5 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy.
  4. Rest for a minute and serve with cinnamon or fruit.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more water and less milk, or choose skimmed milk.
  • Sweeten with fresh berries or grated apple instead of sugar.
  • Weigh the dry oats — 50 g is a standard portion and easy to overpour.

History

Oat porridge has long been a staple in Scotland, Ireland and northern Europe, and today it is a common breakfast around the world.
