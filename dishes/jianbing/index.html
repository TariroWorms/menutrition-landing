Jianbing (Chinese crepe)

Origin: China · Tianjin / Shandong

Jianbing is a thin crepe made from wheat and mung bean batter, cooked on a griddle with an egg, scallions and cilantro. A crisp cracker is folded inside along with hoisin and chili sauce. It is a fast, filling breakfast bought from street vendors.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g flour and 30 g mung bean flour
  • 250 ml water
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 scallions and cilantro
  • 2 tbsp hoisin sauce, plus chili sauce to taste
  • 2 crisp crackers (baocui)

Method

  1. Whisk the flours with water into a thin smooth batter.
  2. Spread a thin layer on a hot oiled non-stick pan.
  3. Crack an egg over the crepe, spread it and sprinkle with scallions and cilantro.
  4. Flip, brush with hoisin and chili sauce and place the cracker inside.
  5. Fold the crepe around the cracker and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Use whole wheat flour for more fiber.
  • Skip the deep-fried cracker for a lighter version.
  • Add lettuce or vegetables inside.

History

Jianbing is a classic breakfast street food of northern China, especially associated with Tianjin and Shandong. Vendors spread the batter in a circle with a wooden tool and finish each one within a minute or two.
