Undhiyu (Gujarati winter mixed vegetables)

Origin: India · Gujarat
350kcal

Undhiyu is a rich mix of winter vegetables, such as surti papdi beans, yam, sweet potato, baby aubergines and potato, cooked with fenugreek dumplings and a fresh green coconut-coriander masala. The vegetables are tender and slightly smoky, with a sweet-savoury green taste and moderate heat. It is eaten with puri or flatbread, especially at the kite festival of Uttarayan.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g small potatoes and 200 g sweet potato
  • 150 g small aubergines
  • 150 g surti papdi or green beans
  • 100 g fenugreek dumplings (muthia)
  • 100 g grated coconut, 50 g peanuts, coriander, green chilli and ginger for the masala
  • 3 tbsp oil (45 ml), carom, salt

Method

  1. Grind coconut, coriander, chilli, ginger, peanuts and salt into a coarse green masala.
  2. Stuff the aubergines and small potatoes with some of the masala.
  3. Heat the oil with carom, add the beans, then the other vegetables and the rest of the masala.
  4. Cover and cook slowly for 40 minutes with a splash of water, shaking the pot instead of stirring.
  5. Add the lightly fried fenugreek dumplings for the last 10 minutes and serve with puri.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the dumplings instead of deep-frying.
  • Use 2 tablespoons of oil in total.
  • Serve with a bowl of plain yogurt.

History

The name is said to come from the Gujarati word for upside-down, as it was traditionally cooked in earthen pots buried upside-down over embers. Surat is especially famous for it.
