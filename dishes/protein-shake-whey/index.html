🥤 Whey Protein Shake

A whey protein shake is one scoop of whey protein powder shaken with milk, water or a mix of both until smooth. Made with milk it is creamy and milky; with water it is thinner and lighter. It is commonly drunk after training or as a quick, high-protein snack.

Recipe

1 servings3 min

Ingredients

  • 1 scoop whey protein powder (about 30 g)
  • 200 ml milk
  • 100 ml cold water
  • A few ice cubes (optional)

Method

  1. Pour the milk and water into a shaker bottle or blender.
  2. Add the protein powder on top of the liquid so it does not clump.
  3. Shake or blend for 20–30 seconds until smooth, add ice if you like and drink straight away.

How to make it lighter

  • Use all water or skimmed milk instead of whole milk for a lighter shake.
  • Stick to one level scoop; the scoop size on the label is the serving the nutrition facts refer to.
  • Choose an unflavoured or low-sugar powder and add cinnamon or a little fruit for flavour.

History

Whey, the liquid left over from cheesemaking, became widely available as a powder in the late 20th century, and shakes made from it are now a fixture of gyms and sports nutrition.
