🥕 Couscous with seven vegetables

Couscous with seven vegetables is a Moroccan couscous topped with a vegetable stew of carrots, zucchini, turnip, cabbage and chickpeas, often with pumpkin, onion and tomato to reach seven. The vegetables simmer in a light, spiced vegetable broth that is spooned over the fluffy grains. It is a colourful dish for sharing.

Recipe

6 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g couscous
  • 3 carrots, halved
  • 2 zucchini, in chunks
  • 2 turnips, quartered
  • 1/4 white cabbage, in wedges
  • 1 can chickpeas (400 g), drained
  • 2 onions and 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1.5 l vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, ginger, turmeric, saffron, salt

Method

  1. Soften the onion and tomato in oil with the spices.
  2. Add the broth, carrots, turnips and cabbage and simmer 25 minutes.
  3. Add the zucchini and chickpeas and cook 15 minutes more.
  4. Soak or steam the couscous with hot broth and fluff with a fork.
  5. Pile the couscous on a platter, arrange the vegetables on top and serve with broth.

How to make it lighter

  • Ladle extra broth and vegetables and take a smaller portion of couscous.
  • Use one tablespoon of oil instead of two.
  • Wholegrain couscous makes the plate more filling.

History

In Morocco, couscous with seven vegetables is a classic dish for the Friday family meal, and the number seven is often said to bring good luck. Recipes vary by region and season, so the exact vegetables change from family to family.
