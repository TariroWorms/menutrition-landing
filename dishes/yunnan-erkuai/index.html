Erkuai (Yunnan rice cakes)

Origin: China · Yunnan

Erkuai is made from steamed rice that is pounded into a smooth, dense dough and cut into thick slices or strips. They are stir-fried with pork, greens and sauce, or grilled and spread with sweet and chili sauces. The texture is soft, sticky and chewy.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g erkuai (rice cake slices)
  • 100 g pork, sliced
  • 150 g napa cabbage
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 scallion
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Soak the rice cake slices in water for 10 minutes if hard.
  2. Fry the pork in oil until browned.
  3. Add garlic and cabbage and stir-fry 2 minutes.
  4. Add the rice cakes, soy sauce and a splash of water.
  5. Toss 3–4 minutes until soft and glossy.
  6. Finish with scallion.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork and extra vegetables.
  • Cut the oil to a minimum.
  • Keep servings moderate: rice cakes are dense in carbohydrates.

History

Erkuai is a staple of Dali and Kunming in Yunnan and is often served as breakfast or a snack. It is similar to rice cakes found elsewhere in Asia, but uses a local rice variety.
