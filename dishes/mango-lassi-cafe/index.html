🥭 Mango Lassi (Café Style)

Café-style mango lassi is the version served in Indian restaurants and cafés: mango pulp blended with yogurt and a splash of milk, sweetened and scented with cardamom. Milk makes it slightly thinner and milder than a home-style lassi, while the mango keeps it thick and fruity. It comes ice-cold in a tall glass.

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g mango pulp
  • 200 g plain yogurt
  • 100 ml milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch ground cardamom
  • Ice cubes

Method

  1. Blend the mango, yogurt, milk, sugar and cardamom until smooth.
  2. Add a few ice cubes and blend briefly again.
  3. Pour into tall glasses and serve cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Ask for it without added sugar — canned mango pulp is often already sweetened.
  • Make it with skimmed milk and low-fat yogurt.
  • Treat a large glass as a dessert rather than a drink alongside one.

History

Lassi has long been a yogurt drink of Punjab and North India; the mango lassi with added milk is the version that spread through Indian restaurants in the UK, the Gulf and beyond, where it is now a menu standard.
