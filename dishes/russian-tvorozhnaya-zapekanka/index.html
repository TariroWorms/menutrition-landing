Tvorozhnaya zapekanka (cottage cheese bake)

Origin: Russia

Tvorozhnaya zapekanka is a simple oven-baked casserole of cottage cheese mixed with eggs, a little sugar and semolina, often with raisins. It bakes into a soft, slightly springy slab with a golden top, mildly sweet and tangy. It is eaten warm or cold for breakfast or as a dessert with sour cream, jam or condensed milk.

What it is made of

cottage cheeseeggsugarsemolinasour creamraisinsvanilla

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g cottage cheese
  • 3 eggs
  • 80 g sugar
  • 60 g semolina
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 50 g raisins
  • vanilla
  • butter for the dish

Method

  1. Mix the cottage cheese with eggs, sugar, sour cream and vanilla until fairly smooth.
  2. Stir in semolina and raisins and leave 15 minutes to swell.
  3. Spread in a buttered baking dish.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 40 minutes until set and golden.
  5. Cool slightly before cutting and serve with yogurt or fruit.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat cottage cheese and halve the sugar.
  • Serve with plain yogurt and berries instead of condensed milk.
  • Replace semolina with oat flakes for more fibre.

History

Zapekanka was a standard dish in Soviet kindergartens and school canteens and remains a favourite home breakfast. It is high in protein thanks to the cottage cheese.
