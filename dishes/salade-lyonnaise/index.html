🥗 Salade Lyonnaise

Salade lyonnaise is a classic bistro salad from Lyon: bitter, curly frisée tossed with warm crisp bacon lardons, crunchy croutons and a sharp vinaigrette, crowned with a soft poached egg. When the yolk breaks it coats the leaves, balancing the bitterness and the salty bacon. It is served as a starter or light lunch.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 head frisée (about 150 g), torn
  • 150 g bacon lardons
  • 2 eggs
  • 60 g bread croutons
  • 3 tbsp vinaigrette (oil, red vinegar, salt, pepper)

Method

  1. Fry the lardons in a dry pan until crisp.
  2. Poach the eggs in simmering water with a splash of vinegar for 3–4 minutes.
  3. Toss the frisée with the vinaigrette and the warm lardons.
  4. Add the croutons, top each plate with a poached egg and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the bacon and drain it well on paper.
  • Use fewer croutons or skip them.
  • Keep the dressing to 1 tbsp per plate.

History

It is a signature dish of Lyon's traditional bouchons, simple restaurants serving local cooking, and remains on bistro menus across France today.
