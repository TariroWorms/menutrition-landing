🍢 Alexandrian Kofta

Alexandrian kofta (kofta iskandarani) is minced beef mixed with grated onion, parsley, spices and a little breadcrumb, shaped into fingers, browned in a pan and then simmered in a tomato sauce. The result is juicy, saucy and warmly spiced. It is served with rice, bread or fries.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g minced beef
  • 1 onion, grated and squeezed
  • 3 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp each cumin and coriander, ½ tsp black pepper, salt
  • 400 g tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Mix the beef, onion, parsley, breadcrumbs and spices and knead 2 minutes.
  2. Shape into finger-sized kofta.
  3. Brown in the oil on all sides, then pour in the tomato sauce.
  4. Cover and simmer 15–20 minutes until cooked through and the sauce thickens.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced beef (5–10% fat).
  • Brown the kofta under the grill instead of in oil: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve with a big salad and a small portion of rice rather than fries.

History

Kofta iskandarani is a popular home and restaurant dish in Egypt, named after the port city of Alexandria. Reliable details of its origin are scarce; today it is eaten across Egypt as an everyday main course.
