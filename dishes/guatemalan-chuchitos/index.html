Chuchitos

Origin: Guatemala

Chuchitos are small, firm Guatemalan tamales made from corn masa, filled with a piece of chicken or pork and a spoonful of tomato-chile sauce. They are wrapped in dried corn husks and steamed. They taste of toasted corn with a savory, lightly spicy center, and are sold warm at markets.

Recipe

12 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g corn masa
  • 300 g chicken, cooked and cubed
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 2 guajillo chiles
  • 1 onion
  • 80 g lard or oil
  • corn husks, soaked
  • salt

Method

  1. Roast tomatoes, chiles and onion and blend into a sauce, cook 10 minutes.
  2. Mix masa with fat, broth and salt into a soft dough.
  3. Spread dough on a husk, add chicken and a spoon of sauce.
  4. Fold and tie the husk.
  5. Steam for 1 hour.
  6. Rest 10 minutes and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less fat in the dough.
  • Choose chicken breast.
  • Add a vegetable side to balance the meal.

History

Chuchitos are a traditional Guatemalan snack with Maya roots, simpler and sturdier than the larger banana-leaf tamales. They are typically eaten with a topping of grated cheese and a bit of salsa.
