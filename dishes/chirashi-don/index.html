🍱 Chirashi don

Chirashi don is a bowl of vinegared sushi rice topped with an assortment of sashimi, slices of sweet tamago omelette, shrimp, strips of nori and pickled ginger. Each bite mixes cool, silky fish with lightly sweet-sour rice. It is served with wasabi on the side and is a common lunch set at sushi bars.

Recipe

2 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked sushi rice, seasoned with rice vinegar, sugar and salt
  • 250 g assorted sashimi (e.g. tuna, salmon, yellowtail, squid)
  • 4 slices tamago (sweet rolled omelette)
  • 4 cooked shrimp
  • 1 sheet nori, shredded
  • 20 g pickled ginger
  • Wasabi to serve

Method

  1. Let the seasoned rice cool to room temperature and divide between two bowls.
  2. Slice the fish into bite-size pieces.
  3. Arrange the sashimi, tamago and shrimp over the rice.
  4. Scatter with nori, add the ginger and serve with a little wasabi.

How to make it lighter

  • Ask for less rice and more fish.
  • Season with wasabi and pickled ginger rather than extra sweetened rice.
  • Pick lean fish such as tuna and white fish over fatty salmon belly.

History

Chirashi don is the bowl form of chirashizushi, a long-standing Japanese sushi style. Sushi bars often use it to serve a varied selection of the day's fish over rice.
