Zrazy with mushrooms

Origin: Belarus

Zrazy are oval meat patties with a surprise inside, here a filling of fried mushrooms and onion. The mince is mixed with soaked bread so the patties stay soft, then they are rolled in breadcrumbs and fried. They are often finished in a little sauce and served with potatoes or buckwheat.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g minced pork
  • 200 g minced beef
  • 1 slice of bread (40 g) soaked in milk
  • 1 egg
  • 250 g mushrooms
  • 1 onion (80 g)
  • 60 g breadcrumbs
  • oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Fry the chopped onion and mushrooms until dry and golden; season and cool.
  2. Mix both minces with the squeezed bread, egg, salt and pepper.
  3. Divide into 8 portions, flatten each, place mushrooms in the centre and close into an oval.
  4. Roll in breadcrumbs.
  5. Fry in a little oil for 4–5 minutes per side, then cover and cook gently for 5 more minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef or turkey and less pork.
  • Bake on a lined tray at 200 °C instead of frying.
  • Serve with buckwheat and vegetables.

History

Zrazy came to Belarusian cooking through the shared culinary tradition of the old Polish–Lithuanian state, where they were a gentry dish. Mushroom fillings suit Belarus, a country rich in forests.
