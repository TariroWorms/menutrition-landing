🍖 Greek lamb chops

Greek lamb chops, known as paidakia, are small rib chops marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic and dried oregano, then grilled fast over high heat. The fat crisps and chars at the edges while the meat stays pink and juicy. They are eaten with the fingers, straight off the bone, with lemon wedges and a simple salad or roasted vegetables.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg lamb rib chops (about 12)
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon, plus lemon wedges to serve
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper.
  2. Coat the chops in the marinade and leave for at least 30 minutes, or up to 4 hours in the fridge.
  3. Heat a grill or griddle pan until very hot.
  4. Grill the chops for 2–3 minutes per side for pink meat, until the fat is crisp and charred.
  5. Rest for 3 minutes, sprinkle with more oregano and serve with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the thick outer fat before grilling — the meat stays juicy and the plate gets noticeably lighter.
  • Let the marinade drip off before the chops go on the grill; each tablespoon of olive oil is about 120 kcal.
  • Count three chops as a portion and fill the rest of the plate with a tomato and cucumber salad or grilled vegetables.

History

Grilled lamb is one of the oldest festive foods in Greece, where sheep farming has shaped mountain cooking for centuries. Today paidakia are a fixture of village tavernas and family grills, especially around Easter and summer gatherings.
