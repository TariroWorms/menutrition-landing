🍰 Red velvet cake

Red velvet cake is a soft, fine-crumbed layer cake with a deep red colour, a light cocoa flavour and a gentle tang from buttermilk. The layers are sandwiched and covered with a rich cream cheese frosting, whose sweetness contrasts with the slightly acidic sponge. It is a popular celebration cake, served in slices.

Recipe

12 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain flour
  • 25 g cocoa powder
  • 250 g sugar
  • 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp vinegar, a pinch of salt
  • 240 ml buttermilk
  • 150 g butter, melted, and 2 eggs
  • 1–2 tsp red food colouring
  • 400 g cream cheese frosting

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 175 °C and line two 20 cm tins.
  2. Whisk the flour, cocoa, sugar, baking soda and salt together.
  3. Whisk the buttermilk, melted butter, eggs, vinegar and food colouring, then stir into the dry ingredients until smooth.
  4. Divide between the tins and bake for 25–30 minutes; cool completely.
  5. Sandwich and cover the layers with the cream cheese frosting.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut thinner slices — a 60 g slice instead of 100 g makes a real difference.
  • Spread the frosting thinly between layers and skip the frosting on the sides.
  • Share a slice or pair a small piece with fresh berries.

History

Red velvet cake is widely associated with the American South, though its exact origin is disputed. Cocoa "velvet" cakes appear in American cookbooks around the turn of the 20th century, and the bright red colour and cream cheese frosting became the familiar standard later in the century.
