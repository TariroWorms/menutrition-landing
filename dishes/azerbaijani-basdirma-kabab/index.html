Basdirma kabab (marinated lamb kebab)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Basdirma kabab is lamb that has been pressed into a marinade of sliced onion, salt, pepper and sometimes a dash of vinegar or pomegranate juice for many hours before grilling. The long marinade makes the meat especially tender and slightly tangy. After grilling over coals, it is piled onto lavash and topped with fresh onion rings dusted with sumac.

What it is made of

lambonionsumacvinegarblack pepper

Recipe

4 servings720 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg lamb leg or loin, in chunks
  • 3 onions (450 g), sliced
  • 1 tbsp wine or apple vinegar
  • 1 red onion and 1 tsp sumac
  • lavash, herbs
  • salt, black pepper

Method

  1. Layer the meat with sliced onion, salt and pepper in a bowl and sprinkle with vinegar.
  2. Press down with a plate and weight and marinate 8–12 hours in the fridge.
  3. Shake off the onion and thread the lamb on skewers.
  4. Grill over glowing coals for 12–15 minutes, turning often.
  5. Serve on lavash with red onion rings, sumac and herbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Pick lean cuts and trim the fat before marinating.
  • Pair with grilled vegetables instead of extra bread.
  • Pomegranate seeds add flavour without fat.

History

Basdirma comes from the Azerbaijani verb for pressing down, describing how the meat is weighed down in its marinade. It is a favourite of picnics and family barbecues.
