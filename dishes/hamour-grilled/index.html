🐟 Grilled hamour

Grilled hamour is Gulf grouper cooked whole or as thick fillets over charcoal or under a hot grill, seasoned simply with garlic, cumin, lemon and a brush of olive oil. The firm white flesh flakes in large, sweet pieces and picks up a light smoky char. It is served with lemon, salad and rice, and is the lighter way to enjoy the Gulf's favourite fish.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole hamour (about 1.2 kg), cleaned and scored, or 800 g thick fillets
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1½ tsp ground cumin
  • Juice of 2 lemons, plus wedges to serve
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the garlic, cumin, lemon juice, olive oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Rub the mixture over the fish and into the cuts; marinate for 20 minutes.
  3. Grill over medium-hot coals or under a grill, 8–10 minutes per side for a whole fish (4–5 for fillets), until the flesh flakes easily.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Grilled hamour is already lean; the extras matter most — keep oil to the marinade only.
  • Pair with a large salad and a small portion of rice.
  • Use lemon and herbs instead of creamy or oily sauces at the table.

History

Hamour is one of the most valued catches of the Arabian Gulf, and grilling fresh fish over coals is a traditional coastal method. Grilled hamour is a staple of seafood restaurants across the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain and Kuwait today.
