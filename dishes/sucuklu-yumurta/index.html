🍳 Sucuklu Yumurta (Eggs with Sucuk)

Origin: Turkey

Sucuklu yumurta is a classic Turkish breakfast of eggs fried together with slices of sucuk, a dry, garlicky and spicy beef sausage. The sausage releases its red, paprika-tinted fat into the pan, and the eggs cook in it until the whites set and the yolks stay soft. It is usually served straight from a small two-handled pan as part of a larger breakfast spread.

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 120 g beef sucuk, thinly sliced
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tsp olive oil (optional)
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Pinch of chili flakes
  • Chopped parsley to serve

Method

  1. Heat a small pan over medium heat and add the sucuk slices, with a little oil only if the pan is dry.
  2. Fry for 1–2 minutes until the slices curl and release their fat.
  3. Crack the eggs between the slices.
  4. Cover and cook for 3–4 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft.
  5. Season with pepper, chili flakes and parsley and serve straight from the pan.

How to make it lighter

  • Sucuk is already salty and fatty, so skip the extra oil — the sausage provides enough fat for frying.
  • Use 60 g of sucuk for two people and add sliced tomatoes or peppers to the pan for volume.
  • Swap one whole egg for two egg whites to lower the fat while keeping the portion size.

History

Sucuk is a fermented, air-dried sausage eaten across Turkey, the Balkans and the Middle East, and frying it with eggs is one of the most common ways it is served at a Turkish breakfast table. Today the dish is a staple of home kitchens and breakfast cafés throughout Turkey.
