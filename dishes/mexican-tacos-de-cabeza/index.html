Tacos de cabeza (beef head tacos)

Origin: Mexico

Tacos de cabeza use meat from a whole steamed beef head, and customers order by cut: cachete (cheek), lengua (tongue), sesos (brain), ojo (eye) or a mix called surtida. The meat is chopped on the board and tucked into small steamed corn tortillas. Onion, cilantro, lime and a sharp green or red salsa cut through its richness.

Recipe

4 servings240 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg beef cheek and tongue
  • 1 onion, halved, plus 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 24 small corn tortillas
  • 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
  • salsa verde and lime to serve

Method

  1. Rinse the meat and place it in a large pot with the halved onion, garlic, bay leaves and salt.
  2. Cover with water and simmer gently, or steam, for 3–3½ hours until very tender.
  3. Peel the tongue and pull or chop the meat into small pieces.
  4. Keep the meat moist with a little cooking liquid.
  5. Steam the tortillas briefly and fill with meat.
  6. Top with chopped onion, cilantro and salsa; serve with lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose tongue and cheek and skip the fattier cuts.
  • Chill the broth and lift off the fat before reusing it.
  • Eat two tacos with a salad of radish and cucumber instead of four.

History

Using the whole animal is an old habit in Mexican cooking, and head meat has long been steamed in large pots at taquerías and markets. The tacos are especially common as a late-night and weekend street food in central Mexico.
