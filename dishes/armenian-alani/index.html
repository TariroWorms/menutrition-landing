Alani (walnut-stuffed dried peaches)

Origin: Armenia
136kcal

Alani are whole peaches that are pitted, flattened and sun-dried, then filled with ground walnuts mixed with sugar and sometimes cinnamon. The fruit becomes chewy and intensely sweet-sour, and the nuts add crunch and richness. They are eaten as a snack or sweet with tea, and often appear on New Year tables. A few pieces go a long way.

Recipe

12 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 12 dried flattened peaches (300 g)
  • 150 g walnuts
  • 50 g sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • hot water for softening

Method

  1. If the peaches are hard, pour hot water over them for 5 minutes and pat dry.
  2. Grind the walnuts coarsely with sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Open each peach where the pit was removed.
  4. Fill with about a tablespoon of the walnut mixture and press closed.
  5. Leave them to firm up for a day on a rack or in the fridge, then serve with tea.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sugar; the dried peaches are sweet enough.
  • Serve one piece with plain yogurt as a balanced dessert.
  • Store in a jar in the fridge to keep the walnuts fresh.

History

Armenia's Ararat Valley is known for peaches and apricots, and drying stuffed fruit is a traditional way to keep the harvest. Alani are sold at Yerevan markets alongside sujukh and other dried sweets.
