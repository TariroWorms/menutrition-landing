🥪 Vegetable Sandwich

Origin: International · café classic

A simple vegetable sandwich layers crisp cucumber, ripe tomato and lettuce on soft bread spread with tangy labneh. The strained yogurt cheese replaces butter or mayonnaise, giving a creamy, slightly sour base that keeps the vegetables fresh and juicy. It is eaten as a quick lunch, a school snack or a light breakfast, often with a pinch of dried mint.

Recipe

1 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread (about 80 g)
  • 40 g labneh
  • ½ cucumber (60 g), sliced
  • 1 small tomato (70 g), sliced
  • 2–3 lettuce leaves
  • Pinch of salt and dried mint

Method

  1. Spread the labneh evenly over both slices of bread.
  2. Layer lettuce, cucumber and tomato on one slice.
  3. Season with salt and dried mint, close the sandwich and cut in half.

How to make it lighter

  • Use thinly sliced bread or one open-faced slice to cut the bread portion.
  • Spread labneh thinly: about 1 tbsp per slice is enough for flavour.
  • Pile on extra cucumber, tomato and lettuce for volume with very few calories.

History

Sandwiches of bread and fresh vegetables are eaten almost everywhere; the version with labneh is especially common in Levantine and Gulf cafés and home kitchens.
