🥣 Haydari

Origin: Turkey

Haydari is a thick Turkish meze of strained yogurt mixed with crushed garlic, chopped dill and walnuts. It is cool, tangy and creamy, with a little crunch from the nuts. It is served chilled on meze platters or alongside grilled meats.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g strained yogurt
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 30 g walnuts, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Stir the yogurt until smooth.
  2. Mix in the garlic, dill, most of the walnuts and a pinch of salt.
  3. Spread on a plate, drizzle with olive oil and scatter the rest of the walnuts on top.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat strained yogurt: the dip stays thick and tangy.
  • Keep the walnuts as a light topping — they are the most calorie-dense part.
  • Scoop it with cucumber and carrot sticks.

History

Haydari is one of the classic cold mezes of Turkish meyhane taverns and kebab houses, and it is especially popular in Istanbul and along the Aegean coast. Similar strained-yogurt dips are found throughout the eastern Mediterranean.
