Chongqing xiaomian (spicy noodles)

Origin: China · Chongqing
525kcal

Xiaomian, literally small noodles, is the everyday breakfast and snack of Chongqing. A bowl is built from a spoonful of chili oil, Sichuan pepper, garlic, soy sauce and lard or oil, to which a ladle of hot noodle water is added. Fresh noodles go in, topped with greens, peanuts and scallion. It is intensely hot and numbing.

Recipe

1 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g fresh wheat noodles
  • 2 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tsp ground Sichuan pepper
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 50 g leafy greens
  • 15 g crushed peanuts

Method

  1. Put chili oil, Sichuan pepper, garlic, soy sauce and a pinch of sugar in a bowl.
  2. Boil the noodles with the greens for 2-3 minutes.
  3. Add a ladle of the cooking water to the bowl.
  4. Lift in the noodles and greens and mix well.
  5. Top with peanuts and scallion and eat hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less chili oil and more broth for fewer calories.
  • Choose whole-wheat noodles for fibre.
  • Add a soft egg or tofu for protein.

History

Small noodle stalls were part of Chongqing's working-class street life, feeding dock workers and porters along the rivers. The dish remains a symbol of the city's food.
