🥤 Orange soda

Orange soda is a sweet, fizzy soft drink made from carbonated water, sugar and orange juice concentrate. It is bright, sugary and lightly tangy, with a much sweeter taste than fresh orange juice. It is sold chilled in cans and bottles and is a common drink with fast food, at parties and with family meals.

sparkling watersugarorange juice concentrate

Recipe

1 servings2 min

Ingredients

  • 330 ml chilled orange soda
  • Ice cubes
  • 1 slice of fresh orange (optional)

Method

  1. Chill the can or bottle well before serving.
  2. Fill a glass with ice.
  3. Pour the soda over the ice and garnish with an orange slice.

How to make it lighter

  • A 330 ml can is about 150 kcal, almost all from sugar — count it like a snack.
  • Mix half soda with half sparkling water and fresh orange slices for the same fizz with half the sugar.
  • Choose a small glass over ice instead of a large bottle.

History

Orange-flavoured sodas are produced by many soft-drink makers and sold in almost every country, often in a standard 330 ml can.
