Mung bean soup (lu dou tang)

Origin: China

Lu dou tang is a light dessert soup of mung beans boiled until they burst, sweetened with a little sugar and sometimes scented with dried tangerine peel. The texture is soft and slightly grainy and the taste is mild and sweet. It is drunk chilled in summer to cool the body and warm in cooler weather.

What it is made of

mung beansugardried tangerine peel

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g dried mung beans
  • 1.2 litres water
  • 60 g sugar
  • 1 piece dried tangerine peel

Method

  1. Rinse the mung beans and soak for 1 hour.
  2. Bring beans, water and tangerine peel to a boil.
  3. Simmer for 40 minutes until the beans burst and the soup thickens.
  4. Stir in the sugar until dissolved.
  5. Serve warm or chill in the refrigerator.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by half and use a little honey or none.
  • Keep the beans in the soup for extra protein and fibre.
  • Serve a small bowl as a dessert in place of a richer sweet.

History

Mung bean soup is a traditional summer refresher across China, valued in folk belief as a cooling food. It is a common home dessert and is also sold in bowls and bottles.
