Chicken and waffles pairs crisp, deep-fried chicken with a warm golden waffle, finished with a pat of butter and a generous pour of maple syrup. The combination plays salty and crunchy against soft, sweet and buttery, which is the whole point of the dish. It is served for brunch or late-night meals, often with hot sauce on the side.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 8 chicken thighs or drumsticks
  • 250 ml milk mixed with 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 200 g plain flour, seasoned with salt, pepper and paprika
  • Oil for frying
  • Waffles: 250 g plain flour, 2 tsp baking powder, 1 tbsp sugar, 2 eggs, 350 ml milk, 60 g melted butter
  • 20 g butter, to serve
  • 60 ml maple syrup

Method

  1. Soak the chicken in the milk and lemon mixture for at least 1 hour.
  2. Dredge in seasoned flour and fry at 165 °C for 12–15 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  3. Whisk the waffle ingredients into a smooth batter.
  4. Cook in a hot waffle iron until crisp and golden.
  5. Serve the chicken on the waffles with a little butter and maple syrup.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the coated chicken instead of deep-frying.
  • Measure the syrup: 1 tbsp maple syrup ≈ 50 kcal, and a light drizzle is enough.
  • Share one waffle between two and add fruit on the side.

History

Waffles with chicken appeared in the American South and in Pennsylvania Dutch cooking in the nineteenth century, but the fried-chicken version is most closely associated with Harlem, where it became a late-night supper club favourite in the 1930s. It later spread through soul food restaurants and brunch menus across the United States.
