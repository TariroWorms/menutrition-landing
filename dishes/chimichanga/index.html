A chimichanga is a large flour tortilla wrapped around a filling of seasoned beef, rice and cheese, then deep-fried until crisp and golden. Inside it stays soft and savoury, outside it crackles. It is served with salsa on top or alongside, as a filling Tex-Mex main course.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 400 g beef, minced or shredded
  • 150 g cooked rice
  • 100 g grated cheese
  • 1 onion, 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika, salt
  • 200 ml tomato salsa
  • Vegetable oil for frying

Method

  1. Fry the onion and garlic, add the beef and spices and cook until browned. Stir in 3 tbsp salsa.
  2. Warm the tortillas so they fold easily.
  3. Spoon rice, beef and cheese onto each tortilla, fold in the sides and roll up tightly.
  4. Fry seam-side down in hot oil, turning, until golden all over. Drain on paper.
  5. Serve with the remaining salsa.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush with a little oil and bake at 200 °C for 20 minutes instead of deep-frying.
  • Use lean beef and half the cheese.
  • Eat half a chimichanga with a big salad.

History

The chimichanga is a Tex-Mex dish usually credited to Arizona, where more than one restaurant claims to have invented it by accidentally dropping a burrito into hot oil. The story is popular but hard to confirm.
