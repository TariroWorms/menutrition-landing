Makroudh of Kairouan (date semolina diamonds)

Origin: Tunisia · Kairouan

Makroudh are diamond-shaped pastries made from a semolina dough wrapped around a date paste, fried until golden and soaked in honey. They are dense, soft inside and pleasantly sticky outside. They are eaten with mint tea or coffee.

What it is made of

Recipe

12 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g fine semolina
  • 150 ml olive oil, for the dough
  • 300 g date paste
  • 1 tsp orange zest
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Oil for frying
  • 200 g honey
  • Water as needed

Method

  1. Rub the semolina with olive oil, then add water to make a firm dough and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the date paste with zest and cinnamon.
  3. Roll the dough into a log, press a groove, fill with date paste and close.
  4. Flatten slightly and cut into diamonds.
  5. Fry in hot oil until golden, then drain.
  6. Dip the hot pieces in warm honey and cool.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake instead of frying to save fat.
  • Use less honey syrup and let it drain.
  • Keep the portion to one or two pieces.

History

The city of Kairouan is famous for this pastry and sells it in specialty shops. It is a traditional treat for religious holidays and family celebrations across Tunisia.
