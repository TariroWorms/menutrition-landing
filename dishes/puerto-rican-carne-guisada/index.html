Carne guisada puertorriqueña (Puerto Rican beef stew)

Origin: Puerto Rico

Carne guisada is a slow-simmered stew of beef chunks and potatoes in a tomato and sofrito sauce with olives and a few capers. The meat turns tender and the broth thickens to a savory gravy. It is served over white rice, sometimes with beans, as everyday home comfort food.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g beef chuck, cubed
  • 400 g potatoes
  • 100 g sofrito
  • 3 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 10 olives and 1 tbsp capers
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 500 ml water or broth
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season the beef and brown it in batches in oil.
  2. Add the sofrito, garlic and oregano and cook for 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the tomato sauce, olives, capers and water.
  4. Cover and simmer gently for 1 hour.
  5. Add the potatoes and cook 30 minutes more until tender.
  6. Adjust the salt and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean cuts and skim off fat before serving.
  • Add carrots and peas for extra vegetables.
  • Serve over brown rice or with a green salad.

History

Beef stew with sofrito is a staple of Puerto Rican home cooking, shaped by Spanish stewing traditions and local seasonings.
