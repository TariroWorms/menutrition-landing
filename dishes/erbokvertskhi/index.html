Erbokvertskhi (eggs fried in ghee)

Origin: Georgia
345kcal

2 gCarbs1 g / 100 g

Erbokvertskhi joins two Georgian words: erbo, clarified butter, and kvertskhi, egg. The eggs are fried in a small pan or clay ketsi in plenty of sizzling ghee, which gives crisp lacy edges and a nutty taste. It is a classic village breakfast, eaten with warm bread dipped into the yolk and butter.

What it is made of

egggheesalt

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 30 g ghee
  • Salt
  • Bread to serve

Method

  1. Heat the ghee in a small pan or ketsi over medium-high heat until it shimmers.
  2. Crack the eggs gently into the hot fat.
  3. Spoon the ghee over the whites so they set while the yolks stay soft.
  4. Season with salt once the edges turn golden and crisp.
  5. Serve immediately in the pan with warm bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and a teaspoon of ghee instead of a deep layer.
  • Add sliced tomatoes or greens to the pan for fibre.
  • Pair with wholegrain bread and limit yourself to one slice.

History

Ghee has long been a staple fat in Georgian households, especially in the mountains, where it kept well through the winter. Erbokvertskhi is one of the simplest ways it is used and remains a popular home breakfast.
