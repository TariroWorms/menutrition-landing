Choereg (Armenian Easter bread)

Origin: Armenia

Choereg is a lightly sweet braided bread with a soft, tender crumb. Its special aroma comes from mahlab, ground cherry kernels, and sometimes a touch of fennel. The shiny egg-washed loaves are sprinkled with sesame seeds and eaten at Easter with tea, cheese or coloured eggs.

Recipe

10 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g flour
  • 10 g dry yeast
  • 200 ml warm milk
  • 120 g butter, melted
  • 2 eggs plus 1 for brushing
  • 100 g sugar
  • 1 tsp ground mahlab
  • salt
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds

Method

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk with a spoon of sugar and wait until foamy.
  2. Mix in the eggs, butter, remaining sugar, mahlab, salt and flour and knead into a smooth soft dough.
  3. Let it rise in a warm place for about 1.5 hours until doubled.
  4. Divide into portions, roll into ropes and braid into loaves.
  5. Let the braids rise 30 minutes, brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with sesame.
  6. Bake at 180 °C for 25–30 minutes until deep golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Enjoy a thin slice with fresh cheese and fruit for a balanced breakfast.
  • Replace a third of the butter with a neutral oil to lower saturated fat.
  • Freeze extra slices so you are not tempted to eat the whole loaf.

History

Braided enriched breads for Easter are shared by many Christian communities of the Eastern Mediterranean, and choereg is the Armenian version. It is also baked in diaspora communities for other church holidays.
