Tapado (Garifuna seafood and coconut stew)

Origin: Honduras · Garifuna

Tapado is a Garifuna seafood stew cooked in coconut milk with fish, shrimp, green banana, plantain and yuca. Garlic, cilantro and a whole chili give it fragrance without too much heat. It is served in bowls with coconut bread or rice.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g firm white fish
  • 250 g shrimp
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 3 green plantains
  • 300 g cassava
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 whole chili
  • Cilantro and salt

Method

  1. Simmer onion, garlic and chili in coconut milk and a little water.
  2. Add cassava and plantain.
  3. Cook 20 minutes until nearly tender.
  4. Add fish and shrimp.
  5. Simmer 10 more minutes without stirring hard.
  6. Season and add cilantro.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk.
  • Add more fish and less root starch for protein.
  • Serve with a green salad.

History

Tapado comes from the Garifuna communities of the Honduran Caribbean coast, who preserved African and Caribbean cooking traditions. It is often prepared on Sundays and during community celebrations.
