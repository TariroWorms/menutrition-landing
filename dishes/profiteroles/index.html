Profiteroles are small, hollow choux pastry puffs split and filled with vanilla ice cream, then covered with warm chocolate sauce. The contrast is the point: crisp, airy pastry, cold creamy ice cream and glossy hot chocolate. They are a classic bistro and restaurant dessert, usually served three to a plate.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 125 ml water
  • 50 g butter
  • 70 g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • Pinch of salt
  • 500 ml vanilla ice cream
  • 100 g dark chocolate
  • 100 ml cream

Method

  1. Bring water, butter and salt to the boil, add the flour all at once and stir over the heat for 1–2 minutes until the dough leaves the pan.
  2. Cool for 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs one at a time until smooth and glossy.
  3. Pipe 18 small mounds onto a lined tray and bake at 200 °C for 20–25 minutes until puffed and golden; cool completely.
  4. Melt the chocolate with the cream over low heat, stirring until smooth.
  5. Split each puff, fill with a small scoop of ice cream and pour over the warm sauce just before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve two puffs instead of three and use small ice cream scoops.
  • Drizzle the chocolate sauce in a thin line rather than covering the puffs.
  • Add fresh berries on the plate so a smaller portion feels like a full dessert.

History

Filled choux pastry puffs have long been a staple of French pâtisserie. Serving them with ice cream and chocolate sauce is the version best known on restaurant menus today.
