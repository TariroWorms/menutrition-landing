Guarapo (fresh sugarcane juice)

Origin: Cuba

Guarapo is the green-gold juice pressed straight from sugarcane stalks. It tastes grassy, sweet and refreshing, and a little lime balances the sugar. Street vendors in Cuba run the canes through a roller press and pour the juice over ice on hot days.

What it is made of

sugarcane juicelime

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg peeled sugarcane stalks (about 500 ml juice)
  • 1 lime
  • ice cubes

Method

  1. Cut the peeled cane into pieces that fit a juicer or roller press.
  2. Press the cane and collect the juice.
  3. Strain the juice through a fine sieve to remove any fibres.
  4. Add a squeeze of lime juice.
  5. Pour over ice and drink right away.

How to make it lighter

  • Drink it in a small glass because it is high in natural sugar.
  • Dilute with sparkling water for a lighter drink.
  • Drink it fresh, since the juice darkens and ferments quickly.

History

Sugarcane has been the backbone of Cuban agriculture since colonial times. Drinking the juice fresh from the press is an everyday habit across the Caribbean and Latin America.
