🌶️ Rocoto Relleno

Origin: Peru · Arequipa

Rocoto relleno is Arequipa's famous stuffed pepper: fiery red rocoto peppers, blanched to tame the heat, filled with minced beef cooked with onion and raisins, then topped with cheese and an egg mixture and baked. It is savoury-sweet, spicy and rich, with melted cheese on top. It is traditionally served with a slice of potato cake.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 4 rocoto peppers
  • 400 g minced beef
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 40 g raisins
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 100 g cheese, sliced
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cut tops off the peppers and remove seeds and veins; blanch 3 times in fresh boiling water with salt and sugar to reduce the heat.
  2. Fry the onion and garlic in the oil, add the beef and cumin and brown; stir in the raisins.
  3. Fill the peppers with the beef mixture.
  4. Top each with cheese and pour over the beaten eggs.
  5. Bake at 180 °C for about 25 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced beef to cut the fat noticeably.
  • Reduce the cheese to a thin slice per pepper — about 25 g.
  • Serve with salad instead of a large potato side.

History

Rocoto relleno is the signature dish of Arequipa in southern Peru and is a staple of the city's traditional picanterías. Its exact origins are not documented, but it is considered part of the region's culinary identity.
