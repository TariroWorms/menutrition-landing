Lamb under the peka (Janjetina ispod čripnje)

Origin: Croatia
595kcal

This dish is lamb and potatoes roasted slowly under a bell-shaped metal lid covered with hot embers. The meat turns very tender and the potatoes soak up the juices and smoke. It is flavoured with garlic, rosemary and olive oil.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg lamb pieces
  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 6 cloves garlic
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Rub the lamb with garlic, salt, pepper and oil.
  2. Place the meat in a baking dish with rosemary.
  3. Add quartered potatoes around it.
  4. Cover with the metal lid and pile hot embers on top.
  5. Cook for about 2 hours, refreshing the embers.
  6. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the lamb.
  • Add more vegetables and fewer potatoes.
  • An oven with a tight lid works at home.

History

The method is a traditional way of cooking in Croatia, especially in Dalmatia and the islands, where the bell is placed directly on the hearth. It is usually made for family gatherings and holidays, and needs to be ordered ahead in restaurants.
