Tom Yum Chicken is a clear, hot and sour Thai soup. Sliced chicken breast and mushrooms cook briefly in a broth scented with lemongrass, galangal and kaffir lime leaves, then the bowl is sharpened with fish sauce and lime juice and fresh chili. The result is light, fiery and aromatic, served hot on its own or with jasmine rice.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g sliced chicken breast
  • 1 l water
  • 3 stalks lemongrass, cut and bruised
  • 5 slices galangal
  • 4 kaffir lime leaves, torn
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 200 g mushrooms, halved
  • 3–4 fresh chilies, crushed
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • Salt and coriander leaves

Method

  1. Simmer the water with lemongrass and galangal and lime leaves for 10 minutes.
  2. Add the mushrooms and chilies and cook for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the sliced chicken breast and simmer just until cooked.
  4. Off the heat, season with fish sauce and lime juice and a little salt; taste and adjust.
  5. Serve hot with coriander on top.

How to make it lighter

  • The clear broth is naturally light; adding more mushrooms makes it more filling.
  • Skip or measure the rice served with it.
  • Order the clear version rather than the creamy one if eating out.

History

Tom yum comes from central Thailand, where hot and sour broths are an everyday dish; the name joins 'tom' (to boil) and 'yum' (a mixed, tangy salad-like flavour). It is now one of Thailand's most recognised dishes worldwide.
