Keema matar is a North Indian and Pakistani curry of minced lamb cooked with green peas in a thick, spiced tomato and onion base. Garam masala and green chili give it warmth, and the peas add sweetness and colour. It is fairly dry rather than soupy and is served with rice or flatbread, often garnished with fresh coriander.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced lamb
  • 200 g green peas
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1½ tsp garam masala, ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1–2 green chilies, slit
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt, fresh coriander

Method

  1. Fry the onions in oil until golden, then add the ginger-garlic paste and chilies for 1 minute.
  2. Add the minced lamb and cook, breaking it up, until browned.
  3. Stir in the tomatoes, turmeric, garam masala and salt; cook until the oil separates, about 10 minutes.
  4. Add the peas and a splash of water, cover and simmer for 10 minutes. Finish with coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced lamb and pour off any fat after browning.
  • Cook with 1 tbsp oil instead of 2 — 1 tbsp is about 120 kcal.
  • Add extra peas or diced vegetables and serve with a modest portion of rice.

History

Keema, meaning minced meat, is a long-standing part of Mughal-influenced cooking in North India and Pakistan, and pairing it with seasonal peas is a common home-style variation. Today keema matar is everyday family food across the region and on Indian restaurant menus worldwide.
