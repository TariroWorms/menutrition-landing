Tarag (Mongolian fermented yogurt)

Tarag is a thick yogurt made from cow, yak or goat milk. It is mildly sour and creamy and is eaten plain or with sugar. It is a staple of summer and a refreshing snack.

What it is made of

milkyogurt

Recipe

4 servings500 min

Ingredients

  • 1 l whole milk
  • 2 tbsp live yogurt as starter

Method

  1. Heat the milk to 40 C.
  2. Stir in the starter.
  3. Cover and keep warm for 8 hours.
  4. Chill in the fridge.
  5. Serve plain or with berries.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat milk.
  • Skip the sugar and add fruit.
  • Choose it as a probiotic breakfast.

History

Dairy products are central to the nomadic diet in Mongolia, and tarag is among the most common. It is traditionally made in clay or wood vessels.
