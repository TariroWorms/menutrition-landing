☕ Chai latte

A chai latte is black tea brewed with warming spices — cardamom, cinnamon and ginger — then sweetened and topped up with steamed, frothy milk. It is creamier and milder than traditional Indian masala chai and is served hot in a large cup, usually as a café drink in the afternoon.

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 250 ml water
  • 2 tsp loose black tea
  • 4 cardamom pods, lightly crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 thin slices fresh ginger
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 300 ml milk

Method

  1. Simmer the water with the cardamom, cinnamon and ginger for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tea and sugar, simmer 2 minutes, then strain.
  3. Heat and froth the milk.
  4. Pour the spiced tea into cups and top with the steamed milk.

How to make it lighter

  • Sugar adds up quickly — 1 tsp is about 16 kcal, so halve it or skip it and lean on the spices.
  • Use skimmed or semi-skimmed milk to cut fat without losing the foam.
  • Order a small size: a 300 ml cup and a 450 ml cup differ by roughly half again in calories.

History

The chai latte is a café adaptation of Indian masala chai, which is boiled with milk and spices; the latte version with steamed milk became popular in Western coffee shops from the 1990s.
