Sancocho de pescado (Colombian fish sancocho)

Origin: Colombia · Caribbean coast

On Colombia's Caribbean coast, sancocho is often made with fish instead of meat. Thick steaks of a firm white fish such as bocachico or snapper are poached in a broth enriched with coconut milk, together with green plantain and yuca. The result is mild, slightly sweet and creamy, and it is eaten with coconut rice and a wedge of lime.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg firm white fish steaks
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 2 green plantains
  • 400 g yuca
  • 1 onion, 1 tomato, 2 garlic cloves
  • Cilantro and lime

Method

  1. Season the fish with lime, salt and garlic and set aside.
  2. Sauté onion and tomato, add 1.5 l water, the plantain chunks and yuca, and cook 20 minutes.
  3. Pour in the coconut milk and bring back to a gentle simmer.
  4. Lay the fish in the pot and poach 10–12 minutes without stirring.
  5. Finish with cilantro and serve with lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk or half milk, half water.
  • Add more broth and fish and less yuca for a lighter bowl.
  • Serve with a salad instead of coconut rice.

History

Coconut and fish are the backbone of cooking in coastal towns such as Cartagena and the islands, so the national sancocho naturally took this form there. It is a common lunch at beach kitchens and family gatherings.
