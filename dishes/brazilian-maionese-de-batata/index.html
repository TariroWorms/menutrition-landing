Maionese de batata (Brazilian potato salad)

Origin: Brazil

Maionese de batata is a cold side of diced boiled potato and carrot with peas, folded into mayonnaise. Despite its name, the mayonnaise is only the dressing; the bulk is soft vegetables. It tastes mild, creamy and slightly sweet from the carrot. Brazilians serve it next to churrasco, roast chicken or rice and beans, especially at weekend gatherings and holidays.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes
  • 2 carrots
  • 150 g green peas, cooked
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs
  • 1/2 small onion, grated
  • 200 g mayonnaise
  • salt, parsley, a little lemon juice

Method

  1. Peel and dice the potatoes and carrots into 1.5 cm cubes.
  2. Boil the carrots for 5 minutes, add the potatoes and cook until just tender.
  3. Drain, spread out and let cool completely.
  4. Mix mayonnaise with grated onion, lemon juice and salt.
  5. Fold in the vegetables, peas and chopped eggs gently.
  6. Chill for 1 hour and sprinkle with parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half mayonnaise and half yogurt for a lighter dressing.
  • Keep the potatoes firm so they hold their shape with less dressing.
  • Add chopped green beans or cucumber for more volume and fewer calories.

History

The salad is a Brazilian cousin of the Russian salad that spread around the world in the twentieth century. In Brazil it took on its own simple form and became one of the most common sides at barbecues.
