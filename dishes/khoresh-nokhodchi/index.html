Khoresh nokhodchi (chickpea and lamb stew)

Khoresh nokhodchi is a simple Persian stew of lamb and tender chickpeas in a turmeric-gold sauce with onion and a little tomato. It is mild, hearty and savory. It is eaten with plain white rice.

Recipe

4 servings130 min

Ingredients

  • 350 g lamb
  • 150 g chickpeas, soaked
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown the onion with turmeric, then the lamb.
  2. Add tomato paste, chickpeas and hot water.
  3. Cover and simmer 1.5 hours until tender.
  4. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use canned chickpeas to save time.
  • Choose lean lamb.
  • Add more chickpeas for fiber.

History

It is an everyday dish of Iranian home kitchens, valued for being filling and inexpensive. Chickpeas are a staple legume across Iran.
