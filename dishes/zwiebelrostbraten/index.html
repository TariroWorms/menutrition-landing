🥩 Zwiebelrostbraten

Origin: Austria · Vienna

Zwiebelrostbraten is a thin slice of beef sirloin pan-fried and topped with a generous pile of onions, then served in a dark, savory gravy. The onions are caramelized or crisp-fried, adding sweetness and crunch to the tender beef. In Austria and Swabia it is typically served with buttered spaetzle or potatoes.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 beef sirloin steaks (about 360 g), pounded thin
  • 3 onions, thinly sliced
  • 300 ml beef stock
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 20 g butter
  • 300 g cooked spaetzle
  • Salt, pepper, mustard, marjoram

Method

  1. Fry the onions slowly in the oil until deep golden; set aside.
  2. Season the steaks, spread thinly with mustard and sear 1–2 minutes per side; keep warm.
  3. Deglaze the pan with the stock, add marjoram and reduce into a gravy.
  4. Toss the spaetzle in the butter until hot.
  5. Plate the beef with the gravy, top with the onions and serve with the spaetzle.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the fat edge from the sirloin before cooking.
  • Toss the spaetzle in half the butter (1 tbsp butter ≈ 100 kcal).
  • Serve a smaller portion of spaetzle with a side salad.

History

The dish is a classic of Viennese and Austrian cooking and is equally popular in Swabia in southern Germany, where it is paired with spaetzle. It is a fixture on traditional inn menus in both regions.
