Pineapple cake (fengli su)

Origin: Taiwan

Pineapple cake is a small square or oval pastry with a crumbly, buttery shell and a thick, sticky pineapple jam centre. It tastes sweet with a gentle tang from the fruit. It is eaten as a tea-time snack and is the classic gift to bring home from a trip to Taiwan.

Recipe

12 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g butter, softened
  • 60 g icing sugar
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 300 g flour
  • 30 g milk powder
  • 400 g pineapple jam

Method

  1. Beat the butter with the icing sugar until pale.
  2. Mix in the egg yolk, then fold in flour and milk powder to make a soft dough.
  3. Divide the dough into 12 pieces and the jam into 12 balls.
  4. Wrap each ball of jam in dough and press into square moulds.
  5. Bake at 170 °C for about 25 minutes, turning once, until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a jam with little added sugar to cut the sweetness.
  • Make smaller pieces so a serving is lighter.
  • Replace part of the butter with a mild oil, accepting a less rich crumb.

History

The pastry became closely associated with Taiwan in the late twentieth century, when bakeries began selling it as a souvenir. In Taiwanese the word for pineapple sounds like a phrase for good fortune, which is part of why it is popular as a gift.
