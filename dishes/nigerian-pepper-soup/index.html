Nigerian pepper soup

Origin: Nigeria

Pepper soup is a thin, fiery, aromatic broth. Goat meat, chicken or catfish simmer in water with a spice mix of calabash nutmeg, uziza seeds and scotch bonnet. It is fragrant, warming and clears the sinuses. It is served in bowls as a starter or comfort food, often at gatherings.

Recipe

6 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1000 g goat meat
  • 40 g pepper soup spice mix (calabash nutmeg, uziza)
  • 2 g scotch bonnet peppers
  • 150 g onions
  • 20 g ground dried crayfish
  • salt
  • stock cubes and salt

Method

  1. Wash the meat and boil with onion and salt for 30 minutes, skimming the surface.
  2. Add water to cover generously and the stock cube.
  3. Add the pepper soup spice mix, crayfish and chopped scotch bonnet.
  4. Simmer 20–40 minutes until the meat is tender.
  5. Taste, adjust and serve piping hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less palm oil and skim the surface to cut saturated fat.
  • Choose lean meat or fish and add extra leafy greens.
  • Season with spices and herbs to reduce salt and stock cubes.

History

Pepper soup is eaten across southern Nigeria and by many communities, with spice mixes that differ by region. Many also see it as a restorative food, for example for new mothers.
