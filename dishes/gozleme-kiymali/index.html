🫓 Gözleme with minced meat

Kıymalı gözleme is a Turkish flatbread of thin, hand-rolled dough folded around a filling of minced beef cooked with onion and spices, then cooked on a large dome-shaped griddle called a sac. The dough becomes flaky and blistered while the filling stays juicy and savoury. It is cut into pieces and served hot, often with ayran or tea.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g plain flour
  • about 220 ml warm water, 1 tsp salt
  • 300 g minced beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp chili flakes, 1 tsp cumin, black pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Mix the flour, salt and water into a soft dough, knead for 5 minutes and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Cook the onion and beef in a pan until browned, season with the spices and cool.
  3. Divide the dough into 4 and roll each piece very thin.
  4. Spread a quarter of the filling over half of each sheet and fold to close.
  5. Cook on a hot griddle or large pan, brushing lightly with oil, for 3–4 minutes per side until spotted and crisp.
  6. Cut into pieces and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush the griddle lightly instead of pouring oil — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use lean beef and add extra onion or peppers to the filling.
  • Serve with a large tomato and cucumber salad.

History

Gözleme is a traditional village bread of rural Anatolia, still often made by women rolling dough with a long thin rolling pin. The name comes from the Turkish göz, 'eye', usually linked to the browned spots on the cooked bread. Today it is sold at markets, roadside stops and cafés across Turkey.
