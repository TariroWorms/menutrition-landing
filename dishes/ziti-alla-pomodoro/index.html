Ziti al pomodoro is long tubular ziti pasta tossed in a simple tomato sauce of garlic, olive oil and fresh basil. The sauce is fresh, sweet and light, clinging to the inside of the tubes. It is a weekday staple in southern Italy, served hot as a first course or a simple main.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 350 g ziti
  • 600 g canned or fresh peeled tomatoes
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • A handful of fresh basil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Warm the olive oil and gently cook the garlic until fragrant, without browning.
  2. Add the tomatoes, crush them and simmer for 15–20 minutes until thickened; season.
  3. Boil the ziti (break long ziti into pieces if you like) until al dente.
  4. Drain, reserving a little pasta water, and toss with the sauce.
  5. Tear in the basil and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep pasta to about 80–90 g dry per person, the standard Italian serving.
  • Use 1–2 tbsp olive oil in the sauce; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Add grilled vegetables or a side of chicken or beans to make it more filling.

History

Ziti are a traditional pasta of Naples and Campania; the name is often linked to 'zita', a local word for bride, as they were a classic of wedding and festive meals. Today ziti with tomato sauce is everyday home cooking across southern Italy.
