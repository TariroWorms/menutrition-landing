🦑 Seppia alla griglia (grilled cuttlefish)

Origin: Italy · Adriatic coast

Seppia alla griglia is cuttlefish cleaned, scored and cooked quickly over a very hot grill until lightly charred outside and tender inside. It is dressed simply with olive oil, lemon, garlic and plenty of chopped parsley. The flavour is sweet and briny with a smoky edge, and it is served as a main course or antipasto with lemon wedges and a green salad.

What it is made of

cuttlefisholive oillemongarlicparsley

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g cuttlefish, cleaned
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  • 15 g parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Score the cuttlefish bodies lightly in a crosshatch and pat dry.
  2. Mix 1 tbsp oil with the garlic, parsley, a squeeze of lemon, salt and pepper and toss the cuttlefish in it for 15 minutes.
  3. Grill over very high heat for 2–3 minutes per side until just opaque and lightly charred.
  4. Drizzle with the remaining oil and serve with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Cuttlefish itself is lean; most of the calories come from the oil, so measure it — 1 tbsp ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use a marinade of lemon, garlic and parsley and brush on oil only lightly.
  • Serve with grilled vegetables or a large salad.

History

Grilled cuttlefish is a common dish in coastal towns along the Italian Adriatic and across the Mediterranean, where cuttlefish are caught in spring. It is a staple of seaside trattorias today.
