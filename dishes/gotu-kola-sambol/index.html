Gotu kola sambol (pennywort salad)

Origin: Sri Lanka

Gotu kola sambol is a fresh salad of finely shredded gotu kola (Asiatic pennywort) with grated coconut, red onion, green chili, lime juice and salt. It is crunchy, slightly bitter, tangy and refreshing. It is eaten with rice and curry, especially for breakfast.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 bunches gotu kola (about 150 g)
  • 50 g grated coconut
  • 1 small red onion
  • 1 green chili
  • juice of 1 lime
  • pinch of salt
  • tomato slices, optional

Method

  1. Wash the leaves well and shred them very finely.
  2. Slice onion and chili thinly.
  3. Combine all ingredients in a bowl.
  4. Season with salt and lime juice.
  5. Mix and serve straight away.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve as a green side alongside curries.
  • Use less coconut for a lighter version.
  • Add the lime juice just before serving so the leaves stay bright green.

History

Gotu kola is widely used in Sri Lankan food and traditional medicine. The sambol is a daily accompaniment, often eaten for its fresh, cooling taste.
