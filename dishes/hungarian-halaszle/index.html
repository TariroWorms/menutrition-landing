Halászlé (Hungarian fisherman's soup)

Origin: Hungary

Halászlé is a deep red river fish soup made with carp, onions and plenty of paprika. The broth is rich, a little sweet from the onions and warming from hot peppers. It is eaten very hot with fresh white bread and sometimes with pasta.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg carp or mixed river fish, in pieces
  • 3 onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 hot pepper or chili paste
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 green pepper
  • 1.2 l water
  • Salt

Method

  1. Boil fish heads and trimmings with half the onions for 25 minutes.
  2. Strain, pressing the solids through a sieve.
  3. Return the broth to the pot with the rest of the onions.
  4. Stir in paprika, pepper and tomato and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Add the fish pieces and cook gently for 15 minutes without stirring.
  6. Season and serve with bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve with plenty of bread rather than fatty garnishes.
  • Choose carp from reputable sources and trim the belly fat.
  • Keep the soup free of added oil; the fish gives enough richness.

History

The soup comes from the fishing communities along the Danube and Tisza rivers. Regions differ in how they prepare it, for example in whether the broth is strained or the fish are cooked in a cauldron over open fire.
