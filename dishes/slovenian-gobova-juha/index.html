Gobova juha (wild mushroom soup)

Origin: Slovenia

Gobova juha is a thick, earthy soup made from forest mushrooms such as porcini and chanterelles. A spoonful of cream makes it velvety and a little parsley freshens the finish. It is served hot in a bowl with rustic bread.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g wild mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 potato, diced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 800 ml vegetable stock
  • 100 ml cream
  • Parsley, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Soften the onion in butter.
  2. Add the mushrooms and cook until their water evaporates.
  3. Stir in the flour, then pour in the stock gradually.
  4. Add the potato and simmer for 20 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream and season.
  6. Finish with chopped parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream and add milk for a lighter soup.
  • Blend part of the soup for a thicker texture without extra flour.
  • Add dried porcini for a stronger flavour with less fat.

History

Mushroom picking is a popular pastime in the forests of Slovenia, and soups like this are a typical way to use the autumn harvest. Dried mushrooms are also used to enjoy the dish through winter.
