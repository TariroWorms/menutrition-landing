Chakhchoukha (torn flatbread in spicy lamb sauce)

Origin: Algeria · Biskra

Chakhchoukha is a hearty dish of thin, torn pieces of dry flatbread soaked in a spicy tomato, lamb and chickpea sauce. The bread softens and absorbs the broth while keeping a pleasant chew. It is served in a large shared dish with the meat on top.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g lamb, cubed
  • 300 g thin dry flatbread (rougag), torn
  • 100 g cooked chickpeas
  • 2 tomatoes, grated
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp harissa
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 l water, salt

Method

  1. Brown the lamb with onion in oil.
  2. Add tomatoes, harissa, salt and water and simmer 1 hour.
  3. Add chickpeas and cook 15 more minutes.
  4. Tear the flatbread into small pieces in a large dish.
  5. Ladle hot sauce over the bread and let it soak for 5 minutes.
  6. Top with the lamb and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and lean meat.
  • Add vegetables such as zucchini or carrots.
  • Moisten the bread with just enough broth to keep portions moderate.

History

It is a festive specialty from Biskra and the Aures region of Algeria, often cooked for weddings and family gatherings. Different regions have their own versions, from red and spicy to white.
