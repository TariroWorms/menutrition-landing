🇮🇷Persian food

Persian cuisine centres on fragrant rice, fresh herbs and slow-cooked stews (khoresh), often flavoured with saffron, dried lime and pomegranate. Kebabs served with rice, herb-rich dishes like ghormeh sabzi and tahdig, the crispy rice crust from the bottom of the pot, are typical.

💡Tip

Rice portions are usually large and often finished with butter or oil, so the rice can outweigh the kebab in calories. Kebab with salad or grilled vegetables is a lighter combination.
