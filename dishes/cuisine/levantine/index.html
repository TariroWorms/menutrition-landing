🧆Levantine food

Levantine cuisine, shared by Lebanon, Syria, Jordan and Palestine, relies on olive oil, chickpeas, bulgur, fresh herbs, yoghurt, grilled meats and flatbread. Many meals start with mezze such as hummus, tabbouleh and baba ghanoush.
