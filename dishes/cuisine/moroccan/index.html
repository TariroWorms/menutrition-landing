🇲🇦Moroccan food

Moroccan cuisine combines couscous, slow-cooked tagines, bread and warm spices such as cumin, cinnamon, ginger and saffron. Dishes often mix savoury and sweet, for example meat with dried fruit, and harira soup and grilled kebabs are popular.
