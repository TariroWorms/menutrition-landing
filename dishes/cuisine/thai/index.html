🇹🇭Thai food

Thai cuisine balances sweet, sour, salty and spicy flavours using fish sauce, lime, chillies, garlic, lemongrass, coconut milk and fresh herbs. Rice or noodles form the base, and a meal usually combines several shared dishes.
