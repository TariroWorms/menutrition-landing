🇦🇹Austrian food

Austrian cuisine is known for breaded and roasted meats, dumplings and pastries, from Wiener schnitzel and Tafelspitz (boiled beef) to goulash. Its coffee-house culture adds cakes and desserts such as apple strudel and Sachertorte.
