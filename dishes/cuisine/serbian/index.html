🇷🇸Serbian food

Serbian cuisine features grilled meats, peppers, cheese and bread, with dishes such as ćevapi, pljeskavica and stuffed peppers. Ajvar (roasted pepper relish), kajmak (a rich, creamy dairy spread) and burek pastry are everyday favourites.
