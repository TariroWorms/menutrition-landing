🇰🇷Korean food

Korean cuisine is built around rice, soups and stews, and many small side dishes (banchan), with kimchi and other fermented foods at its core. Common seasonings include gochujang (chilli paste), soy sauce, garlic and sesame oil.

💡Tip

Banchan and soups are often light, while fried chicken, sweet sauces and rice cakes add up quickly — lean grilled meat wrapped in lettuce is a protein-rich choice.
