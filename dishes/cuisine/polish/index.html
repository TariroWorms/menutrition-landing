🇵🇱Polish food

Polish cuisine relies on potatoes, cabbage, beetroot, pork, rye bread and dairy such as sour cream and twaróg cheese. Pierogi, bigos (hunter's stew) and soups like żurek and barszcz are among the best-known dishes.
