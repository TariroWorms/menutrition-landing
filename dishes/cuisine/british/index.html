🇬🇧British food

British cuisine is based on meat, potatoes, bread and seasonal vegetables, with classics like the roast dinner, fish and chips, pies and the full breakfast. Many of the best-known dishes are served in pubs, cafés and takeaways.

💡Tip

Fried and pastry dishes such as fish and chips or pies are energy-dense — the batter, pastry and chips usually carry most of the calories. A roast with plenty of vegetables is a lighter way to go.
