🇮🇩Indonesian food

Indonesian cuisine is based on rice, coconut, chilli and spice pastes, with dishes such as nasi goreng, satay, rendang and gado-gado. A typical meal is rice served with several side dishes, often accompanied by sambal.

💡Tip

Coconut-milk curries and fried dishes like nasi goreng are calorie-dense, and peanut sauce on satay and gado-gado adds a lot too. Grilled items and vegetable sides are usually lighter.
