🇱🇧Lebanese food

Lebanese cuisine is part of the wider Levantine tradition, featuring olive oil, lemon, garlic, fresh herbs, chickpeas, bulgur and grilled meats. A meal often begins with mezze such as hummus, tabbouleh and fattoush, followed by grills.
