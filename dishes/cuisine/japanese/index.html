🇯🇵Japanese food

Japanese cuisine centres on rice, fish and seafood, soy products such as tofu and miso, vegetables and seaweed, seasoned with soy sauce, dashi broth and mirin. A traditional meal pairs rice and miso soup with several small side dishes.
