🇾🇪Yemeni food

Yemeni cuisine is known for slow-cooked meats, flatbreads and spiced stews, seasoned with the hawaij spice blend and served with zhug, a green chilli sauce. Saltah, a meat stew topped with fenugreek froth, and mandi, rice with slow-cooked meat, are signature dishes.
