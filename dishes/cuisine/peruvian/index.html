🇵🇪Peruvian food

Peruvian cuisine blends Indigenous, Spanish, African and Asian influences, with staples such as potatoes, corn, ají chillies and fresh fish. Ceviche, lomo saltado and ají de gallina are among the best-known dishes.

💡Tip

Ceviche is light and high in protein, while lomo saltado often comes with both rice and fries — skipping one of the two makes a big difference.
