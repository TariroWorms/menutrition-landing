🇮🇹Italian food

Italian cooking builds on a few quality ingredients: olive oil, tomatoes, cheese, herbs and wheat in the form of pasta, bread and pizza. Recipes vary strongly by region, and a traditional meal can include an antipasto, a first course (primo) such as pasta or risotto, and a second course (secondo) of meat or fish.
