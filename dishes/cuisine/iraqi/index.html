🇮🇶Iraqi food

Iraqi cuisine is built on rice, bread, lamb, chicken and legumes, with spices such as cardamom, cumin and dried lime. Well-known dishes include masgouf (grilled fish), dolma, kubba and quzi (lamb with rice).
