🇮🇳Indian food

Indian cuisine varies widely by region and is built on spices, rice, breads such as roti and naan, lentils (dal), vegetables, dairy and, in many areas, meat or fish. Vegetarian dishes play a large role, and a meal often combines several dishes with rice or bread.
