🇵🇹Portuguese food

Portuguese cuisine relies on fish and seafood, olive oil, garlic and bread, with salt cod (bacalhau) prepared in many ways. Grilled sardines, caldo verde soup, hearty stews and custard pastries such as pastéis de nata are well known.

💡Tip

Grilled fish and soups are generally lighter options, while cream- or potato-based bakes and pastries are much richer. Olive oil is used generously, so it adds up.
