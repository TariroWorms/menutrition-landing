🥙Balkan food

Balkan cuisine shares grilled meats, peppers, flaky pastries, cheese and yoghurt across the region, shaped by Mediterranean, Central European and Ottoman influences. Ćevapi, burek, stuffed peppers, sarma (stuffed cabbage leaves) and fresh salads appear in the kitchens of many countries.
