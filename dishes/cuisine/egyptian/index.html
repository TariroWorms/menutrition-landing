🇪🇬Egyptian food

Egyptian cuisine is rich in legumes, bread, rice and vegetables — fava beans, lentils and chickpeas appear in many everyday dishes. Well-known examples include ful medames, ta'ameya (fava bean falafel) and koshari, a mix of rice, pasta, lentils and tomato sauce.
