🇻🇳Vietnamese food

Vietnamese cuisine relies on rice, rice noodles, fresh herbs and fish sauce, balancing salty, sweet, sour and spicy flavours. Noodle soups such as pho, fresh rice-paper rolls and grilled meats served with herbs and dipping sauces are typical.

💡Tip

Fresh rolls and broth-based soups are usually lighter than fried spring rolls or dishes with sweet dipping sauces, which can add noticeable sugar. A large bowl of noodle soup is still filling, mostly thanks to the noodles.
