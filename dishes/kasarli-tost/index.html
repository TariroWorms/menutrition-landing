🥪 Kaşarlı tost (Turkish cheese toast)

Origin: Turkey

Kaşarlı tost is the classic Turkish toasted sandwich: two slices of bread filled with plenty of kaşar, a mild semi-hard cheese, brushed with butter and pressed in a hot ridged grill until crunchy outside and stretchy inside. It is a staple of breakfast, school canteens and büfe kiosks, usually served with a glass of tea or ayran.

Recipe

1 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 2 slices of white bread
  • 60 g kaşar cheese, grated or sliced
  • 10 g butter, softened

Method

  1. Heat a sandwich press or ridged grill pan.
  2. Fill the bread with the cheese.
  3. Brush the outside lightly with butter.
  4. Press and toast for 3–4 minutes until golden and the cheese melts. Cut in half and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 40 g cheese and add sliced tomato or green pepper for volume.
  • Brush with a thin layer of butter only, or toast dry in a non-stick press.
  • Pair it with plain tea and a salad instead of a second toast.

History

Pressed toast sandwiches are a fixture of Turkish snack bars and büfe kiosks. Kaşar is one of Turkey's most widely eaten cheeses, which made it the natural filling.
