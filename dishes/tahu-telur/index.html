Tahu telur (East Javanese tofu omelette)

Origin: Indonesia · East Java

Tahu telur is a thick omelette made with fried tofu pieces and beaten egg, often with bean sprouts and scallion. It is cut into wedges and covered with a sweet, savory peanut and soy sauce, then topped with sprouts and crackers. It is a popular street food in Surabaya and across Java.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g firm tofu, cubed and fried
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 handful bean sprouts
  • 2 scallions, chopped
  • 2 tbsp peanut sauce (peanuts, garlic, chili, palm sugar)
  • 1 tbsp kecap manis
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and white pepper

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with salt, pepper and scallion.
  2. Heat oil in a skillet and fry the tofu until golden.
  3. Pour the egg over the tofu and add the sprouts.
  4. Cook until the underside sets, then flip and cook the other side.
  5. Cut into wedges.
  6. Pour over the peanut sauce and kecap manis and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the tofu instead of pan-frying it.
  • Use a small amount of peanut sauce to keep sugar and fat lower.
  • Add extra sprouts for a lighter plate.

History

The dish is a favorite of street vendors in East Java, especially around Surabaya. It makes a cheap, filling meal from simple ingredients.
