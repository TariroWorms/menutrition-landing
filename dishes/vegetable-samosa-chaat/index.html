🥟 Samosa Chaat

Samosa chaat is a North Indian street snack in which crisp vegetable samosas are broken up and topped with warm spiced chickpeas, cool whisked yogurt, sweet-sour tamarind chutney and fresh mint chutney. Every bite mixes crunchy, creamy, tangy and spicy. It is finished with chopped onion and a pinch of chaat masala and eaten straight away.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 4 vegetable samosas
  • 300 g cooked chickpeas
  • 200 g plain yogurt, whisked
  • 4 tbsp tamarind chutney
  • 3 tbsp mint chutney
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/2 tsp cumin, toasted and ground

Method

  1. Warm the chickpeas with a splash of water, cumin and a pinch of salt for 5 minutes.
  2. Warm the samosas in the oven at 180 °C for 8–10 minutes so they crisp up.
  3. Break each samosa into pieces on a plate and spoon the chickpeas over.
  4. Top with yogurt, tamarind chutney and mint chutney.
  5. Sprinkle with onion and chaat masala and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Use one samosa per plate and add more chickpeas for volume.
  • Use more yogurt and mint chutney and less of the sugary tamarind chutney.
  • Reheat samosas in the oven instead of refrying them.

History

Samosa chaat is a staple of chaat stalls in North Indian cities such as Delhi and Lucknow, where leftover samosas are turned into this layered snack.
