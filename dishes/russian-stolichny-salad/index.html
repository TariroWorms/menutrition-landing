Stolichny salad (capital salad)

Origin: Russia

Stolichny salad is a hearty chopped salad of boiled chicken, potatoes, carrots, eggs, pickled cucumbers and green peas dressed with mayonnaise. Chicken gives it a lighter, cleaner taste than the sausage version of Olivier. It is served cold as a starter or a light main, especially at holidays.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g chicken breast
  • 3 potatoes
  • 1 carrot
  • 3 eggs
  • 3 pickled cucumbers
  • 150 g green peas
  • 150 g mayonnaise
  • salt, pepper

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in salted water for 25 minutes and let it cool.
  2. Boil potatoes and carrot in their skins until tender, then peel.
  3. Hard-boil the eggs.
  4. Dice chicken, vegetables, eggs and pickles into small even cubes.
  5. Add the peas, mix with mayonnaise, season and chill before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Mix mayonnaise half and half with yogurt or sour cream.
  • Use more chicken and fewer potatoes to raise protein.
  • Dress only the portion you serve so leftovers keep longer.

History

The name means 'of the capital' and it was a standard dish in Soviet restaurants, where it was offered as a version of the earlier Olivier salad. Recipes vary, and some cooks add fresh apple or cucumber.
