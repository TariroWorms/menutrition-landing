Matevž (bean and potato mash)

Origin: Slovenia

Matevž is a rustic mash of boiled beans and potatoes, seasoned with garlic and topped with crispy cracklings. It is creamy, filling and gently smoky. It is served as a side with sausages or roast meat, or as a meal with sauerkraut.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried or cooked beans
  • 500 g potatoes
  • 100 g smoked bacon, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Cook the soaked beans until tender (skip if using cooked).
  2. Boil the peeled potatoes in salted water.
  3. Drain both and mash them together coarsely.
  4. Fry the bacon in the olive oil until crisp, then add the onion and garlic.
  5. Stir half the fat into the mash and season.
  6. Top with the cracklings and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use olive oil instead of bacon fat for part of the mash.
  • Beans add fibre and plant protein, so keep the bean ratio high.
  • Serve with a vinegary cabbage salad to cut the richness.

History

It is a traditional peasant dish of the Slovenian coast and Karst region, where beans and potatoes were pantry staples, and a typical winter side.
