🍰 Sfouf (Turmeric Semolina Cake)

Origin: Lebanon · Levant

Sfouf is a bright yellow Lebanese cake made from semolina coloured with turmeric and fragrant with anise. It is dense and slightly crumbly rather than fluffy, only mildly sweet, and each diamond-cut square is topped with pine nuts. It is served in small pieces with tea or coffee.

Recipe

16 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g fine semolina
  • 1½ tsp ground turmeric
  • 200 g sugar
  • 150 ml vegetable oil
  • 300 ml water
  • 1 tsp ground anise
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 30 g pine nuts

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and oil a 25 cm square tin.
  2. Mix semolina, turmeric, anise and baking powder.
  3. Stir in sugar, oil and water to a smooth batter.
  4. Pour into the tin, press pine nuts on top and bake 35–40 minutes until golden.
  5. Cool and cut into diamonds or squares.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut small pieces: a 5 cm square is plenty with tea.
  • Reduce the sugar by a quarter; the anise keeps the flavour full.
  • Use fewer pine nuts — one per piece is traditional.

History

Sfouf is a traditional home cake in Lebanon and is also baked in Syria and Palestine. This oil-based version without eggs or dairy is common during fasting periods and remains a familiar bakery and household sweet.
