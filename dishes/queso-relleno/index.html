Queso relleno (Yucatecan stuffed Edam)

Origin: Mexico · Yucatán
725kcal

Queso relleno is a showpiece of Yucatecan cooking: a ball of Dutch Edam is hollowed out and filled with minced pork cooked with tomato, raisins, olives, capers, almonds and spices. The cheese is wrapped and steamed or baked until soft, then sliced and served with two sauces, a light white sauce called kol and a tomato sauce. The result is sweet, salty and very rich.

Recipe

8 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1 Edam cheese (about 1.8 kg)
  • 500 g ground pork
  • 3 tomatoes, 1 onion, 1 green pepper, chopped
  • 50 g raisins, 50 g green olives, 1 tbsp capers
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped; ground cloves, pepper and oregano to season
  • 500 ml broth thickened with 2 tbsp flour (kol sauce)
  • 300 ml tomato sauce

Method

  1. Peel the wax, cut a lid from the cheese and hollow it, leaving a 2 cm wall.
  2. Brown the pork, add onion, pepper and tomato and cook 15 minutes.
  3. Stir in raisins, olives, capers and chopped egg; season with clove, pepper and oregano.
  4. Fill the cheese, replace the lid and wrap in a cloth or foil.
  5. Steam or bake at 180 °C for 20–30 minutes until soft but not melted.
  6. Slice and serve with warm kol sauce and tomato sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve thin wedges and fill the plate with salad.
  • Use lean pork or turkey for the picadillo.
  • Save the scooped-out cheese for other dishes instead of adding it to the filling.

History

Edam reached the Yucatán through sea trade in the nineteenth century, when the peninsula had closer links with Caribbean and European ports than with central Mexico. Local cooks combined it with a Spanish-style picadillo, creating a dish now served at celebrations.
