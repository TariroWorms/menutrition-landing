Popiah (fresh spring roll)

Origin: Singapore

Popiah is a thin wheat wrapper filled with braised turnip or jicama, egg, shrimp, lettuce, crushed peanuts and sweet sauce. It is soft, juicy, sweet and savory, never fried. People often roll their own at family gatherings.

What it is made of

Popiah skinjicamaeggshrimppeanutsSweet bean sauce

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 8 popiah skins
  • 300 g jicama, shredded and braised
  • 2 eggs, thin omelette strips
  • 100 g cooked shrimp, lettuce leaves
  • sweet bean sauce, garlic, chili, crushed peanuts

Method

  1. Braise jicama with garlic and a little sauce for 20 minutes.
  2. Cook the eggs into a thin omelette and slice.
  3. Lay a skin and spread sweet sauce and chili.
  4. Add lettuce, jicama, egg, shrimp and peanuts.
  5. Roll up and tuck in the ends.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sweet sauce.
  • Add extra vegetables.
  • Skip peanuts if watching fat.

History

Popiah arrived with Hokkien and Teochew migrants and is eaten across Singapore and Malaysia, especially at Qingming and New Year gatherings.
