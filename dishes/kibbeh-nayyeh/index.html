🥩 Kibbeh Nayyeh (Raw Kibbeh)

Origin: Levant · Lebanon

Kibbeh nayyeh is the raw form of kibbeh: very fresh, lean lamb pounded or finely minced with soaked fine bulgur, onion and warm spices into a silky paste. It is spread on a plate, swirled with the back of a spoon, drizzled generously with olive oil and garnished with fresh mint and onion. It is eaten scooped up with bread or lettuce as a prized mezze dish.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g very fresh lean lamb (leg), trimmed
  • 100 g fine bulgur
  • 1 small onion
  • 1 tsp kammouneh or cumin-based spice mix
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh mint leaves

Method

  1. Rinse the bulgur, squeeze dry and keep chilled.
  2. Mince the lamb very finely (twice) or pulse with ice-cold water in a food processor.
  3. Blend onion with spices and salt, then knead with the lamb and bulgur into a smooth paste, keeping everything cold.
  4. Spread on a chilled plate, make grooves with a spoon and drizzle with olive oil.
  5. Garnish with mint and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only very fresh meat from a trusted butcher and keep it cold throughout.
  • Measure the olive oil — 1 tbsp ≈ 120 kcal — rather than pouring freely.
  • Scoop with lettuce leaves and radishes instead of bread.

History

Kibbeh nayyeh is a celebrated Lebanese mezze, traditionally made on special occasions from meat bought from a trusted butcher on the same day.
