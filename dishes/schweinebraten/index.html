🍖 Schweinebraten (Bavarian roast pork)

Origin: Germany · Bavaria

Schweinebraten is Bavarian roast pork, usually shoulder with the skin left on, seasoned with caraway, garlic and salt and roasted on a bed of onions with dark beer until the skin turns into crisp crackling. The pan juices become a rich gravy. It is served in thick slices with potato dumplings and often sauerkraut or cabbage salad.

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork shoulder with skin
  • 2 tsp caraway seeds, lightly crushed
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 onions, quartered
  • 500 ml dark beer
  • Salt and pepper
  • 6 potato dumplings, to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C. Score the skin in a diamond pattern without cutting into the meat.
  2. Rub the meat (not the skin) with garlic, caraway, salt and pepper, and rub salt into the skin.
  3. Put the onions in a roasting tin, add the pork skin side up and pour in the beer and 250 ml water.
  4. Roast for about 2.5 hours, basting the meat now and then and topping up the liquid.
  5. Raise the heat to 220 °C for the last 20 minutes until the skin crackles.
  6. Rest the meat for 10 minutes, strain and skim the pan juices, and serve sliced with the dumplings and gravy.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim the fat off the gravy before serving.
  • Eat the lean meat and leave most of the crackling and fat layer.
  • Have one dumpling and add sauerkraut or a large cabbage salad.

History

Schweinebraten is a classic Sunday dish in Bavaria and is served in almost every traditional inn and beer hall there. Versions with crackling are known as Krustenbraten, and roast pork of this kind is also popular in Austria.
