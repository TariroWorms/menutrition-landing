Lamprais (Sri Lankan Burgher rice parcel)

Origin: Sri Lanka · Burgher cuisine

Lamprais is a banana leaf parcel holding rice cooked in stock, mixed meat curry, frikkadels (meatballs), a boiled egg, brinjal moju and sambol. It is baked so the flavors blend and the leaf perfumes the rice. It is rich, mildly spicy and eaten as a Sunday or celebration meal.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g rice
  • 600 ml stock
  • 300 g mixed beef and pork
  • 4 frikkadels (small meatballs)
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • 2 tbsp seeni sambol
  • eggplant moju
  • banana leaves

Method

  1. Cook rice in stock with spices until fluffy.
  2. Make a dry meat curry and fry the frikkadels.
  3. Place banana leaf squares and add rice.
  4. Add curry, frikkadel, egg, sambol and moju beside the rice.
  5. Fold into tight parcels and tie.
  6. Bake at 180 C for 20 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean meat for the curry.
  • Share one parcel, as it is rich.
  • Add more vegetable sambol.

History

The dish comes from the Dutch Burgher community of Sri Lanka and its name is linked to the Dutch word lump rijst. It reflects Dutch, Malay and Sri Lankan influences.
