🍛 Panang Chicken

Panang chicken is a Thai curry of tender chicken simmered in a thick, rich sauce made from Panang curry paste and coconut milk. Shredded kaffir lime leaves add a fragrant citrus note, and crushed peanuts give the sauce its nutty depth. It is less soupy and slightly sweeter than many Thai curries and is usually served with steamed jasmine rice.

What it is made of

chickenpanang curry pastecoconut milkkaffir lime leavespeanuts

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast or thigh, sliced
  • 3 tbsp Panang curry paste
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 5 kaffir lime leaves, finely shredded
  • 30 g roasted peanuts, crushed
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 red chili, sliced (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oil with a few spoons of coconut milk and fry the curry paste for 2 minutes until fragrant.
  2. Add the chicken and stir until coated and no longer pink outside.
  3. Pour in the remaining coconut milk, add sugar and half the lime leaves; simmer 10–15 minutes until thick.
  4. Stir in most of the peanuts and adjust seasoning with salt.
  5. Serve topped with the remaining lime leaves, peanuts and chili.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk or replace part of it with water to cut fat.
  • Choose chicken breast over thigh and keep peanuts to a sprinkle.
  • Serve with a measured portion of rice (about 150 g cooked) and extra steamed vegetables.

History

Panang (also spelled phanaeng) curry is a long-established part of Thai cuisine; the name is often linked to the island of Penang, though this is not certain. Today it is one of the most popular Thai curries both in Thailand and abroad.
