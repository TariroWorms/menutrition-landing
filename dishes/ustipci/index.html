🫓 Uštipci

Origin: Balkans · Serbia, Bosnia, Montenegro

Uštipci are puffy fried dough balls made from a soft batter of flour, yogurt, egg and salt. They come out golden and lightly crisp outside, airy and tender inside. They are eaten warm for breakfast or as a side, savoury on their own or with a sprinkle of salt, and are a familiar home snack across the Balkans.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 250 g plain yogurt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil for deep-frying

Method

  1. Whisk the yogurt, egg and salt together.
  2. Stir in the flour and baking soda to form a thick, sticky batter; rest for 15 minutes.
  3. Heat the oil to about 170 °C.
  4. Drop spoonfuls of batter into the oil and fry 3–4 minutes, turning, until puffed and golden.
  5. Drain on paper towels and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Drain well on paper towels to remove excess oil.
  • Have 2–3 pieces with a big salad rather than a full plate.
  • Keep the oil at a steady temperature — cooler oil makes greasier dough.

History

Uštipci are a long-standing home dish across Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro and Croatia, with each family keeping its own batter proportions.
