A kids' meal with chicken nuggets is the familiar fast-food combo: a few bite-sized pieces of breaded chicken, a small portion of fries, a little ketchup for dipping and a juice box. The nuggets are crisp outside and soft inside, the fries salty and golden. At home the same plate can be made with oven-baked nuggets and fries, which keeps the idea but gives more control over oil.

Recipe

2 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g chicken breast
  • 50 g wheat flour
  • 40 g dry breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 400 g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp ketchup
  • 2 small boxes fruit juice (200 ml each)
  • Salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220 °C. Cut potatoes into sticks, toss with 1 tbsp oil and salt and bake 30 minutes, turning once.
  2. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces and season with salt and paprika.
  3. Mix flour with a little water into a thin batter, dip the chicken, then roll in breadcrumbs.
  4. Arrange on an oiled tray, brush with the remaining oil and bake 12–15 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve nuggets and fries with ketchup and juice.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake rather than deep-fry both nuggets and fries.
  • Swap the juice box for water and add fruit pieces instead.
  • Serve 4–5 nuggets with sliced cucumber and carrot sticks in place of half the fries.

History

Chicken nuggets became a fast-food staple in the late 20th century, and today kids' meals with nuggets are sold in restaurants all over the world.
