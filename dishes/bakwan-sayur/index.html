Bakwan sayur (Indonesian vegetable fritters)

Origin: Indonesia

Bakwan are crispy fritters of shredded cabbage, carrot and bean sprouts bound in a seasoned flour batter and deep-fried until golden. They are light and crunchy with a savory, garlicky flavor. People eat them as a snack with a fresh bird's-eye chili or a sweet-spicy dipping sauce.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g cabbage, shredded
  • 100 g carrot, grated
  • 100 g bean sprouts
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 150 g flour
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 200 ml cold water, salt and pepper
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix the flour, garlic, salt and pepper with the water to a loose batter.
  2. Fold in the vegetables.
  3. Heat 3 cm of oil in a pan.
  4. Drop spoonfuls of batter into the oil.
  5. Fry until golden and crisp, about 3 minutes per side.
  6. Drain on paper and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Air-fry or pan-fry with little oil to cut the fat.
  • Use whole-wheat flour for more fibre.
  • Add more vegetables and less batter.

History

Bakwan is an everyday snack sold by street vendors across Indonesia, usually hot from the pan. The name is related to Chinese-influenced fritter and meatball dishes common in the country.
