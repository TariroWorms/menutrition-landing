🍛 Vegetable Biryani

Origin: India · Hyderabad and Lucknow

Vegetable biryani layers long-grain basmati rice with spiced mixed vegetables, golden fried onions and saffron, then steams it slowly so the flavours mingle. A spoonful of ghee gives it richness and aroma. The rice stays fluffy and separate, streaked with yellow saffron, and it is often served with raita and a simple salad.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g basmati rice
  • 400 g mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, cauliflower)
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • A pinch of saffron soaked in 3 tbsp warm water
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Soak the rice 30 minutes, then boil in salted water for 5–6 minutes until about 70% cooked; drain.
  2. Fry the onions in ghee until golden; set half aside.
  3. Add the vegetables and garam masala to the pan and cook 8–10 minutes with a splash of water.
  4. Layer half the rice over the vegetables, then the rest, and top with fried onions and the saffron water.
  5. Cover tightly and cook on very low heat for 20 minutes, then fluff gently and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Measure the ghee — 1 tbsp ghee ≈ 120 kcal — and use just enough to fry the onions.
  • Increase the vegetables and reduce the rice for a lighter plate.
  • Serve with a fresh salad rather than fried sides.

History

Biryani is generally associated with the royal kitchens of Mughal India, and Hyderabad and Lucknow became famous for their own styles; vegetable versions are widely cooked across India, especially in vegetarian households.
