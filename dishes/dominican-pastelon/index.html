Pastelón de plátano maduro (ripe plantain casserole)

Origin: Dominican Republic

Pastelón is a baked casserole layered with slices of fried ripe plantain, seasoned ground beef picadillo and melted cheese. The sweet plantain contrasts with the savoury, slightly tangy filling. It is cut into squares like lasagna and served warm with salad.

Recipe

6 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe plantains
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 2 tbsp sofrito
  • 200 g tomato sauce
  • 50 g olives and raisins (optional)
  • 200 g shredded cheese
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • oil for frying

Method

  1. Slice the plantains lengthwise and fry until golden; drain.
  2. Brown the beef with sofrito, add tomato sauce, olives and raisins and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Line a baking dish with half of the plantain slices.
  4. Spread the beef over them and sprinkle with half the cheese.
  5. Cover with remaining plantains, pour over the beaten eggs and top with cheese.
  6. Bake at 180 °C for 30 minutes until set and golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the plantain slices instead of frying them.
  • Use lean beef or turkey.
  • Serve with a large green salad.

History

The dish is often compared to lasagna, with sweet plantains replacing pasta sheets. It is a common celebration and Sunday dish in the Dominican Republic and in Puerto Rico, where it is known as piñón.
