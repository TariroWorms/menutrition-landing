🍰 Opera cake

Origin: France · Paris

Opera cake is a refined French gâteau of thin almond sponge layers (joconde) soaked in coffee syrup, alternated with coffee buttercream and chocolate ganache, and finished with a glossy chocolate glaze. Each slice shows neat, even stripes and tastes intensely of coffee and dark chocolate. It is served in small rectangular slices with coffee.

What it is made of

almond sponge cakecoffee buttercreamchocolate ganache

Recipe

12 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • Joconde: 150 g ground almonds, 150 g icing sugar, 4 eggs, 4 egg whites, 40 g flour, 30 g butter, melted
  • Coffee syrup: 150 ml strong coffee, 80 g sugar
  • Buttercream: 200 g butter, 150 g sugar, 2 egg yolks, 2 tbsp instant coffee
  • Ganache: 150 g dark chocolate, 150 ml cream
  • Glaze: 100 g dark chocolate, 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Whisk the almonds, icing sugar and eggs until pale, fold in the flour, then the whipped egg whites and melted butter. Bake in two trays at 220 °C for 7 minutes.
  2. Make the syrup and the coffee buttercream; heat the cream and pour it over the chopped chocolate for the ganache.
  3. Cut the sponge into three equal sheets.
  4. Layer: sponge soaked in syrup, buttercream, sponge, ganache, sponge, buttercream, chilling between layers.
  5. Pour over the melted chocolate glaze, chill for 2 hours and slice with a hot knife.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut thin slices — a 5 × 5 cm square is a typical serving.
  • Share a slice or save half for later.
  • Pair with black coffee rather than a sweetened drink.

History

Opera cake is usually credited to the Parisian pastry house Dalloyau in the mid-twentieth century, though another Paris pastry chef also claimed it. The name is often linked to the Paris Opera, with the layers said to evoke its stage.
