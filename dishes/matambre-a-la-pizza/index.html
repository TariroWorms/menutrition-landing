Matambre a la pizza (pizza-topped flank)

Origin: Argentina

Matambre a la pizza takes the thin beef flank, softens it, grills it and then covers it with toppings borrowed from pizza. Tomato sauce, melted mozzarella, oregano and sometimes peppers, ham or olives sit on top of a large tender sheet of beef. It is cut into squares or wedges and shared at the table. Children in particular love it.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg beef flank (matambre)
  • 1 l milk for simmering (optional)
  • 250 ml tomato sauce
  • 300 g mozzarella, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, in strips
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt

Method

  1. Trim the flank and simmer it in milk for about 1 hour to tenderise, or grill it slowly from the start.
  2. Drain, salt and grill over medium embers 15–20 minutes per side.
  3. With the better-looking side up, spread the tomato sauce over the meat.
  4. Lay on mozzarella and pepper strips, sprinkle with oregano, cover the grill and cook until the cheese melts.
  5. Cut into squares and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light mozzarella and a thin layer of it to cut fat.
  • Add extra vegetables on top, such as tomato slices, mushrooms or onion.
  • Simmer in water or broth instead of milk if you want fewer calories.

History

This dish is a playful meeting of the Argentine parrilla and the Italian pizza culture of Buenos Aires. Matambre is tough when grilled plainly, so cooks often first simmer it in milk to soften it.
