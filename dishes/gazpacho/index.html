🍅 Gazpacho

Origin: Spain · Andalusia

Gazpacho is a chilled Andalusian soup of raw ripe tomatoes, cucumber, green pepper and garlic, blended with olive oil and a splash of vinegar. It is smooth, cool and refreshing, with a gentle acidity. It is served cold in bowls or glasses on hot summer days, often topped with finely diced vegetables.

What it is made of

tomatocucumberbell peppergarlicolive oilvinegar

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cucumber, peeled and chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp sherry vinegar
  • Salt, cold water as needed

Method

  1. Put the tomatoes, cucumber, pepper and garlic in a blender.
  2. Blend until very smooth, then add the vinegar and salt.
  3. With the motor running, pour in the olive oil so the soup turns creamy.
  4. Thin with a little cold water if needed and pass through a sieve for a silkier texture.
  5. Chill for at least 2 hours and serve topped with diced cucumber and pepper.

How to make it lighter

  • Most of the calories come from the oil — 2 tbsp instead of 3 still gives a creamy body (1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal).
  • Serve it as a starter: a large bowl is filling and very light.
  • Garnish with extra diced vegetables rather than fried toppings.

History

Gazpacho comes from Andalusia in southern Spain, where it began as a simple field-workers' dish of pounded ingredients with oil and vinegar. Tomatoes and peppers, brought from the Americas, became its base only in later centuries. Today it is a summer staple across Spain and far beyond.
