Minchi

Origin: Macau

Minced beef and pork are sautéed with onion and small cubes of fried potato, then seasoned with soy and Worcestershire sauce. A fried egg goes on top and it is served over rice. The dish is savory, a little sweet and very comforting.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g minced beef and pork mixed
  • 2 potatoes, diced and fried
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 4 eggs
  • cooked rice for serving

Method

  1. Fry the diced potatoes until golden and set aside.
  2. Sauté the onion, then brown the mince.
  3. Season with soy and Worcestershire sauce.
  4. Mix in the potatoes and warm through.
  5. Fry the eggs and place on top with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the potato cubes instead of frying.
  • Use lean mince.
  • Add peas or carrot for extra vegetables.

History

Minchi is a Macanese home dish whose name comes from the English word minced. It combines Portuguese-style frying with Chinese soy seasoning.
