🍰 Saffron Cake

Origin: Middle East · Gulf

Saffron cake is a golden sponge cake made with flour, sugar, butter, eggs and milk, perfumed with saffron and ground cardamom. The saffron gives the crumb a warm yellow colour and a floral aroma, and the cake bakes up moist and soft. It is popular in Gulf and Persian-influenced homes, served in squares or slices with Arabic coffee or tea.

Recipe

10 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 200 g sugar
  • 150 g butter, soft
  • 3 eggs
  • 200 ml milk
  • 1 pinch saffron, steeped in 2 tbsp warm milk
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 2 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C and grease a 24 cm tin.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar until light, then beat in the eggs one by one.
  3. Add the saffron milk and cardamom.
  4. Fold in the flour and baking powder alternately with the milk.
  5. Pour into the tin and bake for 35–40 minutes until a skewer comes out clean.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut 12 smaller squares instead of 10.
  • Reduce the sugar by a quarter; the saffron and cardamom carry the flavour.
  • Serve with unsweetened Arabic coffee or tea.

History

Saffron and cardamom are classic flavourings across the Gulf, Iran and South Asia, and saffron-scented cakes are common in home baking throughout the region today.
