Matapa (cassava leaves in peanut and coconut sauce)

Origin: Mozambique

Matapa is made from pounded cassava leaves cooked slowly with ground peanuts, coconut milk and garlic. It is thick, green, creamy and slightly nutty. It is served with rice or cornmeal porridge and sometimes with shrimp.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g cassava leaves, pounded or finely chopped
  • 100 g ground peanuts
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 onion
  • Salt
  • 200 ml water

Method

  1. Simmer the leaves in water for 30 minutes.
  2. Add onion and garlic.
  3. Stir in the ground peanuts.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk.
  5. Cook uncovered 30 minutes, stirring often, until thick.
  6. Season and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Cook cassava leaves thoroughly, as they must be well cooked to be safe.
  • Use light coconut milk to cut calories.
  • Spinach or kale can stand in for cassava leaves.

History

It is one of the best-known dishes of Mozambique, found along the coast and inland. The dish reflects local cassava and groundnut growing and Indian Ocean coconut cooking. Recipes differ by region.
