☕ Granita al caffè

Granita al caffè is Sicily's coffee granita: strong espresso sweetened with sugar, frozen and scraped into fine, dark ice crystals. It is bitter-sweet, intensely aromatic and refreshing. Classically it is served in a glass topped with a generous spoonful of whipped cream (panna), which softens the bitterness as it melts into the ice.

What it is made of

espressosugarcream

Recipe

4 servings240 min

Ingredients

  • 300 ml freshly brewed espresso
  • 200 ml water
  • 90 g sugar
  • 150 ml cream, whipped, for serving

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot espresso, add the water and cool.
  2. Pour into a shallow metal tray and freeze.
  3. Every 30 minutes, scrape with a fork to break up the crystals, for about 3–4 hours.
  4. Spoon into glasses and top with whipped cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Top with a small spoonful of cream instead of a large dollop, or skip it.
  • Use less sugar — the coffee flavour carries the dessert.
  • Serve in an espresso cup or small glass for a modest portion.

History

Coffee granita belongs to the Sicilian granita tradition and is also popular in Naples and elsewhere in southern Italy. In Sicily it is a classic summer breakfast or afternoon refreshment at cafés.
