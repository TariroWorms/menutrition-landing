Ovocné knedlíky (fruit dumplings)

Origin: Czech Republic

Ovocné knedlíky are soft dumplings made from a quark dough, each wrapped around a plum, apricot or strawberry. They are boiled, rolled in melted butter and sprinkled with sugar and grated dry quark. The result is sweet, juicy and filling, usually eaten as a main course in summer.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g quark
  • 200 g flour
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp semolina
  • 12 small plums or apricots
  • 12 sugar cubes
  • 50 g butter
  • 50 g dry quark (tvaroh), grated, and sugar to serve

Method

  1. Mix quark, flour, egg, semolina and a pinch of salt into a soft dough and rest 15 minutes.
  2. Stone the fruit and put a sugar cube in each.
  3. Wrap each fruit in a piece of dough and seal well.
  4. Boil in salted water for 8 minutes after they float.
  5. Roll in melted butter and top with sugar and grated quark.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter and sugar on top.
  • Pick ripe fruit so little sugar is needed.
  • Make smaller dumplings for a lighter portion.

History

They are a traditional Czech summer dish, made when fresh fruit is in season. Many families have their own preferred fruit and dough.
