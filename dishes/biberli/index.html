🍪 Biberli

Origin: Switzerland · Appenzell

Biberli are small Swiss honey gingerbread cakes with a soft filling of almond paste. A dark, spiced honey dough is rolled around a strip of almond paste, cut into rectangles and baked until just set, so they stay soft and chewy. They are fragrant with cinnamon, cloves and aniseed, keep well for weeks, and are eaten with coffee or given as gifts, especially at Christmas.

Recipe

12 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g plain flour
  • 150 g honey
  • 50 g sugar
  • 60 ml water
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tsp gingerbread spice (cinnamon, cloves, aniseed, coriander, nutmeg)
  • 200 g almond paste
  • 1 tbsp honey mixed with 1 tbsp water, for glazing

Method

  1. Warm the honey, sugar and water until the sugar dissolves, then let the mixture cool to lukewarm.
  2. Mix the flour, baking soda and spices, pour in the honey mixture and knead into a smooth dough. Wrap and rest overnight at room temperature.
  3. Roll the dough out to 3 mm and cut into strips 8 cm wide. Roll the almond paste into ropes and lay one along each strip.
  4. Fold the dough over the filling, press the edges together and turn seam side down. Cut into 5 cm pieces.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 10–12 minutes, then brush with the honey glaze while still hot and leave to cool.

How to make it lighter

  • Biberli are dense and sweet, so one small piece with coffee is a typical portion; cut larger ones in half.
  • Make them smaller — 30 g instead of 60 g — so a treat fits more easily into a daily calorie budget.
  • Skip the honey glaze; it adds sugar but little to the flavour once the cakes are wrapped and stored.

History

Honey gingerbread, known as Biber in eastern Switzerland, has a long tradition in Appenzell and St. Gallen, and the almond-filled Biberli are a speciality of the region. Today they are sold in bakeries throughout Switzerland all year round.
