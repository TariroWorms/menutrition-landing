Chicken paprikash is a Hungarian braise of chicken cooked slowly with onion, bell pepper and plenty of sweet paprika, finished with sour cream. The sauce turns a rich brick-orange, creamy and mildly smoky. This version is served over egg noodles, which soak up the sauce.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 200 g sour cream
  • 300 g egg noodles
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper, 300 ml water

Method

  1. Fry the onion in oil until soft and golden, about 8 minutes.
  2. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the paprika, then add the chicken, pepper, salt and water.
  3. Cover and simmer for 35–40 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  4. Cook the noodles in salted water and drain.
  5. Stir a few spoons of hot sauce into the sour cream, then stir it back into the pot and warm without boiling.
  6. Serve the chicken and sauce over the noodles.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken pieces to cut fat noticeably.
  • Use half the sour cream and a lighter variety; the paprika keeps the colour and flavour.
  • Serve a smaller portion of noodles with a cucumber salad on the side.

History

Paprikash (paprikás csirke) belongs to the family of Hungarian paprika stews that grew popular in the 19th century, after paprika became a defining spice of the country. Adding sour cream at the end is what sets paprikash apart from pörkölt, its close relative.
