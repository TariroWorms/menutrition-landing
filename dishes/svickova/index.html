Svíčková is a festive Czech dish of braised beef sirloin served in a smooth, creamy sauce made from the puréed root vegetables it was cooked with. Slices of beef and soft bread dumplings are ladled with the tangy-sweet sauce and topped with cranberry compote. It is a classic Sunday and celebration meal in Czech homes and restaurants.

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef sirloin
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 1/4 celeriac or 2 celery stalks, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 bay leaves and 5 allspice berries
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 500 ml water
  • 200 ml cream
  • Bread dumplings and cranberry compote to serve

Method

  1. Brown the beef on all sides in the oil and set aside.
  2. Fry the carrot, celery and onion in the same pot until golden.
  3. Return the beef, add the spices, vinegar and water, cover and braise at 160°C for about 2 hours until tender.
  4. Remove the beef and spices, blend the vegetables with the cooking liquid, then stir in the cream and simmer until smooth.
  5. Slice the beef and serve with the sauce, sliced bread dumplings and a spoonful of cranberry compote.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve 2–3 slices of dumpling instead of the usual 5–6.
  • Replace half the cream with extra puréed vegetables and cooking liquid for a lighter sauce.
  • Trim visible fat from the beef and keep the cranberry topping to a single spoon.

History

Svíčková is regarded as one of the defining dishes of Czech cuisine. Today it is served at family celebrations and is a staple of traditional Czech restaurants.
