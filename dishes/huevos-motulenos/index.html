Huevos motuleños (Yucatecan breakfast eggs)

Origin: Mexico · Yucatán

Huevos motuleños are a generous Yucatecan breakfast: fried tortillas spread with black beans, topped with fried eggs and covered with a tomato sauce, diced ham, peas and crumbled cheese. Many cooks add fried plantain on the side. The mix of crunchy, soft, salty and slightly sweet makes it very satisfying.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 g refried black beans
  • 250 ml tomato sauce with habanero (optional)
  • 60 g ham, diced
  • 60 g peas, cooked
  • 40 g aged cheese, crumbled
  • 1 ripe plantain (optional)

Method

  1. Fry the tortillas briefly until crisp and drain.
  2. Spread each tortilla with warm black beans.
  3. Fry the eggs and place them on the tortillas.
  4. Spoon hot tomato sauce over the eggs.
  5. Scatter with ham, peas and cheese and serve with fried plantain.

How to make it lighter

  • Use toasted rather than fried tortillas.
  • Poach the eggs instead of frying them.
  • Skip the ham or use turkey ham to cut fat and salt.

History

The dish is named after the town of Motul in Yucatán, where it is said to have been created in the early twentieth century. It is now served for breakfast throughout the peninsula and in many Mexican cafés.
