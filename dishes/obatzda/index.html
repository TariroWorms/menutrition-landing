🧀 Obatzda

Origin: Germany · Bavaria

Obatzda is a Bavarian cheese spread made by mashing ripe Camembert with butter, finely chopped onion, sweet paprika and a splash of beer. It is creamy, tangy and slightly spicy, with a bright orange colour from the paprika. It is a classic of Bavarian beer gardens, served with radishes, onion rings and pretzels.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g ripe Camembert
  • 50 g soft butter
  • 1 small onion, very finely chopped
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 2 tbsp beer
  • Salt, pepper and a pinch of caraway

Method

  1. Cut the Camembert into small pieces and mash with a fork.
  2. Add the soft butter and mix until fairly smooth with a few chunks left.
  3. Stir in the onion, paprika, caraway and beer, then season with salt and pepper.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes and serve with radishes and onion rings.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce the butter by half; the Camembert already gives creaminess.
  • Serve with lots of radishes, cucumber and pepper strips for dipping.
  • Measure out 2–3 tablespoons per person — it is a rich spread.

History

Obatzda comes from Bavaria, where it was a way to use up ripe soft cheese; it is often credited to a Freising beer-garden landlady in the 1920s. Today it is a standard item on beer-garden menus.
