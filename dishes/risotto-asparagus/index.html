🌿 Asparagus Risotto

Origin: Italy · Veneto & Lombardy

Asparagus risotto is a spring classic: Arborio rice slowly stirred with shallots and white wine, with tender asparagus tips folded in near the end. Butter and Parmesan finish it into a creamy, loose texture that still keeps the grains al dente. It is served as a first course or a light main, bright green and fresh-tasting.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g Arborio rice
  • 400 g asparagus
  • 2 shallots, finely chopped
  • 100 ml dry white wine
  • 30 g butter
  • 50 g Parmesan, grated
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.2 l hot water
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Cut the asparagus tips off and slice the stalks; simmer the stalks in the water for flavour.
  2. Soften the shallots in the oil, add the rice and toast for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the wine and let it evaporate.
  4. Add the hot asparagus water a ladle at a time, stirring, for 14 minutes, then add the tips and cook 4 minutes more.
  5. Off the heat, stir in the butter and Parmesan and season.

How to make it lighter

  • Double the asparagus and cut the rice to 240 g for a lighter, larger-looking plate.
  • 1 tbsp butter ≈ 100 kcal: finish with 15 g instead of 30 g.
  • A 250 g portion with a green salad keeps a meal moderate.

History

Risotto agli asparagi is made across northern Italy when asparagus is in season, especially in the Veneto, which is known for its asparagus. Today it is a fixture of spring menus worldwide.
