🦑 Fried calamari

Origin: Mediterranean

Fried calamari are squid rings tossed in flour and deep-fried until golden and crisp. The coating is light and crunchy, while the squid inside stays tender if cooked quickly. They are served hot with lemon wedges as a starter or bar snack across the Mediterranean, the Balkans and Turkey, where the dish is known as kalamar.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g cleaned squid, cut into rings
  • 100 g flour
  • 1 tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper
  • Oil for deep-frying
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Method

  1. Pat the squid rings dry with paper towels.
  2. Mix the flour with salt and pepper and toss the squid in it, shaking off the excess.
  3. Heat the oil to 180 °C and fry the squid in small batches for 1–2 minutes until light golden.
  4. Drain on paper towels and serve immediately with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Grilled or pan-seared squid with lemon has far fewer calories than the fried version.
  • Share a plate as a starter and fill up on salad.
  • Skip creamy dips and use lemon juice instead.

History

Frying squid in flour is a long-standing tradition in coastal kitchens around the Mediterranean, and the dish is now a fixture of seaside taverns and fish restaurants throughout the region.
