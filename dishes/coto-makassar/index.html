Coto Makassar (Makassar beef and peanut soup)

Origin: Indonesia · South Sulawesi

Coto Makassar is a brown, savory soup of beef and offal in a broth thickened with ground peanuts. It is warmly spiced with coriander, cumin, lemongrass and galangal, with a nutty body. It is eaten with ketupat rice cakes or burasa, a coconut rice, and spooned with lime and chili. Many people have it for breakfast.

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef and tripe
  • 2 l water
  • 60 g roasted peanuts, ground
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, 3 cm galangal
  • 2 tsp coriander and 1 tsp cumin
  • shallots, garlic, celery leaf, lime

Method

  1. Boil beef and tripe, discard first water and simmer until tender.
  2. Grind the spices with shallots and garlic and fry.
  3. Add the paste and lemongrass to the broth and simmer 30 minutes.
  4. Stir in ground peanuts and cook until slightly thick.
  5. Slice the meat and serve with celery, lime and rice cakes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean cuts and less offal.
  • Reduce peanuts and skim fat.
  • Serve with fewer rice cakes and extra herbs.

History

The soup is a specialty of Makassar in South Sulawesi and one of the city's best-known dishes. It is traditionally simmered for hours in a clay or large pot.
