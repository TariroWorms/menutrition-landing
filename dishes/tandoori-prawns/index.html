🍤 Tandoori Prawns

Origin: India

Tandoori prawns are large shrimp marinated in yogurt, lemon and tandoori spices, then grilled in a tandoor or under intense heat for just a few minutes. They turn smoky and lightly charred on the outside while staying tender and sweet inside, with a tangy, spiced crust. They are served as a starter with lemon wedges, sliced onion and green chutney.

Recipe

3 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g large raw prawns, peeled and deveined
  • 150 g thick plain yogurt
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp tandoori spice mix
  • 2 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  1. Toss the prawns with lemon juice and salt and leave for 10 minutes.
  2. Mix yogurt, tandoori spices and garlic, coat the prawns and marinate for 20–30 minutes.
  3. Thread onto skewers and brush very lightly with oil.
  4. Grill under a very hot grill or on a griddle for 2–3 minutes per side, until just opaque and charred at the edges.
  5. Serve at once with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Prawns are lean, so keep them that way: grill instead of frying and skip butter basting.
  • Use low-fat yogurt in the marinade.
  • Pair with a large salad or grilled vegetables to make a full but light meal.

History

Tandoori prawns are a restaurant adaptation of North Indian tandoor cooking to seafood, popular in coastal Indian cities and on Indian restaurant menus abroad. They are eaten today wherever tandoori dishes are served.
