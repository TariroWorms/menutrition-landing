🌯 Enchiladas verdes

Enchiladas verdes are corn tortillas filled with shredded chicken and bathed in a bright green tomatillo sauce, then baked and finished with crumbled cheese and a drizzle of cream. The sauce is tangy, fresh and gently spicy, balanced by the rich toppings. They are a staple of home cooking and fondas across Mexico.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 400 g chicken breast
  • 500 g tomatillos, husked
  • 1–2 serrano chilies
  • ½ onion, 1 garlic clove
  • A handful of fresh coriander
  • 80 g fresh crumbly cheese
  • 60 ml cream
  • Salt

Method

  1. Poach the chicken in salted water for 15 minutes and shred it.
  2. Boil tomatillos and chilies for 8 minutes, blend with onion, garlic and coriander, and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Warm the tortillas, fill with chicken, roll and place in a dish.
  4. Cover with the sauce and bake at 190 °C for 15 minutes.
  5. Finish with cheese and cream.

How to make it lighter

  • The tomatillo sauce is very light, so be generous with it and modest with the cream.
  • Heat tortillas in a dry pan rather than frying them.
  • Add a side of sliced cucumber and radish for volume.

History

Green salsa made from tomatillos and chilies is an ancient Mexican preparation, and enchiladas verdes are one of the classic everyday dishes built on it, eaten throughout central Mexico today.
