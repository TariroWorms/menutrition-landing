Macaroni soup with ham (fo tui tung fan)

Origin: Hong Kong

Ham macaroni soup is a bowl of soft macaroni in a clear chicken broth with slices of ham, often with a fried egg on top. It is mild, salty and comforting. It is a classic cha chaan teng breakfast set, served with toast and a drink.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g macaroni
  • 1 l chicken broth
  • 100 g ham slices
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp salt
  • pinch of white pepper

Method

  1. Boil the macaroni until very soft, about 12 minutes.
  2. Heat the broth with salt and pepper.
  3. Fry the eggs sunny side up.
  4. Place macaroni in bowls and add ham.
  5. Pour over the hot broth and top with the egg.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-sodium broth and lean ham.
  • Add vegetables to the broth.
  • Use wholewheat macaroni.

History

The dish reflects the Western influence on Hong Kong's cafes, where Italian-style pasta was reinterpreted as a simple breakfast soup. It is typical of the city's East-meets-West eating culture.
