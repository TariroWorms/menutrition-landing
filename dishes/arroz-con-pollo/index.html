🍚 Arroz con pollo

Arroz con pollo is a comforting one-pot dish of rice cooked with chicken, peas and onion. In Chile it is a simple everyday family meal, with tender rice that absorbs the flavour of the chicken and a little colour from carrot and pepper. It is usually served with a fresh tomato and onion salad.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken thighs or pieces, skinless
  • 300 g long-grain rice
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 150 g green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 600 ml chicken stock or water
  • 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, salt and pepper
  • Chopped parsley to serve

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, paprika and cumin.
  2. Brown the chicken in the oil in a wide pot, then set aside.
  3. In the same pot cook the onion, garlic, carrot and pepper for 5 minutes until soft.
  4. Stir in the rice for 1 minute, then pour in the stock and bring to the boil.
  5. Return the chicken, cover and cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
  6. Add the peas for the last 5 minutes, rest covered for 5 minutes, fluff and sprinkle with parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken and measure the oil: one tablespoon is about 120 kcal.
  • Add more peas, carrot and pepper and a little less rice for extra fibre at the same calories.
  • Serve with a large tomato and onion salad instead of a second portion of rice.

History

Rice and chicken dishes are found across Latin America and trace back to Spanish cooking, and each country has made its own version. In Chile arroz con pollo became a practical home meal, easy to make for a crowd from a single pot. It remains a standard of family lunches and a good way to use leftover roast or boiled chicken.
