Gulf Shakshuka

Origin: Arabian Peninsula · Gulf

Gulf shakshuka is a homestyle breakfast of eggs scrambled into softened tomato and onion with green chili, cumin and black pepper. It is soft, juicy and gently spicy, cooked in minutes in one pan. It is a staple of family breakfasts across the Gulf.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1–2 green chilies, chopped
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil and cook the onion and chili until soft.
  2. Add the tomatoes, cumin, pepper and salt and cook until saucy, about 7 minutes.
  3. Add the eggs and stir gently until softly set.
  4. Serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tsp of oil instead of 1 tbsp — 1 tbsp oil is about 120 kcal.
  • Add more tomato and onion to stretch the dish without extra eggs.
  • Pair with fresh cucumber and tomato slices for a fuller plate.

History

Egg and tomato breakfasts of this kind are cooked in homes throughout Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait and the rest of the Gulf, where shakshuka is one of the most common morning dishes.
