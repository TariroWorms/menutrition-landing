Halawat jazar (Iraqi carrot halva)

Origin: Iraq

Halawat jazar is a soft, fragrant sweet made by cooking grated carrots slowly with milk, sugar and ghee until thick and glossy. Cardamom perfumes it and chopped nuts add crunch. It is served warm or at room temperature with tea.

Recipe

6 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g grated carrots
  • 250 ml milk
  • 100 g sugar
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 40 g chopped pistachios

Method

  1. Cook the carrots in ghee for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the milk and simmer until mostly absorbed.
  3. Stir in sugar and cardamom.
  4. Cook, stirring, until thick and glossy.
  5. Top with pistachios and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by a third; carrots are naturally sweet.
  • Use less ghee and low-fat milk.
  • Keep portions small.

History

Carrot sweets cooked in dairy and sugar are popular across the Middle East and South Asia. In Iraq the dish is a homely dessert for holidays and guests.
