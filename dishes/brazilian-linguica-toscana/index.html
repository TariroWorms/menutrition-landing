Linguiça toscana (Brazilian grilled sausage)

Origin: Brazil · South

Linguiça toscana is a thick, coarsely ground pork sausage that almost always appears on a Brazilian barbecue grill. It is mildly seasoned, so the flavour of the pork and the smoke comes through. Guests eat it sliced as a starter with farofa and vinaigrette salsa, or tucked into a crusty roll.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g linguiça toscana sausages
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Parsley and salt

Method

  1. Light a charcoal grill and let it settle to medium heat.
  2. Prick the sausages lightly and place them on the grill away from the hottest coals.
  3. Turn every few minutes until browned all over and cooked through, about 25 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile mix onion, tomato, vinegar, oil, parsley and salt for a quick vinaigrette.
  5. Rest the sausages for 3 minutes, slice and serve with the vinaigrette.

How to make it lighter

  • Grill over moderate heat so more fat drips away.
  • Serve smaller portions alongside plenty of salad.
  • Choose chicken linguiça for a leaner version.

History

Despite the name, the sausage has little to do with Tuscany; it is a Brazilian style spread by Italian immigrants and later by industrial butchers in the South. It became a fixture of the gaúcho churrasco and is now sold in every Brazilian supermarket.
