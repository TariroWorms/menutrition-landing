🫑 Taktouka

Taktouka is a Moroccan cooked salad of roasted green peppers and tomatoes, simmered with garlic, olive oil and paprika until soft and jammy. It tastes smoky and sweet with a gentle warmth from the spices. It is served warm or at room temperature as one of several small salads at the start of a meal, and scooped up with bread.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 4 green peppers
  • 500 g ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley and coriander
  • Salt

Method

  1. Char the peppers over a flame or under the grill, steam them in a covered bowl, then peel, seed and chop.
  2. Warm the olive oil in a pan, add the garlic, paprika and cumin for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook for 10 minutes until they start to break down.
  4. Stir in the peppers and simmer for 15–20 minutes, until thick and most of the liquid has evaporated.
  5. Season, add the herbs and serve warm or at room temperature.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the olive oil to 1 tbsp and cook in a non-stick pan; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve it as a side to grilled chicken to make a light meal.
  • Scoop it with cucumber slices or lettuce leaves to keep the plate lighter.

History

Taktouka is a standard part of the Moroccan tradition of cooked salads served before the main course, and similar pepper and tomato dishes are made across North Africa.
