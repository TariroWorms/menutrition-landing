🌶️ Çiğ köfte (vegan bulgur köfte)

Origin: Turkey · Şanlıurfa and southeastern Anatolia

Çiğ köfte is a spicy Turkish appetiser made by kneading fine bulgur with tomato paste, onion, isot pepper and cumin until it becomes a soft, cohesive paste. Today it is made without meat, so the bulgur softens through long kneading rather than cooking. It is shaped into small fingers, sprinkled with parsley and eaten wrapped in lettuce leaves with a squeeze of lemon.

Recipe

6 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g fine bulgur
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 2 tbsp isot pepper (Urfa chilli flakes)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 bunch parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp salt, 100 ml hot water
  • Lettuce leaves and lemon wedges to serve

Method

  1. Put the bulgur in a wide bowl, add the onion, isot pepper, cumin and salt and start kneading.
  2. Add the hot water little by little and knead firmly for 15–20 minutes until the bulgur softens.
  3. Knead in the tomato paste until the mixture is smooth, red and holds together.
  4. Mix in most of the parsley.
  5. Squeeze small portions in your fist to form finger shapes and serve with lettuce leaves, lemon and the remaining parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Çiğ köfte has no added fat in this recipe — wrap it in lettuce rather than flatbread to keep it light.
  • Add extra parsley, tomatoes and cucumber on the side to bulk up the plate.
  • Portion by pieces: four or five fingers with salad make a reasonable snack.

History

Çiğ köfte is strongly associated with Şanlıurfa and southeastern Turkey, where it was traditionally kneaded with raw meat. Commercial sale of the raw-meat version is restricted in Turkey, and the meat-free bulgur version now sold by çiğ köfte shops nationwide has become the everyday standard.
