Pempek (Palembang fish cakes with cuko sauce)

Origin: Indonesia · Palembang

Pempek are chewy fish and tapioca cakes, boiled and then fried until golden. They come in shapes such as lenjer, a log, and kapal selam, which hides a boiled egg inside. They are served in slices with cuko, a dark sauce of palm sugar, vinegar, garlic and chili that is sweet, sour and hot. Cucumber and yellow noodles often go on the side.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g white fish, minced
  • 200 g tapioca starch
  • 150 ml cold water, salt
  • 4 boiled eggs
  • 100 g palm sugar, 2 garlic cloves, 3 chilies
  • vinegar, cucumber, oil

Method

  1. Mix fish, starch, water and salt into a firm dough.
  2. Shape logs, or wrap around a whole egg.
  3. Boil until they float, then cool.
  4. Fry in oil until golden.
  5. Simmer palm sugar, garlic, chili and vinegar for cuko and serve over sliced pempek.

How to make it lighter

  • Steam or pan-fry with little oil instead of deep-frying.
  • Use less cuko sauce, which is high in sugar.
  • Add cucumber and extra vegetables on the side.

History

Pempek is the signature food of Palembang in South Sumatra. It is commonly made with local river fish and is eaten as a snack or small meal.
