🍖 Grilled lamb chops

Grilled lamb chops are small rib or loin cuts marinated in garlic, fresh herbs, olive oil and lemon, then cooked fast over high heat. The outside chars and crisps while the inside stays pink and juicy. They are often eaten by hand, straight off the bone, with a simple salad or grilled vegetables.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 8–12 lamb chops (about 1 kg)
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped rosemary and thyme
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice and zest of 1 lemon
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the garlic, herbs, olive oil, lemon zest and juice, salt and pepper.
  2. Coat the chops and marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Heat a grill or griddle pan until very hot.
  4. Grill the chops for 2–3 minutes per side for medium-rare.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes and serve with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the fat cap before cooking — it is the fattiest part of the chop.
  • Brush on the marinade rather than pouring oil over the chops; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve with a big salad or grilled vegetables instead of fries.

History

Grilled lamb with garlic, herbs and lemon is a common way of cooking lamb across the Mediterranean, the Balkans and the Middle East, and lamb chops are a familiar grill and restaurant order today.
