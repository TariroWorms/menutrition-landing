Lubia polo (green bean rice)

Origin: Iran

Lubia polo is a Persian mixed rice in which chopped green beans and small pieces of beef or lamb are layered with half-cooked rice. Tomato paste and cinnamon give it a warm, savory-sweet flavor. It is finished with a crisp golden tahdig and served with yogurt or salad.

What it is made of

Recipe

5 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g basmati rice
  • 250 g green beans
  • 250 g beef, diced
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp turmeric

Method

  1. Brown onion and beef with turmeric, add tomato paste and cinnamon.
  2. Add green beans and cook until nearly tender.
  3. Parboil the rice and drain.
  4. Layer rice and the beef-bean mix in a pot.
  5. Steam on low heat for 45 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil for the tahdig.
  • Add more green beans.
  • Serve with yogurt and salad.

History

It is a popular everyday meal in Iranian homes, especially in summer when green beans are in season.
