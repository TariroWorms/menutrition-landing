Salpicão (Brazilian chicken salad)

Origin: Brazil

Salpicão is a cold salad of shredded chicken mixed with grated carrot, corn, peas, apple and raisins, all bound with mayonnaise. Just before serving it is crowned with a generous handful of thin, crisp potato sticks. The result is creamy, a little sweet and pleasantly crunchy. It is a fixture of Christmas and New Year tables and of family Sunday lunches.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 150 g sweet corn
  • 100 g peas
  • 1 green apple, diced
  • 60 g raisins
  • 200 g mayonnaise
  • 100 g potato sticks
  • Salt, pepper, parsley

Method

  1. Poach the chicken in salted water for 20 minutes, cool and shred finely.
  2. Grate the carrots and dice the apple.
  3. Mix chicken, carrot, corn, peas, apple and raisins in a bowl.
  4. Fold in the mayonnaise and season with salt, pepper and parsley.
  5. Chill for at least 1 hour.
  6. Top with potato sticks right before serving so they stay crisp.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace half of the mayonnaise with plain yogurt.
  • Serve the potato sticks on the side and use only a small sprinkle.
  • Add more grated carrot and shredded cabbage to lighten each portion.

History

The name comes from the Portuguese word for a type of cured sausage once used in similar salads, but in Brazil the dish became chicken-based. Today every family has its own version, and the question of whether raisins belong in it is a classic holiday debate.
