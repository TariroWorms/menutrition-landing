Choros a la chalaca (mussels Callao style)

Origin: Peru

Choros a la chalaca are mussels cooked just until they open and served cold on one half of their shell. Each one is heaped with a bright salsa of finely diced red onion, tomato, corn kernels and ají, dressed with plenty of lime. The result is juicy, tangy and slightly spicy. They are a classic starter in Peruvian seafood restaurants.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg mussels, cleaned
  • 1 red onion (150 g), finely diced
  • 1 tomato (120 g), diced
  • 100 g cooked corn kernels
  • 1 ají amarillo, minced
  • 80 ml lime juice
  • 1 tbsp chopped cilantro, salt

Method

  1. Steam the mussels in a covered pan with a splash of water until they open.
  2. Cool them, remove the top shells and discard any that stayed closed.
  3. Mix onion, tomato, corn, ají and cilantro in a bowl.
  4. Dress the salsa with lime juice and salt and let it rest 5 minutes.
  5. Arrange the mussels on a platter and spoon salsa over each one; serve cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Mussels are lean and rich in iron, so this makes a light starter.
  • Rinse the onion in cold water to soften its bite without adding oil.
  • Serve with a green salad rather than fried sides.

History

The name refers to Callao, the port city next to Lima, whose people are called chalacos. The salsa chalaca itself is also used on other shellfish and on fried fish.
