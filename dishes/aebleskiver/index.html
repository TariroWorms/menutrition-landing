Æbleskiver (Danish pancake puffs)

Origin: Denmark

Æbleskiver are light, airy pancake spheres with a crisp golden shell and a soft center. They have a mild cardamom flavor and are dipped in raspberry jam and dusted with icing sugar. In Denmark they are a classic treat in the weeks before Christmas.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g wheat flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 400 ml buttermilk
  • 50 g butter
  • 30 g sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 40 g fruit jam

Method

  1. Separate the eggs and whisk the yolks with buttermilk, melted butter, sugar and cardamom.
  2. Stir in flour and baking powder to a smooth batter.
  3. Whip the egg whites to soft peaks and fold them in gently.
  4. Heat the hollow pan, brush each hole with butter and fill two thirds with batter.
  5. Cook on medium heat, turning each puff with a skewer until golden and round on all sides.
  6. Dust with icing sugar and serve with jam.

How to make it lighter

  • Use whole-grain flour for part of the flour.
  • Brush the pan lightly with oil rather than extra butter.
  • Serve with berries instead of sweetened jam.

History

The name means apple slices, which hints that apple pieces were once put inside the batter, although today the puffs are usually plain. They are cooked in a cast-iron pan with round hollows and turned with a thin skewer.
