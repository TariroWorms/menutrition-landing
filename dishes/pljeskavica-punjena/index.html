🍔 Stuffed pljeskavica (punjena pljeskavica)

Origin: Serbia · Balkans

Punjena pljeskavica is the stuffed version of the Serbian grilled patty: two thin layers of minced beef and pork are sealed around a filling of kajmak and cheese, then grilled until the outside is smoky and the centre melts. It is served with chopped raw onion, and cutting into it releases a creamy, cheesy core.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g minced beef
  • 200 g minced pork
  • 1 onion, finely chopped, plus 1 onion for serving
  • 80 g kajmak
  • 80 g white cheese, crumbled
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the beef, pork, chopped onion, salt and pepper; knead well and chill 20 minutes.
  2. Divide into 8 portions and press each into a thin round.
  3. Mix the kajmak and cheese and spread it on 4 rounds, leaving a border.
  4. Cover with the remaining rounds and press the edges firmly to seal.
  5. Grill over medium-high heat for 6–7 minutes per side and serve with raw onion.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the filling and use a lower-fat white cheese; most of the extra calories are in the centre.
  • Choose lean minced beef to keep the fat down.
  • Make smaller patties and fill the plate with grilled peppers and salad.

History

The stuffed pljeskavica is a richer variation of the classic Balkan grilled patty and appears on the menus of grill restaurants (roštilj) across Serbia and neighbouring countries.
