🌮 Suadero tacos

Origin: Mexico · Mexico City

Tacos de suadero are filled with thin beef cut, here brisket, slowly confited and then chopped and crisped on a round griddle. The meat is soft and juicy with crunchy edges. Served in small corn tortillas, the tacos are topped with chopped onion and cilantro, a spoonful of red salsa roja and a squeeze of lime.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g beef brisket, cut into thin strips
  • 1 onion, halved, plus 1 onion finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, salt
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 200 ml salsa roja
  • 1 bunch cilantro, 2 limes

Method

  1. Put the beef in a pot with the halved onion, garlic, salt and water just to cover and simmer, covered, for about 2 hours until tender.
  2. Uncover and let the liquid cook away so the meat begins to fry in its own fat and the oil.
  3. Chop the meat and crisp it on a hot griddle for a few minutes.
  4. Warm the tortillas on the griddle.
  5. Fill with meat, top with chopped onion, cilantro and salsa roja and serve with lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Drain the meat well on paper before filling the tortillas — much of the fat stays behind.
  • Two tacos instead of three already trims the plate noticeably.
  • Pile on onion, cilantro, lime and salsa — they add a lot of flavour for very few calories.

History

Suadero tacos are a speciality of Mexico City, where they are sold at street stands that cook the meat in its own fat on a domed griddle. They remain one of the city's signature late-night street foods.
