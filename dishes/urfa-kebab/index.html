🍢 Urfa Kebab

Origin: Turkey · Şanlıurfa

Urfa kebab is a skewer of hand-minced lamb mixed with lamb tail fat and mild, smoky Urfa pepper flakes, grilled over charcoal. It is close to Adana kebab but without the hot chilli, so the flavour is deep and gently warm rather than fiery. It is served with grilled tomatoes and sliced onion.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb, finely minced
  • 50 g lamb tail fat, finely chopped
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tsp Urfa pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 tomatoes

Method

  1. Grate the onion, squeeze out its juice and mix the pulp with the lamb, tail fat, Urfa pepper and salt.
  2. Knead for 5 minutes until sticky, then chill for 30 minutes.
  3. Press the mixture onto flat skewers in long, even sausages.
  4. Grill over hot charcoal for 8–10 minutes, turning, together with the whole tomatoes.
  5. Serve with the grilled tomatoes and sliced raw onion.

How to make it lighter

  • Use leaner lamb and less tail fat; the fat is where most calories come from.
  • Pair one skewer with a large salad instead of rice.
  • Grill extra vegetables such as peppers and tomatoes to bulk up the plate.

History

The kebab is named after Şanlıurfa in southeastern Turkey, where it is a local staple; it is now sold across Turkey alongside Adana kebab as the milder of the two.
