🌿 Gnocchi al Pesto

Origin: Italy · Liguria

Gnocchi al pesto are potato dumplings tossed with a fresh green sauce of pounded basil, pine nuts and parmesan loosened with olive oil. The pesto clings to the soft, pillowy gnocchi and gives a bright, herby, nutty flavour. It is served warm straight after tossing, with a little extra parmesan on top.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g floury potatoes
  • 200 g plain flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 50 g fresh basil leaves
  • 30 g pine nuts
  • 40 g parmesan, grated
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins until tender, peel while warm and press through a ricer.
  2. Mix in the flour and salt to a soft dough, roll into ropes and cut into 2 cm pieces.
  3. Pound or blend the basil, garlic and pine nuts with a pinch of salt, then stir in the parmesan and olive oil.
  4. Boil the gnocchi in salted water until they float, about 2 minutes, and drain, keeping a little water.
  5. Toss the gnocchi off the heat with the pesto, loosening with a spoonful of cooking water, and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Make the pesto with 2 tbsp oil and a few spoonfuls of cooking water — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use less pesto per plate: 1 heaped tbsp per portion still coats the gnocchi well.
  • Bulk out the bowl with steamed green beans or courgette tossed in the same pesto.

History

Pesto comes from Liguria, around Genoa, where it is traditionally pounded in a marble mortar; potato gnocchi with pesto is a common Ligurian pairing.
