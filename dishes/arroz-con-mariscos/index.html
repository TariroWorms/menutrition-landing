🦐 Peruvian arroz con mariscos

Arroz con mariscos is Peru's seafood rice: rice cooked with shrimp, squid and mussels in a deep red-orange base of ají panca chilli paste, then finished with plenty of fresh cilantro. It is moist rather than soupy, gently smoky from the chilli and briny from the shellfish, and is usually served with lime wedges and a sharp onion salsa.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g long-grain rice
  • 300 g peeled shrimp
  • 250 g squid, cut into rings
  • 500 g mussels, cleaned
  • 3 tbsp ají panca paste
  • 1 onion and 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, 1 tsp ground cumin, salt
  • 700 ml hot water
  • 1 large bunch cilantro, chopped, and lime wedges to serve

Method

  1. Steam the mussels in a covered pan with a splash of water until they open; keep the liquid and discard any that stay closed.
  2. Fry the onion, pepper and garlic in the oil until soft, then stir in the ají panca and cumin for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the rice and stir to coat, pour in the hot water plus the mussel liquid, season with salt, cover and cook on low heat for 15 minutes.
  4. Stir in the shrimp and squid, cover and cook for 4–5 minutes more, just until the shrimp turn pink.
  5. Fold in the mussels and most of the cilantro, rest for 3 minutes and serve with lime and the remaining cilantro.

How to make it lighter

  • Rice is the main source of calories: use more shrimp and squid and a little less rice for a lighter, higher-protein plate.
  • Cook the base in 1 tablespoon of oil instead of 2 — each tablespoon is about 120 kcal.
  • Serve with a large onion-and-tomato salad to fill the plate with fewer calories.

History

Arroz con mariscos is a fixture of cevicherías and seafood restaurants along the Peruvian coast. It is often described as a Peruvian cousin of Spanish paella, reworked with local chilli pastes such as ají panca and ají amarillo.
