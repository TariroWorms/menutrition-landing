Sweet potato balls (digua qiu)

Origin: Taiwan

These are small fried balls with a thin crisp shell and a hollow, chewy interior made from sweet potato and tapioca starch. They taste mildly sweet and are usually dusted with a little plum or sugar powder. They are sold in paper bags at night markets and eaten hot.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g sweet potato, steamed and mashed
  • 100 g tapioca starch
  • 30 g sugar
  • 1 l oil for frying

Method

  1. Mash the hot sweet potato with the sugar.
  2. Knead in the tapioca starch until a soft dough forms.
  3. Roll into balls about 3 cm wide.
  4. Fry in oil at 160 C until golden and puffed.
  5. Drain and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Air-fry instead of deep-frying.
  • Make smaller balls for a lighter portion.
  • Skip added sugar if the potatoes are sweet.

History

Sweet potatoes have been an important crop in Taiwan for centuries, and the fried balls are a popular modern way to use them. The hollow center forms as the dough puffs during frying.
