🌶️ Salata hara (spicy salad)

Origin: Levant · Middle East

Salata hara, “hot salad”, is a sharp chopped salad of tomato, onion and fresh green chili with parsley, lemon juice and olive oil. It is fiery, juicy and tangy, and is meant to cut through the richness of grilled meat. It is served in small bowls beside kebabs, shish tawook and shawarma plates, or spooned onto bread.

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2–3 green chilies, finely chopped
  • 1 small bunch parsley, chopped
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Chop the tomatoes, onion and chilies finely; keep the seeds for more heat.
  2. Mix with the parsley in a bowl.
  3. Dress with lemon juice, olive oil and salt and leave for 10 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the oil to 1 tbsp and let the lemon and chili carry the flavour: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use it as a sauce for grilled meat instead of creamy dips.
  • Add diced cucumber to stretch the bowl with very few calories.

History

Spicy chopped salads like this are served with grills in restaurants and homes across the Levant and the wider Arab world.
