🍛 Thai red curry with beef (gaeng phet neua)

Thai red curry with beef combines thin slices of beef with red curry paste, coconut milk and bamboo shoots in a rich, spicy, fragrant sauce. Fish sauce adds savoury depth, and Thai basil leaves stirred in at the end bring an anise-like aroma. It is served hot with steamed jasmine rice.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef sirloin or flank, thinly sliced
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp red curry paste
  • 200 g bamboo shoots, sliced
  • 1–2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp palm or brown sugar
  • 2 kaffir lime leaves
  • A handful of Thai basil leaves

Method

  1. Simmer a third of the coconut milk until the oil begins to separate, then fry the curry paste in it for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the beef and stir until it changes colour.
  3. Pour in the rest of the coconut milk, add the bamboo shoots and lime leaves and simmer for 8–10 minutes.
  4. Season with fish sauce and sugar, stir in the Thai basil and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose a lean cut such as sirloin and trim visible fat.
  • Use light coconut milk or dilute it with water.
  • Fill out the bowl with more bamboo shoots and keep the rice portion small.

History

Gaeng phet, "spicy curry", is a central Thai coconut-milk curry built on a pounded paste of dried red chillies and aromatics, and it is made with many meats. The beef version is a common choice in Thai homes and restaurants.
