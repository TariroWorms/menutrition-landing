Pulla (Finnish cardamom bread)

Origin: Finland

Pulla is a lightly sweet yeast bread, usually braided and brushed with egg before baking. The aroma of cardamom is its signature, and the crumb is soft and a little rich. It is served in slices with coffee and butter.

Recipe

10 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour
  • 250 ml warm milk
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 80 g sugar
  • 2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 80 g soft butter
  • 1 egg plus 1 for brushing
  • Pearl sugar

Method

  1. Mix milk, yeast, sugar, cardamom, egg and flour and knead.
  2. Add butter and knead until smooth.
  3. Let it rise for 1 hour.
  4. Divide into three strands, braid and place on a tray.
  5. Rise 30 minutes, brush with egg, sprinkle sugar and bake at 200 C for 25 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half wholemeal flour for more fibre.
  • Reduce sugar by a quarter without losing much taste.
  • Serve with cheese or egg rather than sweet spreads.

History

Pulla is the centerpiece of the Finnish coffee table and appears at birthdays and family gatherings. Wheat was once costly in Finland, so sweet wheat bread was reserved for festive occasions.
