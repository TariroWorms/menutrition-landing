Beef brisket noodles (Niu nam mian)

Origin: China · Guangdong · Hong Kong

Chunks of beef brisket and tendon are braised until meltingly tender in a soy and star-anise broth, then served over egg noodles. The broth is deeply savory, with a gentle spice and a little sweetness. A few greens and a splash of chili oil are common additions.

Recipe

4 servings170 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef brisket, cubed
  • 150 g beef tendon (optional)
  • 3 slices ginger, 2 star anise
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rock sugar
  • 200 g egg noodles
  • 100 g bok choy

Method

  1. Blanch the beef and tendon for 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Place in a pot with 1.2 liters water, ginger, star anise, soy sauce and sugar.
  3. Simmer gently 2 hours until tender.
  4. Boil the noodles and blanch the greens.
  5. Assemble the noodles in bowls, top with beef and ladle over broth.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim fat from the braising liquid.
  • Choose lean brisket and use fewer noodles.
  • Add extra greens.

History

It is a staple of Hong Kong cha chaan teng and noodle shops. The slow braise is typically done in big batches and kept ready all day.
