Libyan tea with peanuts (Shai bil-kakawiya)

Origin: Libya

This is a strong, dark Libyan tea brewed with plenty of sugar until it develops a thick foam, then served in small glasses with roasted peanuts or almonds floating on top. It is sweet, bitter and nutty at the same time. It is the centre of every long social visit.

What it is made of

teapeanutssugarwater

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp loose black tea
  • 1 l water
  • 80 g sugar
  • 60 g roasted peanuts
  • optional mint leaves

Method

  1. Boil the tea leaves with a little water in a small pot for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the sugar and the rest of the hot water.
  3. Boil again and let it rise twice.
  4. Pour from a height into small glasses to make foam.
  5. Float a few roasted peanuts on each glass.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by half or use a sweetener.
  • Drink it earlier in the day, as it is strong and high in caffeine.
  • Keep the peanut portion small.

History

Tea ceremonies with several rounds are a shared tradition across North Africa. In Libya the three-glass ritual, with foamy tea poured from a height, is a sign of hospitality.
