Momo (Nepalese steamed dumplings)

Origin: Nepal

Momo are small steamed dumplings with a spiced filling of buffalo, chicken or vegetables. The wrapper is thin and tender, and the filling is juicy with ginger, garlic and onion. They are served with a tangy tomato achar.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 300 g minced chicken or buffalo
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • Ginger, garlic, coriander
  • Salt, cumin, timur or pepper
  • Tomato achar to serve

Method

  1. Knead flour with water into a firm dough and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the meat with onion, ginger, garlic, spices and salt.
  3. Roll thin circles and fill each with a spoonful.
  4. Pleat and seal the dumplings.
  5. Steam 10 to 12 minutes and serve with achar.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean meat or vegetables.
  • Steam instead of frying.
  • Use the achar instead of heavier sauces.

History

Momo came to Nepal through the Newar traders and Tibetan neighbours and became the national snack. They are found everywhere from street stalls to restaurants.
