🍝 Spaghetti alla puttanesca

Spaghetti alla puttanesca is a bold Neapolitan pasta in a tomato sauce with black olives, capers, anchovies and garlic. The anchovies melt into the oil and give the sauce a deep savoury base, while olives and capers add briny, salty bursts. It is quick to make from pantry staples and served without cheese.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g spaghetti
  • 400 g peeled tomatoes, crushed
  • 4 anchovy fillets
  • 80 g black olives, pitted and halved
  • 2 tbsp capers, rinsed
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Chilli flakes, parsley and salt

Method

  1. Warm the oil with garlic and chilli, add the anchovies and stir until they melt.
  2. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  3. Stir in the olives and capers and cook 5 minutes more; taste before salting.
  4. Cook the spaghetti until al dente and toss it in the sauce with a splash of cooking water. Finish with parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • The sauce is light; keep the oil to 2 tbsp — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Olives, capers and anchovies are salty, so add little or no salt.
  • Serve 250 g of pasta with a side of grilled vegetables.

History

Puttanesca is a southern Italian dish associated with Naples and the island of Ischia, and it appears in print from around the mid-twentieth century. Several colourful stories explain its name, but none is confirmed.
