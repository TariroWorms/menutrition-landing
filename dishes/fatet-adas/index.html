Fatet adas (lentil soup over bread)

Origin: Jordan

Fatet adas is a hearty bowl in which pieces of toasted bread are soaked with warm cumin-scented lentil soup and finished with browned onions in olive oil. It is earthy, soft and lemony, with crunchy bread bits. It is a humble winter meal or breakfast.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g red lentils
  • 1 l water or stock
  • 2 pita breads
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • juice of 1 lemon

Method

  1. Boil the lentils with cumin and salt until very soft.
  2. Blend lightly, leaving some texture, and add lemon juice.
  3. Toast or fry the torn pita until crisp.
  4. Fry the sliced onions in oil until dark golden.
  5. Layer bread in bowls, ladle the soup over and top with onions.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the bread instead of frying it.
  • Use whole wheat pita.
  • Add spinach to the soup for iron.

History

The dish follows the broad Levantine tradition of fatteh, where bread absorbs a flavorful liquid so that nothing goes to waste. In Jordan the lentil version is an everyday winter comfort food.
