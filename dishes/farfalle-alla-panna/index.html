Farfalle alla panna is a simple, comforting pasta of bow-tie farfalle coated in warm cream, finished with grated parmesan and a little nutmeg. The sauce is silky, mild and rich, clinging to the folds of the pasta. It is quick to make and a popular choice for children and weeknight dinners.

What it is made of

farfallecreamparmesannutmeg

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 350 g farfalle
  • 250 ml cooking cream
  • 60 g parmesan, grated
  • A pinch of ground nutmeg
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the farfalle in salted boiling water until al dente; keep a cup of pasta water.
  2. Heat the cream gently in a wide pan with the nutmeg.
  3. Add the drained pasta and toss to coat.
  4. Stir in the parmesan off the heat, loosening with pasta water until silky.
  5. Season with pepper and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace half the cream with pasta water — the parmesan still makes the sauce creamy.
  • Keep the parmesan to about 15 g per plate.
  • Serve a smaller pasta portion with steamed peas or a big salad.

History

Pasta with cream and parmesan is a simple everyday dish found in Italian family kitchens and on casual restaurant menus.
