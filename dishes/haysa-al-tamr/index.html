Haysa al-tamr (Jordanian date and flour paste)

Origin: Jordan

Haysa is a soft, sticky dessert of mashed dates mixed with flour toasted in butter or ghee. It is rich and caramel-sweet with a hint of cardamom. It is eaten in small portions, often warm with tea or coffee.

What it is made of

datesflourbuttercardamom

Recipe

6 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g pitted dates
  • 100 g flour
  • 60 g butter or ghee
  • 1/2 tsp cardamom
  • 200 ml water
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds

Method

  1. Simmer the dates in water until very soft and mash.
  2. Toast the flour in the butter until golden and nutty.
  3. Add the date mash and cardamom.
  4. Stir over low heat for 5 minutes until smooth.
  5. Shape on a plate and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter.
  • Serve small portions only.
  • Pair with plain yogurt.

History

The dish comes from Bedouin tradition, where dates, flour and butter were energy-rich staples for travel and desert life. It is still eaten across Jordan and the Arabian Peninsula.
