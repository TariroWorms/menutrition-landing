Nan-e barbari (Persian barbari bread)

Origin: Iran

A long, thick oval flatbread with deep finger grooves and a golden, glossy crust. The inside is soft and chewy, and the sesame on top adds a nutty note. It is the classic Iranian breakfast bread, eaten warm with feta, walnuts, butter and tea.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 350 ml warm water
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 10 g salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp flour and 100 ml water for the glaze
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm water into a sticky dough and knead briefly.
  2. Cover and let it rise for 1 hour until doubled.
  3. Whisk the flour and water glaze and heat it until slightly thick, then cool.
  4. Divide the dough into 4 pieces, stretch each into a long oval and press in lengthwise grooves with your fingers.
  5. Brush with glaze, sprinkle with sesame and bake at 250 °C for 10-12 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half whole wheat flour for more fibre.
  • Serve with fresh cheese and herbs instead of butter.
  • Cut thinner slices to control portion size.

History

Barbari is one of the four traditional flatbreads of Iran, baked in a very hot oven. The shiny crust comes from a thin flour-and-water glaze brushed on before baking.
