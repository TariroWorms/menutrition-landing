Masas finas (Argentine petits fours)

Origin: Argentina

Masas finas are trays of tiny, elegant pastries sold by weight in Argentine confiterías. A typical box mixes mini tartlets with fruit or dulce de leche, chocolate-dipped sponges, almond biscuits, small éclairs and puff pastries. Each piece is one or two bites, rich and very sweet. They are bought for visits, birthdays and Sunday afternoon tea.

Recipe

10 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g shortcrust pastry
  • 200 g dulce de leche
  • 100 g dark chocolate
  • 100 g ground almonds
  • 2 egg whites
  • 80 g sugar
  • Fresh fruit and glaze for tartlets

Method

  1. Line mini tartlet moulds with shortcrust and bake at 180 °C for 12 minutes.
  2. Fill half with dulce de leche and dip their tops in melted chocolate.
  3. Fill the rest with fruit and brush with glaze.
  4. Mix ground almonds, sugar and egg whites, pipe small biscuits and bake at 170 °C for 15 minutes.
  5. Arrange the assortment on a tray and keep cool until serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Pick fruit tartlets over chocolate or cream-filled pieces.
  • Two pieces are a reasonable treat; put the box away after serving.
  • Make the tart bases thinner and use less filling at home.

History

The tradition grew from the European-style confiterías that opened in Buenos Aires and other cities in the late 19th and early 20th centuries. Bringing a box of masas finas to a host is still a common Argentine courtesy.
