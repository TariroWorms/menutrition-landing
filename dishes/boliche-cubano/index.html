Boliche (Cuban stuffed pot roast)

Origin: Cuba

Boliche is a lean eye-of-round roast stuffed with chorizo or ham and slow-braised in a sauce of tomato, onion, pepper and wine or vinegar. The meat is sliced into neat rounds that show the red spiral of the filling. It is served with white rice, black beans and plantains.

Recipe

8 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg beef eye round
  • 150 g chorizo, in a stick
  • 200 g tomato sauce
  • 1 onion and 1 pepper (220 g)
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 50 g olives
  • 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp cumin, 1 bay leaf

Method

  1. Make a hole through the length of the roast and insert the chorizo.
  2. Rub with garlic, salt and cumin and rest 1 hour.
  3. Brown on all sides in oil in a heavy pot.
  4. Add onion, pepper, tomato sauce, olives and 300 ml water.
  5. Cover and braise on low for 2.5 hours until tender, turning once.
  6. Rest, slice in rounds and spoon sauce over.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim fat and use lean chorizo or ham as filling.
  • Skim the sauce before serving.
  • Serve with beans and salad.

History

It is a classic Sunday roast in Cuban homes, born from the Spanish tradition of stuffed meat joints. The cut is lean, so long braising in sauce keeps it tender. The slices are often reheated in the sauce the next day.
