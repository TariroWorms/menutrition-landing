🌯 Chicken Caesar wrap

A chicken Caesar wrap rolls the ingredients of a chicken Caesar salad — grilled chicken, crisp romaine lettuce, shaved parmesan and creamy Caesar dressing — into a large soft flour tortilla. Each bite is cool and crunchy from the lettuce, savoury from the chicken and sharp from the cheese and garlicky dressing. It is a popular grab-and-go lunch, eaten cold or lightly toasted.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large flour tortillas
  • 250 g chicken breast
  • 1 tsp oil, salt and pepper
  • 1 small head romaine lettuce, shredded
  • 30 g parmesan, shaved
  • 4 tbsp Caesar dressing

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and oil and grill 5–6 minutes per side; rest and slice.
  2. Toss the lettuce with the dressing and half the parmesan.
  3. Warm the tortillas briefly in a dry pan.
  4. Lay the salad and chicken in the centre, scatter the remaining parmesan and roll tightly, folding in the sides.
  5. Cut in half on the diagonal and serve, or toast seam-side down for a crisp outside.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp dressing per wrap or a yogurt-based light Caesar; 1 tbsp creamy dressing ≈ 70–80 kcal.
  • Load more lettuce and chicken and less parmesan.
  • Choose a smaller tortilla, or eat half the wrap with a side salad.

History

Caesar salad is usually credited to restaurateur Caesar Cardini in Tijuana in the 1920s. The wrap version is a later American café and deli idea, now a common lunch option across the United States and beyond.
