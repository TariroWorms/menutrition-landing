🍮 Layali Lubnan

Layali Lubnan, "Lebanese nights", is a chilled dessert of milk and semolina pudding scented with orange blossom water, topped with a layer of ashta, the thick Lebanese clotted cream. It is finished with chopped pistachios and a drizzle of honey or syrup just before serving. The result is cool, creamy and gently floral, with a little crunch from the nuts.

Recipe

8 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 l milk
  • 100 g fine semolina
  • 80 g sugar
  • 1 tbsp orange blossom water
  • 250 g ashta (clotted cream)
  • 40 g pistachios, chopped
  • 4 tbsp honey

Method

  1. Heat the milk with the sugar, then whisk in the semolina and cook, stirring constantly, until thick, 8–10 minutes.
  2. Stir in the orange blossom water and pour into a dish.
  3. Cool, then chill at least 2 hours until set.
  4. Spread the ashta over the top and scatter with pistachios.
  5. Drizzle with honey just before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar in the pudding by a third — the honey on top adds sweetness.
  • Spread a thinner layer of ashta.
  • Serve small squares or individual cups of about 120 g.

History

Layali Lubnan is a Lebanese home and pastry-shop dessert, especially popular during Ramadan and in summer. It is widely made across the Levant and the Gulf.
