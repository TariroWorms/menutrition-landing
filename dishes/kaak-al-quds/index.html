Ka'ak al-Quds (Jerusalem sesame bread ring)

Origin: Palestine · Jerusalem

Ka'ak al-Quds is a long oval ring of soft, chewy bread coated in sesame seeds, baked until the crust is golden. It is usually sold from street carts and eaten with a small paper twist of za'atar and a hard-boiled egg. The bread is lightly sweet with a toasty sesame aroma.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 300 ml warm water
  • 150 g sesame seeds
  • Za'atar and boiled eggs to serve

Method

  1. Mix flour, salt, sugar, yeast and water and knead for 10 minutes.
  2. Rest the dough for 1 hour until doubled.
  3. Shape into long ropes and join into ovals.
  4. Brush with water and coat generously in sesame seeds.
  5. Bake at 220 °C for 15 minutes and serve with za'atar and egg.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half whole wheat flour.
  • Eat with an egg and vegetables for a balanced breakfast.
  • Go easy on the oil in the za'atar.

History

This street bread is a symbol of Jerusalem's Old City, where vendors carry stacks of it on trays through the markets in the morning. It is a typical quick breakfast for locals and visitors alike.
