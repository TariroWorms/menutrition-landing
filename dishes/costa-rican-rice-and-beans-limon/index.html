Rice and beans Limón (Caribbean coconut rice and beans)

Origin: Costa Rica · Limón

Rice and beans from Limón is the Afro-Caribbean version of the Costa Rican staple, cooked in coconut milk instead of plain water. The rice is soft and creamy with a gentle coconut aroma, and the whole chili adds warmth without too much heat. It is served alongside stewed chicken, fish or pork.

What it is made of

ricered beancoconut milkonionchili peppergarlic

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g rice
  • 200 g cooked red beans
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 200 ml bean cooking liquid
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 whole chili
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Soften chopped onion and garlic in a little oil.
  2. Add the beans, bean liquid and coconut milk and bring to a simmer.
  3. Add the rice, whole chili and salt.
  4. Cover and cook on low heat for 25 minutes without stirring.
  5. Rest for 10 minutes, remove the chili and fluff before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk to cut fat.
  • Choose brown rice for fiber.
  • Serve with grilled fish and a large salad.

History

The dish reflects the Afro-Caribbean community of Limón province, whose cooking is quite different from that of the central highlands. Coconut milk is a typical ingredient there, and the dish is often prepared for family gatherings and festivals.
