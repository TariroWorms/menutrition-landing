🥧 Gibanica

Origin: Serbia · Balkans

Gibanica is a Serbian pie of thin phyllo sheets layered with a filling of crumbled white cheese, eggs and kajmak, a rich clotted dairy cream. Baked until puffed and golden, it has crisp, flaky edges and a soft, savoury, cheesy centre. It is cut into squares and eaten warm for breakfast, often with yogurt, or brought to family celebrations.

Recipe

8 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g phyllo sheets
  • 400 g white brined cheese, crumbled
  • 200 g kajmak
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil for the dish
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and oil a 25 × 35 cm baking dish.
  2. Whisk the eggs with the water and salt, then stir in the crumbled cheese and kajmak.
  3. Lay two phyllo sheets in the dish, spread with a thin layer of filling, then crumple or layer more sheets, repeating until all is used.
  4. Finish with a sheet of phyllo and pour any remaining egg mixture over the top.
  5. Bake 35–40 minutes until puffed and deep golden; rest 10 minutes before cutting.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the pie into 12 squares instead of 8 for a smaller portion.
  • Use less kajmak and more of the crumbled cheese and egg mixture to lighten the filling.
  • Serve with a large tomato and cucumber salad to make the plate more filling.

History

Gibanica is considered one of Serbia's national dishes and is a staple of holiday tables, especially at Christmas and Easter; similar layered cheese pies are found throughout the Balkans.
