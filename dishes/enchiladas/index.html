🌯 Chicken enchiladas

Enchiladas are tortillas rolled around a filling, covered in sauce and baked. This Tex-Mex style version uses soft wheat tortillas filled with chicken, onion and cheese, topped with tomato salsa and more cheese, then finished with a spoon of sour cream. The result is soft, cheesy and tangy, usually served with rice, beans or salad.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 8 wheat tortillas
  • 400 g cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 150 g grated cheese
  • 400 g tomato salsa
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Fresh coriander to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190 °C and spread a third of the salsa in a baking dish.
  2. Mix the chicken with the onion, cumin and half the cheese.
  3. Fill and roll each tortilla, placing them seam-side down in the dish.
  4. Pour over the remaining salsa and sprinkle with the rest of the cheese.
  5. Bake for 20 minutes, then top with sour cream and coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the cheese on top and use a sharp, aged cheese for more flavour per gram.
  • Swap some of the sour cream for extra salsa: 1 tbsp sour cream ≈ 30 kcal.
  • Serve two enchiladas with a big salad instead of rice on the side.

History

Rolling tortillas around food dates back to pre-Hispanic Mexico; the name comes from enchilar, “to season with chili”. Baked, cheese-topped enchiladas with wheat tortillas became a staple of Tex-Mex cooking in the United States in the 20th century.
