Tunisian tajine (baked egg and chicken frittata)

Origin: Tunisia

Tunisian tajine is nothing like the Moroccan stew of the same name: it is a firm, sliceable baked frittata of beaten eggs, shredded chicken, cheese and parsley. It is golden on top, tender inside and lightly seasoned. It is cut into squares and served hot or at room temperature.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • 250 g cooked chicken, shredded
  • 150 g grated cheese
  • 1 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 1 boiled potato, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground pepper
  • Salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 C and oil a baking dish.
  2. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper.
  3. Stir in the chicken, cheese, parsley and potato.
  4. Pour into the dish.
  5. Bake for 35 to 40 minutes until set and golden.
  6. Cool slightly and cut into squares.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cheese and add more vegetables.
  • Use chicken breast without skin.
  • Serve with a fresh tomato salad.

History

The dish is a staple of home cooking and festive tables in Tunisia, especially during Ramadan. The name comes from the baking dish in which it was traditionally cooked.
