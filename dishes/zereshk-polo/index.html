🍚 Zereshk Polo with Chicken

Zereshk polo is Persian saffron rice scattered with tart red barberries that are briefly cooked in butter and a little sugar, served with chicken braised with onion and saffron. The rice is fluffy and fragrant, the barberries add jewel-like colour and a sour bite. It is a favourite at family gatherings and celebrations.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g basmati rice
  • 600 g chicken thighs or legs
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • Pinch of saffron soaked in 3 tbsp hot water
  • 40 g dried barberries, rinsed
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 30 g butter
  • Salt, pepper, turmeric

Method

  1. Soak the rice for 1 hour; boil in salted water for 6–7 minutes and drain.
  2. Fry the onion in a little butter, add the chicken, turmeric, half the saffron and a cup of water; braise covered for 45 minutes.
  3. Return the rice to the pot, cover with a towel-wrapped lid and steam on low heat for 35–40 minutes.
  4. Briefly cook the barberries in the remaining butter with the sugar for 1–2 minutes; do not let them burn.
  5. Mix some rice with the remaining saffron, then serve the rice topped with barberries alongside the chicken.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken and trim visible fat.
  • Keep the butter for the barberries only; 1 tbsp butter ≈ 100 kcal.
  • Fill half the plate with a salad of cucumber, tomato and herbs and keep the rice to about 150 g cooked.

History

Barberries have long been grown in eastern Iran, especially around South Khorasan, and pairing them with saffron rice is a hallmark of Persian festive cooking. The dish is served across Iran and in Persian restaurants abroad.
