🍆 Tepsi Baytinijan

Tepsi baytinijan is an Iraqi tray bake of fried eggplant slices, potato rounds and seasoned beef meatballs, layered with onion, green pepper and tomato and baked in a tomato sauce. The eggplant turns silky, the potatoes soak up the sauce and the top caramelizes in the oven. It is a family dish served with rice.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants, sliced 1 cm thick
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 2 potatoes, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, sliced, plus 400 g crushed tomato
  • 1 onion, half grated and half sliced
  • 2 green peppers, sliced
  • 60 ml oil
  • Salt, black pepper, baharat

Method

  1. Brush eggplant and potato slices with oil and roast or fry until golden.
  2. Mix the beef with grated onion, salt, pepper and baharat and shape into small meatballs; brown them lightly.
  3. In a round tray, layer eggplant, potato, meatballs, sliced onion, pepper and tomato.
  4. Season the crushed tomato with salt and 250 ml water and pour over.
  5. Bake at 190 °C for 45 minutes until the sauce is reduced and the top is browned.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the eggplant brushed with oil instead of deep-frying — eggplant soaks up oil like a sponge.
  • Use lean minced beef and drain fat after browning the meatballs.
  • Serve with a large salad and a small portion of rice.

History

Tepsi takes its name from the round tray it is baked in, and baytinijan is the Iraqi word for eggplant. It is a classic of Iraqi home cooking.
