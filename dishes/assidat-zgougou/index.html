Assidat zgougou (Aleppo pine seed cream)

Origin: Tunisia

Assidat zgougou is a thick, dark brown pudding made from ground Aleppo pine seeds, cooked with sugar and thickened with flour. It has a resinous, slightly bitter and sweet flavor and a smooth custard-like texture. It is topped with almonds, pistachios and pine nuts and served in small cups.

What it is made of

Recipe

8 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g ground Aleppo pine seeds
  • 60 g flour
  • 120 g sugar
  • 1 L water
  • 1 tsp rose water
  • 50 g almonds
  • 30 g pistachios
  • 30 g pine nuts

Method

  1. Soak the ground pine seeds in water and strain through a cloth.
  2. Pour the liquid into a pot and bring to a gentle boil.
  3. Whisk in the flour dissolved in a little water and the sugar.
  4. Cook over low heat, stirring, for 30 minutes until thick.
  5. Add rose water and pour into cups.
  6. Chill and decorate with nuts.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce the sugar by a third.
  • Use plenty of nut topping instead of cream.
  • Serve small cups as a dessert.

History

The dish is prepared for Mawlid, the celebration of the Prophet's birthday, across Tunisia. Families gather to share cups topped with nuts, and neighbors often exchange them.
