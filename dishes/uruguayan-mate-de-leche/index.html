Mate de leche (mate with milk)

Origin: Uruguay
138kcal

Mate de leche is a gentle version of mate in which warm milk, usually sweetened, is poured over the yerba instead of hot water. The milk softens the bitterness and gives a creamy, comforting drink that tastes a little like herbal milky tea. It is often a child's first mate and a winter breakfast in rural homes. It is sipped from a gourd with a bombilla or, more simply, prepared in a cup.

What it is made of

yerba matemilksugar

Recipe

1 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 40 g yerba mate
  • 500 ml milk
  • 2 tsp sugar

Method

  1. Warm the milk with the sugar to about 70 °C, without boiling.
  2. Fill the gourd two thirds full with yerba and form a slope.
  3. Moisten the yerba with a little warm water first.
  4. Insert the bombilla and pour in the warm milk.
  5. Sip and refill with milk as you go.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk and halve the sugar.
  • Flavour with cinnamon or orange peel instead of sugar.
  • Wash the gourd well afterwards; milk residue spoils quickly.

History

Across Uruguay and the Río de la Plata region, mate is part of daily life, and families developed milder versions such as this one for children and cold mornings. Some add orange peel or burnt sugar for extra flavour.
