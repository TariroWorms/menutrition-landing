🍢 Chicken malai tikka

Chicken malai tikka is boneless chicken marinated in cream, soft cheese, yogurt, ginger, garlic and cardamom, then grilled in a tandoor or a very hot oven. Unlike red tikka it uses little or no chili, so the pieces come out pale, juicy and mild with lightly charred edges. It is served as a starter with sliced onion and lemon.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless chicken thighs, in cubes
  • 100 g thick yogurt
  • 60 ml cream
  • 50 g soft cheese
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • Salt, white pepper
  • Sliced onion and lemon to serve

Method

  1. Mix the yogurt, cream, cheese, ginger-garlic paste, cardamom, salt and pepper into a smooth marinade.
  2. Coat the chicken and marinate in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  3. Thread onto skewers and grill at 240 °C or over charcoal for 12–15 minutes, turning once.
  4. Serve hot with onion rings and lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Swap some of the cream for extra yogurt in the marinade.
  • Use chicken breast instead of thighs for leaner pieces.
  • Serve with a large salad and onion rings as the side.

History

Malai tikka is part of the tandoori tradition of North India and Punjab, where meat is marinated and cooked in a clay oven. The creamy, mild version is a staple of North Indian restaurant grills today.
