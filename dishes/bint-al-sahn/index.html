🍯 Bint al sahn (Yemeni honey bread)

Bint al sahn is a Yemeni sweet bread made of many paper-thin layers of egg-enriched dough brushed with ghee, baked into a tall, golden round. It is crisp on top and soft and flaky inside, and is served warm, drenched in honey and sprinkled with black nigella seeds. It is torn apart by hand and shared, often with a cup of tea.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g plain flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp instant yeast, 1 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp salt
  • About 180 ml lukewarm water
  • 120 g ghee, melted
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp nigella seeds

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt, add the eggs and enough water to knead a soft, smooth dough for 10 minutes.
  2. Divide into 12 balls, brush them with ghee, cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Stretch each ball into a very thin round on an oiled surface and brush with ghee.
  4. Stack the rounds in a greased 22 cm round tin, folding the edges in, and brush the top with ghee.
  5. Bake at 180 °C for 35–40 minutes until deep golden.
  6. Pour the honey over the hot bread, sprinkle with nigella seeds and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Ghee adds most of the fat: brushing the layers thinly instead of soaking them saves a lot — 1 tbsp ghee is about 110–120 kcal.
  • Drizzle 1 tablespoon of honey per serving at the table rather than soaking the whole bread.
  • It is rich: one wedge with tea is a full dessert.

History

The Arabic name literally means “daughter of the plate”. It is a celebrated dish of Yemeni home baking, served to guests, at weddings and family gatherings, and during Ramadan. Yemeni restaurants across the Gulf now also offer it as a dessert.
