🍨 Saffron Ice Cream

Origin: Iran · also the Gulf

Saffron ice cream is a rich, golden frozen dessert made from milk, cream and sugar, flavoured with saffron and rosewater and studded with pistachios. It is dense and creamy with a floral aroma, and is served in scoops or between thin wafers. In Iran it is known as bastani sonnati, and it is a staple of dessert shops across the Gulf.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 250 ml cream
  • 150 g sugar
  • 1 pinch saffron (about 0.3 g)
  • 1 tbsp rosewater
  • 40 g pistachios, chopped

Method

  1. Grind the saffron and steep it in 2 tbsp hot milk for 10 minutes.
  2. Warm the rest of the milk with the sugar until dissolved, then cool.
  3. Stir in the cream, saffron milk and rosewater.
  4. Churn in an ice-cream machine until thick, or freeze and stir every 30 minutes for 3 hours.
  5. Fold in the pistachios and freeze until scoopable.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep to one 100 g scoop and enjoy it slowly.
  • Use part semi-skimmed milk instead of cream for a lighter mix.
  • Sprinkle a few pistachios rather than a thick layer; nuts add calories fast.

History

Saffron and rosewater ice cream is strongly associated with Iran, where it is called bastani sonnati, meaning traditional ice cream; it is often linked to the confectioner Akbar Mashti in Tehran, though details vary. Today it is sold widely in Iran and the Gulf states.
