Satsivi (poultry in walnut sauce)

Origin: Georgia

Satsivi is poultry that is poached, sometimes browned, and then served cold under a thick sauce of ground walnuts, garlic, onion and spices thinned with the cooking broth. Blue fenugreek, coriander and saffron-like marigold give the sauce its warm aroma and yellow tint, while vinegar adds a light sour note. It is eaten as a cold main or starter with bread or mchadi corn cakes.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken (1.5 kg)
  • 300 g walnuts
  • 2 onions (200 g)
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp blue fenugreek
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp dried marigold
  • 1 tbsp wine vinegar (15 ml)
  • about 700 ml chicken broth

Method

  1. Poach the chicken in salted water for about 1 hour, then cut it into pieces and keep the broth.
  2. Grind the walnuts with garlic until a paste forms and the oil starts to show.
  3. Fry the onion in a little chicken fat until soft, then add it to the walnut paste with the spices.
  4. Gradually whisk in warm broth until the sauce is like thick cream, then add vinegar and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Pour the sauce over the chicken and chill for at least 3 hours before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless breast meat to cut saturated fat while keeping the protein.
  • Thin the sauce with more broth and use fewer walnuts per portion.
  • A small portion with plenty of fresh vegetables makes a balanced plate.

History

Satsivi is one of the best-known festive dishes in Georgia, and turkey satsivi is traditional for New Year celebrations. The name comes from the Georgian word for cold, since the dish is always served chilled.
