Taiwanese mango shaved ice

Origin: Taiwan

Mango shaved ice is a mound of fine, snowy ice topped with fresh mango cubes, mango syrup and a drizzle of condensed milk. It is cold, fruity and very sweet. People eat it in summer at dessert shops, often as a shared bowl.

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g milk-flavored ice blocks or shaved ice
  • 2 ripe mangoes
  • 3 tbsp mango syrup or puree
  • 3 tbsp condensed milk
  • 1 scoop ice cream

Method

  1. Freeze milk or water into blocks the day before.
  2. Shave the ice into fine fluffy flakes.
  3. Cube one mango and puree the other with a little sugar.
  4. Pile the ice in bowls and top with mango cubes.
  5. Drizzle with mango puree and condensed milk and add ice cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use ripe mango and skip the syrup.
  • Replace condensed milk with a little plain yogurt.
  • Share the bowl.

History

Shaved ice desserts have a long history in Taiwan, and the mango version became a favorite when mango season turned the topping into the star of the bowl. The fine milky ice texture in many shops is a more recent development.
