Popara (Bulgarian bread and milk)

Origin: Bulgaria

Popara is a warm, soft breakfast of stale bread soaked in hot milk or tea. Butter melts into it and crumbled white cheese or sugar tops it. It is mild, comforting and very filling.

What it is made of

breadmilkbutterfeta cheese

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g stale bread
  • 300 ml hot milk
  • 15 g butter
  • 50 g white cheese
  • pinch of salt

Method

  1. Tear the bread into small pieces.
  2. Put it in a bowl.
  3. Heat the milk until steaming.
  4. Pour it over the bread.
  5. Add butter and cover for 5 minutes.
  6. Top with crumbled cheese.

How to make it lighter

  • Use whole-grain bread.
  • Choose low-fat milk.
  • Go easy on the salty cheese.

History

It is a classic way to avoid wasting bread in rural Bulgarian homes. Many Bulgarians remember it as a childhood or sick-day food.
