Zoolbia (Persian fried syrup swirls)

Golden, lattice-like swirls of fried batter soaked in saffron and rosewater syrup. The outside is crisp and the inside juicy and very sweet with a floral aroma. It is a classic treat during Ramadan and at festive gatherings.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g flour
  • 100 g yogurt
  • 100 ml water
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 300 g sugar
  • 200 ml water for syrup
  • 1 g saffron
  • 1 tbsp rose water
  • oil for frying

Method

  1. Whisk flour, yogurt, water and yeast into a smooth thick batter and rest 1 hour.
  2. Boil sugar, water and saffron into a syrup, add rose water and keep warm.
  3. Fill a piping bag with batter and pipe swirling circles into hot oil.
  4. Fry until golden on both sides.
  5. Drain and soak in the syrup for 1-2 minutes, then lift out.

How to make it lighter

  • Soak only briefly to reduce syrup absorption.
  • Eat a small piece with unsweetened tea.
  • Bake or air-fry for a lighter version.

History

Zoolbia is part of the same family of syrup-soaked fried sweets found across the Middle East and South Asia. In Iran it is paired with bamieh and is common in sweet shops.
