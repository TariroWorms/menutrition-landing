Rupjmaizes kārtojums (Latvian rye bread dessert)

Origin: Latvia

This layered dessert alternates caramelised crumbs of dark rye bread with whipped cream and tart berry jam, usually served in glasses. It is sweet, creamy and slightly malty, with a pleasant crunch against the soft cream. It is a typical Latvian way to finish a festive meal.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g dark rye bread, dried
  • 60 g butter
  • 60 g sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 300 ml double cream
  • 200 g lingonberry or blackcurrant jam

Method

  1. Crumble the rye bread into coarse crumbs.
  2. Fry the crumbs with butter, sugar and cinnamon until crisp and caramelised, then cool.
  3. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  4. Layer crumbs, jam and cream in glasses, finishing with cream.
  5. Chill for an hour so the crumbs soften slightly.

How to make it lighter

  • Use whipped Greek yogurt instead of cream.
  • Reduce sugar in the crumbs and use tart unsweetened berries.
  • Serve in small glasses to control the portion.

History

Latvians use their beloved dark rye bread not only for savoury food but also in sweets, a thrifty tradition that turns stale bread into a dessert. It is a common feature of family celebrations.
