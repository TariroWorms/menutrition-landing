Tashreeb bagilla (bread in fava bean broth)

Tashreeb bagilla is a humble Iraqi breakfast of torn flatbread soaked in a warm, garlicky broth of fava beans. A soft egg and a drizzle of oil go on top. It is soft, earthy and very filling.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g shelled fava beans
  • 800 ml water
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 flatbreads, torn
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt, cumin

Method

  1. Simmer the beans in water until very soft, about 25 minutes.
  2. Mash a few beans into the broth and add crushed garlic.
  3. Season with salt and cumin.
  4. Place torn bread in bowls and ladle the beans and broth over.
  5. Top each with a poached or fried egg and a drizzle of oil.

How to make it lighter

  • Use wholemeal bread for more fibre.
  • Poach the egg instead of frying it.
  • Go lighter on the oil.

History

Soaking stale bread in broth is an old way of using every scrap of food across the Arab world. The fava version is a popular cold-weather breakfast in Iraq.
