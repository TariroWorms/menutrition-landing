Skyr with berries (Icelandic cultured dairy)

Origin: Iceland

Skyr is a thick, tangy cultured dairy product made by straining skimmed milk, and it sits between yoghurt and fresh cheese. It is very high in protein and nearly fat-free. Icelanders eat it for breakfast with a splash of milk, berries and a spoon of sugar.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain skyr
  • 60 ml milk
  • 100 g blueberries or strawberries
  • 2 tsp sugar or honey

Method

  1. Spoon the skyr into two bowls.
  2. Stir in a little milk to loosen it.
  3. Wash the berries and scatter on top.
  4. Sprinkle with sugar.
  5. Serve cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sugar and use ripe berries for sweetness.
  • A great high-protein, low-fat base for breakfast.
  • Add oats or nuts for a more filling meal.

History

Skyr has been made in Iceland since settlement times and is mentioned in medieval sagas. Traditional versions used rennet from earlier batches as a starter.
