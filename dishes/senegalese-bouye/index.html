Bouye (Senegalese baobab drink)

Origin: Senegal

Bouye is a pale, creamy drink made from the dry, chalky pulp of baobab fruit, soaked in water and blended with milk, sugar and a little vanilla. It tastes sweet-tart, somewhat like a yogurt drink with a citrus-like edge. It is served ice cold, especially in the heat and during Ramadan.

What it is made of

baobab fruit pulpmilksugarvanilla

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 80 g baobab fruit pulp
  • 500 ml water
  • 300 ml milk
  • 60 g sugar
  • 5 g vanilla sugar

Method

  1. Break the baobab pulp into pieces and soak it in the water for 15 minutes.
  2. Rub with your fingers to separate the pulp from the seeds.
  3. Strain through a sieve, discarding fibres and seeds.
  4. Blend with milk, sugar and vanilla until smooth.
  5. Chill and serve over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar, since the fruit already has natural tartness.
  • Make it with plant milk for a vegan version.
  • Dilute with a little more water for a lighter drink.

History

The baobab, called bouye in Wolof, is a symbol of Senegal and its fruit is rich in vitamin C and fibre. The pulp is gathered, dried and sold in markets, and the drink is a popular home and street refreshment.
