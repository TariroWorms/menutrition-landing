Samak mofa (Hadrami baked fish)

Origin: Yemen · Hadramawt

Samak mofa is a whole fish rubbed with a paste of garlic, chili, cumin, coriander and lemon, then baked in a hot clay tannur until the skin is lightly charred. The flesh is moist and the outside smoky and fiery. It is served with rice, flatbread and a fresh tomato salad.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole fish (800 g), cleaned
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp ground coriander
  • 2 hot chilies
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil (45 ml), salt

Method

  1. Score the fish on both sides.
  2. Pound garlic, chili, spices, lemon and oil into a paste.
  3. Rub the paste inside and out and marinate for 30 minutes.
  4. Heat the oven to 230 C or use a tannur.
  5. Bake 20 to 25 minutes until the flesh flakes and the skin chars lightly.
  6. Serve with rice, bread and tomato salad.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast with less oil for a lighter dish.
  • Serve with plain rice and plenty of salad.
  • Use a firm white fish.

History

The coast of Hadramawt has long lived on fishing, and cooking fish in a tannur is a traditional method there. Fresh local catch is the base of many family and festive meals on the coast.
