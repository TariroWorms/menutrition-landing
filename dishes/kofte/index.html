🧆 Köfte

Origin: Türkiye

Köfte are Turkish meatballs of minced lamb mixed with onion, cumin, parsley and a little soaked bread, which keeps them tender. They are grilled until charred outside and juicy inside. Köfte are served with grilled peppers and tomatoes, piyaz bean salad or rice, or tucked into bread as a quick lunch.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground lamb
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 40 g stale bread, soaked and squeezed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 15 g parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Combine the lamb, onion, bread, cumin, parsley, salt and pepper; knead well.
  2. Rest the mixture in the fridge for 20 minutes.
  3. Shape into small oval patties.
  4. Grill or cook on a hot griddle for 3–4 minutes per side.
  5. Serve with grilled peppers and tomatoes.

How to make it lighter

  • Mix in part lean beef or use lean lamb to lower the fat.
  • Use a grill pan instead of frying in oil.
  • Fill half the plate with salad or grilled vegetables instead of rice.

History

Turkey has dozens of regional köfte styles, and köfte restaurants (köfteci) are found in nearly every town. Recipes vary by city in the meat, spices and binder used.
