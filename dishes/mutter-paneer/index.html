🟢 Mutter paneer

Mutter paneer is a North Indian curry of soft paneer cubes and green peas simmered in a tomato and onion gravy finished with a little cream and garam masala. The sauce is mild, smooth and gently spiced, while the paneer stays firm and milky. It is a staple of home cooking and restaurant menus alike, served with rice or flatbread.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g paneer, cubed
  • 200 g green peas
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, puréed
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp turmeric and chilli powder to taste
  • 60 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Fry the onions in oil until golden, then add ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute.
  2. Add turmeric, chilli and the tomato purée; cook until the oil separates, about 10 minutes.
  3. Add peas and 150 ml water and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Add paneer and garam masala and simmer 5 minutes more.
  5. Stir in the cream, season with salt and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream or replace it with a splash of water — the tomato base stays rich.
  • Increase the peas and reduce the paneer for a lighter, higher-fibre bowl.
  • Measure the oil: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal, and the gravy needs only one.

History

Mutter paneer belongs to the Punjabi and wider North Indian kitchen, where paneer, a fresh unaged cheese, is an everyday source of protein in vegetarian homes. Today it is one of the most common vegetarian curries in Indian restaurants around the world.
