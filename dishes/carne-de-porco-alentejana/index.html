🥘 Carne de porco à alentejana

Carne de porco à alentejana is a Portuguese dish of cubed pork marinated in white wine and garlic, browned and then cooked with clams until they open. It is served with crisp cubes of fried potato and a generous scattering of fresh cilantro. The result combines rich, garlicky meat with briny clam juices in one pan.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g pork shoulder, cubed
  • 500 g clams, cleaned
  • 600 g potatoes, cubed
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 200 ml dry white wine
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp paprika, 1 bay leaf, salt and pepper
  • A large bunch of cilantro, chopped

Method

  1. Marinate the pork with the wine, garlic, paprika, bay leaf, salt and pepper for at least 2 hours.
  2. Fry or oven-roast the potato cubes until golden and crisp.
  3. Drain the pork (keep the marinade) and brown it in the oil over high heat.
  4. Add the marinade and simmer for about 20 minutes until the pork is tender.
  5. Add the clams, cover and cook until they open; discard any that stay closed. Toss in the potatoes and cilantro and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the potatoes in the oven with 1 tbsp oil instead of frying them.
  • Use lean pork loin rather than shoulder.
  • Increase the clams and reduce the pork for a lighter, protein-rich plate.

History

Despite the name, the dish is often linked with the Algarve as much as with the Alentejo, and its exact origin is debated. Today it is a standard of Portuguese restaurants throughout the country.
