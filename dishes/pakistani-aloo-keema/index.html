Aloo keema (minced meat with potatoes)

Origin: Pakistan

Aloo keema is an everyday home dish of minced meat simmered with diced potatoes, tomatoes, onion and warm spices. The sauce is thick and slightly oily, the potatoes soak up the flavour and the meat stays crumbly. It is eaten with roti, paratha or rice and a squeeze of lemon.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g minced beef
  • 400 g potatoes, diced
  • 2 onions (250 g)
  • 2 tomatoes (200 g)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp garam masala, 1 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp turmeric
  • 60 ml oil, salt, coriander

Method

  1. Fry the onions in oil until golden.
  2. Add the ginger-garlic paste and spices and cook for a minute.
  3. Add the mince and fry until it loses its raw colour.
  4. Add the tomatoes, potatoes and 200 ml water.
  5. Cover and simmer 20 minutes until the potatoes are soft, finish with coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean mince and less oil.
  • Add peas or spinach for extra vegetables.
  • Serve with a single roti and a salad.

History

Keema dishes are common across South Asia and appear on many Pakistani family tables because they are quick and economical. Adding potatoes stretches the meat to feed more people.
