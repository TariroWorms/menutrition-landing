Sopa de caracol (Honduran conch soup)

Origin: Honduras · Caribbean coast
380kcal

Sopa de caracol is a rich coconut-milk soup with tender pieces of conch, cassava, green banana and plantain. It is flavoured with garlic, cilantro and sometimes a whole chili for aroma. It is served in deep bowls with lime and tortillas.

Recipe

6 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g conch, cleaned and pounded
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 300 g cassava
  • 2 green bananas
  • 1 ripe plantain
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • Cilantro, salt, lime

Method

  1. Simmer the conch with onion and garlic for 45 minutes until tender.
  2. Add cassava and green bananas.
  3. Pour in coconut milk and simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Add ripe plantain and cook 10 more minutes.
  5. Season with salt.
  6. Finish with cilantro and lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk to cut saturated fat.
  • Add vegetables for fibre.
  • Serve with a side salad.

History

It is the signature dish of the Honduran Caribbean coast and the Bay Islands, with strong Garifuna influence. It is often sold at beach stands and is a national favourite despite its coastal origin.
