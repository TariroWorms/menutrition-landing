Curry mee (coconut curry noodle soup)

Origin: Malaysia

Curry mee is a rich, spicy coconut curry noodle soup. Yellow noodles and thin vermicelli sit in a red-orange broth of chili paste, curry spices and coconut milk. It is topped with tofu puffs, bean sprouts, cockles or shrimp, boiled egg and a spoonful of sambal.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g yellow noodles and vermicelli
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 600 ml stock
  • 3 tbsp chili paste
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 100 g tofu puffs
  • 100 g bean sprouts
  • 100 g cockles or clams, 2 boiled eggs, mint

Method

  1. Fry the chili paste with curry powder until the oil separates.
  2. Add stock and coconut milk and simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Add tofu puffs and let them soak up the broth.
  4. Blanch the noodles and bean sprouts and place in bowls.
  5. Ladle on the broth and top with cockles, egg and mint.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk and add more stock.
  • Skip the oil on top of the broth.
  • Add extra bean sprouts and greens.

History

Variants of the dish are found across Malaysia, notably in Penang and Kuala Lumpur, where it is usually a breakfast or lunch. The cockles are a signature topping in some versions.
