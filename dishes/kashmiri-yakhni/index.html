Kashmiri yakhni (yogurt mutton curry)

Origin: India · Kashmir
300kcal

Kashmiri yakhni is a pale, mild curry in which mutton is slowly cooked in yogurt with fennel, dried ginger and green cardamom. There are no tomatoes or chilli, so the broth stays creamy white and delicately spiced. It tastes mellow, aromatic and slightly sweet. It is usually served with steamed rice.

Recipe

4 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g mutton on the bone
  • 250 g thick yogurt
  • 1 tbsp fennel powder
  • 1 tsp dried ginger powder
  • 5 green cardamom pods, 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp ghee (25 g), salt, 500 ml water

Method

  1. Heat the ghee and sear the mutton lightly with bay leaves and cardamom.
  2. Whisk the yogurt smooth with fennel and ginger powder.
  3. Lower the heat and slowly stir the yogurt into the meat.
  4. Add water and salt, cover and simmer for 60-75 minutes until tender.
  5. Reduce the broth to a creamy sauce and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt and trim the fat from the mutton.
  • Skim the ghee from the top before serving.
  • Serve with steamed vegetables and a small portion of rice.

History

Yakhni is a cooking style of Kashmiri Pandit and Wazwan cuisine, in which meat is cooked in a spiced yogurt base. It reflects Persian and Central Asian influences on the region's food.
