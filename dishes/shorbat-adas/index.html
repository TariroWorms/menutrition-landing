🍲 Shorbat Adas (Red Lentil Soup)

Origin: Middle East · Arab world

Shorbat adas is a smooth soup of red lentils simmered with onion and carrot, seasoned with cumin and turmeric and blended until velvety. It has a warm golden colour and an earthy, gently spiced taste. Each bowl is finished with a generous squeeze of lemon, which brightens the flavour, and it is a classic opener on the iftar table.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1.2 l water
  • Salt; lemon wedges to serve

Method

  1. Soften the onion and carrot in the oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the cumin and turmeric and stir for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the lentils and water, bring to the boil and simmer 20–25 minutes until the lentils fall apart.
  4. Blend until smooth, season with salt and serve with lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Use just 1 tbsp of oil for the whole pot.
  • Add an extra carrot or some zucchini for more volume per bowl.
  • Have a bowl before the main course; it is filling and fairly low in energy.

History

Lentils are among the oldest cultivated crops of the Fertile Crescent, and lentil soup is eaten across the Arab world. Shorbat adas is especially associated with Ramadan, when a bowl is often the first dish served at iftar.
