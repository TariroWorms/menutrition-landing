🍫 Bajadera

Origin: Croatia · Zagreb

Bajadera is a Croatian layered confection of hazelnut nougat and chocolate pressed between thin wafer sheets and cut into small diamonds or squares. Each bite is dense, nutty and chocolatey, with a soft praline centre and a light crunch from the wafers. It is served in small pieces with coffee, especially at Christmas and family celebrations.

Recipe

20 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g hazelnuts, toasted and ground
  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 200 g nougat, chopped
  • 100 g sugar
  • 4 round wafer sheets
  • 50 ml water

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the water over low heat and simmer for 2 minutes.
  2. Melt the nougat and half of the chocolate into the syrup, then stir in the ground hazelnuts.
  3. Spread the mixture between the wafer sheets, pressing each layer flat.
  4. Melt the remaining chocolate and spread it over the top layer.
  5. Weigh down lightly and chill for at least 2 hours, then cut into small diamonds.

How to make it lighter

  • It is calorie-dense, so cut the pieces small — a 20 g diamond is a satisfying treat with coffee.
  • Plate one or two pieces instead of eating from the box.
  • Use a thinner top layer of chocolate to trim a little sugar and fat.

History

Bajadera is best known as a boxed praline produced in Zagreb since the mid-20th century, and homemade versions became a fixture of festive cookie platters across Croatia and the wider former Yugoslavia.
