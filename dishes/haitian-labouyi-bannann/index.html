Labouyi bannann (Haitian plantain porridge)

Origin: Haiti

Labouyi bannann is a warm breakfast porridge made from green plantain, grated or blended and simmered with water until thick. Milk, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and vanilla are stirred in at the end. It is smooth, lightly sweet and comforting, and is given to children and invalids as well as eaten for breakfast.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 green plantains, peeled and grated
  • 750 ml water
  • 400 ml milk
  • 60 g sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Boil the water with the cinnamon stick and a pinch of salt.
  2. Mix the grated plantain with a little cold water to form a slurry.
  3. Pour the slurry into the pot, stirring constantly.
  4. Cook 15 minutes until thick and smooth.
  5. Add milk, sugar, nutmeg and vanilla and heat through.
  6. Remove the cinnamon and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar or sweeten with fruit.
  • Use plant milk for a dairy-free version.
  • Top with sliced banana.

History

Labouyi is a family of porridges in Haitian home cooking, made from plantain, cornmeal, rice flour or oats. The plantain version is especially popular.
