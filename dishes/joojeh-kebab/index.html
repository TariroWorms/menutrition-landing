🍢 Joojeh kabab (saffron chicken kebab)

Joojeh kabab is Persian grilled chicken: boneless pieces marinated in yogurt, grated onion, lemon juice and saffron, then threaded on skewers and cooked over charcoal. The meat stays tender and juicy with a bright yellow colour and a citrusy, floral aroma. It is brushed with melted butter and served with fluffy saffron basmati rice and grilled tomatoes.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g boneless chicken thighs or breast, in 4 cm pieces
  • 1 onion, grated
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 150 g plain yogurt
  • 1/2 tsp ground saffron steeped in 3 tbsp hot water
  • 20 g butter, melted
  • 300 g basmati rice
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the onion, lemon juice, yogurt, half the saffron water, salt and pepper; marinate the chicken at least 4 hours or overnight.
  2. Rinse and soak the rice 30 minutes, parboil 6 minutes, drain, then steam covered on low heat 30 minutes.
  3. Thread the chicken on skewers and grill over hot charcoal 10–12 minutes, turning, until cooked through.
  4. Mix the melted butter with the remaining saffron water and brush over the hot kebabs.
  5. Serve on the rice with grilled tomatoes.

How to make it lighter

  • Chicken breast instead of thigh makes the kebab leaner.
  • Brush with butter only lightly or skip it; the saffron and lemon carry the flavour.
  • Serve a smaller mound of rice with a large herb salad.

History

Joojeh means “young chicken” in Persian, and grilled kebabs served with rice (chelow) are a cornerstone of Iranian restaurant and family cooking. Joojeh kabab is eaten throughout Iran and is a fixture of Persian restaurants abroad.
