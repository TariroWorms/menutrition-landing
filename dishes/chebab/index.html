🥞 Chebab (Emirati saffron pancakes)

Chebab are soft, spongy Emirati pancakes made from a yeasted batter of flour, egg and milk, flavoured with saffron and cardamom. They are cooked on a griddle until golden underneath and full of small holes on top. Chebab are a breakfast favourite, served warm with date syrup.

Recipe

4 servings80 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 300 ml warm milk
  • 1 tsp dried yeast
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • A pinch of saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm water
  • A pinch of salt, a little oil for the pan
  • Date syrup to serve

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, yeast, cardamom and salt with the egg, milk and saffron water into a smooth, thick batter.
  2. Cover and leave in a warm place for 1 hour until bubbly.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled non-stick pan over medium heat.
  4. Pour in a ladle of batter and cook for 2–3 minutes until the top is set and full of holes; turn briefly if you like.
  5. Serve warm with a drizzle of date syrup.

How to make it lighter

  • Cook in a non-stick pan with only a wipe of oil.
  • Use date syrup sparingly — about 1 tbsp per serving.
  • Make smaller pancakes and pair two with fresh fruit.

History

Chebab are a traditional breakfast across the UAE and neighbouring Gulf countries, often made for family weekend breakfasts and during Ramadan. They are now also served in Emirati cafés and hotel breakfasts.
