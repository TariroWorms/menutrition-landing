🍢 Shish Tawook

Origin: Levant · Lebanon

Shish tawook is cubes of chicken breast marinated in yogurt, lemon juice, garlic, tomato paste and paprika, then threaded on skewers and grilled. The yogurt and lemon tenderise the meat, while the tomato paste and paprika give it its orange colour and light smoky edge. It is tender, tangy and served with salad, pickles and grilled vegetables.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast, in 3 cm cubes
  • 120 g plain yogurt
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Mix the yogurt, lemon, garlic, tomato paste, paprika, oil, salt and pepper.
  2. Coat the chicken and marinate 2 hours or overnight in the fridge.
  3. Thread onto skewers and grill over medium-high heat 10–12 minutes, turning, until cooked through.
  4. Serve hot with salad and lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt in the marinade; the flavour barely changes.
  • Pair it with a big salad instead of fries.
  • Grill rather than pan-fry so no extra fat is needed.

History

Shish tawook is the Levantine and Turkish skewered chicken; the name joins şiş (skewer) with tavuk, Turkish for chicken. It became a staple of Lebanese grills and is now sold across the Middle East and the Gulf.
