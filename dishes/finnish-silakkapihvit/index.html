Silakkapihvit (fried Baltic herring)

Origin: Finland

Silakkapihvit are two small herring fillets sandwiched around chopped dill and coated in rye flour or crumbs. They fry up golden and crisp on the outside, soft inside. They are served with mashed potatoes and a cold sauce.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g Baltic herring fillets (about 16)
  • 3 tbsp chopped dill
  • 80 g rye flour
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Lemon wedges

Method

  1. Rinse and pat the fillets dry; salt lightly.
  2. Put dill on half of the fillets and cover each with another fillet, skin side out.
  3. Press gently and coat in rye flour.
  4. Melt butter in a pan over medium heat.
  5. Fry 3 minutes per side until golden; serve with lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and little oil instead of butter.
  • Herring is rich in omega-3, so it works well once or twice a week.
  • Serve with a salad rather than mashed potatoes to cut calories.

History

Baltic herring, called silakka, has been a basic fish along the Finnish coast for centuries. Fried herring is an everyday home dish, and herring has its own market days in coastal cities.
