🍖 Kokoretsi

Kokoretsi is a Greek spit roast of lamb offal — liver, lungs, heart and sweetbreads — threaded on a skewer and wrapped tightly in cleaned intestines. It turns slowly over charcoal for hours until the casing is crisp and the inside is tender. Seasoned with oregano and lemon, it is sliced and served hot.

Recipe

8 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg lamb offal (liver, heart, lungs, sweetbreads), cleaned
  • 600 g lamb intestines, cleaned
  • 2 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 lemons
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cut the offal into chunks and season with oregano, lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  2. Thread onto a long spit and wind the intestines tightly around it.
  3. Roast slowly over low charcoal for 2.5–3 hours, turning constantly.
  4. Slice and serve with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep the portion to about 150–200 g alongside other dishes.
  • Serve with a big tomato and cucumber salad or with greens.
  • Squeeze plenty of lemon for flavor instead of extra oil.

History

Kokoretsi is closely tied to Greek Easter, when it is roasted beside the whole lamb on the spit. Similar dishes exist across the Balkans and in Turkey as kokoreç.
