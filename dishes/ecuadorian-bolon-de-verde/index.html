Bolón de verde (green plantain ball)

Origin: Ecuador

A bolón is made by frying or boiling chunks of green plantain, mashing them while hot with butter and then kneading in crumbled cheese, chicharrón (crispy pork) or both. The mixture is shaped into a fist-sized ball and often browned again in a pan. It is dense, salty and crisp on the outside. It comes with a fried egg, coffee and sometimes a little stew.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 4 green plantains
  • 40 g butter
  • 150 g fresh cheese, crumbled
  • 150 g chicharrón or cooked pork belly, chopped
  • oil for frying
  • salt
  • 4 eggs to serve

Method

  1. Peel the plantains and cut them into thick chunks.
  2. Fry the chunks in oil over medium heat until soft and golden, about 10 minutes.
  3. Mash them while hot with butter and a pinch of salt.
  4. Knead in the cheese and/or pork and shape four tight balls.
  5. Brown the balls in a lightly oiled pan and serve with fried eggs.

How to make it lighter

  • Boil or roast the plantain instead of frying it to cut fat a lot.
  • Choose the cheese version over chicharrón for less saturated fat.
  • Serve with a poached egg and fresh tomato salad.

History

Bolón de verde is one of the best-known breakfasts of Ecuador's coast, especially in Guayaquil and Manabí. Cafés called huecas serve it from early morning. It is also popular in Quito today.
