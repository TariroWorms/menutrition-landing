🥤 Peruvian golden soda (Inca Kola)

Origin: Peru · Lima

This bright yellow Peruvian soda is flavoured with lemon verbena (hierba luisa), giving it a sweet, fruity taste often compared to bubblegum or cream soda. It is very fizzy and very sweet. In Peru it is served ice-cold alongside everyday meals, famously with ceviche, chicken and chifa dishes.

What it is made of

lemon verbenasugarsparkling water

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 15 g fresh or 5 g dried lemon verbena
  • 150 g sugar
  • 150 ml water
  • 1 litre cold carbonated water
  • Ice to serve

Method

  1. Bring the water and sugar to a simmer until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Add the lemon verbena, take off the heat and steep for 15 minutes, then strain and cool.
  3. Pour 40–50 ml of syrup into each glass with ice.
  4. Top up with carbonated water and stir gently.

How to make it lighter

  • A 330 ml glass of the sweetened soda is about 100 kcal; make it with half the syrup.
  • Brew lemon verbena as an unsweetened iced tea for the same aroma without sugar.
  • Drink it with a meal in a small glass rather than a large bottle.

History

The golden soda was created in Lima in the 1930s and became so popular that it is widely seen as Peru's national soft drink. This homemade version uses a lemon verbena syrup and sparkling water.
