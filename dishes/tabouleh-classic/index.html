🌿 Classic Tabbouleh with Mint

Origin: Levant · Lebanon

This classic tabbouleh combines finely chopped parsley with fresh mint, diced tomato and fine bulgur, tossed with lemon juice and olive oil. The mint gives a cooling lift to the peppery parsley and sharp lemon, and the salad stays light and juicy. It is served cold as part of a mezze, alongside grilled meats or with crisp lettuce leaves for scooping.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 40 g fine bulgur
  • 150 g flat-leaf parsley leaves
  • 30 g fresh mint leaves
  • 3 tomatoes, finely diced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 60 ml lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Rinse the bulgur and soak it in the lemon juice for 15 minutes.
  2. Chop the washed and dried parsley and mint very finely.
  3. Dice the tomatoes and slice the spring onions.
  4. Toss herbs, tomato, spring onion and bulgur together.
  5. Season with olive oil, salt and pepper and serve straight away.

How to make it lighter

  • Add extra parsley and tomato and keep the bulgur modest — classic tabbouleh is mostly herbs.
  • Measure the olive oil: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Pair it with grilled chicken for a light, filling meal.

History

Tabbouleh with mint is the version most often found in Lebanese homes and restaurants, and the salad as a whole traces back to the mountain villages of Lebanon and Syria. Lebanon even celebrates a National Tabbouleh Day each July.
