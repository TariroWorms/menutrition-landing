Hunan tiger skin peppers (Hu pi qing jiao)

Origin: China · Hunan

Long green chilies are pressed in a dry wok until their skins blister and wrinkle like tiger stripes. They are then tossed with garlic, soy sauce, vinegar and a pinch of sugar. The dish is smoky, tangy and mildly hot, and usually served as a side or appetizer.

Recipe

3 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g long green chilies
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • pinch of salt

Method

  1. Wash and dry the chilies and trim the stems.
  2. Heat a dry wok and press the chilies flat with a spatula until blistered.
  3. Add a little oil and fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Add garlic and cook until fragrant.
  5. Season with the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and a pinch of salt, toss and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use very little oil; the wok does the work.
  • Cut salt and soy for a lighter side.
  • Serve with plain rice or tofu.

History

The dish is a staple of Hunan home cooking, valued for how a few ingredients make a vivid side. The blistered skin is what gives it its name.
