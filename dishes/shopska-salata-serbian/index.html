🥗 Shopska Salad (Serbian Style)

Origin: Serbia · Balkans

Shopska salad is a chopped salad of ripe tomatoes, cucumber, onion and roasted peppers, covered with a snowy layer of grated sirene, a white brined cheese. In Serbia it is a fixture of every grill house, eaten alongside ćevapi and other grilled meats. It is fresh, crunchy and salty, dressed simply with oil, vinegar and salt.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe tomatoes, diced
  • 2 cucumbers, diced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 roasted peppers, peeled and chopped
  • 150 g sirene cheese, grated
  • 1 tbsp sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • Salt and parsley

Method

  1. Combine the tomatoes, cucumbers, onion and peppers in a bowl.
  2. Dress with oil, vinegar and salt and toss.
  3. Grate the cheese generously over the top and scatter with parsley.
  4. Serve immediately, before the vegetables release too much juice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cheese and grate it finely so it still covers the salad.
  • Measure the oil: 1 tbsp ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve it as the side to grilled meat and skip extra starchy sides.

History

Shopska salad is named after the Shopi people of western Bulgaria and is widely said to have been popularised by Bulgarian state tourism in the 1950s–60s. It quickly spread across the Balkans, and Serbia, North Macedonia and Bulgaria each serve their own version today.
