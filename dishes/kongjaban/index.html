Kongjaban (braised black soybeans)

Recipe

8 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g dried black soybeans (about 500 g soaked)
  • 60 ml soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp honey or rice syrup
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 250 ml water

Method

  1. Soak the beans overnight and drain.
  2. Toast the beans in a dry pan for 5 minutes to remove the raw smell.
  3. Add water and soy sauce and simmer covered for 30 minutes until tender.
  4. Stir in sugar and oil, then cook uncovered until the sauce reduces.
  5. Add honey and sesame seeds, stir until glossy, and cool.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar and honey by half for a lower-sugar side.
  • Use low-sodium soy sauce.
  • Serve a spoonful per person; it is rich in protein and fiber.

History

A long-lasting home side dish in Korea, made in a batch and kept in the fridge for the week. Black soybeans are also used in other traditional foods.
