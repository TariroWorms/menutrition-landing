Kerak telor (Jakarta spiced egg and rice omelette)

Origin: Indonesia · Jakarta

Kerak telor is a small, thick omelette of glutinous rice and duck or chicken egg, cooked in a pan over charcoal and flipped upside down to toast over the flames. It is sprinkled with toasted grated coconut, fried shallots and dried shrimp, and has a spicy, chewy-crisp bite. It is a classic of Betawi street food.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g glutinous rice, soaked 4 hours
  • 2 eggs
  • 3 tbsp toasted grated coconut (kerisik)
  • 1 tbsp dried shrimp, ground
  • 2 tbsp fried shallots
  • 1 cm piece of ginger, grated, 1 tsp ground pepper and salt
  • 1 tsp galangal, grated
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Drain the rice.
  2. Beat the eggs with ginger, galangal, pepper and salt.
  3. Heat a pan with a little oil and spread the rice over it.
  4. Pour in the egg and cook on low heat until set.
  5. Flip the pan upside down over a flame to brown the top.
  6. Sprinkle with coconut, dried shrimp and fried shallots and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only a thin film of oil in a non-stick pan.
  • Go easy on the chilli if serving children.
  • Pair with cucumber slices for freshness.

History

It is a traditional dish of the Betawi people of Jakarta and is closely linked to the Jakarta Fair, where it is sold every year. Vendors still cook it on small charcoal stoves.
