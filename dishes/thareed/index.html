🍲 Thareed (lamb and bread stew)

Thareed is a comforting dish of thin flatbread torn into pieces and soaked in a lamb and vegetable stew flavoured with tomato and baharat spice mix. The bread softens into the rich broth, while chunks of tender lamb, potato, carrot and zucchini sit on top. It is served in a large shared bowl and is a favourite at iftar during Ramadan across the Gulf.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb on the bone, in chunks
  • 3 thin flatbreads (regag or similar)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped, and 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 potatoes, 2 carrots and 1 zucchini, in chunks
  • 2 tsp baharat
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1.2 litres water, salt

Method

  1. Brown the onion in the oil, add the lamb and baharat and sear for 5 minutes.
  2. Add tomatoes, tomato paste and water and simmer covered for 1 hour.
  3. Add the potatoes and carrots and cook 20 minutes, then the zucchini for 10 more.
  4. Tear the bread into a deep dish, ladle the broth over it to soak and top with the meat and vegetables.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the lamb and skim fat off the broth.
  • Use more vegetables and less bread in each bowl.
  • Choose a lean cut such as leg for a lighter stew.

History

Thareed is one of the oldest recorded Arab dishes, mentioned in early Islamic sources, and the Prophet Muhammad is reported to have praised it. It remains a Ramadan staple in the Gulf states and Yemen today.
