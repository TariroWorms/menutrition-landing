Zeamă (Moldovan sour chicken noodle soup)

Origin: Moldova

Zeamă is the everyday chicken soup of Moldova, a clear golden broth with homemade noodles, carrot and onion. Its signature is the sour tang from borș, a fermented wheat-bran liquid, and a generous handful of fresh lovage. It is served piping hot, often with a spoon of sour cream or a chopped hot pepper on the side.

Recipe

6 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, 1.2 kg
  • 3 l water
  • 2 carrots, 200 g
  • 1 onion, 120 g
  • 1 parsnip, 80 g
  • 150 g egg noodles
  • 250 ml borș (or lemon juice)
  • 1 bunch lovage, 30 g
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Cover the chicken with cold water and bring to a gentle simmer, skimming the foam.
  2. Add the whole onion, carrot and parsnip and cook for 50 minutes.
  3. Lift out the chicken, shred the meat and discard bones and skin.
  4. Strain the broth, slice the carrot and return it with the meat.
  5. Boil the noodles directly in the broth until tender, about 6 minutes.
  6. Stir in the borș and chopped lovage and simmer for 3 minutes.
  7. Taste, adjust salt and sourness, and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim the broth and remove the skin to keep the soup light.
  • Use whole-wheat noodles or fewer noodles for more fibre.
  • Use lemon juice if you cannot find borș, adding it at the end.

History

Sour soups built on fermented bran are shared across Moldova and Romania and were long a way to make a plain broth lively without citrus. Zeamă is traditionally cooked for family Sundays and for guests, and is a common remedy for colds.
