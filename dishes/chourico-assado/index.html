🔥 Flaming chouriço

Chouriço assado is a Portuguese smoked pork sausage, rich with paprika and garlic, grilled at the table over flaming brandy in a glazed clay dish shaped like a pig. The skin crackles and chars, the inside stays juicy and smoky. It is served sliced as a starter or petisco, often with olives and salad.

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 chouriço sausage (about 350 g)
  • 60 ml brandy or other strong spirit
  • Coarse salt

Method

  1. Score the chouriço with shallow diagonal cuts.
  2. Spread a layer of coarse salt in the clay dish (or a heatproof metal tray) and set the sausage on the grid above it.
  3. Pour the brandy over the salt, carefully light it and let the sausage grill in the flames, turning once, for 6–8 minutes until blistered.
  4. Slice and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Treat it as a shared starter: 2–3 slices per person.
  • Let the fat drip into the dish and leave it there rather than mopping it up.
  • Serve with plenty of olives, tomato and green salad alongside.

History

Smoked chouriço is made all over Portugal, and flame-grilling it in an assador de barro, a clay dish with a grid on top, is a long-standing tavern custom. It is now a familiar starter in Portuguese restaurants at home and abroad.
