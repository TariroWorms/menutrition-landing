Tom Kha is a Thai coconut soup built on galangal and lemongrass. Sliced chicken thigh and mushrooms simmer gently in coconut milk until tender, and a squeeze of lime at the end gives the creamy broth its sour, fragrant edge. It is served hot in a bowl, often alongside steamed rice.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g sliced chicken thigh
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 300 ml water
  • 5 slices galangal
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, cut and bruised
  • 200 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1–2 fresh chilies (optional)
  • Salt and coriander leaves

Method

  1. Bring the water and half the coconut milk to a gentle simmer with the galangal and lemongrass for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the sliced chicken thigh and mushrooms and simmer until just cooked through.
  3. Pour in the remaining coconut milk and heat without boiling hard so it stays smooth.
  4. Off the heat, stir in the lime juice, season with salt and add chilies to taste.
  5. Serve hot, garnished with coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half coconut milk and half water or stock for a lighter broth.
  • Add extra mushrooms and vegetables to fill the bowl with fewer calories.
  • Keep rice on the side and measure it, since it adds a lot to the meal.

History

Tom kha is a classic of central Thai cooking; the name means boiled galangal soup. It is served in homes and restaurants across Thailand and is one of the best-known Thai dishes abroad.
