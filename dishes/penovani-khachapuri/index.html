Penovani khachapuri

Origin: Georgia

Penovani khachapuri is the flaky cousin of the classic cheese bread. Squares of puff pastry are filled with grated brined cheese, folded into envelopes and baked until crisp. The buttery layers shatter around a soft, salty centre. Bakeries across Georgia sell it as a quick snack with tea or coffee.

What it is made of

puff pastryImeruli cheesesulguni cheeseeggbutter

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g puff pastry
  • 250 g Imeruli cheese, grated
  • 150 g sulguni, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 10 g butter, melted

Method

  1. Roll the pastry thin and cut into 6 squares.
  2. Mix the cheeses with half the beaten egg.
  3. Place a mound of filling in the centre of each square.
  4. Fold the corners to the middle and press to seal.
  5. Brush with the remaining egg.
  6. Bake at 200 °C for 20–25 minutes until puffed and golden.
  7. Brush with melted butter and let cool a few minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose puff pastry made with butter rather than hydrogenated fats.
  • Add chopped spinach to the filling for volume and fewer calories per bite.
  • Count one square as a full snack; it is energy-dense.

History

'Penovani' means layered, referring to the laminated dough. It is a popular bakery item in Georgian towns and is often made at home with ready-made puff pastry.
