Gua bao (Taiwanese pork belly bun)

Origin: Taiwan

Gua bao is a folded steamed bun, like a small clamshell, filled with a slice of soy-braised pork belly, tangy pickled mustard greens, sweet ground peanut and cilantro. It is soft, rich and sweet-salty with a fresh herbal finish. It is eaten by hand and is a popular night market snack.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 8 small steamed buns
  • 400 g pork belly
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 100 g pickled mustard greens
  • 3 tbsp crushed peanuts
  • cilantro

Method

  1. Braise the pork belly with soy sauce, sugar and water for 90 minutes until tender.
  2. Slice into thick slabs.
  3. Steam the buns for 8 minutes.
  4. Open each bun and add pork, pickles and a little braising sauce.
  5. Top with peanuts and cilantro.

How to make it lighter

  • Use leaner cuts or trim fat from the pork.
  • Add extra pickled greens and cilantro.
  • Choose one bun per portion plus a salad.

History

It is often called a Taiwanese hamburger and is linked to Hokkien-influenced cooking on the island. Variants today include chicken, vegetarian or fried fillings.
