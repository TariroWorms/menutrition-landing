Bulgarian moussaka (potato and mince bake)

Origin: Bulgaria

Bulgarian moussaka is built on potatoes rather than eggplant. Sautéed minced meat and onion are layered with potatoes, then topped with a whisked yogurt and egg mixture that sets golden. It is homey, savory and served hot.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes
  • 500 g minced pork or beef
  • 1 onion
  • 3 eggs
  • 250 g yogurt
  • paprika, salt, oil

Method

  1. Dice the potatoes and parboil them.
  2. Sauté onion, then mince, until browned.
  3. Season with paprika and salt.
  4. Layer potatoes and meat in a dish.
  5. Whisk eggs with yogurt and pour over.
  6. Bake at 190 C for 35 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean mince.
  • Bake rather than fry the potatoes.
  • Use low-fat yogurt.

History

The name came from the Ottoman-era kitchens shared across the Balkans. Bulgarians adapted it with potatoes and a yogurt topping instead of béchamel.
