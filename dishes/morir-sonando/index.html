Morir soñando (Dominican orange and milk drink)

Origin: Dominican Republic

The name means "to die dreaming", and the drink is a cold, creamy blend of orange juice and milk over crushed ice. It tastes like an orange creamsicle, sweet with a light tang. Dominicans enjoy it as a refreshing treat on hot days.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 250 ml freshly squeezed orange juice
  • 250 ml evaporated milk
  • 40 g sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup crushed ice

Method

  1. Squeeze the oranges and strain out the pulp if you prefer.
  2. Chill the evaporated milk well.
  3. Stir the sugar and vanilla into the milk until dissolved.
  4. Fill two tall glasses with crushed ice.
  5. Pour in the orange juice, then slowly add the milk mixture and stir lightly.
  6. Serve at once, as the juice can curdle the milk if it stands.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat evaporated milk and less sugar.
  • Add the milk slowly and serve immediately so it does not separate.
  • Try a splash of sparkling water for a lighter version.

History

Morir soñando is a classic Dominican refresher and is also loved in Puerto Rico and among Dominican communities abroad. It is a simple home drink and a staple of local juice stands.
