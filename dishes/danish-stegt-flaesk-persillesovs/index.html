Crispy pork with parsley sauce (stegt flæsk)

Origin: Denmark

Stegt flæsk med persillesovs is thin slices of pork belly fried until crisp and served with boiled potatoes and creamy parsley sauce. The salty crisp pork contrasts with the mild, herby sauce. It is a hearty everyday classic.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g pork belly in thin slices
  • 800 g potatoes
  • 40 g butter
  • 40 g flour
  • 500 ml milk
  • 4 tbsp chopped parsley
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until tender, about 20 minutes.
  2. Melt butter, stir in flour and cook 1 minute.
  3. Whisk in the milk gradually and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in plenty of parsley, salt and pepper.
  5. Fry the pork slices in a dry pan until golden and crisp.
  6. Drain on paper.
  7. Serve with potatoes and sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Drain the fat well and choose leaner slices.
  • Make the sauce with low-fat milk.
  • Add a salad of pickled beetroot for freshness.

History

It is often named as Denmark's national dish, and in a 2014 public vote it won that title. It is a home-style meal that is also served in traditional restaurants.
