Bird cherry cake (Cheryomukhovy tort)

Origin: Russia · Siberia
396kcal

This Siberian cake is made with flour ground from dried bird cherries, stones and all. The flour gives the sponge a dark chocolate-brown colour and a nutty, almond-like flavour with a light crunch. The layers are soaked and spread with sweetened sour cream. It is served in slices with tea, usually after resting overnight.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g ground bird cherry
  • 300 ml hot milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 150 g sugar
  • 120 g flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 400 g sour cream (15%)
  • 80 g powdered sugar

Method

  1. Pour hot milk over the ground bird cherry and leave to swell for at least 1 hour.
  2. Beat eggs with sugar until fluffy.
  3. Fold in the bird cherry mixture, then flour with baking powder.
  4. Bake in a lined tin at 180 °C for about 40 minutes and cool.
  5. Whisk sour cream with powdered sugar.
  6. Cut the sponge into two layers, spread with cream and chill overnight.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 10% sour cream or mix it with thick yogurt for a lighter frosting.
  • Cut the sugar in the sponge by a third — the bird cherry is naturally aromatic.
  • Serve thin slices; the fibre-rich flour makes it quite filling.

History

Bird cherry grows widely across Siberia and the Urals, where the dried and ground berries have long been used in home baking. The cake is strongly associated with Siberian regional cooking and is still made in many families today.
