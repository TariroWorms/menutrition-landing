🥩 Black pepper beef

Black pepper beef is a Cantonese-style stir-fry of tender strips of beef tossed over high heat with onion, bell pepper and a glossy soy sauce glaze loaded with coarsely cracked black pepper. It is savoury, slightly sweet and pleasantly hot from the pepper rather than chilli, with crisp-tender vegetables. It is served straight from the wok with steamed rice.

Recipe

3 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g beef flank or sirloin, thinly sliced across the grain
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp cornstarch and 1 tsp sugar
  • 1–2 tsp coarsely cracked black pepper
  • 1 onion, cut into wedges
  • 1 green and 1 red bell pepper, cut into squares
  • 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

  1. Toss the beef with 1 tbsp soy sauce and the cornstarch and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok until smoking, sear the beef in one layer for 1–2 minutes and lift it out.
  3. Add the remaining oil, stir-fry the onion and peppers for 2 minutes, then add the garlic and black pepper for 30 seconds.
  4. Return the beef, add the rest of the soy sauce, the sugar and 2 tbsp water, and toss for 1 minute until glossy.
  5. Serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose a lean cut such as flank or sirloin and trim visible fat.
  • Double the onion and peppers for more volume on the plate.
  • Stir-fry in a well-heated wok with 1 tablespoon of oil instead of two — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Black pepper beef is a staple of Cantonese and Hong Kong restaurant cooking and of Chinese restaurants around the world, often served on a sizzling iron plate.
