🥞 Haemul Pajeon (Scallion Seafood Pancake)

Origin: South Korea · Busan

Haemul pajeon is a savory Korean pancake built on whole scallion stalks laid in the pan and topped with seafood and a flour-and-egg batter. Fried until crisp and golden, it is cut into squares and dipped into a tangy soy-vinegar sauce. It is a classic shared appetizer, often eaten with friends on rainy days.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch scallions, whole stalks
  • 200 g mixed seafood (squid, shrimp, clams)
  • 100 g flour
  • 1 egg
  • 150 ml cold water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • Chili flakes to taste

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar and chili flakes for the dipping sauce.
  2. Whisk the flour, egg and cold water into a thin batter.
  3. Heat the oil in a wide pan, lay the scallions side by side and scatter the seafood over them.
  4. Pour the batter evenly on top and fry 4–5 minutes until crisp underneath.
  5. Flip, cook another 4 minutes, cut into squares and serve with the sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Fry with 1 tbsp oil in a non-stick pan; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use more scallion and seafood and less batter.
  • Dip lightly — the sauce is salty, and a little goes a long way.

History

Pajeon is a long-standing Korean pan-fried dish, and the version from Dongnae in Busan, made with seafood and scallions, is especially well known. Today it is served in Korean homes and pubs across the country.
