Etchmiadzin tolma (stuffed summer vegetables)

Origin: Armenia · Vagharshapat

Etchmiadzin tolma brings together three summer vegetables, tomatoes, sweet peppers and small eggplants, all hollowed out and filled with the same herby mix of minced meat and rice. They cook side by side so the tomato juice bastes everything. The result is soft, fragrant and a little sweet, eaten warm with yogurt and bread.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 4 tomatoes
  • 4 sweet peppers
  • 4 small eggplants
  • 400 g minced beef
  • 80 g rice
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp chopped basil, cilantro and parsley
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cut lids off the tomatoes and peppers and scoop out the insides; keep the tomato pulp.
  2. Halve the eggplants lengthwise, hollow them a little and salt them for 15 minutes.
  3. Mix the beef with rice, onion, herbs, chopped tomato pulp, salt and pepper.
  4. Fill the vegetables loosely and set them upright in a deep pan; replace the lids.
  5. Add a glass of water, cover and bake at 180 °C for about 1 hour.
  6. Rest 10 minutes and serve with yogurt.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef or a mix with lentils to make it lighter.
  • Bake rather than fry the eggplants first, as some recipes suggest.
  • Pair with a green salad instead of extra bread.

History

The dish takes its name from Etchmiadzin, the town better known as Vagharshapat and home to the Mother See of the Armenian Apostolic Church. It is a favourite in late summer when gardens overflow with vegetables.
