Kata (Azerbaijani layered flatbread)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Kata is a round flatbread made of thin dough brushed with butter, folded into layers and filled with onion, pumpkin, greens or just sugar and butter. It comes out flaky and soft inside, savoury or lightly sweet depending on the filling. It is eaten warm with tea or alongside soup.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 200 ml warm water
  • 5 g dry yeast
  • 100 g melted butter
  • 300 g pumpkin, grated
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • Salt, 1 tsp sugar

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt and water into a soft dough and let it rise for 40 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion lightly in a little butter, add the pumpkin and cook until soft; season and cool.
  3. Divide the dough into 4 balls and roll each one very thin.
  4. Brush with butter, spread a layer of filling, fold the edges to the centre and roll out gently again.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 20 minutes or cook on a dry griddle, turning once.
  6. Brush with a little butter and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush the layers lightly with oil instead of soaking them in butter.
  • Use more pumpkin or greens in the filling to add fibre and volume.
  • Mix part wholemeal flour into the dough for slower-digesting carbs.

History

Kata is a home bake found across many regions of Azerbaijan, and each family has its favourite filling. Similar layered flatbreads exist throughout the Caucasus and Central Asia.
