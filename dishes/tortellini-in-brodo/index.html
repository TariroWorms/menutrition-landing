🍲 Tortellini in Brodo

Origin: Italy · Bologna, Emilia-Romagna

Tortellini in brodo are tiny hand-folded egg-pasta rings stuffed with pork, prosciutto and Parmesan, simmered and served in a clear meat broth. The filling is savoury and rich, the pasta thin and tender, and the broth light and fragrant. It is a festive Bolognese first course, often topped with a little extra Parmesan.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g fresh egg pasta dough
  • 100 g minced pork
  • 50 g prosciutto, finely chopped
  • 60 g grated Parmesan
  • pinch of nutmeg
  • 1.5 l clear meat broth
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook the pork briefly in a dry pan, then blend with prosciutto, Parmesan, nutmeg and pepper into a smooth filling.
  2. Roll the pasta very thin and cut into 3–4 cm squares.
  3. Place a little filling on each square, fold into a triangle, seal and join the two corners around a fingertip.
  4. Simmer the tortellini in the broth for 3–4 minutes until tender.
  5. Serve in deep bowls with plenty of hot broth.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep the portion to 12–15 tortellini and fill the bowl with broth.
  • Chill the broth and lift off the set fat before using it.
  • Prosciutto and Parmesan are salty, so skip extra salt and cheese at the table.

History

Tortellini are a speciality of Emilia-Romagna, with Bologna and Modena both claiming them; a popular legend links their shape to the navel of Venus. Served in capon or meat broth, they are the traditional Christmas first course in Bologna.
