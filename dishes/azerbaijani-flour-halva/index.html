Un halvasi (Azerbaijani flour halva)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Un halvasi is made by slowly toasting flour in butter until it smells of biscuits and turns golden brown, then stirring in a hot syrup scented with saffron, cardamom and rose water. It thickens into a soft, dense paste that is spread on a plate and patterned with a spoon. It is eaten in small portions with tea and bread.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 150 g butter or ghee
  • 250 g sugar
  • 350 ml water
  • a pinch of saffron
  • 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tbsp rose water

Method

  1. Boil sugar and water for 5 minutes, add saffron, cardamom and rose water and keep warm.
  2. Melt the butter in a heavy pan and add the flour.
  3. Toast over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, for 15–20 minutes until golden brown and nutty.
  4. Carefully pour in the hot syrup while stirring; it will spit and thicken quickly.
  5. Cook for 2–3 minutes more until the mixture leaves the sides of the pan.
  6. Spread on a plate, press a pattern with a spoon and serve warm or at room temperature.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 100 g butter instead of 150 g; the toasted flour still gives a deep flavour.
  • Reduce sugar by a quarter and add more saffron and cardamom.
  • A serving of 40–50 g is enough with tea.

History

In Azerbaijan flour halva is traditionally prepared for mourning gatherings and remembrance days, as well as on Thursdays before Novruz. Sharing it with neighbours is part of the custom.
