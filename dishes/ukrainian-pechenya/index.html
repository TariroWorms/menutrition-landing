Pechenya (pork and potatoes in a clay pot)

Origin: Ukraine

Pechenya is a rustic Ukrainian pot roast in which chunks of pork and potato bake together in individual clay pots. The meat releases its juices into the potatoes, so everything turns soft, savory and deeply flavored. It is served straight from the pot, often with pickles and dark bread.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g pork shoulder
  • 800 g potatoes
  • 2 onions
  • 1 carrot
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 120 g sour cream
  • 300 ml water or broth
  • bay leaf, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Cut the pork into cubes and brown it quickly in a hot pan.
  2. Fry the onion and grated carrot until soft.
  3. Peel and cube the potatoes.
  4. Layer meat, vegetables and potatoes in clay pots, adding garlic, bay leaf, salt and pepper.
  5. Pour in water or broth to half the height and top with sour cream.
  6. Cover and bake at 180 °C for about 90 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork loin and trim visible fat.
  • Replace part of the potatoes with cabbage or mushrooms.
  • Swap sour cream for plain yogurt added at the end.

History

Cooking in clay pots inside a wood-fired oven is an old village tradition across Ukraine. Today the dish is made in ordinary ovens, but the pots are still used to keep it moist.
