Fried herring (Stekt strömming)

Origin: Sweden

Stekt strömming is a dish of small Baltic herring fillets coated in rye flour or breadcrumbs and fried in butter until golden. The fillets are crisp outside and moist inside with a rich, slightly sweet fish flavour. They are served with mashed potato, lingonberry jam, pickled cucumber and brown butter.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g Baltic herring fillets
  • 60 g rye flour
  • 50 g butter
  • 700 g potatoes
  • 50 ml milk
  • 10 g fresh dill
  • salt – to taste
  • white pepper – to taste

Method

  1. Season the herring fillets with salt and pepper.
  2. Dip in the rye flour or crumbs, pressing to coat.
  3. Heat butter in a wide pan until foaming.
  4. Fry the fillets for 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Meanwhile boil the potatoes and mash them with the milk. Serve the herring with the mash, dill and lingonberry jam.

How to make it lighter

  • Pan-fry in less fat and drain on paper.
  • Pair with a fresh cucumber salad rather than butter sauce.
  • Herring is rich in omega-3, so it makes a great weekly fish.

History

Baltic herring is one of the most traditional fish of the Swedish coast and has been eaten for centuries. Fried strömming is an everyday classic at home and in lunch restaurants.
