Kacchi biryani (Dhaka-style mutton biryani)

Origin: Bangladesh · Dhaka

Kacchi biryani is the celebratory biryani of Dhaka, where raw mutton marinated in yogurt, spices and fried onion is layered with parboiled fragrant rice and potatoes. The pot is sealed and cooked slowly so meat juices flavour the rice. It is rich, aromatic and typically served with borhani, a salted spiced yogurt drink.

Recipe

6 servings210 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g mutton, bone-in
  • 500 g aromatic rice
  • 300 g potatoes
  • 250 g yogurt, 3 onions fried (200 g)
  • 100 g ghee (4 tbsp oil)
  • garam masala, ginger-garlic paste, saffron milk, salt

Method

  1. Marinate the mutton overnight in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices and half the fried onion.
  2. Parboil the rice with whole spices until 70 percent done and drain.
  3. Layer the meat with potatoes at the bottom of a heavy pot.
  4. Spread the rice on top and finish with ghee, fried onion and saffron milk.
  5. Seal the pot with dough or foil and cook on low heat for 1 hour 15 minutes.
  6. Rest 15 minutes, then fold the layers gently and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 60 g ghee instead of 100 g.
  • Choose lean cuts and trim visible fat.
  • Serve with cucumber salad to balance richness.

History

Biryani reached Bengal with Mughal court cooking, and the raw-meat kacchi style is a hallmark of Dhaka. It is the dish of weddings and large gatherings in Bangladesh.
