Bun kebab is Karachi's favourite street sandwich: a spiced lentil patty dipped in beaten egg and fried on a griddle, tucked into a soft toasted bun with raw onion, tomato and a tangy green or tamarind chutney. It is soft, spicy and saucy, sold hot from roadside stalls as a cheap snack or quick meal.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 4 soft buns
  • 200 g chana dal (split chickpeas), soaked for 2 hours
  • 1 tsp each cumin, chili powder and garam masala, salt
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 onion, sliced into rings, and 1 tomato, sliced
  • 4 tbsp green chutney (coriander, mint, green chili, lemon)
  • 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Boil the dal with the spices until soft and dry; mash and shape into 4 patties.
  2. Dip each patty in beaten egg and fry on a hot griddle until golden on both sides.
  3. Toast the halved buns on the griddle.
  4. Spread the buns with chutney, add the patty, onion and tomato.
  5. Serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Cook the patties on a non-stick pan with 1 tsp oil instead of shallow frying.
  • Load the bun with extra onion, tomato and cucumber.
  • Skip extra sweet chutney and use the herb chutney instead.

History

Bun kebab is a street food of Pakistan, above all Karachi, where it is sold from carts and small stalls across the city.
