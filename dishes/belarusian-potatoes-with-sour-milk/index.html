Boiled potatoes with sour milk

Origin: Belarus

This is one of the simplest and most loved summer meals in Belarus: hot boiled potatoes sprinkled with dill, eaten alongside a bowl of cold sour milk. The contrast between the warm, starchy potato and the cool, tangy milk is the whole point. It is light, refreshing and very quick.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes
  • 600 ml sour milk (curdled milk)
  • 20 g dill
  • salt and black pepper — to taste

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes, or scrub young ones and leave the skin on.
  2. Boil them in salted water until tender, about 20 minutes.
  3. Drain and let the steam evaporate for a minute.
  4. Sprinkle generously with chopped dill.
  5. Pour the cold sour milk into bowls or glasses.
  6. Serve the hot potatoes with the sour milk on the side.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep the skins on young potatoes for more fiber and potassium.
  • Use low-fat kefir if sour milk is not available.
  • Add sliced cucumber or radish for crunch.

History

Sour milk, made by letting fresh milk curdle naturally in a warm place, was a daily product in Belarusian villages. Paired with potatoes, it was standard fare during summer field work.
