Chimichurri dominicano (chimi burger)

Origin: Dominican Republic

The chimi is a Dominican street burger made with a seasoned ground beef or pork patty on a soft roll. It is topped with shredded cabbage, tomato and a pink sauce of mayonnaise and ketchup. It is sold from food carts late at night and tastes savoury, juicy and fresh from the cabbage.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 egg
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 4 soft rolls
  • 200 g shredded cabbage
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 3 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 3 tbsp ketchup

Method

  1. Mix the beef with egg, garlic, oregano and salt.
  2. Shape into four patties.
  3. Griddle 4 minutes per side until cooked through.
  4. Mix mayonnaise and ketchup into a pink sauce.
  5. Warm the rolls and spread with sauce.
  6. Fill with patty, cabbage and tomato.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean meat or turkey.
  • Choose a whole-grain roll and light mayonnaise.
  • Add extra cabbage and tomato for fibre.

History

The name is thought to be a playful take on the English word burger or on the Argentine chimichurri sauce, although the Dominican chimi has nothing to do with that sauce. It is a classic of Dominican nightlife and street food.
