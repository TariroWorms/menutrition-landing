Kachamak (Bulgarian cornmeal porridge)

Origin: Bulgaria

Kachamak is a thick porridge of cornmeal stirred with water until smooth and glossy, then topped with crumbled white cheese and melted butter. It is warming, mild and filling, with a salty topping. It is a traditional mountain dish, sometimes served with cracklings or yogurt.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g coarse cornmeal
  • 800 ml water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 60 g white brined cheese
  • 30 g butter
  • paprika to finish

Method

  1. Bring the salted water to a boil.
  2. Whisk in the cornmeal in a thin stream.
  3. Lower the heat and stir with a wooden spoon for 25-30 minutes until thick.
  4. Spoon the porridge into bowls.
  5. Melt the butter with paprika.
  6. Top with cheese and butter and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter and top with yogurt instead.
  • Choose wholegrain cornmeal for more fiber.
  • Pair with vegetables for a balanced meal.

History

Cornmeal replaced older grains in the Balkans after maize arrived from the Americas. In Bulgarian mountain villages kachamak became an everyday food, similar to Romanian mamaliga and Italian polenta.
