Tostadas de tinga are crisp fried tortillas piled with tinga, shredded chicken stewed in a smoky chipotle and tomato sauce. They are topped with shredded lettuce, a drizzle of cream, crumbled cheese and slices of avocado. Every bite mixes crunch, smoke, gentle heat and cool creaminess.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 8 tostadas
  • 400 g chicken breast
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 chipotle chillies in adobo
  • 100 g shredded lettuce
  • 60 ml Mexican-style cream
  • 60 g crumbled fresh cheese
  • 1 avocado
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in salted water for 20 minutes, then shred it.
  2. Blend tomatoes with the chipotles.
  3. Soften the onion in the oil, add the tomato sauce, simmer 5 minutes, then stir in the chicken and cook until thick.
  4. Top each tostada with lettuce and tinga.
  5. Finish with cream, cheese and avocado slices.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the tortillas instead of frying them to make the tostadas.
  • Use a teaspoon of cream per tostada and plenty of lettuce.
  • Two tostadas with a generous chicken layer make a filling meal.

History

Tinga is associated with the state of Puebla in central Mexico, where it is a long-standing home and market dish. Served on tostadas, it is a common snack and party food across the country.
