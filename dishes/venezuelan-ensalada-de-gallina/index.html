Ensalada de gallina (Venezuelan chicken salad)

Origin: Venezuela

Ensalada de gallina is the cool, creamy side on the Venezuelan Christmas plate. Boiled hen is shredded and folded with diced potato, carrot, green peas and often apple, all bound with mayonnaise. It is mild and slightly sweet, a soft contrast to the rich hallacas and pan de jamón. Leftovers are just as popular the next day in sandwiches.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg hen or chicken (500 g cooked meat), plus 1 onion and herbs for poaching
  • 700 g potatoes, 2 carrots (200 g)
  • 150 g green peas
  • 1 green apple (150 g)
  • 150 g mayonnaise
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, salt, pepper, lime juice

Method

  1. Simmer the hen in salted water with onion and herbs until tender, about 1 hour; cool and shred the meat.
  2. Boil the diced potatoes and carrots until just tender, add the peas for the last 3 minutes and drain.
  3. Dice the apple and toss with a little lime juice.
  4. Mix vegetables, apple and chicken with mayonnaise, salt and pepper.
  5. Fold in chopped eggs and chill for at least 1 hour before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half mayonnaise and half plain yogurt.
  • Add more carrot and peas, fewer potatoes.
  • Remove all chicken skin before shredding.

History

The salad comes from the family of potato-and-mayonnaise salads that spread through Latin America in the 20th century. In Venezuela it became fixed as one of the three parts of the Christmas plate, together with hallacas and pan de jamón.
