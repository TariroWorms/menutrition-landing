🥣 Soupe au Pistou

Origin: France · Provence

Soupe au pistou is a Provençal summer vegetable soup of white beans, zucchini, potato and small pasta, simmered until thick and chunky. Just before serving, a spoonful of basil pesto with garlic is stirred into each bowl, so the hot soup releases a burst of fresh basil aroma. It is served warm or at room temperature as a hearty main.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g cooked white beans
  • 2 zucchini, diced
  • 2 potatoes, diced
  • 2 carrots and 1 onion, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 80 g small pasta
  • 4 tbsp basil pesto
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1.8 l water, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Soften the onion and carrots in the oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the water, potatoes, tomatoes and salt and simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Add the zucchini and beans and cook 10 minutes more.
  4. Add the pasta and cook until tender, about 10 minutes.
  5. Mix the pesto with the garlic and stir a spoonful into each bowl when serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 2 tsp of pesto per bowl — it is the richest part of the soup.
  • Double the zucchini and halve the pasta for a lighter bowl.
  • Make a big pot — it keeps well for several days and portions easily.

History

The soup is a speciality of Provence and especially the Nice area, and it is closely related to Italian minestrone with pesto from neighbouring Liguria. Pistou is the Provençal word for the pounded basil and garlic sauce.
