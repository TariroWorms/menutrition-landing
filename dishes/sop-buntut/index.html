Sop buntut (Indonesian oxtail soup)

Origin: Indonesia

Sop buntut is a clear, gently spiced soup with large pieces of oxtail, carrots and potatoes. The broth is rich from the bones yet light, flavored with nutmeg, cloves, garlic and pepper. The meat falls off the bone after long simmering. It is served with rice, sambal and lime, with fried oxtail as a popular variation.

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g oxtail
  • 2 l water
  • 2 carrots (200 g), 2 potatoes (250 g)
  • 4 garlic cloves, 1 onion
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg, 3 cloves, pepper
  • celery leaf, fried shallots, lime

Method

  1. Blanch oxtail 5 minutes and discard the water.
  2. Simmer it in fresh water with onion and spices for 2 hours.
  3. Add carrots and potatoes and cook 25 minutes.
  4. Season with salt and pepper and skim fat.
  5. Serve with celery leaf, fried shallots and lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Chill the broth and remove the fat layer.
  • Add more vegetables than meat.
  • Use less salt and flavor with lime and herbs.

History

The soup shows European influence in its vegetables and spices and is a favorite in Jakarta restaurants and hotels. It is widely seen as a comforting dish for family meals.
