Bastani sonnati (Persian saffron ice cream)

Origin: Iran

A golden, creamy ice cream flavoured with saffron and rosewater, scattered with chopped pistachios and often with small chunks of frozen cream. The taste is floral and rich with a subtle bitterness from saffron. It is sometimes served between thin wafers or with faloodeh.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 250 ml cream
  • 120 g sugar
  • 1 g saffron steeped in 3 tbsp hot water
  • 1 tbsp rose water
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 50 g chopped pistachios

Method

  1. Heat the milk and cream with the sugar until steaming.
  2. Whisk the yolks and temper with hot milk.
  3. Return to the pan and cook gently until it coats a spoon.
  4. Stir in saffron and rose water, then chill fully.
  5. Churn, fold in pistachios, and freeze until firm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use part milk and less cream to lower fat.
  • Reduce sugar slightly; saffron gives strong flavour.
  • Serve a smaller scoop with fresh fruit.

History

Bastani sonnati means traditional ice cream and is a favourite across Iran's cafes. Classic versions use thickened milk and cream, with saffron as the signature flavour.
