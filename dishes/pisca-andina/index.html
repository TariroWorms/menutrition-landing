Pisca andina (Andean breakfast soup)

Origin: Venezuela · Andes

Pisca andina is the morning soup of the cold mountain towns of Mérida and Táchira. A light broth is simmered with diced potato, scallion, garlic and lots of cilantro; an egg is poached in it and a splash of milk and cubes of fresh white cheese are added just before serving. It is mild, creamy and warming, eaten with arepas made from wheat or corn.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 l chicken or vegetable broth
  • 400 g potatoes, diced
  • 3 scallions, 2 garlic cloves
  • 20 g cilantro
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 ml milk
  • 100 g fresh white cheese, cubed, salt

Method

  1. Gently fry the chopped scallion and garlic in a pot with a little oil.
  2. Add the broth and potatoes and simmer 15 minutes until the potatoes are soft.
  3. Stir in the chopped cilantro and season.
  4. Crack the eggs into the simmering broth one by one and poach for 3–4 minutes.
  5. Add the milk, heat without boiling, and serve each bowl with an egg and a few cubes of cheese.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skimmed milk and a light cheese.
  • Add spinach or chard for extra vegetables.
  • Make it with water and herbs instead of broth to cut sodium.

History

Pisca is a traditional breakfast of the Venezuelan Andes, where mornings are cool and a hot soup is welcome. It is still served in homes, roadside inns and markets throughout the region.
