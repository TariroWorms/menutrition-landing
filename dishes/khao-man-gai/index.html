🍗 Khao Man Gai

Khao man gai is Thai poached chicken served over rice cooked in the chicken broth with garlic and ginger. Slices of tender chicken come with cucumber and a sharp dipping sauce of soy sauce, chili, ginger and garlic, usually alongside a small bowl of clear broth. The rice is fragrant and glossy, the chicken silky.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (about 1.4 kg)
  • 400 g jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 40 g ginger, sliced
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chili sauce
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken with half the ginger and 2 garlic cloves in water to cover for about 40 minutes, then cool it briefly.
  2. Fry 2 chopped garlic cloves and some ginger in the oil, add the rice and stir to coat.
  3. Cook the rice in about 600 ml of the chicken broth until tender.
  4. Mix the soy sauce, chili sauce, sugar and remaining minced ginger and garlic into a dipping sauce.
  5. Slice the chicken and serve over the rice with cucumber, the sauce and a bowl of hot broth.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin before slicing to cut fat noticeably.
  • Skim the fat off the broth before cooking the rice.
  • Ask for half rice and extra cucumber at street stalls.

History

Khao man gai is the Thai version of Hainanese chicken rice, brought to Southeast Asia by migrants from Hainan in southern China. It is now one of the most common street-food lunches in Bangkok.
