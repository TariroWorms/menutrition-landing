🍲 Testi Kebab (Pottery Kebab)

Origin: Turkey · Cappadocia

Testi kebab is a Turkish stew of beef and vegetables such as onion, pepper, tomato and garlic, slow-cooked with a little butter inside a sealed clay jug. At the table the jug is cracked open with a knife or hammer and the fork-tender meat and rich juices are poured out. It is served with rice pilaf.

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g beef chuck, cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 green peppers, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt, black pepper, red pepper flakes

Method

  1. Mix the beef with the vegetables, tomato paste, salt and spices.
  2. Pack into a clay jug or a covered casserole and add the butter and 100 ml water.
  3. Seal the opening with a dough stopper or foil.
  4. Cook at 180 °C for 2½–3 hours until the meat is very tender.
  5. Break or open the jug at the table and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the beef before cooking.
  • Halve the butter — the meat and vegetables give plenty of juices on their own.
  • Serve with a salad and a small portion of rice.

History

Testi kebab is strongly associated with Cappadocia in central Anatolia, where pottery from towns like Avanos is used to cook it. Much of its popularity today comes from restaurants serving tourists in the region, where opening the jug is part of the meal.
