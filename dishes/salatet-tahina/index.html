🥣 Tahini salad (salatet tahina)

Origin: Egypt

Salatet tahina is the Egyptian tahini sauce served with grills: sesame paste whisked with lemon juice, garlic, cumin and a spoon of yogurt until smooth and pourable, then finished with chopped parsley. It is nutty, tangy and garlicky with a thin, creamy texture. It is eaten with bread, kofta, grilled chicken and falafel.

Recipe

4 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g tahini
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp plain yogurt
  • 60–80 ml cold water
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley, salt

Method

  1. Whisk the tahini with lemon juice, garlic and cumin; it will thicken at first.
  2. Add the water a little at a time, whisking, until smooth and pourable.
  3. Stir in the yogurt and salt, then the parsley, and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Tahini is dense in calories, so serve 2–3 tbsp as a dip rather than a full bowl.
  • Thin it with more water and lemon so the same amount covers more food.
  • Use it instead of a creamier dressing on salads and grilled vegetables.

History

Tahini-based sauces have long been part of Egyptian and Levantine cooking; in Egypt salatet tahina is a standard side served with grills and street food.
