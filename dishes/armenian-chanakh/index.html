Chanakh (lamb baked in clay pots)

Origin: Armenia
544kcal

Chanakh is named after the earthenware pot it is cooked in. Chunks of lamb go in first, followed by eggplant, potato, tomato, pepper and onion, and the pots bake slowly until everything is soft and steeped in rich juices. Each person gets their own pot, eaten with bread and herbs.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless lamb, in chunks
  • 2 eggplants, in thick slices
  • 3 potatoes, in chunks
  • 3 tomatoes, quartered
  • 2 sweet peppers, in strips
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • Basil, cilantro, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Salt the eggplant slices for 15 minutes, then rinse and pat dry.
  2. Divide the lamb between 4 clay pots and season.
  3. Add onion, potato, eggplant, pepper and tomato, seasoning as you go.
  4. Pour a few spoons of water into each pot and add herb sprigs.
  5. Cover and bake at 170 °C for about 2 hours.
  6. Serve in the pots, sprinkled with fresh herbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean leg of lamb and trim the fat.
  • Fill the pots with extra vegetables and less meat.
  • Skip the bread on the side; the potatoes already provide starch.

History

Cooking in clay pots is an old tradition in Armenia and the wider Caucasus, and chanakh is one of the best-known pot dishes. Similar recipes exist in Georgian and Azerbaijani kitchens.
