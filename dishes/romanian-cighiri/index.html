Cighiri (Transylvanian liver rolls)

Origin: Romania · Transylvania
330kcal

Cighiri are small parcels of chopped pork liver, heart and onion, spiced with pepper and garlic and wrapped in lacy caul fat. Roasted in the oven, the fat melts and bastes the filling, leaving a crisp thin shell around a soft, savoury centre. They are eaten hot or cold with bread, mustard and pickles.

What it is made of

pork livercaul fatoniongarlicblack pepper

Recipe

6 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork liver
  • 200 g pork heart or lean meat
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 200 g caul fat
  • salt, pepper, marjoram

Method

  1. Soak the caul fat in warm water and spread it out flat.
  2. Mince the liver and heart coarsely and mix with onion, garlic, salt, pepper and marjoram.
  3. Cut the caul fat into squares and put a spoon of filling on each.
  4. Wrap into tight parcels and place seam down in a baking dish.
  5. Roast at 190°C for 35 to 40 minutes until browned and cooked through.

How to make it lighter

  • Eat small portions; offal is rich in iron and vitamin A but also in cholesterol.
  • Trim the caul fat generously or use less of it.
  • Serve with raw cabbage salad and mustard instead of bread.

History

Using every part of the pig is a long tradition in Romanian villages, and liver dishes were among the first made at the winter slaughter. Cighiri are especially linked with Transylvania and the Banat.
