🧀 Paneer Tikka (Tandoori Paneer)

Origin: India · Punjab

Tandoori paneer is cubes of fresh Indian cheese marinated in spiced yogurt with ginger and garlic, then grilled in a tandoor until the edges are charred. Paneer does not melt, so it keeps its shape and turns smoky outside and soft and milky inside. It is a popular vegetarian starter, served on skewers with lemon and green chutney.

Recipe

3 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g paneer, cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 150 g thick plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp tandoori spice mix
  • 15 g ginger, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 green pepper and 1 onion, cut into chunks
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  1. Mix yogurt, tandoori spices, ginger, garlic and salt into a thick marinade.
  2. Toss the paneer, pepper and onion in it and leave for 30 minutes to 2 hours.
  3. Thread onto skewers, alternating paneer and vegetables.
  4. Grill under a very hot grill or on a hot pan for 8–10 minutes, turning, until charred at the edges.
  5. Serve hot with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Paneer is rich, so keep to about 100 g of cheese per person and add more vegetables to the skewers.
  • Brush very lightly with oil: 1 tsp oil ≈ 40 kcal.
  • Serve with salad rather than naan or rice for a lighter meal.

History

Tandoori paneer developed as a vegetarian counterpart to tandoori meats in North Indian restaurants, applying the same yogurt marinade and tandoor roasting to paneer. It is now one of the most popular vegetarian starters across India.
