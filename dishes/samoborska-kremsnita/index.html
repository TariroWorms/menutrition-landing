Samobor cream slice (Samoborska kremšnita)

Origin: Croatia · Samobor

This is a tall slice of two sheets of crisp puff pastry sandwiching a thick vanilla custard topped with whipped cream. It is light, creamy and not too sweet. It is traditionally eaten with a fork and a coffee.

What it is made of

puff pastrycustardmilkeggsugarcream

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 2 sheets puff pastry
  • 500 ml milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 80 g sugar
  • 40 g cornstarch
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 200 ml cream
  • icing sugar

Method

  1. Bake the puff pastry sheets until golden and cool.
  2. Whisk yolks, sugar, starch and a little milk.
  3. Heat the rest of the milk and stir in the mixture.
  4. Cook until thick, then cool.
  5. Fold in whipped cream and spread on one sheet.
  6. Top with the second sheet, chill and dust with sugar.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar in the custard.
  • Cut small portions.
  • Use low-fat milk in the cream.

History

It is the signature dessert of the town of Samobor near Zagreb, where it is sold in cafés and pastry shops. Locals take pride in the airy custard and the thin layers of pastry.
