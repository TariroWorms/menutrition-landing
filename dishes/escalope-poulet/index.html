🍗 Chicken escalope

Escalope de poulet is a thin, flattened chicken breast fillet pan-fried quickly in butter until golden on the outside and tender inside. It is simple, mild and juicy, and takes on the nutty flavour of browned butter. In France it is an everyday dish, served with vegetables, rice or a green salad and a squeeze of lemon.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts, about 180 g each
  • 15 g butter
  • Salt, black pepper
  • ½ lemon
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley

Method

  1. Cut each breast horizontally or pound it to an even 1 cm thickness.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Melt the butter in a pan over medium-high heat until foaming.
  4. Cook the escalopes 3 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve with lemon juice and parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and only 1 tsp butter per escalope: 5 g butter ≈ 37 kcal.
  • Pound the meat thin so it cooks quickly and needs less fat.
  • Serve with steamed vegetables and a lemon squeeze instead of a cream sauce.

History

An escalope is a thin boneless slice of meat, a technique long used in French cooking. Chicken escalopes are a staple of French home cooking and school and canteen menus today.
