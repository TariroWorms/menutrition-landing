🧀 Chicken parmigiana

Origin: United States · Italian-American cuisine

Chicken parmigiana is a breaded chicken cutlet baked under tomato sauce, melted mozzarella and grated parmesan. The crumb coating stays crisp at the edges while the cheese bubbles and browns on top, making a rich, comforting main course. It is served hot, usually with a side of salad or vegetables.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 4 small chicken breasts (about 600 g), flattened
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 100 g breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 400 ml tomato passata with 1 garlic clove and basil
  • 150 g mozzarella, sliced
  • 40 g parmesan, grated
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season the chicken, dip in egg and coat in breadcrumbs.
  2. Fry in the oil 3 minutes per side until golden; place in a baking dish.
  3. Simmer the passata with garlic and basil for 10 minutes and spoon over the chicken.
  4. Top with mozzarella and parmesan.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 15–20 minutes until the cheese bubbles.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the breaded cutlets on a rack instead of frying them to save most of the oil.
  • Use half the mozzarella, or a light mozzarella; the parmesan already gives plenty of flavour.
  • Serve with a green salad rather than a side of pasta.

History

The dish adapts the southern Italian melanzane alla parmigiana, layered aubergine with tomato and cheese; Italian immigrants in the United States began making it with breaded veal and chicken. Chicken parm is now a fixture of Italian-American restaurants and is also popular in Australia.
