Khoresh aloo esfenaj (spinach and prune stew)

Origin: Iran

Khoresh aloo esfenaj combines lamb, wilted spinach and dried plums in a sweet-and-sour sauce. The plums melt into the spinach, giving a tangy, slightly sweet flavor. It is eaten with white rice.

What it is made of

lambspinachonionturmericlemon

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g lamb
  • 500 g spinach
  • 10 dried plums
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Brown onion and lamb with turmeric, add water and simmer 1 hour.
  2. Fry the chopped spinach briefly in oil.
  3. Add spinach and plums to the meat.
  4. Simmer 30 minutes.
  5. Adjust with lemon juice and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb.
  • Add less sugar-rich fruit if you watch carbs.
  • Serve with a smaller portion of rice.

History

It is a traditional Persian home dish, part of the sweet-sour khoresh tradition that pairs meat with fruit. It is especially common in cooler months.
