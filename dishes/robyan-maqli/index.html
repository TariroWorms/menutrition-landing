🍤 Robyan Maqli (Fried Shrimp)

Origin: Kuwait · Bahrain · Gulf

Robyan maqli is Gulf-style fried shrimp: plump shrimp marinated with garlic, turmeric and chili, dusted in seasoned flour and fried until crisp and golden. The coating is light and crunchy, the shrimp juicy, with a warm, gently spicy kick. It is served hot with lemon wedges, often as a starter or alongside rice.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 80 g flour
  • Oil for frying
  • Salt
  • Lemon wedges

Method

  1. Toss the shrimp with garlic, turmeric, chili and salt; leave 15 minutes.
  2. Coat the shrimp lightly in the flour and shake off the excess.
  3. Fry in hot oil at about 180 °C for 2–3 minutes until golden.
  4. Drain on paper and serve hot with lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Shake off excess flour — a thinner coat absorbs less oil.
  • Pan-fry in a little oil or use an air fryer to cut calories noticeably.
  • Pair with salad instead of fries to keep the meal moderate.

History

Shrimp has long been fished in the waters of the Gulf, and fried robyan is a favourite in seafood restaurants in Kuwait and Bahrain today.
