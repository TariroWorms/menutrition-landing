🥣 Overnight Bircher muesli

Origin: Switzerland · Zurich

Bircher muesli is rolled oats soaked overnight with yogurt and grated apple until soft and creamy, then finished with chopped nuts, a little honey and fresh berries. It is cool, thick and spoonable, with a mild tang from the yogurt and natural sweetness from the fruit. It is eaten cold for breakfast, straight from a bowl or a jar.

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g rolled oats
  • 200 g plain yogurt
  • 100 ml water
  • 1 large apple, coarsely grated with the skin
  • 2 tsp honey
  • 30 g hazelnuts or walnuts, chopped
  • 100 g mixed berries

Method

  1. Mix the oats, yogurt and water in a bowl or two jars.
  2. Stir in the grated apple, cover and refrigerate overnight, or for at least 4 hours.
  3. In the morning stir, loosen with a splash of water if it is too thick.
  4. Top with the berries and nuts, drizzle with honey and serve cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Nuts are the most calorie-dense part: a small handful (about 15 g per bowl) is plenty for crunch.
  • The apple and berries add enough sweetness that the honey can be halved or skipped.
  • Use low-fat plain yogurt rather than a sweetened flavoured one.

History

Bircher muesli was created around 1900 by the Swiss physician Maximilian Bircher-Benner, who served it to patients at his Zurich sanatorium. The story goes that he was inspired by a dish of raw apple and oats he was offered on a hike in the Swiss Alps. It became a Swiss breakfast staple and is now eaten worldwide.
