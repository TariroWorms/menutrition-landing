Kyselica (sauerkraut soup)

Origin: Slovakia

Kyselica is a sour, hearty Slovak soup made from sauerkraut and its brine, potatoes and slices of smoked sausage. A spoon of sour cream softens the tang. It warms well in winter and is often eaten with bread.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g sauerkraut
  • 200 ml sauerkraut brine
  • 3 potatoes
  • 150 g smoked sausage
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp caraway
  • 4 tbsp sour cream

Method

  1. Fry the sliced sausage and chopped onion in a pot for 5 minutes.
  2. Add sauerkraut, caraway and 800 ml of water.
  3. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Add diced potatoes and the brine and cook until soft.
  5. Serve with a spoon of sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Rinse the sauerkraut if you want less salt.
  • Use less sausage and more potatoes.
  • Choose low-fat sour cream.

History

Soups based on fermented cabbage are common across Central Europe, and kyselica is a rural Slovak version for cold months when fresh vegetables were scarce.
