Gallo pinto nicaragüense (Nicaraguan rice and beans)

Origin: Nicaragua

Nicaraguan gallo pinto is cooked rice and red beans fried together with onion and garlic, producing a speckled, savoury mix. It is typically made with leftover rice and beans and served at breakfast with eggs, fried cheese and plantain. It is also a daily side dish at lunch and dinner.

What it is made of

ricered beanonionoilgarlic

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked rice
  • 250 g cooked red beans
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 100 ml bean cooking liquid
  • Salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan.
  2. Fry onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add beans and a little cooking liquid, mash lightly.
  4. Add rice and mix well.
  5. Fry 8-10 minutes, stirring, until dry and fragrant.
  6. Season and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use brown rice for more fibre.
  • Cut the oil to 1 tbsp.
  • Pair with eggs and vegetables.

History

Gallo pinto is considered Nicaragua's national dish and is eaten most days across the country. Its name means spotted rooster, referring to the speckled look of rice and beans.
