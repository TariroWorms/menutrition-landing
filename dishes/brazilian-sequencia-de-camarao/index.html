Sequência de camarão (shrimp tasting feast)

Origin: Brazil · Santa Catarina

Sequência de camarão is less a single recipe than a meal: a run of shrimp dishes brought to the table one after another. Typically there are plain fried shrimp with garlic, breaded shrimp, shrimp in tomato sauce and a creamy shrimp stew, plus rice, pirão and salad. It is a shared, unhurried lunch eaten by the sea.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg shrimp, peeled
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 eggs
  • 100 g flour
  • 100 g breadcrumbs
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • Oil for frying, rice to serve

Method

  1. Season the shrimp with salt, lime and garlic and split them into four portions.
  2. Fry the first portion quickly in hot oil with garlic.
  3. Dip the second portion in flour, egg and breadcrumbs and deep-fry until golden.
  4. Simmer the third portion briefly in a tomato and onion sauce.
  5. Cook the last portion in coconut milk with tomato for a creamy stew; serve all courses in turn with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Grill or pan-sear more of the shrimp instead of deep-frying.
  • Fill up on salad and the tomato-based courses.
  • Keep the rice portion small since there is plenty of food.

History

The format is famous on Santa Catarina's coast, around Florianópolis and nearby beach towns, where shrimp fishing is an important local activity. Seafood restaurants there built their reputation on serving it all-you-can-eat style.
