Mbongo tchobi (Cameroonian black spice sauce)

Origin: Cameroon · Bassa

Mbongo tchobi is a dark, almost black sauce made from charred mbongo bark and spices, simmered with fish or meat. It is smoky, peppery and aromatic. It is served with plantains, bobolo or rice.

What it is made of

fishmbongo spicegingeronionred palm oil

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g catfish or tilapia
  • 2 tbsp mbongo spice mix
  • 30 g ginger
  • 1 onion
  • 50 ml palm oil
  • Salt, 400 ml water

Method

  1. Toast and grind the mbongo with ginger and onion.
  2. Fry the paste briefly in oil.
  3. Add water and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Add the fish pieces.
  5. Cook gently 15 minutes and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil.
  • Serve with plantain and greens.
  • Choose a lean white fish.

History

The dish belongs to the Bassa people of the Littoral and Centre regions and is one of Cameroon's best known specialties. Burning the spices is what gives the color.
