Bulalo (Filipino beef marrow soup)

Origin: Philippines · Batangas

Bulalo is a clear, deeply beefy soup made by simmering shank and marrow bones for hours. Sweet corn, cabbage and bok choy go in at the end. The marrow is scooped out and spread on rice. It is a favourite on cool days, especially in the highlands of Batangas.

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shank with bone
  • 2 marrow bones
  • 2 corn cobs, halved
  • ¼ small cabbage, wedges
  • 1 onion; 1 tbsp peppercorns
  • fish sauce and salt to taste

Method

  1. Blanch shank and bones in boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and rinse.
  2. Return to a clean pot with 2.5 litres of water, onion and peppercorns.
  3. Simmer gently 2.5 to 3 hours until the meat is tender.
  4. Add corn and cook 15 minutes.
  5. Add cabbage, season with fish sauce and cook 5 minutes more.

How to make it lighter

  • Chill the broth and lift off the solid fat before reheating.
  • Use plenty of vegetables to balance the richness.
  • Limit the marrow portion if watching saturated fat.

History

The soup is strongly associated with Batangas province, particularly the cool town of Tagaytay nearby, where roadside restaurants serve it. The name refers to the marrow bone itself. It reflects the Filipino habit of using every part of the animal.
