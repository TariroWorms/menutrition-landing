🍗 Ruz Bukhari with Chicken

Ruz Bukhari is a Saudi rice dish of long-grain rice cooked in a spiced chicken broth tinted with tomato paste, then topped with sweet carrot strips and plump raisins. Cumin and cardamom give it a warm aroma, and the chicken is roasted or browned until golden. The result is fluffy and gently sweet-savoury, served on a large shared platter.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g rice
  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 2 carrots, cut into thin strips
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 60 g raisins
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 4 cardamom pods
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 l water, salt

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in the water with salt, cumin and cardamom for 30 minutes; keep the broth.
  2. Brown the chicken in a hot oven or pan until golden.
  3. In a pot, fry the tomato paste in 1 tbsp oil, add 800 ml of the broth and bring to a boil.
  4. Add the rinsed rice, cover and cook on low heat for 20 minutes until fluffy.
  5. Sauté the carrots and raisins in the remaining oil until soft.
  6. Serve the rice topped with carrots, raisins and chicken.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin before serving to cut fat.
  • Use fewer raisins or skip the sautéing and steam the carrots instead.
  • Keep the rice to one ladle and take an extra piece of chicken for protein.

History

As the name suggests, the dish is linked to Bukhara in Central Asia and is usually credited to Central Asian communities who settled in the Hejaz, especially around Mecca and Medina. It is related to the plov of Uzbekistan and is now a popular home and restaurant dish across Saudi Arabia.
