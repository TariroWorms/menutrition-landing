Cantonese wonton noodles (Yun tun mian)

Origin: China · Guangdong · Hong Kong
420kcal

Thin, springy egg noodles are served in a clear broth flavoured with dried flounder and shrimp shells, topped with plump shrimp wontons. The broth is light yet deeply savory, and the noodles keep a firm bite. It is a classic quick meal in Hong Kong and Guangzhou.

Recipe

2 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g thin egg noodles
  • 8 shrimp wontons (shrimp, pork, wrapper)
  • 500 ml broth with dried flounder
  • 100 g bok choy or choy sum
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • pinch of white pepper

Method

  1. Bring the broth to a simmer and season with white pepper.
  2. Boil the noodles for 30 seconds, rinse and drain.
  3. Poach the wontons in boiling water for 3 minutes.
  4. Blanch the greens for 1 minute.
  5. Assemble noodles, wontons and greens, ladle over broth and add sesame oil.

How to make it lighter

  • Add more greens and less noodles.
  • Skim the broth and keep it light and low in salt.
  • Skip the sesame oil to cut fat.

History

The dish developed in Guangzhou and spread to Hong Kong, where small noodle shops perfected a compact bowl. It remains an everyday favourite and a cultural symbol of the city.
