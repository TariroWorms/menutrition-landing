Ebi tempura uramaki is an inside-out sushi roll: the rice is on the outside, coated in sesame seeds, and the nori wraps a filling of crispy battered shrimp, creamy avocado and fresh cucumber. Each slice combines crunch, softness and a mild vinegared-rice tang. It is usually cut into eight pieces.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked sushi rice
  • 4 shrimp tempura pieces
  • 1/2 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cucumber, cut into sticks
  • 2 nori sheets
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds

Method

  1. Lay a nori sheet on a bamboo mat covered with cling film.
  2. Spread half the rice evenly over the nori and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
  3. Flip the sheet so the rice faces down.
  4. Arrange shrimp tempura, avocado and cucumber along the near edge.
  5. Roll tightly with the mat, then cut into 8 pieces with a wet knife; repeat with the second sheet.

How to make it lighter

  • Order or make 4–6 pieces and add a cucumber salad instead of a second roll.
  • The tempura batter adds most of the fat; a roll with plain cooked shrimp is lighter.
  • Go easy on dipping sauces and sweet glazes, which add calories quickly.

History

Inside-out (uramaki) rolls developed outside Japan, most famously in North America in the 1960s–70s, and shrimp tempura rolls are now a standard item at sushi bars worldwide.
