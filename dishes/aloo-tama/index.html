Aloo tama (potato and bamboo shoot curry)

Origin: Nepal

Aloo tama is a Nepali sour curry of potatoes, fermented bamboo shoots and black-eyed peas. It is tangy, earthy and savoury, with a thin gravy. It is eaten with rice, especially in Newar and Kathmandu homes.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g potatoes, cubed
  • 100 g fermented bamboo shoots
  • 100 g black-eyed peas, boiled
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 tbsp oil, turmeric, fenugreek seeds
  • Garlic, ginger, chilli, salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil and fry fenugreek seeds, garlic and ginger.
  2. Add the bamboo shoots and tomato.
  3. Stir in the turmeric, potatoes and salt.
  4. Add the peas and water.
  5. Simmer until the potatoes are tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Rinse the bamboo shoots for a milder sourness.
  • Use little oil.
  • Serve with brown rice and greens.

History

Fermented bamboo shoots, called tama, are a signature ingredient in Nepali cooking and are also found in the foods of Himalayan communities. The dish is often made in the monsoon season.
