Arrachera (grilled marinated skirt steak)

Origin: Mexico · Northern Mexico

Arrachera is beef skirt steak, a thin, well-marbled cut, marinated with lime or orange juice, garlic, onion and pepper to tenderise it. It is grilled quickly over very hot charcoal so the outside chars while the inside stays juicy. Sliced across the grain, it is served with grilled spring onions, guacamole, salsa and tortillas, or tucked into tacos.

What it is made of

beef steaklimegarliconionblack pepper

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g skirt steak
  • juice of 3 limes
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 onion (70 g)
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • salt; spring onions and tortillas to serve

Method

  1. Blend the lime juice, garlic, onion and pepper into a marinade.
  2. Coat the steak and leave in the fridge for 1–4 hours.
  3. Heat the grill until very hot and pat the meat dry.
  4. Salt it and grill 3–4 minutes per side.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, slice thinly across the grain and serve with grilled spring onions and tortillas.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat before marinating.
  • Serve over salad with salsa instead of in several tortillas.
  • Grill nopales or vegetables alongside to fill half the plate.

History

Skirt steak was long considered a humble butcher's cut in northern Mexico and Texas. Grilled arrachera became the centrepiece of the carne asada tradition of the north and is now found on menus throughout the country.
