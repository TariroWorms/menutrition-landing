🥧 Pastel de papa

Pastel de papa is a Chilean baked casserole of seasoned minced beef and onion under a thick layer of golden mashed potato. The beef filling, known as pino, is typically flavoured with cumin and often includes slices of hard-boiled egg, olives and raisins. It is a comforting family meal, served hot straight from the baking dish.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 60 ml milk and 1 tbsp butter
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 3 onions, chopped
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
  • 10 black olives
  • 2 tbsp raisins
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Boil the peeled potatoes in salted water until very tender, drain and mash with the milk, butter and a pinch of salt.
  2. Fry the onions in the oil until soft and translucent.
  3. Add the beef, cumin, salt and pepper and cook for about 15 minutes, stirring, to make the pino.
  4. Spread the pino in a baking dish and top with the egg, olives and raisins.
  5. Cover with the mashed potato and smooth the top with a fork.
  6. Bake at 200 °C for 25–30 minutes until the surface is golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Mash the potato with a little milk and less butter; the filling is already rich enough.
  • Choose lean mince and drain off any fat before adding the onion mix to the dish.
  • Pair with a large raw salad, which adds fibre and volume for few calories.

History

This is the potato-topped cousin of the better-known pastel de choclo, and it comes from Chilean home cooking, where potato is a long-standing staple. The same meat filling is used for empanadas, which makes it a practical way to use up a batch of pino. It is a common dish for weekday family lunches.
