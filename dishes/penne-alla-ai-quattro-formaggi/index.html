🧀 Penne ai quattro formaggi (four-cheese penne)

Origin: Italy · Northern Italy

Penne ai quattro formaggi is penne coated in a sauce of four melted cheeses: gorgonzola, fontina, parmesan and mozzarella. The gorgonzola brings a sharp blue-cheese tang, fontina and mozzarella melt into a stretchy, velvety sauce, and parmesan adds a salty finish. It is rich, so it is usually served in modest portions straight from the pan.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 360 g penne
  • 60 g gorgonzola
  • 60 g fontina, diced
  • 60 g mozzarella, diced
  • 40 g parmesan, grated
  • Black pepper, pinch of nutmeg

Method

  1. Cook the penne in salted water until al dente; keep a cup of pasta water.
  2. Melt the gorgonzola and fontina in a wide pan with 100 ml pasta water over low heat, stirring.
  3. Add the drained penne and toss to coat.
  4. Stir in the mozzarella and parmesan until melted and creamy, adding more pasta water if needed.
  5. Season with pepper and nutmeg and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cheese and add more pasta water for a lighter sauce.
  • Choose a reduced-fat mozzarella as one of the four cheeses.
  • Keep the pasta to 80 g dry per person and serve with a green salad.

History

Four-cheese sauces draw on the cheeses of northern Italy, such as gorgonzola from Lombardy and fontina from the Aosta Valley. The combination of cheeses varies from cook to cook, and the dish is common today in Italian homes and trattorias.
