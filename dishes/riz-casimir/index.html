🍛 Riz Casimir

Riz Casimir is a Swiss classic of mildly curried chicken in a creamy sauce, served over rice with pineapple and banana. The fruit adds sweetness to the gentle curry, and toasted almonds give crunch. The result is fruity, creamy and comforting — a retro dish still common in Swiss canteens and restaurants.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g rice
  • 500 g chicken breast, in strips
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 200 ml cream
  • 200 ml water
  • 200 g pineapple, in chunks
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 20 g flaked almonds
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Brown the chicken in the oil, then add the onion and soften.
  3. Stir in the curry powder, then the water and cream; simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Warm the pineapple and banana briefly in a pan.
  5. Serve the chicken over rice, topped with the fruit and toasted almonds.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream and more water for a lighter sauce; 100 ml cream ≈ 300 kcal.
  • Keep the rice to about 150 g cooked per plate and add a side salad.
  • Toast the almonds dry, without oil, and use just a sprinkle.

History

Riz Casimir is usually credited to a Swiss restaurant chain in the early 1950s, when exotic fruit and curry were fashionable. It remains a familiar dish in Swiss homes and staff canteens.
