🥗 Mixed green salad (salade mêlée)

Origin: France · Switzerland

Salade mêlée is a simple French-style mixed salad of tender leaves with sliced tomato, cucumber and grated carrot, tossed in a sharp vinaigrette. It is crisp, fresh and light, and it appears on bistro menus as a starter or as the side that comes with a main course. The vegetables change with the season, but the dressing is always simple.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g mixed salad leaves
  • 2 tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1/2 cucumber, sliced
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp red vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Wash and dry the salad leaves well.
  2. Whisk the oil, vinegar, salt and pepper into a vinaigrette.
  3. Arrange the leaves, tomato, cucumber and carrot in a bowl.
  4. Dress just before serving and toss gently.

How to make it lighter

  • Most of the calories come from the dressing: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal, so measure it instead of pouring.
  • Add more cucumber and leaves for volume at almost no extra calories.
  • Serve the vinaigrette on the side and dip each forkful.

History

Mixed salads of this kind are a standard starter in France and French-speaking Switzerland, where the name simply means “mixed salad”.
