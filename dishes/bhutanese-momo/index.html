Bhutanese momo (steamed dumplings)

Origin: Bhutan

Bhutanese momo are round, steamed wheat dumplings filled with minced beef or pork and cabbage, or with cheese and greens. They are juicy and gently seasoned with ginger, garlic and spring onion. They are dipped in a fiery ezay chilli sauce.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g wheat flour
  • 150 ml water
  • 300 g minced beef
  • 150 g cabbage, finely chopped
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp ginger and garlic
  • 2 spring onions
  • salt, pepper

Method

  1. Knead flour with water into a firm dough and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Mix beef, cabbage, onion, ginger, garlic and spring onion with salt and pepper.
  3. Roll the dough into thin circles.
  4. Fill each and pleat to seal.
  5. Steam for 15 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with chilli sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean mince and more cabbage.
  • Try a cheese and spinach filling for a vegetarian option.
  • Steam instead of frying.

History

Momo came to Bhutan from Tibetan food culture and are now a favourite at festivals and family gatherings. They are often eaten with ezay, a fresh chilli relish.
