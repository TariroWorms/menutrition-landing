🍎 French apple tart

Tarte aux pommes is the classic French apple tart: a buttery pastry base covered with overlapping, paper-thin apple slices arranged in a rosette. It is baked until the edges of the apples turn golden, then brushed with warm apricot jam for a glossy finish. It is lightly sweet and fruity, served warm or cool.

Recipe

8 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour
  • 100 g cold butter, diced, plus 20 g melted
  • 3–4 tbsp cold water and a pinch of salt
  • 4 apples, peeled, cored and thinly sliced
  • 40 g sugar
  • 3 tbsp apricot jam

Method

  1. Rub the flour, salt and cold butter together, add the water and form a dough; chill for 30 minutes.
  2. Roll out and line a 24 cm tin.
  3. Arrange the apple slices in overlapping circles, brush with melted butter and sprinkle with sugar.
  4. Bake at 190 °C for 35–40 minutes until the apples are tender and golden at the edges.
  5. Warm the jam with 1 tbsp water and brush it over the hot tart.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the melted butter on top and rely on the jam glaze.
  • Use sweet apple varieties and cut the sugar in half.
  • Cut 10 slices instead of 8.

History

Apple tarts have been baked in France for centuries, with regional styles in Normandy, Alsace and elsewhere. The thin, glazed rosette version is a staple of French bakeries and home cooking.
