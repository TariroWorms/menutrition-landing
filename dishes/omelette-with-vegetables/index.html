A vegetable omelette is made of beaten eggs cooked in a pan with diced tomato, onion and bell pepper. The eggs set soft and fluffy around the vegetables, which stay juicy and slightly sweet. It is a quick breakfast or light meal, served folded or flat, straight from the pan.

Recipe

1 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 1 small tomato, diced
  • 1/2 onion, finely chopped
  • 1/2 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tsp vegetable oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan and soften the onion and pepper for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the tomato and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper and pour over the vegetables.
  4. Cook on medium-low heat until almost set, then fold and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and just a teaspoon of oil — a tablespoon is about 120 kcal.
  • Add more vegetables and use 2 eggs instead of 3.
  • Serve with a salad rather than buttered toast.

History

Omelettes in many forms are cooked around the world, and this simple vegetable version is a common home breakfast in many countries.
