Salada de palmito (hearts of palm salad)

Origin: Brazil

Salada de palmito combines rounds of tender heart of palm with tomato, lettuce and thin onion, dressed simply with olive oil, lime or vinegar and salt. Hearts of palm have a delicate, slightly nutty taste and a texture between asparagus and artichoke. The salad is cool and light, a common starter at churrascarias and home lunches. It also often includes olives or quail eggs.

Recipe

4 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g hearts of palm (jarred), drained
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 small head of lettuce
  • 1/2 red onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Salt, pepper, a few olives

Method

  1. Slice the hearts of palm into 1 cm rounds.
  2. Cut the tomatoes into wedges and the onion into thin rings.
  3. Tear the lettuce and arrange it on a platter.
  4. Top with hearts of palm, tomato, onion and olives.
  5. Whisk oil, lime juice, salt and pepper and pour over just before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Rinse jarred hearts of palm to reduce the salt.
  • Add grilled chicken or chickpeas to turn it into a main course.
  • Use lime juice generously and a little less oil.

History

Heart of palm is harvested from the inner core of certain palm trees, and Brazil is one of its main producers and consumers. Today much of it comes from cultivated species such as pupunha, which regrow after harvesting.
