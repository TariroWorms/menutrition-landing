Mondongo (tripe stew)

Origin: Uruguay

Mondongo is tripe that has been cleaned, boiled until tender and then stewed in a tomato sauce with onion, peppers, carrot, potato and chickpeas. The tripe becomes soft with a gentle chew, and the sauce is rich and lightly spiced with paprika. It is eaten as a main course with bread on cold days.

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg cleaned beef tripe
  • 400 g potatoes
  • 2 carrots (200 g)
  • 1 onion, 1 red bell pepper
  • 400 g crushed tomato
  • 200 g cooked chickpeas
  • 10 g paprika, 2 bay leaves, 2 garlic cloves
  • 15 ml oil, 500 ml broth

Method

  1. Rinse the tripe well, cover with water, bring to a boil and discard the water.
  2. Simmer the tripe in fresh water with bay leaves for about 2 hours until tender, then cut into strips.
  3. Cook the onion, pepper, garlic and carrot in the oil until soft.
  4. Add paprika, tomato and broth, then the tripe, and simmer 20 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes and chickpeas and cook 20 minutes more until the sauce thickens.
  6. Season and serve hot with bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Tripe is naturally lean and high in protein, so keep the added oil low.
  • Skip the chorizo that some cooks add.
  • Use more chickpeas and fewer potatoes for extra fiber.

History

Tripe stews came to the Río de la Plata with Spanish and Italian settlers, and the region's abundant cattle made offal cheap and plentiful. Mondongo remains a familiar home dish in Uruguay and Argentina.
