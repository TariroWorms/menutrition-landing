Buko pandan (young coconut pandan dessert)

Origin: Philippines

Buko pandan is a chilled dessert of tender young coconut strips and green pandan gelatin cubes in sweet cream. It is cool, fragrant and lightly sweet, with a soft chew. It is served at parties and in warm weather.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g young coconut strips
  • 1 pandan gelatin block, cubed
  • 250 ml cream
  • 200 ml milk
  • 60 g sugar
  • Pandan extract

Method

  1. Make pandan jelly and cut into cubes.
  2. Mix cream, milk, sugar and pandan extract.
  3. Add coconut strips and jelly.
  4. Chill for 2 hours.
  5. Serve cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light cream.
  • Reduce sugar.
  • Serve in small cups.

History

Buko, young coconut, and pandan leaves are staples of Filipino sweets. Buko pandan salad is a modern festive dessert, often served at gatherings and fiestas.
