Empanada de jamón y queso (ham and cheese empanada)

Origin: Argentina

The ham and cheese empanada is one of the most ordered flavors in Argentine pizzerias and empanada shops. Diced cooked ham and mozzarella, sometimes with a pinch of oregano, melt into a soft, stringy filling inside a golden crust. Children especially love it, and it is a staple of any mixed dozen ordered for a gathering.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 12 empanada discs (about 400 g)
  • 200 g cooked ham, diced
  • 250 g mozzarella, diced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 egg, beaten, for brushing

Method

  1. Mix ham, mozzarella and oregano.
  2. Place a spoonful in the center of each disc.
  3. Fold and press the edges firmly so cheese cannot escape.
  4. Crimp the border with a fork or a repulgue.
  5. Brush with beaten egg.
  6. Bake at 220 °C for 15 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a light mozzarella and lean ham.
  • Add spinach or tomato to the filling for more volume.
  • Eat one or two with a green salad rather than a whole dozen.

History

It is a modern city favorite rather than a regional classic, popularized by the empanada delivery shops of Buenos Aires. It sits alongside beef, chicken and humita as a standard menu option.
