🥬 Palak Paneer

Palak paneer is a North Indian curry of soft paneer cheese cubes in a smooth, bright-green spinach sauce. The spinach is blanched and puréed, then cooked with onion, garlic and garam masala and finished with a little cream for richness. The sauce is mild, velvety and aromatic, and it is served with rice, naan or roti.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer, cut into cubes
  • 400 g spinach
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, chopped
  • 1 green chilli (optional)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 60 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Blanch the spinach in boiling water for 1–2 minutes, cool in cold water and blend with the chilli into a smooth purée.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the onion for 6–8 minutes until soft and golden; add the garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Stir in the garam masala and salt, then add the spinach purée and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the paneer cubes and cook gently for 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream, heat through and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Paneer is rich: use 150–200 g for four servings and add more spinach.
  • Use less cream or swirl in just a spoonful at the end.
  • Serve with a measured portion of rice or one roti instead of buttery naan.

History

Palak paneer is closely associated with Punjabi home and restaurant cooking and became one of the best-known vegetarian dishes on Indian restaurant menus worldwide.
