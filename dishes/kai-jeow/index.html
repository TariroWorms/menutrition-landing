🍳 Kai jeow (Thai omelette)

Kai jeow is the Thai omelette: eggs beaten with fish sauce and a squeeze of lime, then poured into very hot oil so they puff up into a fluffy, golden cake with crisp, lacy edges. It is served on top of steamed jasmine rice, usually with a little chilli sauce on the side. Quick, cheap and satisfying, it is everyday comfort food in Thailand.

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • 1–2 fresh chillies, sliced
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 300 g cooked jasmine rice

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with the fish sauce and lime juice until frothy.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok until it is very hot and just smoking.
  3. Pour the eggs in from a little height so they puff up; cook for 1 minute, flip and cook 30 seconds more.
  4. Drain briefly and serve over rice with sliced chillies.

How to make it lighter

  • Cook in 1 tbsp oil in a non-stick pan — less puffy, but 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal saved for each one left out.
  • Blot the omelette on paper before serving.
  • Serve with a smaller portion of rice and a side of sliced cucumber.

History

Kai jeow is a staple of Thai home cooking and street food stalls, eaten at any time of day.
