A shish tawook platter is the full restaurant plate: grilled skewers of chicken marinated in yogurt, garlic and lemon, served with a pile of fries, a pot of toum garlic sauce and warm pita. Pickles and a little salad often come alongside. The chicken is juicy and garlicky, and the platter is large enough to be a full, hearty meal.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast, in cubes
  • 120 g plain yogurt
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 500 g potatoes, cut into fries
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 100 g garlic sauce (toum)
  • 4 pita breads

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with yogurt, garlic, lemon and salt for at least 2 hours.
  2. Toss the potatoes with the oil and salt and bake at 220 °C for 30 minutes, turning once.
  3. Thread the chicken on skewers and grill 10–12 minutes until cooked through.
  4. Plate the skewers with the fries, garlic sauce and warm pita.

How to make it lighter

  • Oven-bake the fries instead of deep-frying them.
  • Swap the fries for salad or grilled vegetables.
  • Keep the garlic sauce to one tablespoon; it is very rich in oil.

History

Shish tawook is the Levantine and Turkish skewered chicken; the name joins şiş (skewer) with tavuk, Turkish for chicken. It became a staple of Lebanese grills and is now sold across the Middle East and the Gulf.
