Zuppa Toscana is a creamy soup of crumbled sausage, sliced potatoes, kale and garlic, finished with cream. The sausage seasons the broth with fennel and pepper, the potatoes turn soft and the kale adds a fresh, slightly bitter note. It is served hot as a hearty starter or a light meal.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g sausage, casings removed
  • 400 g potatoes, thinly sliced
  • 100 g kale, shredded
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 l water
  • 100 ml cream
  • Salt, pepper, chili flakes

Method

  1. Brown the crumbled sausage in a pot, then remove and drain off most of the fat.
  2. Sauté the onion and garlic in the same pot for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the water and potatoes and simmer 15 minutes until tender.
  4. Return the sausage, add the kale and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream, season and heat through without boiling.

How to make it lighter

  • Drain the fat after browning the sausage and use a little less sausage.
  • Halve the cream and add more kale for a lighter bowl.
  • Choose a cup-sized portion as a starter rather than a large bowl.

History

Despite its Italian name, this creamy version is an Italian-American creation popularized by American restaurants, loosely inspired by rustic Tuscan soups. It is now a common home recipe across the United States.
