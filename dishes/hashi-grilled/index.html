🍖 Grilled camel (hashi)

Grilled hashi is tender meat from a young camel, marinated with garlic, cumin, black pepper, lemon and olive oil and grilled over coals. The meat is lean and fine-grained with a mild, slightly sweet flavour, somewhere between beef and lamb. It is served in chunks or skewers with rice or flatbread and a fresh salad.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g young camel meat (hashi), cut into 4 cm cubes
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1½ tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Mix the garlic, cumin, pepper, lemon juice, olive oil and salt.
  2. Toss the meat in the marinade and leave for at least 2 hours, or overnight in the fridge.
  3. Thread onto skewers and grill over hot coals for 8–12 minutes, turning, until browned outside and juicy inside.
  4. Rest 5 minutes and serve with lemon and salad.

How to make it lighter

  • Camel meat is naturally lean — keep it that way by using just 1 tbsp of oil in the marinade.
  • Serve with a large salad and a small portion of rice or one flatbread.
  • Grill rather than pan-fry, and do not baste with extra oil.

History

Camel meat has been eaten for centuries by the Bedouin of the Arabian Peninsula, and hashi — a young camel — is valued for its tenderness. Today grilled camel is found in specialist restaurants in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the wider Gulf, especially for gatherings.
