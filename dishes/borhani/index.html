Borhani (Bangladeshi spiced yogurt drink)

Origin: Bangladesh

Borhani is a thin, salty-sour yogurt drink blended with fresh mint, green chili, ground mustard and roasted cumin. It is cooling, sharp and a little hot. It is served alongside rich biryani and meat dishes to cut the fat.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g plain yogurt
  • 300 ml cold water
  • 1 tsp ground mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp roasted cumin
  • 1 green chili
  • small bunch fresh mint
  • 1 tsp black salt

Method

  1. Grind mint, chili, mustard and cumin to a paste.
  2. Whisk yogurt with cold water until smooth.
  3. Stir in the paste and salt.
  4. Strain if you prefer it smooth.
  5. Chill and serve in small glasses.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt.
  • Go easy on salt.
  • Serve small glasses beside heavy dishes.

History

Borhani is closely linked with festive biryani meals in Bangladesh, especially in Dhaka. It acts as both a digestive and a palate cleanser.
