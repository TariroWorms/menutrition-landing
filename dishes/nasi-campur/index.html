Nasi campur (Indonesian mixed rice plate)

Origin: Indonesia

Nasi campur is a plate of steamed rice served with small portions of different dishes. A typical plate has shredded chicken, a fried or boiled egg, tempeh, vegetables, peanuts and a spoon of sambal. Every region and warung makes its own mix, so no two plates are quite alike. It is an everyday lunch for many people across the country.

Recipe

2 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g steamed rice
  • 100 g shredded chicken
  • 1 egg, fried
  • 60 g tempeh, fried
  • 80 g boiled vegetables
  • 1 tbsp sambal, peanuts

Method

  1. Cook the rice.
  2. Season and fry the tempeh and egg.
  3. Cook and shred the chicken, then season it.
  4. Blanch and dress the vegetables.
  5. Plate rice with all items and sambal.

How to make it lighter

  • Pick more vegetables and grilled protein.
  • Reduce rice and fried items.
  • Go easy on sambal and oil.

History

The name means mixed rice. The dish reflects the Indonesian habit of sharing many small dishes with rice, and it is found from Java to Bali.
