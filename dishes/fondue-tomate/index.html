🍅 Tomato Fondue

Tomato fondue, or fondue à la tomate, is a cheese fondue melted with tomatoes, white wine and garlic. The tomato turns the sauce pinkish and adds a fresh, tangy edge to the creamy cheese. In the Valais and around Lake Geneva it is often poured over or eaten with boiled potatoes rather than bread.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g mixed Swiss cheese (e.g. Gruyère and Emmental), grated
  • 300 ml dry white wine
  • 1 garlic clove, halved
  • 400 g ripe tomatoes, peeled and chopped
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • Black pepper and nutmeg to taste
  • Boiled small potatoes and raw vegetables for dipping

Method

  1. Rub the inside of a fondue pot with the cut garlic, then leave the garlic in the pot.
  2. Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes until they break down into a thick sauce.
  3. Add the wine and warm over medium heat until steaming, without boiling.
  4. Toss the cheese with the cornstarch and add it by handfuls, stirring in a figure-eight until each addition melts.
  5. Season with pepper and nutmeg, set the pot over a burner at the table and dip potatoes and vegetables on long forks.

How to make it lighter

  • Dip mostly vegetables such as broccoli, peppers, cherry tomatoes and mushrooms instead of loading up on potatoes.
  • Fondue is dense: decide on your share before you start — about 150–200 g of the melted cheese is a realistic single serving.
  • Serve it with a large green salad dressed with vinegar and a little oil so the cheese is not the whole meal.

History

Fondue with tomato is a regional variation of Swiss cheese fondue, eaten today mainly in French-speaking Switzerland, especially the Valais.
