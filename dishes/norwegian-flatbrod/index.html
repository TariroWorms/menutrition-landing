Flatbrød (Norwegian crisp flatbread)

Origin: Norway

Flatbrød is a very thin, crisp flatbread made from barley or whole-grain flour, water and salt, without yeast. It is rolled out, baked until dry and breaks into large crackers. It tastes toasty and slightly nutty and keeps for months. It is eaten with butter, cheese, brown cheese or soup.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g barley flour
  • 150 g whole-wheat flour
  • 2.5 dl water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Mix flours, salt, oil and water into a stiff dough.
  2. Rest for 15 minutes.
  3. Roll the dough out as thinly as possible.
  4. Bake on a hot griddle or at 200 C until crisp and lightly browned.
  5. Cool on a rack and break into pieces.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose whole-grain versions for more fibre.
  • Use as a low-fat cracker with cottage cheese.
  • Keep in an airtight tin so it stays crisp.

History

Flatbrød is one of Norway's oldest breads and was baked in large batches two or three times a year so it would last through winter. Today it is sold in shops, and traditional griddle baking is still a rural tradition.
