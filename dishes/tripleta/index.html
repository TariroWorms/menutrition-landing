Tripleta (Puerto Rican three-meat sandwich)

Origin: Puerto Rico

Tripleta is a big sandwich on a crusty roll stacked with three meats, usually pork, steak and ham, topped with lettuce, tomato, crispy potato sticks and a pink sauce. It is juicy, salty and crunchy at once. It is a typical late-night and kiosk sandwich meant to be eaten with both hands.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 long bread rolls
  • 150 g roast pork
  • 150 g thin steak
  • 100 g ham
  • 2 slices cheese
  • 2 handfuls potato sticks
  • lettuce and tomato
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp ketchup

Method

  1. Sear the steak on a hot griddle and slice thin.
  2. Warm the roast pork and ham on the griddle.
  3. Toast the rolls and spread with mayonnaise and ketchup.
  4. Layer the three meats and the cheese so it melts slightly.
  5. Add lettuce, tomato and potato sticks.
  6. Close the roll, press and cut in half.

How to make it lighter

  • Use whole-grain rolls and less sauce.
  • Choose lean cuts and fewer potato sticks.
  • Add extra salad greens for freshness.

History

The tripleta is a street sandwich of the island, commonly found at food kiosks and often compared to a Puerto Rican take on a loaded sub. Its fillings vary from stand to stand.
