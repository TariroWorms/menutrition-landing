🧀 Ramadan Cheese Rolls

Ramadan cheese rolls are thin spring roll pastry sheets wrapped around white cheese and chopped parsley, then fried until golden and crisp. Inside, the cheese melts into a soft, gooey filling. They are one of the most common savoury bites on iftar tables, served hot next to soup and samosas.

What it is made of

spring roll pastrycheeseparsleyoil

Recipe

6 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 20 spring roll pastry sheets
  • 300 g white cheese (feta-style or akkawi), crumbled
  • 100 g mozzarella, grated
  • 1 bunch parsley, finely chopped
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix the white cheese, mozzarella and parsley.
  2. Place a spoonful of filling near one corner of a pastry sheet.
  3. Fold in the sides and roll tightly, sealing the edge with a little water.
  4. Fry in hot oil for 2–3 minutes, turning, until golden.
  5. Drain on paper towels and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Air-fry or bake brushed with a little oil instead of deep-frying — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use lighter cheeses and add more parsley to the filling.
  • Pair 2–3 rolls with soup and salad for a balanced iftar.

History

Cheese rolls made with ready-made spring roll pastry are a modern household favourite across the Gulf, the Levant and Egypt, prepared above all during Ramadan for iftar.
