Kada (sweet butter pastry)

Origin: Georgia

Kada is a sweet pastry with a soft yeasted dough wrapped around a filling of flour rubbed with butter and sugar. When baked, the filling turns into a sandy, caramel-scented layer. It is cut into wedges and eaten with tea or coffee.

What it is made of

flourbuttersugareggyeastmilk

Recipe

8 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • Dough: 400 g flour, 7 g yeast, 150 ml warm milk, 1 egg, 80 g butter, 50 g sugar
  • Filling: 150 g flour, 150 g soft butter, 150 g sugar, 1 tsp vanilla sugar
  • 1 egg yolk for glazing

Method

  1. Make a soft dough from the dough ingredients and let it rise for 1 hour.
  2. Rub the filling ingredients together into moist crumbs.
  3. Roll the dough into a large circle and spread the filling over it.
  4. Roll up, coil or fold into a round, flatten gently and brush with yolk.
  5. Bake at 180 °C for 30–35 minutes until deep golden; cool before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 30% less butter in the filling and add ground walnuts for richness.
  • Cut thin wedges and share; one slice is enough with tea.
  • Swap part of the sugar for raisins or chopped dried apricots.

History

Kada is baked across Georgia and is closely related to gata, a similar pastry known in neighbouring Armenia and Azerbaijan. Families often make it for holidays and guests, and village bakeries sell it in many shapes.
