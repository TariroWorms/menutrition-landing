Ginger scallion lobster (Jiang cong long xia)

Origin: China · Guangdong

Lobster pieces are lightly dusted, flash-fried and tossed in a wok with plenty of ginger, scallions and a glossy oyster-sauce gravy. The sweet flesh stays juicy while the aromatics perfume the sauce. It is a celebratory banquet dish, eaten with rice to soak up the gravy.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lobster, about 700 g, cut in pieces
  • 50 g ginger, sliced
  • 4 scallions, cut in sections
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 200 ml chicken stock

Method

  1. Dust the lobster pieces with cornstarch.
  2. Fry them in hot oil for 1 minute and remove.
  3. Stir-fry ginger, garlic and scallion whites in a wok.
  4. Add stock and oyster sauce and bring to a boil.
  5. Return the lobster, cover for 3 minutes and finish with scallion greens.

How to make it lighter

  • Steam the lobster instead of frying it.
  • Thicken with less oyster sauce.
  • Serve with greens.

History

Lobster cooked with ginger and scallion is a staple of Cantonese banquets, where seafood is picked live from tanks. The pairing of ginger and scallion is a classic way to bring out sweetness in shellfish.
