Agua de pepino con limón (cucumber lime water)

Origin: Mexico

Agua de pepino is a bright green agua fresca made by blending cucumber with lime and water. It tastes clean, grassy and tart, a little like a lime soda without the fizz. It is one of the lightest drinks at a taquería or lunch counter and very welcome on hot days.

What it is made of

cucumberlimesugarwater

Recipe

6 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large cucumbers (about 600 g)
  • Juice of 4 limes
  • 1.5 l cold water
  • 70 g sugar
  • A few mint leaves (optional)
  • Ice

Method

  1. Peel the cucumbers if the skin is bitter and cut into chunks.
  2. Blend with 500 ml water and the mint until very smooth.
  3. Strain for a clear drink, or leave unstrained for more fiber.
  4. Add the remaining water, lime juice and sugar and stir.
  5. Serve very cold over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sugar or use a few drops of honey; cucumber and lime are refreshing on their own.
  • Leave it unstrained to keep the fiber.
  • Add a pinch of salt after exercise for a homemade electrolyte drink.

History

Cucumber arrived in Mexico with the Spanish and became common in markets. It joined the long list of fruits and vegetables turned into aguas frescas, a tradition sold in markets and street stalls throughout the country.
