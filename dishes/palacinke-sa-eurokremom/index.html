🥞 Palačinke with Chocolate Spread

Origin: Balkans · Southeastern Europe

These Balkan crepes are made from a thin batter of flour, eggs and milk and cooked quickly in a hot pan. While still warm they are spread with a sweet chocolate spread and scattered with chopped walnuts, then rolled or folded into quarters. The result is soft and rich with a little crunch, a favourite dessert in cafés and homes across the region.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 300 ml milk
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 tsp oil for the pan
  • 100 g chocolate spread
  • 40 g walnuts, chopped

Method

  1. Whisk flour, eggs, milk and salt into a smooth, thin batter; rest 10 minutes.
  2. Cook thin crepes in a lightly oiled hot pan, about 1 minute per side.
  3. Spread each warm crepe with chocolate spread.
  4. Sprinkle with chopped walnuts, roll or fold, and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use about 1 tbsp of spread per crepe and spread it thinly.
  • Keep walnuts to a small sprinkle; they add flavour but are calorie-dense.
  • Add sliced banana or strawberries so fewer crepes feel like a full dessert.

History

Palačinke are eaten throughout the former Yugoslav countries, and a chocolate spread filling is one of the most requested versions in pancake shops there. Walnuts are a common addition in Balkan desserts.
