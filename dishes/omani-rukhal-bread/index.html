Rukhal bread (Omani paper-thin bread)

Origin: Oman

Rukhal is a very thin, crisp Omani flatbread that looks like a large sheet of paper. It is cooked quickly on a hot convex griddle and is crunchy at the edges and slightly soft in the middle. People eat it with honey, ghee, date syrup or alongside savory dishes.

Recipe

6 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain flour
  • 300 ml water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Mix the flour, water, salt and oil into a loose batter.
  2. Heat a griddle until very hot.
  3. Spread a thin layer of batter quickly over the surface.
  4. Cook until the edges lift and turn golden.
  5. Peel off and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve with honey sparingly or with vegetables.
  • Use half whole-wheat flour for more fibre.
  • Brush with only a little oil.

History

It is a traditional bread of Omani homes and is still made in markets and bakeries. Its thinness comes from stretching a loose dough over the hot surface.
