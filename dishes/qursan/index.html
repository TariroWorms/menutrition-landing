Qursan is a Najdi dish of very thin, dry flatbread torn into pieces and layered in a pot with a rich stew of lamb, tomato and onion spiced with baharat. The bread soaks up the broth and turns soft and layered, almost like a savoury bread pudding. It is served from a large shared platter with the meat on top.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb on the bone
  • 300 g thin dry flatbread (qursan sheets)
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp baharat
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1.5 l water
  • Salt

Method

  1. Brown the onions in the oil, add the lamb and baharat and sear on all sides.
  2. Add tomatoes, water and salt; simmer covered for about 1.5 hours until the lamb is tender.
  3. Lift out the meat and keep warm.
  4. Layer torn flatbread in the pot, ladling broth over each layer until soft.
  5. Spread on a platter and top with the lamb.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the lamb and skim the broth.
  • Add courgette or pumpkin to the stew for volume.
  • Measure out a portion of bread before topping with meat.

History

Qursan is a traditional dish of central Saudi Arabia, made with thin sheets of bread that keep for a long time, and it remains a winter favourite in Najdi homes.
