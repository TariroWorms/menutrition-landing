Moro de guandules con coco (rice with pigeon peas and coconut)

Origin: Dominican Republic

Moro de guandules con coco is rice cooked together with green pigeon peas, sofrito and coconut milk. The grains stay separate and take on a mild coconut sweetness with savoury herbs. It is a favourite side for roast pork and fried fish, especially at Christmas.

Recipe

6 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g long-grain rice
  • 250 g cooked pigeon peas
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 3 tbsp sofrito
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 300 ml water
  • chopped cilantro
  • salt

Method

  1. Warm the oil and cook the sofrito for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the pigeon peas and stir.
  3. Add the rice and stir to coat.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk and water, season with salt and bring to a boil.
  5. Cover and cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
  6. Rest 5 minutes, fluff and add cilantro.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk to reduce the fat.
  • Mix in brown rice for more fibre.
  • Serve with grilled fish or chicken.

History

Moro dishes of rice and beans are found across the Spanish Caribbean. The pigeon pea, brought from Africa, is the signature legume of Dominican celebrations.
