🍌 Chocolate-hazelnut and banana crêpe

Origin: France

This is one of the most popular sweet crêpes in France: a warm, thin crêpe spread with chocolate-hazelnut spread and filled with slices of banana, then folded into quarters. The spread melts into a gooey layer that wraps around the soft fruit. It is sold at street stands and crêperies and eaten warm as a dessert or afternoon treat.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 ready-made crêpes (about 60 g each)
  • 2 tbsp chocolate-hazelnut spread (about 30 g)
  • 1 ripe banana, sliced
  • Optional: a pinch of cinnamon

Method

  1. Warm a crêpe in a dry non-stick pan over medium heat for 30 seconds.
  2. Spread half the chocolate-hazelnut spread over one half of the crêpe.
  3. Arrange half the banana slices on top and sprinkle with cinnamon if you like.
  4. Fold into quarters, warm 30 seconds more and serve. Repeat with the second crêpe.

How to make it lighter

  • The spread is the densest part: 1 tbsp of chocolate-hazelnut spread ≈ 80 kcal, so measure it rather than spreading freely.
  • Use more banana and less spread for the same sweetness.
  • Share one crêpe as a dessert after a meal rather than eating two as a snack.

History

Sweet crêpes have long been sold at French street stands and crêperies, and chocolate-hazelnut spread with banana became one of the most requested fillings in the second half of the 20th century. Today the combination is found at crêpe stands across Europe and beyond.
