Russian pea soup (gorokhovy sup)

Origin: Russia

Gorokhovy sup is a thick, golden soup in which split peas cook down until they almost dissolve. Smoked ribs or other smoked meat give it a deep, savoury, slightly bacon-like flavour, while potato and carrot add body. It is usually served hot with rye bread or small crunchy croutons.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g split peas
  • 500 g smoked pork ribs
  • 3 potatoes (350 g)
  • 1 carrot (100 g)
  • 1 onion (100 g)
  • 1 tbsp sunflower oil
  • 2.5 l water
  • salt, pepper, bay leaf, dill, rye bread for croutons

Method

  1. Rinse the peas and soak them in cold water for 2-3 hours.
  2. Cover the ribs with water, bring to a boil and simmer for 40 minutes, skimming.
  3. Add the drained peas and cook for 40 minutes until they start to fall apart.
  4. Add diced potato and cook for 15 minutes more.
  5. Fry the chopped onion and grated carrot in oil and stir them into the soup.
  6. Take the meat off the bones, return it to the pot, season and add a bay leaf.
  7. Serve sprinkled with dill, with rye croutons.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean smoked chicken or turkey instead of pork ribs to cut the fat.
  • Skip the frying step and simmer the onion and carrot directly in the soup.
  • Replace white-bread croutons with a slice of wholegrain rye.

History

Peas have been grown in Russia for centuries and pea dishes were common on peasant tables. The version with smoked meat became a classic home and canteen soup during the Soviet era.
