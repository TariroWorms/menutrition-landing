Chicken stewed with mushrooms (xiaoji dun mogu)

Origin: China · Dongbei
368kcal

This is a slow-simmered stew of bone-in chicken with dried hazel mushrooms and glass noodles in a dark soy broth. The mushrooms add a deep forest aroma and the noodles absorb the juices. It is a homey northeast dish served in a big bowl.

Recipe

4 servings65 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g bone-in chicken pieces
  • 30 g dried mushrooms
  • 80 g glass noodles
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 piece of ginger and 2 scallions
  • 700 ml water

Method

  1. Soak the dried mushrooms in warm water for 30 minutes and keep the liquid.
  2. Brown the chicken pieces in a little oil.
  3. Add ginger, scallion, soy sauce, the mushrooms and their strained liquid.
  4. Cover and simmer for 35 minutes.
  5. Add the soaked noodles and cook for 8 more minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin to cut fat.
  • Add extra vegetables like cabbage.
  • Use low-sodium soy sauce.

History

The dish is tied to the forests of northeastern China, where mushrooms were gathered and dried for winter. Chicken stewed with them is a familiar family meal throughout the region.
