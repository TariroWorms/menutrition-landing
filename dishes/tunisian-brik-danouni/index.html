Brik danouni (Tunisian tuna brik)

Origin: Tunisia

Brik danouni is a thin sheet of pastry folded around a filling of tuna, mashed potato, capers and parsley, with an egg cracked in the middle. It is fried until golden and crackly while the yolk stays runny. It is tangy, salty and rich, and usually eaten by hand with a squeeze of lemon.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 4 sheets of warqa or spring roll pastry
  • 200 g canned tuna, drained
  • 150 g boiled potato, mashed
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 4 small eggs
  • 300 ml oil for frying
  • lemon wedges

Method

  1. Mix the tuna, potato, capers and parsley and season.
  2. Place a spoonful of filling in the centre of each sheet.
  3. Make a small hollow and crack an egg into it.
  4. Fold into a triangle or half moon and seal the edges with water.
  5. Fry in hot oil for 1 to 2 minutes per side until golden.
  6. Drain on paper and serve at once with lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the brik with a brush of oil instead of deep frying.
  • Use tuna in water to reduce fat.
  • Add more parsley and a salad to balance the meal.

History

Brik is one of the best-known Tunisian dishes and is traditionally served at the evening meal during Ramadan. The tuna version is a popular variation of the classic egg brik.
