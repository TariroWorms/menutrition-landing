Sobrebarriga (Bogotá braised flank steak)

Origin: Colombia · Bogotá

Sobrebarriga is a whole flank steak simmered slowly with onion, garlic, tomato and herbs until it becomes very tender. In the Bogotá style it is then brushed with mustard, covered in breadcrumbs and browned in the oven, giving a crisp crust over juicy meat. It is served in slices with boiled or salted potatoes, rice and the reduced cooking sauce.

Recipe

6 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg beef flank in one piece
  • 1 onion, 3 tomatoes, 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin, thyme and bay leaf
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 800 g small potatoes
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season the flank with salt, pepper, cumin and garlic.
  2. Place it in a pot with onion, tomatoes, herbs and water to cover; simmer for 2 hours until tender.
  3. Remove the meat, strain and reduce the cooking liquid into a sauce.
  4. Boil the potatoes in some of the broth.
  5. Brush the meat with mustard, sprinkle with breadcrumbs and bake at 220 °C for 15 minutes.
  6. Slice and serve with potatoes and the sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat before cooking and skim the broth.
  • Skip the breadcrumb crust for a gluten-free, lighter version.
  • Serve with a salad and one starch rather than both rice and potatoes.

History

Flank is an economical cut, and long braising made it a popular family dish in the Andean highlands around Bogotá. Versions are also cooked in the Caribbean region, often finished in a tomato sauce instead of breadcrumbs.
