🥭 Mango Lassi

Origin: India · Punjab

Mango lassi is a thick, chilled Indian yogurt drink blended with ripe mango, a little sugar and a pinch of cardamom. It is smooth, tangy and fruity at the same time, and its creaminess makes it a natural partner for spicy curries and grills. It is served cold in a tall glass, sometimes as a dessert in its own right.

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain yogurt
  • 250 g ripe mango flesh
  • 1–2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 pinch ground cardamom
  • 4 ice cubes

Method

  1. Put the yogurt, mango, sugar and cardamom in a blender.
  2. Add the ice and blend until completely smooth.
  3. Taste and add a little more sugar if the mango is tart.
  4. Pour into glasses and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt for a lighter lassi.
  • Skip the sugar when the mango is very ripe — 1 tbsp sugar ≈ 50 kcal.
  • Make it thinner with cold water and ice for a larger, lighter glass.

History

Lassi is a traditional yogurt drink of Punjab and North India, made sweet or salted for centuries. The mango version became widespread with Indian restaurants abroad and is now one of the best-known Indian drinks worldwide.
