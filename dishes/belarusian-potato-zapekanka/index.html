Potato zapekanka (Belarusian potato bake)

Origin: Belarus

Bulbyanaya zapyakanka is a homely bake of mashed potato with a layer of fried minced pork and onion in the middle. The top is brushed with egg and sour cream, so it turns golden while the inside stays soft. It is cut into squares and served hot, often with extra sour cream or a pickled cucumber.

Recipe

6 servings80 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 400 g minced pork
  • 2 onions (160 g)
  • 2 eggs
  • 150 g sour cream
  • 30 g butter
  • salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Boil the peeled potatoes in salted water, drain and mash with butter.
  2. Fry the chopped onion until soft, add the minced pork and cook until browned; season.
  3. Beat one egg into the mash once it has cooled slightly.
  4. Spread half the mash in a greased dish, cover with the meat, then top with the remaining mash.
  5. Whisk the second egg with sour cream, brush over the top and bake at 190 °C for 30–35 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork or turkey mince to cut the fat.
  • Mash with some of the cooking water instead of extra butter.
  • Serve with a large cabbage or cucumber salad instead of a second portion.

History

Potatoes became the backbone of Belarusian cooking in the 19th century, and the country is famous for its many potato dishes. Casseroles like this were an everyday way to stretch a little meat into a family meal.
