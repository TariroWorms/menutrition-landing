Tarkhuna (tarragon lemonade)

Origin: Georgia

Tarkhuna is a sweet, fizzy soft drink with the fresh, slightly aniseed taste of tarragon. The commercial version is famous for its vivid green colour, while homemade versions are paler. It is served ice cold with meals or on hot days.

What it is made of

tarragonsugarlemonsparkling water

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 50 g fresh tarragon
  • 100 g sugar
  • 150 ml water
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 l chilled sparkling water
  • Ice

Method

  1. Bring sugar and water to a boil to make a syrup.
  2. Remove from heat, add the tarragon and steep for 20 minutes.
  3. Blend briefly, then strain through a fine sieve.
  4. Stir in the lemon juice and chill.
  5. Mix the syrup with sparkling water and serve over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the syrup for a drink with half the sugar.
  • Add extra lemon and mint for freshness without sweetness.
  • Sweeten with a little stevia if you want it nearly calorie-free.

History

The drink is linked to the Georgian soda tradition associated with Mitrofan Lagidze, and it became widely known across the Soviet Union under the name Tarkhun. Today it is still a familiar bottled drink in Georgia and neighbouring countries.
