🍯 Yemeni honey fatta

Yemeni honey fatta is a sweet dish of warm flatbread torn into small pieces and mashed with melted ghee and honey into a soft, sticky mass. Sliced banana and chopped dates are often folded in or scattered on top. It is eaten with the fingers from a shared bowl, usually at breakfast or as a sweet course, and is very rich and filling.

What it is made of

flatbreadhoneygheebananadates

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 3 flatbreads (about 300 g)
  • 3 tbsp ghee, melted
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 2 bananas, sliced
  • 6 dates, pitted and chopped
  • A pinch of ground cardamom

Method

  1. Warm the flatbreads and tear them into very small pieces.
  2. Pour over the melted ghee and cardamom and mash with your hands or a spoon until soft and evenly coated.
  3. Drizzle in most of the honey and mix again.
  4. Top with banana, dates and the remaining honey and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp ghee instead of 3 — 1 tbsp ghee ≈ 110–120 kcal.
  • Let the banana and dates provide sweetness and halve the honey; 1 tbsp honey ≈ 60 kcal.
  • Serve it as a shared dessert in small bowls of about 100 g.

History

Fatta with honey and ghee is part of everyday and festive cooking in Yemen, where both honey and bread-based dishes have a long tradition. Today it is served in Yemeni homes and restaurants, including Yemeni restaurants across the Gulf.
