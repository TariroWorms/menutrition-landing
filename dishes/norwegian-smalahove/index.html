Smalahove (Norwegian salted and smoked sheep's head)

Origin: Norway · Voss
876kcal

Smalahove is half of a sheep's head that is salted, dried and smoked, then boiled or steamed for a few hours. The meat is soft and rich, especially around the cheeks, and tastes salty and smoky. It is served with boiled potatoes and mashed swede. It is eaten in the weeks before Christmas, mostly in western Norway.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 salted smoked sheep's head half
  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 500 g swede
  • Salt (if needed)
  • Butter
  • Mustard for serving

Method

  1. Soak the head in cold water overnight, changing the water once.
  2. Place in a large pot with fresh water and simmer for 2-3 hours.
  3. Boil the potatoes and swede separately.
  4. Mash the swede with a little butter.
  5. Serve the meat with the potatoes and mash.

How to make it lighter

  • Eat in small portions, as the meat is rich and salty.
  • Soak well to lower the sodium.
  • Serve with a lot of boiled vegetables.

History

The dish is traditionally linked to the Voss area and the neighbouring fjord districts, where every part of the animal was used. Today it is a special seasonal meal that many people regard as a local tradition.
