Fish head curry (kari kepala ikan)

Origin: Malaysia

Fish head curry is a bold, sour and spicy dish of Malaysia and Singapore. A large red snapper head is simmered in a curry gravy of spices, tamarind and a little coconut milk with okra, eggplant and tomato. The cheeks and collar are tender and gelatinous, and the gravy is mopped up with rice or bread.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 1 red snapper head (about 800 g)
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 2 tbsp tamarind pulp
  • 150 g okra
  • 1 eggplant
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 100 ml coconut milk
  • onion, garlic, ginger, chili, curry leaves, oil

Method

  1. Fry onion, garlic, ginger and curry leaves in oil until soft.
  2. Add the curry powder mixed with water and cook until fragrant.
  3. Pour in 500 ml water, tamarind and coconut milk; simmer.
  4. Add eggplant, okra and tomatoes and cook 8 minutes.
  5. Add the fish head, simmer gently 12 minutes and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the coconut milk and more tamarind and vegetables.
  • Serve with plain rice rather than fried rice.
  • Skim the oil from the surface.

History

The dish is a well-known product of the Indian communities of Malaysia and Singapore, adapted to local tastes with tamarind and coconut. It is also a favourite at Chinese and Malay eateries.
