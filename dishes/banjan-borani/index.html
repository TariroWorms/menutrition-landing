Banjan borani (Afghan eggplant with garlic yogurt)

Origin: Afghanistan

Banjan borani is a platter of golden fried eggplant slices spread with a soft tomato and onion sauce and finished with cool garlic yogurt. The contrast of warm, silky eggplant and tangy yogurt is the heart of the dish. Dried mint is scattered over the top, and flatbread is used to scoop it up.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g eggplant
  • 4 tbsp oil (60 ml) for frying
  • 1 onion (150 g)
  • 300 g tomatoes
  • 250 g yogurt with 2 crushed garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried mint, salt

Method

  1. Slice the eggplant, salt it and rest 15 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Fry the slices in oil until golden on both sides and drain.
  3. Cook the onion and tomatoes with salt for 15 minutes into a thick sauce.
  4. Layer eggplant and sauce on a platter.
  5. Mix the yogurt with garlic and salt and spoon over the top.
  6. Sprinkle with dried mint and serve with bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the eggplant in the oven with a brush of oil instead of frying.
  • Use strained yogurt for extra protein.
  • Serve as a side with grilled meat rather than a main.

History

Borani is the Afghan name for dishes of vegetables paired with garlicky yogurt, found in several forms across Afghan and Persian kitchens. Eggplant and pumpkin are the two most popular versions.
