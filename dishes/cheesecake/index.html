🍰 Baked cheesecake

Origin: United States · New York

Baked cheesecake is a dense, creamy dessert of cream cheese, sugar and eggs poured over a crumbly base of crushed graham crackers and butter, then baked slowly until just set. The filling is smooth and rich with a gentle tang, contrasting with the crisp, sweet crust. It is served chilled in slices, plain or with fresh fruit.

Recipe

10 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g graham crackers, crushed
  • 80 g butter, melted
  • 750 g cream cheese, room temperature
  • 180 g sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix the cracker crumbs with melted butter, press into a 23 cm springform tin and bake 10 minutes at 170 °C.
  2. Beat the cream cheese with sugar, vanilla and salt until smooth.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing on low just until combined.
  4. Pour over the base and bake at 150 °C for 55–65 minutes, until the centre still wobbles slightly.
  5. Turn off the oven, leave the cake inside 1 hour, then chill at least 4 hours before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut thinner slices — a 12-slice cake instead of 8 trims each portion by a third.
  • Top with fresh berries rather than sweet sauces or syrups.
  • Use a reduced-fat cream cheese and a thinner crust to lower the total.

History

Cheese-based cakes go back to ancient Greece and Rome, but the modern American version took shape after cream cheese was developed in New York State in the 1870s. The tall, dense New York-style cheesecake became famous in the city's delis and restaurants in the early twentieth century.
