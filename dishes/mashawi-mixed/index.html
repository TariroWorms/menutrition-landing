🍢 Mixed Grill (Mashawi)

Origin: Levant · Middle East

Mashawi is the classic Middle Eastern mixed grill: skewers of marinated lamb cubes, chicken pieces and spiced minced kofta cooked over charcoal. Expect smoky, charred edges and juicy centres, seasoned with baharat and onion. It is served on a large platter with grilled onions, tomatoes and fresh herbs for sharing.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g lamb, cubed
  • 400 g boneless chicken thighs, cubed
  • 300 g minced lamb for kofta
  • 2 onions (1 grated, 1 in wedges)
  • 3 tsp baharat
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1½ tsp salt
  • 1 bunch parsley

Method

  1. Marinate lamb and chicken separately with 1 tsp baharat, 1 tbsp oil and salt each for 1 hour.
  2. Mix minced lamb with grated onion, chopped parsley, remaining baharat and salt; shape around skewers.
  3. Thread meats onto separate skewers, adding onion wedges.
  4. Grill over hot charcoal: chicken 10–12 minutes, lamb 8–10, kofta 6–8, turning often.
  5. Serve on a platter with grilled onion and parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Load your plate with chicken pieces, the leanest of the three meats.
  • Trim visible fat from the lamb before marinating.
  • Fill half the plate with grilled vegetables and salad to stretch a 250 g meat portion.

History

Grilling skewered meat over charcoal is an old tradition across the Levant, Turkey and the Arabian Peninsula, and the mixed platter is a fixture of restaurants throughout the region today.
