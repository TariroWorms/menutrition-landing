Fuul (Yemeni fava bean stew)

Origin: Yemen

Yemeni fuul is a thick stew of cooked fava beans simmered with tomato, onion, garlic, cumin and chili until the beans partly break down. It is cooked and served bubbling in a small clay pot and finished with oil and fresh herbs. Scooped up with flatbread, it is a warm, filling breakfast.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g cooked fava beans (or 200 g dried)
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 green chili
  • 3 tbsp olive oil (45 ml), parsley and lemon

Method

  1. Soak and cook dried beans until soft, or drain canned beans.
  2. Fry the onion, garlic and chili in oil until golden.
  3. Add tomatoes and cumin and cook 5 minutes.
  4. Add the beans with a little water and simmer 15 minutes, mashing some.
  5. Season, add lemon and parsley and serve in a clay pot with bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and more tomato.
  • Serve with whole wheat bread.
  • Add chopped egg or vegetables for variety.

History

Fava beans have been a staple across the Arabian Peninsula and the Nile region for centuries. In Yemen fuul is a common breakfast and street food, often eaten with lahoh or tannur bread.
