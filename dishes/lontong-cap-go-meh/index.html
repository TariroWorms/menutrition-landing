Lontong cap go meh (rice cakes in coconut curry)

Origin: Indonesia · Peranakan Java

Lontong cap go meh is a festive dish of compressed rice cake sliced and served in a mild yellow coconut curry with chayote or bamboo shoot, shredded chicken and boiled egg. Sambal, fried shallots and crackers are added at the table. It is rich but gentle and meant to be shared.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g lontong (compressed rice cakes), sliced
  • 300 g chicken thigh, cooked and shredded
  • 4 boiled eggs, halved
  • 200 g chayote or bamboo shoot, strips
  • 400 ml coconut milk and 300 ml water
  • 5 shallots, 3 garlic cloves, 2 cm galangal, 1 tsp turmeric, ground
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, 1 bay leaf
  • Salt, sambal and fried shallots to serve

Method

  1. Fry the ground spices with lemongrass and bay leaf until fragrant.
  2. Add water and coconut milk and bring to a simmer.
  3. Add the chayote and cook until tender, about 15 minutes.
  4. Season with salt.
  5. Arrange the lontong in bowls and ladle the curry over.
  6. Top with chicken, egg, fried shallots and sambal.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk for a lighter curry.
  • Add more vegetables and fewer rice cakes.
  • Choose skinless chicken breast for less fat.

History

The dish is linked to the Peranakan Chinese communities of Java and is traditionally served at Cap Go Meh, the fifteenth day of Lunar New Year celebrations. It combines Chinese festive custom with Javanese coconut curries.
