🧀 Pan-Fried Tomme

Tomme poêlée is a thick slice of tomme, a mild mountain cheese, seared in a hot pan until a golden crust forms while the inside turns soft and melting. It is served straight away on a bed of dressed green salad, the cool leaves balancing the rich, nutty cheese. A quick starter or light lunch in Alpine France.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g tomme cheese, in 2 thick slices
  • 150 g mixed salad leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • Pepper and chopped herbs

Method

  1. Dress the salad with oil, vinegar and pepper and divide between plates.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium-high heat without fat.
  3. Sear the cheese slices 1–2 minutes per side until golden and starting to soften.
  4. Slide the cheese onto the salad and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut thinner slices: the cheese is rich, so 60–80 g per person is plenty.
  • Use a non-stick pan with no added fat.
  • Make the salad portion large and the dressing light; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Tomme is a family of simple farmhouse cheeses made in the French Alps, especially Savoie. Pan-frying slices of it is a common home and bistro preparation there today.
