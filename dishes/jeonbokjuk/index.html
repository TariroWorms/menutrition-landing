Jeonbokjuk (abalone rice porridge)

Origin: South Korea · Jeju

Jeonbokjuk is a silky rice porridge cooked with finely sliced abalone, sometimes with the green innards that tint it faintly jade. It tastes of the sea, nutty from sesame oil, with a pleasant chew from the shellfish. It is served hot in small bowls with a few side dishes.

What it is made of

Abalonericesesame oilcarrot

Recipe

4 servings55 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice (180 g)
  • 150 g abalone, cleaned and sliced
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil (15 ml)
  • 1 l water or light broth
  • 30 g carrot, minced
  • salt

Method

  1. Soak the rice 30 minutes and drain.
  2. Sauté the abalone slices briefly in sesame oil.
  3. Add the rice and toast 2 minutes.
  4. Pour in the water and simmer 30 minutes, stirring often, until creamy.
  5. Add carrot, season with salt and cook 5 minutes more.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep the oil modest to control the fat.
  • Serve with a vegetable side to balance the meal.
  • Season lightly if you need to limit sodium.

History

Abalone has long been prized on the island of Jeju, where women divers harvest it by hand. Abalone porridge is regarded in Korea as a restorative, special dish.
