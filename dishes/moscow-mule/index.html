The Moscow mule is a long cocktail of vodka, spicy ginger soda and fresh lime juice, served over ice. It tastes sharp, gingery and lightly sweet, with a fizzy finish. It is traditionally served in a copper mug that keeps the drink very cold, garnished with a wedge of lime.

Recipe

1 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 50 ml vodka
  • 120 ml ginger soda
  • 15 ml fresh lime juice
  • Ice cubes
  • 1 lime wedge to garnish

Method

  1. Fill a copper mug or tall glass with ice.
  2. Pour in the vodka and lime juice.
  3. Top with cold ginger soda and stir gently once.
  4. Garnish with a lime wedge and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Ginger soda adds most of the sugar: a lighter, less sweet ginger soda cuts calories noticeably.
  • Use more ice and more lime with less ginger soda for a sharper, lighter drink.
  • Count it in your daily budget: one mule of about 250 ml is a meaningful snack's worth of calories.

History

The Moscow mule is usually credited to the early 1940s in the United States, where, the story goes, a vodka importer and a ginger soda producer created it together in Los Angeles to promote their products. The copper mug became its signature and helped make vodka popular in America.
