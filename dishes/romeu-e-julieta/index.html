Romeu e Julieta (guava paste with white cheese)

Origin: Brazil · Minas Gerais

Romeu e Julieta is the simplest of Brazilian desserts: a slice of firm, ruby-red goiabada next to a slice of soft, mildly salty Minas cheese. Eaten together in one bite, the sweet fruit and the milky cheese balance each other perfectly. It is served after lunch, in sandwiches, and has inspired pies, ice creams and cheesecakes.

Recipe

4 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g goiabada (guava paste)
  • 200 g Minas fresh cheese or another mild white cheese

Method

  1. Cut the goiabada into slices about 1 cm thick.
  2. Cut the cheese into slices of the same size.
  3. Arrange them alternately on a plate.
  4. Serve at room temperature, or warm the cheese briefly on a griddle for a softer version.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more cheese than guava paste to add protein and cut sugar.
  • Choose a low-fat fresh cheese or ricotta.
  • Look for goiabada with reduced sugar.

History

Guava paste and fresh cheese have been eaten together in Minas Gerais for generations. The romantic name, Romeo and Juliet, is said to have spread through an advertising campaign in the mid twentieth century that presented the two as the perfect couple.
