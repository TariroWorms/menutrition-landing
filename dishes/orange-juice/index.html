Orange juice is the juice pressed from fresh oranges, served chilled as a breakfast drink around the world. Freshly squeezed juice is bright, sweet and slightly tart, with or without pulp depending on how it is strained. A typical glass needs three to four medium oranges.

What it is made of

orange juice

Recipe

1 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 3–4 oranges (about 600 g)
  • Ice cubes, optional
  • A few mint leaves to garnish, optional

Method

  1. Roll the oranges on the worktop to soften them, then cut them in half.
  2. Squeeze them on a citrus press or juicer.
  3. Strain if you prefer it without pulp and serve chilled, over ice if you like.

How to make it lighter

  • A glass of juice holds the sugar of several oranges without their fibre — eating a whole orange is more filling.
  • Pour a smaller 150 ml glass instead of 250 ml.
  • Dilute it half and half with sparkling water for a lighter drink.

History

Orange juice is drunk worldwide today, with Brazil and the United States among the largest producers of oranges for juice.
