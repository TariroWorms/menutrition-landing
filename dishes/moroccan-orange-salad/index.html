🍊 Moroccan orange salad

Origin: Morocco · Maghreb

Moroccan orange salad is a simple dessert of peeled orange slices sprinkled with sugar and ground cinnamon and perfumed with a few drops of orange blossom water. The oranges release their juice into a light, fragrant syrup. It is served chilled at the end of a meal, often after tagines or couscous, as a fresh and juicy finish.

What it is made of

orangecinnamonorange blossom watersugar

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 large oranges
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp orange blossom water
  • A few fresh mint leaves to garnish

Method

  1. Cut the peel and white pith off the oranges with a sharp knife.
  2. Slice the oranges into thin rounds and arrange them on a platter.
  3. Sprinkle with sugar and drizzle with orange blossom water.
  4. Chill for at least 30 minutes so the juices form a light syrup.
  5. Dust with cinnamon and garnish with mint just before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sugar entirely: ripe oranges are sweet enough with cinnamon and orange blossom.
  • 1 tbsp of sugar ≈ 48 kcal, so measure it rather than sprinkling freely.
  • Serve one orange per person as a light dessert after a filling main course.

History

Oranges are widely grown in Morocco, and orange blossom water is a classic flavouring in Moroccan sweets. This salad is a common home and restaurant dessert across the country today.
