🌮 Chicken tacos (tacos de pollo)

Tacos de pollo are small corn tortillas filled with seasoned shredded or grilled chicken, topped with chopped onion, fresh cilantro, a squeeze of lime and green salsa verde. The chicken is juicy and lightly spiced, the toppings keep everything fresh and sharp. They are one of the most familiar everyday tacos, easy to make at home and served two or three per plate.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless chicken thighs or breast
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp dried oregano, salt, pepper
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed, and juice of 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 12 small corn tortillas
  • 1 onion, finely chopped, and 1 bunch cilantro
  • 200 ml salsa verde

Method

  1. Rub the chicken with cumin, oregano, garlic, lime juice, salt and pepper and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Cook it in the oil in a hot pan or on a grill for 5–6 minutes per side until cooked through.
  3. Rest the chicken for a few minutes, then slice or shred it.
  4. Warm the tortillas on a dry pan.
  5. Fill with chicken, top with onion, cilantro and salsa verde and serve with lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Chicken breast is leaner than thigh if you want to keep the filling lighter.
  • Pile on onion, cilantro, lime and salsa — they add a lot of flavour for very few calories.
  • Warm the tortillas on a dry pan instead of frying them in oil; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Chicken tacos are eaten all over Mexico, at home, at markets and at taco stands, and are among the most common tacos on Mexican menus abroad.
