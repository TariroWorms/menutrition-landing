Jjimdak (Andong braised chicken)

Origin: South Korea · Andong
420kcal

Jjimdak is chicken simmered with potatoes, carrots and sweet potato noodles in a dark, glossy soy sauce sweetened with sugar. The result is tender meat, soft vegetables and slippery noodles that soak up the sauce. It is savory and gently sweet, sometimes with a chili kick, and eaten from a shared plate with rice.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken pieces
  • 2 potatoes (300 g)
  • 1 carrot (100 g)
  • 100 g dried sweet potato noodles
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce (60 ml)
  • 2 tbsp sugar (25 g)
  • garlic, ginger, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 300 ml water

Method

  1. Blanch the chicken for 5 minutes and drain.
  2. Soak the noodles in warm water for 30 minutes.
  3. Simmer the chicken with soy sauce, sugar, garlic, ginger and water for 20 minutes.
  4. Add potatoes and carrot and cook 15 minutes until tender.
  5. Add the noodles for the last 5 minutes and finish with sesame oil.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin to cut fat.
  • Use less sugar for a lighter sauce.
  • Add more vegetables such as mushrooms.

History

Andong braised chicken is associated with the city of Andong in North Gyeongsang, where it is said to have been popularized in the local market. It has since spread across Korea and is a typical family and gathering dish.
