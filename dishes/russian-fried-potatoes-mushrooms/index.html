Fried potatoes with mushrooms (zharenaya kartoshka s gribami)

Origin: Russia

This homely skillet dish combines sliced potatoes fried until crisp at the edges with mushrooms and sweet browned onion. The mushrooms add an earthy, savoury depth, while dill brightens everything at the end. It is eaten hot straight from the pan, often with pickles and a spoon of sour cream.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 400 g mushrooms (wild or champignons)
  • 1 large onion
  • 4 tbsp sunflower oil
  • salt, black pepper
  • small bunch of dill

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into thin sticks or slices; pat them dry.
  2. Slice the mushrooms and fry them in 1 tbsp oil until their liquid evaporates and they brown; set aside.
  3. Heat the remaining oil in a wide skillet and lay in the potatoes in an even layer.
  4. Fry over medium-high heat without stirring for 7–8 minutes, then turn and continue until golden and tender.
  5. Add sliced onion and the mushrooms for the last 5–7 minutes and season with salt and pepper.
  6. Sprinkle with chopped dill and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and halve the oil, adding a splash of water with a lid to finish cooking.
  • Increase the share of mushrooms relative to potatoes for fewer calories per portion.
  • Serve with a fresh cucumber and tomato salad instead of bread.

History

In Russia mushroom picking is a beloved autumn pastime, and wild mushrooms fried with potatoes are a typical dacha meal. Today cultivated champignons are commonly used year-round.
