Pliatsok (Galician layered sheet cake)

Origin: Ukraine

Pliatsok is the Galician name for a family of rectangular layered cakes baked on a tray and cut into squares. Thin layers of sponge or shortcrust alternate with cream, jam or poppy seed and are often topped with chocolate glaze. Each family has its own versions, and several kinds are set out together on holiday tables.

Recipe

12 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 200 g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 g sugar
  • 200 g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 300 ml milk custard or buttercream

Method

  1. Rub the butter into the flour, then add yolks, half the sugar and sour cream.
  2. Divide the dough in two, add cocoa to one part and chill both.
  3. Roll each into a sheet and bake at 180 °C for 15-20 minutes.
  4. Whip the whites with the remaining sugar and spread on one layer, then bake 10 minutes more.
  5. Assemble the layers with custard or buttercream.
  6. Chill for several hours and cut into squares.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut small squares; it is a rich festive cake.
  • Use a light custard instead of buttercream.
  • Add a layer of tart fruit or berries to balance the sweetness.

History

The word pliatsok is typical of Lviv and the wider Galicia region, where baking traditions were shaped by Central European, especially Austrian and Polish, influences. Tray cakes are a fixture of Christmas, Easter and family celebrations there.
