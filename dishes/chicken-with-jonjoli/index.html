Chicken with jonjoli

Origin: Georgia
480kcal

This home-style stew combines chicken with jonjoli, the pickled flower buds of the bladdernut shrub. The buds add a pleasant sourness and a slightly crunchy bite, balanced by soft onions, garlic and fresh herbs. It is served hot with bread or mchadi corn cakes.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 200 g pickled jonjoli, drained
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tomatoes, grated
  • 20 g butter
  • bunch of cilantro, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Brown the chicken in butter in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the onions and cook until soft.
  3. Add the tomatoes and 150 ml water, cover and stew for 30 minutes.
  4. Stir in the jonjoli and stew 10 minutes more.
  5. Finish with crushed garlic and chopped cilantro; rest 5 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin from the chicken to cut fat considerably.
  • Rinse the jonjoli briefly to reduce salt.
  • Serve with a big green salad instead of extra bread.

History

Jonjoli is a much-loved Georgian pickle, usually eaten as a cold appetizer with onion and oil, especially in western Georgia. Cooking it with poultry is a homely way to bring its tangy flavour into a main dish.
