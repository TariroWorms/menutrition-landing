Túró Rudi (chocolate-coated curd bar)

Origin: Hungary

Túró Rudi is a small bar of sweetened curd cheese covered in a thin shell of chocolate. It tastes lightly tangy and sweet and has a soft, dense filling that contrasts with the snappy coating. Hungarians eat it as a cold snack straight from the fridge.

What it is made of

Recipe

8 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g quark
  • 60 g powdered sugar
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 150 g dark chocolate

Method

  1. Drain the quark well and mash it until smooth.
  2. Mix in sugar, soft butter and vanilla.
  3. Shape the mixture into small bars and freeze for 30 minutes.
  4. Melt the chocolate gently.
  5. Dip each bar into the chocolate, let it set in the fridge and keep it cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose low-fat quark for a lighter bar.
  • Sweeten with a little honey instead of sugar.
  • Keep the chocolate layer thin.

History

It became a household snack in Hungary in the 1960s and is closely tied to national food memory. Homemade versions use fresh quark, sugar and melted chocolate.
