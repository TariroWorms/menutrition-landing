Fusilli all'arrabbiata is pasta in a fiery tomato sauce: garlic and dried or fresh chili are warmed in a little oil, then tomatoes are added and simmered until thick. Arrabbiata means "angry" in Italian, a nod to the heat of the chili. The dish is finished with chopped parsley and is entirely plant-based.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g fusilli
  • 800 g peeled tomatoes, crushed
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1–2 dried red chilies, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • A small bunch of parsley, chopped
  • Salt

Method

  1. Warm the oil in a pan and fry the garlic and chili gently for a minute.
  2. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt and simmer for about 20 minutes until thick.
  3. Cook the fusilli in salted boiling water until al dente.
  4. Drain and toss the pasta in the sauce over the heat for a minute.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • The chili does the work, so the sauce needs only a little oil — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve a moderate pasta portion with a large green salad.
  • Stir grilled vegetables such as peppers or aubergine into the sauce to add volume.

History

Penne all'arrabbiata is a classic of Rome and the Lazio region, and the sauce is now used with many pasta shapes, fusilli included. It became widely known in the 20th century and remains a staple of Roman trattorias.
