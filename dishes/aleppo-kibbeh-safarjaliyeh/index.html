Kibbeh safarjaliyeh (Aleppo quince kibbeh)

Origin: Syria · Aleppo

Kibbeh safarjaliyeh is a sweet-and-sour Aleppo stew of small bulgur and lamb kibbeh balls simmered with quince in a pomegranate molasses broth. The fruit turns soft and rosy while the shells stay tender. It is served with rice and is a signature of Aleppo's rich home cooking.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g fine bulgur
  • 300 g minced lamb for shell and filling
  • 2 quinces, cut into wedges
  • 2 tbsp pomegranate molasses
  • 1 onion
  • 1 litre water
  • 1 tbsp pine nuts for the filling
  • salt, allspice

Method

  1. Knead the bulgur with lamb and spices into a smooth shell mixture.
  2. Fill small balls with fried spiced lamb and pine nuts.
  3. Simmer quince wedges in water with pomegranate molasses for 20 minutes.
  4. Add the kibbeh balls.
  5. Cook 25 minutes until tender and the sauce thickens.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb and less oil.
  • Reduce the molasses if you want less sugar.
  • Serve with a green salad instead of extra rice.

History

Aleppo is famed for its many kibbeh types, and fruit-based stews like this one show the city's taste for balancing sweet and sour. It is prepared in autumn when quinces are in season.
