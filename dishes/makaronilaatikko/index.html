Makaronilaatikko (Finnish macaroni casserole)

Origin: Finland

Makaronilaatikko is a Finnish home-style casserole of macaroni and minced meat bound with a savory egg and milk custard. The top bakes golden with a slightly crisp edge while the inside stays soft. It is usually eaten with ketchup and pickled cucumber.

Recipe

6 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g macaroni
  • 300 g minced beef or mixed mince
  • 1 onion
  • 3 eggs
  • 500 ml milk
  • 20 g butter, salt

Method

  1. Boil the macaroni for a few minutes less than the packet says and drain.
  2. Brown the onion and minced meat in butter and season.
  3. Mix the macaroni and meat in a baking dish.
  4. Whisk eggs with milk and salt and pour over.
  5. Bake at 200 C for about 40 minutes until set and golden.
  6. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean mince and add grated carrot.
  • Use whole-grain pasta for fibre.
  • Replace some milk with water to lighten.

History

It is a typical school and family meal in Finland, and one of the traditional laatikko casseroles. It is an economical dish that uses pasta and mince, and is easy to make for a crowd.
