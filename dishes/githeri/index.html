Githeri (Kenyan maize and beans)

Origin: Kenya

Githeri is a hearty mix of whole maize kernels and beans boiled together until tender, then often fried with onion and tomato. It is soft, mild and filling. It is a common lunch for schoolchildren and workers.

What it is made of

cornbeansoniontomatooil

Recipe

5 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g dried maize kernels
  • 200 g dried beans
  • 1 onion (100 g)
  • 2 tomatoes (200 g)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • salt

Method

  1. Soak maize and beans overnight.
  2. Boil together 1.5 hours until tender.
  3. Fry the onion in oil until golden.
  4. Add tomato and cook until soft.
  5. Stir in the maize and beans with some cooking water and simmer 10 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use canned beans and maize to save time.
  • Add carrots or potatoes for extra vegetables.
  • High in fibre and plant protein.

History

Githeri comes from the Kikuyu and Meru peoples of central Kenya, where maize and beans were traditional crops. Today it is a popular, affordable meal across the country.
