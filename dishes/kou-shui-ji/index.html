Saliva chicken (kou shui ji)

Origin: China · Sichuan

The name means that the sauce is so tempting your mouth waters. Chicken is gently poached so it stays juicy, chilled in cold water, chopped and covered with a red sauce of chili oil, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, garlic and ground Sichuan pepper. Sesame, peanuts and scallion finish it. It is a cold starter.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g chicken legs
  • 3 tbsp chili oil with sediment
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp black vinegar and 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp sesame paste
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced, and 1 tsp ground Sichuan pepper
  • 20 g peanuts and sesame, toasted; ginger and scallion

Method

  1. Bring water with ginger and scallion to a gentle simmer and poach the chicken for 20-25 minutes.
  2. Plunge the chicken into iced water so the skin stays springy.
  3. Mix chili oil, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, sesame paste, garlic and Sichuan pepper.
  4. Chop the chicken into pieces and arrange on a plate.
  5. Pour the sauce over and scatter peanuts, sesame and scallion.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin and use less chili oil, favoring the flavored sediment over the fat.
  • Poach breast for a leaner version.
  • Add shredded cucumber under the chicken for volume.

History

The dish is part of Sichuan's tradition of cold dishes seasoned with complex sauces. It has long been a standard on Sichuan restaurant menus.
