Galayet bandora (Jordanian sautéed tomatoes)

Origin: Jordan

Galayet bandora is a quick dish of chopped tomatoes fried in olive oil with plenty of garlic and green chili until they collapse into a thick, glossy mixture. It is tangy, garlicky and pleasantly hot. It is eaten for breakfast or as a mezze, scooped up with warm flatbread.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g ripe tomatoes
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 green chilies
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Pinch of cumin
  • Chopped parsley

Method

  1. Chop the tomatoes, garlic and chilies.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the garlic and chilies for a minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and salt and cook over medium heat.
  4. Stir until the juices evaporate and the mixture thickens.
  5. Top with parsley and serve with bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and a non-stick pan.
  • Serve with whole wheat bread.
  • Add an egg for a protein-rich breakfast.

History

This simple tomato dish is common across Jordan and the Levant, where it is a reliable everyday breakfast. Cooks often finish it with a squeeze of lemon or a little tomato paste.
