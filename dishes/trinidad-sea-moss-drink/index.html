Sea moss drink

Origin: Trinidad and Tobago

Sea moss is a thick, silky drink made from boiled Irish moss seaweed blended with milk, sugar, nutmeg, cinnamon and vanilla. It is smooth, lightly sweet and fragrant, usually served cold. In Trinidad it is a popular energy drink sold at street stalls and roadside shops.

What it is made of

sea mossmilksugarnutmegvanillacinnamon

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 30 g dried sea moss, washed
  • 1 l water
  • 500 ml milk
  • 80 g sugar
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Rinse the sea moss well and soak for 30 minutes.
  2. Simmer in water for 25 minutes until it softens and gels.
  3. Drain and blend with the milk until smooth.
  4. Add sugar, nutmeg, cinnamon and vanilla and blend again.
  5. Strain if you want a smoother drink.
  6. Chill and serve over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar or sweeten with a little honey.
  • Choose low-fat or plant milk.
  • Keep portions modest because natural iodine is concentrated in sea moss.

History

The drink comes from Caribbean folk traditions, where sea moss is valued as a nourishing food. It is commonly sold at beach stands and markets across Trinidad and Tobago.
