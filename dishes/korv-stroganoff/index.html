Korv Stroganoff (sausage stroganoff)

Korv Stroganoff is a weeknight dish of falukorv sausage strips simmered in a creamy tomato sauce. It is mild, savoury and slightly sweet, with a soft, comforting texture. It is served over boiled rice and is a favourite with children.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g falukorv or other mild sausage
  • 1 onion
  • 20 g butter
  • 40 g tomato paste
  • 200 ml cream
  • 100 ml water
  • 200 g rice
  • salt to taste
  • white pepper to taste

Method

  1. Cook the rice according to the packet.
  2. Slice the sausage into strips and fry in butter until lightly browned.
  3. Add chopped onion and fry until soft, then stir in tomato paste.
  4. Pour in cream and water and simmer for about 10 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and pepper and serve over the rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a lower-fat sausage or turkey sausage.
  • Replace some cream with milk or light cream.
  • Serve with a side of vegetables or salad.

History

The dish borrows the name of beef stroganoff but uses cheap, mild sausage instead of beef. It became a popular family dinner in Swedish homes in the 20th century.
