Htamin jin (Shan fermented rice with fish and tomato)

Origin: Myanmar · Shan State

Htamin jin is a tangy rice dish from the Inle Lake area. Cooked rice is lightly fermented, then kneaded with flaked fish, tomato, garlic oil and chili into soft balls. It is eaten at room temperature with fresh herbs and crisp fried garlic.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked rice, lightly fermented
  • 150 g cooked flaked fish
  • 150 g tomato, mashed
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • handful of cilantro
  • salt

Method

  1. Mix cooked rice with a little water and leave a few hours to turn lightly sour.
  2. Fry garlic in oil until golden.
  3. Mash tomato and mix with fish, chili and garlic oil.
  4. Knead the rice with the fish-tomato mix and salt.
  5. Shape into balls and serve with herbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and more tomato.
  • Add raw cucumber and cabbage on the side.
  • Choose a lean white fish.

History

The dish is tied to the Intha people of Inle Lake, whose cooking relies on lake fish and tomatoes grown on floating gardens.
