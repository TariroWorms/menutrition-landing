Brown stew chicken (Jamaican)

Origin: Jamaica

Brown stew chicken is a Jamaican everyday classic. Marinated pieces are browned in caramelized sugar, then simmered with tomato, peppers, onion and thyme into a dark, glossy gravy. It is savory with a hint of sweetness and usually served with rice and peas.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 onion and 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves and 2 sprigs thyme
  • 1 scotch bonnet, whole
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil and salt

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with soy sauce, garlic, thyme, salt and half the onion for 1 hour.
  2. Heat oil with the sugar until it turns dark amber, then add the chicken and brown it well.
  3. Add the remaining vegetables and the whole pepper.
  4. Pour in a little water, cover and simmer 30-35 minutes.
  5. Uncover to thicken the gravy and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin before cooking to cut fat.
  • Use only a small spoon of sugar for browning.
  • Add extra vegetables for a lighter plate.

History

Browning meat in burnt sugar is a long-standing Caribbean technique that gives color and depth. The dish is a staple of Jamaican home cooking and small restaurants.
