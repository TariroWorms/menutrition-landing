Rakott krumpli (layered potato bake)

Origin: Hungary

Rakott krumpli is a comforting casserole of sliced potatoes, hard-boiled eggs and smoked sausage layered with sour cream. The top turns golden and crisp while the inside is soft and creamy. It is served hot straight from the dish, often with pickles.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes
  • 6 eggs, hard-boiled
  • 200 g smoked sausage, sliced
  • 250 g sour cream
  • 100 ml milk
  • Paprika, salt, pepper
  • Butter for the dish
  • Breadcrumbs (optional)

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins, then peel and slice.
  2. Grease an ovenproof dish and add a layer of potatoes.
  3. Add sliced eggs, sausage, salt, paprika and some sour cream.
  4. Repeat the layers and finish with potatoes.
  5. Mix remaining sour cream with milk and pour over.
  6. Bake at 190 °C for 40 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light sour cream and lean turkey sausage.
  • Add a layer of steamed vegetables to reduce the starch ratio.
  • Serve with a pickled cucumber salad.

History

The name means "layered potatoes", and the dish is a staple of Hungarian family cooking, popular for Lent and weekday lunches. It uses humble ingredients that are always at hand.
