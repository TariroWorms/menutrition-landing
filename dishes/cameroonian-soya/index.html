Soya (Cameroonian grilled beef skewers)

Origin: Cameroon

Soya is thinly sliced beef rubbed with a spicy peanut mix and grilled on skewers over charcoal. It is smoky, peppery and nutty, served with raw onion, tomato and extra chili. It is a classic night-time street snack.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g beef sirloin
  • 80 g ground roasted peanuts
  • 1 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ginger, garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt
  • 1 onion, 2 tomatoes

Method

  1. Slice the beef thin.
  2. Mix peanuts with spices and salt.
  3. Rub the beef with oil and spice mix.
  4. Thread onto skewers and rest 30 minutes.
  5. Grill until browned and serve with onion and tomato.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean beef.
  • Brush with little oil.
  • Add more raw vegetables.

History

Soya is the Cameroonian cousin of suya found across West Africa, which originated among Hausa communities in the north. Roadside grills are everywhere at night.
