Boerewors (South African coiled sausage)

Origin: South Africa

Boerewors, meaning farmer's sausage, is a coarse, juicy sausage of beef mixed with pork or lamb and spiced with roasted coriander seed, black pepper, nutmeg, clove and a splash of vinegar. It is shaped into a long coil and grilled over coals, giving a smoky, savoury flavour. It is the star of the braai and is served with pap, chakalaka or in a roll as a boerie roll.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g coarse minced beef
  • 300 g minced pork belly
  • 2 tbsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp cloves
  • 50 ml vinegar
  • 20 g salt
  • sausage casings

Method

  1. Mix the beef and pork with spices, salt and vinegar.
  2. Chill for an hour so the flavours blend.
  3. Fill soaked natural casings loosely.
  4. Coil the sausage and secure it with skewers.
  5. Grill over medium coals for 20 minutes, turning once.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose a leaner mix and drain fat while grilling.
  • Serve with salad and grilled vegetables instead of bread.
  • Do not pierce the casing to keep the juices in.

History

The sausage descends from the sausages of the Dutch settlers at the Cape, and the spice blend shows Cape Malay influence. It is central to South African braai culture.
