Frango com quiabo (chicken with okra)

Origin: Brazil · Minas Gerais

Frango com quiabo is a home-cooked stew of bone-in chicken and okra in a light, golden sauce. The okra is often fried separately first so it stays firm and less slimy. It is traditionally eaten with soft angu (cornmeal polenta) or white rice.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 400 g okra, sliced
  • 1 onion, 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Parsley, spring onion, salt

Method

  1. Season the chicken with garlic and salt and brown it in oil.
  2. Add onion and tomato and cook until soft.
  3. Pour in a little water, cover and simmer for 35 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile fry the okra in a separate pan until it stops being sticky.
  5. Add the okra to the chicken, cook 10 minutes more and finish with herbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin before cooking.
  • Sauté the okra in a non-stick pan with little oil.
  • Serve with a small portion of angu and a big salad.

History

The dish is closely associated with Minas Gerais family cooking, and okra itself came to Brazil with enslaved Africans. It is often served at Sunday lunches and has been championed by well-known mineiro cooks.
