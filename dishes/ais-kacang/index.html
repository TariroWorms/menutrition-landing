Ais kacang (ABC shaved ice dessert)

Origin: Malaysia

Ais kacang, also called ABC, is a mountain of shaved ice covered in colourful syrups. Underneath are red beans, sweet corn, grass jelly, cendol and sometimes palm seed and peanuts. It is finished with evaporated or condensed milk and eaten quickly, before it melts.

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g shaved ice
  • 60 g cooked red beans
  • 40 g sweet corn
  • 40 g grass jelly cubes
  • 2 tbsp palm sugar syrup
  • 2 tbsp rose syrup
  • 3 tbsp evaporated milk
  • peanuts, optional

Method

  1. Place beans, corn and jelly in the bottom of a tall bowl.
  2. Pile the shaved ice into a dome on top.
  3. Drizzle with rose and palm sugar syrups.
  4. Pour over the evaporated milk.
  5. Serve at once with a long spoon.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less syrup and plain evaporated milk.
  • Add fresh fruit instead of extra syrup.
  • Share a bowl; it is mostly sugar.

History

The name means bean ice, and the dessert is found in kopitiams and food courts across Malaysia and Singapore. ABC stands for air batu campur, meaning mixed ice.
