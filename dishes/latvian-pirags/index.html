Pīrāgi (Latvian bacon and onion buns)

Origin: Latvia

Pīrāgi are small crescent-shaped buns of soft yeast dough wrapped around finely diced smoked bacon and onion. They come out of the oven glossy, with a juicy, salty centre and a slightly sweet crumb. They are eaten warm as a snack, with soup, or with a glass of kefir.

Recipe

8 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g plain flour
  • 250 ml warm milk
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 1 egg (60 g) plus 1 for glazing
  • 200 g smoked bacon, diced
  • 2 onions (250 g), chopped
  • 40 g butter, salt, sugar

Method

  1. Mix the yeast with warm milk and a spoon of sugar and let it foam.
  2. Knead with flour, egg, butter and salt into a soft dough; rest for an hour.
  3. Fry the bacon until just rendered, add the onion and cook until soft, then cool.
  4. Cut the dough into pieces, fill each with the mixture and shape into crescents.
  5. Rest 20 minutes, brush with beaten egg and bake at 200 C for 18-20 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean back bacon and less of it to cut the fat.
  • Swap half the flour for wholemeal to add fibre.
  • Bake rather than fry them and serve with a salad.

History

Pīrāgi are a staple of Latvian home baking and are traditionally made for celebrations and family gatherings. Bacon and onion is the classic filling, though many households also make versions with peas or cabbage.
