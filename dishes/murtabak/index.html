Murtabak (stuffed griddled flatbread)

Origin: Malaysia

Murtabak is a stuffed savoury pancake sold at mamak stalls. A thin, stretched dough is filled with spiced minced meat, egg and onion, folded into a square parcel and fried on a griddle until golden and crisp outside. It is cut into pieces and served with a thin curry gravy and pickled onions.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour, plus 1 tsp salt
  • 200 ml water
  • 300 g minced beef
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 onions
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 2 scallions
  • ghee or oil for frying

Method

  1. Knead flour, water and salt into a soft dough and rest, oiled, for 1 hour.
  2. Cook the beef with onion and curry powder; cool and mix with beaten eggs and scallions.
  3. Divide the dough into balls and stretch each very thin on an oiled surface.
  4. Spoon filling in the centre and fold the edges into a square.
  5. Fry in ghee until golden on both sides; cut and serve with curry sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean mince or chicken.
  • Fry in little oil on a non-stick pan.
  • Serve with salad and pickled onion instead of extra sauce.

History

The dish came to Southeast Asia with Muslim traders and cooks from the Indian subcontinent and the Arab world. It is now a staple of Malaysian, Singaporean and Indonesian night markets.
