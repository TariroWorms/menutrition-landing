🫒 Labneh with olive oil

Origin: Levant · Middle East

Labneh with olive oil is the simplest way to serve strained yogurt: a thick, tangy layer generously drizzled with good olive oil and finished with za'atar and dried mint. The oil softens the sharpness of the labneh and makes it rich and silky. It is a fixture of Levantine breakfast tables.

Recipe

4 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g labneh
  • 4 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp za'atar
  • ½ tsp dried mint

Method

  1. Spread the labneh on a plate in swirls.
  2. Drizzle generously with olive oil.
  3. Sprinkle with za'atar and mint and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Most of the calories come from the oil — each tablespoon is about 120 kcal, so drizzle lightly.
  • Use labneh from low-fat yogurt.
  • Pair it with vegetables and olives for a more filling plate.

History

Strained yogurt has been made in the Levant and the wider Middle East for a very long time as a way to keep milk longer. Today labneh is part of the classic breakfast and mezze table in Lebanon, Syria, Jordan and Palestine.
