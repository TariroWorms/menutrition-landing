Tilslørte bondepiker (veiled country lasses)

Origin: Norway

Tilslørte bondepiker is a layered dessert of soft apple compote, buttery toasted breadcrumbs and whipped cream. The contrast of crunchy crumbs, tart fruit and cool cream is what makes it appealing. It is served in a glass so the layers show.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g apples
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 100 g breadcrumbs
  • 40 g butter
  • 2 tbsp sugar for the crumbs
  • 200 ml whipped cream

Method

  1. Peel and slice the apples.
  2. Simmer with sugar and water until soft, then mash and cool.
  3. Melt the butter in a pan.
  4. Toast the breadcrumbs with sugar until golden and crisp.
  5. Cool the crumbs completely.
  6. Layer compote and crumbs in glasses.
  7. Top with whipped cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter and sugar for the crumbs.
  • Use wholegrain breadcrumbs for fibre.
  • Swap cream for thick yogurt for a lighter version.

History

The dessert is part of the older Scandinavian home-cooking tradition and uses ingredients every household had: apples, bread and cream. The name refers to the cream that veils the layers.
