Piyaju (lentil and onion fritters)

Origin: Bangladesh

Piyaju are small round fritters made from ground lentils mixed with chopped onion, green chilli, cilantro and spices, then deep-fried. They are crunchy outside and soft and grainy inside, with a gentle heat. They are eaten hot, often with chutney, and are a must-have on the Ramadan iftar table.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g moong or chana dal, soaked
  • 2 onions (200 g), finely chopped
  • 3 green chillies
  • cilantro, cumin, turmeric, salt
  • oil for frying

Method

  1. Drain the soaked dal and grind coarsely with a little water.
  2. Mix in onion, chillies, cilantro, spices and salt.
  3. Shape small balls or flat rounds.
  4. Heat oil to 175 °C and fry until deep golden.
  5. Drain on paper and serve hot with chutney.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry at 200 °C to cut the oil by half.
  • Add grated vegetables to increase fibre.
  • Serve with tamarind or mint chutney instead of rich sauces.

History

Piyaju is a classic Bangladeshi iftar snack found at stalls and in homes during Ramadan. It belongs to the broad South Asian family of lentil fritters.
