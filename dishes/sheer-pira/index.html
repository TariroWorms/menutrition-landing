Sheer pira (Afghan milk fudge)

Origin: Afghanistan

Sheer pira is a dense, fudge-like sweet made by cooking milk and milk powder with sugar and ghee until thick. It is scented with cardamom and topped with pistachios. It is sweet, creamy and rich, and is served in small squares with tea.

Recipe

12 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 200 g milk powder
  • 150 g sugar
  • 3 tbsp ghee (40 g)
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 40 g chopped pistachios

Method

  1. Simmer the milk with sugar in a heavy pan for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in the milk powder, ghee and cardamom.
  3. Cook on low heat, stirring constantly, for 15-20 minutes until the mass pulls from the sides.
  4. Press into a greased tray.
  5. Scatter pistachios on top and press in.
  6. Cool and cut into squares.

How to make it lighter

  • Eat one small square; it is very rich.
  • Cut the sugar by a third and use less ghee.
  • Serve with unsweetened tea.

History

Milk-based sweets cooked down slowly are part of South and Central Asian confectionery, with sheer pira served at celebrations and for guests in Afghan homes.
