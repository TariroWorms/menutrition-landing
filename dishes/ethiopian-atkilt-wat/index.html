Atkilt wat (Ethiopian cabbage and potato stew)

Origin: Ethiopia
150kcal

Atkilt wat is a gentle vegetable stew in which cabbage, potato and carrot are simmered with onion, garlic and turmeric. It is soft, lightly sweet and golden in color. It is a staple of fasting days and sits beside lentils and greens on the injera.

What it is made of

cabbagepotatocarrotonionturmeric

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cabbage
  • 300 g potato
  • 150 g carrot
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp turmeric, salt and pepper
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 150 ml water

Method

  1. Cook sliced onion in oil until soft.
  2. Add garlic and turmeric and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Add potatoes and carrots with water and cook 10 minutes.
  4. Add cabbage, cover and simmer 15 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and pepper and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce the oil to 1-2 tablespoons for a lighter dish.
  • Cut vegetables evenly so they cook together.
  • Add a pinch of berbere if you like some heat.

History

The dish belongs to the large family of Ethiopian fasting-day vegetable stews. It uses vegetables available through much of the year in the highlands and needs little more than oil and spices.
