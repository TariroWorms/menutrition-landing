🧋 Thai iced tea (cha yen)

Origin: Thailand

Thai iced tea is strong black tea brewed dark, sweetened with sugar and condensed milk, then poured over crushed ice. Its bold, slightly smoky tea flavour meets a creamy, caramel-like sweetness, and the drink is famous for its deep orange colour in street-stall versions. It is sold in tall glasses or plastic bags at markets and cafes across Thailand.

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 tsp loose black tea (or Thai tea mix)
  • 500 ml boiling water
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 4 tbsp sweetened condensed milk
  • Crushed ice

Method

  1. Steep the tea in boiling water for 5 minutes, then strain.
  2. Stir in the sugar and condensed milk while the tea is hot.
  3. Cool slightly, pour over glasses filled with crushed ice and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the added sugar; condensed milk already sweetens it.
  • Halve the condensed milk and use more ice for a lighter glass.
  • Order the small size and treat it as a dessert rather than a thirst-quencher.

History

Cha yen is a fixture of Thai street drink stalls, where tea is brewed through a cloth filter and sweetened to order. Its popularity has spread with Thai restaurants worldwide.
