Arroz de cuxá (Maranhão vinegar-leaf rice)

Origin: Brazil · Maranhão

Arroz de cuxá is the signature rice of São Luís in Maranhão. The cuxá is a paste of cooked vinagreira (roselle) leaves, ground toasted sesame, dried shrimp and seasonings, stirred into rice to give it a greenish color and a pleasantly sour taste. It is usually served with fried or grilled fish and seafood.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g rice
  • 200 g vinagreira leaves
  • 80 g dried shrimp, ground
  • 40 g sesame seeds, toasted and ground
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 20 ml oil
  • 700 ml water

Method

  1. Blanch the vinagreira leaves and chop them finely.
  2. Fry onion and garlic in oil until soft.
  3. Add leaves, shrimp and sesame; cook 5 minutes into a paste.
  4. Stir in the rice and toast briefly.
  5. Add water, season and simmer covered for 18 minutes until tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Rinse dried shrimp to reduce salt.
  • Use brown rice for more fiber.
  • Serve with grilled fish rather than fried for a lighter meal.

History

Cuxá is considered one of the most typical preparations of Maranhão cuisine, often linked to the mix of African, Indigenous and Portuguese influences there. Vinagreira, a hibiscus species, grows widely in the state's gardens.
