Roti john (egg and meat baguette omelette)

Origin: Malaysia

Roti john is a street-food sandwich cooked on a flat griddle. A split baguette is pressed onto a layer of beaten egg mixed with spiced minced meat and onion, fried until the egg sets and the bread turns crisp. It is finished with chili and mayonnaise sauces and cut into pieces.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 baguettes (about 300 g)
  • 300 g minced beef
  • 1 onion
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 2 tbsp chili sauce
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • oil, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Fry the onion and minced beef with curry powder until cooked.
  2. Beat the eggs and mix in the meat.
  3. Split the baguettes and cut into lengths that fit the pan.
  4. Pour the egg mixture onto the pan and press the bread cut side down.
  5. Fry until crisp, flip, cut up and drizzle with sauces.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean turkey mince.
  • Add vegetables to the egg mix.
  • Use less mayonnaise and wholemeal bread.

History

The dish is popular in Malaysia and Singapore and was supposedly created when a local cook adapted a European sandwich to the available ingredients. It is a typical late-night hawker food.
