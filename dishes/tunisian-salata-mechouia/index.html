Salata mechouia (Tunisian grilled salad)

Origin: Tunisia

Salata mechouia is a smoky Tunisian salad of grilled peppers and tomatoes chopped with garlic and olive oil, often with hot chili and caraway. It is usually topped with tuna, boiled egg and olives. It is served as an appetizer with bread.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 4 green peppers
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 hot chili
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp caraway
  • 1 can tuna
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • olives

Method

  1. Grill peppers, tomatoes and chili until blackened.
  2. Peel off the skins and remove the seeds.
  3. Chop the vegetables finely and mix with garlic and caraway.
  4. Dress with olive oil and salt.
  5. Top with tuna, egg and olives.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less olive oil.
  • Choose tuna in water.
  • Serve as a vegetable side without tuna for vegetarians.

History

The salad shows the Tunisian love of chili and grilled vegetables and is found at almost every table and restaurant. The charred skin gives its characteristic smoky taste.
