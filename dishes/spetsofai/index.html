Spetsofai is a rustic Greek stew of sliced country sausage cooked with sweet bell peppers, tomato, onion and chili. The peppers turn soft and sweet, the sausage releases its flavour into the sauce and the chili adds gentle heat. It is served hot with crusty bread or as part of a meze spread.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g country sausage, sliced
  • 3 bell peppers, sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 400 g tomatoes, chopped
  • 1–2 fresh chilies, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, oregano

Method

  1. Brown the sausage slices in the oil and set aside.
  2. Cook the onion and peppers in the same pan until soft.
  3. Add the tomatoes and chili and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Return the sausage, season with oregano and pepper and simmer 15 minutes more until thick.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a leaner sausage and more peppers.
  • Pour off the fat released when browning the sausage.
  • Eat it as a meze portion with salad rather than a large main.

History

Spetsofai is linked to the Pelion area of Thessaly in central Greece, where it was traditionally a simple farmhouse dish. Today it is found in tavernas throughout Greece.
