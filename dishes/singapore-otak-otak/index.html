Otak-otak

Origin: Singapore

Otak-otak is a mixture of fish, coconut milk, chili and herbs wrapped in banana leaf and grilled until set, firm and smoky. The inside is soft, spicy and fragrant. It is eaten as a snack or side with rice.

What it is made of

fishcoconut milkchili pepperlemongrassbanana leafegg

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g white fish fillet
  • 100 ml coconut milk
  • spice paste: 4 chilies, 3 shallots, 2 lemongrass, 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 egg, kaffir lime leaf
  • banana leaves

Method

  1. Blend the fish with the spice paste into a paste.
  2. Mix in coconut milk, egg and lime leaf.
  3. Spoon onto banana leaf pieces and fold into packets.
  4. Pin with toothpicks.
  5. Grill over medium heat 5 minutes per side.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk.
  • Steam instead of grilling.
  • Serve with cucumber.

History

The dish is a Malay and Peranakan speciality found across Singapore and Malaysia, with each region varying the spices and wrapping. The name means 'brains' in Malay, a reference to the soft texture.
