Khao poon (Lao curry noodle soup)

Origin: Laos

Khao poon is a bowl of fine rice vermicelli in a red, coconut-enriched chicken and lemongrass broth with a lively chili kick. It is topped at the table with shredded cabbage, bean sprouts, banana blossom and fresh herbs. The mix of hot soup and cool crunchy toppings is what makes it special.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g rice vermicelli, cooked
  • 300 g chicken, cooked and shredded
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp red curry paste
  • 2 stalks lemongrass
  • 500 ml chicken stock
  • Cabbage, bean sprouts and herbs to serve

Method

  1. Fry the curry paste with lemongrass in a little oil for 2 minutes.
  2. Add stock and bring to a simmer for 15 minutes.
  3. Stir in coconut milk and shredded chicken and warm through.
  4. Divide the noodles among bowls.
  5. Ladle over the broth and top with cabbage, sprouts and herbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk to lower the fat.
  • Load the bowl with more raw vegetables.
  • Season with lime rather than extra fish sauce.

History

The soup is a festival and family-gathering dish in Laos, where it is prepared in large pots to serve many guests. Recipes vary by region and by household.
