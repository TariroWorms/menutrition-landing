🥭 Mango Pomelo Sago

Origin: Hong Kong

Mango pomelo sago is a chilled Hong Kong dessert soup of blended mango and coconut milk, filled with chewy sago pearls, diced mango and bursting pomelo segments. It is sweet, fruity and lightly creamy, with a pleasant contrast between the soft pearls and the juicy citrus. It is served cold in a bowl or glass.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 60 g small sago pearls
  • 2 ripe mangoes (about 500 g flesh)
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 100 g pomelo segments, separated into small pieces
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 200 ml cold water

Method

  1. Boil the sago in plenty of water for 10 minutes, cover and leave for 10 minutes until clear; rinse under cold water.
  2. Dice a third of the mango and set aside.
  3. Blend the remaining mango with the coconut milk, water and sugar until smooth.
  4. Stir in the sago and chill for at least 1 hour.
  5. Serve topped with the diced mango and pomelo.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk or replace half of it with water.
  • Skip the sugar when the mangoes are very sweet.
  • Add more pomelo and less sago for a fresher, lighter bowl.

History

Mango pomelo sago is usually credited to a Hong Kong restaurant in the 1980s, where it was created as a dessert for a menu and quickly spread to dessert shops across the city. It is now a classic of Hong Kong dessert houses and Cantonese restaurants worldwide.
