Air-fried chicken

Air-fried chicken is chicken — usually thighs, drumsticks or wings — rubbed with spices and a little oil and cooked in an air fryer, where fast-moving hot air crisps the skin without deep-frying. The outside turns golden and crunchy while the meat stays juicy. It is served on its own with a salad, in wraps or bowls, or as a quick weeknight main.

What it is made of

chickenspicesolive oil

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thighs or drumsticks, bone in
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Lemon wedges and chopped parsley to serve

Method

  1. Pat the chicken very dry with paper towels — dry skin crisps best.
  2. Mix the oil with the paprika, garlic powder, cumin, pepper and salt and rub it all over the chicken.
  3. Preheat the air fryer to 200 °C. Arrange the chicken in one layer without crowding.
  4. Cook for 20–25 minutes, turning halfway, until golden and the thickest piece reaches 75 °C inside.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes and serve with lemon and parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Chicken breast or skinless thighs are leaner than skin-on pieces; much of the fat sits in the skin.
  • An oil spray or 1 tsp of oil is enough in an air fryer — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Pair it with a big salad or roasted vegetables instead of fries to keep the plate light.

History

Air fryers — compact countertop convection ovens — became popular home appliances in the 2010s, and spiced chicken quickly became one of the most cooked recipes in them. Today air-fried chicken is an everyday home dish in many countries.
