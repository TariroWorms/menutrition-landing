Njeguški cheese

Origin: Montenegro

Njeguški sir is a firm cow’s and sheep’s milk cheese from the village of Njeguši beneath Mount Lovćen. It is salty, creamy and slightly sharp, and is often kept in oil in a jar to mature. It is sliced for the meze board next to smoked ham, olives and bread.

Recipe

6 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g Njeguški or similar firm cheese
  • 100 ml olive oil
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • bread and olives to serve

Method

  1. Cut the cheese into cubes or wedges.
  2. Pack them into a clean jar with bay leaves and peppercorns.
  3. Cover completely with olive oil and close the jar.
  4. Keep in a cool place for at least a week.
  5. Serve with bread, olives and ham.

How to make it lighter

  • It is salty, so a small portion is enough.
  • Use a reduced-fat cheese for a lighter version.
  • Pair with cucumber and tomatoes rather than bread.

History

Njeguši is a small highland village known for its cured ham and cheese, and the cheese is made in households and small dairies. Keeping it in oil is a traditional way to preserve it for the winter.
