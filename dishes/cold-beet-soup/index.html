🥣 Cold beet soup (šaltibarščiai)

Origin: Lithuania · Baltic region

Cold beet soup, known in Lithuania as šaltibarščiai, is a vivid pink summer soup made of grated cooked beetroot stirred into chilled kefir with cucumber, dill and spring onion. It is cool, tangy and refreshing, topped with halves of hard-boiled egg. Traditionally it is served with hot boiled or roasted potatoes on the side.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 3 medium beetroots, cooked and peeled (about 400 g)
  • 1 litre kefir, well chilled
  • 2 cucumbers, diced or grated
  • 1 bunch dill, chopped
  • 3 spring onions, chopped
  • 2 eggs, hard-boiled
  • Salt, a little cold water

Method

  1. Grate the cooked beetroot or cut it into thin strips.
  2. Mix it with the cucumber, dill, spring onion and a pinch of salt and let it stand for 5 minutes.
  3. Pour in the cold kefir, stir until evenly pink and thin with a little cold water if you like.
  4. Chill for at least 15 minutes.
  5. Serve in bowls topped with halved eggs and extra dill.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat kefir — it keeps the same tang with fewer calories.
  • The soup itself is light; watch the side of potatoes and choose boiled over fried.
  • Add more cucumber and beetroot for a bigger, more filling bowl.

History

Chilled beet and soured-milk soups are eaten across Lithuania, Latvia, Poland and Belarus in summer. In Lithuania šaltibarščiai is a national favourite, celebrated each year at a pink soup festival in Vilnius.
