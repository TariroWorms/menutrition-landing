Molletes (bean and cheese open sandwiches)

Origin: Mexico

Molletes are split bolillo rolls, toasted with a little butter, spread with refried beans and baked under a layer of melted cheese. They are crunchy at the edges, soft and creamy in the middle, and finished with fresh pico de gallo. They make a cheap, quick breakfast or late-night snack.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 bolillo rolls
  • 300 g refried beans
  • 150 g Chihuahua or Oaxaca cheese, grated
  • 20 g butter
  • 150 g pico de gallo

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C and split the rolls lengthwise.
  2. Spread a little butter on the cut sides and toast them for 5 minutes.
  3. Spread each half generously with refried beans.
  4. Cover with grated cheese and bake until it melts and bubbles.
  5. Top with pico de gallo and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Scoop out some of the soft crumb to use less bread.
  • Use beans cooked without lard and a lighter cheese.
  • Pile on more pico de gallo and add avocado instead of extra cheese.

History

Molletes are a staple of Mexico City cafés and home kitchens and are often made with leftover bread. Popular variations add chorizo, bacon or mushrooms on top of the beans.
