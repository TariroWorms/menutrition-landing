Somlói galuska (Somló sponge trifle)

Origin: Hungary

Somlói galuska is a dessert of three kinds of sponge cake, cocoa-rum syrup, vanilla custard, walnuts and raisins, topped with chocolate sauce and cream. It is soft, sweet and indulgent with a little crunch from the nuts. It is served in a bowl and eaten with a spoon.

Recipe

8 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 3 sponge cake layers (plain, cocoa, walnut)
  • 200 ml vanilla custard
  • 60 g raisins
  • 60 g chopped walnuts
  • 100 g dark chocolate
  • 150 ml cream
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 2 tbsp sugar

Method

  1. Bake the three sponge types and cool them.
  2. Cut the sponges into pieces.
  3. Layer the pieces in a bowl with custard, raisins and walnuts.
  4. Moisten with cocoa-sugar syrup.
  5. Melt chocolate with a little cream for the sauce.
  6. Pour over the dessert and top with whipped cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less syrup and sugar.
  • Serve in small bowls.
  • Replace part of the cream with Greek yoghurt.

History

The dessert was created in the twentieth century by a Budapest restaurateur and is now a Hungarian restaurant classic. Its name refers to the Somló wine region, but it is not made with wine.
