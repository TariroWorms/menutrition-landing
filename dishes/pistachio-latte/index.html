☕ Pistachio Latte

A pistachio latte is espresso topped with steamed milk and sweetened with pistachio syrup, often finished with a sprinkle of chopped pistachios. It is creamy and mildly sweet, with a gentle nutty, almost marzipan-like flavour softening the coffee. It is served hot in a large cup or poured over ice in summer.

Recipe

1 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 double espresso (60 ml)
  • 250 ml milk
  • 20 ml pistachio syrup
  • 5 g pistachios, chopped

Method

  1. Pull the espresso into a large cup and stir in the pistachio syrup.
  2. Steam the milk until hot and glossy with a light foam.
  3. Pour the milk over the espresso, holding back the foam, then spoon the foam on top.
  4. Sprinkle with chopped pistachios and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the syrup; 10 ml still gives a clear pistachio flavour.
  • Use skimmed or semi-skimmed milk, the largest part of the drink.
  • Choose a small cup size and skip the pistachio topping on busy-calorie days.

History

The pistachio latte is a modern coffee-shop drink that spread widely in the 2010s and 2020s as cafés added flavoured lattes to their menus, and it is now a common seasonal and year-round option in many countries.
