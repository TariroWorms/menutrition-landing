🍚 Mexican arroz con leche

Mexican arroz con leche is a creamy rice pudding: rice first softened in water with a cinnamon stick, then simmered slowly in milk, sweetened with condensed milk and finished with vanilla and plump raisins. It is soft and spoonable, fragrant with cinnamon, and served warm or chilled with more ground cinnamon dusted on top.

Recipe

6 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g white rice
  • 500 ml water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 l milk
  • 200 ml condensed milk
  • 60 g raisins
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Ground cinnamon, to serve

Method

  1. Rinse the rice, then simmer it with the water and cinnamon stick for 10–12 minutes until the water is mostly absorbed.
  2. Add the milk and cook over low heat, stirring often, for 20–25 minutes until the rice is very soft and the mixture is creamy.
  3. Stir in the condensed milk and raisins and simmer 5 minutes more.
  4. Remove the cinnamon stick, stir in the vanilla and serve warm or chilled, dusted with ground cinnamon.

How to make it lighter

  • A 200 g bowl is about 290 kcal; a 150 g dessert cup is around 220 kcal.
  • Use semi-skimmed milk and half the condensed milk — the cinnamon and raisins keep it sweet and fragrant.
  • Serve it in small cups with fresh fruit on top rather than in a large bowl.

History

Rice puddings reached the Americas with the Spanish, and arroz con leche became a staple dessert across Latin America. The Mexican version is known for its use of true cinnamon sticks and, in many homes, condensed milk for extra richness.
