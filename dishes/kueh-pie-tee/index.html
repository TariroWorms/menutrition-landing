Kueh pie tee (Peranakan pastry cups)

Origin: Singapore · Peranakan

Kueh pie tee are thin, crisp little cups made from fried batter and filled just before eating. The filling is a soft, lightly sweet mix of braised jicama and carrot topped with shrimp and egg. Each cup is finished with a dab of chili sauce and eaten in one bite. It is a classic party snack in Peranakan homes.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g plain flour
  • 200 ml water, 1 egg
  • 300 g jicama, shredded
  • 100 g carrot, shredded
  • 150 g cooked shrimp (80 g chopped)
  • 2 tbsp chili sauce, oil for frying

Method

  1. Whisk flour, water, egg and salt into a thin batter.
  2. Heat the mould in oil, dip it in the batter and fry until golden.
  3. Release the cups and drain on paper.
  4. Sauté jicama and carrot with garlic until soft and season.
  5. Fill each cup with the vegetables, shrimp and a little chili sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the cups in a mini-muffin tin to save oil.
  • Use more vegetables and fewer cups per portion.
  • Choose a low-sugar chili sauce.

History

The dish belongs to Peranakan cooking, which blends Chinese and Malay influences in Singapore and Malaysia. The cups are traditionally made with a special brass mould dipped in batter and hot oil.
