A caffè latte is a shot of espresso topped up with a large amount of steamed milk and a thin layer of microfoam. Because it is mostly milk, it tastes mild, smooth and slightly sweet, with the coffee in the background. It is served in a tall glass or a wide cup and is one of the most ordered drinks in coffee shops.

Recipe

1 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 18 g finely ground espresso coffee
  • About 30 ml water (one espresso shot)
  • 250 ml milk

Method

  1. Brew the espresso into a large cup or glass.
  2. Steam or heat the milk to about 60–65 °C, whisking or frothing to create a light foam.
  3. Pour the milk into the espresso, holding back the foam with a spoon.
  4. Top with a thin layer of foam and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Ask for skimmed milk to cut fat while keeping the volume.
  • Order a small size — a tall latte is mostly milk, so size drives the calories.
  • Skip flavoured syrups and added sugar.

History

In Italy, caffè latte simply means coffee with milk and is traditionally a home breakfast drink. The espresso-bar version with steamed milk and foam spread through coffee shops in the US from the 1980s and from there worldwide.
