Ciorbă de burtă is Romania's famous sour tripe soup. Beef tripe is simmered for hours until tender, cut into thin strips and returned to a broth thickened with sour cream and egg yolk, then sharpened with vinegar and garlic. The result is creamy, tangy and rich. It is served hot with extra garlic sauce, vinegar and a fresh chilli to bite on the side.

Recipe

6 servings240 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg cleaned beef tripe
  • 1 carrot, 1 onion and 1 piece of celery root for the stock
  • 200 g sour cream
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 3–4 tbsp vinegar
  • Salt, fresh chilli peppers to serve

Method

  1. Simmer the tripe in salted water for 30 minutes, drain, then cover with fresh water and simmer with the vegetables for 3–4 hours until tender.
  2. Lift out the tripe and cut it into thin strips; strain the broth and return the tripe to it.
  3. Whisk the sour cream with the egg yolks, then whisk in a ladle of hot broth.
  4. Stir the mixture into the pot off the boil and heat gently without boiling.
  5. Season with garlic, vinegar and salt, and serve with fresh chillies.

How to make it lighter

  • Use part sour cream and part broth for the liaison to make a lighter soup.
  • Skim the fat from the chilled broth before finishing the soup.
  • A single bowl of the soup is a filling, moderate meal.

History

Tripe soups are common across the Balkans and Eastern Europe, and ciorbă de burtă is one of the best-known dishes of Romanian cooking. Today it is a staple of Romanian restaurants and is often eaten as a restorative dish after celebrations.
