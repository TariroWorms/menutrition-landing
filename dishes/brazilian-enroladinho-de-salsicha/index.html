Enroladinho de salsicha (Brazilian sausage roll)

Origin: Brazil

Enroladinho de salsicha is a frankfurter wrapped in a spiral of soft, slightly sweet bread dough, brushed with egg and baked, or sometimes fried. The outside is golden and tender, the inside juicy and salty. It is a favourite at children's birthday parties, school lunches and bakeries across Brazil, usually served in small pieces. Kids love its simple flavour.

Recipe

16 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour
  • 10 g dry yeast
  • 250 ml warm milk
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 50 g butter, soft
  • 1 egg + 1 yolk for brushing
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 8 frankfurters, halved

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt; add milk, egg and butter and knead 8 minutes.
  2. Let rise covered for 1 hour until doubled.
  3. Divide into 16 pieces and roll each into a long thin rope.
  4. Wrap each rope in a spiral around half a frankfurter.
  5. Rest 15 minutes, brush with yolk and bake at 190 °C for 20 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake rather than fry them.
  • Use chicken or turkey sausages with less fat.
  • Make them mini-sized and serve with carrot sticks.

History

It belongs to the large family of Brazilian salgados, snacks that fill party trays and bakery counters. Versions with ham and cheese are just as common.
