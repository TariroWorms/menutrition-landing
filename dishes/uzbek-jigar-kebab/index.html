Jigar kebab (lamb liver skewers)

Origin: Uzbekistan
330kcal

Jigar kebab threads cubes of fresh lamb liver onto skewers, alternating them with small pieces of tail fat or wrapping them in caul fat. Over the coals the fat melts into the liver, keeping it soft and adding a smoky richness. It is seasoned simply with salt, cumin and pepper and eaten hot with onion and bread.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb liver
  • 150 g tail fat or caul fat
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • black pepper
  • salt

Method

  1. Remove membranes from the liver and cut it into 3 cm cubes.
  2. Cut the fat into thin pieces.
  3. Thread liver and fat alternately onto skewers.
  4. Grill over hot coals for 6–8 minutes, turning often.
  5. Season with salt, cumin and pepper and serve with sliced onion.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less fat between the liver pieces.
  • Liver is very rich in vitamin A, so keep portions moderate.
  • Add grilled vegetables to the same skewers.

History

Liver kebab is a classic on Uzbek grill menus and is usually cooked quickly so that the inside stays pink and tender. It is often ordered alongside other kebabs as part of a mixed selection.
