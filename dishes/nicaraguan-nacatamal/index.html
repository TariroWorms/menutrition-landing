Nacatamal (Nicaraguan pork and rice masa tamale)

Origin: Nicaragua

Nacatamal is a large tamale made of masa seasoned with achiote and sour orange, filled with pork, rice, potato, tomato, onion and sometimes olives or mint. It is wrapped in plantain leaves and boiled for hours. It is a hearty weekend breakfast served with bread and coffee.

Recipe

8 servings300 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g masa harina
  • 300 g pork shoulder, cubed
  • 150 g rice, soaked
  • 2 potatoes, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tsp achiote
  • Juice of 1 sour orange
  • Plantain leaves
  • Salt, mint

Method

  1. Season the pork with achiote, sour orange and salt.
  2. Mix masa with achiote water and lard until soft.
  3. Spread masa on a leaf and add pork, rice, potato, tomato and onion.
  4. Top with more masa and wrap tightly in leaves.
  5. Tie into bundles.
  6. Boil 4 hours and unwrap to serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork and less lard.
  • Share one among two.
  • Pair with vegetable salad.

History

Nacatamales are a Nicaraguan tradition especially popular on weekends and holidays, when whole families prepare dozens. The dish descends from pre-Hispanic corn-and-meat tamales.
