🍗 Peruvian arroz con pollo with beer

Origin: Peru · Lima

This version of Peruvian arroz con pollo cooks chicken and rice in a cilantro purée loosened with dark beer, which adds a malty depth to the herbal green rice. Peas and diced carrot are stirred through for colour and sweetness. The result is soft, herby rice with tender pieces of chicken, traditionally served with lime and a sharp red-onion salsa.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken thighs or legs (about 800 g)
  • 350 g long-grain rice
  • 2 large bunches cilantro (about 100 g)
  • 200 ml dark beer
  • 150 g peas
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped, and 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, 1 tsp ground cumin, salt and pepper
  • 550 ml water

Method

  1. Season the chicken and brown it in the oil, then set aside.
  2. Blend the cilantro and garlic with 150 ml of the water until smooth.
  3. Fry the onion until soft, add the cilantro purée and cumin and cook for 3 minutes, then pour in the beer and let it bubble for 2 minutes.
  4. Return the chicken with the remaining water and simmer covered for 20 minutes.
  5. Add the rice, carrot and peas, season, cover and cook on low heat for 18–20 minutes. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Take the skin off the chicken to cut much of the fat.
  • Use 1 tablespoon of oil instead of 2 — each tablespoon is about 120 kcal.
  • Serve a smaller mound of rice and double the peas and carrot for more volume with fewer calories.

History

Chicken and rice arrived in Peru with Spanish cooking and became green there thanks to a generous cilantro purée. Many Peruvian cooks add a splash of dark beer to the cooking liquid, and the dish remains one of the country's best-loved family lunches.
