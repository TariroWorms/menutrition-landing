Bozartma (chicken stewed with onion and tomato)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Bozartma is a simple stew in which chicken or lamb is first browned and then cooked slowly with a large amount of onion and tomatoes. The onion melts into a thick, sweet sauce coloured with saffron and turmeric. Fresh herbs are scattered on top before serving. It goes well with plain rice or bread.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 4 onions
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 30 g butter
  • a pinch of saffron
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • cilantro, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Brown the chicken in the butter in a heavy pot.
  2. Add the sliced onions and cook covered on low heat for 15 minutes.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes, turmeric, salt and pepper.
  4. Simmer covered for 40 minutes until the chicken is very tender.
  5. Stir in the infused saffron and cook 5 minutes more.
  6. Sprinkle with cilantro and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin before browning.
  • Use a spoon of oil instead of butter.
  • Serve with salad or bulgur instead of a large portion of rice.

History

Bozartma is a traditional Azerbaijani way of stewing meat, known in both chicken and lamb versions. The name is linked to the browning of the meat before stewing.
