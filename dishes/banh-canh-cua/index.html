Bánh canh cua (crab thick noodle soup)

Origin: Vietnam · South

Bánh canh cua is a thick, silky soup of chewy tapioca-rice noodles in a crab broth, with crab meat, shrimp and quail eggs. It is warm and comforting, finished with pepper and herbs.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g thick tapioca-rice noodles
  • 600 ml crab and pork broth
  • 150 g crab meat
  • 100 g shrimp
  • 4 quail eggs
  • 2 tbsp tapioca starch
  • garlic, scallion, pepper

Method

  1. Prepare the crab and pork broth, simmering crab shells and pork bones for about an hour if making it from scratch.
  2. Season and thicken lightly with dissolved tapioca starch.
  3. Boil the noodles until chewy and translucent.
  4. Add crab meat, shrimp and quail eggs.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top with scallion and pepper.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less starch for a thinner broth.
  • Add leafy greens to the bowl.
  • Reduce salt and fish sauce.

History

Bánh canh means "soup cake", and the thick noodles are the hallmark of the dish. Versions with crab are popular across southern and central Vietnam.
