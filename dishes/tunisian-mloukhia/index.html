Tunisian mloukhia (dark lamb stew)

Origin: Tunisia

Mloukhia is a nearly black, glossy stew built on finely ground dried mallow leaves cooked for hours in olive oil with lamb. The flavor is deep, earthy and slightly bitter, with a velvety texture. It is served hot with crusty bread and often with a wedge of lemon.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 4 tbsp dried mallow powder
  • 1 large onion, grated
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 80 ml olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 700 ml water
  • Salt, to taste

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in some olive oil with the onion.
  2. Add garlic, coriander and salt, then cover with water and simmer 1 hour.
  3. Whisk the mallow powder with a little cold water into a smooth paste.
  4. Stir the paste into the pot with the remaining oil.
  5. Cook on very low heat for about 1.5 hours, stirring often, until the oil rises and the sauce is dark.
  6. Serve hot with bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim off excess oil at the end to cut fat.
  • Choose lean lamb cuts and trim visible fat.
  • Serve with whole-grain bread instead of white.

History

The dish is part of Tunisian and wider North African home cooking, where drying leaves for the winter was a common practice. It is typically a festive Sunday or holiday meal and is cooked patiently, since the sauce needs constant attention.
