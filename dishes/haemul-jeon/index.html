🥞 Haemul Jeon (Seafood Pancake)

Origin: South Korea

Haemul jeon is a Korean pan-fried pancake packed with mixed seafood such as squid, shrimp and mussels, bound with a simple flour-and-egg batter and plenty of scallion. It is crisp at the edges and tender inside, cut into wedges and shared at the table as a side dish or snack.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g mixed seafood (squid, shrimp, mussels), chopped
  • 100 g flour
  • 1 egg
  • 150 ml cold water
  • 1 bunch scallions, cut into 6 cm lengths
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, egg, cold water and a pinch of salt into a smooth batter.
  2. Stir in the seafood and scallions.
  3. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat and spread the mixture into a thin round.
  4. Fry 4–5 minutes until golden underneath, flip and cook another 4 minutes.
  5. Cut into wedges and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and just 1 tbsp oil; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Make the batter thinner and add more seafood and scallion per pancake.
  • Share one pancake between two as a side rather than a main.

History

Jeon, pan-fried dishes coated in flour and egg, have long been part of Korean holiday and ancestral-rite tables. Seafood versions are especially associated with coastal areas and are a common rainy-day dish in Korea today.
