🥗 Bún bò Nam Bộ

Origin: Vietnam · Hanoi

Bún bò Nam Bộ is a Vietnamese rice-vermicelli salad bowl: cool rice vermicelli topped with beef stir-fried with garlic, roasted peanuts and plenty of fresh herbs, dressed with a sweet-sour fish sauce and lime dressing. It is tangy, nutty and fresh, with warm meat over cold vermicelli. It is mixed at the table just before eating.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g rice vermicelli
  • 400 g beef sirloin, thinly sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 60 g roasted peanuts, crushed
  • A large handful each of mint, Thai basil and lettuce
  • 4 tbsp fish sauce, 2 tbsp sugar, juice of 2 limes, 120 ml water
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Cook the vermicelli, rinse in cold water and drain well.
  2. Stir the fish sauce, sugar, lime juice and water into a dressing.
  3. Stir-fry the beef with garlic in very hot oil for 2 minutes.
  4. Fill bowls with lettuce, herbs and vermicelli; top with the beef and peanuts.
  5. Spoon over the dressing and mix before eating.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp of peanuts per bowl — about 8 g, enough for crunch.
  • Swap part of the vermicelli for shredded lettuce and cucumber.
  • Make the dressing with less sugar and more lime.

History

Despite its name, which means 'southern-style beef vermicelli', the dish is usually said to have been created in Hanoi, in northern Vietnam. It is a popular lunch in Hanoi and is now found throughout the country.
