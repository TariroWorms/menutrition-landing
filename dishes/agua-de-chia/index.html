Agua de chía con limón (lime chia water)

Origin: Mexico

Agua de chía is a simple lime agua fresca with chia seeds stirred in and left to swell. The seeds form tiny jelly-like beads that give the drink a pleasant texture against the sharp lime. It is sold at markets and taquerías and is seen as a refreshing, slightly more filling choice.

What it is made of

limechia seedssugarwater

Recipe

6 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 l cold water
  • Juice of 6 limes
  • 80 g sugar
  • 3 tbsp chia seeds (about 30 g)
  • Ice

Method

  1. Stir the chia seeds into 500 ml of the water and leave for 15 minutes, stirring now and then.
  2. Mix the rest of the water with the lime juice and sugar until dissolved.
  3. Combine with the soaked chia.
  4. Chill for at least 30 minutes so the seeds swell fully.
  5. Stir well and serve over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar to 40 g or use a little honey.
  • Chia adds fiber and some omega-3 fats, so it is more satisfying than plain limeade.
  • Drink plenty of water as well when you eat a lot of chia.

History

Chia is native to Mexico and Central America and was an important crop long before the Spanish arrived. Its use faded for centuries, but it remained in drinks like this one and has become popular again worldwide.
