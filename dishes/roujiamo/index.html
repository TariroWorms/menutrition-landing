Roujiamo (Chinese pork flatbread sandwich)

Origin: China · Shaanxi

Roujiamo is a small round flatbread split open and stuffed with chopped pork that has been braised until meltingly tender in soy sauce and spices. The bread is crisp outside and soft inside, and the meat is tender and juicy. Chopped chilies or cilantro are often added.

Recipe

4 servings130 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork belly or shoulder
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 star anise and 1 stick of cinnamon
  • 1 piece of ginger
  • 2 green chilies and a bunch of cilantro
  • 4 flatbread buns (mo)

Method

  1. Boil the pork for 5 minutes and rinse it.
  2. Simmer with soy sauce, spices, ginger and water for 1.5 hours until very tender.
  3. Chop the meat finely with some of the braising liquid.
  4. Split the toasted flatbreads open.
  5. Fill with the pork and top with chilies and cilantro.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean pork shoulder and remove visible fat.
  • Add more cilantro and fresh vegetables.
  • Eat half a sandwich with soup as a lighter meal.

History

The dish is from Shaanxi and is often called a Chinese hamburger by visitors. It is widely seen as one of the oldest meat sandwiches in the world, with roots in the region around Xi'an.
