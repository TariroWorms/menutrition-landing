🍗 Hainanese Chicken Rice

Hainanese chicken rice pairs gently poached, silky chicken with rice cooked in the chicken broth with garlic and ginger, so each grain is fragrant and savory. It is served at room temperature with cucumber slices, a drizzle of soy sauce and a ginger or chili sauce on the side, often with a small bowl of the broth.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (about 1.4 kg)
  • 300 g jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 5 cm ginger, sliced, plus 1 tbsp grated
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt
  • Scallions to garnish

Method

  1. Rub the chicken with salt, place in a pot with sliced ginger and 2 cloves garlic, cover with water and bring to a simmer.
  2. Poach gently for 40 minutes, then plunge the chicken into iced water.
  3. Fry the remaining garlic and grated ginger in the oil, add the rice and stir for 2 minutes.
  4. Cook the rice with 450 ml of the poaching broth until tender.
  5. Carve the chicken and serve over the rice with cucumber, soy sauce and scallions.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin before serving to cut fat noticeably.
  • Skim the fat off the broth before cooking the rice.
  • Take a smaller rice portion and add more cucumber on the side.

History

The dish was brought to Southeast Asia by migrants from Hainan island in southern China, who adapted their home-style poached chicken. It became a hawker-centre staple in Singapore and Malaysia, and is widely regarded as a national dish of Singapore.
