Mate gelado (Rio iced mate)

Origin: Brazil · Rio de Janeiro

Mate gelado is a cold tea brewed from toasted yerba mate leaves, sweetened and often mixed with lemonade. It is light, earthy and refreshing with a citrus edge. On Rio's beaches vendors carry it in metal tanks on their backs and pour it to order, often alongside the cookie Biscoito Globo.

What it is made of

yerba matelemonsugar

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 30 g toasted yerba mate (chá mate)
  • 1 l water
  • 60 g sugar
  • 100 ml fresh lemon juice
  • ice

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil and remove from heat.
  2. Add the mate and steep for 5 minutes.
  3. Strain and stir in the sugar while hot.
  4. Cool, then chill in the fridge.
  5. Mix with lemon juice and serve over plenty of ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the sugar or use a sweetener; the lemon keeps it lively.
  • Mate contains caffeine, so go easy late in the day.
  • Unsweetened mate with lemon is almost calorie-free.

History

Toasted mate became popular in Rio in the twentieth century, and selling it on the sand is a well-known part of carioca beach culture. The 'mate com limão' served by beach vendors is considered a cultural icon of the city.
