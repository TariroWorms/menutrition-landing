🥟 Lamb ouzi in phyllo

Origin: Syria · Levant

Ouzi in phyllo is a baked parcel of crisp pastry wrapped around rice spiced with baharat, tender lamb, green peas, carrots, raisins and toasted almonds. Each portion is shaped into a dome or bundle and baked until the shell is golden and flaky. Inside, the filling is fragrant, sweet-savoury and rich, and it is often served with salads or pickles.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 12 sheets phyllo pastry
  • 300 g long-grain rice
  • 400 g lamb, diced small
  • 150 g green peas
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 40 g raisins
  • 50 g almonds, toasted
  • 2 tsp baharat, 1 tsp cinnamon, salt
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in 1 tbsp oil, season with spices and salt, add water and simmer for 40 minutes until tender.
  2. Add the rice, carrot and enough water to cook it through, about 15 minutes; stir in the peas, raisins and almonds.
  3. Line small bowls with 2 crossed phyllo sheets brushed lightly with oil.
  4. Fill with the rice mixture, fold the pastry over and turn the parcels out onto a tray, seam down.
  5. Brush the tops lightly with oil and bake at 190 °C for 20–25 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush the phyllo with a thin film of oil — each tablespoon adds about 120 kcal.
  • Use lean lamb and cut the almonds and raisins by half.
  • One parcel with a big green salad makes a full plate.

History

Phyllo-wrapped ouzi is especially associated with Syrian and Lebanese celebration cooking, where individual pastry parcels became a party-friendly version of the large roast lamb and rice platter served at weddings and holidays.
