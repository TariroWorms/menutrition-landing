Lawar (Balinese chopped vegetable and coconut salad)

Origin: Indonesia · Bali
195kcal

Lawar is a Balinese mixture of finely chopped green beans or jackfruit, grated coconut and minced meat, bound with a fragrant spice paste. It is fresh, crunchy and spicy, with lime and shrimp paste notes. In some versions blood is added, while other versions are mostly vegetable. It is eaten with rice as part of a festive plate, often next to babi guling.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g green beans, blanched
  • 80 g grated coconut, toasted
  • 100 g cooked minced pork or chicken
  • 4 shallots, 3 garlic cloves, 3 chilies
  • 1 tsp shrimp paste
  • lime juice, salt

Method

  1. Chop the green beans finely.
  2. Grind shallots, garlic, chilies and shrimp paste into a paste.
  3. Fry the paste lightly.
  4. Mix with beans, coconut and meat.
  5. Add lime juice and salt; serve fresh.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less coconut or lean meat.
  • Make a vegetarian version with extra beans.
  • Serve alongside plenty of rice and vegetables.

History

Lawar is central to Balinese feasts, where groups of men traditionally prepare it together. It is part of the traditional communal food of ceremonies.
