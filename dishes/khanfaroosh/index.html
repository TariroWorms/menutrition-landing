🍩 Khanfaroosh

Khanfaroosh is a small Emirati fritter made from a saffron- and cardamom-scented batter of ground rice, egg and sugar. Spoonfuls are fried until golden, giving a crisp shell and a light, airy inside. It is served warm with Arabic coffee or tea, especially at breakfast and during Ramadan and Eid gatherings.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g ground rice
  • 2 eggs
  • 80 g sugar
  • 1 pinch saffron, soaked in 60 ml warm water
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar until pale.
  2. Stir in the saffron water, cardamom and ground rice; add water until the batter is thick but droppable.
  3. Rest the batter for 15 minutes.
  4. Heat oil to about 170 °C and fry spoonfuls until golden on both sides.
  5. Drain on paper towels and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Two or three pieces are a typical serving; count them before sitting down with coffee.
  • Draining well on paper towels removes surface oil.
  • Pair with unsweetened Arabic coffee instead of sweet tea.

History

Khanfaroosh is a traditional sweet of the UAE and neighbouring Gulf countries. It is still made at home and sold at heritage festivals across the Emirates.
