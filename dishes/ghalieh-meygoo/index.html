Ghalieh meygoo (Persian Gulf shrimp stew)

Origin: Iran · Persian Gulf

Ghalieh meygoo is a spicy, sour shrimp stew from the Persian Gulf coast. Shrimp are cooked briefly in a sauce of fried cilantro, garlic, tamarind and chili. It is bold and fragrant, and best served over plain white rice.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g peeled shrimp
  • 1 onion, chopped (120 g)
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 100 g chopped cilantro
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chili, salt
  • 300 ml water

Method

  1. Fry the onion in oil until golden; add garlic and turmeric.
  2. Add the cilantro and fry until deep green.
  3. Stir in tamarind, chili, salt and water; simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Add the shrimp.
  5. Cook 5 minutes until just pink and opaque; do not overcook.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and keep the sauce herb-heavy.
  • Shrimp cook fast; add them last to keep them tender.
  • Serve with a small portion of rice and salad.

History

Southern Iranian ghalieh stews combine local seafood with tamarind and fresh herbs. The shrimp version is especially popular in Bushehr, Hormozgan and Khuzestan.
