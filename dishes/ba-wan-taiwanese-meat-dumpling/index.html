Ba-wan (Taiwanese meat dumpling)

Origin: Taiwan · Changhua
350kcal

Ba-wan is a round, translucent dumpling with a soft, chewy wrapper made from starch and a filling of pork, bamboo shoots and mushrooms. It is steamed or fried gently and served with a sweet and mildly savory red sauce. People eat it as a street snack or a light meal with a bowl of soup.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g sweet potato starch
  • 50 g rice flour
  • 300 g pork, diced
  • 80 g bamboo shoots
  • 4 dried shiitake, soaked
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce

Method

  1. Mix the starches with hot water to make a smooth sticky dough.
  2. Stir-fry the pork, bamboo shoots and mushrooms with soy sauce and let cool.
  3. Press a layer of dough into a small cup, add filling and cover with more dough.
  4. Steam the dumplings for 20 minutes until translucent.
  5. Serve with a sauce of soy, sugar and a little thickened chili paste.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork and plenty of bamboo shoots.
  • Steam rather than fry to keep the dish lighter.
  • Go easy on the sweet sauce.

History

Ba-wan is usually linked with Changhua in central Taiwan, where it is a local specialty. It is one of the best-known Taiwanese snacks, and the clear starch skin is its defining feature.
