Tagliatelle ai funghi are ribbon pasta tossed with mushrooms sautéed with garlic, bound in a light cream sauce and finished with chopped parsley. The mushrooms bring an earthy, savoury depth while the cream makes the sauce smooth and mild. It is a comforting vegetarian first course found on menus throughout Italy, especially in autumn.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 160 g dried tagliatelle
  • 300 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 100 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the tagliatelle in salted water until al dente; keep some cooking water.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan and sauté the mushrooms over high heat until golden.
  3. Add the garlic for 1 minute, then pour in the cream and simmer for 2 minutes.
  4. Toss in the pasta with a splash of cooking water, season and finish with parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Double the mushrooms and reduce the pasta: mushrooms add volume for very few calories.
  • Use half the cream and thin the sauce with pasta water.
  • Sauté in a non-stick pan so 1 tbsp of oil is enough; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Pasta with mushrooms is eaten across Italy, with porcini versions popular in the mountain and forest regions during the autumn mushroom season.
