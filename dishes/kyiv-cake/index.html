Kyiv cake (Kyivskyi tort)

Origin: Ukraine · Kyiv
430kcal

Kyiv cake is the capital's most famous dessert: two airy, crunchy layers of meringue baked with hazelnuts or cashews, joined by a rich buttercream that is partly flavored with cocoa. The top is piped with cream flowers and the sides are covered in crushed nuts. It is very sweet, chewy inside and crisp outside, and is cut into small pieces with tea.

What it is made of

egg whitehazelnutssugarbuttermilkcocoaflour

Recipe

10 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 5 egg whites
  • 250 g sugar
  • 150 g roasted hazelnuts
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 200 g butter
  • 150 ml milk
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 100 g sugar for cream
  • 1 tbsp cocoa

Method

  1. Whisk egg whites to firm peaks, adding sugar gradually.
  2. Fold in chopped hazelnuts mixed with flour.
  3. Spread into two 22 cm circles on baking paper.
  4. Bake at 120 °C for about 2 hours and leave to dry in the oven.
  5. Cook milk, yolk and sugar into a thick custard, cool, then beat with butter; flavor part with cocoa.
  6. Sandwich the layers with cream, decorate the top and coat the sides with nuts.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve very small slices; it is calorie-dense.
  • Reduce the buttercream layer and add fresh berries.
  • Make mini meringue nests instead of a whole cake for portion control.

History

The cake was created at the Karl Marx confectionery factory in Kyiv in the 1950s and soon became a symbol of the city, often brought home as a gift by visitors. The original factory recipe remains a trade secret, so home versions vary.
