Tarator (Bulgarian cold yogurt soup)

Origin: Bulgaria

Tarator is a cold summer soup of yogurt thinned with water and filled with diced cucumber, garlic, dill and ground walnuts. It is cooling, tangy and garlicky with a nutty finish. Bulgarians serve it in a bowl or glass, often with a drizzle of sunflower oil.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g plain yogurt
  • 300 ml cold water
  • 2 cucumbers, diced (about 400 g)
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 3 tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 50 g walnuts, ground
  • 1 tbsp sunflower oil
  • salt

Method

  1. Stir the yogurt and cold water until smooth.
  2. Dice the cucumbers and add them to the bowl.
  3. Add crushed garlic, dill and a pinch of salt.
  4. Stir in most of the walnuts.
  5. Chill for at least 30 minutes.
  6. Serve cold with a drizzle of oil and the remaining walnuts on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt for a lighter soup.
  • Reduce the oil and keep the walnuts for healthy fats.
  • Add ice cubes instead of extra oil to keep it refreshing.

History

Cold yogurt and cucumber soups appear across the Balkans and Anatolia, each with its own name. Tarator is the Bulgarian version and is a staple of hot summer days, usually eaten alongside fried fish or grilled meat.
