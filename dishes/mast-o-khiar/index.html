Mast-o-khiar is a cool Persian yogurt dish with diced cucumber, dried mint, chopped walnuts and raisins. It is creamy, crunchy and lightly sweet-salty all at once. It is served chilled as a side to rice dishes and kebabs, or eaten as a light snack, sometimes thinned with water into a summer soup.

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g plain yogurt
  • 2 cucumbers (about 300 g), finely diced
  • 1 tbsp dried mint
  • 30 g walnuts, chopped
  • 30 g raisins
  • ½ tsp salt

Method

  1. Soak the raisins in warm water for 10 minutes and drain.
  2. Stir the yogurt until smooth.
  3. Mix in cucumber, mint, salt, raisins and most of the walnuts.
  4. Chill for at least 30 minutes and top with the remaining walnuts to serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt for a lighter bowl with the same texture.
  • Increase the cucumber and halve the raisins to cut sugar.
  • Keep walnuts to about 5–8 g per serving as a topping.

History

Yogurt-and-cucumber dishes are eaten across Iran, Turkey and the Caucasus, and mast-o-khiar is a fixture of the Iranian table today, especially in summer. Its precise origin is not recorded.
