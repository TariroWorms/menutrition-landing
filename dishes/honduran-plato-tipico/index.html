Plato típico hondureño (Honduran typical plate)

Origin: Honduras

The plato típico is the classic Honduran combination plate. It brings together grilled beef, refried red beans, fried ripe plantain, rice, fresh cheese, cream and a little pickled cabbage. Tortillas are served alongside, and everything is eaten together.

Recipe

2 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g beef steak
  • 200 g refried red beans
  • 2 ripe plantains
  • 200 g cooked rice
  • 100 g fresh cheese
  • 60 g sour cream
  • Pickled cabbage
  • 4 tortillas

Method

  1. Season and grill the beef.
  2. Heat the refried beans.
  3. Fry plantain slices until caramelised.
  4. Arrange everything on a large plate.
  5. Add cheese, cream and cabbage.
  6. Serve with tortillas.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean beef and limit the fried plantain.
  • Use brown rice for fibre.
  • Go lighter on cheese and cream.

History

It is considered the national plate of Honduras and is served in restaurants across the country. Each component shows a core ingredient of Honduran cooking: beans, plantain, corn and cattle products.
