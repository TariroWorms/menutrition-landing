Pašticada (Dalmatian braised beef)

Origin: Croatia · Dalmatia

Pašticada is Dalmatia's festive beef dish: a large cut of beef studded with garlic and cloves, marinated in vinegar overnight, then braised slowly with vegetables, prunes and wine until the sauce turns dark and glossy. The sauce is sweet-sour and deeply savoury, and it is spooned over homemade gnocchi. It is cooked for weddings and holidays.

Recipe

8 servings300 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg beef round or topside
  • 100 ml vinegar
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 6 cloves
  • 2 carrots, 200 g
  • 2 onions, 250 g
  • 80 g prunes
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 200 ml red wine or broth
  • 1 kg gnocchi, cooked

Method

  1. Stud the beef with garlic and cloves and marinate in vinegar overnight.
  2. Brown the meat in oil in a heavy pot.
  3. Add the onion, carrot, prunes, tomato paste and the wine or broth.
  4. Cover and braise very slowly for 3.5 hours, turning the meat occasionally.
  5. Lift out the meat and blend the sauce until smooth.
  6. Slice the beef and return it to the sauce for 10 minutes.
  7. Serve over gnocchi.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim fat from the sauce before serving.
  • Use leaner beef and fewer prunes for less sugar.
  • Serve with fewer gnocchi and more vegetables.

History

The dish is closely tied to the Dalmatian coast and its long contact with Italian cooking, with a name close to Italian stracotto and pasticcio. It is traditionally a dish for celebration because of the long preparation.
