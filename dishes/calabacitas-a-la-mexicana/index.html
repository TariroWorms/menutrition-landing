Calabacitas a la mexicana

Origin: Mexico

Calabacitas is a simple vegetable sauté of diced zucchini-type squash, corn kernels, tomato and onion, often finished with crumbled queso fresco. It is light, sweet from the corn and only mildly spicy. It works as a side for grilled meat, a taco filling or a light vegetarian lunch with tortillas.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g Mexican squash or zucchini, diced
  • 200 g corn kernels
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 jalapeño (optional)
  • 60 g queso fresco
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Soften the onion and garlic in the oil.
  2. Add the corn and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Stir in the squash and jalapeño and cook 5 minutes.
  4. Add the tomatoes, salt, cover and cook 5 more minutes.
  5. Top with crumbled queso fresco before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the cheese for a vegan version, or use less of it.
  • Add black beans to turn it into a full meal.
  • Keep the squash slightly crisp to keep more texture and nutrients.

History

Squash and corn are two of the oldest crops of Mesoamerica and have been cooked together for a very long time. Calabacitas remains one of the most common vegetable dishes in Mexican homes and in northern New Mexico.
