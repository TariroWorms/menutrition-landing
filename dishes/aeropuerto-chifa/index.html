Aeropuerto (chifa fried rice with noodles)

Origin: Peru · Chifa

Aeropuerto, literally “airport”, is a big plate where arroz chaufa and stir-fried noodles meet in the same wok with chicken, omelette strips, bean sprouts and spring onion. Some versions are topped with a fried egg. It is filling, cheap and very popular with students and workers.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g cold cooked rice
  • 250 g egg noodles, boiled
  • 300 g chicken, diced
  • 3 eggs
  • 150 g bean sprouts
  • 5 spring onions
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Make a thin omelette, cut into strips and set aside.
  2. Stir-fry the chicken with ginger in a very hot wok.
  3. Add rice and toss until hot and slightly toasted.
  4. Add the noodles, bean sprouts and soy sauce and toss well.
  5. Fold in omelette, spring onion and sesame oil.
  6. Serve hot, optionally with a fried egg on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more bean sprouts and fewer noodles.
  • Skip the extra fried egg.
  • Use low-sodium soy sauce.

History

It is a modern chifa creation popular in Lima; the playful name is often explained by the idea that “everything lands” on one plate. Recipes vary widely between restaurants.
