🍝 Orecchiette with sausage and broccoli rabe

Origin: Italy · Apulia and Campania

This southern Italian pasta tosses orecchiette with crumbled sausage and friarielli (broccoli rabe), sautéed with garlic and chilli. The slightly bitter greens balance the rich, savoury sausage, and the ear-shaped pasta catches the crumbs and juices. It is a hearty, rustic dish served hot.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g dried orecchiette
  • 250 g sausage, casing removed
  • 500 g broccoli rabe (friarielli), trimmed
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 fresh chilli or 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Blanch the broccoli rabe in salted boiling water for 3 minutes, lift out and chop. Keep the water.
  2. Brown the crumbled sausage in 1 tbsp oil for 6–8 minutes.
  3. Add the remaining oil, garlic and chilli, then the greens, and sauté for 5 minutes.
  4. Cook the orecchiette in the greens' water until al dente.
  5. Drain and toss with the sausage and greens, adding a splash of cooking water.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 150 g sausage instead of 250 g and more greens.
  • Pour off the fat the sausage releases before adding the garlic.
  • Choose a leaner poultry sausage to lower the fat.

History

Sausage with friarielli is a classic pairing from Naples and Campania, while orecchiette come from neighbouring Apulia. Bringing them together is a popular combination in southern Italian home and trattoria cooking.
