Baku pakhlava (Azerbaijani walnut and saffron pastry)

Origin: Azerbaijan · Baku

Baku pakhlava is made from thin sheets of yeasted dough layered with a filling of ground walnuts, sugar and cardamom, then cut into diamonds. Each piece is topped with a nut half and brushed with saffron and egg yolk, giving it a deep amber colour. After baking it is soaked with butter and honey syrup, so it is very sweet, fragrant and dense.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour
  • 200 ml warm milk
  • 1 egg plus 1 yolk
  • 5 g dry yeast
  • 400 g walnuts, ground
  • 300 g sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 150 g butter
  • A pinch of saffron
  • 150 g honey and 100 ml water for syrup

Method

  1. Make a soft dough from flour, milk, egg, yeast and a pinch of salt; let it rise for 1 hour.
  2. Mix ground walnuts, sugar and cardamom for the filling.
  3. Divide dough into 10 pieces and roll each into a very thin sheet the size of your tray.
  4. Layer sheets and filling in a buttered tray, finishing with a sheet, then cut into diamonds and top each with a walnut half.
  5. Brush with saffron water mixed with egg yolk and bake at 180 °C for about 45 minutes.
  6. Pour melted butter and warm honey syrup over the hot pakhlava and let it soak for several hours.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut very small diamonds: one piece is plenty with tea.
  • Reduce the syrup by a third; the walnuts and saffron carry the flavour.
  • Keep it for festive days rather than everyday dessert.

History

Pakhlava is the essential sweet of Novruz, the spring new-year holiday, in Azerbaijan, and its diamond shape is often linked to stars or fire in popular explanation. The Baku style is distinguished from Sheki pakhlava, which is a completely different rice-flour sweet.
