Crossing the bridge noodles (guoqiao mixian)

Recipe

2 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g rice noodles
  • 1 l strong chicken broth
  • 100 g raw chicken or pork, thinly sliced
  • 100 g fish slices
  • 2 quail eggs
  • 100 g greens
  • 50 g tofu skin
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Bring the broth to a rolling boil and pour into a deep, heated bowl.
  2. Float a thin layer of oil on top to hold in heat.
  3. Slide in the eggs first, then the meat and fish slices.
  4. Add the greens and tofu skin.
  5. Add the noodles last and stir briefly.
  6. Top with scallion and eat at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Slice meat very thinly so it cooks safely in the hot broth.
  • Use only a thin film of oil.
  • Fill the bowl with extra vegetables.

History

The name comes from a story of a scholar's wife who carried food across a bridge to her husband, keeping it hot with a layer of oil; it is a popular tale rather than documented history. The dish is now a symbol of Yunnan.
