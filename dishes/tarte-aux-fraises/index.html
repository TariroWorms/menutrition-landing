🍓 Strawberry tart

Tarte aux fraises is a French tart of crisp shortcrust filled with vanilla pastry cream and covered with rows of fresh strawberries. The fruit is often brushed with a light sugar glaze to make it shine. It is creamy, juicy and fresh, and is a classic of late spring and early summer, served chilled.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain flour, 100 g cold butter, 1 egg yolk, 2 tbsp cold water
  • 500 ml milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 80 g sugar plus 1 tbsp for the glaze
  • 40 g cornflour
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 500 g strawberries, hulled and halved

Method

  1. Make the pastry, chill for 30 minutes, line a 22 cm tin and blind-bake at 180 °C for 20–25 minutes.
  2. Heat the milk; whisk the yolks, sugar and cornflour, pour in the hot milk, return to the pan and stir until thick. Add vanilla and cool.
  3. Spread the cooled pastry cream into the tart case.
  4. Arrange the strawberries on top in tight rows.
  5. Warm 1 tbsp sugar with 2 tbsp water and brush it over the fruit; chill before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Make the pastry cream with semi-skimmed milk.
  • Use a thinner layer of cream and more strawberries.
  • Cut 10 slices instead of 8 for a smaller portion.

History

Fresh fruit tarts on crème pâtissière are a long-standing part of French pâtisserie, and the strawberry version is the best-known seasonal example. It is sold in bakeries across France when local strawberries are in season.
