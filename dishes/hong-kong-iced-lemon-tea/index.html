Hong Kong iced lemon tea

Origin: Hong Kong

Strong black tea is cooled over ice and loaded with thin slices of fresh lemon and a spoon of syrup. It is refreshing, bright and slightly bitter. Diners press the lemon with a spoon to release more flavor.

What it is made of

tealemonsugarwater

Recipe

1 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 2 black tea bags, 250 ml hot water
  • 1 lemon, thinly sliced
  • 1-2 tbsp sugar syrup
  • ice cubes

Method

  1. Brew the tea for 5 minutes and remove bags.
  2. Stir in syrup while still warm.
  3. Fill a tall glass with ice and lemon slices.
  4. Pour the tea over and stir.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the amount of syrup.
  • Use a zero-calorie sweetener instead of syrup.
  • Add more lemon for flavor instead of sugar.

History

It is a standard drink of the Hong Kong cha chaan teng, the local tea-and-toast cafés. The lemon slices are meant to be pressed rather than left whole.
