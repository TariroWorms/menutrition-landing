Ube halaya (purple yam jam)

Origin: Philippines

Ube halaya is a thick, smooth purple spread made from boiled and mashed purple yam cooked slowly with milk, coconut milk and sugar. It is sweet and creamy with a subtle vanilla-nutty flavor and a vivid violet color. It is eaten as a dessert, on bread, or used in cakes and halo-halo.

Recipe

10 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g purple yam
  • 300 ml coconut milk
  • 200 ml milk
  • 150 g sugar
  • 50 g butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Boil, peel and mash the purple yam until very smooth.
  2. Heat coconut milk and milk with sugar.
  3. Add the yam and stir continuously over low heat.
  4. Add the butter and vanilla and cook 30 minutes until very thick.
  5. Cool in a dish and chill.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by a third.
  • Use plant milk and plant-based butter for a vegan jam.
  • Serve a small scoop with fruit.

History

Ube, the purple yam, has long been grown in the Philippines and is the base of many sweets. Halaya is traditionally prepared for fiestas and Christmas, stirred for a long time in a big pan.
