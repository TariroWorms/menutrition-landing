🍝 Trofie al Pesto Genovese

Origin: Italy · Liguria, Genoa

Trofie al pesto genovese pairs Liguria's hand-rolled twisted pasta with raw basil pesto made from fresh basil, pine nuts, garlic, parmesan and olive oil. The pesto is never cooked, only tossed with the hot pasta, so it stays bright green and fragrant. The texture is chewy and the flavour herby, nutty and garlicky.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g trofie
  • 50 g fresh basil leaves
  • 30 g pine nuts
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 50 g parmesan, grated
  • 80 ml olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Pound or blend the garlic, pine nuts and a pinch of salt.
  2. Add the basil and work it briefly, then mix in the parmesan and olive oil.
  3. Boil the trofie in salted water until al dente.
  4. Drain, keeping a little cooking water, and toss the pasta off the heat with the pesto, loosening with the water.

How to make it lighter

  • Use about 25 g pesto per plate; 1 tbsp olive oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Loosen the pesto with pasta water instead of extra oil.
  • Add steamed green beans or courgette to bulk up the plate without much energy.

History

Trofie are traditionally made in the Recco and Camogli area east of Genoa, and pesto genovese is the region's signature sauce. Basil-based pesto in its modern form is documented in Ligurian cookbooks from the 19th century.
