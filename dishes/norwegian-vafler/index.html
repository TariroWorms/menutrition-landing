Norwegian vafler (heart-shaped waffles)

Origin: Norway
290kcal

Norwegian vafler are thin, soft waffles baked in a five-heart iron, flavoured with cardamom. They are eaten warm, folded or in triangles, topped with jam, sour cream or brown cheese. They are tender rather than crisp, with a buttery, lightly sweet taste. Vafler are a coffee-time treat and are served at family gatherings and fundraising events.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 3 eggs
  • 500 ml milk
  • 100 g melted butter
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp cardamom
  • 1 tsp baking powder

Method

  1. Whisk flour, sugar, cardamom and baking powder together.
  2. Whisk in the eggs and milk to make a smooth batter.
  3. Stir in the melted butter and rest 20 minutes.
  4. Cook the batter in a hot waffle iron until golden.
  5. Serve with jam and sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar and add fresh berries.
  • Replace some of the flour with wholemeal.
  • Use yogurt instead of sour cream as topping.

History

Waffles with heart-shaped patterns have been made in Scandinavia for centuries. In Norway, a waffle afternoon is a social tradition, and the dish is also eaten on Norway's national day.
