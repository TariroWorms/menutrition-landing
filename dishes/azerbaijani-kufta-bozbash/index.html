Kufta bozbash (meatball soup)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Kufta bozbash is a clear, golden soup with fist-sized lamb meatballs, chickpeas and chunks of potato. Each meatball is mixed with rice and herbs and usually has a dried sour plum tucked in the centre. The broth is tinted with saffron and finished with dried mint. It is served in deep bowls, often with bread and fresh herbs on the side.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced lamb
  • 2 onions
  • 60 g rice
  • 150 g chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 3 potatoes
  • 8 dried sour plums (alycha)
  • a pinch of saffron
  • 1 tsp dried mint, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Simmer the soaked chickpeas in 2 litres of water for about an hour.
  2. Grate one onion and mix it with the lamb, rice, salt and pepper.
  3. Shape large balls, pressing a dried plum into the middle of each.
  4. Add the meatballs to the chickpea broth and simmer gently for 30 minutes.
  5. Add potato chunks and the second onion, sliced, and cook 20 minutes more.
  6. Stir in saffron infused in a little hot water and season.
  7. Serve sprinkled with dried mint.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim the fat from the chilled broth before reheating.
  • Use lean lamb or mix it half and half with beef.
  • Add extra vegetables instead of some of the potatoes.

History

Bozbash is one of the best-known soups of Azerbaijani home cooking, with many regional variants. The meatball version is a festive and everyday favourite alike, and similar soups are made across the Caucasus.
