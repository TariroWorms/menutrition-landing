Fuži with truffles

Origin: Croatia · Istria

Fuži are hand-rolled pasta squares folded into short quills, a staple of Istria. Here they are tossed in a creamy butter and cheese sauce and finished with shavings of fresh truffle. The dish is earthy, rich and aromatic.

What it is made of

pastatrufflecreamparmesanbutter

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g fuži or penne
  • 60 g butter
  • 150 ml cream
  • 50 g parmesan
  • 20 g fresh truffle
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Boil the pasta in salted water until al dente.
  2. Melt butter in a wide pan.
  3. Add cream and simmer briefly.
  4. Stir in half of the parmesan.
  5. Toss in the drained pasta with a little cooking water.
  6. Serve topped with shaved truffle and cheese.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace some cream with pasta water.
  • Use whole-wheat pasta for fibre.
  • A small amount of truffle is enough for the flavour.

History

Istria is known for its white and black truffles, which are foraged in the forests of the Mirna valley. Fuži are typically made for weddings and feasts, often with game or truffles.
