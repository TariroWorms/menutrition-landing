🍚 Bulgur pilavı

Bulgur pilavı is Turkey's everyday bulgur pilaf: coarse cracked wheat simmered with sautéed onion, green peppers and tomato until each grain is fluffy and separate. It has a nutty, savoury taste with a gentle tomato tang. It is served as a side with grilled meat, stews and beans, or on its own with a spoon of pickles.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g coarse bulgur
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 green peppers, chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, grated, or 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 600 ml hot water
  • Salt, black pepper

Method

  1. Soften the onion and peppers in the oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tomato and cook for 2–3 minutes.
  3. Stir in the bulgur, salt and pepper, then pour in the hot water.
  4. Cover and simmer on low heat for 15 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  5. Rest covered under a cloth for 10 minutes, then fluff and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp oil instead of 2 — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Double the peppers and tomato for more volume per portion.
  • Serve a 150–200 g portion alongside a big salad or lean grilled meat.

History

Bulgur has been eaten in Anatolia and the Middle East for thousands of years, and bulgur pilaf is a cornerstone of Turkish home cooking, especially in the south-east and in rural kitchens. It is often preferred to rice as a cheaper, more filling everyday grain.
