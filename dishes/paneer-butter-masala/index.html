🍛 Paneer Butter Masala

Paneer butter masala is a rich North Indian curry of soft paneer cubes in a smooth tomato sauce enriched with butter, cream and ground cashews. Dried fenugreek leaves add a distinctive, slightly bitter aroma that balances the mild sweetness of the gravy. It is creamy and gently spiced, and is served with rice or flatbreads.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g paneer, cubed
  • 30 g butter
  • 400 g tomatoes, chopped
  • 40 g cashews, soaked
  • 100 ml cream
  • 1 tsp dried fenugreek leaves
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Simmer the tomatoes and soaked cashews with a splash of water for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth.
  2. Melt the butter, add chili powder and garam masala, and pour in the tomato-cashew purée.
  3. Simmer 10 minutes until slightly thickened; season with salt.
  4. Add the paneer and cream and heat gently for 5 minutes.
  5. Crush the fenugreek leaves over the top and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the butter and cream; the cashews still give a creamy sauce. 1 tbsp butter ≈ 100 kcal.
  • Use fewer paneer cubes and add steamed peas or bell pepper for volume.
  • Serve with a measured portion of plain rice instead of buttered flatbread.

History

The dish is a vegetarian sibling of butter chicken, a style of tomato-butter gravy usually credited to Punjabi restaurant cooks in Delhi in the mid-20th century. It is now a staple of Indian restaurant menus worldwide.
