🥦 Beef and broccoli

Origin: United States · Chinese-American cuisine

Beef and broccoli is a Chinese-American stir-fry of thin slices of tender beef and bright green broccoli florets tossed in a glossy brown sauce of soy sauce, oyster sauce, garlic and ginger. The beef is silky, the broccoli keeps a little crunch and the sauce is savoury with a gentle sweetness. It is served hot over steamed rice.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g flank or sirloin steak, thinly sliced across the grain
  • 500 g broccoli, cut into florets
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced, and 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp cornflour mixed with 120 ml water
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

  1. Toss the beef with 1 tbsp of the soy sauce and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the remaining soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar and cornflour water in a bowl.
  3. Blanch the broccoli in boiling water for 1 minute, drain well.
  4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok until smoking and sear the beef in batches for 1–2 minutes. Set aside.
  5. Add the remaining oil, fry the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, then add the broccoli and stir-fry for 1 minute.
  6. Return the beef, pour in the sauce and toss until it thickens and coats everything. Serve over rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Double the broccoli and halve the rice for a lighter, more filling plate.
  • Use 1 tbsp oil in a non-stick wok — each tablespoon is about 120 kcal.
  • Choose lean sirloin and trim visible fat before slicing.

History

Beef and broccoli is usually described as an American adaptation of Cantonese stir-fried beef with gai lan, Chinese broccoli, made with the broccoli that was easier to find in the United States. It became one of the most familiar dishes on Chinese-American restaurant and takeaway menus.
