🌶️ Htipiti (Greek Feta and Roasted Pepper Dip)

Origin: Greece

Htipiti is a Greek meze dip of roasted red peppers blended with feta, garlic, olive oil and a little chili. It is smoky, salty and tangy with a gentle heat, and its texture can be smooth or slightly chunky. It is served cold as part of a meze spread, with grilled meat or vegetables.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 3 red bell peppers
  • 200 g feta
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 small red chili or ½ tsp chili flakes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp vinegar
  • Pinch of dried oregano

Method

  1. Roast the peppers under a hot grill or over a flame until blackened, then cover for 10 minutes.
  2. Peel, deseed and roughly chop the peppers.
  3. Crumble the feta and blend or mash it with the peppers, garlic, chili, olive oil and vinegar.
  4. Leave a little texture, taste and adjust the chili; sprinkle with oregano.
  5. Chill for 30 minutes and serve with vegetables or grilled meat.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more roasted pepper and less feta for a lighter dip with the same colour.
  • Cut the olive oil to 1 tbsp; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Scoop it with cucumber, carrot or pepper sticks to keep a meze portion light.

History

Htipiti is especially associated with northern Greece, notably Thessaloniki, where it is a common meze in ouzeries and tavernas. The name is usually linked to a Greek word for 'beaten', referring to how the feta is mashed.
