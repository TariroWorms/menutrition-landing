Uyghur polo

Origin: China · Xinjiang

Polo is the Uyghur rice pilaf: lamb and onion are browned in oil, then carrots, rice and water are layered over and steamed until the grains are tender and separate. The carrots turn sweet and golden, with raisins adding small bursts of sweetness. It is the standard festive dish of Xinjiang.

What it is made of

Recipe

5 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g rice
  • 500 g lamb with bone
  • 500 g carrots, julienned
  • 2 onions
  • 80 g raisins
  • 100 ml oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • salt

Method

  1. Heat oil, brown the lamb and onions well.
  2. Add carrots and fry 5 minutes.
  3. Add 500 ml hot water and salt, simmer 30 minutes.
  4. Spread the rinsed rice on top and add water just above the rice.
  5. Cover, cook gently until the water is absorbed.
  6. Place raisins on top, steam 15 minutes, then mix and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the oil by a third.
  • Use lean lamb and serve a smaller meat portion.
  • Serve with a fresh tomato and onion salad.

History

Polo is the Uyghur form of the pilaf eaten across Central Asia and Iran. In Xinjiang it is served at weddings, festivals and when guests arrive.
