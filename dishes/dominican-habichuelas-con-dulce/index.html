Habichuelas con dulce (sweet bean cream)

Origin: Dominican Republic
260kcal

Habichuelas con dulce is a thick sweet cream made from red beans blended with coconut milk, evaporated milk, sugar and warm spices. It is served chilled or lukewarm with cubes of sweet potato, raisins and crunchy cookies called galletas de leche on top. The flavour is mellow, creamy and lightly spiced with cinnamon and cloves.

Recipe

8 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried red beans
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 200 ml evaporated milk
  • 150 g sugar
  • 200 g sweet potato, diced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cloves
  • 50 g raisins
  • milk crackers to serve

Method

  1. Boil the beans until very soft and blend them with their liquid into a smooth cream.
  2. Return to the pot and add the coconut milk, evaporated milk, sugar, cinnamon and cloves.
  3. Simmer 30 minutes, stirring often.
  4. Add the diced sweet potato and raisins and cook 20 minutes until soft.
  5. Remove the cinnamon and cloves.
  6. Serve warm or chilled topped with crushed crackers.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce the sugar by a third.
  • Use light coconut milk and skip the evaporated milk for a lighter version.
  • Serve in small bowls.

History

It is the traditional dessert of Lent and Holy Week in the Dominican Republic, shared with neighbours and relatives. Sweetened beans combine Spanish, African and local ingredients.
