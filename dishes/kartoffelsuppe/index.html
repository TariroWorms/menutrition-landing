🍲 Kartoffelsuppe (German potato soup)

Kartoffelsuppe is a thick German potato soup simmered with leek and onion, flavoured with smoky bacon and nutmeg and finished with a little cream. Part of the soup is mashed or blended so it becomes velvety while keeping some soft chunks of potato. It is a warming everyday lunch, especially in autumn and winter.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg floury potatoes, diced
  • 80 g smoked bacon, diced
  • 1 leek, sliced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1.2 litres water
  • 100 ml cream
  • Salt, black pepper, nutmeg, chopped parsley

Method

  1. Fry the bacon in a large pot until crisp; lift out half for garnish.
  2. Add the onion and leek and soften for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the potatoes and water, season and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Mash or blend about half of the soup, stir in the cream and nutmeg and heat through.
  5. Serve topped with the reserved bacon and parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the bacon and use it only as a crisp topping.
  • Use 50 ml cream, or skip it and blend more of the potatoes for creaminess.
  • Add carrots or celery to stretch the pot with fewer calories per bowl.

History

Potato soup became a staple across Germany once potatoes were widely grown from the 18th century. Every region has its own version, and it remains a fixture of home cooking and canteen menus.
