Suancai jiaozi (sour cabbage dumplings)

Origin: China · Dongbei

These dumplings are filled with minced pork and chopped fermented napa cabbage, which adds a pleasant tang that cuts through the richness of the meat. They are usually boiled and dipped in vinegar, soy sauce or chili oil. They are a favorite winter dish in the northeast.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 180 ml water
  • 300 g minced pork
  • 200 g fermented napa cabbage
  • 1 tbsp ginger and scallions, chopped
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Method

  1. Knead the flour with water into a smooth dough and rest it for 30 minutes.
  2. Squeeze the chopped cabbage dry and mix with pork, ginger, scallion and sesame oil.
  3. Roll the dough into thin rounds.
  4. Place a spoonful of filling on each and pleat the edges closed.
  5. Boil in water for 6-7 minutes until they float, then serve with vinegar.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork or add mushrooms to the filling.
  • Steam instead of pan-frying leftovers.
  • Go easy on the dipping sauce to limit sodium.

History

Dumplings are a staple of northern China, and in the northeast the long winters made fermented cabbage a natural filling. Families often make large batches together for the cold season.
