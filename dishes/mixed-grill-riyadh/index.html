🍢 Mixed grill (Riyadh style)

Riyadh-style mixed grill is a restaurant platter of chicken shish tawook, lamb kofta and lamb tikka grilled over charcoal and laid on flatbread with grilled tomatoes. It comes with a creamy garlic sauce for dipping. The meats are juicy and smoky, and the bread underneath soaks up the juices, making it a generous meal often shared by two or more.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast, cubed, marinated in lemon, garlic and paprika
  • 400 g minced lamb mixed with onion and parsley (kofta)
  • 400 g lamb leg, cubed, marinated in tikka spices
  • 4 tomatoes, halved
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt
  • 4 flatbreads
  • Garlic sauce, to serve

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken and lamb cubes for at least 1 hour.
  2. Shape the kofta mixture onto flat skewers and thread the other meats onto skewers.
  3. Grill over charcoal or a grill pan, turning, until cooked through.
  4. Grill the tomatoes until soft.
  5. Lay the bread on a platter, top with the meats and tomatoes and serve with garlic sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Go heavier on the tawook and lighter on kofta, which is the fattiest skewer.
  • Garlic sauce is calorie-dense: keep to about one tablespoon.
  • Add a large salad and eat less of the bread underneath.

History

Grill restaurants serving Levantine and Turkish-style skewers are a big part of dining out in Riyadh and across Saudi Arabia, especially for late-evening family meals.
