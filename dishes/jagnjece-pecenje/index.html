🍖 Roast Lamb (Jagnjeće Pečenje)

Jagnjeće pečenje is Balkan roast lamb: a leg or shoulder rubbed with garlic, rosemary and salt and slowly roasted with potatoes until the meat is tender and the skin crisp. The potatoes cook in the lamb juices and turn golden and soft. It is a festive dish, served hot in generous pieces with fresh salad.

What it is made of

lambgarlicrosemarypotato

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1.8 kg lamb leg or shoulder, bone in
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 3 sprigs rosemary
  • 1 kg potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 250 ml water
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Make small cuts in the lamb and push in slivers of garlic and rosemary needles; rub with salt, pepper and half the oil.
  2. Place in a roasting tin with the water, cover with foil and roast at 170 °C for 1.5 hours.
  3. Add the potatoes tossed with the remaining oil and salt around the meat.
  4. Uncover and roast at 200 °C for another 50–60 minutes, basting, until the lamb is browned and the potatoes are golden.
  5. Rest the lamb for 10 minutes, then carve and serve with the potatoes.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the outer fat before roasting and skim fat from the pan juices.
  • Serve a larger share of salad and fewer potatoes.
  • Leg is leaner than shoulder.

History

Roast lamb, often whole and spit-roasted, is a traditional centrepiece of celebrations, weddings and holidays in Serbia and across the former Yugoslavia. Oven-roasted cuts with potatoes are the everyday home version.
