Babi guling (Balinese spit-roasted pig)

Origin: Indonesia · Bali

Babi guling is a whole young pig stuffed with a turmeric-rich spice paste and slowly roasted over a fire until the skin is glassy and crisp. The meat inside is juicy and fragrant with lemongrass, galangal, garlic and chili. It is served as slices of meat and skin with rice, lawar, blood sausage and a spicy sambal. It is a festive dish in Bali and a favorite at street food stalls.

What it is made of

porkturmericlemongrassgalangalgarlicchili pepper

Recipe

8 servings240 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork belly and shoulder
  • 8 shallots, 6 garlic cloves
  • 4 cm turmeric, 3 cm galangal, 2 lemongrass stalks
  • 6 chilies, 1 tsp coriander seeds
  • salt, oil
  • cassava leaves

Method

  1. Grind the aromatics and spices into a paste and fry briefly.
  2. Rub it into the meat and wrap around lemongrass and cassava leaves.
  3. Tie the meat into a roll.
  4. Roast at 170 C for 3 hours, basting with the juices.
  5. Raise the heat at the end to crisp the skin and slice.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin and visible fat.
  • Serve with lawar and vegetables.
  • Eat a small portion of meat only.

History

Babi guling is tied to Balinese ceremonies, where roast pig is offered at feasts. Today it is also sold daily in warungs, especially in Ubud and Gianyar.
