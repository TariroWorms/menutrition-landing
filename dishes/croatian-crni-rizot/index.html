Crni rižot (Dalmatian black risotto)

Origin: Croatia · Dalmatia

Crni rižot is a glossy black risotto flavoured with cuttlefish and its ink, gently cooked with onion, garlic and olive oil. The rice is creamy, tinted jet black, and tastes of the sea with a mild sweetness from the cuttlefish. It is a signature dish of the Dalmatian coast, finished with parsley and often served with lemon.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g cleaned cuttlefish
  • 300 g arborio rice
  • 1 onion, 100 g
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 60 ml olive oil
  • 2 sachets cuttlefish ink, 8 g
  • 1 l fish stock
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Slice the cuttlefish into thin strips.
  2. Soften the onion and garlic in olive oil.
  3. Add the cuttlefish and cook gently for 15 minutes with a little stock.
  4. Stir in the rice and toast for 1 minute.
  5. Add the ink and ladle in the stock a little at a time, stirring for 18 minutes.
  6. Season with salt and pepper and rest 2 minutes.
  7. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and more stock for a lighter risotto.
  • Add peas or spinach for extra vegetables.
  • Serve a modest portion with a green salad.

History

Cuttlefish and rice were brought together on the Adriatic, where Venetian and Dalmatian cooking have long exchanged ideas. Black risotto is now served in konobas all along the Croatian coast.
