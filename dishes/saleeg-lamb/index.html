🍚 Lamb saleeg

Lamb saleeg is the meat version of the Hejazi milk rice: short-grain rice simmered in lamb broth and milk until creamy, scented with cardamom and a little mastic, and served under tender boiled lamb. A spoon of melted butter is poured on top before serving. It is rich, soft and gently spiced, and is a dish for family gatherings.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg lamb on the bone, in pieces
  • 500 g short-grain rice, rinsed
  • 1 l milk
  • 2 l water
  • 5 cardamom pods, 2 bay leaves
  • 2–3 mastic grains, crushed with a pinch of sugar
  • 1 onion, halved
  • 40 g butter, salt

Method

  1. Simmer the lamb with the onion, cardamom, bay leaves and salt for about 1 1/2 hours until tender; strain and keep the broth.
  2. Cook the rice in about 1.2 l of broth, stirring, until most of it is absorbed.
  3. Add the milk gradually and the mastic, stirring over low heat until thick and creamy.
  4. Melt the butter.
  5. Serve the rice on a platter with the lamb on top and the butter poured over.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the visible fat from the lamb and skim the broth well.
  • Use only half the butter on top: 10 g butter ≈ 72 kcal.
  • Take more lamb and less rice to keep the portion higher in protein.

History

Saleeg belongs to the cooking of the Hejaz region of Saudi Arabia, around Taif, Mecca and Jeddah, where versions with lamb are served at family meals and occasions.
