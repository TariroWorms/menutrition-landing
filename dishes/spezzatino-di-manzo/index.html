Spezzatino di manzo is an Italian beef stew of chunks of beef braised slowly with carrot, celery, potato and tomato in red wine. Long cooking makes the meat tender and the sauce rich and slightly thick. It is served hot as a hearty main course, often on its own as a one-pot meal.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g stewing beef, cubed
  • 2 carrots, sliced
  • 2 celery sticks, sliced
  • 500 g potatoes, cubed
  • 400 g tomatoes, chopped
  • 150 ml red wine
  • 1 onion, 2 tbsp olive oil, rosemary, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Brown the beef in the oil in batches.
  2. Add the onion, carrot and celery and cook until soft.
  3. Pour in the wine and let it bubble for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the tomatoes and a little water, cover and simmer for 1.5 hours.
  5. Add the potatoes and rosemary and cook 30 minutes more until tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the beef before cooking.
  • Add extra carrots and celery instead of more potato.
  • Skim the fat from the surface before serving.

History

Spezzatino, from spezzare, 'to break into pieces', is a home-style stew cooked in many regional versions across Italy. It remains a typical family dish for cold months.
