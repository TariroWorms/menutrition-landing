Kletski (Belarusian dumplings)

Origin: Belarus

Kletski are small, soft dumplings made from mashed or grated potato bound with flour and egg. They are dropped into boiling salted water and cooked until they float. Served in a bowl with a generous spoonful of crackling salo and golden onion, they are simple, filling home food.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes
  • 120 g wheat flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100 g salo (cured pork fatback)
  • 1 onion
  • salt and black pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Boil half the potatoes and mash them; finely grate the rest and squeeze out the liquid.
  2. Combine both potatoes with egg, flour and salt into a soft, sticky dough.
  3. With a wet spoon, drop walnut-size pieces into boiling salted water.
  4. Cook for 5–7 minutes after they rise to the surface, then lift out with a slotted spoon.
  5. Meanwhile fry the diced salo until crisp, add chopped onion and cook until golden.
  6. Pour the salo and onion over the kletski and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Top with a little sour cream and dill instead of salo for a lighter bowl.
  • Add grated zucchini to the dough to stretch it with vegetables.
  • Serve a moderate portion with a big green salad.

History

Kletski are everyday peasant fare known throughout Belarus, Lithuania and Poland under similar names. Families made them from whatever was at hand, so recipes range from pure flour dough to almost all potato.
