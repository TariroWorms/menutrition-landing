Jocón

Origin: Guatemala

Jocón is a Guatemalan stew of chicken simmered in a thick green sauce made from tomatillos, cilantro, green onions and toasted seeds. The sauce is tangy, herby and slightly nutty. It is served with white rice and warm tortillas.

Recipe

6 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken (1.3 kg), cut up
  • 500 g tomatillos
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 4 green onions
  • 80 g pumpkin seeds
  • 30 g sesame seeds
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tortilla
  • salt

Method

  1. Poach the chicken with salt until cooked and keep the broth.
  2. Toast the seeds and tortilla.
  3. Boil the tomatillos until soft.
  4. Blend with cilantro, onions, garlic and seeds, adding broth.
  5. Simmer the sauce with the chicken for 20 minutes.
  6. Season and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin from the chicken.
  • Use fewer seeds for a lighter sauce.
  • Serve with extra vegetables.

History

Jocón is a traditional dish from Guatemala's Indigenous and rural cooking, where ground seeds and herbs make the sauce. It is a common family meal and a typical lunch in many regions.
