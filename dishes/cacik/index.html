🥒 Cacık

Origin: Türkiye

Cacık is a cool Turkish dish of thick yogurt mixed with finely chopped or grated cucumber, crushed garlic, dill and dried mint, finished with a drizzle of olive oil. It can be thick enough to serve as a dip or thinned with cold water into a refreshing soup, and it is a classic partner for grilled meats, rice and kebabs in summer.

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g plain yogurt
  • 1 large cucumber (about 300 g)
  • 1–2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill
  • 1 tsp dried mint
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • Cold water to thin (optional)

Method

  1. Grate or finely dice the cucumber, salt lightly and squeeze out excess water.
  2. Whisk the yogurt with the garlic and a pinch of salt until smooth.
  3. Stir in the cucumber, dill and half the mint; thin with cold water if you want a soup.
  4. Chill, then serve with the olive oil and remaining mint on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt for a lighter version with the same tang.
  • Skip or halve the olive oil drizzle — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve it as a side instead of a creamy sauce on grilled meat — it adds volume for few calories.

History

Cacık is eaten all over Türkiye and is a close relative of Greek tzatziki and other yogurt-and-cucumber dishes across the Balkans and the Middle East.
