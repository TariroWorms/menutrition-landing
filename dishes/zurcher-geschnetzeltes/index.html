🍽️ Zürcher Geschnetzeltes with Rösti

Origin: Switzerland · Zurich

Zürcher Geschnetzeltes is thinly sliced veal quickly sautéed and finished in a sauce of mushrooms, onion, white wine and cream. The veal stays tender and the sauce is smooth and rich. In Zurich it is almost always served with rösti, a crisp golden cake of grated potato fried in a pan.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g veal, cut into thin strips
  • 250 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 100 ml dry white wine
  • 150 ml cream
  • 800 g potatoes, parboiled and grated, for rösti
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Salt, pepper, parsley

Method

  1. For the rösti, season the grated potatoes and fry them in 2 tbsp oil as a flat cake, about 10 minutes per side.
  2. Sear the veal strips in batches in 1 tbsp oil for 1–2 minutes and set aside.
  3. Sauté the onion and mushrooms until soft.
  4. Add the wine and reduce by half, then add the cream and simmer 3 minutes.
  5. Return the veal just to warm through, season and sprinkle with parsley; serve with the rösti.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace part of the cream with a splash of the veal cooking juices and water.
  • Make the rösti in a non-stick pan with less oil (1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal).
  • Add a green salad and serve a smaller wedge of rösti.

History

The dish is named after Zurich and is regarded as a signature of the city's cuisine. Serving it with rösti is the classic presentation in Swiss restaurants today.
