Aveluk salad (sautéed dried sorrel)

Origin: Armenia
225kcal

This warm or cold salad turns braided dried sorrel into a dark, silky dish. The soaked leaves are boiled briefly, squeezed and fried with plenty of onion, then tossed with garlic and chopped walnuts. It tastes sour, savoury and nutty, a little like cooked spinach with lemon. It is served as a starter or side with lavash and cheese.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 80 g dried aveluk
  • 2 onions (250 g)
  • 3 tbsp sunflower oil (45 ml)
  • 50 g walnuts, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • salt, black pepper

Method

  1. Soak the sorrel 30 minutes in warm water, then rinse several times.
  2. Boil it for 10 minutes, drain, squeeze and cut into short pieces.
  3. Fry the sliced onion in oil until soft and golden.
  4. Add the sorrel and fry 8–10 minutes, stirring.
  5. Stir in crushed garlic and walnuts, season and serve warm or cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp oil and a nonstick pan to save about 200 kcal.
  • Squeeze the sorrel well so it fries instead of steaming.
  • A spoon of yogurt on the side balances the sourness without much fat.

History

Aveluk is one of the wild greens Armenians have long dried for winter. The sautéed version is a common meze on festive and fasting tables alike.
