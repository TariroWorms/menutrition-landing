Gongura mutton (sorrel mutton curry)

Origin: India · Andhra Pradesh

Gongura mutton pairs tender mutton with the sharp, almost lemony leaves of the gongura plant. The leaves melt into a dark, tangy gravy that is hot with green chillies. It is bold and rich rather than sweet. It is usually eaten with plain steamed rice.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g mutton on the bone
  • 200 g gongura leaves
  • 2 onions (250 g)
  • 6 garlic cloves, 4 green chillies
  • 3 tbsp oil (40 ml)
  • 1 tsp turmeric, salt

Method

  1. Wilt the gongura in a dry pan, then mash it into a paste with the chillies.
  2. Brown the sliced onions in oil, add garlic and turmeric.
  3. Add the mutton and fry for 10 minutes until sealed.
  4. Pour in 400 ml water, cover and simmer 40 minutes until almost tender.
  5. Stir in the gongura paste and salt, cook 15 minutes more until the oil separates.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean leg cuts and skim the fat before adding the leaves.
  • Halve the oil and the dish still works.
  • Serve with a large portion of cucumber salad and less rice.

History

Gongura is a staple leafy green of Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, valued for its sourness. Meat cooked with it is a long-standing festive and home dish of the region.
