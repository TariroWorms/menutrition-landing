Štruklji (Slovene rolled dumplings)

Origin: Slovenia

Štruklji are rolls of thin dough spread with a filling such as cottage cheese and tarragon, then boiled or baked and cut into slices. They are soft and creamy with a herby, slightly tangy taste. They are served with browned butter and breadcrumbs, or as a side with meat dishes.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 1 egg
  • 150 ml warm water
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt
  • 500 g cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp chopped tarragon
  • 50 g breadcrumbs
  • 50 g butter

Method

  1. Knead flour, 1 egg, water, oil and salt into a smooth dough and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Mix cottage cheese, 2 eggs, sour cream, tarragon and salt for the filling.
  3. Roll the dough very thin on a cloth.
  4. Spread the filling, roll up and wrap in the cloth or gauze.
  5. Simmer in salted water for 35 minutes.
  6. Slice and serve with butter-fried breadcrumbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat cheese and light sour cream.
  • Bake them instead of boiling, and use less butter for the breadcrumbs.
  • Serve with a green salad.

History

Štruklji are a traditional Slovene dish with many regional versions, from cottage cheese and tarragon to walnut, apple or buckwheat. They are made across the country and are a common festive and Sunday food.
