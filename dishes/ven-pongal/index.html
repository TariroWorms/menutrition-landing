Ven pongal (savoury rice and lentil porridge)

Origin: India · Tamil Nadu

Ven pongal is a soft, creamy porridge of rice and split moong dal seasoned with ghee, black pepper, cumin and ginger. It is mild, buttery and comforting with a gentle peppery warmth. It is a classic breakfast, eaten with coconut chutney and sambar.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g raw rice
  • 75 g split moong dal
  • 900 ml water
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 10 curry leaves
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 10 cashews

Method

  1. Dry-roast the dal for 2 minutes, then rinse with the rice.
  2. Pressure- or pot-cook rice and dal with water and salt until very soft.
  3. Mash lightly to a creamy consistency.
  4. Fry cashews, pepper, cumin, ginger and curry leaves in ghee.
  5. Stir the tempering into the pongal and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the ghee and add vegetables for a lighter plate.
  • Use brown rice or millet for more fibre.
  • Pair with sambar for extra protein.

History

Pongal takes its name from the Tamil harvest festival, when rice is boiled until it overflows as a sign of plenty. The savoury version is also a temple offering and an everyday breakfast.
