🍝 Tagliatelle in Cream Sauce

Tagliatelle alla panna are ribbon pasta simmered briefly in cream, then tossed with grated parmesan and a pinch of nutmeg. The sauce is silky, mild and rich, coating every strand without any other flavourings competing. It is a quick, comforting first course and a frequent base for added mushrooms or vegetables.

What it is made of

tagliatellecreamparmesannutmeg

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 160 g dried tagliatelle
  • 150 ml cream
  • 40 g parmesan, grated
  • 1 pinch grated nutmeg
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cook the tagliatelle in salted water until al dente; keep some cooking water.
  2. Warm the cream in a wide pan and simmer for 2 minutes until slightly thickened.
  3. Add the pasta, parmesan, nutmeg and a splash of cooking water and toss until glossy.
  4. Season with pepper and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace part of the cream with pasta cooking water to keep the sauce silky with fewer calories.
  • Keep it to a starter-size portion of about 80 g dry pasta per person.
  • Toss in steamed peas or spinach to add volume to each plate.

History

Tagliatelle with cream and parmesan is a simple, popular pasta in Italian restaurants and homes, especially in the north, where fresh ribbon pasta is a tradition.
