Tsuivan (Mongolian steamed noodles)

Origin: Mongolia

Hand-cut flour noodles are steamed over a stew of mutton, carrots, cabbage and onion, then mixed together in the pot. The noodles absorb the meaty juices and have a pleasant chew. It is a filling everyday dish and is eaten with a fork or chopsticks.

Recipe

4 servings80 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour, 130 ml water
  • 300 g mutton, sliced
  • 2 carrots, 1/4 cabbage
  • 1 onion, 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp oil, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Make a firm noodle dough and rest it.
  2. Roll thin, oil and cut into strips.
  3. Brown the mutton, add onion, carrot, garlic and a little water.
  4. Lay noodles on top and steam for 20 minutes.
  5. Add cabbage, mix everything and cook a few minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean meat and more vegetables.
  • Add less oil.
  • Use wholemeal flour for the noodles.

History

Tsuivan is a Mongolian household staple made with simple ingredients available to nomadic herders. The noodles are steamed rather than boiled.
