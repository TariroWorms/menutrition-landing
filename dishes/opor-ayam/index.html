Opor ayam (Javanese chicken in coconut milk)

Origin: Indonesia · Java

Opor ayam is chicken braised in a pale, mild coconut milk curry flavored with shallots, garlic, candlenuts, coriander, lemongrass and galangal. The sauce is rich and creamy but not hot, with a gentle spice warmth. It is a classic served with ketupat or lontong rice cakes at Lebaran, and with sambal goreng and krupuk. The leftover curry is said to taste even better the next day.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken pieces
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 6 shallots, 3 garlic cloves
  • 3 candlenuts, 1 tsp coriander
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, 3 cm galangal, 2 bay leaves
  • salt, oil

Method

  1. Grind shallots, garlic, candlenuts and coriander into a paste.
  2. Fry the paste with lemongrass, galangal and bay leaves until fragrant.
  3. Add chicken and coat with the paste.
  4. Pour in coconut milk and 200 ml water and simmer 35 minutes.
  5. Season and serve with rice cakes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken and light coconut milk.
  • Skim fat from the sauce.
  • Add vegetables to the curry.

History

Opor ayam is a Javanese dish associated with Eid al-Fitr celebrations in Indonesia. Its mild flavor suits the festive table where several rich dishes are served together.
