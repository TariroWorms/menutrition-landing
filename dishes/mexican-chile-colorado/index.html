Chile colorado (beef in red chile sauce)

Origin: Mexico · Northern Mexico

Chile colorado is a northern Mexican stew of beef chunks cooked until tender in a smooth sauce made from soaked dried chiles. The sauce is earthy and mildly smoky rather than fiercely hot. It is eaten with flour tortillas, rice or beans, and leftovers often end up in burritos.

What it is made of

beefdried chilesgarliconioncuminoreganobroth

Recipe

6 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef chuck, cubed
  • 8 dried guajillo or ancho chiles
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 500 ml beef broth
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Toast the chiles briefly, remove seeds and soak in hot water for 20 minutes.
  2. Blend the chiles with garlic, half the onion, cumin, oregano and some broth until smooth.
  3. Brown the beef in oil in batches with the remaining chopped onion.
  4. Strain the chile sauce over the meat and add the rest of the broth.
  5. Cover and simmer for about 2 hours until the beef is tender, then season with salt.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean chuck and skim the fat off the surface before serving.
  • Serve with beans and corn tortillas instead of flour tortillas for more fibre.
  • Make it a day ahead; once chilled, the fat solidifies and is easy to remove.

History

The dish belongs to the ranching cooking of Chihuahua, Sonora and neighbouring states, where beef and dried chiles were everyday staples. It also became a fixture of Mexican-American cooking in the US Southwest.
