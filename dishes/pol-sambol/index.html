Pol sambol (Sri Lankan coconut relish)

Origin: Sri Lanka

Pol sambol is a coarse relish of freshly grated coconut pounded with red chili, shallots, lime juice and often dried Maldive fish. It is fiery, tangy and salty, with a creamy sweetness from the coconut. It accompanies hoppers, string hoppers, kiribath and rice.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 coconut, grated (about 200 g)
  • 1 small onion or 4 shallots
  • 2 dried red chilies
  • 1 tsp Maldive fish flakes
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Grind dried chilies with salt coarsely.
  2. Add onion and pound lightly.
  3. Mix in the coconut and Maldive fish.
  4. Squeeze lime juice over the top.
  5. Taste and adjust salt.

How to make it lighter

  • Use it as a condiment, not a side dish.
  • Reduce the amount of coconut.
  • Skip Maldive fish for a vegan version.

History

Sambols are an everyday part of Sri Lankan meals and pol sambol is the best known. It is traditionally made on a stone grinding slab.
