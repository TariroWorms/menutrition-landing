Shkmeruli (chicken in garlic cream)

Origin: Georgia · Racha

Shkmeruli is young chicken that is flattened, fried until golden and then baked or simmered in a sauce of milk or cream loaded with crushed garlic. The skin stays partly crisp while the meat soaks up the mellow, very garlicky sauce. It is traditionally served sizzling in a clay ketsi dish, with bread to mop up the sauce.

What it is made of

chickengarlicmilkcreambutter

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 small chicken (1 kg)
  • 10 garlic cloves
  • 250 ml milk
  • 150 ml cream (20%)
  • 30 g butter
  • salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Halve the chicken along the backbone, flatten it and season with salt.
  2. Fry it skin side down in butter until golden, then turn and brown the other side.
  3. Crush the garlic with salt and mix it with milk and cream.
  4. Place the chicken in a clay or baking dish and pour the garlic sauce over it.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 15–20 minutes until the sauce bubbles and thickens slightly.

How to make it lighter

  • Use milk only, without cream, to save a good share of the fat.
  • Remove the skin before eating for a leaner portion.
  • Serve with a large green salad instead of extra bread.

History

The dish is named after the village of Shkmeri in the Racha region of western Georgia. Today it is one of the most popular chicken dishes in Georgian restaurants.
