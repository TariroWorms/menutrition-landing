Ghalieh mahi (Persian Gulf fish stew)

Origin: Iran · Bushehr

Ghalieh mahi is a tangy, herb-heavy fish stew from the Bushehr coast. Chunks of white fish simmer in a sauce of cilantro, fenugreek, garlic and tamarind with a bit of chili. It is bright green-brown, sour and warming, usually served over plain rice.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g firm white fish, in chunks
  • 1 onion, chopped (150 g)
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 100 g chopped cilantro
  • 1 tbsp dried fenugreek leaves
  • 2 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp chili, salt, 400 ml water

Method

  1. Fry the onion in oil until golden, add garlic and turmeric.
  2. Stir in the cilantro and fenugreek and fry until dark.
  3. Add tamarind, chili, salt and water.
  4. Simmer 15 minutes to build the sauce.
  5. Add the fish and cook 10 minutes gently without stirring.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil; the herbs already give richness.
  • Serve with plain rice in a small portion and salad.
  • Choose lean fish like snapper or grouper.

History

Ghalieh dishes are cornerstones of southern Iranian cooking, where tamarind arrived through Gulf trade routes. The use of herbs and tamarind sets this cuisine apart from the milder cooking of the north.
