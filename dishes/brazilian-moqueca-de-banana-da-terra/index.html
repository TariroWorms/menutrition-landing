Moqueca de banana-da-terra (plantain moqueca)

Origin: Brazil · Bahia
420kcal

This meat-free moqueca replaces fish with thick slices of ripe plantain, layered with onion, tomato and peppers and stewed in coconut milk and orange dendê oil. The plantain turns soft and sweet against the rich, fragrant sauce. It is served with white rice and farofa.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe plantains (about 800 g)
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 30 ml dendê oil
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 lime, juiced
  • 20 g cilantro

Method

  1. Peel the plantains and cut into thick diagonal slices; toss with lime juice and salt.
  2. Layer half the onion, tomato and pepper in a clay pot or wide pan.
  3. Add the plantain, then the remaining vegetables.
  4. Pour over coconut milk and dendê oil.
  5. Cover and simmer gently for 20 minutes, then finish with cilantro.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk and half the dendê oil to cut fat.
  • Add more peppers and tomatoes to increase volume and fiber.
  • Serve with a modest portion of rice instead of farofa.

History

Moqueca is a cornerstone of Bahian cooking, shaped by Afro-Brazilian traditions and the use of dendê palm oil. Plantain versions have long existed and are popular with vegetarians as a variation on the classic fish stew.
