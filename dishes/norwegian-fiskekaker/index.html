Fiskekaker (Norwegian fish cakes)

Origin: Norway
368kcal

Fiskekaker are flat patties of ground white fish mixed with onion, milk and a little flour. They are fried in butter until golden outside and soft inside. They are usually eaten warm with boiled potatoes, cooked carrots and a light sauce, and they are also sliced cold on bread. The taste is mild, fresh and gently sweet.

What it is made of

white fishonionmilkflourbutter

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g white fish fillet
  • 1 small onion
  • 200 ml milk
  • 40 g flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Butter for frying

Method

  1. Grind the fish with the onion and salt in a food processor.
  2. Add the flour and slowly mix in the milk until the mass is soft but holds its shape.
  3. Rest the mixture in the fridge for 20 minutes.
  4. Shape into flat patties with wet hands.
  5. Fry in butter over medium heat for 4 minutes per side until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the patties on a tray instead of frying to cut fat.
  • Serve with a large portion of vegetables.
  • Choose lean fish such as cod or haddock and keep the butter to a thin film in the pan.

History

Fish cakes are a typical everyday dish of the Norwegian coast, where lean white fish was always at hand. They remain a favourite family dinner and a common product in shops.
