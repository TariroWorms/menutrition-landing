Kjøttkaker (Norwegian meat patties in gravy)

Origin: Norway

Kjøttkaker are oval beef patties seasoned with onion, nutmeg and ginger, browned and then simmered in brown gravy. They are served with boiled potatoes, peas or cabbage and often lingonberry jam. The flavour is savory and gently spiced, and the gravy ties the plate together. It is considered one of the most typical Norwegian home dinners.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 onion
  • 200 ml milk
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 800 g potatoes
  • 300 g peas
  • 300 ml beef stock, butter for frying, nutmeg, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Mix beef, grated onion, milk, 1 tbsp flour, salt, nutmeg and pepper into a smooth mixture.
  2. Shape into oval patties and brown in butter on both sides.
  3. Remove them and make gravy from the pan with the remaining flour and stock.
  4. Return the patties to the gravy and simmer 15 minutes.
  5. Serve with boiled potatoes, peas and lingonberry jam.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef and bake the patties instead of frying.
  • Thicken the gravy with less flour and leave out any cream.
  • Serve with extra vegetables and fewer potatoes.

History

Kjøttkaker are comparable to Swedish and Danish meatballs and have been a family dinner staple in Norway for generations. They are often served on Sundays and in canteens across the country.
