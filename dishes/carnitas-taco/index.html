🌮 Carnitas tacos

Origin: Mexico · Michoacán

Carnitas tacos are tortillas filled with slow-cooked pork that is tender inside and crisp at the edges, topped simply with chopped white onion, fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime. Salsa is added to taste. They are a staple of Mexican street food and taquerias, usually eaten two or three at a time.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork shoulder, cut into chunks
  • 12 small tortillas
  • 1 white onion, finely chopped
  • A bunch of cilantro, chopped
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • 1 orange, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp cumin, 2 bay leaves, salt
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil

Method

  1. Put the pork in a pot with the orange juice, garlic, cumin, bay leaves, salt and water to half cover; simmer about 2 hours until tender and the liquid has evaporated.
  2. Add the oil and let the pork fry in its own fat, turning, until golden and crisp at the edges.
  3. Shred or chop the pork.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  5. Fill with pork, top with onion and cilantro and serve with lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Count your tacos: two tacos with plenty of onion and cilantro make a satisfying portion.
  • Drain the pork well on paper before filling the tortillas.
  • Use small tortillas rather than large ones.

History

Carnitas originate in Michoacán, and tacos are the most common way they are eaten across Mexico and in Mexican restaurants abroad.
