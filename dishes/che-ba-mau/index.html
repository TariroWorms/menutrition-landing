🍧 Chè ba màu (three-colour dessert)

Chè ba màu, 'three-colour dessert', is a layered Vietnamese drink-dessert served in a tall glass: sweet red beans, mashed mung beans and green pandan jelly, topped with crushed ice and coconut milk. Stirred together it is cold, creamy and chewy at once. It is especially popular on hot days and sold by street vendors and dessert shops.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g red beans, soaked overnight
  • 100 g split mung beans, soaked for 2 hours
  • 100 g sugar
  • 10 g gelatin powder
  • 300 ml pandan juice (blended pandan leaves and water, strained)
  • 250 ml coconut milk with a pinch of salt
  • Crushed ice

Method

  1. Simmer the red beans in water for about 1 hour until soft, then stir in 40 g sugar.
  2. Simmer the mung beans until soft, mash with 30 g sugar into a thick paste.
  3. Heat the pandan juice with the remaining sugar, dissolve the gelatin in it, set in a tray in the fridge and cut into strips.
  4. Layer red beans, mung bean paste and pandan jelly in tall glasses.
  5. Top with crushed ice and coconut milk and stir before eating.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the sugar in the beans and let the coconut milk add the sweetness.
  • Use light coconut milk and more crushed ice.
  • Make a smaller glass — the beans make it filling.

History

Chè ba màu is one of the best-known members of the Vietnamese chè family and is closely linked with southern Vietnam. It is also loved in Vietnamese communities abroad, where it is often sold as 'rainbow dessert'.
