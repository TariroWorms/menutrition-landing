🦐 Ceviche mixto

Origin: Peru · Pacific coast

Ceviche mixto is the seafood version of Peruvian ceviche: cubes of raw white fish, shrimp and rings of squid marinated in lime juice with hot rocoto pepper and thin slices of red onion. The mix of textures — tender fish, springy shrimp and squid — makes it a favourite at cevicherías, where it is served cold as a starter or light lunch.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g very fresh white fish fillet, cubed
  • 200 g peeled shrimp
  • 200 g squid, cut into rings
  • Juice of 10 limes (about 200 ml)
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 rocoto or hot red chili, finely chopped
  • Salt

Method

  1. Blanch the shrimp and squid in boiling salted water for 1 minute, then cool them in iced water and drain.
  2. Season the fish with salt and chili in a cold glass bowl.
  3. Add the shrimp and squid, pour over the lime juice and stir; leave for 3–5 minutes.
  4. Rinse the onion in cold water, drain, add to the bowl and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • All the seafood here is lean, so the dish fits most budgets on its own; watch the portions of sides.
  • Skip fried corn or fried squid toppings that are sometimes added in restaurants.
  • Pair it with a green salad to make a full, light meal.

History

Mixed seafood ceviche is a staple of Peruvian cevicherías along the coast, especially in Lima, where the shrimp and squid are often briefly blanched before marinating.
