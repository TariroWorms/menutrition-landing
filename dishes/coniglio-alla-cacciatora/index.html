🍅 Rabbit cacciatore

Coniglio alla cacciatora is rabbit braised "hunter's style" with garlic, rosemary, white wine, tomato and olives. The lean meat cooks slowly until it falls from the bone, while the sauce turns thick, herby and slightly tangy. It is a rustic Italian main course, served with polenta, potatoes or simple vegetables.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 rabbit (about 1.2 kg), cut into pieces
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 sprigs rosemary
  • 150 ml dry white wine
  • 400 g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 80 g black olives
  • Salt, black pepper

Method

  1. Season the rabbit and brown it on all sides in the olive oil with the garlic and rosemary.
  2. Pour in the white wine and let it bubble until mostly evaporated.
  3. Add the tomatoes and olives, cover and simmer gently for about 1 hour, turning the pieces now and then, until tender.
  4. Uncover for the last 10 minutes to thicken the sauce and adjust the seasoning.

How to make it lighter

  • Rabbit is very lean, so most of the fat comes from oil and olives — 1 tbsp of olive oil is enough to brown it.
  • Serve with grilled vegetables or a salad instead of a large portion of polenta for a lighter plate.
  • Count the olives: a handful adds noticeably to the total.

History

Alla cacciatora dishes belong to the rural cooking of central Italy, where game and farmyard meats were braised with whatever herbs and vegetables were at hand. Recipes vary from region to region; rabbit and chicken are the most common versions today.
