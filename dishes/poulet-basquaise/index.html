🍗 Poulet Basquaise

Poulet basquaise is Basque-style chicken braised in a colourful sauce of red and green peppers, tomatoes and onion, flavoured with Bayonne ham and the mild heat of Espelette pepper. The chicken becomes tender and the sauce sweet, smoky and slightly spicy. It is served hot, usually with rice or potatoes.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg chicken pieces (thighs and drumsticks)
  • 2 red and 2 green bell peppers, in strips
  • 500 g tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 80 g Bayonne ham, in strips
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp Espelette pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt and brown it in the olive oil in a large casserole; set aside.
  2. Fry the ham briefly, then add the onions and peppers and cook for 10 minutes until soft.
  3. Add the garlic, tomatoes and Espelette pepper and cook for 5 minutes.
  4. Return the chicken, cover and simmer for 35–40 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Uncover for the last 10 minutes to thicken the sauce; adjust the salt and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin before browning to cut the fat noticeably.
  • Use 1 tbsp oil instead of 2; 1 tbsp olive oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve with a small portion of rice and extra peppers from the sauce.

History

The dish comes from the French Basque Country, where its pepper-and-tomato base is closely related to the local piperade. Bayonne ham and Espelette pepper, both regional products with protected status, give it its character.
