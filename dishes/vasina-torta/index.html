🍰 Vasina Torta

Origin: Serbia · Belgrade

Vasina torta is a classic Serbian layered cake built on a biscuit base, with a thick walnut and chocolate filling and a smooth buttercream layer on top. It is rich, nutty and dense rather than airy, and is cut into small slices. It is a traditional cake for slavas (family saint-day celebrations) and holidays.

What it is made of

walnutchocolatebuttercreambiscuit

Recipe

12 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g plain biscuits
  • 200 g walnuts, ground
  • 150 g dark chocolate, melted
  • 150 g soft butter
  • 150 g powdered sugar
  • 100 ml hot water

Method

  1. Line a 20 cm tin with the biscuits.
  2. Mix the ground walnuts with the melted chocolate, 75 g sugar and the hot water; spread over the biscuits.
  3. Beat the butter with the remaining sugar until fluffy to make the buttercream.
  4. Spread the buttercream over the walnut layer and smooth the top.
  5. Chill for at least 4 hours before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut thin slices — a small square goes a long way with a cake this rich.
  • Use less buttercream on top and finish with grated chocolate instead.
  • Serve with unsweetened coffee or tea rather than a sweet drink.

History

The cake's name means 'Vasa's cake', and it is usually linked to a Belgrade family of the late 19th or early 20th century, though accounts of who Vasa was differ.
