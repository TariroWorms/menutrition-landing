🥘 Kafta bil sanieh

Kafta bil sanieh is a Levantine tray bake of seasoned minced lamb mixed with parsley and onion, pressed into patties and arranged with sliced potatoes and tomatoes. Everything bakes in a light tomato sauce until the meat is juicy and the potatoes are soft and full of flavour. It is a simple, hearty family dinner, often served with a fresh salad.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g minced lamb
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 30 g parsley, chopped
  • 3 potatoes, sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, sliced
  • 300 ml tomato sauce mixed with 200 ml water
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp seven-spice or allspice, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Mix the lamb with the onion, parsley, spices and salt; shape into flat patties.
  2. Lightly brush the potato slices with oil and bake at 200 °C for 15 minutes.
  3. Arrange the patties, potatoes and tomatoes in rows in a tray.
  4. Pour the tomato sauce over, cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake for 10 more minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced lamb or mix it with lean beef.
  • Bake the potatoes instead of frying them, as in this recipe.
  • Replace part of the potatoes with zucchini or eggplant slices.

History

Sanieh means “tray” in Levantine Arabic, and dishes baked this way in a round tray are typical of home cooking in Lebanon, Syria, Palestine and Jordan. Kafta bil sanieh remains an everyday family dish across the region.
