🍖 İzmir Köfte

Origin: Turkey · İzmir

İzmir köfte are oval meatballs of seasoned minced meat, briefly browned and then baked in a tray with wedges of potato, green peppers and tomatoes in a light tomato sauce. The köfte turn soft and juicy, the potatoes absorb the sauce and the peppers add a fresh note. It is a homely one-tray dish, served hot with rice or salad.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 3 medium potatoes, cut into wedges
  • 3 green peppers, halved
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 300 ml hot water
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the beef with onion, cumin, salt and pepper; shape into finger-length oval köfte.
  2. Brown the köfte quickly in a hot pan with a little oil.
  3. Toss the potato wedges with the remaining oil and salt and arrange them in a baking tray with the köfte, peppers and tomato slices.
  4. Dissolve the tomato paste in the hot water and pour over the tray.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 35–40 minutes until the potatoes are tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced beef and skip browning the köfte in oil; they can go straight into the tray.
  • Replace some potato with extra peppers or zucchini.
  • Serve with a large salad instead of rice.

History

As the name suggests, the dish is associated with the Aegean city of İzmir, and it is now a standard home-cooked meal across Turkey.
