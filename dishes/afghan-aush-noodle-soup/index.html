Afghan aush (noodle soup)

Aush is a thick, hearty noodle soup with beans, chickpeas, spinach and tomato, finished with garlic yogurt, mint and a meat sauce. It is tangy, herby and filling. Families make it in cold weather and for guests.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g noodles
  • 100 g cooked kidney beans
  • 100 g cooked chickpeas
  • 200 g spinach
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 150 g minced beef
  • 200 g yogurt with garlic
  • 1.2 l water or stock

Method

  1. Fry onion and minced beef with spices until browned.
  2. Add tomatoes, beans and chickpeas and the stock.
  3. Simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Add spinach and noodles and cook until tender.
  5. Top with garlic yogurt.
  6. Sprinkle with dried mint.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef or omit it for a vegetarian bowl.
  • Use whole-grain noodles.
  • Add extra vegetables for more fibre.

History

Aush is the Afghan relative of the Persian ash family of thick soups. Afghan versions are known for their yogurt-and-mint finish.
