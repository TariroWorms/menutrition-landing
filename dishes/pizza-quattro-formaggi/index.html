🧀 Pizza quattro formaggi

Pizza quattro formaggi — “four cheeses” — is usually a white pizza topped with mozzarella, gorgonzola, parmesan and fontina. Each cheese plays a role: mozzarella for stretch, fontina for creamy melt, gorgonzola for a blue tang and parmesan for a nutty, salty finish. It is rich and indulgent, best eaten hot from the oven.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g ready pizza dough (or homemade)
  • 100 g mozzarella, torn
  • 50 g fontina, diced
  • 40 g gorgonzola, crumbled
  • 20 g parmesan, grated
  • 1 tsp olive oil

Method

  1. Preheat the oven with a stone or tray inside to the maximum.
  2. Stretch the dough by hand into a 30 cm round, leaving a slightly thicker rim.
  3. Brush the base lightly with oil and scatter the mozzarella and fontina.
  4. Dot with gorgonzola and sprinkle with parmesan.
  5. Bake on the hottest setting of the oven (250–280 °C) on a preheated stone or tray for 7–10 minutes until the crust is blistered.
  6. Rest for a minute and slice.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the total cheese by a third — four strong cheeses still give plenty of flavour.
  • Share one pizza between two people and add a large salad.
  • This is one of the richest pizzas; log it carefully and keep portions to 2–3 slices.

History

Quattro formaggi is a standard on Italian pizzeria menus; the exact cheeses vary by region and pizzeria, and no single origin is recorded.
