Embutido (Filipino steamed meatloaf)

Origin: Philippines

Embutido is a pork meatloaf rolled around boiled egg and sausage, studded with raisins, carrot and bell pepper. It is steamed, chilled and sliced into rounds that show a pretty cross-section. It is eaten with rice or in bread, with ketchup on the side.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g ground pork
  • 3 eggs, one hard-boiled
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 50 g raisins
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 60 g breadcrumbs
  • 2 sausages
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Mix the pork, raw eggs, carrot, raisins, bell pepper, breadcrumbs, salt and pepper.
  2. Shape into a flat rectangle on foil.
  3. Lay the boiled egg and sausage in the center.
  4. Roll tightly and seal the foil.
  5. Steam 60 minutes, cool and slice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean ground pork or chicken.
  • Add more grated vegetables.
  • Replace some breadcrumbs with oats.

History

The name comes from the Spanish word for cured meats, and the dish is a Filipino cousin of Spanish meat rolls. It is a classic party and holiday food, often made ahead.
