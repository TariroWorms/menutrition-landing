🍢 Iraqi Tikka (Lamb Tikka Kebab)

Origin: Iraq

Iraqi tikka is chunks of lamb marinated with onion, salt and a little tomato paste, threaded onto skewers and grilled over charcoal until charred outside and juicy within. It is chunkier and meatier than minced kebab, with a smoky, savoury taste. It is served with sumac-dusted onions, grilled tomatoes and fresh herbs.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g boneless lamb, cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp sumac
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the lamb with grated onion, tomato paste, salt and pepper.
  2. Marinate for at least 2 hours in the fridge (time not included).
  3. Thread the cubes onto skewers.
  4. Grill over hot charcoal for 10–12 minutes, turning, until charred and cooked to your liking.
  5. Sprinkle with sumac and serve with grilled tomatoes and onions.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose leg rather than shoulder for leaner cubes.
  • Thread vegetables such as onion and tomato between the meat to stretch a portion.
  • Skip any fat brushed on the skewers; the marinade keeps the meat moist.

History

Lamb tikka is a mainstay of Iraqi grill houses and home barbecues today, usually ordered alongside minced kebab.
