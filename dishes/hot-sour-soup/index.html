🍜 Hot and Sour Soup

Hot and sour soup is a Chinese chicken-broth soup made tangy with vinegar and peppery with white pepper. It is filled with strips of tofu and crunchy wood ear mushrooms, lightly thickened, and finished with ribbons of beaten egg. The result is silky, warming and sharp, served as a starter in Chinese restaurants.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 litre chicken broth
  • 10 g dried wood ear mushrooms, soaked and sliced
  • 200 g firm tofu, in thin strips
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp ground white pepper
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 3 tbsp water
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 2 spring onions, sliced

Method

  1. Bring the broth to a simmer, add the mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tofu and simmer 2–3 minutes; season with salt.
  3. Stir in the cornstarch mixture and cook until slightly thickened.
  4. Turn off the heat, stir in the vinegar and white pepper.
  5. Slowly pour in the beaten eggs while stirring gently to form ribbons; top with spring onion.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a fat-skimmed homemade or low-fat broth.
  • Add extra mushrooms and tofu to make a bowl filling enough to be a light meal.
  • Use less cornstarch for a thinner, lighter soup; the flavour comes from vinegar and pepper.

History

Versions of hot and sour soup are eaten in several regions of China, including Sichuan and Beijing, with the heat coming mainly from white pepper rather than chili. It became one of the best-known starters in Chinese restaurants abroad.
