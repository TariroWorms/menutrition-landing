Lahori fish (spiced fried fish)

Origin: Pakistan · Lahore

Lahori fish is made from boneless pieces of freshwater fish marinated in ginger, garlic, lemon and chilli, dipped in a batter of gram flour with carom seeds and deep-fried until crisp and golden. Inside the fish is white and moist, outside it is crunchy and sharp with spice. It is served hot with chutney, lemon and naan on Lahore's food streets.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g white freshwater fish fillets, in chunks
  • 100 g gram flour
  • 1 tbsp rice flour (10 g)
  • 20 g ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice (30 ml)
  • 1 tsp carom seeds, 2 tsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin
  • oil for frying (about 500 ml), salt, water

Method

  1. Marinate the fish with paste, lemon, chilli and salt for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the gram flour, rice flour, carom seeds, spices and water into a thick batter.
  3. Dip the fish pieces in the batter.
  4. Fry in oil at 180°C for 4-5 minutes until golden and crisp.
  5. Drain and serve with chutney and lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Air-fry or bake the coated fish at 220°C.
  • Fry in small batches and drain well on paper to keep the coating less greasy.
  • Serve with a large salad rather than bread.

History

Fried fish from the river catch is a famous speciality of Lahore, sold at dedicated fish stalls on winter evenings. The batter with carom seeds is the signature of the style.
