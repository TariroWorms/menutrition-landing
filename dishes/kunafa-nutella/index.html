🍫 Chocolate kunafa

Chocolate kunafa is a modern take on the Arab classic: crisp kataifi strands baked with ghee around a molten centre of chocolate hazelnut spread. Once golden, it is moistened with a little sugar syrup, so every slice combines crunchy pastry with a gooey, sweet chocolate layer. It is served warm, when the filling is still runny.

Recipe

8 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g kataifi (shredded kunafa pastry)
  • 100 g ghee, melted
  • 250 g chocolate hazelnut spread
  • 150 ml sugar syrup (sugar and water)

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 190 °C. Pull the kataifi into short strands and rub with the ghee.
  2. Press half into a round tin, spread the chocolate hazelnut spread over it leaving a small border, and cover with the rest of the pastry.
  3. Bake for 30–35 minutes until golden and crisp.
  4. Spoon over the syrup and leave for 5 minutes before slicing and serving warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the spread in a thin layer — it still tastes strongly of chocolate.
  • Skip most of the syrup; the filling is already sweet.
  • Cut 12 small pieces rather than 8 large ones.

History

Chocolate kunafa is a recent variation on traditional kunafa that spread through dessert shops and social media in the Gulf and the Levant. It is now a common item on modern Arab dessert menus.
