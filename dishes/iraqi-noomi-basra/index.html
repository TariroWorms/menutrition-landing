Noomi Basra (dried lime infusion)

Origin: Iraq · Basra

Noomi Basra is a tart, slightly smoky infusion made by steeping dried black limes in hot water. It is deep amber in colour and can be served hot in winter or chilled in summer. A little sugar or honey softens its sharp, earthy sourness.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 dried black limes
  • 1 litre water
  • 1 to 2 tsp sugar or honey (optional)
  • ice, for serving cold

Method

  1. Pierce the dried limes with a knife.
  2. Bring them to a boil in the water.
  3. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Strain and sweeten lightly if you like.
  5. Serve hot or pour over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sugar for an almost calorie-free drink.
  • Add a mint leaf for freshness.
  • Do not drink it too strong on an empty stomach.

History

Dried limes, called noomi, are a signature of Basra and the Gulf, where they are used in stews and drinks. The infusion is valued as a refreshing and soothing drink.
