Mujdei (Moldovan garlic sauce)

Origin: Moldova

Mujdei is a punchy sauce of garlic pounded with salt, then loosened with cold water or broth and a little oil. It is thin, pungent and sharp, made to be spooned over fried meats, grilled fish, boiled potatoes or mămăligă. Used sparingly it lifts rich food in the way a squeeze of lemon would.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 8 garlic cloves, 40 g
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 100 ml cold water or cooled broth
  • 30 ml sunflower or olive oil
  • 1 tsp vinegar

Method

  1. Peel the garlic and pound it with the salt to a smooth paste in a mortar.
  2. Add the water or broth a little at a time, stirring constantly.
  3. Stir in the oil and the vinegar.
  4. Taste and adjust salt.
  5. Let it rest 10 minutes so the flavours settle.
  6. Serve in a small bowl alongside meat or potatoes.

How to make it lighter

  • A teaspoon goes a long way, so serve it in small amounts.
  • Use broth instead of oil-heavy versions for fewer calories.
  • Pair with grilled lean meat or boiled potatoes.

History

Garlic sauces of this kind appear all along the Balkans and Eastern Mediterranean, each with its own base. In Moldova and Romania mujdei is the standard partner to fried and boiled meats and to festive roasts.
