Tofu thoke (Shan chickpea tofu salad)

Origin: Myanmar · Shan State

Tofu thoke is a Shan salad of soft or sliced chickpea tofu dressed with tamarind, soy, chili oil, onion and herbs. It is tangy, nutty and lightly spicy, and is served cool as a snack or light meal.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g chickpea tofu, sliced
  • 1 small red onion
  • 2 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp tamarind paste
  • 20 g cilantro
  • 1 tbsp roasted peanuts

Method

  1. Slice the chickpea tofu into thin pieces or squares.
  2. Mix soy sauce, lime, tamarind and chili oil into a dressing.
  3. Slice the onion thinly and rinse it briefly in water.
  4. Toss tofu with dressing, onion and cilantro.
  5. Top with crushed peanuts and serve cool.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less chili oil to reduce fat.
  • Add shredded cabbage and cucumber for more volume.
  • Skip the peanuts if avoiding allergens.

History

Shan "tofu" is not made from soy but from chickpea or yellow pea flour cooked into a thick porridge that sets. It is a signature ingredient of Shan State cooking.
