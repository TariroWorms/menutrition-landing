🫓 Gobhi paratha

Gobhi paratha is a whole wheat flatbread stuffed with grated cauliflower seasoned with green chili, cumin and herbs, rolled thin and cooked on a hot griddle with ghee. The outside turns crisp and flaky in spots while the filling stays soft and spiced. It is a popular breakfast, eaten hot with pickle or a fresh chutney.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g whole wheat flour (atta)
  • About 180 ml water
  • 300 g cauliflower, finely grated
  • 1 green chili, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 40 g ghee
  • Salt

Method

  1. Knead the flour, water and a pinch of salt into a soft dough and rest 20 minutes.
  2. Squeeze excess moisture from the grated cauliflower and mix with chili, cumin, cilantro and salt.
  3. Divide the dough into 8 balls, flatten each, fill with cauliflower and seal.
  4. Roll out gently into 18 cm rounds.
  5. Cook on a hot griddle, brushing each side with a little ghee, until golden spots appear.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush with ½ tsp ghee per side, or cook dry and add a little at the end.
  • Make the parathas smaller and fill them generously with cauliflower.
  • Eat one paratha with a fresh salad instead of two.

History

Stuffed parathas are a breakfast staple in Punjab and across North India, where cauliflower versions are especially common in winter when the vegetable is in season.
