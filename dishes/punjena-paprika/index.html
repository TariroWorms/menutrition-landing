🫑 Punjena Paprika (Stuffed Peppers)

Origin: Serbia · Balkans

Punjena paprika are bell peppers stuffed with a mixture of ground meat, rice and onion, then baked or braised in a tomato sauce until soft. The peppers turn sweet and tender while the filling stays juicy, and the tomato sauce thickens around them. They are a home-style main dish across the former Yugoslavia, especially in summer.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 8 bell peppers
  • 500 g ground meat
  • 100 g rice, rinsed
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 500 ml tomato passata
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Cut the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds.
  2. Soften the onion in the oil, then mix with the meat, rice, paprika, salt and pepper.
  3. Fill the peppers loosely with the mixture and stand them in a deep dish.
  4. Pour the passata and 300 ml water around them.
  5. Cover and bake at 180 °C for about 60 minutes until the rice is cooked; serve with the sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean ground meat to reduce fat noticeably.
  • Replace part of the rice with finely chopped vegetables such as carrot or zucchini.
  • Make one or two peppers a portion and add a salad on the side.

History

Stuffed vegetables spread across the Balkans with Ottoman cooking, and stuffed peppers became a staple of Serbian, Bosnian and Croatian home kitchens. Today they are a common family dish throughout the region.
