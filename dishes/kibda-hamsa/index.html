🍳 Kibda Hamsa (Saudi Liver Fry)

Origin: Saudi Arabia · Hejaz

Kibda hamsa is lamb liver cut into small pieces and quick-fried over high heat with onion, tomato and green chili, seasoned with cumin and finished with a squeeze of lemon. The liver stays tender and slightly pink inside while the sauce turns thick and spicy. It is a popular Saudi breakfast, especially on weekends, eaten hot straight from the pan.

Recipe

3 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb liver
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt

Method

  1. Trim the liver and cut into 2 cm pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a wide pan over high heat and fry the sliced onion until soft.
  3. Add the liver and sear 2–3 minutes, stirring, until browned outside.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes, chilies, cumin and salt and cook 3–4 minutes more until saucy.
  5. Squeeze over lemon juice and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan with 1 tbsp oil — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Add extra tomato and chili for volume without many calories.
  • Add a side of sliced cucumber and fresh herbs to make the plate more filling.

History

Kibda hamsa is a well-loved breakfast in Saudi Arabia, sold from early-morning stalls and cooked at home, particularly in the Hejaz region.
