🍖 Steamed spare ribs with black bean

Origin: China · Guangdong

Steamed spare ribs with black bean is a dim sum favourite: bite-sized pieces of rib marinated with fermented black beans, garlic, ginger and chili, then steamed in small dishes. The meat turns tender and juicy, and the beans give a deep, salty and pungent flavour. The ribs are served in their own savory juices.

Recipe

3 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g spare ribs, chopped into 3 cm pieces
  • 2 tbsp fermented black beans, rinsed and mashed
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 10 g fresh ginger, minced
  • 1 red chili, sliced
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Rinse the ribs well and pat dry.
  2. Mix the black beans, garlic, ginger, chili, sugar, oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Coat the ribs in the mixture and marinate for 30 minutes.
  4. Spread in a shallow heatproof dish and steam for 20–25 minutes until tender.
  5. Serve hot with the juices.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the ribs before marinating.
  • Rinse the black beans well to reduce saltiness.
  • Serve with plenty of steamed vegetables and a small bowl of rice.

History

The dish is a long-standing item on Cantonese dim sum menus in Guangdong and Hong Kong teahouses, and is also cooked at home over rice.
