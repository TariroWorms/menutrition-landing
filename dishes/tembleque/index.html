Tembleque (Puerto Rican coconut pudding)

Origin: Puerto Rico

Tembleque is a pale, jiggly pudding of coconut milk, sugar and cornstarch cooked until thick and chilled until set, then turned out and dusted with cinnamon. It is lightly sweet and tastes of fresh coconut, with a smooth, wobbly texture. It is a traditional holiday dessert and also an everyday treat.

Recipe

8 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 ml coconut milk
  • 100 g sugar
  • 80 g cornstarch
  • 250 ml water
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • ground cinnamon for dusting

Method

  1. Dissolve the cornstarch in the cold water.
  2. Heat coconut milk with the sugar, salt and cinnamon stick until hot.
  3. Strain in the cornstarch mixture, stirring constantly.
  4. Cook on low until very thick, about 10 minutes.
  5. Pour into a wet mold or small cups and cool, then chill for 4 hours.
  6. Turn out and dust with cinnamon.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk and less sugar.
  • Serve small portions with fresh fruit.
  • Add a splash of vanilla instead of more sugar.

History

The name comes from the Spanish word for trembling, referring to its wobble. Coconut-based desserts are central to Puerto Rican sweets, especially around Christmas.
