Lanttulaatikko (Finnish rutabaga casserole)

Origin: Finland

Lanttulaatikko is a baked Finnish casserole of mashed rutabaga with cream, egg, a touch of syrup, nutmeg and breadcrumbs. It tastes earthy, mildly sweet and creamy, with a golden crumb top. It is a traditional side dish for the Christmas table.

Recipe

6 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g rutabaga
  • 100 ml cream
  • 1 egg, salt, a pinch of nutmeg
  • 30 g dark syrup
  • 20 g breadcrumbs
  • 20 g butter

Method

  1. Peel and dice the rutabaga and boil until soft.
  2. Drain well and mash smooth.
  3. Stir in cream, egg, syrup, salt and nutmeg.
  4. Pour into a greased dish.
  5. Sprinkle with breadcrumbs and dot with butter.
  6. Bake at 175 °C for about 60 minutes until set and golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use milk instead of cream.
  • Reduce the syrup.
  • Use wholegrain breadcrumbs or oats for the topping.

History

Rutabaga is a common root vegetable in Nordic cooking and keeps well through winter. The casserole is one of the classic Finnish laatikko dishes served at Christmas with ham.
