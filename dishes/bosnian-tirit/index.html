Tirit (Bosnian bread in broth)

Origin: Bosnia and Herzegovina

Tirit is a humble Bosnian dish of torn flatbread soaked in warm chicken broth. It is topped with shredded chicken, melted butter and garlic. The bread soaks up the broth and becomes soft and comforting. It is served in a deep dish and often prepared for religious gatherings.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 4 pieces of stale flatbread
  • 1 litre chicken broth
  • 300 g cooked chicken
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • parsley

Method

  1. Tear the bread into pieces and place in a deep dish.
  2. Heat the broth with the garlic.
  3. Pour the hot broth over the bread and let it soak.
  4. Top with shredded chicken.
  5. Pour over melted butter and sprinkle with parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter for a lighter dish.
  • Choose whole-grain bread.
  • Use chicken breast without skin.

History

Tirit, like the Middle Eastern tharid, is an old way to use stale bread. In Bosnia it has been associated with festive meals, especially after the fasting month.
