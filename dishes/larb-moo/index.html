🌶️ Larb Moo

Origin: Thailand · Isan

Larb moo is the pork version of the Isan minced-meat salad. Minced pork is tossed with lime juice, fish sauce, chili, sliced shallot, mint and toasted ground rice for a dish that is tangy, herby and fiery. It is eaten at room temperature with sticky rice, raw cabbage, long beans and cucumber.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced pork
  • 3 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1–2 tsp chili flakes
  • 3 shallots, thinly sliced
  • Large handful of mint
  • 2 tbsp toasted rice powder
  • Cabbage, long beans and cucumber to serve

Method

  1. Cook the pork in a pan with a splash of water, breaking it up, until no longer pink, 5–6 minutes.
  2. Remove from the heat and season with lime juice, fish sauce and chili.
  3. Fold in shallots and mint.
  4. Add the toasted rice powder and toss well.
  5. Serve with raw vegetables.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced pork and pour off the pan fat.
  • Fill up on the raw vegetables and keep sticky rice to a small ball.
  • Taste before adding all the fish sauce — it is very salty.

History

Larb comes from the Lao culinary tradition shared by Laos and Isan, the northeastern region of Thailand. Larb moo is one of the most popular versions in Thailand and is a staple of Isan street stalls.
