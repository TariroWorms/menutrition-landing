Macanese pork chop bun

A thin bone-in pork chop marinated in soy, garlic and pepper is fried until golden and tucked inside a crusty roll with a crackly crust and soft center. Some versions add a spoon of sauce or a slice of lettuce. It is a classic grab-and-go sandwich in Macau.

Recipe

1 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 pork chop, 120 g
  • 1 crusty roll
  • 1 tsp soy sauce, 1 garlic clove
  • pinch of sugar and white pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Tenderize the chop and marinate 20 minutes.
  2. Pan-fry 3 minutes per side until golden.
  3. Warm the roll briefly in the oven.
  4. Split the roll and place the chop inside.
  5. Serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the chop instead of frying.
  • Add lettuce and tomato.
  • Choose a wholemeal roll.

History

The sandwich reflects Macau's mix of Portuguese bread culture and Chinese seasoning. It is a staple of street stalls and cafés.
