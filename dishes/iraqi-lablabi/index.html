Lablabi (Iraqi chickpeas in broth)

Origin: Iraq

Iraqi lablabi is a bowl of soft chickpeas in a hot, garlicky broth sharpened with lemon and scented with cumin. It is ladled over torn pieces of bread and finished with oil and chilli. Street vendors sell it on cold mornings as a cheap, filling breakfast.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g dried chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 1.2 litres water or broth
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 flatbreads, torn
  • salt, chilli flakes

Method

  1. Simmer the soaked chickpeas in water until very soft, about 60 minutes.
  2. Crush the garlic with salt and stir it into the pot.
  3. Season with cumin and lemon juice.
  4. Place torn bread in bowls.
  5. Ladle chickpeas and broth over, then finish with oil and chilli.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more chickpeas and less bread to cut carbs.
  • Choose wholemeal bread.
  • Keep salt low and add more lemon.

History

Chickpeas boiled in broth are a staple across the Levant and Mesopotamia. The Iraqi version is eaten mostly in winter.
