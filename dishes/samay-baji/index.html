Samay baji (Newari festive platter)

Origin: Nepal · Newar

Samay baji is the classic Newar ritual platter built around beaten rice. It is topped with spiced grilled meat, a boiled egg, black roasted soybeans, ginger, pickled radish and a spicy potato or bean side. Each bite mixes chewy, crunchy, smoky and sharp tastes.

Recipe

2 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g beaten rice (chiura)
  • 150 g choila (grilled spiced buffalo or chicken)
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • 60 g roasted black soybeans
  • 40 g pickled radish
  • 20 g fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil
  • salt

Method

  1. Prepare the grilled spiced meat as for choila.
  2. Roast the soybeans in a dry pan until fragrant.
  3. Boil and halve the eggs.
  4. Warm the beaten rice slightly if desired.
  5. Arrange the rice in a mound on a plate.
  6. Place the meat, eggs, soybeans, radish and ginger around it and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use chicken instead of buffalo for a lighter plate.
  • Skip frying the accompaniments.
  • Add fresh vegetables for fibre.

History

The dish has a ceremonial role in Newar life and is offered at weddings, festivals and rites of passage. Each element is believed to carry auspicious meaning.
