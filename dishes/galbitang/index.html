Galbitang (Korean short rib soup)

Origin: South Korea

Galbitang is a clear soup of beef short ribs simmered for hours with radish, garlic and scallions, often with glass noodles. The broth is light yet deeply beefy and clean. It is a restorative dish served at celebrations and family meals.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g beef short ribs
  • 200 g radish
  • 50 g glass noodles
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 scallions
  • 2 l water
  • soy sauce and salt

Method

  1. Soak the ribs in cold water for 30 minutes.
  2. Boil 10 minutes, drain and rinse.
  3. Simmer with radish and garlic for 1.5 hours.
  4. Add the soaked noodles and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Season with soy sauce and salt, add scallions.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim fat from the broth after chilling.
  • Add carrot and shiitake mushrooms for more vegetables.
  • Season lightly with salt and let each person add more at the table.

History

Galbitang is one of the traditional Korean beef soups and is linked to special occasions and to giving strength to the elderly.
