🍖 Mixed grill family platter

A mixed grill family platter is a large sharing tray of charcoal-grilled chicken pieces, lamb cubes and beef kofta, served on rice with grilled tomatoes and onions and plenty of flatbread. The meats are smoky and juicy, each seasoned differently. It is ordered for groups and placed in the middle of the table for everyone to share.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken thighs, cubed
  • 400 g lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 400 g minced beef for kofta, mixed with chopped onion and parsley
  • 300 g basmati rice
  • 4 tomatoes and 2 onions, halved, for grilling
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp seven-spice (baharat), salt
  • 4 flatbreads

Method

  1. Season chicken and lamb with oil, spices and salt; shape the beef mixture onto flat skewers.
  2. Cook the rice in salted water or stock.
  3. Grill the skewers over charcoal or a grill pan, turning, until cooked through.
  4. Grill the tomatoes and onions until soft and charred.
  5. Arrange rice on a large platter with the meats and vegetables on top and bread on the side.

How to make it lighter

  • Fill up on chicken, which is leaner than the lamb and kofta.
  • Take one piece of bread or a small scoop of rice, not both.
  • Add a big salad to the table and serve yourself vegetables first.

History

Shared grill platters are a fixture of restaurants across the Levant, the Gulf and Turkey, built from the same skewers sold individually. Today the family platter is a standard order for gatherings and weekend meals throughout the Middle East.
