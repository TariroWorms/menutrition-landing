Mondongo dominicano (tripe stew)

Origin: Dominican Republic

Mondongo is a thick stew of beef tripe simmered until soft with tomato, peppers, onion, potatoes and carrots. The broth is rich, slightly gelatinous and fragrant with cilantro and oregano. It is served hot with white rice and avocado, often as a weekend meal.

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g beef tripe
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 green pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 2 potatoes
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 bunch cilantro
  • 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp oregano

Method

  1. Clean the tripe with lime and salt and rinse well.
  2. Boil in water for 60-90 minutes until tender and cut into strips.
  3. Make a base of onion, garlic, pepper and tomato in oil.
  4. Add the tripe and enough of its broth to cover, plus oregano.
  5. Add the potatoes and carrots and simmer 30 minutes.
  6. Finish with cilantro and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the fat and skim the broth for a lighter stew.
  • Add more vegetables.
  • Serve with salad instead of a large portion of rice.

History

Tripe stews are common throughout Spain and Latin America, derived from Iberian dishes like callos. In the Dominican Republic mondongo is a popular dish at family gatherings.
