A waffle is a batter of flour, eggs, milk, sugar and melted butter baked between two hot patterned plates. The grid pattern gives it a crisp golden shell and a soft, airy inside. It is eaten warm for breakfast or dessert, often dusted with sugar or topped with fresh fruit.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g wheat flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 400 ml milk
  • 30 g sugar
  • 80 g butter, melted
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch salt

Method

  1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder and salt in a bowl.
  2. Beat the eggs with the milk, then stir in the melted butter.
  3. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and mix until just smooth; rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Preheat and lightly grease the waffle iron.
  5. Ladle in the batter and bake for 4–5 minutes until golden and crisp. Serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Top with fresh berries instead of syrup or whipped toppings.
  • Make thinner waffles or share one large waffle to keep the portion smaller.
  • 1 tbsp maple-style syrup adds about 50 kcal — measure it instead of pouring.

History

Wafer-like cakes baked between iron plates were made in medieval Europe, and the deep-grid waffle became strongly associated with Belgium and the Netherlands. Belgian-style waffles gained wide international fame in the twentieth century and are now a café and street-food staple worldwide.
