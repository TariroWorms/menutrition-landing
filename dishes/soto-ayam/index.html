🍲 Soto Ayam

Soto ayam is Indonesia's golden chicken soup: a clear broth tinted yellow with turmeric and perfumed with lemongrass, ladled over shredded chicken, glass noodles, bean sprouts and a halved boiled egg. It tastes light, fragrant and gently tangy once a squeeze of lime goes in, and it is eaten for breakfast, lunch or a late supper, often with rice on the side.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken thighs or breast
  • 2 lemongrass stalks, bruised
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric (or 3 cm fresh)
  • 3 garlic cloves and 4 shallots, pounded
  • 3 cm ginger, sliced
  • 100 g glass noodles
  • 150 g bean sprouts
  • 4 eggs, boiled and halved
  • 1.5 l water, salt, 1 tbsp oil
  • Lime wedges, celery leaves, chilli

Method

  1. Fry the pounded garlic, shallots, ginger and turmeric in the oil for 2–3 minutes until fragrant.
  2. Add the water, lemongrass, chicken and salt; simmer 30–35 minutes until the chicken is cooked.
  3. Lift out the chicken, shred it and return the broth to a gentle simmer.
  4. Soak the glass noodles in hot water for 5 minutes and drain; blanch the bean sprouts for 30 seconds.
  5. Divide noodles, sprouts and chicken between bowls, add half an egg and ladle over the hot broth.
  6. Finish with celery leaves, chilli and lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless breast and skim the fat off the broth to keep it lean.
  • Fill the bowl with extra bean sprouts and fewer noodles for more volume per calorie.
  • Skip a side of rice — the noodles already provide the starch.

History

Soto is a family of spiced soups found across Indonesia, and soto ayam is the most widespread version, with regional styles from Lamongan, Madura and Kudus. It is sold everywhere from street carts to home kitchens across Java and beyond.
