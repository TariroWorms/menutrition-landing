🥞 Blini with caviar

Blini with caviar is a celebrated Russian appetiser: small warm buckwheat pancakes topped with a spoonful of sour cream, glistening fish roe and a sprig of dill. The soft, nutty blini balance the salty, briny pop of the caviar. It is served as a starter at festive tables, especially at New Year and during Maslenitsa week.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 16 small buckwheat blini (made with milk, eggs and butter)
  • 100 g caviar or salmon roe
  • 120 g sour cream
  • A small bunch of dill

Method

  1. Make or warm the blini in a low oven or a dry pan for a few minutes.
  2. Arrange the blini on a platter.
  3. Top each with a teaspoon of sour cream.
  4. Add a small spoonful of caviar on top of the sour cream.
  5. Garnish with a dill sprig and serve straight away.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light sour cream (10–15% fat) and keep it to one teaspoon per blin.
  • Caviar is rich but used in small amounts — the blini and sour cream carry most of the calories, so count the number of blini.
  • Serve with a crisp cucumber and dill salad to round out the plate.

History

Blini have been eaten in Russia for centuries, and serving them with caviar became a hallmark of festive Russian tables, from aristocratic dinners to modern celebrations. Red salmon roe is the more everyday choice today, while black sturgeon caviar remains a luxury.
