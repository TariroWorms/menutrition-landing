🍲 Tripe Soup (İşkembe Çorbası)

İşkembe çorbası is a classic Turkish tripe soup: cleaned tripe simmered until tender, cut into strips and returned to its broth, which is thickened with egg and lemon and finished with butter. At the table it is seasoned with garlic steeped in vinegar. The soup is tangy, garlicky and silky, and is traditionally eaten late at night or early in the morning.

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g cleaned beef tripe
  • 2 L water
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 30 g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 60 ml vinegar
  • Salt, pepper and chilli flakes

Method

  1. Rinse the tripe well, cover with water and simmer for 2–2.5 hours until very tender.
  2. Remove the tripe, cut into thin strips and return to the strained broth; season with salt.
  3. Whisk the egg yolk with lemon juice, slowly whisk in a ladle of hot broth, then stir the mixture back into the pot without boiling.
  4. Melt the butter with chilli flakes and drizzle over the soup.
  5. Mix the garlic with vinegar and serve it alongside so everyone can season to taste.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the butter drizzle or skip it; most of the flavour comes from garlic and lemon.
  • Skim the fat from the broth after simmering.
  • Tripe is low in calories but filling, so a large bowl fits easily into a light meal.

History

Tripe soup has a long history in Ottoman and Turkish cooking, and dedicated tripe-soup shops (işkembeci) still operate in Istanbul and other cities, often open late into the night. It is commonly eaten after a night out.
