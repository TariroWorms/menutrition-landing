Sparkling mineral water is natural mineral water with carbon dioxide, either naturally present at the spring or added at bottling. It has lively bubbles and a clean, slightly salty or mineral taste that varies with the source. It is served chilled, often with a slice of lemon or lime, as a table water with meals or on its own.

Recipe

1 servings1 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml sparkling mineral water, chilled
  • Ice cubes (optional)
  • A slice of lemon or lime or a few mint leaves (optional)

Method

  1. Chill the bottle well.
  2. Pour into a glass over ice if you like.
  3. Add a slice of citrus or some mint and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • It has no calories, so it is an easy swap for soft drinks and juice.
  • Flavour it with citrus slices, cucumber or mint instead of syrups.
  • Keep a bottle on the table with meals to sip between bites.

History

Naturally carbonated springs have been valued in Europe for centuries, and bottled sparkling mineral water became widely sold in the 19th century. Today it is drunk all over the world as an everyday alternative to still water and sweet soft drinks.
