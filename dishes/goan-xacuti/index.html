Chicken xacuti (Goan chicken curry)

Origin: India · Goa

Xacuti, pronounced 'sha-koo-ti', is a Goan curry whose sauce is made from dry-roasted coconut and a long list of whole spices. The result is a dark, thick, nutty gravy with warm and peppery notes. Poppy seeds add body. It is usually made with chicken and eaten with bread or rice.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken pieces, bone-in
  • 80 g grated coconut, dry-roasted
  • 1 tbsp poppy seeds
  • 4 dried red chillies, 1 tbsp coriander seeds
  • 2 onions (250 g), 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp oil (15 ml), turmeric, nutmeg, salt

Method

  1. Dry-roast the coconut, poppy seeds, chillies and coriander seeds until dark and fragrant.
  2. Grind with garlic and water into a smooth paste.
  3. Brown the onions in oil, add the chicken and turmeric and fry 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the spice paste and 350 ml water and bring to a simmer.
  5. Cook covered 30 minutes until the chicken is tender and the gravy thick.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin before cooking.
  • Dry-roasting means the dish needs very little oil.
  • Serve with a small portion of rice and extra vegetables.

History

Xacuti is associated with the Catholic and Hindu kitchens of Goa and is often served at festive and celebration meals. Its spice blend is typically freshly ground at home.
