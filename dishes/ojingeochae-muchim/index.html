Ojingeochae muchim (seasoned dried shredded squid)

Ojingeochae muchim is shredded dried squid tossed in a sticky, sweet and spicy red sauce. It is pleasantly chewy and has a deep seafood aroma. It is served cold as a side dish with rice.

What it is made of

Recipe

8 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g shredded dried squid
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp gochugaru
  • 2 tbsp sugar or corn syrup
  • 1 tbsp mayonnaise (optional)
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds

Method

  1. Cut the squid into short pieces and soak briefly in warm water.
  2. Squeeze out the water.
  3. Stir gochujang, gochugaru, sugar, garlic and sesame oil in a pan over low heat for 1 minute.
  4. Remove from heat and add the squid.
  5. Mix well, adding mayonnaise if you like, and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the mayonnaise and reduce sugar to cut calories.
  • Eat a small portion since dried squid is high in sodium.
  • Pair with fresh vegetables or plain rice.

History

It is a popular banchan and lunch-box item in Korea, and also a favorite snack to eat with drinks. Pre-shredded dried squid is sold in packs, so the dish is quick to make.
