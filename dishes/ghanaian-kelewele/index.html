Kelewele (spiced fried plantain)

Origin: Ghana

Kelewele is a popular Ghanaian snack of ripe plantain cubes marinated in ginger, chili and spices and deep-fried until crisp outside and soft inside. It is sweet, hot and aromatic at once. It is sold in the evening at street corners, wrapped in paper, often with roasted peanuts. It also works as a side with beans or rice.

What it is made of

plantaingingerscotch bonnet pepperclovesoil

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g ripe plantain
  • 20 g fresh ginger
  • 3 g scotch bonnet pepper
  • 1 g cloves
  • 200 ml vegetable oil
  • 3 g salt

Method

  1. Peel ripe plantain and cut into 2 cm cubes.
  2. Mash ginger, chili, cloves and salt into a paste with a little water.
  3. Coat the plantain cubes and rest 15 minutes.
  4. Fry in hot oil in batches until deep golden.
  5. Drain on paper and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the cubes with a spoon of oil.
  • Serve with unsalted peanuts for protein.
  • Choose plantain that is yellow with black patches, not fully black, for less sugar.

History

Kelewele is a favourite street food in Accra and other Ghanaian cities, usually sold at dusk. Plantain is a daily crop in the country and this is one of its most loved preparations.
