Patacón zuliano (Maracaibo plantain sandwich)

Origin: Venezuela · Zulia
858kcal

The patacón zuliano replaces bread with two large discs of green plantain that are fried, pressed flat and fried again until crisp. Between them go shredded beef or chicken, salty white cheese, shredded cabbage, tomato and plenty of sauces. It is huge, messy and deeply satisfying. In Maracaibo it is the classic late-night street food.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large green plantains (600 g)
  • 250 g cooked shredded beef
  • 100 g salty white cheese
  • 100 g shredded cabbage
  • 1 tomato (100 g)
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise and ketchup (30 g)
  • oil for frying

Method

  1. Peel the plantains and cut each lengthwise into two thick halves.
  2. Fry them at 160 °C for 4 minutes until soft but pale.
  3. Press each piece flat between two boards to a wide thin disc.
  4. Fry again at 180 °C until golden and crisp; drain on paper.
  5. Top one disc with beef, cheese, cabbage, tomato and sauces, then cover with a second disc.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the flattened plantain at 220 °C brushed with a little oil instead of deep-frying.
  • Swap the beef for grilled chicken breast and go easy on mayonnaise.
  • Share one patacón between two people with extra salad.

History

The sandwich is credited to the street vendors of Maracaibo, capital of Zulia state, where it became popular in the second half of the 20th century. It is now sold across Venezuela, but locals still consider the Zulia version the real one.
