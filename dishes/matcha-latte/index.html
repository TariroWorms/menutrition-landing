🍵 Matcha Latte

Origin: Japan

A matcha latte is powdered Japanese green tea whisked into a smooth concentrate and topped with steamed milk. It is vibrant green, creamy and gently grassy, with a natural sweetness from the milk. It is served hot in a cup or over ice, and many cafés offer it as an alternative to coffee drinks.

What it is made of

Recipe

1 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 2 g matcha powder (1 tsp)
  • 30 ml hot water (about 80 °C)
  • 220 ml milk

Method

  1. Sift the matcha into a bowl.
  2. Add the hot water and whisk briskly in a zigzag until frothy and smooth.
  3. Steam or heat the milk and froth it.
  4. Pour the milk over the matcha, or pour the matcha over iced milk for a cold version.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skimmed or semi-skimmed milk to cut calories.
  • Skip added syrups; the milk already gives sweetness.
  • A smaller 180 ml cup is an easy way to trim the total.

History

Matcha has been part of Japanese tea ceremony for centuries, while the milk-based latte is a modern café invention that became popular internationally in the 2010s.
