Pyachysta (braised whole pork)

Origin: Belarus

Pyachysta is a festive Belarusian dish in which a big piece of pork, or sometimes game or poultry, is cooked whole. It is first browned, then braised slowly with onion, carrot and spices until meltingly tender. The meat is sliced at the table and served with its juices, potatoes and pickles.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork shoulder in one piece
  • 2 onions (160 g)
  • 2 carrots (150 g)
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 400 ml water or stock
  • salt

Method

  1. Rub the pork with salt, pepper and crushed garlic and leave for at least 1 hour.
  2. Brown the meat on all sides in a heavy pot.
  3. Add the onions and carrots cut into large pieces, the bay leaves and the liquid.
  4. Cover and braise at 160 °C for about 2.5 hours, turning the meat once or twice.
  5. Rest the pork for 15 minutes, slice and pour over the reduced juices.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat before cooking and skim the fat from the juices.
  • Serve with boiled potatoes and plenty of vegetables rather than bread.
  • Leftovers make a good lean sandwich filling with mustard and greens.

History

The name comes from an old word for cooking meat in one piece, and the dish was traditionally made for holidays and family gatherings. It is often mentioned among the classic dishes of the old Belarusian gentry and village table.
