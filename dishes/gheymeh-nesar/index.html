Gheymeh nesar (Qazvin jeweled rice with lamb)

Origin: Iran · Qazvin

Gheymeh nesar is a festive layered rice from Qazvin. Tiny cubes of lamb are cooked with saffron and layered with rice, barberries, slivered pistachios, almonds and candied orange peel. The result is fragrant, lightly sweet and tart.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g basmati rice
  • 300 g lamb, finely diced
  • 1 onion
  • 50 g barberries, plus 2 tbsp sugar for the topping
  • 50 g pistachios and almonds
  • orange peel
  • 1 pinch saffron, oil and salt

Method

  1. Parboil the rice and drain.
  2. Cook the lamb with the onion until tender and golden, and add the saffron.
  3. Fry barberries briefly with sugar, and candy the orange peel.
  4. Layer rice, lamb and toppings in the pot.
  5. Steam on low heat 40 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and sugar for the toppings.
  • Keep a good balance of rice to meat and add a vegetable side.
  • Choose lean lamb.

History

It is traditionally made for special occasions and weddings in Qazvin. Its small meat pieces give it the name gheymeh.
