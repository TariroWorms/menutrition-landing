Pörkölt (Hungarian meat stew)

Origin: Hungary

Pörkölt is a braised meat stew whose sauce is made mainly from slowly cooked onions and paprika. It is thicker and less soupy than goulash, with a deep sweet-savoury flavour. It is usually served with nokedli dumplings, boiled potatoes or bread.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g pork shoulder or beef, cubed
  • 3 large onions, chopped
  • 2 tbsp lard or oil
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 green pepper
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • salt, caraway, about 200 ml water

Method

  1. Cook the onions gently in lard until golden.
  2. Take off the heat and stir in the paprika.
  3. Add the meat, salt and a splash of water.
  4. Cover and simmer for 1 hour 30 minutes, adding water sparingly.
  5. Add pepper, tomato and garlic for the last 20 minutes.
  6. Serve with dumplings, rice or potatoes.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the visible fat and use a lean cut.
  • Serve with a cucumber salad and fewer dumplings.
  • Use the oil sparingly; the onions give most of the body.

History

Pörkölt is a core dish of Hungarian home cooking and was shaped by the arrival of paprika in the national cuisine. It can be made with beef, pork, veal or chicken.
