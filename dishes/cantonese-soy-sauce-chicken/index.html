Soy sauce chicken (Chi you ji)

Origin: China · Guangdong

A whole chicken is poached in a soy-based liquor of rock sugar, wine and spices until lacquered a deep mahogany. The meat is tender and perfumed, with a savory-sweet glaze. It is chopped and served with rice or noodles, often with a little of the cooking liquid.

What it is made of

chickensoy sauceshaoxing winegingerstar anisesugar

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken, about 1.3 kg
  • 200 ml light soy sauce
  • 50 ml dark soy sauce
  • 80 g rock sugar
  • 100 ml Shaoxing wine
  • 3 slices ginger, 2 star anise
  • 600 ml water

Method

  1. Combine soy sauces, sugar, wine, ginger, star anise and water in a pot and bring to a boil.
  2. Lower in the chicken, breast side down.
  3. Simmer gently 10 minutes, turn and simmer 10 more.
  4. Turn off the heat and let it sit covered for 20 minutes.
  5. Lift out, brush with the liquid, rest and chop.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin before eating.
  • Dilute the liquor to use less soy.
  • Pair with plenty of vegetables.

History

It is a staple of Cantonese roast-meat shops in Guangdong and Hong Kong. The same braising liquid, a "master stock", is reused and refreshed many times.
