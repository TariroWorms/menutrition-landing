Parippu (Sri Lankan red lentil curry)

Origin: Sri Lanka

Parippu is a soft, creamy dhal of red lentils simmered with turmeric and finished with coconut milk and a tempering of onion, curry leaves and mustard seed. It is mild, comforting and gently aromatic. It is on almost every rice-and-curry table in Sri Lanka.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g red lentils
  • 700 ml water
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 onion
  • 2 green chilies
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 150 ml coconut milk
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • salt

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils and simmer with water, turmeric and half the onion.
  2. Cook until soft, about 20 minutes.
  3. Stir in coconut milk and salt.
  4. Temper mustard seeds, onion and curry leaves in a little oil.
  5. Pour the tempering over the dhal and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk.
  • Serve with brown rice.
  • Add leafy greens for fiber.

History

Parippu is the everyday lentil dish of Sri Lankan households and a fixture of rice-and-curry meals. Its simple ingredients make it a daily staple.
