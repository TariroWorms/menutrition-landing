Chickpea salad is a hearty, fresh bowl of boiled chickpeas tossed with diced tomato, cucumber and plenty of chopped parsley. A simple dressing of lemon juice and olive oil brings it together. It is nutty, juicy and filling, and works as a light lunch, a side dish or a packed meal.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cans chickpeas (about 480 g drained), or 200 g dried chickpeas, cooked
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1 large bunch parsley, chopped
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, ½ tsp cumin

Method

  1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas.
  2. Dice the tomato and cucumber and chop the parsley.
  3. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oil, cumin, salt and pepper.
  4. Toss everything together and leave for 10 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Measure the olive oil — 1 tbsp ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Add more cucumber and tomato and fewer chickpeas for a lighter, bigger bowl.
  • Use extra lemon juice and herbs for flavour instead of more oil.

History

Salads built on chickpeas and fresh vegetables are eaten across the Eastern Mediterranean and the Middle East, where chickpeas have been a staple for thousands of years.
