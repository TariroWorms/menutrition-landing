Ciorbă de fasole cu afumătură (Romanian smoked bean soup)

Origin: Romania

A thick soup of white beans with smoked pork ribs or ham hock, carrot, onion and tomato. It is soured with vinegar or borș and has a smoky, hearty flavor. In restaurants it is often served in a hollowed bread loaf.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g dried white beans
  • 300 g smoked ribs
  • 2 carrots
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 l water
  • vinegar, parsley

Method

  1. Soak beans overnight and boil 30 minutes, then drain.
  2. Cover with fresh water and add ribs.
  3. Add diced onion and carrot.
  4. Simmer until beans are soft, about 1 hour.
  5. Stir in tomato paste.
  6. Season with vinegar and parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose less fatty smoked meat.
  • Skim the fat.
  • Eat without the bread bowl to cut carbs.

History

Beans and smoked meat were winter staples across Romania. The bread bowl serving is a restaurant tradition.
