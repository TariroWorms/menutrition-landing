Buttered toast is sliced bread browned under a grill or in a toaster until crisp and golden, then spread with butter while still hot so it melts into the surface. The outside crunches while the middle stays slightly soft. It is a staple breakfast in many countries, eaten on its own or with jam and a cup of tea.

What it is made of

breadbutter

Recipe

1 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 2 slices bread (about 50 g)
  • 10 g butter
  • A pinch of salt (optional)

Method

  1. Toast the bread in a toaster or under a hot grill until golden on both sides.
  2. Spread with butter immediately so it melts in.
  3. Serve straight away.

How to make it lighter

  • Spread butter thinly: 10 g butter is about 75 kcal.
  • Choose wholegrain bread for more fibre and fullness per slice.
  • Have one buttered slice with a side of fresh fruit or tomatoes instead of two.

History

Toasting bread is an old way of using up and reviving bread, and buttered toast is now a standard breakfast across Europe, North America and beyond.
