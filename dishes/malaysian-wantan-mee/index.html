Wantan mee (Malaysian char siu noodles)

Origin: Malaysia

Malaysian wantan mee is a plate of springy egg noodles tossed in dark soy sauce, lard or oil and served with slices of sweet roasted char siu pork. Boiled wontons and blanched choy sum come alongside, with pickled green chilies and a small bowl of clear broth. The dry version is the most popular, although a soup version also exists.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g fresh egg noodles
  • 150 g char siu pork, sliced
  • 12 wontons (pork and shrimp filling)
  • 200 g choy sum
  • 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • pickled green chilies

Method

  1. Boil the wontons for 4 minutes and lift out.
  2. Blanch the choy sum for 1 minute.
  3. Cook the noodles briefly, drain well and rinse in hot water.
  4. Toss with dark soy, oyster sauce and oil.
  5. Top with char siu, wontons and greens and serve with broth.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean char siu and trim the fat.
  • Reduce the oil and soy in the dressing.
  • Add double greens.

History

The dish came from Cantonese cooking and was adapted in Malaysia, where the noodles tend to be darker and more savoury. It is sold at hawker stalls and kopitiams all day long.
