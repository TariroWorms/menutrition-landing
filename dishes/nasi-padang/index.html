Nasi Padang (Minang rice with assorted dishes)

Origin: Indonesia · West Sumatra

Nasi Padang is a plate of steamed rice surrounded by small portions of Minangkabau dishes. Typical choices are beef rendang, curried vegetables, boiled cassava leaves, egg in chili sauce and a spoon of green chili sambal. The flavors range from sweet coconut to fiery chili, and the rice brings them together. In restaurants the dishes are brought to the table and you pay for what you eat.

Recipe

2 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g steamed rice
  • 100 g beef rendang
  • 100 g cassava leaves, boiled
  • 1 boiled egg in chili sauce (50 g)
  • 50 g curried jackfruit or vegetables
  • 1 tbsp green chili sambal

Method

  1. Cook the rice until fluffy.
  2. Prepare or reheat the rendang until the sauce is almost dry.
  3. Boil the cassava leaves and season with coconut milk and salt.
  4. Simmer the egg in a chili paste until coated.
  5. Plate rice with all the sides and a spoon of sambal.

How to make it lighter

  • Go for vegetable dishes and grilled items more than fried ones.
  • Ask for less sauce and coconut gravy.
  • Use half the rice and add extra greens.

History

Nasi Padang comes from the Minangkabau people of West Sumatra, who spread their cooking across Indonesia through migration. Padang restaurants are now found in nearly every Indonesian city.
