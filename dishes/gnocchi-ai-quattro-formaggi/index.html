🧀 Gnocchi ai Quattro Formaggi

Gnocchi ai quattro formaggi are soft potato dumplings tossed in a sauce of four melted cheeses: gorgonzola, fontina, parmesan and mozzarella. The gnocchi are pillowy and light, while the sauce is rich, stretchy and gently pungent from the blue cheese. It is served straight from the pan, piping hot, often as a first course.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g floury potatoes
  • 200 g plain flour
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 80 g gorgonzola, diced
  • 80 g fontina, diced
  • 60 g mozzarella, diced
  • 40 g parmesan, grated
  • 100 ml gnocchi cooking water

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins until tender, peel while warm and press through a ricer.
  2. Mix in the flour and salt to a soft dough, roll into ropes and cut into 2 cm pieces.
  3. Melt the gorgonzola, fontina and mozzarella gently with the hot cooking water in a wide pan over low heat, stirring until smooth.
  4. Boil the gnocchi in salted water until they float, then lift them into the cheese sauce.
  5. Toss with half the parmesan, sprinkle over the rest and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Use two cheeses instead of four, at half the total amount, and thin the sauce with cooking water.
  • Keep the portion to about 200 g and add a large green salad alongside.
  • Grate parmesan finely — a little covers more and adds plenty of flavour.

History

Four-cheese sauces are a modern Italian classic served with both pasta and gnocchi, and the dish is widely found on restaurant menus in Italy and abroad.
