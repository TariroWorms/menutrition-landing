Guilin rice noodles (guilin mifen)

Origin: China · Guangxi

Guilin mifen is a bowl of soft, slippery rice noodles topped with slices of braised pork or beef, roasted peanuts, pickled long beans and scallions. The broth is clear and savory and often seasoned at the table with chili, pickles and vinegar. It is a common breakfast and snack in Guilin.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g fresh rice noodles
  • 150 g braised pork slices
  • 30 g roasted peanuts
  • 40 g pickled long beans
  • 500 ml savory broth
  • 2 scallions
  • chili sauce to taste

Method

  1. Heat the broth and keep it simmering.
  2. Dip the noodles in boiling water for 20 seconds and drain.
  3. Place noodles in bowls and top with sliced braised pork.
  4. Add peanuts, pickled beans and scallions.
  5. Ladle over hot broth and add chili sauce as desired.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork or chicken and skim fat from the broth.
  • Add fresh greens for fibre.
  • Use half the pickles and chili sauce to lower sodium.

History

Rice noodles are a staple in southern China, where rice is the main crop. Guilin noodles are known for the meat braised in a spiced master sauce and for the variety of toppings added at the stall.
