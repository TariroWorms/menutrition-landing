🥒 Tzatziki

Tzatziki is a cool Greek dip of thick strained yogurt mixed with grated, squeezed cucumber, garlic, olive oil and fresh dill. It is creamy, tangy and refreshing, with a clear garlic bite. It is served as a meze with raw vegetables, and as a sauce for souvlaki, gyros and grilled meat or fish.

Recipe

6 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g strained (Greek-style) yogurt
  • 1 cucumber
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • Salt

Method

  1. Grate the cucumber, salt it lightly and squeeze out as much liquid as possible.
  2. Mix the yogurt with the garlic, lemon juice and olive oil.
  3. Stir in the cucumber and dill and season with salt.
  4. Chill for at least 30 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat strained yogurt for a lighter dip with the same thickness.
  • Serve with cucumber, pepper and carrot sticks for dipping.
  • Use tzatziki in place of richer creamy sauces with grilled meat.

History

Tzatziki is a staple of Greek tavernas and is related to similar yogurt-and-cucumber dishes across the eastern Mediterranean, such as Turkish cacık. Its name is usually traced to the Turkish word for that dish.
