Riskrem (Norwegian rice cream)

Origin: Norway

Riskrem is cooked rice porridge that is cooled and folded with softly whipped cream. It is smooth, lightly sweet and flecked with vanilla. A warm red berry sauce is poured over it at the table.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g short-grain rice
  • 500 ml milk
  • 200 ml whipped cream
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 vanilla pod
  • 200 g red berries
  • 1 tbsp sugar for the sauce

Method

  1. Simmer the rice with milk and vanilla for 40 minutes, stirring often.
  2. Stir in the sugar and cool completely.
  3. Remove the vanilla pod.
  4. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  5. Fold the cream into the cold rice.
  6. Heat the berries with sugar and a splash of water until saucy.
  7. Spoon the berry sauce over each portion.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace half the cream with plain yogurt to cut the fat.
  • Use less sugar and let the berries add sweetness.
  • Cook the rice in low-fat milk for the same creaminess.

History

It grows out of the everyday rice porridge tradition and became a festive dessert, especially around Christmas. A whole almond is often hidden in the bowl for a small game.
