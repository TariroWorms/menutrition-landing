Pampaku (Bolivian earth-oven meats and vegetables)

Origin: Bolivia

Pampaku is a festive meal of beef, pork, chicken, potatoes, sweet potatoes, corn and fava beans cooked together under heat in a covered hole or large pot. The ingredients stay juicy and take on a light smoky aroma. It is eaten with the hands, often at weekend gatherings.

What it is made of

Recipe

8 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g beef
  • 500 g pork
  • 800 g potatoes
  • 400 g sweet potatoes
  • 4 ears of corn
  • 300 g fava beans
  • 2 tbsp aji paste
  • salt, herbs

Method

  1. Season the meats with salt, aji and herbs.
  2. Line a large pot with leaves or a cloth.
  3. Layer meats first, then potatoes, sweet potatoes, corn and beans.
  4. Cover tightly and cook on low heat for 2.5 hours.
  5. Open and serve everything on one large platter.

How to make it lighter

  • Use leaner cuts of meat.
  • Double the vegetables relative to meat.
  • Season with herbs rather than extra salt.

History

The cooking method resembles the Andean earth oven, where food is covered and cooked by hot stones. Today the pampaku is also made in large pots in the home or at restaurants.
