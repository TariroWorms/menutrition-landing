Chueotang (Namwon loach soup)

Origin: South Korea · Namwon
220kcal

Chueotang is a hearty, earthy soup made from loach that is cooked and strained into a smooth broth. It is thickened with ground perilla seeds and filled with cabbage or radish greens, soybean paste and a little chili. Served boiling hot with rice, it is seasoned at the table with sansho pepper or chopped chili.

What it is made of

Loachnapa cabbageperilla leavesdoenjanggarlic

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g loach, cleaned
  • 300 g napa cabbage or radish greens
  • 3 tbsp ground perilla seeds (30 g)
  • 2 tbsp doenjang (35 g)
  • 4 garlic cloves (15 g)
  • 1 l water
  • chili, salt

Method

  1. Boil the loach in water for 20 minutes until falling apart.
  2. Mash and strain through a sieve to remove bones, keeping the broth.
  3. Blanch the greens and mix with doenjang and garlic.
  4. Add the greens to the broth with the perilla seeds and simmer 25 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and chili and serve hot with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip extra salt: doenjang already seasons the soup.
  • Add more greens for fibre.
  • Pair with a small portion of rice to keep the meal balanced.

History

Loach soup is a rural Korean dish, especially associated with the town of Namwon, and is eaten as a nourishing meal in autumn. Regional versions differ in thickness and seasoning.
