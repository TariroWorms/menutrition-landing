🫑 Egyptian Mixed Mahshi

Egyptian mixed mahshi is a platter of bell peppers, zucchini, eggplant and cabbage leaves stuffed with rice seasoned with dill, tomato and herbs, then simmered in a tomato broth. The vegetables turn soft and the rice absorbs the herby, tangy liquid. It is a family-style dish served warm, often as a centrepiece of Friday lunch.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 3 bell peppers
  • 3 small zucchini
  • 3 small eggplants
  • ½ head cabbage, leaves separated
  • 300 g short-grain rice, rinsed
  • 1 bunch dill, chopped
  • 400 g tomatoes, grated
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Blanch the cabbage leaves until pliable; hollow out the peppers, zucchini and eggplants.
  2. Mix the rice with dill, onion, half the tomato, oil, salt and pepper.
  3. Fill the vegetables loosely and roll the cabbage leaves around spoonfuls of filling.
  4. Pack tightly into a pot, add the remaining tomato and water to almost cover.
  5. Simmer covered for 45–60 minutes until the rice is tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Fill the vegetables loosely: more vegetable and less rice per bite.
  • Use 1 tbsp oil in the filling; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve with a cucumber and tomato salad on the side.

History

Stuffed vegetables are common across the former Ottoman lands, and mahshi is one of the best-loved home dishes in Egypt today, made in large batches for family meals and holidays.
