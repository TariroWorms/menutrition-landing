Phaksha paa (Bhutanese pork with chillies)

Origin: Bhutan

Phaksha paa is a rich Bhutanese stew of pork belly slices simmered with dried red chillies and white radish. The fat melts into a fiery, savoury sauce. It is served with red rice.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork belly, sliced
  • 3 tbsp dried red chilli flakes
  • 200 g white radish, sliced
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 200 ml water
  • salt

Method

  1. Place the pork in a pot with water and bring to a boil.
  2. Add chilli, garlic and ginger and simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Add the radish and onion.
  4. Cook until the pork is tender and the radish soft, about 20 minutes.
  5. Reduce the sauce a little.
  6. Season and serve with red rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat or use leaner pork.
  • Add extra radish and greens.
  • Skim off fat before serving.

History

Pork and dried meat were practical in cold mountain settings. Phaksha means pork in Dzongkha, and the dish is a favourite for gatherings.
