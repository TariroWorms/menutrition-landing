A poke bowl is a bowl of seasoned sushi rice topped with cubes of raw tuna dressed in soy sauce and sesame oil, plus avocado and edamame. It combines cool, silky fish with slightly sweet, vinegared rice and creamy and crunchy toppings, and is eaten as a filling one-bowl lunch.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g sushi rice
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar and 1 tsp sugar
  • 250 g sashimi-grade tuna, cubed
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 100 g shelled edamame
  • 1 small cucumber, sliced

Method

  1. Rinse and cook the rice, then season with the vinegar, sugar and a pinch of salt; cool slightly.
  2. Toss the tuna with the soy sauce and sesame oil and chill 10 minutes.
  3. Cook the edamame in boiling water 3 minutes and cool.
  4. Divide the rice between bowls and top with tuna, avocado, edamame and cucumber.

How to make it lighter

  • Ask for half rice and half greens, or extra cucumber, to cut the carbs.
  • Go easy on the sauce: 1 tbsp sesame oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Half an avocado is plenty; it is the main source of fat after the oil.

History

Poke, meaning "to cut into pieces" in Hawaiian, began as a fishermen's snack of raw fish seasoned with salt and seaweed; soy sauce and sesame oil came with Japanese influence in Hawaii. The rice bowl format spread from Hawaii to the US mainland and worldwide in the 2010s.
