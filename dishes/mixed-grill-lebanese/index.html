🍢 Lebanese mixed grill

Origin: Lebanon · Levant

A Lebanese mixed grill brings together three classic skewers: spiced lamb kafta, marinated chicken shish tawook and tender lamb cubes, grilled over charcoal with onions and tomatoes. It arrives on a platter lined with thin pita that soaks up the juices, with toum, the fluffy Lebanese garlic sauce, on the side. The meats are charred outside and juicy within.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g minced lamb mixed with chopped onion and parsley (kafta)
  • 400 g chicken breast, cubed, marinated in lemon, garlic and paprika
  • 400 g lamb leg, cubed
  • 2 onions and 3 tomatoes, quartered
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp seven-spice, salt
  • 4 thin pita breads
  • Toum (garlic sauce), to serve

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken and lamb cubes with oil, spices and salt for at least 1 hour.
  2. Shape the kafta mixture around flat skewers.
  3. Thread the chicken, lamb, onions and tomatoes onto skewers.
  4. Grill over hot charcoal, turning, until the meats are cooked through and charred at the edges.
  5. Serve on pita with grilled vegetables and toum.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose more chicken tawook; it is the leanest of the three meats.
  • Toum is rich, so take a teaspoon rather than a spoonful.
  • Swap part of the bread for tabbouleh-style salad of herbs and tomato.

History

Charcoal grilling of meat skewers is an old tradition across the Levant, and Lebanese grill houses made the combined platter a restaurant staple. Today mixed grill is one of the most ordered dishes in Lebanese restaurants at home and abroad.
