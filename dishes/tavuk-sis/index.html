🍢 Chicken shish (tavuk şiş)

Origin: Turkey

Tavuk şiş is Turkish chicken shish kebab: cubes of chicken marinated in yogurt, paprika, grated onion and black pepper, threaded on skewers and grilled over charcoal until charred at the edges and juicy inside. The yogurt tenderises the meat. It is served with grilled peppers, tomatoes, onion salad and rice or flatbread.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g boneless chicken, cubed
  • 150 g plain yogurt
  • 1 onion, grated, juice only
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Mix yogurt, onion juice, paprika, pepper, oil and salt.
  2. Coat the chicken and marinate for at least 2 hours.
  3. Thread onto skewers.
  4. Grill over high heat for 10–12 minutes, turning, until cooked through and charred.

How to make it lighter

  • Use chicken breast for the leanest skewers.
  • Serve with grilled vegetables and salad instead of a large bed of rice.
  • The yogurt marinade needs only a little oil — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

'Şiş' simply means skewer in Turkish. Chicken shish is a staple of Turkish kebab houses and is eaten across the Middle East, where it is known as shish tawook.
