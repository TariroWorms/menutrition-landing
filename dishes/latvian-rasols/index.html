Rasols (Latvian potato salad)

Origin: Latvia

Rasols is the Latvian version of the festive potato salad found across Eastern Europe: boiled potatoes, carrots, pickles, eggs, peas and diced meat or ham bound with mayonnaise. It is creamy, savoury and slightly tangy from the pickles. It is a fixture on New Year and birthday tables and is often served with rye bread.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g boiled potatoes
  • 150 g boiled carrots
  • 150 g cooked ham or chicken
  • 150 g pickled cucumbers
  • 100 g green peas
  • 3 boiled eggs
  • 150 g mayonnaise with a spoon of sour cream

Method

  1. Cool the boiled vegetables and eggs completely.
  2. Dice potatoes, carrots, ham, pickles and eggs into even small cubes.
  3. Add peas and gently toss everything together.
  4. Fold in mayonnaise and sour cream, season with salt and pepper.
  5. Chill for at least an hour before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace half the mayonnaise with plain yogurt.
  • Use cooked chicken breast in place of ham.
  • Add extra peas and pickles for volume with few calories.

History

Versions of this salad spread through the region in the twentieth century, and each country developed its own. In Latvia it is a standard holiday dish that every family makes slightly differently.
