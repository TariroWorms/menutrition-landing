🍰 Turkish Revani

Origin: Turkey

Turkish revani is a semolina sponge cake made with yogurt, baked until golden and then soaked in lemon-scented sugar syrup. It is often finished with a generous sprinkle of desiccated coconut, which sticks to the syrupy surface. The texture is moist and soft with a slight grain, and it is served in squares with Turkish tea.

Recipe

12 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 200 g fine semolina
  • 100 g plain flour
  • 150 g sugar
  • 250 g yogurt
  • 80 ml oil
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 30 g desiccated coconut
  • For the syrup: 300 g sugar, 350 ml water, juice of 1 lemon

Method

  1. Boil sugar and water for 8 minutes, add lemon juice and leave to cool.
  2. Heat the oven to 180 °C and oil a baking tin.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the sugar, then mix in semolina, flour and baking powder; stir in yogurt and oil.
  4. Spread in the tin and bake 30–35 minutes until golden.
  5. Pour the cool syrup over the hot cake, sprinkle with coconut and let it soak before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep pieces small, around 60–80 g.
  • Reduce the syrup sugar by a third, or use less syrup overall.
  • Skip the coconut topping to save some fat.

History

Revani is a long-standing dessert of Turkish home baking and pastry shops, related to the syrup-soaked semolina cakes found across the former Ottoman world. The coconut topping is a popular modern Turkish touch.
