Laphet thoke (Burmese tea leaf salad)

Origin: Myanmar

Laphet thoke is a salad of fermented tea leaves tossed with shredded cabbage, tomato, chili, garlic, lime and a crunchy mix of fried beans, peanuts and sesame. The tea leaves give a pleasantly bitter, tangy and umami taste. It is served at gatherings and eaten as a snack or meal.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp fermented tea leaves
  • 150 g shredded cabbage
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 3 tbsp mixed fried beans and peas
  • 2 tbsp roasted peanuts
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 chili

Method

  1. Mash the tea leaves with lime juice and fish sauce.
  2. Mix with the chili and fry garlic.
  3. Arrange cabbage and tomato in a bowl.
  4. Add the tea leaf dressing.
  5. Top with fried beans, peanuts and sesame and toss at the table.

How to make it lighter

  • Use fewer fried beans to cut fat.
  • Add more cabbage and tomato.
  • Mind the caffeine in the evening.

History

Pickled tea has long been part of Burmese culture, including social ceremonies and offered to guests. The salad is a national favourite. The tea contains some caffeine, so it can be mildly stimulating.
