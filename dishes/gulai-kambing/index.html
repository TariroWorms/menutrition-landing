Gulai kambing (Indonesian goat curry)

Origin: Indonesia

Gulai kambing is a golden, creamy curry of goat meat simmered in coconut milk and a ground spice paste. It is fragrant with turmeric, lemongrass, galangal and lime leaf, with a warm chili kick. The meat becomes very tender while the sauce turns thick and glossy. It is usually eaten with white rice or rice cakes.

What it is made of

goat meatcoconut milkturmericlemongrassgalangalchili pepper

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g goat meat, cubed
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 4 shallots, 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 cm turmeric, 3 cm galangal, 1 stalk lemongrass
  • 3 chilies, 3 lime leaves
  • salt, oil

Method

  1. Blend shallots, garlic, turmeric, galangal and chilies into a paste.
  2. Fry the paste in oil until fragrant.
  3. Add the goat, lemongrass and lime leaves and stir to coat.
  4. Pour in coconut milk and 300 ml water and simmer 90 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and cook until the meat is tender and the sauce thick.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the meat before cooking.
  • Use light coconut milk or half milk and half stock.
  • Serve with vegetables and a small portion of rice.

History

Gulai is a family of curries found all over Sumatra and the Malay world, with roots in trade contacts with Indian cooks. Goat gulai is popular at gatherings and festive meals.
