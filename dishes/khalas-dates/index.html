🌴 Khalas dates

Origin: Saudi Arabia · Al-Ahsa

Khalas is one of the most prized date varieties of the Gulf: medium-sized, amber to golden brown, with soft flesh and a rich caramel, toffee-like sweetness. The dates are eaten as they are, a few at a time. They are traditionally offered to guests with Arabic coffee and are a common way to break the fast in Ramadan.

What it is made of

khalas dates

Recipe

4 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 120 g khalas dates (about 12 dates)
  • Water to rinse
  • Arabic coffee to serve

Method

  1. Rinse the dates briefly under cold water and pat them dry.
  2. Arrange them on a small plate.
  3. Serve at room temperature, traditionally with Arabic coffee.

How to make it lighter

  • Count your dates: three dates (about 30 g) are a typical serving.
  • Pair dates with a protein-rich snack to stay full longer.
  • Keep the dish on the table small rather than serving a large bowl.

History

Khalas dates are grown above all in the Al-Ahsa oasis of eastern Saudi Arabia and are also widely cultivated in the UAE and other Gulf countries. They are one of the most popular varieties for serving with coffee across the region today.
