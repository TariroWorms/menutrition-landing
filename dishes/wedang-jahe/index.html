Wedang jahe (Javanese hot ginger drink)

Origin: Indonesia · Java

A warming drink of sliced ginger simmered with palm sugar, lemongrass and sometimes cinnamon or cloves. It is sharp, sweet and aromatic, and leaves a pleasant heat in the throat. Javanese people drink it in the evening and on rainy days, and when they feel a cold coming.

What it is made of

gingerpalm sugarlemongrasswater

Recipe

3 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g fresh ginger, sliced
  • 1 l water
  • 60 g palm sugar
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, bruised
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 cloves

Method

  1. Bruise the sliced ginger with the back of a knife.
  2. Bring the water to a boil with ginger, lemongrass and spices.
  3. Simmer gently for 15 minutes.
  4. Stir in the palm sugar until dissolved.
  5. Strain and serve hot in small cups.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar in half for a nearly calorie-free tea.
  • Add a squeeze of lime for vitamin C.
  • Keep the ginger slices in the pot for a stronger infusion.

History

Wedang is the Javanese word for a hot drink. Ginger drinks are an old part of Javanese home remedies and street-stall menus, and the drink is especially common in Yogyakarta and Central Java.
