Kondré (Cameroonian plantain and goat stew)

Origin: Cameroon · West

Kondré is a thick stew of unripe plantain and goat meat cooked with ginger, spices and palm oil. The plantain turns soft and starchy, thickening the sauce. It is a celebration dish in the Western Highlands.

Recipe

6 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g goat meat
  • 6 green plantains (900 g)
  • 60 ml palm oil
  • 2 onions (200 g)
  • 30 g ginger
  • 1 chili, salt, 1 l water

Method

  1. Brown the goat with onion, ginger and chili.
  2. Add water and simmer 40 minutes.
  3. Add peeled plantain chunks.
  4. Pour in the palm oil.
  5. Cook 40 minutes until soft and thick.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less palm oil.
  • Trim the visible fat from the meat.
  • Add greens.

History

Kondré is associated with the Bamileke and Grassfields communities of the West. It is often cooked for weddings, family reunions and festive gatherings.
