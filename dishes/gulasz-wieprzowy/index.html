Gulasz wieprzowy is a Polish braised stew of pork shoulder cubes cooked with onions, sweet paprika, bell peppers and tomato until the meat is tender and the sauce thick and brick-red. A spoonful of sour cream stirred in at the end makes it smooth and mildly tangy. It is served as a home-style main course, typically with potatoes, buckwheat groats or a simple salad.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g pork shoulder, cubed
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 2 bell peppers, sliced
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 400 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt, pepper, 2 garlic cloves

Method

  1. Brown the pork in the oil in batches and set aside.
  2. Soften the onions and garlic in the same pot, then stir in the paprika.
  3. Return the meat, add the tomatoes and water, season and simmer covered for about 1 hour.
  4. Add the peppers and cook 15 minutes more until the sauce thickens.
  5. Take off the heat, stir in the sour cream and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean pork loin or trim the shoulder well to cut the fat.
  • Use less sour cream or add it to your own plate only — about 1 tbsp per serving.
  • Serve with a big salad or steamed vegetables instead of a large side of potatoes.

History

Goulash reached Poland from Hungary and became a fixture of home cooking and canteens, adapted to local tastes with pork and sour cream. Today gulasz wieprzowy is one of the most common everyday stews in Polish kitchens.
