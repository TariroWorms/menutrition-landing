🍝 Cavatelli with sausage and broccoli rabe

Cavatelli with sausage and friarielli is a southern Italian pasta of small hand-rolled shells sautéed with crumbled sausage, broccoli rabe, garlic and chili. The greens bring a pleasant bitterness that balances the savoury, fatty sausage, while the chili adds gentle heat. The pasta is tossed in the pan juices and served hot.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g cavatelli
  • 300 g sausage, casing removed
  • 500 g broccoli rabe, trimmed
  • 2 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 fresh or dried chili
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Blanch the broccoli rabe in salted boiling water for 3 minutes, drain and chop roughly.
  2. Brown the crumbled sausage in 1 tbsp oil, then add the garlic, chili and greens and sauté for 5 minutes.
  3. Cook the cavatelli in the same salted water until al dente.
  4. Toss the pasta into the pan with a splash of cooking water and the rest of the oil and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 200 g sausage and double the greens to keep the flavour with less fat.
  • Drain the fat from the pan after browning the sausage.
  • Keep to 80 g of dry pasta per person and fill the bowl with broccoli rabe.

History

Friarielli is the Neapolitan name for the tender shoots of broccoli rabe, and sausage with friarielli is a classic pairing in Naples and Campania. Combining them with cavatelli, a traditional pasta of southern Italy, is a common home and trattoria dish in the region today.
