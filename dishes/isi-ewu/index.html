Isi ewu (spiced goat head)

Origin: Nigeria · Igbo

Isi ewu is boiled goat head cut into pieces and tossed in a bright orange paste of palm oil, potash, chili and ground seeds. It is rich, tender and gelatinous, with a hot pepper kick and a little bitterness from utazi leaves. It is a celebratory dish, often eaten with drinks in a group.

What it is made of

goat headred palm oiledible potashonionchili pepperutazi leaves

Recipe

6 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1 goat head, cleaned and cut (about 1.2 kg)
  • 100 ml palm oil
  • 1 tsp potash, dissolved
  • 2 tbsp ground ehuru
  • 2 chili peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 10 utazi leaves
  • salt, stock cube

Method

  1. Boil the goat head pieces with onion and salt until tender, 1.5-2 hours.
  2. Dissolve the potash and take the clear liquid.
  3. Whisk palm oil with the liquid to a yellow paste.
  4. Season with ehuru, chili and stock cube.
  5. Toss the hot meat in the paste.
  6. Garnish with onion and utazi.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim fat from the stock and trim skin.
  • Limit the palm oil in the paste.
  • Serve in small portions with salad.

History

The name means goat head in Igbo, and the dish is a specialty of south-eastern Nigeria. It is served at parties and in pepper-soup bars, usually in a wooden bowl.
