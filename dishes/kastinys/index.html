Kastinys (Samogitian butter and sour cream spread)

Origin: Lithuania · Samogitia

Kastinys is a thick, creamy spread made by whipping soft butter with sour cream until light and fluffy. It tastes rich and slightly tangy and can be seasoned with salt, garlic or fresh dill. It is spooned onto hot boiled potatoes or spread on dark rye bread.

Recipe

8 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g soft butter
  • 200 g sour cream
  • 1 clove garlic, crushed
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • pinch of pepper

Method

  1. Beat the butter until pale and fluffy.
  2. Gradually add the sour cream, beating until smooth.
  3. Stir in garlic, dill, salt and pepper.
  4. Chill for 30 minutes to firm up.
  5. Serve with hot boiled potatoes or rye bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a small spoonful: it is very rich.
  • Replace half the butter with thick yogurt.
  • Use plenty of herbs to add flavour with few calories.

History

It is a traditional dairy specialty of Samogitia in western Lithuania, where butter and sour cream were everyday farm products. It is often served at celebrations and with potato dishes.
