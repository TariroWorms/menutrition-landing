Kazan kebab (Qozon kabob)

Origin: Uzbekistan

Qozon kabob is meat and potatoes cooked the Uzbek way, in a heavy cast-iron kazan. Lamb ribs are seared in their own fat until crisp, then covered and left to steam until tender, while potato chunks fry in the same pot. The dish is finished with raw onion rings, cumin and fresh herbs. It is a favourite at roadside eateries and family weekends.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg lamb ribs
  • 800 g potatoes
  • 2 onions
  • 60 ml oil
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp cumin, salt, black pepper
  • Fresh dill and cilantro

Method

  1. Cut the ribs into single bones and season with salt, pepper and cumin.
  2. Heat oil in a kazan and fry the ribs on high until browned on all sides.
  3. Add a splash of water and garlic, cover and cook on low 40 minutes until tender.
  4. Remove the lid, add potato wedges and fry, turning, until golden and crisp.
  5. Serve topped with thin onion rings and chopped herbs.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose meaty ribs and pour off rendered fat before frying the potatoes.
  • Bake the potatoes separately in the oven with little oil.
  • Serve with a large raw vegetable salad.

History

The name simply means kebab from the kazan, the round-bottomed pot used across Central Asia. It is a common dish in Uzbek chaikhanas, the traditional teahouses.
