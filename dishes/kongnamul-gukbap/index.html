🍲 Kongnamul Gukbap (Bean Sprout Soup with Rice)

Origin: South Korea · Jeonju

Kongnamul gukbap is a Korean soup of soybean sprouts simmered in a light broth and served over rice. Garlic, scallion and gochugaru chili flakes give it a gentle heat, and a few drops of sesame oil finish the bowl. It is clean, crunchy and warming, and is a popular breakfast in Korea.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g soybean sprouts
  • 1.2 l water
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tsp gochugaru
  • 300 g cooked rice
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil, add the sprouts and simmer covered for 10 minutes.
  2. Add garlic and salt and simmer 5 more minutes.
  3. Place rice in bowls and ladle the soup with sprouts over it.
  4. Top with scallion, gochugaru and a few drops of sesame oil.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less rice and more sprouts for a lighter bowl.
  • Keep sesame oil to a teaspoon — 1 tsp is about 40 kcal.
  • Season with chili and garlic rather than extra salt.

History

Kongnamul gukbap is strongly associated with the city of Jeonju, where many restaurants specialize in it and serve it in hot earthenware bowls.
