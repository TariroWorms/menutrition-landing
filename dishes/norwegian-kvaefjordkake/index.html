Kvæfjordkake (Norwegian meringue custard cake)

Origin: Norway

Kvæfjordkake is a layered cake with a soft sponge base, a crisp meringue top scattered with almonds, and a filling of vanilla custard or cream. The sponge is moist, the meringue chewy and crisp, and the filling smooth and lightly sweet. It is cut into squares and served at coffee gatherings. In Norway it is sometimes called the world's best cake.

What it is made of

eggsugarflouralmondscustardmeringue

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • Sponge: 4 egg yolks, 100 g sugar, 100 g butter, 150 g flour, 150 ml milk, 1 tsp baking powder
  • Meringue: 4 egg whites, 200 g sugar, 40 g sliced almonds
  • Filling: 300 ml vanilla custard or whipped cream

Method

  1. Whisk the yolks with sugar, then mix in butter, flour, milk and baking powder.
  2. Spread the batter in a tray. Whisk the egg whites with the sugar to stiff peaks and spread over the batter.
  3. Sprinkle with sliced almonds and bake at 175 C for 35 minutes.
  4. Cool completely.
  5. Cut in half, fill with vanilla custard and put the meringue on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the sugar in the sponge.
  • Fill with skimmed-milk custard instead of cream.
  • Serve small squares.

History

The cake takes its name from Kvæfjord in northern Norway, where it was created in the mid-twentieth century. It was voted Norway's national cake in a poll and is famous across the country.
