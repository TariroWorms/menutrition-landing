Jibarito (plantain sandwich)

Origin: Puerto Rico

A jibarito replaces the bread with two crisp, flattened slices of fried green plantain holding steak, lettuce, tomato, cheese and garlic mayonnaise. The plantain gives a crunchy, savory-sweet bite and the garlic mayonnaise adds richness. It is eaten hot, usually by hand, with the sauce dripping down.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large green plantains
  • 250 g thin steak
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • lettuce and tomato
  • 4 slices cheese
  • oil for frying
  • salt

Method

  1. Mix mayonnaise with crushed garlic.
  2. Peel and slice the plantains in half lengthwise or into thick chunks.
  3. Fry until tender, then flatten and fry again until crisp.
  4. Sear the steak with garlic and salt and slice thin.
  5. Spread garlic mayonnaise on two plantain slices and add steak, cheese, lettuce and tomato.
  6. Top with the remaining plantain slices and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the plantain slices on a rack instead of frying twice.
  • Use grilled chicken or lean steak.
  • Go light on mayonnaise and add more vegetables.

History

The jibarito was created in Chicago's Puerto Rican community in the 1990s and takes its name from the Puerto Rican word for a country dweller. It has since become an icon of Puerto Rican cooking abroad.
