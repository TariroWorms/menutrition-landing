Galinhada (Brazilian chicken and rice)

Origin: Brazil · Goiás
578kcal

Galinhada is a one-pot dish of chicken pieces browned and then cooked with rice until the grains absorb all the juices. In Goiás it is coloured bright yellow with açafrão-da-terra (turmeric) and often cooked with pequi, a cerrado fruit with a strong, cheesy aroma. The result is moist, savoury and comforting. It is finished with parsley and spring onion and served straight from the pot.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • 400 g rice
  • 4 pequi fruits (optional)
  • 2 tsp turmeric
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tomatoes
  • Parsley, spring onion, oil, salt

Method

  1. Season the chicken with garlic and salt for 30 minutes.
  2. Brown it well in a little oil in a heavy pot.
  3. Add onion, tomato and pequi and cook 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in rice and turmeric to coat.
  5. Add hot water to cover by 2 cm and simmer covered 20 minutes.
  6. Rest off the heat 5 minutes.
  7. Scatter parsley and spring onion and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin before browning.
  • Use breast meat for a leaner pot.
  • Add peas, carrot or okra for more vegetables.

History

Galinhada is a country dish of central Brazil, popular at family gatherings and the June festivals. Versions exist across Minas Gerais and the Centre-West, but turmeric and pequi make the Goiás style distinctive.
