Shuba (herring under a fur coat)

Origin: Russia

Shuba, short for "herring under a fur coat", is a layered salad: diced salted herring and onion at the bottom, then boiled potato, carrot and egg, each spread with mayonnaise, finished with a thick layer of grated beet. The colours are striking and the taste is salty, sweet and creamy at once. It is cut into wedges and served cold.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g salted herring fillet
  • 1 small onion
  • 3 potatoes
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 large beets
  • 3 eggs
  • 250 g mayonnaise

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes, carrots, beets and eggs, cool and peel.
  2. Dice the herring and chop the onion finely.
  3. Layer on a platter: herring with onion, grated potato, mayonnaise, grated carrot, mayonnaise, grated egg.
  4. Top with grated beet and spread the remaining mayonnaise over it.
  5. Chill for at least 6 hours before cutting.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace half the mayonnaise with thick yogurt.
  • Use more beet and carrot layers and less potato.
  • Serve as a starter, not a main.

History

Shuba is a fixture of the New Year table in Russia and other former Soviet countries, usually prepared the day before so the layers can settle.
