Chicha venezolana (rice and milk drink)

Origin: Venezuela

Venezuelan chicha is a creamy, non-alcoholic drink made by cooking rice until very soft and blending it with milk, condensed milk, sugar and vanilla. It is served over ice, dusted with cinnamon and sometimes topped with more condensed milk. The texture is thick, almost like a drinkable rice pudding. Street vendors called chicheros sell it from carts in Caracas and other cities.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g white rice
  • 1 litre water
  • 500 ml cold milk
  • 150 g sweetened condensed milk
  • 50 g sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla, ground cinnamon, ice

Method

  1. Soak the rice for 1 hour, then cook it in the water until very soft, about 25 minutes.
  2. Let it cool completely.
  3. Blend the rice and its cooking water with milk, condensed milk, sugar and vanilla until smooth.
  4. Chill well; add a little water if it is too thick.
  5. Serve over ice dusted with cinnamon.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sugar and rely on a little condensed milk.
  • Use semi-skimmed or plant milk.
  • Serve a small glass as a dessert rather than a thirst-quencher.

History

The word chicha names fermented drinks across Latin America, but the Venezuelan street version is sweet, milky and unfermented. Chicheros with their carts are a familiar sight in Venezuelan cities.
