🥩 Viande Séchée (Air-Dried Beef)

Origin: Switzerland · Valais

Viande séchée is lean beef that has been salted, seasoned with herbs and air-dried in cool mountain air for several weeks. It is sliced paper-thin and has a deep red colour, a firm yet tender bite and an intense, concentrated beefy flavour. It is served cold on a platter, often with pickles, or rolled around fresh vegetables as a snack.

Recipe

4 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g viande séchée, thinly sliced
  • 1 cucumber (200 g), cut into sticks
  • 8 cherry tomatoes
  • Fresh herbs to garnish
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Arrange the slices loosely on a cold platter.
  2. Add cucumber sticks and cherry tomatoes around the meat.
  3. Grind over black pepper and scatter with fresh herbs. Serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • A 50 g portion goes a long way: fan out the slices so they look generous.
  • Roll slices around cucumber or pepper sticks for a crunchy, filling snack.
  • Pair with vegetables to keep the platter light.

History

Air-drying meat is a long-standing preservation method in the Alps; the best-known Swiss version is Bündnerfleisch from Graubünden, while the Valais makes its own viande séchée, which has protected regional status.
