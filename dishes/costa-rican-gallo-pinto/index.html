Costa Rican gallo pinto (rice and black beans)

Origin: Costa Rica

Gallo pinto is the national breakfast of Costa Rica: leftover rice and black beans sautéed with onion, sweet pepper and cilantro, and seasoned with the local Salsa Lizano. It tastes savory, earthy and slightly sweet-tangy. It comes with eggs, sour cream, cheese and fried plantain.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked rice
  • 250 g cooked black beans with 100 ml of their liquid
  • 1 small onion
  • 1/2 sweet pepper
  • 2 tbsp cilantro
  • 2 tbsp Salsa Lizano or similar sauce
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a wide pan and soften onion and pepper.
  2. Add the beans with their liquid and the sauce.
  3. Cook for 5 minutes so the liquid reduces.
  4. Fold in the rice and warm through, stirring.
  5. Add cilantro, adjust salt and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and brown rice.
  • Top with an egg instead of fried plantain.
  • Rinse canned beans to cut sodium.

History

Gallo pinto is eaten across Central America with local variations, and Costa Rica's version is known for its sauce and for being served every morning. Many families make it from the previous day's rice and beans.
