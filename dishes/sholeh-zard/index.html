🍮 Sholeh Zard

Sholeh zard is a Persian rice pudding coloured bright yellow with saffron and perfumed with rose water. Rice is cooked until very soft and creamy, then sweetened and finished with a little butter. It is served chilled in bowls, decorated with lines of ground cinnamon and slivered almonds, and has a soft, fragrant, gently sweet taste.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g short-grain rice
  • 1.5 l water
  • 200 g sugar
  • ½ tsp saffron threads, ground and steeped in 3 tbsp hot water
  • 60 ml rose water
  • 20 g butter
  • 30 g slivered almonds
  • Ground cinnamon to decorate

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and simmer in the water for about 45 minutes, stirring often, until very soft.
  2. Add the sugar and stir until dissolved; cook 10 more minutes.
  3. Stir in the saffron water, rose water, butter and most of the almonds; cook on low 15–20 minutes until thick.
  4. Pour into bowls, decorate with cinnamon and the remaining almonds, and chill.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce the sugar by a quarter; saffron and rose water carry the flavour.
  • Serve in small dessert cups of about 100 g.
  • Leave out the butter at the end; the pudding stays creamy from the rice starch.

History

Sholeh zard means 'yellow pudding' in Persian. It is traditionally cooked in large pots and shared with neighbours as nazri, a votive offering, especially during Muharram and on religious occasions, and it is also served at Nowruz and other family gatherings.
