Steamed fish (Jamaican style)

Origin: Jamaica

Jamaican steamed fish is a whole snapper simmered gently in a shallow broth of okra, carrot, bell pepper, thyme and allspice, with a little butter. The fish stays tender and the broth is light but flavorful. It is served with bammy, crackers or boiled bananas.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 red snappers or similar fish (about 1 kg), cleaned
  • 100 g okra, sliced
  • 1 carrot and 1 bell pepper, in strips
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 sprigs thyme and 4 allspice berries
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 300 ml water, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season the fish with salt, pepper and garlic.
  2. Simmer the water with onion, carrot, pepper, thyme and allspice for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the okra and place the fish on top.
  4. Cover and cook 12-15 minutes over medium-low heat.
  5. Add the butter, adjust seasoning and serve with the broth.

How to make it lighter

  • Use little butter for a lighter dish.
  • Add more vegetables to stretch the broth.
  • Remove skin and bones before serving to children.

History

Steamed fish is a common dish in Jamaican restaurants and homes, especially in coastal areas. The method keeps the fish moist with little fat.
