Papanași (Romanian cheese doughnuts)

Papanași are rings of soft cheese dough fried until golden, with a small ball on top. They are served warm with a generous spoon of sour cream and sour cherry or berry jam. Crisp outside and tender inside, they are a favorite dessert.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g cottage cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 150 g flour
  • 30 g sugar
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • oil for frying
  • 100 g sour cream
  • 80 g jam

Method

  1. Mix cheese, eggs, sugar, flour and baking powder.
  2. Rest the dough 10 minutes.
  3. Shape rings with small balls.
  4. Fry in hot oil until golden.
  5. Drain on paper.
  6. Top with sour cream and jam.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake instead of frying.
  • Use less jam.
  • Share a portion.

History

They are a classic of Romanian restaurants and homes, and are sometimes baked too. They are linked to the country's tradition of sweet cheese pastries.
