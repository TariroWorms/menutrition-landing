🍣 Spicy tuna roll

Origin: United States · Japanese-American sushi

A spicy tuna roll is a sushi roll of seasoned rice and nori wrapped around chopped raw tuna mixed with chili sauce and a touch of sesame oil. It is soft, savoury and gently hot, with the clean taste of fresh fish. It is cut into six or eight pieces and served with pickled ginger and wasabi.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked sushi rice
  • 4 sheets nori
  • 200 g sushi-grade tuna, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp chili sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 spring onion, sliced
  • Rice vinegar, sugar and salt to season the rice

Method

  1. Season the warm rice with rice vinegar, a little sugar and salt; let it cool.
  2. Mix the tuna with chili sauce, sesame oil and spring onion.
  3. Spread rice over a nori sheet on a bamboo mat, leaving a strip free at the top.
  4. Lay a line of tuna along the bottom and roll tightly.
  5. Cut each roll into 6–8 pieces with a wet knife.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a thinner layer of rice to lower the carbs.
  • Add cucumber strips to the filling for volume.
  • Order or make it without extra creamy sauces on top.

History

The spicy tuna roll is a Japanese-American creation that spread from sushi bars in the United States, where it became one of the most ordered rolls. It is now common on sushi menus worldwide.
