Pandesal (Filipino bread rolls)

Origin: Philippines

Pandesal are small, pillowy yeast rolls with a gently sweet taste and a fine crust of breadcrumbs. They are best hot from the oven in the early morning, eaten plain, with butter or filled with cheese or corned beef. They are a standard Filipino breakfast.

Recipe

12 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 7 g yeast
  • 60 g sugar
  • 250 ml warm milk
  • 40 g butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Breadcrumbs for rolling

Method

  1. Mix yeast, milk and sugar and let foam.
  2. Knead with flour, egg, butter and salt until smooth.
  3. Rise 1 hour.
  4. Shape into a log, cut into pieces and roll in breadcrumbs.
  5. Rise 30 minutes, bake at 180 C for 15 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Try half whole wheat flour.
  • Cut the sugar a little.
  • Pair with eggs or beans rather than sweet spreads.

History

The name comes from Spanish for salt bread, though the modern roll is soft and mildly sweet. Neighborhood bakeries traditionally bake them at dawn.
