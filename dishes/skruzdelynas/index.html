Skruzdėlynas (Lithuanian ant-hill honey cake)

Origin: Lithuania
258kcal

Skruzdėlynas is a pile of thin fried dough strips glued together with warm honey and sugar and shaped into a mound that looks like an anthill. The strips are crisp, sweet and sticky, sometimes with poppy seeds or nuts. It is served in wedges at festive tables and holidays.

Recipe

10 servings80 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 3 egg yolks (60 g)
  • 100 ml sour cream
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • pinch of salt
  • oil for deep frying
  • 250 g honey and 50 g sugar for glazing

Method

  1. Knead flour, yolks, sour cream, sugar and salt into a firm dough and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Roll thin and cut into thin matchstick strips.
  3. Fry in hot oil in small batches until golden; drain well.
  4. Heat honey with sugar until runny and stir in the strips.
  5. Pile into a mound on a plate, wet your hands and press into shape; cool until set.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the strips instead of frying.
  • Use less honey and eat a small wedge.
  • Add seeds for fibre rather than extra sugar.

History

The name means ant-hill, a reference to the shape, and it is a traditional Lithuanian festive sweet. Honey has long been central to Lithuanian baking because of the country's beekeeping tradition.
