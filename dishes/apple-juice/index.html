Apple juice is the pressed juice of fresh apples, sold either clear and filtered or cloudy with some of the fruit pulp left in. It tastes sweet and mildly tart, with no added sugar needed. It is served chilled on its own, at breakfast or as a base for other drinks.

What it is made of

apple juice

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg sweet, juicy apples
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Ice cubes, to serve

Method

  1. Wash the apples, cut them into quarters and remove the cores.
  2. Run them through a juicer, or blend with a splash of water and strain through a fine sieve or cloth, pressing well.
  3. Stir in the lemon juice to keep the colour bright and serve straight away over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • A 250 ml glass is about 115 kcal — roughly the same as a large apple, but far less filling.
  • Dilute it half and half with sparkling water: the same glass, about half the calories.
  • Choose a small 150 ml glass with a meal rather than drinking juice between meals.

History

Apples have been pressed for juice wherever they are grown, and today the drink is made and sold around the world, often blended from several apple varieties.
