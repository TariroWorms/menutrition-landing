Bauyr kuyrdak (Kazakh fried liver)

Origin: Kazakhstan
425kcal

Bauyr kuyrdak is a quick fry-up of liver cut into small pieces and cooked in hot fat with sliced onions and black pepper. The liver stays soft and slightly pink inside while the onions turn sweet and golden. It is served hot with bread or potatoes and is a favourite dish when an animal has just been slaughtered.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb or beef liver
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp oil or lamb fat
  • Salt, black pepper
  • Fresh herbs to serve

Method

  1. Trim the liver and cut into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Heat the fat in a heavy pan until very hot.
  3. Fry the liver in batches for 2-3 minutes until browned.
  4. Add the onions and fry for 5 minutes more until soft.
  5. Season with salt and pepper, sprinkle with herbs and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a little oil instead of tail fat.
  • Serve with a large salad rather than bread.
  • Do not overcook: tender liver needs only a few minutes.

History

Kuyrdak is the Kazakh name for dishes of fried meat and offal, traditionally made from the freshest parts on the day of slaughter. The liver version is one of the simplest and most common.
