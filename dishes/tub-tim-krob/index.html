🍧 Tub Tim Krob

Origin: Thailand

Tub tim krob, 'crispy rubies', is a Thai dessert of diced water chestnuts coated in tapioca starch and coloured ruby red, boiled until they become chewy and jewel-like. They are served in a bowl of sweetened coconut milk with crushed ice and strips of jackfruit. The contrast of crunchy centres, slippery coating and cold, creamy coconut makes it a favourite on hot days.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g water chestnuts, peeled and diced
  • 100 g tapioca starch
  • A few drops red food colouring or beetroot juice
  • 250 ml coconut milk
  • 60 g sugar
  • 100 g ripe jackfruit, cut into strips
  • Crushed ice
  • 1 pinch salt

Method

  1. Toss the diced water chestnuts with the red colouring and leave for 10 minutes.
  2. Coat them well in the tapioca starch and shake off the excess.
  3. Boil in plenty of water for 2–3 minutes until they float and the coating turns translucent, then transfer to iced water.
  4. Warm the coconut milk with the sugar and salt until dissolved, then cool.
  5. Serve the rubies and jackfruit in bowls with the coconut milk and crushed ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more crushed ice and less coconut milk in each bowl.
  • Cut the sugar in the coconut syrup by a third; the jackfruit adds sweetness.
  • Mixing the coconut milk with some water lightens it noticeably.

History

Tub tim krob is a traditional Thai dessert sold at street stalls and markets across Thailand today, especially in the hot season.
