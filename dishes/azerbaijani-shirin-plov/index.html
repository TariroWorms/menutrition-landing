Shirin plov (Azerbaijani sweet pilaf)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Shirin plov is a meatless, gently sweet pilaf. Steamed saffron rice is served with a topping of chestnuts, dried apricots, raisins and sometimes dates, warmed in butter with a little sugar. It is fragrant, golden and comforting, and can close a meal or be served on its own.

What it is made of

basmati ricechestnutsdried apricotsraisinsbuttersaffronsugar

Recipe

6 servings80 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g basmati rice
  • 200 g peeled chestnuts
  • 120 g dried apricots
  • 80 g raisins
  • 80 g butter
  • 30 g sugar
  • 0.5 g saffron, salt

Method

  1. Soak the rice, boil 6 minutes in lightly salted water and drain.
  2. Steam it covered with half the butter on low heat for 35–40 minutes.
  3. Boil or roast the chestnuts until tender.
  4. Warm the remaining butter, add apricots, raisins, chestnuts and sugar with a splash of water and cook 10 minutes.
  5. Mix saffron water through part of the rice and serve with the fruit on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the sugar; dried fruit is sweet already.
  • Use a spoon of butter in the fruit and none in the rice.
  • Serve a small dessert portion rather than a main.

History

Shirin means sweet. Sweet plov with dried fruit is traditionally made for Novruz and other celebrations in Azerbaijan, when chestnuts and dried fruit are plentiful in winter stores.
