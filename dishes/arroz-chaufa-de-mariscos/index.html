Arroz chaufa de mariscos (seafood fried rice)

Arroz chaufa de mariscos is the seafood version of Peru's favourite chifa dish. Day-old rice is fired in a very hot wok with shrimp, squid rings, scrambled egg, ginger and plenty of spring onion, then seasoned with soy sauce and a drop of sesame oil. The result is smoky, savoury and generous. It is eaten as a main course, often with a little ají on the side.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g cooked rice, cold
  • 250 g peeled shrimp
  • 200 g squid rings
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 spring onions
  • 20 g ginger, 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Beat the eggs and cook a thin omelette in the wok; cut into strips.
  2. Heat oil until smoking and sear the shrimp and squid for 1–2 minutes; set aside.
  3. Fry the ginger, garlic and white part of the spring onions for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the rice and toss over high heat until it starts to toast.
  5. Return the seafood and egg, add soy sauce and sesame oil, and finish with green onion.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half brown rice for extra fibre.
  • Add bean sprouts or bell pepper to stretch the portion with vegetables.
  • Choose low-sodium soy sauce and skip extra salt.

History

Chaufa comes from the Chinese communities that settled in Peru from the 19th century, whose restaurants are called chifas. The name is usually linked to Cantonese words for fried rice, and today chaufa is cooked in homes all over the country.
