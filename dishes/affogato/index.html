🍨 Affogato

Affogato is the simplest Italian dessert: a scoop or two of cold vanilla ice cream in a small glass or cup with a shot of hot espresso poured over it at the table. The coffee melts the edges of the ice cream into a sweet, creamy, bittersweet sauce while the centre stays frozen. It is eaten straight away with a spoon, as a dessert and an after-dinner coffee in one.

What it is made of

Recipe

1 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 2 small scoops vanilla ice cream (about 100 g)
  • 1 shot freshly made hot espresso (about 30 ml)
  • A pinch of cocoa powder to dust (optional)

Method

  1. Chill a small glass or cup in the freezer for a few minutes.
  2. Put the ice cream in the glass.
  3. Brew the espresso and pour it straight over the ice cream at the table.
  4. Eat immediately with a spoon.

How to make it lighter

  • Use one scoop of ice cream instead of two — the coffee still makes it feel like a full dessert.
  • Choose a light vanilla ice cream with less fat and sugar.
  • Skip extra toppings such as chocolate sauce or biscuits.

History

Affogato means “drowned” in Italian, after the ice cream drowned in coffee. It is a fixture of Italian gelaterie and cafés, though its exact origin is not documented, and it is now served in coffee shops and restaurants around the world.
