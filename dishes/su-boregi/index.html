🥧 Su böreği (water börek)

Origin: Turkey

Su böreği is a Turkish layered pastry in which sheets of dough are briefly boiled, then stacked in a tray with a filling of crumbled white cheese and parsley, brushed with a mixture of egg and milk, and baked. The result is soft and tender inside, almost like a cheesy pasta bake, with a golden top. It is cut into squares and served warm with tea.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh yufka (thick pastry sheets)
  • 300 g feta or white cheese, crumbled
  • 1 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 2 eggs
  • 250 ml milk
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Mix the cheese with parsley.
  2. Whisk the eggs, milk, oil and a pinch of salt.
  3. Briefly dip each yufka sheet in boiling salted water, then in cold water, and lay it in an oiled tray.
  4. Brush the layers with the egg mixture, adding the cheese filling halfway through the stack.
  5. Finish with a few more sheets, brush the top and bake at 190 °C for 35–40 minutes until golden.
  6. Rest 10 minutes and cut into squares.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut small squares: about 150 g is one portion.
  • Use a lighter cheese and less of it, adding more parsley.
  • Serve with salad, tomatoes and cucumbers instead of a second piece.

History

Börek dishes developed in Ottoman cuisine, and su böreği is known as one of the more laborious to make by hand. It is a popular breakfast and festive dish across Turkey.
