🍆 Garlic eggplant

Origin: China · Sichuan

Garlic eggplant is a Chinese stir-fry of soft eggplant strips coated in a glossy sauce of garlic, chili, vinegar, sugar and soy sauce. The eggplant turns silky and absorbs the sweet, sour and spicy flavours. It is served hot as a side dish with rice, and its sauce is close to the Sichuan “fish-fragrant” style.

Recipe

3 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g eggplant, cut into strips
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1–2 fresh chilies, sliced
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 spring onions

Method

  1. Mix the vinegar, sugar, soy sauce and 3 tbsp water.
  2. Steam the eggplant strips for 8 minutes until tender, or stir-fry them in the oil until soft.
  3. Heat the oil, fry the garlic and chili for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the eggplant and sauce and toss until glossy; finish with spring onions.

How to make it lighter

  • Steam the eggplant first instead of frying — it soaks up a lot of oil.
  • Use 1 tbsp oil in total; 1 tbsp oil is about 120 kcal.
  • Halve the sugar and add a little more vinegar for balance.

History

Eggplant in garlic sauce is widely associated with Sichuan cooking, where yuxiang or “fish-fragrant” sauces combine garlic, chili, vinegar and sugar without any fish. Versions of it are now standard in Chinese restaurants around the world.
