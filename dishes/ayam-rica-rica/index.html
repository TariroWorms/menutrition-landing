Ayam rica-rica (North Sulawesi spicy chicken)

Origin: Indonesia · North Sulawesi

Ayam rica-rica is chicken pieces cooked in a bright, very hot paste of red chilies, shallots, garlic and ginger, finished with lemongrass, lime leaf and basil. The sauce clings to the meat and is more fragrant than oily. It is served with plain rice to soften the heat.

What it is made of

chickenchili peppershallotsgarlicgingerlemongrasstomato

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken pieces
  • 8 red chilies and 2 bird's-eye chilies
  • 6 shallots, 4 garlic cloves, 3 cm ginger
  • 2 stalks lemongrass, bruised
  • 3 lime leaves
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 handful basil
  • 1 tbsp oil, salt, lime juice

Method

  1. Grind the chilies, shallots, garlic and ginger into a paste.
  2. Fry the paste in oil with the lemongrass and lime leaves until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken and stir to coat.
  4. Pour in 150 ml water, cover and cook 25 minutes.
  5. Add the tomato and cook until the sauce is thick.
  6. Finish with basil and lime juice and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken to cut the fat.
  • Remove chili seeds to keep the heat lower.
  • Serve with plenty of steamed vegetables and a small portion of rice.

History

The dish comes from the Minahasan cuisine of North Sulawesi, which is known for generous use of chili and fresh herbs. "Rica" is a local word for chili.
