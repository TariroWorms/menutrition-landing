Muaddas is an Emirati rice dish cooked together with brown lentils and topped with onions fried until deep golden. Turmeric and cumin give it a warm colour and an earthy flavour, and a spoon of ghee makes it rich. The rice and lentils turn soft and comforting. It is served as a hearty side or simple main course.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g basmati rice, soaked 20 minutes
  • 150 g brown lentils
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Salt
  • About 700 ml water

Method

  1. Boil the lentils in water for 15 minutes until half-cooked, then drain.
  2. Fry the onions in the ghee until deep golden; set half aside for topping.
  3. Add the cumin and turmeric to the pot, then the drained rice and lentils, salt and 700 ml water.
  4. Bring to the boil, cover and cook on low heat for 18–20 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, fluff and serve topped with the reserved onions.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp of ghee instead of 2 — 1 tbsp ghee ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Increase the lentils and reduce the rice for a more filling plate with more protein.
  • Pair a modest portion with a large salad or cooked vegetables.

History

Rice and lentil dishes are cooked across the Gulf, the Levant and South Asia, and muaddas is the Emirati home-style version. It remains an everyday family dish in the UAE and neighbouring Gulf countries.
