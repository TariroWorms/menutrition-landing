Sop konro (Makassar beef rib soup)

Origin: Indonesia · South Sulawesi

Sop konro is a dark, aromatic soup of beef ribs simmered until tender in a broth colored by black keluak nuts. The spices are coriander, galangal, lemongrass and nutmeg, giving a deep earthy flavor without much heat. The ribs are served in the bowl with a little broth and rice on the side, with lime and chili. A grilled version, konro bakar, is brushed with sweet soy and served with the soup.

What it is made of

beef short ribskeluak nutscoriandergalangallemongrassshallots

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g beef ribs
  • 1.8 l water
  • 3 keluak nuts, mashed
  • 4 shallots, 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 cm galangal, 1 lemongrass, 2 tsp coriander, 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • salt, lime, celery leaf

Method

  1. Boil the ribs briefly and discard the water.
  2. Grind shallots, garlic, galangal, coriander, nutmeg and the keluak meat.
  3. Fry the paste in a little oil.
  4. Add the ribs, paste, lemongrass and water and simmer 2 hours.
  5. Season, skim fat and serve with lime and celery leaf.

How to make it lighter

  • Chill and remove the fat from the broth.
  • Use lean ribs or trim visible fat.
  • Serve with vegetables rather than a large portion of rice.

History

Konro is a classic dish of Makassar in South Sulawesi, associated with the Bugis and Makassarese cooking tradition. The name refers to the ribs themselves.
