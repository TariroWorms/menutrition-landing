Jamaican hard dough bread

Origin: Jamaica

Hard dough bread is a tight-crumbed, slightly sweet loaf that is firm and chewy. It is made from a stiff, well-kneaded dough baked in a rectangular tin. It slices cleanly for sandwiches and is a breakfast staple with butter, cheese or fried eggs.

Recipe

12 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • about 250 ml warm milk or water

Method

  1. Mix the flour, sugar, salt and yeast.
  2. Add butter and warm liquid to form a stiff dough.
  3. Knead 12-15 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  4. Let it rise 1 hour, shape into a loaf and rise 40 minutes in a tin.
  5. Bake at 190 C for 35 minutes until golden and hollow-sounding.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half whole wheat flour for fiber.
  • Serve with eggs or beans rather than sugary spreads.
  • Slice thin to control portions.

History

Hard dough bread is a staple of Jamaican bakeries. Its dense texture suited hot climates and made it good for carrying and storing.
