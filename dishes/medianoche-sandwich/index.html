Medianoche (Cuban midnight sandwich)

Origin: Cuba

The medianoche is a smaller, softer cousin of the Cuban sandwich, served on a golden, slightly sweet egg bread. It holds the same roast pork, ham, Swiss cheese, pickles and mustard, pressed until warm and lightly crisp. The sweet bread against the salty filling is what sets it apart.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 sweet egg rolls (about 200 g)
  • 100 g roast pork, sliced
  • 80 g ham, sliced
  • 60 g Swiss cheese
  • 4 pickle slices (40 g)
  • 1 tbsp mustard
  • 1 tbsp butter (15 g)

Method

  1. Slice the rolls open and spread with mustard.
  2. Layer ham, pork, cheese and pickles inside.
  3. Brush the outside with a little butter.
  4. Press in a hot pan or sandwich press for 2-3 minutes per side.
  5. Cook until warm, golden and the cheese has melted.
  6. Slice in half and serve right away.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cheese and extra pickles for a lighter version.
  • Swap in a wholegrain roll for more fiber.
  • Pair with a salad instead of fries.

History

The name means "midnight" and refers to the late-night snack eaten after Havana nightclubs. The sweet egg bread, similar to challah, distinguishes it from the standard Cuban sandwich. It is now sold in cafés across Cuba and Cuban communities in the United States.
