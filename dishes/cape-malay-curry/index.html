Cape Malay curry

Origin: South Africa · Cape

Cape Malay curry is a mild, aromatic curry sweetened with apricot jam or dried fruit and brightened with vinegar. It is fragrant rather than fiery. It is eaten with rice, sambals and roti.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken pieces
  • 2 onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 400 g tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 2 tbsp apricot jam
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Fry the onions in oil until golden.
  2. Add garlic and curry powder and cook 1 minute.
  3. Add the chicken and brown lightly.
  4. Stir in tomatoes, jam and vinegar.
  5. Cover and simmer 40 minutes.
  6. Serve with rice and a sambal.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken to lower the fat.
  • Cut back the jam for less sugar.
  • Serve with brown rice and a fresh salad.

History

It reflects the heritage of enslaved and exiled people from Southeast Asia and elsewhere who shaped Cape cooking. The spice blends combine Indian, Indonesian and local influences. Bo-Kaap in Cape Town is famous for it.
