🍢 Chicken Souvlaki

Chicken Souvlaki is a Greek grill favourite: cubes of chicken marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic and dried oregano, threaded onto skewers and grilled until charred outside and juicy inside. The lemon and oregano give it a bright, herby flavour. It is served hot off the grill with lemon wedges, a tomato and cucumber salad or grilled vegetables.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g boneless chicken thighs or breast, cut into 3 cm cubes
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt, pepper
  • Lemon wedges, tomato and cucumber, to serve

Method

  1. Mix the oil, lemon, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper and marinate the chicken for 1–4 hours.
  2. Thread onto skewers, leaving small gaps between the pieces.
  3. Grill over high heat for 10–14 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Rest 3 minutes and serve with lemon and salad.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless breast instead of thighs for the leanest skewers.
  • Shake off excess marinade before grilling — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Add pepper, onion and tomato pieces to the skewers to make the portion bigger.

History

Grilling small pieces of meat on skewers has a long history in Greece, and the name souvlaki, 'little skewer', is a diminutive of souvla, 'skewer'. Today souvlaki is one of the most popular street foods in Greece.
