Suja (Bhutanese butter tea)

Origin: Bhutan

Suja is a savoury tea made from strongly brewed leaves, butter and salt, churned until smooth and cloudy. It tastes closer to a thin broth than to sweet tea and is sipped hot, often with roasted rice or puffed maize. It is offered to guests on arrival and refilled until the cup is left alone.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 tsp black tea leaves
  • 800 ml water
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 120 ml milk
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Boil the tea leaves in the water for 5 minutes until very dark.
  2. Strain the tea into a jug.
  3. Add butter, milk and salt.
  4. Churn with a whisk or blend for 30 seconds until frothy.
  5. Reheat gently and pour into small cups.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter or skimmed milk for fewer calories.
  • Keep the salt modest if you watch blood pressure.
  • Drink in small cups alongside a light snack.

History

Butter tea is part of daily life across the Himalayan region, where it supplies warmth and energy at altitude. In Bhutan it is traditionally churned in a wooden cylinder, although a blender now does the job at home.
