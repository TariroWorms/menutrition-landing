Chajá (Uruguayan meringue cake)

Origin: Uruguay

Chajá is a light cake built from layers of sponge moistened with syrup, whipped cream, canned peaches and broken meringue. The outside is covered in cream and crushed meringue, so every bite is soft and crunchy at once. It tastes milky and fruity rather than heavy. Uruguayans serve it at birthdays and family celebrations.

What it is made of

sponge cakemeringuecreampeachsugar

Recipe

10 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1 sponge cake, 24 cm (about 400 g)
  • 400 ml whipping cream
  • 60 g sugar
  • 1 can of peaches in syrup (about 800 g)
  • 200 g crisp meringues

Method

  1. Slice the sponge into three thin layers.
  2. Whip the cream with the sugar to soft peaks.
  3. Brush the first layer with peach syrup, spread cream, add sliced peaches and crushed meringue.
  4. Repeat with the second layer and top with the third.
  5. Cover the cake in cream, press crushed meringue all over and chill 2 hours.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace half of the cream with thick Greek yogurt for the filling.
  • Use fresh peaches and dilute the syrup with water.
  • A thin 100 g slice gives the full experience.

History

The cake was created in the 1920s at a pastry shop in Paysandú, Uruguay, and named after the chajá, a large local bird with fluffy plumage. It is still produced there and copied by bakers all over the country.
