Pitepalt (Norrbotten potato dumplings)

Origin: Sweden · Norrbotten

Pitepalt are large, dense dumplings made from grated raw potato and barley flour, stuffed with salted pork. They are hearty and chewy with a mild, earthy flavour and a salty meaty centre. They are boiled and served with melted butter and lingonberry jam.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes
  • 200 g barley flour
  • 200 g salted pork belly
  • 1 onion
  • salt to taste
  • 40 g butter
  • 100 g lingonberry jam

Method

  1. Grate the raw potatoes and squeeze out some liquid, then mix with barley flour and salt into a stiff dough.
  2. Dice the pork and onion.
  3. Shape the dough around the pork and onion into large balls.
  4. Simmer in salted water for about 1 hour.
  5. Serve with melted butter and lingonberry jam.

How to make it lighter

  • Use leaner pork or smoked turkey for the filling.
  • Serve with a salad and less butter.
  • Use wholegrain barley flour for more fibre.

History

Pitepalt comes from the Piteå area in the far north of Sweden, where barley was an important grain. It is a typical northern comfort food.
