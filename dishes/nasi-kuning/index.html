Nasi kuning (Indonesian turmeric coconut rice)

Origin: Indonesia
570kcal

Nasi kuning is rice cooked with coconut milk, turmeric, lemongrass and pandan until it is golden, soft and fragrant. It is served with side dishes such as fried chicken, shredded omelet, fried tempeh, peanuts and sambal. The rice is often shaped into a cone called tumpeng for celebrations. It is also a common breakfast sold in small packets.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g rice
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 2 cm turmeric, 1 lemongrass stalk
  • 2 pandan leaves, 2 bay leaves
  • salt
  • shredded omelet, fried shallots for serving

Method

  1. Wash and drain the rice.
  2. Boil it with coconut milk, turmeric, lemongrass, pandan, bay leaves and salt until the liquid is absorbed.
  3. Steam the rice for 20 minutes.
  4. Fluff and shape into a cone if desired.
  5. Serve with chicken, omelet, tempeh and sambal.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk or half milk and half water.
  • Serve with grilled protein and vegetables.
  • Keep portions moderate.

History

The yellow color has long been linked to celebration in Indonesia, so nasi kuning appears at birthdays, openings and other special occasions. It is eaten across the archipelago in many regional versions.
