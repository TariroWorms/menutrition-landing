Milanesa de carne (Argentine beef milanesa)

Origin: Argentina

Milanesa de carne is a thin slice of beef, usually round or rump, soaked in egg seasoned with garlic and parsley, coated in breadcrumbs and fried. The result is crisp outside and tender inside. It is one of the most common home meals in Argentina, served with mashed potatoes, fries or salad and a squeeze of lemon.

What it is made of

beefeggbreadcrumbsgarlicparsleylemon

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g thin beef cutlets (nalga or cuadrada)
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 200 g breadcrumbs
  • Oil for frying, salt
  • Lemon wedges to serve

Method

  1. Beat eggs with garlic, parsley and salt.
  2. Soak the cutlets in the egg for 15 minutes.
  3. Press each cutlet firmly into breadcrumbs on both sides.
  4. Heat 1 cm of oil in a large pan.
  5. Fry 2–3 minutes per side until golden, then drain on paper.
  6. Serve hot with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake on an oiled tray at 220 °C for 15 minutes, turning once.
  • Serve with salad rather than fries.
  • Use panko for more crunch with less oil absorbed.

History

The dish came with Italian immigrants in the late 19th and early 20th centuries and is related to the cotoletta of Milan. In Argentina it was made with beef and became an everyday staple.
