Alcapurrias (Puerto Rican banana and yautía fritters)

Origin: Puerto Rico

Alcapurrias are torpedo-shaped fritters made from grated green banana and yautía, filled with seasoned ground beef and deep-fried. The outside is crunchy and golden, the inside soft and slightly sticky. They are a favorite street snack at roadside kiosks, especially along the island's beaches.

What it is made of

Green plantainyautía (taro)ground beefsofritoannatto oil

Recipe

8 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 4 green bananas
  • 300 g yautía
  • 250 g ground beef
  • 60 g sofrito
  • 2 tbsp annatto oil
  • salt
  • oil for frying

Method

  1. Brown the beef with sofrito and a pinch of salt to make the filling.
  2. Grate the bananas and yautía finely and mix with annatto oil and salt.
  3. Take a portion of masa and flatten in your palm.
  4. Add a spoonful of filling and fold the masa over to form an oblong.
  5. Fry in hot oil at 180 C for 4-5 minutes until deep golden.
  6. Drain and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the fritters on a rack with oil spray.
  • Use lean beef or chicken for the filling.
  • Serve with a salad to balance the meal.

History

Alcapurrias are one of the best-known fried snacks of Puerto Rico, sold at beach kiosks such as those in Piñones. They reflect the island's mix of indigenous, African and Spanish cooking.
