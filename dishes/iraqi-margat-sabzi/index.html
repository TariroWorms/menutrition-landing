Margat sabzi (Iraqi herb and lamb stew)

Origin: Iraq

Margat sabzi is a deep green Iraqi stew built on huge amounts of parsley, coriander and scallions. Lamb and red beans give it body, while whole dried limes add a sour, slightly bitter note. It is ladled over steamed rice and usually served with raw onion and pickles.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g lamb shoulder, cubed
  • 150 g cooked kidney beans
  • 1 large onion
  • 200 g chopped parsley, cilantro and scallions
  • 2 dried limes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 700 ml water
  • salt, pepper, turmeric

Method

  1. Brown the onion and lamb in oil with turmeric.
  2. Add water and simmer for 60 minutes until the meat is tender.
  3. Fry the chopped herbs in a little oil until they darken and shrink.
  4. Stir the herbs, beans and pierced dried limes into the pot.
  5. Simmer 30 more minutes, season, and serve over rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb and skim the fat for a lighter stew.
  • Add more beans and less meat to raise the fibre.
  • Serve with a modest portion of brown rice.

History

Herb stews with dried lime are a classic of Mesopotamian home cooking. Families make it regularly for Friday lunch or for guests.
