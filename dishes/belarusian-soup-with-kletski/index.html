Soup with kletski

Origin: Belarus

Soup with kletski is a clear, gentle broth filled with tiny flour-and-egg dumplings, diced potato and vegetables. The dumplings are soft and slightly chewy and soak up the flavor of the stock. It is everyday family food, often given to children and to anyone feeling unwell.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 l pork or chicken broth
  • 300 g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 100 g wheat flour
  • 1 egg
  • 60 ml water
  • 10 g dill
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste; a little oil for frying

Method

  1. Bring the broth to a boil and add the diced potato.
  2. Fry the chopped onion and grated carrot in a little oil and add them to the pot.
  3. Whisk egg with water and salt, then stir in the flour to make a thick, sticky batter.
  4. When the potato is almost tender, drop small pieces of batter from a teaspoon into the simmering soup.
  5. Cook for 5 minutes until the dumplings float and are cooked through.
  6. Season and finish with chopped dill.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skimmed chicken stock for a very light soup.
  • Add extra vegetables like zucchini or green peas.
  • Make the dumplings with part whole-wheat flour for more fiber.

History

Dumpling soups of this kind have long been common across Belarus and its neighbors as a cheap, quick meal. The broth could be meat, chicken or simply vegetable, depending on the season and the household.
