Toast Skagen

Origin: Sweden

Toast Skagen is a slice of buttered toast topped with a cool, creamy salad of small shrimp, dill and mayonnaise, finished with a spoonful of roe. It is fresh and silky with a hint of lemon and a pop of salty roe. It is served as an elegant starter.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g peeled shrimp
  • 80 g mayonnaise
  • 60 g crème fraîche
  • 10 g fresh dill
  • 1 pc red onion
  • 1 pc lemon
  • 4 pc white bread slices
  • 30 g butter
  • 40 g vendace roe
  • salt – to taste

Method

  1. Pat the shrimp dry and mix with mayonnaise, crème fraîche, chopped dill and lemon juice.
  2. Season with salt and pepper.
  3. Fry the bread in butter until golden on both sides and trim the crusts.
  4. Pile the shrimp mixture on each toast.
  5. Top with roe, red onion and a sprig of dill.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more crème fraîche than mayonnaise for a lighter topping.
  • Toast the bread with less butter.
  • Serve with a side of crisp salad.

History

The dish is attributed to the restaurateur Tore Wretman, who created it in the 1950s and named it after the Danish tip of Skagen. It has become one of the best-known Swedish starters.
