🧄 Shish Tawook with Garlic Sauce

Origin: Saudi Arabia · Riyadh

This is the shish tawook plate as served in Riyadh grills: yogurt-and-lemon marinated chicken skewers with a generous cup of thick, white toum garlic sauce and warm pita bread. The chicken is juicy and lightly smoky, the garlic sauce sharp and creamy, and the bread is used to scoop everything up. It is a filling, popular takeaway meal.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast, in cubes
  • 120 g plain yogurt
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 2 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 120 g garlic sauce (toum)
  • 4 pita breads
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt, lemon, paprika, crushed garlic, salt and pepper for 2 hours.
  2. Thread onto skewers and grill 10–12 minutes, turning, until cooked through.
  3. Warm the pita on the grill.
  4. Serve the skewers with the garlic sauce in a small cup and the bread on the side.

How to make it lighter

  • Garlic sauce is mostly oil: use a teaspoon at a time rather than the whole cup.
  • Have one pita instead of two.
  • Add a large salad of tomato, cucumber and lettuce to fill the plate.

History

Shish tawook is the Levantine and Turkish skewered chicken; the name joins şiş (skewer) with tavuk, Turkish for chicken. It became a staple of Lebanese grills and is now sold across the Middle East and the Gulf. In Saudi Arabia the plate usually comes with a large serving of garlic sauce.
