🍮 Suspiro a la Limeña

Origin: Peru · Lima

Suspiro a la limeña is a classic dessert from Lima made of two layers: a thick, silky caramel custard of condensed and evaporated milk enriched with egg yolks, topped with a cloud of glossy meringue dusted with cinnamon. The base is rich and very sweet, while the meringue is light and airy. It is served chilled in small glasses or bowls.

Recipe

6 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 395 g condensed milk
  • 400 ml evaporated milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 egg whites
  • 120 g sugar
  • 60 ml water
  • Ground cinnamon to dust

Method

  1. Cook the condensed and evaporated milk in a heavy pan over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, for 20–25 minutes until thick and pale caramel in colour.
  2. Remove from the heat, quickly whisk in the egg yolks and vanilla, return to low heat for 1 minute, then pour into glasses and cool.
  3. Boil the sugar and water to a thick syrup (about 118°C).
  4. Whisk the egg whites to soft peaks and slowly pour in the hot syrup, whisking until glossy and cool.
  5. Pipe or spoon the meringue over the custard and dust with cinnamon; chill before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve it in small glasses — about 80–100 g is a satisfying portion of such a rich dessert.
  • Make the base layer thinner and the meringue layer taller; the meringue is lighter than the milk custard.
  • Share one glass between two as a treat after a light meal.

History

The dessert is closely associated with Lima. Its name, 'sigh of a Lima woman', is often said to have been coined by the Peruvian poet José Gálvez, who compared it to the sweetness of his wife's sigh.
