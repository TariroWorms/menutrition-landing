🍛 Lamb keema

Lamb keema is minced lamb cooked quickly with onion, tomato and green peas in a fragrant blend of cumin and garam masala. The mince turns browned and crumbly, coated in a thick, spiced tomato masala, with sweet pops of peas throughout. It is a hearty everyday dish, scooped up with flatbread or served with rice.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced lamb
  • 200 g green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 3 garlic cloves and 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, salt, fresh coriander

Method

  1. Heat the oil and fry the cumin seeds for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the onions and cook until golden, then the garlic and ginger.
  3. Add the minced lamb and stir-fry over high heat, breaking it up, until browned.
  4. Add the tomatoes, garam masala and salt and cook for 10 minutes until thick.
  5. Stir in the peas, cook for 5 minutes more and finish with coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Brown the mince first and pour off the fat it releases.
  • Use lean mince and double the peas for a lighter, filling portion.
  • Serve with salad or a small portion of rice rather than buttered flatbread.

History

Keema, from the Urdu and Hindi word for minced meat, is an everyday dish across North India and Pakistan. Keema matar, with peas, is one of its most common home-style versions.
