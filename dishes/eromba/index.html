Eromba (Manipuri mashed vegetables with fermented fish)

Origin: India · Manipur

Eromba is a Manipuri dish of boiled vegetables, such as potato, beans and bamboo shoot, mashed together with fermented fish (ngari) and fiery chilli. The texture is rustic and chunky, and the taste is pungent, smoky and spicy. It is served as a side with steamed rice and is part of the everyday Meitei meal.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g potatoes, boiled
  • 150 g mixed vegetables such as beans and bamboo shoot, boiled
  • 2 tbsp roasted ngari or dried fermented fish (30 g)
  • 4 green chillies (or dried chillies roasted)
  • 2 spring onions
  • coriander, salt

Method

  1. Boil the vegetables until just soft and drain.
  2. Roast the ngari and chillies on a dry pan until aromatic.
  3. Mash the roasted fish and chillies together to a rough paste.
  4. Add the boiled vegetables and mash coarsely together with salt.
  5. Mix in chopped spring onion and coriander and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use fewer chillies to lower heat.
  • Reduce ngari to cut the sodium.
  • Add extra boiled greens.

History

Ngari, the fermented fish of Manipur, is a core flavouring of Meitei cooking. Eromba is a simple home dish and the vegetables can vary with the season.
