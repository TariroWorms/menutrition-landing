🦐 Seafood salad (insalata di mare)

Insalata di mare is a chilled salad of tender boiled calamari, shrimp and mussels dressed with lemon juice, olive oil and chopped parsley. It is light and bright, with sweet seafood and a sharp citrus dressing. It is served cold as a starter, especially on Christmas Eve and in summer by the sea.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g calamari, cut into rings
  • 300 g raw shrimp, peeled
  • 500 g mussels, cleaned
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, crushed
  • A bunch of parsley, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Steam the mussels in a covered pan with a splash of water for 4–5 minutes until open; discard any that stay closed and remove the meat.
  2. Boil the calamari for 2 minutes and the shrimp for 2–3 minutes in salted water; drain and cool.
  3. Whisk the lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, salt and pepper.
  4. Toss the seafood with the dressing and parsley and chill for at least 30 minutes before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the olive oil to 1–2 tbsp and add more lemon juice: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve it on a bed of rocket or sliced celery to add volume.
  • Boil, never fry, the calamari to keep the salad light.

History

Seafood salad is made all along the Italian coast, and in southern Italy it is a familiar dish of the Christmas Eve seafood dinner.
