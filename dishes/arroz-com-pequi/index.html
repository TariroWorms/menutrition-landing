Arroz com pequi (rice with pequi fruit)

Origin: Brazil · Goiás

Arroz com pequi is the signature rice of Goiás, cooked with whole pequi fruits until the yellow pulp tints the grains and fills them with its perfume. The flavour is intense, buttery and a little like strong cheese; people either love it or not. Pieces of chicken are often added and turmeric deepens the colour. Pequi is gnawed gently, never bitten, because the stone is covered in sharp spines.

What it is made of

ricepequi fruitchickenturmericoniongarlic

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g rice
  • 8 pequi fruits
  • 400 g chicken pieces (optional)
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt, spring onion

Method

  1. Brown the chicken, if using, in the oil.
  2. Add onion, garlic and the pequi and fry 5 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the rice and turmeric.
  4. Pour in hot water to cover by 2 cm and season.
  5. Simmer covered 18–20 minutes.
  6. Rest 5 minutes and add spring onion.
  7. Warn diners to scrape the pulp with their teeth and not bite the stone.

How to make it lighter

  • Pequi is rich in fat, so cook it with minimal extra oil.
  • Use skinless chicken breast.
  • Serve with a large green salad.

History

Pequi is a native fruit of the cerrado savanna and an emblem of Goiás cooking. Rice with pequi is eaten all over the state, especially in the harvest season from roughly October to January.
