🍮 Vietnamese Coffee Flan (Bánh Flan Cà Phê)

Origin: Vietnam

Vietnamese coffee flan is a silky steamed custard of eggs, sweetened condensed milk and vanilla, set over a layer of dark caramel and flavoured with strong coffee. Steaming gives it a smooth, wobbly texture, and the bitter coffee balances the sweetness of the milk and caramel. It is turned out of small moulds and often served chilled with a spoonful of extra coffee and crushed ice.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g sugar for the caramel
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 ml sweetened condensed milk
  • 300 ml water
  • 80 ml strong brewed coffee
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Method

  1. Cook the sugar with 2 tbsp water until amber, then pour into 6 small moulds.
  2. Whisk the eggs gently with condensed milk, water, coffee and vanilla without making foam, then strain.
  3. Pour the custard over the caramel and cover each mould.
  4. Steam over low heat for 20–25 minutes until just set, cool and chill for 3 hours. Turn out to serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Make smaller moulds: an 80–100 g flan satisfies a sweet craving.
  • Use a thinner caramel layer to cut the added sugar.
  • Serve with a splash of unsweetened coffee instead of extra condensed milk.

History

Bánh flan came to Vietnam with French crème caramel during the colonial period and became a street and café dessert; pouring Vietnamese coffee over it, or mixing coffee into the custard, is a popular local twist.
