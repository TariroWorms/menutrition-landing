Tacos de chorizo (Mexican chorizo tacos)

Origin: Mexico

Mexican chorizo is a fresh, loose pork sausage seasoned with dried chiles, vinegar and spices, very different from the cured Spanish kind. For tacos it is crumbled into a hot pan and fried until its red fat renders and the bits turn crisp, often mixed with diced potato to stretch it. A spoonful goes onto a warm tortilla with onion, cilantro and salsa verde.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g fresh Mexican chorizo, casing removed
  • 250 g potatoes, diced and par-boiled
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • cilantro and salsa verde to serve

Method

  1. Fry the chorizo in a dry pan over medium heat, breaking it up.
  2. When the fat renders, add the potatoes.
  3. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring, until the potatoes brown.
  4. Pour off excess fat.
  5. Warm the tortillas on a griddle.
  6. Fill with chorizo and top with onion, cilantro and salsa.

How to make it lighter

  • Mix half chorizo and half potato or beans to reduce fat.
  • Spoon off the rendered fat before serving.
  • Look for chicken or soy chorizo as a leaner option.

History

Chorizo arrived with Spanish settlers, and Mexican cooks reshaped it with local chiles and vinegar into the fresh sausage known today; Toluca is particularly famous for it. Chorizo tacos are a fixture of taquerías and breakfast stands across the country.
