Dolmeh felfel (Persian stuffed peppers)

Origin: Iran

Dolmeh felfel is made of bell peppers stuffed with a filling of rice, split peas, herbs and ground meat. They are simmered in a light tomato sauce until the peppers are soft and the rice is cooked through. The flavor is savory with a fresh, herby lift.

What it is made of

bell pepperriceground beefherbstomato sauce

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 6 medium bell peppers
  • 150 g rice
  • 60 g split peas, pre-cooked
  • 250 g ground beef
  • 1 onion, grated (100 g)
  • 80 g chopped herbs (parsley, dill, mint)
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt, turmeric, 400 ml water

Method

  1. Cut the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds.
  2. Mix rice, split peas, meat, onion, herbs, salt and turmeric.
  3. Fill the peppers two-thirds full to leave room for the rice to swell.
  4. Stand them in a pot and replace the tops.
  5. Mix the tomato paste, oil and water; pour around and simmer 50-60 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean meat and plenty of herbs.
  • Replace some rice with more split peas for fibre.
  • Skim any excess oil from the sauce.

History

Dolmeh covers a big family of stuffed vegetables in Persian cooking, including tomatoes, eggplants and peppers. It is a typical family dish for lunch or festive tables.
