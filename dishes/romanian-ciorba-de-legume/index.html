Ciorbă de legume (Romanian sour vegetable soup)

Origin: Romania

Ciorbă de legume is a clear sour soup with carrot, celery root, parsnip, potato, peppers and green beans. Its tang comes from borș, a fermented wheat-bran liquid, or from lemon or sauerkraut juice, and its aroma from fresh lovage and parsley. It is served hot with a hot pepper on the side and is a daily lunch starter at home.

Recipe

6 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 2 g carrots, diced
  • 1 g celery root and 1 parsnip, diced
  • 2 g potatoes, diced
  • 1 g onion and 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 200 g green beans
  • 2 g tomatoes
  • 200 ml borș or lemon juice and sauerkraut brine
  • 30 g lovage and parsley, chopped

Method

  1. Put 2.5 litres of water in a pot and add the onion, carrots, celery root and parsnip.
  2. Simmer 15 minutes, then add potatoes, pepper and green beans.
  3. Cook another 15 minutes until all vegetables are tender.
  4. Add the tomatoes and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the borș or lemon juice, salt, lovage and parsley and bring just to a boil.

How to make it lighter

  • Add a handful of lentils or beans to raise the protein.
  • Skip frying the vegetables in oil first for a very low-fat soup.
  • Use lemon and only a pinch of salt to keep sodium low.

History

Ciorbă is the Romanian family of sour soups, and its name comes from Turkish çorba. Souring with fermented wheat bran or lemon is typical of Romanian cuisine and distinguishes it from the neighbouring cooking. The vegetable version is the usual choice on fasting days.
