Imbakbka (Libyan spicy lamb pasta)

Origin: Libya

Imbakbka is a Libyan one-pot pasta in which short macaroni absorbs a spicy lamb and tomato sauce as it cooks. The result is rich, red and fragrant with chili and cumin. It is a favourite family meal, especially during Ramadan.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 350 g lamb on the bone, cut up
  • 300 g short macaroni
  • 2 onions
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tomatoes, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp hot chili paste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp black pepper, salt, 800 ml water

Method

  1. Brown the lamb and onions in oil.
  2. Stir in the garlic, tomato paste, grated tomatoes, chili paste and spices.
  3. Add 800 ml water and simmer for 40 minutes until the lamb is tender.
  4. Add the macaroni and top up with hot water if needed.
  5. Cook until the pasta is done and the sauce is thick.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and skim the fat from the sauce.
  • Choose wholewheat macaroni.
  • Reduce the chili for children.

History

The dish reflects the Italian influence on Libyan food, with pasta adopted and then seasoned in a local, spicier way. The name is thought to echo the bubbling sound of the pot.
