Seafood risotto is Arborio rice slowly stirred with garlic and white wine, then cooked with shrimp and mussels until creamy. The shellfish release their juices into the rice, giving a sweet, briny flavour, and fresh parsley lifts it at the end. It is served hot, usually with the mussels still in their shells on top.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g Arborio rice
  • 300 g shrimp, peeled
  • 700 g mussels, cleaned
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 120 ml dry white wine
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.2 l hot water
  • Parsley, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Steam the mussels in a covered pan with a splash of water until open; strain and keep the liquid.
  2. Fry the garlic in the oil, add the rice and toast 2 minutes.
  3. Add the wine and let it evaporate.
  4. Add the mussel liquid and hot water a ladle at a time, stirring, for about 16 minutes.
  5. Add the shrimp for the last 3 minutes, then the mussels and plenty of parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • 1 tbsp olive oil ≈ 120 kcal: 1 tbsp is enough for the whole pan.
  • Increase the shrimp and mussels and reduce the rice for more protein per calorie.
  • A 250 g portion instead of 350 g saves about 175 kcal.

History

Risotto ai frutti di mare is cooked along Italy's coasts and is now a standard on Italian restaurant menus around the world.
