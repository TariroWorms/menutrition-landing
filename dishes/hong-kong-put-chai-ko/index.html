Put chai ko (red bean rice cake)

Origin: Hong Kong

A small, soft, slightly chewy cake of rice flour and brown sugar steamed in a little bowl, with sweet red beans on top. It is only mildly sweet and eaten warm from a skewer or small cup. Street vendors traditionally sell it from a cart.

What it is made of

rice flourred beansugarwater

Recipe

6 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g rice flour
  • 50 g sugar
  • 350 ml water
  • 100 g cooked sweet red beans

Method

  1. Whisk flour, sugar and water until smooth.
  2. Spoon red beans into small greased bowls.
  3. Pour batter over the beans.
  4. Steam over high heat for 20 minutes.
  5. Cool a little and unmold with a stick.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by a third.
  • Use unsweetened red beans mashed with a little sweetener.
  • Enjoy a single small cake as a snack.

History

The name refers to the small ceramic bowls in which the cake is steamed. It is a traditional Hong Kong street snack.
