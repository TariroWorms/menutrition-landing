Lompe (potato flatbread hot dog)

Origin: Norway

Lompe is a thin, soft potato flatbread, wrapped around a boiled sausage to make Norway's classic hot dog. The sausage is topped with ketchup, mustard, fried onion or shrimp salad. The flatbread is mild and pillowy, so it takes the place of a bun. It is a popular street snack at kiosks and sporting events.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 4 lompe flatbreads
  • 4 hot dog sausages
  • 4 tbsp ketchup
  • 2 tbsp mustard
  • 4 tbsp fried onions
  • shrimp salad (optional)

Method

  1. Boil or grill the sausages until hot.
  2. Warm the lompe for 30 seconds in a dry pan or microwave.
  3. Place a sausage on each flatbread.
  4. Add ketchup, mustard and fried onion.
  5. Fold the lompe around the sausage and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose chicken sausage and add shredded salad.
  • Use less ketchup and more fresh vegetables.
  • Prefer whole-grain lompe where available.

History

Lompe is a thinner relative of lefse, made from potato and a small amount of flour. The sausage-in-lompe became a Norwegian fast-food standard in the second half of the twentieth century.
