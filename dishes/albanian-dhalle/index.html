Dhallë (Albanian salted yogurt drink)

Origin: Albania

Dhallë is a cold drink made by thinning plain yogurt with water and a pinch of salt. It is tangy, light and very refreshing. It is the classic partner to burek, grilled meat and other rich food, and a summer thirst quencher.

Recipe

4 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain yogurt
  • 400 ml cold water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • ice cubes
  • dried or fresh mint (optional)

Method

  1. Put the yogurt in a jug.
  2. Pour in half the water and whisk until smooth.
  3. Add the rest of the water and the salt.
  4. Whisk until a little foam forms.
  5. Taste and adjust the salt.
  6. Serve over ice with a pinch of mint.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt for a lighter drink.
  • Keep the salt modest if you watch your sodium.
  • Add cucumber slices for a fresher version.

History

Diluted yogurt drinks are found from Turkey to the Balkans and Central Asia. In Albania dhallë is an everyday drink, traditionally made at home and sold alongside pastries in bakeries.
