Megrelian kharcho (chicken in walnut sauce)

Origin: Georgia · Samegrelo

Unlike the soup of the same name, Megrelian kharcho is a dense stew in which chicken or beef simmers in a thick sauce of ground walnuts, garlic and spices. Megrelian cooking is known for its heat, so hot pepper and a touch of marigold-tinted khmeli suneli give the sauce colour and bite. It is traditionally served with ghomi, a firm cornmeal porridge, and slices of sulguni cheese.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces
  • 250 g walnuts
  • 2 onions
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp khmeli suneli
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1–2 hot chilies, salt

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in 1 litre of water for 40 minutes, then remove and cut into pieces.
  2. Grind the walnuts with garlic, chili, spices and salt into a paste.
  3. Dilute the paste with about 600 ml of the hot broth.
  4. Cook the chopped onions in a little broth until soft.
  5. Add the chicken and walnut sauce and simmer 15–20 minutes, stirring.
  6. Serve hot with ghomi or polenta.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless breast meat to reduce fat.
  • Reduce walnuts and add more broth for a lighter sauce.
  • Serve a smaller portion with plenty of salad.

History

Samegrelo in western Georgia is famous for spicy, walnut-rich dishes, and this stew is one of its signatures. Ghomi, made from cornmeal or millet, is its traditional companion.
