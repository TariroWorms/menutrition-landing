Shrimp saganaki is a Greek dish of shrimp simmered in a thick tomato sauce, splashed with ouzo and finished with crumbled feta that softens into the sauce. It is briny, sweet and herby with a faint anise note, topped with fresh parsley and served bubbling in the pan as a starter or light main.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g raw shrimp, peeled
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 30 ml ouzo
  • 80 g feta, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a wide pan and simmer the tomatoes for 10 minutes until thick.
  2. Add the shrimp and cook 2 minutes.
  3. Pour in the ouzo and let it bubble for 1 minute.
  4. Scatter over the feta and cook 3 minutes more until the shrimp are pink and the cheese softens.
  5. Season, sprinkle with parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp oil instead of 2; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Halve the feta and add more parsley for freshness.
  • Serve with a green salad instead of extra sides.

History

Like other saganaki dishes, it takes its name from the small two-handled pan it is cooked in. It is a staple of seaside tavernas across Greece and the islands, though no reliable record of its first appearance exists.
