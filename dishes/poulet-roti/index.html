🍗 Poulet Rôti (French Roast Chicken)

Poulet rôti is French roast chicken: a whole bird rubbed with butter, stuffed with lemon, garlic and thyme and roasted until the skin is golden and crisp and the meat juicy. The pan juices make a simple, fragrant sauce. It is a Sunday-lunch classic, carved at the table and served with potatoes, green beans or a salad.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (about 1.6 kg)
  • 30 g butter, softened
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • 1 head garlic, halved crosswise
  • 5 sprigs thyme
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes ahead and heat the oven to 210 °C.
  2. Rub the skin with the butter and season well inside and out with salt and pepper.
  3. Put the lemon, half the thyme and one garlic half inside; place the chicken in a roasting tin with the rest of the garlic and thyme.
  4. Roast for about 1 hour 10 minutes, basting with the juices twice, until the thigh juices run clear (74 °C).
  5. Rest the chicken for 10–15 minutes, then carve and spoon the pan juices over.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the skin before eating to cut a large share of the fat.
  • Choose breast meat for a leaner plate; thighs are juicier but richer.
  • Skim the fat off the pan juices before spooning them over the meat.

History

Roast chicken has long been a centrepiece of the French family Sunday meal, and rotisserie chickens are a familiar sight at French markets and butchers' shops. Bresse chickens are especially prized for roasting.
