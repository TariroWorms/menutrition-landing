Posta negra cartagenera (Cartagena black beef)

Origin: Colombia · Cartagena

Posta negra is Cartagena's festive beef dish: a whole eye of round is seared and then braised for hours in a sauce built on caramelised panela and cola, with onion, garlic, tomato and a splash of Worcestershire or soy sauce. The sauce reduces to a sticky, almost black glaze that is sweet, tangy and deeply savoury. It is sliced and served with coconut rice, fried plantain slices and a fresh salad.

Recipe

6 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg beef eye of round
  • 150 g panela, grated
  • 500 ml cola
  • 2 onions, 1 tomato, 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire or soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Rub the beef with garlic, salt, pepper and the Worcestershire or soy sauce and marinate for a few hours.
  2. Melt the panela in the oil until dark caramel, then brown the meat in it on all sides.
  3. Add onions and tomato, then pour in the cola and enough water to half cover.
  4. Cover and braise gently 2–2.5 hours, turning the meat now and then.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce to a thick glaze; slice and serve with the sauce.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the panela and use sugar-free cola to cut the sugar.
  • Trim all visible fat from the beef before cooking.
  • Serve with salad and boiled yuca instead of coconut rice.

History

The dish is a well-known part of the Afro-Caribbean cooking of Cartagena de Indias and the Bolívar coast. It is typically prepared for family gatherings and special lunches.
