Strogonoff de frango (Brazilian chicken stroganoff)

Origin: Brazil

Brazilian stroganoff is a quick family favourite of chicken strips in a pink, creamy sauce made with tomato, a little ketchup and mustard, mushrooms and cream. It is always served with white rice and topped with crunchy batata palha, thin fried potato sticks. The flavour is mild, slightly sweet and rich.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken breast, in strips
  • 200 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves
  • 150 g tomato passata
  • 1 tbsp ketchup and 1 tsp mustard
  • 200 ml cream
  • Rice and potato sticks to serve

Method

  1. Season the chicken and brown it in a little oil.
  2. Add onion and garlic and cook until soft.
  3. Add mushrooms, tomato, ketchup and mustard and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Lower the heat, stir in the cream and warm gently without boiling.
  5. Serve over rice, sprinkled with potato sticks.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light cream or Greek yoghurt.
  • Skip the potato sticks or use only a small handful.
  • Add extra mushrooms and serve with steamed vegetables.

History

The dish is an adaptation of the Russian beef stroganoff that became popular in Brazil in the second half of the twentieth century. Brazilians made it their own with chicken, tomato and canned cream.
