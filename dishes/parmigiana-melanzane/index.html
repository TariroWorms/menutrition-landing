🍆 Eggplant parmigiana (parmigiana di melanzane)

Origin: Italy · Campania and Sicily

Parmigiana di melanzane is a southern Italian bake of fried or roasted eggplant slices layered with tomato sauce, mozzarella, parmesan and basil. The eggplant becomes meltingly soft, the cheese stretchy and the top golden. It is served in squares, warm or at room temperature, as a main course or a generous side.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants (about 1 kg), sliced 1 cm thick
  • 700 g tomato sauce
  • 300 g mozzarella, sliced
  • 80 g parmesan, grated
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh basil, salt

Method

  1. Salt the eggplant slices for 30 minutes, pat dry, brush with oil and roast at 220 °C until golden.
  2. Spread sauce in a baking dish and layer eggplant, sauce, mozzarella, parmesan and basil.
  3. Repeat the layers, finishing with sauce and parmesan.
  4. Bake at 190 °C for 35 minutes and rest 15 minutes before cutting.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the eggplant instead of deep-frying: it soaks up far less oil.
  • Measure the oil: 1 tbsp olive oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Cut into 8 squares instead of 6 and serve with a salad.

History

Both Campania and Sicily claim the dish, and its exact origin is debated. The name is often linked to parmesan, though some suggest it comes from the Sicilian word for the slats of a shutter, recalling the overlapping layers. It is a fixture of Sunday and holiday tables in southern Italy.
