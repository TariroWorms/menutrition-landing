🍓 Strawberry pierogi

Origin: Poland

Pierogi z truskawkami are sweet Polish dumplings filled with fresh strawberries and a little sugar. As they boil, the fruit softens into a warm, juicy filling inside the tender dough. They are served hot with sour cream sweetened with sugar, and are a favourite summer lunch or dessert during strawberry season.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain flour
  • 150 ml warm water, 1 tbsp oil, a pinch of salt
  • 400 g strawberries, hulled and halved
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 150 g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp sugar for the sour cream

Method

  1. Knead the flour, water, oil and salt into a smooth dough, cover and rest for 20 minutes.
  2. Roll the dough thin and cut 8 cm rounds.
  3. Place a strawberry half or two on each round with a pinch of sugar and seal the edges tightly.
  4. Boil in salted water for 2–3 minutes after they float.
  5. Mix the sour cream with sugar and spoon it over the hot pierogi.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a light sour cream or just 1 tbsp per serving.
  • Skip the sugar in the filling — ripe strawberries are sweet enough.
  • Treat 5–6 pierogi as a dessert portion rather than a full meal.

History

Fruit pierogi are a long-standing summer tradition in Poland, made with whatever is in season — strawberries, blueberries, cherries or plums. Strawberry pierogi are especially popular in June and July, when Polish strawberries reach the markets.
