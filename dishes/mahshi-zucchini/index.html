🥒 Stuffed Zucchini (Mahshi Kousa)

Mahshi kousa is a Levantine and Egyptian dish of small zucchini hollowed out and stuffed with rice, minced lamb and warm spices, then simmered in a tomato sauce until tender. The zucchini become soft and the filling juicy and fragrant. It is served hot with some of the cooking sauce spooned over.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 12 small zucchini
  • 150 g short-grain rice, rinsed
  • 250 g minced lamb
  • 400 g tomatoes, grated
  • 1 tsp seven spices (allspice, cinnamon, pepper)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Hollow out the zucchini with a corer, leaving thin walls.
  2. Mix the rice, lamb, spices, salt and a little grated tomato.
  3. Fill the zucchini about three-quarters full to leave room for the rice to swell.
  4. Lay them in a pot, add the remaining tomato, oil and water to cover.
  5. Simmer covered for about 45 minutes until the zucchini and rice are tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced lamb, or drain the fat from the sauce before serving.
  • Keep the zucchini count up and the rice in the filling modest.
  • Serve with salad rather than extra rice.

History

Stuffed zucchini is a staple of home cooking across Syria, Lebanon, Palestine, Jordan and Egypt, with each region seasoning it slightly differently.
