Cuñapé (Santa Cruz cassava cheese bread)

Origin: Bolivia · Santa Cruz

Cuñapé are small, chewy rolls made from cassava starch and fresh cheese, baked until lightly golden. They are crisp on the outside and soft and cheesy inside, and naturally gluten free. They are a classic breakfast and snack in Santa Cruz, often with coffee.

Recipe

8 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cassava starch
  • 250 g fresh cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 80 ml milk
  • 50 g butter
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Method

  1. Crumble the cheese and mix with starch and salt.
  2. Add egg, melted butter and milk to form a dough.
  3. Knead until smooth and not sticky.
  4. Shape into small rolls.
  5. Bake at 200°C for 20 to 25 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lower-fat cheese.
  • Eat one or two rather than a whole basket.
  • Pair with fruit for a balanced breakfast.

History

Cuñapé belongs to the cheese-and-cassava bread family of the Guaraní region, which also includes chipa in Paraguay. Santa Cruz bakeries sell them warm in the morning.
