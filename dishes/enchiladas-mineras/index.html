Enchiladas mineras (miners’ enchiladas)

Origin: Mexico · Guanajuato
665kcal
per serving · 350 g
190kcal

Enchiladas mineras are corn tortillas dipped in a brick-red guajillo chili sauce, quickly fried, then filled with fresh cheese and onion. They are piled with diced potatoes and carrots fried in the same sauce, and finished with lettuce, cream and pickled chilies. The flavor is earthy and mildly smoky rather than very hot.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 6 guajillo chilies, seeded
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 300 g potatoes, diced and boiled
  • 200 g carrots, diced and boiled
  • 200 g queso fresco, crumbled
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • Shredded lettuce, 4 tbsp cream, pickled jalapeños
  • 2 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Soak the chilies in hot water for 15 minutes, then blend with the garlic, salt and 250 ml soaking water and strain.
  2. Dip each tortilla in the sauce and fry briefly in a little oil on both sides.
  3. Fill with cheese and onion, fold and arrange on plates.
  4. In the same pan, fry the potatoes and carrots with a few spoons of sauce until lightly browned.
  5. Top the enchiladas with the vegetables, lettuce, a little cream, more cheese and pickled chilies.

How to make it lighter

  • Warm the dipped tortillas on a dry griddle instead of frying them.
  • Use a small drizzle of yogurt in place of cream.
  • Add extra lettuce and pickled vegetables to make the plate lighter and more filling.

History

The dish is closely tied to the city of Guanajuato and its long silver-mining history. It is said to have been a filling, affordable meal for miners, and it is still sold at the city’s markets and food stalls.
