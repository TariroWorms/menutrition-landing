Melktert (South African milk tart)

Origin: South Africa

Melktert is a pastry shell filled with a soft vanilla-scented milk custard and dusted with cinnamon. It is gently sweet and silky rather than rich. It is a staple of bake sales and tea tables.

Recipe

8 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 1 sweet pastry shell (200 g flour, 100 g butter)
  • 500 ml milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 60 g sugar
  • 30 g flour
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Press the pastry into a tart tin and blind-bake 15 minutes.
  2. Heat the milk with vanilla.
  3. Whisk eggs, sugar and flour and slowly add the hot milk.
  4. Return to the pot and stir until thickened.
  5. Pour into the shell.
  6. Bake 15 minutes at 170 °C, cool and dust with cinnamon.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat milk for a lighter filling.
  • Cut smaller slices and serve with berries.
  • Reduce the sugar in the custard slightly.

History

The tart comes from Dutch settlers at the Cape, who made milk-based custard pastries. It appears in Afrikaner cookbooks from the early twentieth century. Many families treasure their own version.
