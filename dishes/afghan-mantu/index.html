Afghan mantu (steamed meat dumplings)

Origin: Afghanistan

Mantu are thin dough dumplings filled with spiced minced beef and onion, steamed and served on yogurt. They are topped with a tomato and lentil sauce and a sprinkle of dried mint. The dish is festive and filling.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 200 g minced beef
  • 2 onions (200 g)
  • 250 g yogurt with 1 garlic clove
  • 100 g cooked yellow split peas or lentils
  • 150 g tomato sauce
  • 1 tbsp dried mint

Method

  1. Make a firm dough with flour, water and salt and rest it.
  2. Mix minced beef with chopped onion, salt, pepper and coriander.
  3. Roll the dough thin and cut squares.
  4. Fill and fold the corners over.
  5. Steam for 30 minutes.
  6. Serve on garlic yogurt with sauce and mint.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef or chicken.
  • Use less oil in the sauce.
  • Serve with a large green salad.

History

Mantu are part of a wide family of Central Asian dumplings. In Afghanistan they are a classic guest dish, often made at celebrations.
