Bulviniai blynai (Lithuanian potato pancakes)

Origin: Lithuania

Bulviniai blynai are pancakes of finely grated potato and onion, lightly bound with egg and flour and fried until golden and crisp at the edges. Inside they stay soft and savoury. They are eaten hot with sour cream, sometimes with bacon bits or apple sauce.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potatoes, grated
  • 1 onion (100 g), grated
  • 1 egg (60 g)
  • 3 tbsp flour (30 g)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 60 ml oil for frying
  • 150 g sour cream

Method

  1. Grate potatoes and onion and squeeze out excess liquid.
  2. Mix with egg, flour and salt.
  3. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  4. Spoon in the batter, flatten and fry 3-4 minutes per side until crisp.
  5. Drain on paper and serve hot with sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Fry in a little oil, or bake on parchment at 220 °C.
  • Serve with yogurt instead of sour cream.
  • Add grated carrot or zucchini for extra vegetables.

History

Potatoes became the backbone of Lithuanian cooking from the nineteenth century, and potato pancakes are one of the simplest everyday dishes made from them. Similar pancakes are loved across the Baltic and Eastern Europe.
