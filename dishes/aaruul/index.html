Aaruul (Mongolian dried curd)

Origin: Mongolia

Aaruul is hard, dried curd made from soured milk, cut or pressed into small pieces. It is very sour, chewy and long-lasting. People nibble it as a snack or soften it in tea.

What it is made of

curdmilksugar

Recipe

8 servings240 min

Ingredients

  • 2 l sour milk or yogurt
  • 2 tbsp sugar (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the sour milk gently until curds separate.
  2. Strain through cloth and press out the whey.
  3. Mix in the sugar if using, then shape or cut the curd into small pieces.
  4. Dry in the sun or a low oven for several hours.
  5. Store dry in a cloth bag.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose unsweetened versions.
  • Eat small portions because it is very concentrated.
  • Soak a piece in tea to soften it for young children.

History

It is a long-keeping dairy food of herding families, made in summer and stored for winter. Sweet and fruity variants also exist.
