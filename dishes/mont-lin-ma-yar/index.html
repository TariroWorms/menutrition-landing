Mont lin ma yar (Burmese quail-egg rice pancakes)

Origin: Myanmar

Mont lin ma yar are small round pancakes cooked in a hot dimpled pan, with a quail egg set in each hollow. The rice and coconut batter crisps at the edges and stays soft in the middle. They are eaten hot from street stalls, dipped in a sweet-sour chili sauce.

Recipe

2 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g rice flour
  • 100 ml coconut milk
  • 150 ml water
  • 8 quail eggs
  • 2 spring onions
  • 1 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • salt

Method

  1. Whisk rice flour, coconut milk, water and salt into a loose batter.
  2. Heat an oiled dimpled pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in batter to fill each hollow about two thirds.
  4. Crack a quail egg into each and sprinkle with spring onion and cilantro.
  5. Cover and cook until the base is crisp and the egg is just set, then serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush the pan with very little oil to cut fat.
  • Use a non-stick pan and reduce coconut milk with water.
  • Serve with a vegetable salad instead of extra sauce.

History

Cooked in a cast-iron pan with half-round moulds, this snack is typical of Burmese street-food stalls. The name refers to the "couple" shape of the paired halves.
