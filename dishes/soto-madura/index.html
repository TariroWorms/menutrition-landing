Soto Madura (Madurese beef and offal soup)

Origin: Indonesia · Madura

Soto Madura is a golden turmeric soup with sliced beef and offal in a clear, fragrant broth. It is brightened at the table with lime juice, sambal and fried shallots. Some versions include vermicelli, bean sprouts or a boiled egg. It is served with rice or rice cakes and eaten for breakfast or lunch.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g beef and tripe
  • 1.5 l water
  • 3 shallots, 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 cm turmeric, 1 lemongrass stalk
  • 100 g bean sprouts
  • lime, fried shallots, sambal

Method

  1. Boil the beef and tripe, discard the first water, and simmer in fresh water until tender.
  2. Blend shallots, garlic and turmeric and fry briefly.
  3. Add the paste and lemongrass to the broth and simmer 30 minutes.
  4. Slice the meat thinly.
  5. Serve in bowls with sprouts, fried shallots, lime and sambal.

How to make it lighter

  • Use mostly lean beef and limit the offal.
  • Skim fat from the broth.
  • Add extra sprouts and herbs for volume.

History

The soup comes from the island of Madura off East Java, where soto is a street food staple. Madurese migrants have carried it to many Indonesian cities.
