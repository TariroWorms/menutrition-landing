Changua (Bogotá milk and egg soup)

Origin: Colombia · Bogotá

Changua is a simple, soothing soup eaten for breakfast in Bogotá and the surrounding highlands. Milk and water are brought to a simmer with scallion, eggs are poached directly in the pot, and the soup is poured over pieces of stale bread or almojábana and sprinkled with cilantro. It tastes mild, milky and savoury, and is valued on cold mornings and as a cure after a late night.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 500 ml water
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 scallions, chopped
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 4 slices day-old bread
  • Salt

Method

  1. Simmer milk, water, scallions and a little salt for 5 minutes.
  2. Crack the eggs one by one into the gently simmering liquid.
  3. Poach 3–4 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks soft.
  4. Place bread in bowls, ladle the soup and an egg into each.
  5. Sprinkle with cilantro and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skimmed or semi-skimmed milk for a lighter bowl.
  • Swap white bread for wholegrain toast.
  • It is already low in calories and high in protein — a good light breakfast.

History

The soup belongs to the traditional cooking of the Cundinamarca and Boyacá highlands, where dairy is plentiful and mornings are chilly. It is still served in many Bogotá homes and old-style breakfast cafés.
