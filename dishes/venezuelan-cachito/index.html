Cachito (Venezuelan ham roll)

Origin: Venezuela

A cachito looks like a croissant but is made from a soft, enriched bread dough rather than laminated pastry. The dough is rolled around diced ham, sometimes with bacon or cheese, and baked until shiny and golden. It is tender, slightly sweet and salty at the same time. Venezuelans buy it at the panadería for breakfast, usually with coffee.

Recipe

12 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 250 ml warm milk
  • 60 g butter
  • 50 g sugar
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 1 egg plus 1 yolk for brushing
  • 300 g cooked ham, finely diced

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, milk, egg and a pinch of salt; knead in the butter until smooth.
  2. Let the dough rise for 1 hour until doubled.
  3. Roll into a large circle and cut into 12 wedges.
  4. Place ham at the wide end of each wedge and roll up into a crescent.
  5. Rise 30 minutes, brush with yolk and bake at 180 °C for 18–20 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean ham or turkey ham.
  • Replace half the flour with whole wheat flour.
  • Eat one cachito with fruit rather than two.

History

The cachito is linked to the Portuguese and other European bakers who ran many bakeries in Caracas during the 20th century. It has since become one of the most popular bakery items in the country.
