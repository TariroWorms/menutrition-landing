Rakhine mont di (fish noodle soup)

Origin: Myanmar · Rakhine

Mont di is the signature noodle dish of Rakhine State. Thin rice noodles are served in a hot, sour, chili-rich fish broth, topped with herbs, fried onion and a squeeze of lime. It is brothy and punchy, and is often eaten for breakfast.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g cooked thin rice noodles
  • 500 ml fish broth
  • 200 g white fish fillet
  • 1 onion
  • 1 stalk lemongrass
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • handful of cilantro
  • 1 lime

Method

  1. Simmer fish with onion and lemongrass in water until cooked.
  2. Flake the fish and strain the broth.
  3. Season the broth with chili and fish sauce.
  4. Place noodles in bowls and add the flaked fish.
  5. Ladle on hot broth and finish with cilantro and lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Use fewer fried toppings.
  • Add extra greens such as water spinach.
  • Keep sodium low by using less fish sauce.

History

Rakhine cooking, on the Bay of Bengal coast, leans on fish and chili, and mont di is the region's best-known dish, often prepared for festivals and guests.
