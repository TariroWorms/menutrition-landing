Bacalaítos (Puerto Rican salt cod fritters)

Origin: Puerto Rico

Bacalaítos are thin, lacy fritters made from a loose flour batter with flaked salt cod, garlic and herbs, fried until crisp and golden. They taste salty and savory with a crunchy edge and soft center. They are a beloved street food, eaten hot straight from the paper.

What it is made of

salt codwheat flourgarlicparsleybaking powderwater

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g salt cod, soaked and flaked
  • 200 g wheat flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 250 ml cold water
  • oil for frying

Method

  1. Soak the salt cod overnight, changing the water, then boil briefly and flake.
  2. Mix the flour and baking powder in a bowl.
  3. Add the garlic, parsley and cod, then stir in water to form a loose batter.
  4. Heat 2 cm oil to 180 C.
  5. Drop spoonfuls of batter and flatten slightly; fry 2-3 minutes per side.
  6. Drain and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Soak the cod well to reduce salt.
  • Fry in a shallow layer and blot on paper.
  • Serve with lime and a green salad.

History

Salt cod arrived in the Caribbean through colonial trade and became a pantry staple across Puerto Rico. Bacalaítos are a common kiosk snack at festivals and beaches.
