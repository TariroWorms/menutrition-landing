Conchitas a la parmesana (baked scallops)

Origin: Peru

Conchitas a la parmesana are Peruvian bay scallops baked in their own shells under a blanket of parmesan. A drop of lime, a little butter and sometimes a splash of white wine go in before the cheese. They come out bubbling and golden, salty and sweet at once. They are served as a starter, a few shells per person.

What it is made of

scallopsparmesanbutterlimewhite wine

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 16 scallops in half shells (about 400 g meat)
  • 80 g parmesan, grated
  • 30 g butter
  • 2 limes
  • 30 ml dry white wine (optional)
  • black pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oven grill to high.
  2. Place the scallops on their shells on a baking tray.
  3. Add a few drops of lime juice, a little wine and a small piece of butter to each.
  4. Cover generously with grated parmesan and a pinch of pepper.
  5. Grill for 4–5 minutes until the cheese is golden and bubbling; serve at once with lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the butter; the cheese already adds plenty of fat.
  • Use less parmesan and finish with chopped parsley for freshness.
  • Pair with a large salad to turn a starter into a balanced meal.

History

The dish is often linked to Italian immigrants who settled on the Peruvian coast and brought parmesan with them. It became a staple of cevicherías, especially where scallops are farmed, such as around Paracas and Sechura.
