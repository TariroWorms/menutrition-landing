🥔 Causa de pollo

Causa de pollo is the chicken version of Peru's chilled layered potato dish. Smooth mashed yellow potato seasoned with aji amarillo chili is layered with shredded chicken bound in mayonnaise and slices of ripe avocado, then served cold. It is creamy and mildly spicy, with soft potato, rich chicken filling and buttery avocado in every bite.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg yellow potatoes
  • 2–3 tbsp aji amarillo paste
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300 g cooked chicken breast, shredded
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 ripe avocados, sliced
  • Salt

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins, peel and mash until very smooth.
  2. Season the mash with aji amarillo, lime juice, oil and salt and let it cool.
  3. Mix the chicken with the mayonnaise and a pinch of salt.
  4. Layer potato, chicken and avocado in a ring or lined dish, finishing with potato. Chill for 30 minutes and unmould.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the mayonnaise and loosen the chicken with lime juice — 1 tbsp of mayonnaise is about 90 kcal.
  • Choose skinless chicken breast for more protein and less fat.
  • A portion of about 150 g with a green salad makes a balanced light lunch.

History

Causa is built on Peru's native yellow potatoes and aji amarillo chili, and the chicken filling is one of the most common variations alongside tuna. The origin of the name is uncertain, with several popular stories in circulation. Today causa de pollo is served across Peru as a starter or light lunch.
