Milk soup with homemade noodles

Origin: Belarus

Milk soup with noodles is a gentle breakfast or supper dish loved by children. Thin ribbons of homemade egg noodles are cooked directly in hot milk, which thickens slightly from the flour. A little sugar and a pat of butter make it soft and comforting.

What it is made of

milkegg noodlesbuttersugar

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 1000 ml milk
  • 150 g wheat flour
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • a pinch of salt

Method

  1. Knead the flour, egg, water and a pinch of salt into a stiff dough and rest it for 20 minutes.
  2. Roll the dough very thin, let it dry slightly and cut into narrow strips.
  3. Bring the milk to a simmer with the sugar and a pinch of salt.
  4. Add the noodles, shaking off excess flour, and cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring.
  5. Take off the heat and stir in the butter.
  6. Serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk and halve the sugar.
  • Whole-wheat flour noodles add fiber.
  • Top with fresh berries instead of extra sugar.

History

Milk noodle soup has long been a common breakfast across Belarus and the wider region, served at home and in school and kindergarten canteens. Homemade noodles were rolled thin and dried in advance.
