Chakalaka (South African spicy vegetable relish)

Origin: South Africa

Chakalaka is a warm vegetable relish made from onion, carrot, peppers, tomato and chilli, usually with baked beans, flavoured with curry powder. It is tangy, mildly to very spicy and slightly sweet. It is an essential side at a braai, served with pap, bread or grilled meat.

Recipe

6 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 green pepper
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 1 tin baked beans (400 g)
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 chilli
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • salt

Method

  1. Fry onion in oil until soft and add curry powder and chilli.
  2. Add the grated carrot and pepper and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes and simmer until soft.
  4. Stir in the baked beans.
  5. Cook 10 minutes until thick and season.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose beans without added sugar.
  • Use more vegetables and less oil.
  • Serve with pap or bread as a filling vegetarian meal.

History

The relish is associated with the townships around Johannesburg, where cooks combined affordable vegetables and spices. It later became popular throughout South Africa and neighbouring countries.
