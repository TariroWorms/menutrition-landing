Galubtsy (stuffed cabbage rolls)

Origin: Belarus

Galubtsy are soft cabbage leaves rolled around a filling of minced pork, rice and fried onion. They are stewed slowly in a sauce of tomato, carrot and sour cream until the cabbage is silky. They are served hot with the sauce and an extra spoon of sour cream.

Recipe

6 servings110 min

Ingredients

  • 1 white cabbage (about 1.5 kg)
  • 500 g minced pork
  • 120 g rice
  • 1 onion (80 g)
  • 1 carrot (75 g)
  • 300 g tomato passata
  • 150 g sour cream
  • salt, pepper, bay leaf

Method

  1. Blanch the whole cabbage and separate 12–14 soft leaves; trim the thick ribs.
  2. Par-boil the rice and mix with the mince, half the fried onion, salt and pepper.
  3. Place filling on each leaf and roll into neat parcels.
  4. Fry the remaining onion with grated carrot, add tomato, sour cream and 300 ml water.
  5. Lay the rolls in a pot, pour over the sauce and stew covered for 50–60 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean mince or half mince and half mushrooms.
  • Use brown rice or buckwheat for more fibre.
  • Make the sauce with yogurt instead of sour cream.

History

Stuffed cabbage rolls are found across Eastern Europe, and in Belarus they are a staple of family meals and holiday tables. The name comes from the word for 'dove', although the origin of the name is not certain.
