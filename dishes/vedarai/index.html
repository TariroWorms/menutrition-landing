Vėdarai (Lithuanian potato sausages)

Origin: Lithuania

Vėdarai are thick sausages of grated potato, bacon and onion stuffed into pork intestine casings and baked until the skin is crisp. Inside they are soft, dense and peppery, similar to a baked kugel. They are cut into slices and eaten hot with sour cream or a bacon sauce.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg potatoes, peeled and grated
  • 200 g bacon, diced
  • 1 onion (150 g), grated
  • 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 clove garlic
  • pork casings, cleaned
  • 100 g sour cream

Method

  1. Squeeze excess liquid from the grated potatoes.
  2. Mix with bacon, onion, garlic, salt and pepper.
  3. Stuff the mixture loosely into the casings and tie the ends.
  4. Prick the sausages and place in a baking dish with a little water.
  5. Bake at 180 C for 1.5 hours, turning once, until browned; serve with sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean back bacon and less of it.
  • Bake rather than finish in fat.
  • Pair with sauerkraut or a green salad.

History

Vėdarai belong to the old Lithuanian peasant tradition of using potatoes, the main crop, with the fat and offal left from the autumn pig slaughter. They are often made for holidays and winter gatherings.
