This oyakodon donburi is a lighter-seasoned take on the classic chicken-and-egg rice bowl. Chicken and sliced onion are simmered in dashi sweetened with mirin, then softly set with beaten egg and served over a deep bowl of steamed rice. Without strong soy seasoning, the bowl tastes mild, sweet and comforting, with silky egg and tender chicken.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g chicken thigh, bite-sized pieces
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 eggs, lightly beaten
  • 180 ml dashi
  • 3 tbsp mirin
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 400 g hot cooked Japanese rice
  • Spring onion to garnish

Method

  1. Simmer the dashi, mirin and salt in a small pan.
  2. Add the onion and chicken and cook for 6–7 minutes.
  3. Pour over the egg, cover and cook for about 1 minute until barely set.
  4. Slide over the rice in bowls and garnish with spring onion.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce the rice to about 150 g per bowl and add a side of greens.
  • Use chicken breast for a leaner topping.
  • Cut the mirin by half — it is the main source of sugar in the sauce.

History

Donburi — rice bowls with a topping — became popular in Japan from the 19th century, and the chicken-and-egg oyakodon is among the best known. It is eaten everywhere from family kitchens to quick lunch counters across Japan.
