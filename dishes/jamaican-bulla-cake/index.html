Bulla cake (Jamaican molasses cake)

Origin: Jamaica

Bulla is a flat, round Jamaican cake made from flour, molasses and brown sugar with ginger and nutmeg. It is dense, chewy and not too sweet, with a dark spiced flavor. It is often eaten with pear (avocado) or cheese as a snack.

Recipe

10 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 100 g brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp molasses
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp ginger and 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 60 g butter or oil
  • about 100 ml water

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  2. Mix flour, baking soda and spices.
  3. Dissolve sugar and molasses in warm water with the melted butter.
  4. Combine to a stiff dough and roll into discs 1 cm thick.
  5. Bake 20-25 minutes until firm and dark, then cool.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar and add whole wheat flour.
  • Eat with cheese or avocado to balance the sweetness.
  • Make smaller discs for portion control.

History

Bulla is a traditional Jamaican snack sold in shops and schools. It uses simple pantry ingredients and has a long shelf life.
