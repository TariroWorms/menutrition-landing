Camarones a la diabla (devilled shrimp)

Origin: Mexico

Camarones a la diabla are shrimp cooked quickly in a deep red, very spicy sauce made from dried chiles, chipotle in adobo, tomato and garlic. The sauce is glossy and smoky, with heat that builds as you eat. It is usually served with white rice, a simple salad and tortillas to soak up the sauce.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g peeled shrimp
  • 4 guajillo chiles, soaked
  • 2 chipotles in adobo
  • 2 tomatoes (250 g)
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 onion (60 g)
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 tbsp oil, salt

Method

  1. Blend the soaked guajillos, chipotles, tomatoes, garlic and onion with a little soaking water until smooth.
  2. Heat butter and oil in a wide pan and fry the sauce for 6–8 minutes until it darkens and thickens.
  3. Season with salt.
  4. Add the shrimp and cook 3–4 minutes, just until pink and curled.
  5. Serve at once with rice and lime wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace the butter with a teaspoon of oil to cut saturated fat.
  • Serve over cauliflower rice or with a big salad for a lighter plate.
  • Use one chipotle and seeded chiles to tame the heat for sensitive eaters.

History

The dish is a staple of seafood restaurants along both Mexican coasts and in inland marisquerías. Its name, “devil style”, simply points to the heat, and cooks vary the chiles freely.
