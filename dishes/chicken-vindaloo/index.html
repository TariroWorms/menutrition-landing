🌶️ Chicken vindaloo

Chicken vindaloo is a fiery, tangy curry of chicken and potato simmered in a paste of dried red chillies, garlic, ginger and vinegar. The vinegar gives it a sharp, sour edge that balances the heat, and the potatoes soak up the deep red sauce. It is eaten with plain rice.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken thighs, in pieces
  • 8–10 dried red chillies, soaked
  • 6 garlic cloves and 3 cm ginger
  • 4 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp turmeric, 4 cloves, 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tbsp oil, salt

Method

  1. Blend the chillies, garlic, ginger, vinegar and spices to a smooth paste.
  2. Coat the chicken with half the paste and salt and marinate for 1–2 hours.
  3. Fry the onion in oil until golden, add the rest of the paste and cook for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the chicken, potatoes and 300 ml water; cover and simmer for 35–40 minutes until tender.
  5. Adjust salt and vinegar and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken and 1 tbsp oil instead of 2.
  • Swap some potato for cauliflower or green beans.
  • Serve with a modest portion of plain rice.

History

Vindaloo comes from Goa and is usually traced to a Portuguese dish of meat marinated in vinegar and garlic, adapted by Goan cooks with local chillies and spices. Chicken vindaloo became widely known through Indian restaurants abroad, where the dish is famous for its heat.
