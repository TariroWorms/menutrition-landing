Mongolian mutton soup (shul)

Origin: Mongolia

Shul is a simple soup of mutton and bones simmered with onion, then thickened with rice or potatoes. The broth is clear, meaty and lightly salty. It is a warming everyday meal in cold weather.

What it is made of

muttonricepotatoonionsalt

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g mutton with bones
  • 1 l water
  • 60 g rice
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • salt

Method

  1. Cover the mutton with cold water and bring to a boil.
  2. Skim the foam and simmer 1 hour.
  3. Add the rice and diced potatoes.
  4. Add the onion and cook 20 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim fat for a lighter soup.
  • Add more vegetables.
  • Use brown rice.

History

Boiling meat in water is the most common cooking method of Mongolian herders. The soup is adjusted with whatever grain or vegetable is at hand.
