Lablabi (Tunisian chickpea soup)

Origin: Tunisia

Lablabi is a Tunisian breakfast and street soup of chickpeas in a garlicky, cumin-scented broth, poured over torn stale bread. Each bowl is finished with harissa, olive oil, lemon, capers and often a poached egg or tuna. It is warm, spicy and very filling.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g dried chickpeas, soaked
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp harissa
  • 4 slices stale bread
  • 1 lemon
  • salt

Method

  1. Boil the soaked chickpeas until very soft.
  2. Add garlic, cumin and salt to the broth.
  3. Tear the bread into bowls.
  4. Ladle the chickpeas and broth over the bread.
  5. Top with harissa, olive oil and lemon juice.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce olive oil and bread for a lighter bowl.
  • Add an egg for extra protein.
  • Use wholegrain bread.

History

It is a classic winter dish across Tunisia, sold in small cafés in the early morning. It is a good example of making a hearty meal from dried chickpeas and leftover bread.
