Matsvnis supi (yogurt soup)

Origin: Georgia

Matsvnis supi is a comforting soup based on matsoni, the tangy Georgian cultured milk, cooked gently with rice and onions. An egg is whisked in to keep the yogurt smooth while it heats. Dried mint and fresh coriander or dill give it a fresh, slightly sour flavour, and it is eaten warm.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g matsoni or plain yogurt
  • 70 g rice
  • 1 onion
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp dried mint
  • 20 g coriander or dill, salt

Method

  1. Cook the rice in 700 ml of salted water for 12 minutes.
  2. Soften the chopped onion in butter.
  3. Whisk the yogurt with the egg until smooth.
  4. Temper the yogurt with a ladle of hot rice water, then pour it into the pot.
  5. Heat gently, stirring constantly, without letting it boil hard.
  6. Add the onion, mint and herbs and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat yogurt for fewer calories.
  • Replace butter with a little oil.
  • Add a handful of spinach with the herbs for extra vegetables.

History

Matsoni is a staple fermented dairy product in Georgia and appears in both drinks and cooked dishes. Warm yogurt soups like this are common throughout the Caucasus and neighbouring regions.
