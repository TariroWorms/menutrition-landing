Pelau (Trinidadian chicken and pigeon pea rice)

Origin: Trinidad and Tobago

Pelau is a Trinidadian one-pot dish that starts with chicken browned in caramelized sugar, which gives it a deep brown color and a sweet-savory edge. Rice, pigeon peas, carrot and coconut milk join the pot with green seasoning and the whole lot steams together. It is served at family lunches with coleslaw or salad.

Recipe

6 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken pieces
  • 400 g parboiled rice
  • 200 g pigeon peas, cooked
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 tbsp green seasoning
  • 1 onion
  • 1 whole hot pepper
  • 500 ml stock

Method

  1. Season the chicken with green seasoning and salt and leave for 1 hour.
  2. Caramelize the sugar in oil until dark, add the chicken and brown it.
  3. Add the onion and carrot and stir.
  4. Add rice, peas, coconut milk and stock with the pepper.
  5. Bring to a boil, cover and cook on low for 25 minutes.
  6. Rest 10 minutes and fluff.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken and less sugar.
  • Add more vegetables and peas.
  • Choose light coconut milk.

History

Pelau is closely related to pilaf and to West African jollof, and it came to Trinidad through the many cultures that settled there. It is a regular on Sunday tables and at festive gatherings.
