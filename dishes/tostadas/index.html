Tostadas are flat tortillas fried until crisp and used as an edible plate for toppings. A classic version is layered with black beans, shredded chicken, lettuce, cheese and a spoon of sour cream. They are crunchy, fresh and satisfying, and eaten by hand as a snack or light meal.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 8 tortillas
  • 250 g cooked black beans
  • 300 g cooked chicken, shredded
  • 100 g shredded lettuce
  • 60 g grated cheese
  • 80 g sour cream
  • oil for frying
  • salt, cumin

Method

  1. Fry the tortillas in hot oil until crisp and golden, then drain on paper.
  2. Mash the beans with a pinch of salt and cumin and warm them.
  3. Spread beans on each tostada and add the chicken.
  4. Top with lettuce, cheese and a small spoon of sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the tortillas at 200 °C for 8–10 minutes instead of deep-frying.
  • Pile on more lettuce and beans and use less cheese and sour cream.
  • Two loaded tostadas are a full meal; count them before adding a third.

History

Tostadas grew out of the Mexican habit of frying or toasting day-old tortillas so they would not go to waste. Today they are sold everywhere in Mexico, with toppings that change from region to region.
