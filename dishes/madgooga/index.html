🌴 Madgooga

Origin: Saudi Arabia · Najd

Madgooga is a traditional Saudi date sweet made by pounding pitted dates with toasted sesame seeds, ghee and a little cardamom until they form a dense, chewy paste. It is pressed into a bowl or rolled into small balls and eaten in small pieces with Arabic coffee. Its taste is deeply sweet and nutty from the toasted sesame.

What it is made of

Recipe

8 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g soft dates, pitted
  • 60 g sesame seeds
  • 40 g ghee
  • ½ tsp ground cardamom

Method

  1. Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan until golden.
  2. Warm the ghee in the pan and add the dates, mashing them with a spoon.
  3. Add the cardamom and most of the sesame and pound or knead into a smooth paste.
  4. Press into a dish or roll into small balls and coat with the remaining sesame.

How to make it lighter

  • Roll it into 20 g balls so each piece is easy to count.
  • Use half the ghee; the dates still bind well.
  • Enjoy one piece with coffee instead of several dates plus a sweet.

History

Madgooga, from the Arabic for 'pounded', is a home-made sweet of central and northern Saudi Arabia, where dates have long been a staple. It is still commonly served with Arabic coffee to guests and in winter.
