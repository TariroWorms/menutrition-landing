🥤 Tamarind Drink (Tamr Hindi)

Origin: Middle East · Egypt and the Levant

Tamr hindi is a sweet-and-sour drink made by soaking tamarind pulp in water, straining it and sweetening it with sugar. Served ice-cold, it is dark amber, tart and refreshing, with a fruity, slightly caramel note. It is one of the classic Ramadan drinks for breaking the fast and is sold by street vendors in Egypt and the Levant.

What it is made of

tamarindwatersugar

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g tamarind pulp
  • 1 l water
  • 100 g sugar
  • Ice to serve

Method

  1. Soak the tamarind pulp in 500 ml hot water for 20 minutes, breaking it up with your fingers.
  2. Strain through a fine sieve, pressing the pulp to extract as much liquid as possible.
  3. Stir in the sugar and the remaining cold water until dissolved.
  4. Chill well and serve over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the sugar and add a little more water for a tarter, lighter drink.
  • Serve in a small 200 ml glass to keep sugar per portion in check.
  • Sugar adds about 4 kcal per gram, so measure it rather than pouring by eye.

History

The Arabic name means Indian date, reflecting how tamarind reached the Arab world through trade with India. Tamarind drinks have long been sold by street vendors in Cairo, Damascus and other cities, and they remain a fixture of Ramadan tables today.
