Alloco (Ivorian fried plantain)

Origin: Ivory Coast

Alloco is ripe plantain cut into thick slices and fried until caramelized outside and soft within. It is served hot with a sauce of onion, tomato and chili. Eaten as a side or a full street meal with grilled fish or egg.

What it is made of

plantainoniontomatochili pepperoil

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 4 very ripe plantains (800 g)
  • 300 ml vegetable oil for frying
  • 2 onions (200 g)
  • 3 tomatoes (300 g)
  • 1 hot chili
  • Salt

Method

  1. Peel the plantains and slice them diagonally.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the slices until deep golden on both sides.
  3. Drain on paper.
  4. Soften chopped onion in a little oil, add tomato and chili.
  5. Cook the sauce until thick, season and serve beside the plantain.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the slices to cut the fat.
  • Choose less ripe plantain for less sugar.
  • Serve with grilled fish and extra vegetables.

History

A staple of Abidjan street stalls and maquis restaurants, it is also found across West Africa under other names. Plantain frying is a long-standing technique in the region.
