Kompot (fruit drink)

Origin: Belarus

Kompot is the everyday fruit drink of Belarusian homes, made by briefly boiling apples, cherries, currants or whatever the garden offers. It is light, gently sweet and slightly tart, with soft fruit left in the jug. It is served chilled with lunch in summer and warm in winter.

What it is made of

applecherriesberriessugar

Recipe

8 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 2.5 l water
  • 300 g apples
  • 200 g cherries
  • 150 g currants or other berries
  • 100 g sugar

Method

  1. Wash the fruit; core and slice the apples.
  2. Bring the water to a boil with the sugar.
  3. Add the apples and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Add the cherries and berries and simmer 3 minutes more.
  5. Cover and leave to infuse until cool, then chill.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the sugar or replace it with a spoon of honey after cooling.
  • Use very ripe fruit for natural sweetness.
  • Eat the cooked fruit too for extra fiber.

History

Kompot is common across Eastern Europe and was a standard drink in school and workplace canteens. Many families also preserve it in jars for the winter.
