Lapte de pasăre (Romanian floating islands)

Origin: Romania

Lapte de pasăre, bird's milk, is a spoon dessert of airy egg-white dumplings poached in milk and set on a pale vanilla custard made from the same milk. It is light and sweet, with a soft cloud-like texture. Served cold in bowls, it is a nostalgic children's favourite and a staple of home Sunday meals.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 800 ml milk
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 100 g sugar
  • 10 g cornstarch
  • 1 vanilla pod or 1 tsp extract

Method

  1. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks, adding 40 g sugar gradually.
  2. Heat the milk with vanilla to just below a boil.
  3. Drop spoonfuls of meringue into the milk, poach 2 minutes per side and lift them onto a plate.
  4. Whisk the yolks with 60 g sugar and the cornstarch, then pour in the hot milk, stirring.
  5. Cook the custard gently until it coats a spoon, cool, pour into bowls and top with the meringues.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk and less sugar for a lighter dessert.
  • Add fresh berries for fibre and vitamin C.
  • Serve small bowls; it is already one of the lightest desserts.

History

Floating islands of this type are known across Europe, with the French île flottante as the best-known relative. In Romanian homes the dessert became a standard because it needs only eggs, milk and sugar. The name likens its lightness to something as rare as bird's milk.
