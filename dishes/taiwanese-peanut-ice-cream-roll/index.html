Peanut ice cream roll (huasheng juan bing qilin)

Origin: Taiwan · Yilan

This dessert is a thin soft crepe-like wrapper filled with scoops of ice cream, shavings of peanut brittle and fresh cilantro leaves. It is sweet, nutty and cold, with the herb adding a surprising fresh note. It is eaten by hand as a street snack.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g flour
  • 200 ml water
  • 1 egg
  • 100 g peanut brittle, shaved
  • 8 scoops ice cream
  • fresh cilantro

Method

  1. Whisk flour, water and egg into a thin batter.
  2. Cook very thin crepes on a flat pan and cool.
  3. Shave the peanut brittle into a fine pile on each crepe.
  4. Add two scoops of ice cream.
  5. Add cilantro, roll up and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a smaller scoop of ice cream.
  • Choose a plain wrapper and extra fruit.
  • Use unsweetened ground peanuts instead of brittle.

History

The roll comes from the Yilan area in northeast Taiwan, where it is sold from stalls. The thin wrapper is similar to the one used for savory spring roll snacks.
