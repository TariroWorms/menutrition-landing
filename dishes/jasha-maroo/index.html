Jasha maroo (Bhutanese chicken stew)

Origin: Bhutan

Jasha maroo is a light, brothy chicken stew from Bhutan with tomato, garlic, ginger, onion and fresh chillies. It is gently spicy and fresh with coriander. It is eaten with red rice and other side dishes.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken, diced
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • fresh coriander, salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil and fry onion, garlic and ginger.
  2. Add the chicken and brown lightly.
  3. Add tomatoes and chillies.
  4. Pour in 300 ml water and simmer 20 minutes.
  5. Season with salt.
  6. Finish with coriander and serve with red rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless breast for a leaner dish.
  • Add vegetables such as carrots.
  • Skim the surface fat.

History

Chicken is a common household meat in Bhutan and this stew is an everyday dish. Jasha means chicken and maroo means spicy or buttery stew.
