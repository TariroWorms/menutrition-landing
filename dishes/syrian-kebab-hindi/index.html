Kebab hindi (Aleppo meatballs in tomato sauce)

Origin: Syria · Aleppo

Kebab hindi is a tray of small spiced beef and lamb meatballs baked in a thick sauce of tomatoes, onions and sweet peppers. The meat is tender and fragrant with allspice, while the sauce turns jammy and slightly sweet. It is served hot with rice or flatbread to mop up the juices.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef or lamb
  • 1 grated onion + 2 sliced onions
  • 1/2 bunch parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp allspice, salt, pepper
  • 4 tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Knead the meat with grated onion, parsley and spices.
  2. Shape into walnut-sized balls and brown briefly in oil.
  3. Fry the sliced onions and garlic until soft, add tomato paste and a cup of water.
  4. Place the meatballs and tomato slices in a baking tray and pour the sauce around.
  5. Bake at 190 C for 30 minutes and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef and skip the pre-frying to cut fat.
  • Add peppers and zucchini to stretch the tray with vegetables.
  • Serve with brown rice or bulgur for more fibre.

History

The dish belongs to the home cooking of Syria, especially Aleppo, where tomato-based meat trays are a family staple. The name refers to tamarind, called Indian date in Arabic, which some cooks add to the sauce.
