Sbiten (spiced honey drink)

Origin: Belarus

Sbiten is a warming winter drink of honey dissolved in hot water and simmered with spices and herbs. It is sweet and aromatic, with the heat of ginger and the scent of clove and cinnamon. It is sipped hot from mugs, especially on frosty days and during winter fairs.

What it is made of

honeycinnamongingerspicesmint

Recipe

6 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 l water
  • 200 g honey
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 5 cloves
  • 20 g fresh ginger
  • a sprig of mint or thyme

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil and dissolve the honey in it.
  2. Skim off any foam.
  3. Add the sliced ginger, cinnamon and cloves and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Add the herbs, cover and steep 5 minutes.
  5. Strain and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the honey and more ginger for a less sweet drink.
  • Add lemon for vitamin C.
  • Not suitable for babies under one year because of the honey.

History

Sbiten was a popular street drink across the East Slavic lands long before tea became common. Vendors sold it hot from large insulated vessels.
