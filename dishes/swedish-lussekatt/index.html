Saffron bun (Lussekatt)

Origin: Sweden

Lussekatt is a golden, S-shaped bun coloured and scented with saffron, with a raisin pressed into each curl. The dough is soft and lightly sweet, with a floral, slightly bitter saffron note. It is eaten with coffee or mulled drinks in the weeks before Christmas.

Recipe

12 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g wheat flour
  • 250 ml milk
  • 100 g butter
  • 100 g quark or yoghurt
  • 1 g saffron threads
  • 100 g sugar
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • raisins and 1 egg to finish

Method

  1. Warm the milk with the butter and crushed saffron.
  2. Mix with flour, sugar, yeast and quark into a soft dough.
  3. Knead and let rise until doubled.
  4. Roll into ropes, shape each into an S and press a raisin into each end.
  5. Brush with egg and bake at 225 °C for about 8 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce the sugar slightly; saffron gives flavour without it.
  • Use wholemeal flour for half the amount.
  • Serve one bun with unsweetened tea.

History

The buns belong to St Lucia's Day on 13 December, when children in white gowns carry them in processions. The shape and the saffron colour are meant to bring light in the darkest time of the year.
