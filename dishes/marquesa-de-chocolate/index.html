Marquesa de chocolate (chocolate biscuit icebox cake)

Origin: Venezuela

Marquesa de chocolate is a no-bake cake made of Maria biscuits briefly dipped in milk and layered with a cream of melted chocolate, butter, sugar and egg yolks. After a night in the fridge the biscuits soften to a cake-like texture under the firm chocolate. It is rich, cold and very chocolatey. It is a favourite at children’s birthdays and is cut into slices.

Recipe

10 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g Maria biscuits
  • 250 ml milk for dipping
  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 150 g butter
  • 80 g sugar
  • 3 egg yolks (pasteurised)

Method

  1. Melt the chocolate with the butter over low heat and let it cool slightly.
  2. Beat the yolks with the sugar until pale, then mix in the chocolate.
  3. Quickly dip biscuits in milk and lay a layer in a lined dish.
  4. Spread with chocolate cream and repeat, finishing with cream.
  5. Cover and chill at least 6 hours or overnight before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Use cocoa and less butter for a lighter cream.
  • Choose high-cocoa chocolate, which needs less sugar.
  • Serve thin slices with berries.

History

The dessert became popular in Venezuelan homes in the 20th century, when packaged Maria biscuits were cheap and widely available. Every family tends to have its own version of the cream.
