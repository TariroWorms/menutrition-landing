Mangonada (mango chamoy slush)

Origin: Mexico

A mangonada is a thick frozen mango drink layered in a cup with ribbons of chamoy, a salty, sour and mildly spicy fruit sauce, and a dusting of chili-lime seasoning. Fresh mango cubes go on top, and a tamarind candy straw is pushed in. Every sip swings between sweet, sour, salty and hot.

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g frozen mango chunks
  • 150 g fresh mango, diced
  • 120 ml water or mango juice
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 4 tbsp chamoy
  • 2 tsp chili-lime seasoning
  • 2 tamarind candy straws

Method

  1. Blend the frozen mango with water and lime juice until thick and smooth.
  2. Swirl a spoon of chamoy and a pinch of seasoning around the inside of each cup.
  3. Layer the mango slush with more chamoy and seasoning.
  4. Top with the diced fresh mango and a final drizzle of chamoy.
  5. Add a tamarind straw and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Use water instead of juice and skip extra sugar; ripe mango is sweet enough.
  • Go light on chamoy and seasoning to keep sodium down.
  • Leave out the candy straw to cut about 50 kcal.

History

The mangonada is a modern street treat that became popular in the late twentieth century at paleterías and snack stands. It is also known as chamoyada and has spread widely in the United States.
