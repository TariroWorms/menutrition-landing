Tavë kosi

Origin: Albania · Elbasan

Tavë kosi is lamb baked with rice and covered with a thick, golden yoghurt and egg custard. The meat is tender, the rice soaks up the juices, and the top is set and lightly tangy. It is the national dish of Albania and is served straight from the baking dish.

What it is made of

lambriceyogurteggbutter

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g lamb, in pieces
  • 100 g rice
  • 500 g yoghurt
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 tbsp butter (30 g)
  • 1 tbsp flour, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in butter, season and cover with a little water; cook 40 minutes until tender.
  2. Spread the rice in a baking dish and place the lamb on top.
  3. Whisk yoghurt, eggs and flour until smooth with salt.
  4. Pour over the lamb and rice.
  5. Bake at 190 C for 35 minutes until set and golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb and trim fat.
  • Choose low-fat yoghurt for the topping.
  • Serve with a large tomato salad.

History

The dish is closely linked to Elbasan and is often called a national dish of Albania. It is also known in neighbouring countries, but the Albanian version is considered the reference.
