Bsissa (Tunisian roasted barley and chickpea mix)

Origin: Tunisia

Bsissa is a fine, nutty flour made from roasted barley, chickpeas and sometimes other grains and spices, mixed with olive oil and sugar. It is crumbly and fragrant. It is eaten by the spoonful, stirred into milk or water as a drink, or used as a travel snack.

Recipe

8 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g barley
  • 100 g chickpeas
  • 50 g wheat or fenugreek (optional)
  • 1 tsp anise or fennel seeds
  • 80 ml olive oil
  • 60 g sugar

Method

  1. Toast the barley, chickpeas and spices separately in a dry pan until fragrant.
  2. Cool completely.
  3. Grind to a fine flour in a mill or blender.
  4. Mix the flour with the sugar.
  5. Add olive oil gradually and rub until it is a damp crumb.
  6. Store in a jar; eat by the spoon or stir into milk.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by half or use a little date paste.
  • Use less oil and serve in small portions.
  • Stir into plain yogurt for a filling breakfast.

History

Bsissa is an old energy food of Tunisia and Libya, traditionally prepared at home and often given to new mothers and travellers. It is also made for religious and family celebrations.
