Lohikeitto (Finnish salmon soup)

Origin: Finland

Lohikeitto is a comforting Finnish soup of salmon cubes, potatoes, leek and carrot in a light creamy broth. The fresh dill lifts the flavor and the salmon is added at the end so it stays tender. It is typically served with rye bread and butter.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g salmon fillet
  • 350 g potatoes
  • 100 g leek
  • 80 g carrot
  • 500 ml fish or vegetable stock
  • 200 ml cream
  • 20 g dill
  • 2 g allspice

Method

  1. Simmer diced potatoes, carrot and leek in the stock for 15 minutes.
  2. Add allspice and salt.
  3. Pour in the cream and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add the salmon cubes and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until just opaque.
  5. Season and stir in plenty of chopped dill.
  6. Serve hot with rye bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace part of the cream with milk for a lighter soup.
  • Add more vegetables for volume.
  • Do not overcook the salmon to keep it moist.

History

Salmon soup became a well-known Finnish dish in the twentieth century and is now served in homes, lunch restaurants and Helsinki market halls. The cream is sometimes replaced with milk for a lighter version.
