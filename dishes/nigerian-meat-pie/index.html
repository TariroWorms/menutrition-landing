Nigerian meat pie

Origin: Nigeria

Nigerian meat pie is a hand-held pie of crumbly buttery pastry filled with spiced minced beef, diced potato and carrot in a thick gravy. It is savory with a gentle pepper warmth. It is sold in bakeries and at parties and eaten warm as a snack or light lunch.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g flour
  • 200 g cold butter
  • 1 egg + 1 for glaze
  • 5 tbsp cold water
  • 400 g minced beef
  • 1 potato, 1 carrot, diced
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp thyme
  • 1 tbsp flour for gravy
  • salt, pepper

Method

  1. Rub butter into flour and salt, mix in egg and water to form dough; chill for 30 minutes.
  2. Brown beef with onion, then add potato, carrot, curry, thyme, salt and a little water.
  3. Simmer until vegetables are soft, thicken with flour and cool.
  4. Roll dough out and cut circles.
  5. Fill, fold over and crimp the edges.
  6. Brush with egg and bake at 190 C for 25-30 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean mince and drain fat.
  • Make the pastry with part wholemeal flour.
  • Add more vegetables to the filling and use less pastry.

History

The pie came with British baking traditions during the colonial era and was reshaped by local seasoning, with curry powder, thyme and pepper in the filling. It is now a staple of Nigerian bakeries and celebrations.
