🍋 Lebanese lemonade

Origin: Lebanon · Levant

Lebanese lemonade is fresh lemon juice blended with mint, sugar and cold water into a tart, bright green drink. The mint is blended rather than just steeped, which gives it a strong herbal flavour and a slightly frothy texture. It is served very cold, often alongside mezze and grilled dishes in summer.

What it is made of

lemonmintsugarwater

Recipe

4 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 4 lemons (about 160 ml juice)
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 800 ml cold water
  • A large handful of fresh mint leaves

Method

  1. Squeeze the lemons and strain out the seeds.
  2. Dissolve the sugar in a little of the water.
  3. Blend the lemon juice, sugared water, remaining water, mint until smooth and pale green.
  4. Taste, adjust the sugar and serve at once, well chilled.

How to make it lighter

  • Almost all the calories come from sugar: each tablespoon adds about 48 kcal.
  • Halve the sugar and add more mint and ice for flavour.
  • Order it 'no sugar' or lightly sweet in cafés and sweeten to taste yourself.

History

Lemon and mint drinks are found across the Levant, and this blended version is a standard on juice-bar and restaurant menus in Lebanon today.
