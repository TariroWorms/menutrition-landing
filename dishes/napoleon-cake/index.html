🍰 Napoleon cake

Napoleon cake is a tall layered dessert of many thin, crisp sheets of puff pastry sandwiched with vanilla pastry cream, then covered with crushed pastry crumbs. After resting overnight the layers soften slightly and the cake becomes tender and creamy while keeping a flaky texture. It is a classic celebration cake in Russia, Ukraine and the former Soviet countries, cut into tall slices.

Recipe

12 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g puff pastry
  • Pastry cream: 700 ml milk, 4 egg yolks, 150 g sugar, 40 g flour, 1 tsp vanilla extract, 50 g butter
  • 1 tbsp sugar for dusting

Method

  1. Make the cream: whisk yolks, sugar and flour, add hot milk, then cook stirring until thick; stir in butter and vanilla and cool.
  2. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Roll the pastry thin and cut 8 circles of 24 cm; prick well.
  3. Bake each layer 8–10 minutes until golden and crisp; keep the trimmings.
  4. Stack the layers, spreading cream evenly between each and over the top and sides.
  5. Crush the baked trimmings and press them over the cake; dust lightly with sugar.
  6. Refrigerate at least 8 hours before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut thin slices: a 12-slice cake can easily give 16 portions.
  • Make the pastry cream with less sugar — vanilla carries the flavour.
  • Serve with fresh berries to make a smaller slice feel like more.

History

Napoleon cake is the Russian and Eastern European take on the French mille-feuille. A popular story links its name to the centenary of Napoleon's defeat in 1812, celebrated in Russia in 1912, but this is not documented. It became a home baking classic in Soviet times and remains a favourite birthday cake today.
