Tomato and egg stir-fry (xihongshi chao jidan)

Origin: China

This home-style dish combines soft curds of scrambled egg with juicy tomatoes cooked until they form a light sauce. A little sugar balances the acidity. It is usually eaten with steamed rice and is one of the first dishes many Chinese people learn to cook.

Recipe

3 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 3 ripe tomatoes (400 g)
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • salt

Method

  1. Beat eggs with a pinch of salt.
  2. Scramble in hot oil until barely set and set aside.
  3. Stir-fry the wedged tomatoes with a pinch of salt until soft and saucy.
  4. Add sugar and return the eggs.
  5. Toss briefly and finish with scallions.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less oil and a non-stick pan.
  • Add an extra tomato for more vitamins and a lighter dish.
  • Serve with brown rice for more fibre.

History

Tomato and egg is among the most common everyday dishes in Chinese homes. Tomatoes arrived in China only in recent centuries, and the dish became a household classic through its simplicity.
