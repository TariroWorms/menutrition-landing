Callaloo (Jamaican steamed greens)

Origin: Jamaica

Jamaican callaloo is a dish of leafy greens, sliced thin and cooked briefly with onion, tomato, garlic and thyme. It is soft, earthy and lightly peppery. It is served at breakfast with fried dumplings or ackee, or as a side with fish and rice.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g callaloo or amaranth leaves, chopped
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 sprig thyme
  • 1/2 scotch bonnet, whole
  • 2 tbsp oil and salt

Method

  1. Wash the greens well and chop them, discarding thick stems.
  2. Warm the oil and cook onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Add tomato, thyme and the whole pepper and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Add the greens with a splash of water, cover and steam 10 minutes.
  5. Season with salt, remove the pepper and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Cook with less oil to keep it light.
  • Do not overcook so the greens keep nutrients and color.
  • Serve with beans or eggs for a balanced meal.

History

Callaloo is the name of leafy greens used across the Caribbean, and in Jamaica it usually means amaranth. Recipes differ from island to island, and the Jamaican version is a simple steamed vegetable dish.
