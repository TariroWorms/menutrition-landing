🍅 Gnocchi with Tomato and Basil

Gnocchi with tomato and basil are potato dumplings tossed in a fresh, quickly cooked tomato sauce, finished with torn basil and grated parmesan. The gnocchi stay soft and pillowy while the sauce is light, sweet and fragrant. It is a simple, fast dish served hot as a weeknight main or a first course.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g potato gnocchi
  • 500 g ripe tomatoes, chopped, or 400 g tomato sauce
  • 1 garlic clove, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 handful fresh basil
  • 30 g parmesan, grated
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Warm the garlic in the oil for 1 minute, add the tomatoes with a pinch of salt and cook 10 minutes until saucy.
  2. Boil the gnocchi in salted water until they float, about 2 minutes.
  3. Lift the gnocchi into the sauce, add the torn basil and toss gently.
  4. Serve topped with parmesan and black pepper.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep the parmesan to about 1 tbsp per plate.
  • Add a big handful of spinach or chopped courgette to the sauce for more volume.
  • Weigh out about 200 g of gnocchi with sauce per portion.

History

Potato gnocchi with a fresh tomato and basil sauce is one of the most common ways the dish is served in Italy and in Italian restaurants worldwide.
