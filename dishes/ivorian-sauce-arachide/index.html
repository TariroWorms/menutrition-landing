Sauce arachide (Ivorian peanut stew)

Sauce arachide is a creamy peanut stew with chicken, tomato and onion. It is thick, nutty and mildly spicy, with the oil rising in orange beads on top. It is served over steamed rice.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken pieces
  • 150 g natural peanut butter
  • 3 tomatoes (300 g)
  • 2 onions (200 g)
  • 1 hot chili
  • Salt, 600 ml water

Method

  1. Brown the chicken with onion.
  2. Add tomato and chili and cook down 10 minutes.
  3. Dilute the peanut butter in warm water.
  4. Pour it in and simmer 40 minutes, stirring.
  5. Serve over rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use unsweetened peanut butter.
  • Skim excess oil.
  • Serve with plenty of vegetables.

History

Peanut sauces are found all over West Africa, and the Ivorian version is a family Sunday classic. Peanuts arrived in the region centuries ago and became a key sauce ingredient.
