Flying Jacob (Flygande Jacob)

Origin: Sweden

Flygande Jacob is a casserole of chicken, bacon and sliced banana baked in a cream and chili sauce and topped with salted peanuts. It is creamy and mildly sweet with a gentle tomato-chili tang and a salty crunch on top. It is served with rice.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g chicken breast
  • 100 g bacon
  • 2 pc bananas
  • 300 ml cream
  • 80 g chili sauce
  • 40 g roasted salted peanuts
  • 200 g rice
  • salt – to taste

Method

  1. Cut the chicken into pieces and fry with the bacon until lightly browned.
  2. Place the chicken and bacon in a baking dish and top with sliced bananas.
  3. Mix cream with the chili sauce and pour over.
  4. Bake at 200°C for about 25 minutes.
  5. Scatter with peanuts and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light cream and chicken breast only to cut fat.
  • Reduce the bacon amount.
  • Serve with a big salad to balance the richness.

History

The dish was created in the 1970s by an air-freight employee called Ove Jacobsson, hence the name. It quickly became a Swedish party and family favourite.
