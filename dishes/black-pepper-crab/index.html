Black pepper crab (Singapore)

Origin: Singapore

Black pepper crab is crab pieces tossed in a sticky sauce of coarsely ground black pepper, butter, garlic and oyster sauce. The taste is peppery, buttery and slightly sweet, with a dry, clinging coating on the shell. Diners crack the claws by hand and lick the sauce off their fingers.

Recipe

3 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg crab, cut up
  • 3 tbsp coarse black pepper
  • 40 g butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, 3 shallots
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar

Method

  1. Toast the pepper in a dry pan until fragrant.
  2. Fry garlic and shallots in butter.
  3. Add pepper, oyster sauce, soy sauce and sugar.
  4. Toss in the crab and cook covered 8-10 minutes.
  5. Stir until the sauce clings and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter and more garlic.
  • Choose low-sodium soy sauce.
  • Share with a salad to balance.

History

The dish grew up beside chilli crab in Singapore's seafood restaurants and is now a standard of the city. The generous black pepper gives heat without chili.
