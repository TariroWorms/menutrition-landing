🥪 Churrasco chileno

Origin: Chile · Santiago

The Chilean churrasco is a sandwich of thin grilled beef steak in a fresh bun, topped with mashed avocado, sliced tomato and mayonnaise. The soft bun, juicy beef and creamy toppings make it a beloved Chilean fast-food classic. It is typically eaten at lunch from sandwich bars.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g thin beef steaks
  • 4 round buns
  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 3 tomatoes, sliced
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season the steaks with salt and pepper.
  2. Grill or pan-fry them in the hot oil for 1–2 minutes per side, so they stay tender.
  3. Split the buns and warm them on the grill.
  4. Mash the avocado with a pinch of salt.
  5. Fill each bun with the steak, avocado, tomato slices and mayonnaise and serve immediately.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace some of the mayonnaise with extra mashed avocado or a spoon of plain yoghurt; mayonnaise is dense in calories.
  • Pick lean steak and trim the fat: protein stays high while fat goes down.
  • Add extra tomato and lettuce for volume, and use a smaller wholegrain bun.

History

The churrasco is one of the best known Chilean sandwiches and is found in sandwich bars and cafés across the country. It uses a thin cut of beef cooked quickly on a hot grill. The same sandwich with tomato and green beans is the chacarero, and with avocado and tomato it is sometimes called an Italiano in Chile. It remains a staple of casual eating in Chile.
