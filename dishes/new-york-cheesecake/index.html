🍰 New York Cheesecake

Origin: USA · New York City

New York cheesecake is a tall, dense baked cheesecake made from cream cheese, sugar, eggs and vanilla on a crisp graham cracker crust, often finished with a layer of sour cream. Its texture is rich, smooth and firm rather than airy, with a gentle tang. It is served chilled in slim wedges, sometimes with fruit.

Recipe

12 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g graham crackers, crushed
  • 80 g butter, melted
  • 900 g cream cheese, room temperature
  • 200 g sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 200 g sour cream
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

  1. Mix the cracker crumbs with the butter, press into a 23 cm springform tin and bake 10 minutes at 175 °C.
  2. Beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth, then add the eggs one at a time with the vanilla and lemon juice.
  3. Pour over the crust and bake at 160 °C for 60–70 minutes until set at the edges but slightly wobbly in the centre.
  4. Spread with sour cream mixed with a little sugar and bake 10 minutes more.
  5. Cool in the oven with the door ajar, then chill for at least 6 hours before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut 12–16 slim slices instead of 8 large ones.
  • Top with fresh berries rather than syrups or extra sauce.
  • Use light cream cheese and sour cream to reduce the fat.

History

Cheesecake has a long European history, but the New York style took shape in the city's delis and restaurants in the early 20th century, after cream cheese became widely available. It is now a classic American dessert served across the country.
