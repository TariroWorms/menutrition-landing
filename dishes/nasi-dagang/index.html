Nasi dagang (coconut rice with tuna curry)

Origin: Malaysia · Terengganu

Nasi dagang is a rich east-coast rice dish. A blend of regular and glutinous rice is steamed with coconut milk, shallots and fenugreek until fragrant and sticky. It is served with a thin orange tuna curry, pickled vegetables and sometimes a boiled egg.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g long-grain rice
  • 150 g glutinous rice
  • 400 ml coconut milk (half for the rice, half for the curry)
  • 1 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • 3 shallots
  • 2 lemongrass stalks
  • 300 g tuna, cubed
  • salt, plus ginger, turmeric, tamarind and chili for the curry

Method

  1. Soak both rices for 1 hour and drain.
  2. Mix with coconut milk, sliced shallots, fenugreek and salt.
  3. Steam for 30 minutes until tender and glossy.
  4. Simmer a spiced paste of ginger, turmeric, chili and lemongrass with the remaining coconut milk and tamarind.
  5. Add the tuna, cook for 10 minutes and serve beside the rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk in the rice.
  • Serve with extra pickled vegetables.
  • Keep the portion of rice moderate and add more fish.

History

The dish is closely tied to Terengganu and Kelantan, where it is sold for breakfast from roadside stalls. Its name is often explained as rice of the traders, though the origin is told in different ways.
