Pidpenky with potatoes (fried honey mushrooms)

Origin: Ukraine

Pidpenky are honey mushrooms that grow in clusters on tree stumps, and frying them with potatoes is a favourite autumn meal. The potatoes turn golden and crisp at the edges while the mushrooms add a deep, woodsy flavour. A spoon of sour cream and plenty of dill finish the pan.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g honey mushrooms (or other mushrooms)
  • 700 g potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 3 tbsp sunflower oil
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • 1 bunch dill
  • salt, pepper

Method

  1. Clean the mushrooms and boil them for 10 minutes; drain.
  2. Peel the potatoes and cut into thin batons or slices.
  3. Fry the potatoes in oil over medium heat until almost tender.
  4. In another pan fry the onion, then add the mushrooms until browned.
  5. Combine everything, season and fry together for 5 minutes.
  6. Stir in sour cream, sprinkle with dill and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a non-stick pan and half the oil.
  • Replace some potato with courgette or cauliflower.
  • Skip the sour cream or use yogurt instead.

History

Mushroom foraging is a strong tradition in Ukraine, especially in the forested north and the Carpathians. Honey mushrooms are among the most commonly picked species each autumn.
