Berkoukes (Algerian large couscous stew)

Origin: Algeria

Berkoukes is a thick stew of large, hand-rolled couscous pearls simmered in a rich broth with lamb, vegetables and chickpeas. The grains swell and become soft and plump, making the dish more like a porridge-soup. It is warming and filling, served in deep bowls.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g large couscous (berkoukes)
  • 400 g lamb, cubed
  • 2 tomatoes, grated
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 100 g cooked chickpeas
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1.5 L water

Method

  1. Brown the lamb and onion in oil.
  2. Add tomatoes, spices and water, and simmer 1 hour.
  3. Add carrots and chickpeas and cook 15 minutes.
  4. Stir in the berkoukes grains.
  5. Cook 20 minutes, stirring, until plump and soft.
  6. Serve hot in deep bowls.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb and trim fat.
  • Add more vegetables like turnips and zucchini.
  • Use less oil.

History

Berkoukes is a winter dish from Algeria, particularly known in the north and east, and is often served during cold months and Ramadan. The grains are traditionally rolled by hand in large batches.
