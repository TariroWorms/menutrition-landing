Prosciutto e melone is a classic Italian summer antipasto of chilled cantaloupe wedges draped with paper-thin slices of cured ham. The sweet, juicy melon and the salty, silky prosciutto balance each other in every bite. It needs no cooking and is served as a starter or light lunch.

What it is made of

prosciutto crudocantaloupe

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe cantaloupe melon (about 1.2 kg)
  • 120 g prosciutto crudo, thinly sliced
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • A few fresh mint or basil leaves

Method

  1. Chill the melon, then halve it, scoop out the seeds and cut into 8 wedges.
  2. Remove the rind if you like, so the slices are easy to eat.
  3. Drape a slice of prosciutto over or around each wedge.
  4. Finish with black pepper and a few herb leaves and serve cold.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 2–3 slices of prosciutto per person and more melon; the ham is the saltier, denser part.
  • Trim the visible white fat from the prosciutto slices.
  • Serve it as a starter before a light main rather than with extra sides.

History

Pairing melon with cured ham is a long-standing Italian custom, especially in regions known for prosciutto such as Emilia-Romagna and Friuli. It is now a fixture of summer menus across Italy and beyond.
