Gaz (Isfahan nougat)

Origin: Iran · Isfahan

A soft, chewy white nougat packed with pistachios or almonds and lightly scented with rosewater. Traditionally it contains a sweet tree sap, though modern versions use sugar syrup and egg white. It is sold in boxes dusted with starch and eaten in small slices with tea.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g sugar
  • 100 g glucose syrup or honey
  • 60 ml water
  • 1 egg white
  • 100 g pistachios
  • 1 tbsp rose water
  • starch for dusting

Method

  1. Cook sugar, syrup and water to 140 C.
  2. Whisk the egg white to stiff peaks.
  3. Slowly pour the hot syrup into the egg white while whisking until thick and glossy.
  4. Fold in pistachios and rose water.
  5. Press into a tray dusted with starch, cool, and cut into pieces.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut into small squares to manage sugar.
  • Add more nuts and less syrup for protein.
  • Share it with fruit and unsweetened tea.

History

Gaz is the signature sweet of Isfahan, long associated with the city's bazaars. Its production is a seasonal craft and it is a very common souvenir across Iran.
