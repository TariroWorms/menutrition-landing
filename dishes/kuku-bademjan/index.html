Kuku bademjan (Persian eggplant frittata)

Origin: Iran

Kuku bademjan is a baked frittata of mashed roasted eggplant, eggs, garlic and saffron. It is soft, lightly smoky and slices neatly. It is eaten warm or cold with bread, yogurt or pickles.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g eggplant
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 small onion, grated (80 g)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp flour
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric and a pinch of saffron in 1 tbsp water
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Roast or grill whole eggplants until soft; peel and mash.
  2. Fry the onion and garlic briefly in oil.
  3. Mix eggplant, eggs, flour, onion, garlic, turmeric, saffron water, salt and pepper.
  4. Pour into an oiled baking dish.
  5. Bake at 180 C for 30-35 minutes until set and golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast rather than fry the eggplant to cut the oil.
  • Add herbs for extra flavor and fiber.
  • Cut into small squares and serve with salad.

History

Kuku is a core category of Persian egg dishes, often made with herbs, potatoes or vegetables. The eggplant version is common at home and at picnics.
