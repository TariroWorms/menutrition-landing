Corn on the cob is a whole ear of sweetcorn boiled until the kernels are tender and juicy, then brushed with a little butter and sprinkled with salt. The kernels are sweet, crisp and bursting with juice. It is eaten by hand as a side dish at barbecues and summer meals, or as a snack sold at markets and beaches.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 ears of sweetcorn, husked
  • 20 g butter
  • Salt to taste
  • Water for boiling

Method

  1. Bring a large pot of water to the boil.
  2. Add the corn and cook for 5–8 minutes until the kernels are tender.
  3. Drain, brush each ear with a little butter and sprinkle with salt. Serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush the butter on thinly — 1 tsp of butter is about 35 kcal.
  • Season with herbs, chilli or lime instead of extra butter.
  • One ear is a typical side portion next to grilled meat or salad.

History

Maize was domesticated in what is now Mexico thousands of years ago, and corn on the cob is eaten today across the Americas and much of the world, especially in late summer when sweetcorn is in season.
