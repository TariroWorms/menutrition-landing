Sukuma wiki (Kenyan sautéed greens)

Origin: Kenya

Sukuma wiki is a simple dish of collard greens or kale, sliced thin and cooked quickly with onion and tomato. It is lightly savory and still slightly crisp. It is the usual partner of ugali and grilled meat.

What it is made of

kaleoniontomatooil

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g collard greens or kale
  • 1 onion, sliced (100 g)
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped (200 g)
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 garlic clove
  • salt

Method

  1. Wash and slice the greens into thin ribbons.
  2. Fry the onion in oil until soft and golden.
  3. Add garlic and tomato and cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add the greens and a splash of water.
  5. Cook covered 5-7 minutes, season and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use just a spoonful of oil to keep it light.
  • Do not overcook to preserve vitamins.
  • Add a little lemon juice at the end.

History

The name is Swahili for "push the week", because the leafy greens are cheap and help stretch a household budget until the next payday. It is among the most common everyday dishes in Kenya.
