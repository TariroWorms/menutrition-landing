Grilled chicken thigh is boneless thigh meat marinated in garlic, oil and spices and cooked over high heat until charred outside and juicy within. Thighs have a little more fat than breast, so they stay moist and full of flavour. They are served with rice, salad, grilled vegetables or flatbread almost anywhere in the world.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g boneless chicken thighs
  • 3 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp black pepper
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Mix garlic, spices, lemon, oil and salt and coat the chicken; marinate for 30 minutes or longer.
  2. Heat a grill or griddle pan until very hot.
  3. Grill the thighs for 5–6 minutes per side until charred and cooked through.
  4. Rest for 3 minutes and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat and remove the skin before cooking.
  • Use just enough oil to coat the meat; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Weigh a 150–180 g portion and fill the rest of the plate with vegetables.

History

Spiced, grilled chicken thighs are a staple of home cooking and grill restaurants in many cuisines today, from the Middle East to the Americas.
