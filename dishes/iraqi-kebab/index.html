Iraqi Kebab

Origin: Iraq

Iraqi kebab is a simple, much-loved street dish: minced lamb kneaded with grated onion, parsley and salt, pressed onto wide flat skewers and grilled over charcoal until smoky and juicy. The meat is soft, rich and full of grill flavour. It is served off the skewer with a generous sprinkle of tangy sumac, sliced onion and grilled tomatoes.

What it is made of

minced lambonionparsleysumacsalt

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g minced lamb (with some fat)
  • 1 onion, finely grated and squeezed
  • 1 small bunch parsley, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tsp sumac
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced, to serve

Method

  1. Mix the lamb with grated onion, parsley and salt and knead for a few minutes until sticky.
  2. Chill for 20 minutes, then press portions onto flat skewers.
  3. Grill over hot charcoal for 8–10 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  4. Toss the sliced onion with sumac and parsley.
  5. Serve the kebab with the sumac onions and grilled tomatoes.

How to make it lighter

  • Ask for leaner minced lamb, or mix half lamb and half lean beef.
  • Grilling lets fat drip away; skip any extra oil.
  • Fill half the plate with sumac onions, tomatoes and salad greens.

History

Charcoal-grilled minced lamb kebab is one of the most common street and restaurant foods in Iraq today, from Baghdad grills to family barbecues.
