Kazanlak ponichka (Bulgarian donut)

Origin: Bulgaria · Kazanlak

These donuts are golden rings of airy yeast dough with a crisp skin. They are lightly sweet and are dusted with powdered sugar. Locals eat them warm for breakfast or as a snack with milk or tea.

Recipe

6 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour
  • 250 ml warm milk
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 50 g sugar
  • 1 egg
  • oil for frying
  • powdered sugar

Method

  1. Mix flour, milk, yeast, sugar and egg into a soft dough.
  2. Knead 8 minutes and let rise for an hour.
  3. Roll out to 1.5 cm and cut rings.
  4. Rest the rings for 20 minutes.
  5. Fry in hot oil until golden on both sides.
  6. Drain and dust with sugar.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake instead of frying to cut fat.
  • Drain well on paper.
  • Skip the sugar topping or use less.

History

They are a street and bakery favorite in the town of Kazanlak, known also for its rose valley. Fried dough rings are common across the Balkans.
