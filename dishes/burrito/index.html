A burrito is a large flour tortilla wrapped around a filling of rice, black beans, seasoned beef, cheese and salsa, then toasted until lightly crisp. Each bite mixes soft rice and beans with savoury meat, melted cheese and a fresh, tangy salsa, often with a spoonful of sour cream. It is a filling, hand-held meal.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 200 g long-grain rice
  • 400 g minced or finely diced beef
  • 1 onion and 2 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 400 g cooked black beans
  • 100 g grated cheese
  • 150 g tomato salsa
  • 4 tbsp sour cream
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook the rice. Brown the beef with onion, garlic, cumin, salt and pepper for 8–10 minutes.
  2. Warm the beans with a splash of water and lightly mash some of them.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan so they fold without tearing.
  4. Lay out each tortilla and fill the centre with rice, beans, beef, cheese, salsa and a spoonful of sour cream.
  5. Fold in the sides, roll up tightly and toast seam-side down in the pan for 1–2 minutes per side.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef and cut the cheese and sour cream by half; the salsa keeps it juicy.
  • Make it a burrito bowl over lettuce without the tortilla to drop a large share of the carbohydrates.
  • Fill with more beans and peppers and less rice for extra fibre and fullness.

History

Burritos are associated with northern Mexico, especially Chihuahua and Sonora, where wheat flour tortillas are common. The large, rice-filled version is usually credited to Mexican-American cooks in California, especially San Francisco's Mission District in the 1960s. Today it is a staple of Mexican and Tex-Mex restaurants worldwide.
