Pork trotters and ginger vinegar

Origin: Hong Kong · Guangdong
595kcal
per serving · 350 g

Pork trotters are simmered with chunks of fresh ginger and peeled boiled eggs in sweet black vinegar until the sauce turns sticky and tangy. The meat is soft and gelatinous, the eggs dark and flavorful. It is a warming dish, traditionally served to new mothers.

What it is made of

porkgingereggvinegarsugar

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork trotters, chopped
  • 500 g fresh ginger, sliced
  • 6 boiled eggs, peeled
  • 800 ml sweet black vinegar
  • 100 g brown sugar

Method

  1. Blanch the trotters for 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Fry the ginger dry until fragrant.
  3. Add trotters, vinegar and sugar.
  4. Simmer covered for 2 hours.
  5. Add the eggs for the last 30 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim the fat before serving.
  • Use less sugar; the sweet black vinegar already adds sweetness.
  • Eat in small portions with rice.

History

In Cantonese tradition this dish is prepared for women after childbirth and when a baby is celebrated. The ginger is thought to warm the body.
