Pan mee (hand-torn noodle soup)

Origin: Malaysia

Pan mee is a comforting bowl of chewy, hand-torn noodles in a savoury anchovy broth. The dough of flour, egg and water is pinched into thin pieces and dropped into the soup. It is topped with minced pork, mushrooms, a soft egg, crispy anchovies and leafy greens, often with a chili paste on the side.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g plain flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100 ml water
  • 150 g minced pork
  • 8 dried anchovies for broth, plus extra fried
  • 4 shiitake mushrooms
  • 200 g greens
  • soy sauce, garlic, 2 eggs

Method

  1. Knead flour, egg, water and salt into a smooth dough and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Simmer anchovies in water for 20 minutes and strain for the broth.
  3. Fry pork, garlic and mushrooms with soy sauce.
  4. Tear the dough into thin pieces and boil in the broth until they float.
  5. Add greens and a poached egg; top with pork and crispy anchovies.

How to make it lighter

  • Use fewer anchovies to reduce salt.
  • Add more greens and mushrooms.
  • Make the dough with half wholemeal flour.

History

The dish is closely linked to the Hakka community in Malaysia, and Kuala Lumpur is known for its pan mee stalls. The noodles are made fresh in the shop.
