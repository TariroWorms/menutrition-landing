Pappardelle ai funghi is a simple Italian pasta of wide ribbons tossed with mushrooms sautéed in garlic, a splash of cream and plenty of fresh parsley. The sauce is earthy and silky, coating the broad noodles without weighing them down. It is served hot as a vegetarian first course, especially in mushroom season.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g pappardelle
  • 500 g mixed mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 150 ml cooking cream
  • A handful of chopped parsley
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Cook the pappardelle in salted water until al dente; keep a cup of the pasta water.
  2. Sauté the mushrooms in the oil over high heat until browned, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the cream, season and simmer for 2 minutes.
  4. Toss with the drained pasta, loosening with pasta water if needed.
  5. Finish with parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light cooking cream or half the cream plus pasta water.
  • Double the mushrooms and serve a smaller amount of pasta.
  • Brown the mushrooms in a non-stick pan with just 1 tsp oil.

History

Mushroom sauces for wide egg-style ribbons are common across central and northern Italy, where wild mushrooms such as porcini are gathered in autumn. The creamy version is a widespread modern trattoria and home style rather than a single regional recipe.
