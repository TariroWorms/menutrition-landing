🥟 Pirozhki with meat

Pirozhki are small Russian hand pies made from soft yeast dough. This version is filled with minced beef, fried onion, chopped hard-boiled egg and fresh dill, then baked until golden. The crust is light and slightly sweet, the filling savoury and juicy. They are eaten warm as a snack, with soup or on the go.

Recipe

12 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g plain flour, 7 g dried yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt
  • 250 ml warm water and 3 tbsp oil
  • 400 g minced beef
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • A small bunch of dill, chopped
  • 1 beaten egg for glazing

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water and oil into a soft dough and let it rise for 1 hour.
  2. Fry the onion, add the beef and cook until browned; cool, then mix in the chopped eggs, dill, salt and pepper.
  3. Divide the dough into 12 pieces, flatten each, add a spoon of filling and pinch closed.
  4. Place seam-side down on a tray, rest for 20 minutes and brush with beaten egg.
  5. Bake at 190 °C for 20–25 minutes until golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced beef and drain the fat after frying.
  • Bake rather than deep-fry them.
  • Have one or two with a bowl of vegetable soup as a full meal.

History

Pirozhki have been part of Russian and Eastern Slavic home baking for centuries, made both baked and fried with countless fillings. Meat pirozhki are a classic of Russian home cooking, bakeries and train-station kiosks to this day.
