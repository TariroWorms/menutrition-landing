Kimchi Pancake (Kimchijeon)

Origin: South Korea

Kimchijeon is a savoury Korean pancake made from chopped kimchi and scallions folded into a simple flour and egg batter, then pan-fried until the edges turn crisp and lacy. The inside stays soft and tangy with a gentle chilli heat. It is cut into wedges and shared as a snack or side dish.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g ripe kimchi, chopped, plus 3 tbsp kimchi juice
  • 100 g wheat flour
  • 1 egg
  • 100 ml cold water
  • 2 scallions, cut into short lengths
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil

Method

  1. Mix the flour, egg, water and kimchi juice into a smooth batter.
  2. Fold in the kimchi and scallions.
  3. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan, spread the batter thinly and fry 4 minutes until golden underneath.
  4. Flip and cook 3–4 minutes more, pressing gently, then cut into wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tbsp oil instead of 2 in a good non-stick pan: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Spread the batter thin so each bite has more crisp kimchi and less batter.
  • Serve with a crunchy cucumber salad to make a smaller portion more filling.

History

Jeon, pan-fried savoury pancakes, have a long history in Korean cooking, and the kimchi version is one of the most popular at home. In Korea it is often eaten on rainy days, and a popular saying links the sizzle of the pan to the sound of rain.
