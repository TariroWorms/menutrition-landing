Kotleta pa-minsku (Minsk-style chicken cutlet)

Origin: Belarus

Kotleta pa-minsku is a Belarusian cousin of chicken Kiev: a flattened chicken breast is wrapped around chilled butter with dill and parsley. It is coated twice in egg and breadcrumbs and fried, so the crust is crunchy while the melting butter keeps the meat juicy. It is served with mashed potato or vegetables.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 4 chicken breasts (600 g)
  • 80 g butter
  • 1 tbsp chopped dill and parsley
  • 2 eggs
  • 50 g flour
  • 100 g breadcrumbs
  • oil for frying
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Mix the soft butter with herbs, shape into 4 small logs and freeze for 20 minutes.
  2. Butterfly and pound the chicken breasts thin; season.
  3. Wrap each breast tightly around a butter log, sealing the ends.
  4. Dredge in flour, dip in egg, roll in breadcrumbs and repeat the egg and crumbs.
  5. Fry in hot oil for 3–4 minutes until golden, then finish in the oven at 180 °C for 10 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the cutlets entirely in the oven brushed with a little oil.
  • Use half the butter and add more herbs and garlic.
  • Serve with a large salad instead of mashed potato.

History

Stuffed chicken cutlets of this kind became popular in Soviet restaurants, and Minsk had its own named version. It is still a familiar dish on Belarusian restaurant menus.
