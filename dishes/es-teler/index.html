Es teler

Origin: Indonesia

Es teler is an Indonesian iced fruit cocktail of ripe avocado, young coconut flesh and jackfruit strips, topped with shaved ice and a drizzle of sweetened condensed milk. It is creamy, cold and tropical, with soft avocado contrasting the fragrant, chewy jackfruit. It is sold everywhere from street carts to food courts as a refreshing dessert.

What it is made of

avocadococonutjackfruitcondensed milkice

Recipe

4 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 200 g young coconut flesh, in strips
  • 150 g ripe jackfruit, in strips
  • 120 ml coconut water
  • 80 g sweetened condensed milk
  • Shaved ice

Method

  1. Scoop the avocado into small pieces.
  2. Divide avocado, coconut and jackfruit between four glasses.
  3. Add a little coconut water to each glass.
  4. Top with shaved ice and drizzle with condensed milk; serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Condensed milk is the most calorie-dense part: 1 tbsp ≈ 60 kcal, so use one spoon per glass.
  • Add more ice and coconut water for volume.
  • Half an avocado per glass is plenty.

History

Es teler is generally said to have been popularised in Jakarta in the 1980s, when a version of the recipe won a cooking competition and was turned into a chain of stalls. Today it is one of Indonesia's most popular iced desserts.
