Töltött káposzta (Hungarian stuffed cabbage)

Origin: Hungary
420kcal

Töltött káposzta consists of sour cabbage leaves wrapped around minced pork, rice and paprika, then stewed on a bed of sauerkraut. The result is tangy, savoury and deeply comforting. It is served with a spoonful of sour cream and bread.

Recipe

6 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole pickled cabbage (about 1 kg) or leaves
  • 500 g minced pork
  • 100 g rice
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 200 g sauerkraut
  • 100 g smoked sausage
  • sour cream, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Soften the onion, mix with pork, rice, paprika, salt and pepper.
  2. Separate the cabbage leaves and trim thick ribs.
  3. Roll a spoonful of filling in each leaf.
  4. Spread sauerkraut and sausage in a pot and layer the rolls on top.
  5. Add water to barely cover and simmer for 1 hour 15 minutes.
  6. Serve with sour cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean pork or turkey mince and skip the smoked sausage.
  • Rinse the sauerkraut if you want less salt.
  • Serve with light yoghurt instead of sour cream.

History

The dish is a festive favourite in Hungary, especially at Christmas and weddings, and similar stuffed cabbage rolls are found throughout Central and Eastern Europe. It is often reheated for the following day as it tastes better after resting.
