Api morado (Bolivian hot purple corn drink)

Origin: Bolivia

Api morado is a hot, thick, deep purple drink made from purple corn flour or kernels simmered with sugar, cinnamon, cloves and citrus. It is warm, lightly spiced and gently sweet. It is a traditional breakfast and cold-weather drink in the Bolivian highlands, usually eaten with a pastry.

What it is made of

purple cornsugarcinnamoncloveslemon

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g purple corn flour
  • 1.5 L water
  • 100 g sugar
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 lemon, juice and peel

Method

  1. Mix the corn flour with a little cold water.
  2. Bring the remaining water to a boil with cinnamon, cloves and peel.
  3. Stir in the corn mixture and cook 25 minutes, stirring often.
  4. Add sugar and lemon juice.
  5. Serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar or a little honey instead.
  • Pair with a whole-grain pastry.
  • It is naturally fat free and vegan.

History

Purple corn has been grown in the Andes for centuries. Api is sold by street vendors in the early morning and is also served on All Saints' Day.
