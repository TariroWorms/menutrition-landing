🍕 Pizza prosciutto e rucola

Pizza prosciutto e rucola is a white pizza — mozzarella without tomato — topped after baking with prosciutto crudo, fresh arugula and shavings of parmesan. The contrast is the point: hot, chewy crust and melted cheese against cool, salty ham and peppery leaves. It is served whole and is one of the most ordered pizzas in Italian restaurants.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g ready pizza dough (or homemade)
  • 150 g mozzarella, torn
  • 80 g prosciutto crudo, thinly sliced
  • 40 g arugula
  • 20 g parmesan, shaved
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Method

  1. Preheat the oven with a stone or tray inside to the maximum.
  2. Stretch the dough by hand into a 30 cm round, leaving a slightly thicker rim.
  3. Scatter the mozzarella over the base and drizzle with half the oil.
  4. Bake on the hottest setting of the oven (250–280 °C) on a preheated stone or tray for 7–10 minutes until the crust is blistered.
  5. Top the hot pizza with prosciutto, arugula and parmesan, drizzle with the rest of the oil and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Shave the parmesan thinly — a little goes a long way.
  • Use less mozzarella and spread it thinly — cheese is the most calorie-dense topping.
  • Pile on the arugula and share the pizza alongside a salad.

History

The combination is a modern pizzeria classic rather than a historic recipe, and no single origin is recorded. It is popular throughout Italy and in Italian restaurants worldwide.
