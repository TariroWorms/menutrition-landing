Qofte të fërguara (Albanian fried meatballs)

Origin: Albania

Qofte are small beef meatballs mixed with grated onion, mint and crumbled white cheese, then fried until crusty outside and juicy within. They taste savory with a cool herbal note. Serve them hot with tomatoes, fried peppers and bread, or as a meze with yogurt.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 100 g feta, crumbled
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 1 egg
  • 40 g breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp chopped mint
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp oil for frying

Method

  1. Combine the beef, feta, onion, egg, breadcrumbs, mint and salt.
  2. Knead for 2 minutes until the mixture holds together.
  3. Rest it in the fridge for 20 minutes.
  4. Shape into walnut-sized balls.
  5. Fry in a little oil over medium heat for 8 minutes, turning, until browned.
  6. Drain on paper and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the meatballs on a tray instead of frying to save oil.
  • Use lean beef or half turkey.
  • Serve with a big tomato and cucumber salad.

History

Meatballs of seasoned minced meat are common across the Balkans and the former Ottoman lands. In Albania the version with white cheese and mint is a staple of home cooking and of taverns.
