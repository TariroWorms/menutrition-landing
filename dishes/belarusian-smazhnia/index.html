Smazhnia (Belarusian meat flatbread)

Origin: Belarus

Smazhnia is a thin round of dough covered with raw minced meat, then topped with tomato, onion, peppers or mushrooms and a layer of grated cheese, and baked until the meat is cooked and the cheese bubbles. It looks a lot like pizza but tastes more like a meat pie, juicy and savoury. In Belarus it is sold in cafés and bakeries as a quick, filling snack.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 180 ml water, 1 tbsp oil, 5 g yeast, salt
  • 400 g minced pork or beef
  • 2 tomatoes, 1 onion, 1 bell pepper
  • 150 g mushrooms
  • 150 g grated cheese

Method

  1. Knead a dough from flour, water, yeast, oil and salt and let rise for 40 minutes.
  2. Season the minced meat with salt, pepper and a grated onion.
  3. Roll the dough into two thin rounds and place on a lined tray.
  4. Spread the raw minced meat evenly over the dough, right to the edges.
  5. Top with sliced tomato, pepper and mushrooms, then cover with cheese.
  6. Bake at 210 °C for 20-25 minutes until the meat is cooked and the cheese golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean minced beef or turkey and a lighter cheese.
  • Load more vegetables and less cheese on top.
  • Make the base with part wholemeal flour and roll it very thin.

History

Smazhnia is a fairly modern dish that became popular in Belarusian cafés and homes in the late 20th century and is often presented as the national answer to pizza. The name comes from the Belarusian word for frying.
