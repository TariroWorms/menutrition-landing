Aloo pie

Origin: Trinidad and Tobago

Aloo pie is a golden fried turnover of soft wheat dough stuffed with mashed potato seasoned with cumin, garlic and pepper. The shell is crisp outside and tender inside, and the filling is soft and warm. It is a favorite Trinidadian street snack, often served with tamarind sauce or chutney.

Recipe

6 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 200 ml warm water
  • 500 g boiled potatoes, mashed
  • 1 onion, minced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp hot pepper
  • oil for frying, salt

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and water into a soft dough and let rise for 1 hour.
  2. Cook onion and garlic briefly in a little oil.
  3. Mix with the mashed potatoes, cumin and pepper.
  4. Roll the dough into small circles.
  5. Fill each with potato, fold and seal the edges.
  6. Fry in hot oil until golden and drain.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake instead of frying to save oil.
  • Use whole wheat flour for the dough.
  • Serve with tamarind chutney instead of heavy sauces.

History

Aloo pie reflects the Indo-Trinidadian heritage of the island, where South Asian flavors blended into local street food. It is commonly sold alongside doubles from roadside stalls.
