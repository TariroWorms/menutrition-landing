Mämmi (Finnish Easter pudding)

Origin: Finland

Mämmi is a thick brown pudding made from rye flour, malted rye and water, flavored with Seville orange zest. It tastes sweet and malty with a hint of bitterness. It is eaten cold with cream or milk and a spoon of sugar.

Recipe

6 servings270 min

Ingredients

  • 1 l water
  • 200 g rye flour
  • 100 g rye malt
  • 50 g molasses or dark syrup
  • Zest of 1 bitter orange
  • 200 ml cream
  • Sugar to taste

Method

  1. Boil half of the water and whisk in the flour and malt.
  2. Add the remaining water, molasses and zest.
  3. Leave to sweeten for 1 hour in a warm place.
  4. Pour into a dish and bake at 150 C for 3 hours.
  5. Cool completely and serve cold with cream.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve with milk instead of cream.
  • Add fresh berries instead of extra sugar.
  • Keep portions small, since the pudding is quite sweet.

History

The pudding has been eaten in Finland around Easter for centuries and was originally linked to the days when cooking was forbidden. Today it is sold in shops during spring.
