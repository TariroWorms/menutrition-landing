🍹 Lemon mint drink

This lemon mint drink blends lemon juice, fresh mint, sugar and ice with water until frothy and icy. It is sweet-sour, very fresh and almost spoonable when made thick. Cafés serve it in tall glasses with a mint sprig, as a cooling partner to hot weather and rich food.

What it is made of

lemonmintsugarwaterice

Recipe

4 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 4 lemons (about 160 ml juice)
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 800 ml cold water
  • A large handful of fresh mint leaves
  • 2 cups of ice

Method

  1. Squeeze the lemons and strain out the seeds.
  2. Dissolve the sugar in a little of the water.
  3. Blend the lemon juice, sugared water, remaining water, mint and ice until smooth and pale green.
  4. Taste, adjust the sugar and serve at once, well chilled.

How to make it lighter

  • Almost all the calories come from sugar: each tablespoon adds about 48 kcal.
  • Halve the sugar and add more mint and ice for flavour.
  • Order it 'no sugar' or lightly sweet in cafés and sweeten to taste yourself.

History

Blended lemon and mint is a modern café drink that became a fixture of restaurant menus across the Gulf and the wider Middle East in recent decades.
