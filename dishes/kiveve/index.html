Kiveve (Paraguayan squash and cheese porridge)

Origin: Paraguay

Kiveve is a smooth, golden dish made by cooking squash until it falls apart, then thickening it with cornmeal and enriching it with milk and fresh cheese. The taste is mild, sweet and savory at once. It is served as a side dish, especially next to grilled meat and on festive tables.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g squash, peeled and cubed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 300 ml milk
  • 80 g fine cornmeal
  • 150 g fresh cheese, cubed
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Soften the onion in oil in a wide pot.
  2. Add the squash and a splash of water, cover and cook until very soft, about 20 minutes.
  3. Mash the squash roughly with a fork.
  4. Pour in the milk and bring it to a gentle simmer.
  5. Sprinkle in the cornmeal while stirring to avoid lumps.
  6. Cook 8 minutes until thick, then fold in the cheese and salt.
  7. Serve hot while the cheese is just melting.

How to make it lighter

  • Use low-fat milk and a lighter fresh cheese to trim fat.
  • Add extra squash and less cornmeal for a lower-carb, more vegetable-rich version.
  • Serve it next to lean grilled meat instead of rice or bread.

History

The dish comes from the Guarani culinary tradition, where squash and corn are old staples. It is usually associated with family gatherings and the Holy Week table in Paraguay. Recipes vary by household in how thick and how cheesy the porridge is.
