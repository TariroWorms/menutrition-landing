Boiled white rice is plain polished rice cooked in salted water until the grains are soft and fluffy. It has a mild, neutral taste that pairs with almost any dish. It is served as a side to curries, stews, grilled meat, fish and vegetables.

What it is made of

ricewatersalt

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g long-grain white rice
  • 600 ml water
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water until it runs almost clear.
  2. Bring the water and salt to a boil and add the rice.
  3. Cover and simmer on low heat for 15–18 minutes until the water is absorbed.
  4. Remove from heat, rest covered for 5 minutes and fluff with a fork.

How to make it lighter

  • Measure the portion: about 150 g cooked rice is a standard side.
  • Fill half the plate with vegetables and a quarter with rice.
  • Cook it plain without added fat to keep it light.

History

Rice was domesticated in Asia thousands of years ago and is today a staple food for a large share of the world's population. Plain boiled rice is eaten daily from East Asia to the Middle East, Africa and the Americas.
