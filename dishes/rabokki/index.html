🍜 Rabokki

Rabokki combines two Korean street-food favourites: chewy tteokbokki rice cakes and instant ramyeon noodles, simmered together in a sweet-spicy gochujang sauce. Slices of fish cake, a halved boiled egg and scallions finish the pot. The noodles soak up the red sauce, and it is eaten straight from a shallow pan, often shared.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g rice cakes (tteok)
  • 1 pack ramyeon noodles (about 110 g, no seasoning)
  • 2 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 100 g fish cake, sliced
  • 2 eggs, boiled
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 500 ml water

Method

  1. Soak the rice cakes in water for 10 minutes if hard.
  2. Bring the water to a boil and stir in gochujang and sugar.
  3. Add rice cakes and fish cake; simmer 6–8 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  4. Add the noodles and cook 3 minutes more.
  5. Top with halved eggs and scallions; serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the noodles and add cabbage for volume.
  • Cut the sugar in the sauce.
  • Share one pan between two and add an extra egg for protein.

History

Rabokki is a modern Korean snack-bar dish that grew out of tteokbokki, eaten today at bunsik restaurants across South Korea.
