Borani bademjan (eggplant and yogurt dip)

Origin: Iran

Borani bademjan is a creamy dip of fried eggplant folded into thick yogurt with garlic. The eggplant is silky and rich, the yogurt tangy. It is served as a starter or side with flatbread.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g eggplant, sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 250 g thick yogurt
  • 2 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp dried mint
  • salt
  • walnuts or fried onion to garnish (optional)

Method

  1. Salt the eggplant slices and rest for 15 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Fry in oil until golden and soft on both sides.
  3. Mash the eggplant roughly with a fork.
  4. Mix the yogurt with garlic and salt, then fold in the eggplant.
  5. Top with dried mint and a drizzle of oil; serve with bread.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake or air-fry the eggplant slices to use much less oil.
  • Use low-fat yogurt for a lighter dip.
  • Serve with raw vegetables instead of bread.

History

Borani is a family of Persian yogurt dishes made with cooked vegetables, and eggplant is one of the best loved versions. It is typically served at the start of a meal alongside herbs and bread.
