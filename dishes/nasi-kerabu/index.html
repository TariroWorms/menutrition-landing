Nasi kerabu (blue herb rice)

Origin: Malaysia · Kelantan

Nasi kerabu is the famous blue rice of Kelantan. The rice is tinted with butterfly-pea flowers and served with a heap of raw herbs, bean sprouts, long beans, cucumber and toasted coconut. Fried fish or chicken, a crispy fish cracker and spicy sambal complete the plate, which is mixed by hand before eating.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g jasmine rice
  • 10 butterfly-pea flowers
  • 200 g mackerel, fried
  • 100 g bean sprouts
  • 100 g long beans
  • 1 cucumber
  • 50 g toasted grated coconut
  • Herbs (mint, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaf), plus sambal and fish crackers to serve

Method

  1. Steep the flowers in warm water and strain the blue liquid.
  2. Cook the rice with the blue water and a pinch of salt.
  3. Shred the herbs, vegetables and cucumber finely.
  4. Fry or grill the fish; toast the coconut in a dry pan until golden and pound it with a little salt.
  5. Serve the rice with the vegetables, herbs, coconut, fish and sambal.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose grilled instead of fried fish.
  • Use less coconut and more raw vegetables.
  • Serve with sambal on the side to control chili and oil.

History

The dish belongs to the east coast of the Malay Peninsula, where it is a common breakfast. The blue colour is natural and comes from flowers soaked in water.
