Sobolo (Ghanaian hibiscus drink)

Origin: Ghana

Sobolo is a deep ruby drink brewed from dried hibiscus petals with ginger, cloves and pineapple peel, then sweetened and served very cold. It is tart like cranberry, with a warm spice undertone and a fruity finish. It is sold in bottles and bags at markets, parties and funerals alike.

What it is made of

hibiscusgingerpineapplesugar

Recipe

6 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 50 g dried hibiscus petals
  • 30 g fresh ginger
  • 100 g pineapple peel and core
  • 4 cloves
  • 60 g sugar
  • 1500 ml water

Method

  1. Rinse the hibiscus petals, then simmer with ginger, cloves and pineapple peel in water for 15-20 minutes
  2. Turn off the heat and steep for another 10 minutes
  3. Strain, pressing the petals to release all the colour
  4. Stir in sugar until dissolved
  5. Chill and serve over ice, with a slice of pineapple if you like

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar or use a little pineapple juice or honey instead
  • Dilute with extra water or sparkling water for a lighter drink
  • Brew a stronger concentrate and keep it in the fridge for 3-4 days

History

The same drink is called bissap in neighbouring French-speaking regions and zobo in Nigeria. Sobolo is rooted in the northern and coastal communities of Ghana and is valued as a refreshing everyday cooler.
