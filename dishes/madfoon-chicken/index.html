Chicken madfoon is a Saudi and Yemeni rice dish in which spiced chicken is sealed and slow-cooked over rice, so the juices drip down and flavour the grains. The chicken is seasoned with garlic, cardamom, cinnamon and black lime, and the rice is cooked with onion. The meat comes out very tender and is served on a large tray over the aromatic rice.

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (about 1.2 kg), quartered
  • 400 g basmati rice, soaked
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 black limes, pierced
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Rub the chicken with garlic, cardamom, salt, pepper and 1 tbsp oil; leave for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the onions in the remaining oil, add the cinnamon, black limes and 800 ml water and bring to a boil.
  3. Add the rice, cook for 5 minutes and transfer to a deep roasting tray.
  4. Place the chicken on a rack over the rice, seal the tray tightly with foil.
  5. Cook at 170 °C for about 1.5 hours until the chicken is tender and the rice has absorbed the juices.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the chicken skin before serving to cut fat noticeably.
  • Serve a palm-sized portion of rice and fill half the plate with salad.
  • Use 1 tbsp oil instead of two: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Madfoon means 'buried' in Arabic, after the traditional method of cooking meat in a sealed pit or underground oven. Today it is widely cooked in home ovens and restaurants across Saudi Arabia and Yemen.
