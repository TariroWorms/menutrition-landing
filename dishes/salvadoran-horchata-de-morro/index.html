Horchata de morro (Salvadoran seed and rice drink)

Origin: El Salvador

Horchata de morro is made from toasted and ground morro seeds, rice, cacao and spices steeped in water and sweetened. It is nutty, lightly spiced and distinctly different from rice-only horchata. It is served very cold and is a classic drink for Christmas and Easter.

Recipe

8 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g morro seeds, toasted
  • 50 g rice
  • 1 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 litre water
  • 100 g sugar
  • 250 ml milk (optional)
  • Ice

Method

  1. Toast and grind the morro seeds with the rice and cocoa.
  2. Soak the mixture with cinnamon in the water for 4 hours.
  3. Blend and strain well.
  4. Stir in sugar and milk.
  5. Chill in the fridge.
  6. Serve over ice.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by half for a lighter drink.
  • Use plant milk for a dairy-free option.
  • Strain well to keep the texture smooth.

History

The recipe is a Salvadoran take on horchata, using the local jicaro or morro seed. It is especially common at Christmas and as a refreshing drink with festive meals.
