🍜 Khao Piak Sen

Khao piak sen is a Lao chicken noodle soup with thick, slightly chewy rice noodles cooked directly in a broth flavoured with galangal. The noodles release starch, giving the soup a silky body. It is topped with shredded chicken, fresh dill and cilantro and served with lime wedges and chili on the side.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken thighs or legs
  • 250 g thick fresh rice noodles
  • 40 g galangal, sliced
  • 2 l water
  • 1 small bunch dill, chopped
  • 1 small bunch cilantro, chopped
  • 2 limes, cut into wedges
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken and galangal in the water for about 40 minutes.
  2. Remove the chicken, shred the meat and discard the bones.
  3. Season the broth with salt and bring it back to a boil.
  4. Add the rice noodles and cook until tender and the broth thickens slightly.
  5. Serve topped with chicken, dill and cilantro, with lime on the side.

How to make it lighter

  • Use skinless chicken breast for a leaner bowl.
  • Skim the fat from the broth before adding the noodles.
  • Add extra greens such as bean sprouts or bok choy for volume.

History

Khao piak sen is a staple of Lao home cooking and street food, commonly eaten for breakfast. It is found throughout Laos and in Lao communities abroad.
