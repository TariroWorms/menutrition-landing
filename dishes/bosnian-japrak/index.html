Japrak (Bosnian stuffed leaves)

Origin: Bosnia and Herzegovina

Japrak is a Bosnian dish of chard or grape leaves wrapped around minced beef and rice. The little rolls simmer in a light broth until the leaves are silky and the filling is juicy. The taste is gentle and savory with a faint tang. It is served warm, often with a spoon of yogurt.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g chard or grape leaves
  • 300 g minced beef
  • 100 g rice
  • 1 onion, grated
  • 200 ml tomato passata
  • 1 tsp black pepper and salt

Method

  1. Blanch the leaves briefly and trim the thick stems.
  2. Mix the beef, rice, onion, pepper and salt.
  3. Put a spoonful of filling on each leaf and roll tightly.
  4. Pack the rolls in a pot and cover with passata and water.
  5. Simmer gently for 50 minutes until the rice is cooked.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef and skip the added oil.
  • Serve with plain yogurt instead of sour cream.
  • Add more leaves and less rice to lower the carbs.

History

Stuffed leaves came to the Balkans through Ottoman cooking and became a household dish across Bosnia. Families still make large pots of it for gatherings.
