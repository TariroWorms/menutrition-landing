Chicken burger

Origin: International

A chicken burger is a grilled chicken breast fillet served in a soft bun with crisp lettuce, sliced tomato and a spread of mayonnaise. The chicken is juicy with light char from the grill, the vegetables add freshness and the mayonnaise brings creaminess. It is eaten by hand as a quick lunch, often with fries or salad.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 4 small chicken breast fillets (about 500 g)
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp oil, 1 tsp paprika, ½ tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Flatten the chicken to an even 1.5 cm thickness.
  2. Rub with oil, paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  3. Grill or pan-cook over medium-high heat for 5–6 minutes per side until cooked through.
  4. Toast the buns and spread with mayonnaise.
  5. Assemble with lettuce, tomato and the chicken.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a light mayonnaise or thin the spread with yogurt; 1 tbsp regular mayonnaise ≈ 90 kcal.
  • Keep the chicken grilled, not breaded and fried.
  • Choose a side salad instead of fries, or serve the chicken in lettuce leaves.

History

Chicken burgers are eaten worldwide, from fast-food chains to home grills, as a lighter-feeling alternative to the beef burger.
