Sopa de quinua (quinoa vegetable soup)

Origin: Peru · Andes

Sopa de quinua is an everyday highland soup of quinoa simmered with potatoes, carrots, celery, and peas. The grains soften and release starch, giving the broth a gentle body. Cubes of fresh cheese and chopped parsley or huacatay are added just before serving. It is mild enough for children.

Recipe

6 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g quinoa, well rinsed
  • 300 g potatoes
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery stalk
  • 100 g peas
  • 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves
  • 100 g fresh cheese (queso fresco)
  • 1.8 l vegetable broth, parsley, salt

Method

  1. Rinse the quinoa several times to remove its bitter coating.
  2. Sauté onion and garlic in a little oil until soft.
  3. Add the broth and quinoa and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Add diced potatoes, carrot and celery and cook for 15 minutes.
  5. Stir in the peas and cook 5 minutes more.
  6. Season, add cubed cheese and parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Skip the cheese or use a little for a vegan, lower-fat soup.
  • Add spinach or chard at the end for extra iron.
  • Make a big batch; it reheats well and thickens overnight.

History

Quinoa has been grown around Lake Titicaca for thousands of years and was a staple long before the arrival of the Spanish. Soups like this remain a common home and school lunch in Puno and other highland regions.
