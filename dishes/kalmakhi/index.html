Kalmakhi (Georgian trout with tarragon)

Origin: Georgia

Kalmakhi is the Georgian word for trout, and the fish from cold mountain rivers is a favourite across the country. The whole fish is stuffed with tarragon, grilled on skewers over coals or fried in butter, and served with tart pomegranate seeds or a splash of pomegranate juice. The flesh is delicate and moist, lifted by the anise notes of tarragon.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 4 small whole trout, cleaned
  • 1 bunch tarragon
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 pomegranate
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt, black pepper

Method

  1. Pat the trout dry and season inside and out with salt and pepper.
  2. Fill each cavity with tarragon sprigs.
  3. Grill over hot coals or fry in butter for 4-5 minutes per side until the skin is crisp.
  4. Meanwhile, remove the pomegranate seeds.
  5. Serve the fish scattered with pomegranate seeds, lemon wedges and extra tarragon.

How to make it lighter

  • Grill instead of frying to avoid extra butter.
  • Serve with steamed vegetables or a fresh salad rather than fries.
  • Squeeze pomegranate juice over the fish instead of making a buttery sauce.

History

Trout is caught in the fast rivers of the Caucasus and has long been part of Georgian cooking, especially near Borjomi and in Racha. Tarragon, called tarkhuna, is one of the most loved herbs in Georgia and pairs naturally with fish.
