Firni (Azerbaijani rice flour milk pudding)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Firni is a silky pudding of milk, rice flour and sugar, cooked gently until it thickens and then scented with rose water. It is poured into bowls and served warm or chilled, usually with a sprinkle of cinnamon or a few chopped nuts. Mild and soothing, it is a favourite for children and festive tables alike.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 litre milk
  • 80 g rice flour
  • 100 g sugar
  • 1 tbsp rose water
  • Pinch of salt
  • Ground cinnamon to serve

Method

  1. Whisk the rice flour into 200 ml of cold milk until smooth.
  2. Heat the rest of the milk with sugar and salt until just simmering.
  3. Pour in the rice flour mixture while whisking constantly.
  4. Cook on low heat, stirring, for 10–15 minutes until thick and creamy.
  5. Stir in the rose water, pour into bowls and dust with cinnamon.
  6. Serve warm or chill for 2 hours.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk to cut fat with little change in taste.
  • Reduce the sugar to 60 g and top with fresh fruit for sweetness.
  • Serve in small 150 g bowls.

History

Rice flour puddings of this kind are found from the Caucasus to Iran and South Asia, and firni is a common home dessert in Azerbaijan. It is often prepared during Ramadan evenings and for Novruz.
