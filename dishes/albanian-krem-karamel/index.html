Krem karamel (Albanian caramel custard)

Origin: Albania

Krem karamel is a baked egg custard turned out of its mold so the dark caramel runs over the top. It is smooth, wobbly and gently sweet with a bittersweet sauce. It is a common dessert in homes, cafés and restaurants.

What it is made of

milkeggsugarvanillacaramel

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 4 eggs
  • 100 g sugar for the custard
  • 80 g sugar for the caramel
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • pinch of salt

Method

  1. Melt the caramel sugar with a splash of water until amber.
  2. Pour it into the molds and tilt to coat the bases.
  3. Whisk the eggs with the remaining sugar, vanilla and salt, then whisk in the warm milk.
  4. Strain the mixture into the molds.
  5. Set the molds in a tray of hot water and bake at 160 °C for 40 minutes.
  6. Cool completely, chill, then turn out onto plates.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk and cut the sugar a little.
  • Serve small molds to control portions.
  • Add fresh fruit instead of extra sauce.

History

Baked custards with caramel are found across Europe and arrived in Albanian kitchens through neighboring cuisines. Today it is one of the most common restaurant and home desserts in the country.
