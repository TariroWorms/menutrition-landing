Roast pork with crackling (flæskesteg)

Origin: Denmark

Flæskesteg is roast pork with a golden, blistered crackling crust. It is carved into slices and served with browned potatoes, gravy and sweet-sour red cabbage. The meat is juicy and the rind crunchy.

Recipe

6 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg pork loin with rind
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 4 bay leaves
  • 500 g red cabbage
  • 800 g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar
  • 300 ml water for gravy

Method

  1. Score the rind in narrow lines and rub with salt.
  2. Place bay leaves under the roast in a tray.
  3. Roast at 180 C for about 90 minutes.
  4. Raise to 250 C for 10 minutes until the rind blisters.
  5. Rest the roast under foil for 15 minutes.
  6. Simmer shredded cabbage with vinegar and a little sugar.
  7. Boil potatoes and make gravy from the pan juices.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove the rind fat from your portion.
  • Serve with plenty of cabbage for fibre.
  • Thicken gravy lightly instead of with cream.

History

It is regarded as Denmark's unofficial national dish and is the centrepiece of Christmas dinner, but also of many Sunday lunches. Scoring the rind and salting it well are the keys to good crackling.
