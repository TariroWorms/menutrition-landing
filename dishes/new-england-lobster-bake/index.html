🦞 New England Lobster Bake

A New England lobster bake is a festive seafood feast in which whole lobsters, clams, corn on the cob, potatoes and sausage are steamed together, traditionally layered with seaweed. Everything absorbs a briny, smoky sea flavour: sweet lobster meat, tender clams, juicy corn and savoury sausage. It is served piled on a table and eaten with the hands.

What it is made of

lobsterclamscornpotatosausage

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 4 live lobsters (about 600 g each)
  • 1 kg clams, scrubbed
  • 4 ears of corn, halved
  • 800 g small potatoes
  • 400 g smoked sausage, cut into chunks
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 lemons
  • Fresh seaweed or 1 l water, salt, bay leaf

Method

  1. Put the potatoes, onion, bay leaf and seaweed or salted water in a very large pot; cover and steam for 15 minutes.
  2. Add the sausage and corn and steam 5 minutes more.
  3. Lay the lobsters on top, cover and steam 10–12 minutes.
  4. Add the clams and steam until they open, 5–7 minutes; discard any that stay shut.
  5. Tip everything onto a platter and serve with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Lobster and clams are lean; fill up on them and keep the sausage to a couple of pieces.
  • Serve with lemon and herbs rather than rich dips.
  • Choose corn over a second helping of potatoes, and add a big green salad.

History

The lobster bake grew out of the coastal clambake, a seaside cooking method in which food is steamed over hot stones under seaweed. It is a summer tradition along the coasts of Maine and Massachusetts and is often made today in a large stockpot.
