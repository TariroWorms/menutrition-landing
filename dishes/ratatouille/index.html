🍆 Ratatouille

Ratatouille is a Provençal vegetable stew of eggplant, zucchini, bell peppers, tomato and onion, gently cooked with herbs until soft and melded. The vegetables keep some shape while releasing a sweet, aromatic sauce. It is served hot or at room temperature as a side for grilled meat or fish, or as a main with bread or rice.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 1 eggplant (about 300 g), cubed
  • 2 zucchini, cubed
  • 2 bell peppers, cubed
  • 4 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme and basil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Sauté the eggplant in 1 tbsp oil until lightly browned, then set aside.
  2. Sauté the zucchini and peppers for 5 minutes and set aside.
  3. Soften the onion and garlic in the remaining oil, then add the tomatoes and cook 10 minutes.
  4. Return all vegetables to the pan with the herbs, salt and pepper.
  5. Cover and simmer gently for 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally.

How to make it lighter

  • Roast the vegetables on a tray with a light brush of oil instead of frying in batches — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Serve it as a large side to bulk up a meal without adding much energy.
  • Eggplant soaks up oil — salt it for 20 minutes and pat dry before cooking.

History

Ratatouille is usually traced to the Nice area of Provence and to summer home cooking; the name is linked to the French verb 'touiller', to stir. It is now known worldwide as an emblem of Mediterranean cooking.
