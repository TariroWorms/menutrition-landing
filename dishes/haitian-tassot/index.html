Tassot (Haitian fried goat)

Origin: Haiti

Tassot is a bar and party favorite in Haiti. Pieces of goat are marinated in sour orange juice, garlic and hot pepper, simmered until the meat is tender and the liquid has gone, and then fried in the remaining fat until the edges are crunchy. It is served with pikliz and fried plantains.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g goat meat, cubed
  • 100 ml sour orange juice
  • 5 cloves garlic
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 3 sprigs thyme
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Marinate the goat with juice, garlic, onion, thyme and salt for 3 hours.
  2. Cook with a little water, covered, for 90 minutes until tender.
  3. Uncover and let the liquid evaporate.
  4. Lift out the pieces and dry them.
  5. Fry in hot oil until crisp and browned.
  6. Serve with pikliz.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean cuts and trim fat.
  • Roast in the oven instead of frying.
  • Serve with a large portion of vegetables.

History

Tassot is often made with goat, though beef and turkey versions exist. Slow cooking followed by frying is a technique shared with griot.
