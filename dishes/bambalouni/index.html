Bambalouni (Tunisian fried doughnut)

Origin: Tunisia · Sidi Bou Said
297kcal

Bambalouni is a large ringless doughnut of yeast dough fried until puffy and golden, then rolled in sugar. It is crisp outside, soft and airy inside, and best eaten warm. Vendors sell it by the seaside as a breakfast or afternoon snack.

Recipe

8 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g flour
  • 7 g dry yeast
  • 1 egg
  • 60 g sugar, plus extra for coating
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 250 ml warm water
  • Oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt and egg with warm water into a soft, sticky dough.
  2. Knead for 10 minutes and let rise for 1 hour.
  3. Divide into balls and rest 15 minutes.
  4. Stretch each into a thick oval disc.
  5. Fry in hot oil on both sides until puffed and golden.
  6. Drain and roll in sugar while warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Dust with a little sugar rather than rolling heavily.
  • Drain on paper to reduce oil.
  • Share one piece between two people.

History

It is closely linked with the hillside town of Sidi Bou Said, where it is sold hot to visitors and locals. Similar fried dough sweets exist across the Mediterranean.
