Sayur lodeh (Javanese vegetable coconut stew)

Origin: Indonesia · Java

Sayur lodeh is a soft, creamy vegetable stew in coconut milk, seasoned with shallot, garlic, galangal and a little coriander. Cabbage, long beans, eggplant, young jackfruit or tempeh are common additions. It is eaten with rice and a crisp side such as fried tempeh.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g cabbage, shredded
  • 100 g long beans, cut in pieces
  • 1 eggplant (200 g), cubed
  • 100 g tempeh, cubed
  • 400 ml coconut milk and 400 ml water
  • 5 shallots, 3 garlic cloves, 2 cm galangal, ground
  • 1 tsp coriander seed, 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and a little palm sugar

Method

  1. Fry the ground spice paste with the bay leaf until fragrant.
  2. Pour in the water and coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Add the tempeh, eggplant and long beans and cook 10 minutes.
  4. Add the cabbage.
  5. Simmer a further 5 to 10 minutes without a hard boil so the milk does not split.
  6. Season and serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use light coconut milk to cut the fat by about a third.
  • Add more vegetables and less coconut milk for a lighter version.
  • Serve with brown rice for extra fibre.

History

Lodeh is a staple of Javanese home cooking and appears at everyday and ceremonial meals alike. Each region has its own mix of vegetables and spices.
