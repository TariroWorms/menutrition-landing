Vacío (grilled flank)

Vacío is the flank of the beef, a wide flat cut with a membrane and fat on one side. Grilled slowly, the fat side crisps like crackling while the meat stays loose-grained and very juicy. It has a strong beefy taste and needs nothing but salt. Slices are often tucked into bread or eaten with chimichurri and salad.

What it is made of

beef flankcoarse saltchimichurri

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg beef flank (vacío) in one piece
  • Coarse salt
  • Chimichurri to serve

Method

  1. Light the fire and wait for an even bed of gentle embers.
  2. Salt the meat side of the flank.
  3. Grill fat side down for about 1 hour 15 minutes without moving it.
  4. Salt the top, turn and grill 20–30 minutes more until the fat is crisp.
  5. Rest 5 minutes, slice across the grain and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Leave the fat on while grilling for juiciness, then trim it on your plate.
  • Pair with grilled peppers and onions for fibre.
  • A 150 g portion fits most high-protein meal plans.

History

Vacío is one of the most popular cuts at Argentine parrillas and is little known by that name outside the Río de la Plata region. Its long cooking time makes it a typical centrepiece of a weekend asado.
