🥘 Classic papet vaudois

This classic papet vaudois pairs braised pork sausage with a soft stew of leeks and potatoes enriched with cream. The vegetables cook down until creamy and rustic, soaking up the savoury juices of the sausage. It is a simple, filling winter plate from western Switzerland, served hot with the sausage sliced on top.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g pork sausage
  • 800 g leeks, sliced
  • 600 g potatoes, diced
  • 100 ml cream
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 300 ml water
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Soften the leeks in the oil for 10 minutes over low heat.
  2. Add the potatoes and water, season and bring to a simmer.
  3. Place the sausage on top, cover and braise gently for about 45 minutes.
  4. Take out the sausage, stir in the cream and season.
  5. Serve the stew with the sliced sausage on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream; mashing some potato thickens the stew instead.
  • Prick the sausage before cooking and skim off fat from the surface.
  • Fill half the plate with the leek and potato and keep the sausage portion modest.

History

Papet vaudois is the emblematic dish of the canton of Vaud in western Switzerland, traditionally made with local pork sausages. The name papet describes the thick, mash-like texture of the leeks and potatoes. It remains a staple of Vaud home cooking and village festivals.
