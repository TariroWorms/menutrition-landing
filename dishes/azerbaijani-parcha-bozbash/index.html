Parcha bozbash (lamb and chickpea soup)

Origin: Azerbaijan

Parcha bozbash is the 'pieces' version of bozbash, made with chunks of lamb on the bone instead of meatballs. The meat cooks slowly with chickpeas until tender, then potatoes and onion are added. A few sour plums give the broth a gentle tartness, while saffron adds colour. It is a warming, filling meal in a bowl.

Recipe

4 servings130 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g lamb on the bone, in pieces
  • 150 g chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 4 potatoes
  • 2 onions
  • 6 dried sour plums
  • a pinch of saffron
  • 1 tsp dried mint, salt, pepper

Method

  1. Cover the lamb and chickpeas with 2.5 litres of water and bring to the boil.
  2. Skim the foam and simmer for 1.5 hours until the meat is tender.
  3. Add whole small potatoes or large chunks and the sliced onions.
  4. Add the dried plums and cook another 20 minutes.
  5. Stir in the saffron, steeped in a little hot water, and season with salt and pepper.
  6. Serve in deep bowls with dried mint on top.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the lamb before cooking.
  • Cook a day ahead and lift off the set fat.
  • Serve with a large herb plate instead of extra bread.

History

'Parça' means 'piece' in Azerbaijani, which distinguishes this soup from the meatball bozbash. Both are classic home dishes, and recipes vary from region to region and family to family.
