🥬 Caldo verde

Caldo verde is Portugal's best-known soup: potatoes and garlic simmered and blended into a smooth base, then filled with very finely shredded kale and finished with slices of smoky chorizo and a thread of olive oil. It is light but warming, green and slightly smoky, and traditionally served in clay bowls.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g floury potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 200 g kale or Portuguese couve, very finely shredded
  • 100 g chorizo, thinly sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1.5 l water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Simmer the potatoes and garlic in salted water for 20 minutes until soft, then blend until smooth.
  2. Add the shredded kale and simmer for 5 minutes until just tender.
  3. Brown the chorizo slices briefly in a dry pan.
  4. Serve the soup with chorizo slices on top and a little olive oil.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 2–3 slices of chorizo per bowl — the flavour carries far.
  • Drizzle 1 tsp oil per bowl instead of a tablespoon.
  • Add more kale for a bigger bowl with few extra calories.

History

Caldo verde comes from the Minho region in northern Portugal and is now eaten all over the country, especially at festivals such as the June Santos Populares, where it is served late into the night.
