🥗 Country salad (salade paysanne)

Salade paysanne, or “country salad”, is a French bistro salad of mixed greens topped with warm fried bacon, a soft or hard-boiled egg, tomato and crunchy croutons. The hot, salty bacon and toasted bread contrast with the cool leaves. It is substantial enough to be eaten as a lunch main course.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g mixed salad leaves
  • 100 g bacon, cut into strips
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 60 g croutons
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp red vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Boil the eggs for 7–9 minutes, cool and halve them.
  2. Fry the bacon in a dry pan until crisp.
  3. Whisk the oil and vinegar with salt and pepper.
  4. Toss the leaves and tomatoes with the dressing.
  5. Top with the warm bacon, eggs and croutons and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the croutons or skip them to cut the starch.
  • Drain the bacon on paper towels and use less of it.
  • Dress the leaves lightly: 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.

History

Salads named “paysanne” are found on bistro and brasserie menus across France; the name refers to hearty rustic ingredients rather than to one fixed recipe.
