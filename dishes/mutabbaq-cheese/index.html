🧀 Cheese Mutabbaq

Cheese mutabbaq is the meat-free version of the Hijazi street snack: thin dough folded around beaten egg, melting white cheese and spring onion, then fried on a griddle until crisp outside and gooey inside. It is cut into squares and eaten hot from the stall.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g ready-made thin dough sheets or stretched bread dough
  • 200 g white cheese, grated or crumbled
  • 3 eggs
  • 4 spring onions, chopped
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Black pepper

Method

  1. Mix the cheese, beaten eggs, spring onions and pepper.
  2. Stretch a dough sheet very thin on an oiled surface.
  3. Put a quarter of the filling in the centre and fold into a square parcel.
  4. Fry on an oiled griddle for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and crisp.
  5. Cut into squares and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose a lower-fat white cheese and use 150 g instead of 200 g.
  • Brush the griddle with oil instead of pouring it (1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal).
  • Share one mutabbaq and add a tomato-cucumber salad.

History

Cheese mutabbaq is sold alongside the meat version at mutabbaq stalls in the Hejaz region of Saudi Arabia and in Yemen.
