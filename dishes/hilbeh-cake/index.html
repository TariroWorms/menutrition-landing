Hilbeh cake (Levantine fenugreek semolina cake)

Origin: Palestine · Levant
264kcal

Hilbeh cake is a moist semolina cake perfumed with ground fenugreek and topped with almonds, then soaked in a light syrup. The fenugreek gives a warm, slightly bitter, maple-like aroma. It is cut into diamonds and eaten with tea or coffee.

Recipe

12 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g semolina
  • 1 tbsp ground fenugreek
  • 100 g sugar
  • 100 ml oil
  • 200 ml yogurt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • almonds for topping
  • 200 g sugar and 200 ml water for syrup

Method

  1. Mix the semolina, fenugreek, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Stir in the oil and yogurt and rest for 20 minutes.
  3. Spread in a tray, cut into diamonds and add almonds.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 30 minutes; meanwhile boil the sugar and water for 5 minutes for the syrup and let it cool.
  5. Pour cold syrup over the hot cake.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the syrup by a third.
  • Choose small pieces.
  • Use plain yogurt for lightness.

History

Fenugreek has long been used in the Levant as a home remedy and flavouring, especially for mothers after birth. Semolina cakes soaked in syrup are a common sweet across the region.
