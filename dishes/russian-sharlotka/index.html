Sharlotka (Russian apple sponge)

Sharlotka is the everyday Russian apple cake: a light batter of whipped eggs, sugar and flour poured over sliced tart apples and baked. It comes out tall and fluffy with a thin crisp crust and juicy apple pockets. It is served warm with tea, sometimes dusted with icing sugar or cinnamon.

What it is made of

appleeggsugarflourcinnamon

Recipe

8 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 180 g sugar
  • 180 g flour
  • 700 g tart apples
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • butter for the tin

Method

  1. Peel, core and slice the apples and toss them with cinnamon.
  2. Whisk the eggs with sugar for 8–10 minutes until pale and thick.
  3. Gently fold in the sifted flour.
  4. Put the apples into a buttered tin and pour the batter over them.
  5. Bake at 180 °C for 40–45 minutes until golden and a skewer comes out clean.

How to make it lighter

  • Use more apples and less sugar.
  • Replace part of the flour with whole-wheat flour.
  • Serve with plain yogurt instead of ice cream.

History

The name echoes the French charlotte, but the Russian home version became a simple sponge with apples. It is especially popular in autumn when apples from dachas are plentiful.
