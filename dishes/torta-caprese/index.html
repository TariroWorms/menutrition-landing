🍫 Torta Caprese

Origin: Italy · Capri, Campania

Torta caprese is a flourless Italian cake made from dark chocolate, ground almonds, butter, eggs and sugar. It bakes into a thin crisp crust over a dense, fudgy and slightly moist interior with a nutty flavour. It is usually dusted with icing sugar and served in thin slices.

Recipe

10 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g dark chocolate
  • 200 g ground almonds
  • 150 g butter
  • 4 eggs
  • 150 g sugar
  • pinch of salt
  • 1 tbsp icing sugar for dusting

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 170 °C and line a 24 cm tin.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter together gently, then let cool slightly.
  3. Beat eggs with sugar and salt until pale and thick.
  4. Fold in the chocolate mixture, then the ground almonds.
  5. Pour into the tin and bake 35–40 minutes until set at the edges and slightly soft in the centre.
  6. Cool completely and dust with icing sugar.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut 12 slices instead of 8: the cake is rich and a thin slice satisfies.
  • Serve with fresh berries instead of a scoop of ice cream.
  • Use chocolate with 70% cocoa for more flavour per gram and less sugar.

History

The cake is named after the island of Capri, and local lore says it was born when a baker forgot to add flour, though this story cannot be verified. It is now a staple of pastry shops along the Amalfi Coast and in Naples, and is naturally gluten-free.
