Jeju black pork BBQ (Heukdwaeji gui)

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g black pork belly, sliced 1 cm thick
  • 2 tsp coarse salt
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, sliced
  • 8 lettuce leaves
  • 2 scallions, shredded
  • 4 tbsp fermented anchovy sauce or ssamjang

Method

  1. Heat a grill or heavy pan until very hot.
  2. Lay the pork slices without crowding.
  3. Grill 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and crisp at the edges.
  4. Add garlic slices to the pan to cook in the rendered fat.
  5. Cut into bite-sized pieces and serve with salt, sesame oil, scallions and lettuce for wrapping.

How to make it lighter

  • Wrap in plenty of lettuce and vegetables to add fiber.
  • Drain excess fat on paper before eating.
  • Keep portions modest and skip the sugary sauces.

History

Jeju island is known for its native black pigs, raised on the island for generations. Grilling the meat simply lets its flavor stand out.
