Verashchaka (ribs and sausage in gravy)

Origin: Belarus

Verashchaka is a close cousin of machanka: pork ribs and homemade smoked sausage stewed in a thin, peppery gravy thickened with flour. The sauce is savory rather than creamy, perfumed with bay and black pepper. It is served piping hot in a clay or iron pot, with pancakes rolled up and dipped into it.

Recipe

6 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g pork ribs
  • 300 g smoked pork sausage
  • 2 pcs onion
  • 3 tbsp rye flour
  • 700 ml pork or chicken broth
  • 2 pcs bay leaves
  • 8 pcs black peppercorns
  • salt and black pepper — to taste
  • thin pancakes (blini), to serve

Method

  1. Cut the ribs into pieces and the sausage into thick slices and brown them well in a pot.
  2. Add chopped onion and cook until golden.
  3. Dry-toast the flour in a pan until lightly colored and stir it into the meat.
  4. Pour in the broth, add bay leaves and peppercorns and simmer covered for about 1 hour.
  5. Season with salt, then bake the pot uncovered at 180 °C for 15 minutes to concentrate the sauce.
  6. Serve in the pot with warm blini.

How to make it lighter

  • Chill the stew and lift off the solid fat before reheating.
  • Use lean chicken sausage in place of pork sausage.
  • Pair with a crunchy pickled vegetable plate to balance the richness.

History

The dish is often linked to the Belarusian word for sizzling, describing the hiss of meat in a hot pan. It has been a winter and holiday dish in Belarus and neighboring regions for a long time.
