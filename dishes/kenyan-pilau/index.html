Kenyan pilau (spiced beef rice)

Origin: Kenya · Swahili coast

Kenyan pilau is rice simmered in a rich beef broth with browned onions and a blend of cumin, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves. The grains are fragrant and the meat is tender. It is eaten with kachumbari and is a feast-day favourite.

Recipe

6 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g basmati rice
  • 500 g beef, cubed
  • 3 onions (300 g)
  • 2 potatoes (250 g)
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp pilau masala
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • salt

Method

  1. Brown the onions deeply in oil.
  2. Add beef, garlic and pilau masala and sear.
  3. Add water and simmer until the beef is tender, about 45 minutes.
  4. Add potatoes and the washed rice with water to cover by 2 cm.
  5. Cook covered on low heat 20 minutes until the rice is done.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean beef and half the oil.
  • Add carrots and peas for more vegetables.
  • Serve with plenty of kachumbari.

History

Pilau reached the East African coast through Arab and Indian traders and became part of Swahili cooking. In Kenya it is a classic for weddings, holidays and family gatherings.
