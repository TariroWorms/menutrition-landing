Bake and shark

Origin: Trinidad and Tobago · Maracas Bay

Bake and shark is the beach food of Trinidad. A seasoned shark fillet is deep fried and tucked into a split, puffy fried bread called a bake. Then comes a long list of toppings: chadon beni sauce, garlic sauce, tamarind, pineapple, slaw, lettuce and hot pepper sauce. It is messy, crunchy and fresh at once.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g shark fillets
  • 250 g flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 150 ml water
  • Garlic and chadon beni seasoning
  • Oil for frying
  • Lime, lettuce, tomato, slaw and sauces to serve

Method

  1. Marinate the shark in lime juice, garlic and chadon beni for 30 minutes.
  2. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, sugar and water into a soft dough and rest 20 minutes.
  3. Shape into rounds and deep fry until they puff and turn golden to make the bakes.
  4. Coat the shark in seasoned flour and fry until crisp.
  5. Split a bake and fill with fish.
  6. Add toppings and sauces and eat at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a smaller bake or air-fry the fish.
  • Fill generously with vegetables.
  • Choose a different fish if you limit mercury.

History

The dish is most closely tied to Maracas Bay on the north coast of Trinidad, where stalls have sold it for decades. Many stalls now also offer other fish.
