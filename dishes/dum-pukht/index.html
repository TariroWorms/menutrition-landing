Dum pukht (sealed-pot lamb)

Origin: Pakistan · Khyber Pakhtunkhwa

Dum pukht is a slow-cooking method and a dish: lamb marinated in yogurt, onion and mild spices is sealed in a heavy pot with dough and cooked on very low heat in its own steam. The meat becomes extremely tender and the sauce small, thick and aromatic. It is served with naan or plain rice.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 700 g lamb, in pieces
  • 200 g yogurt
  • 2 onions (250 g), fried
  • 40 g ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp cardamom, pinch of saffron
  • 60 ml ghee, salt, dough for sealing

Method

  1. Marinate the lamb with yogurt, paste, spices and salt for at least 3 hours.
  2. Layer the lamb with fried onions and ghee in a heavy pot.
  3. Seal the lid with a rope of dough.
  4. Cook on the lowest heat for 90-120 minutes without opening.
  5. Break the seal, stir gently and serve with naan.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean lamb leg and less ghee.
  • Serve with salad and a smaller bread portion.
  • Replace some yogurt with low-fat yogurt.

History

Dum pukht means roughly cooking by steam in a sealed pot and is linked to the Mughal-era courts and the cooking of Awadh. In Pakistan it is cooked at home and in restaurants, particularly with lamb.
