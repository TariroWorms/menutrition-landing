Shrimp kabsa is the seafood take on the Gulf's best-known rice dish. Long-grain rice is cooked in a tomato broth scented with garlic, baharat, dried lime and cardamom, then topped with shrimp sautéed in the same spices. The result is fragrant and lightly tangy, with sweet, juicy shrimp over fluffy orange-tinted rice.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g peeled shrimp
  • 400 g basmati rice, rinsed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tomatoes, grated, and 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tsp baharat
  • 1 dried lime, pierced, and 3 cardamom pods
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 700 ml water, salt

Method

  1. Soften the onion and half the garlic in 1 tbsp oil, add the tomatoes, tomato paste, baharat, dried lime and cardamom and cook for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the water and salt, bring to a boil, stir in the rice, cover and cook on low heat for 20 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile sauté the shrimp with the remaining garlic and oil and a pinch of baharat for 3–4 minutes.
  4. Fluff the rice, spread it on a platter and top with the shrimp.

How to make it lighter

  • Shrimp is lean, so most of the energy comes from rice and oil — keep the rice to about one cup cooked per person.
  • Sauté the shrimp in a non-stick pan with a teaspoon of oil.
  • Add extra tomato, onion and a side salad to make the plate bigger without many calories.

History

Kabsa is a staple across the Arabian Peninsula, and along the Gulf coast, where fishing and pearling shaped local cooking, it is often made with shrimp or fish instead of meat. Today shrimp kabsa is served in homes and restaurants throughout the Gulf states.
