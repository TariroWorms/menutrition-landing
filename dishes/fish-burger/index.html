A fish burger is a crisp fried fillet of white fish served in a soft bun with lettuce, a spoonful of mayonnaise and a squeeze of lemon. The fish is flaky and mild inside its golden coating, while the cool lettuce and creamy sauce balance the crunch. It is a fast-food and pub favourite, usually eaten with fries or a side salad.

Recipe

2 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 white fish fillets (about 120 g each)
  • 2 burger buns
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 lemon
  • 3 tbsp flour for dusting
  • Oil for frying, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Season the fish with salt, pepper and a little lemon juice, then dust with flour.
  2. Fry in 1 cm of hot oil for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and cooked through; drain.
  3. Mix the mayonnaise with a squeeze of lemon.
  4. Toast the buns, spread with lemon mayonnaise, add lettuce and the fish and close.

How to make it lighter

  • Pan-fry the fish in a little oil or bake it instead of deep-frying.
  • Use half the mayonnaise: 1 tbsp mayonnaise ≈ 90 kcal.
  • Serve with salad rather than fries.

History

Fried fish sandwiches are eaten across Europe, North America and beyond, from fast-food chains to seaside kiosks.
