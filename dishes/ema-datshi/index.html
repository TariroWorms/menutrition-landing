Ema datshi (Bhutanese chilli and cheese stew)

Origin: Bhutan

Ema datshi is Bhutan's national dish, a stew of large green or red chillies cooked in a creamy cheese sauce with onion and tomato. The chillies are treated as a vegetable, so it is both fiery and rich. It is eaten with red rice.

Recipe

3 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g large green chillies, sliced
  • 200 g fresh cheese (farmer's cheese) or feta-style, crumbled
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 250 ml water
  • salt

Method

  1. Place chillies, onion, tomato and garlic in a pot with the water.
  2. Simmer 10 minutes until the chillies are soft.
  3. Add the butter.
  4. Stir in the crumbled cheese.
  5. Cook 5 more minutes until the sauce is thick and creamy.
  6. Serve with red rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Deseed the chillies for a milder dish.
  • Use less butter and a lighter cheese.
  • Serve with plenty of rice to soften the heat.

History

Chillies arrived in Bhutan centuries ago and became central to local cooking, where they are eaten as a vegetable rather than a spice. Ema means chilli and datshi means cheese.
