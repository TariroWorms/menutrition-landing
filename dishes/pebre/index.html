🍅 Pebre

Origin: Chile · Central Chile

Pebre is the everyday Chilean table salsa: finely chopped tomato, onion, coriander and garlic with chili, oil and a splash of vinegar or lemon. It is fresh, herby and mildly to very spicy depending on the chili. It is served with bread, grilled meat, empanadas and seafood.

Recipe

6 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe tomatoes, finely diced
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 bunch fresh coriander, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1–2 hot green chilies, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp vinegar or lemon juice
  • Salt

Method

  1. Soak the chopped onion in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain to soften its bite.
  2. Combine the tomatoes, onion, coriander, garlic and chili in a bowl.
  3. Add the oil, vinegar or lemon juice and salt.
  4. Stir well and let it rest for 15 minutes so the flavours blend.
  5. Serve at room temperature with bread or grilled meat.

How to make it lighter

  • It is mostly vegetables, so it adds flavour for very few calories; the oil is the main source of kcal.
  • Use just one tablespoon of oil and a little more lemon juice for an even lighter salsa.
  • Spoon it onto lean grilled meat or fish instead of creamy sauces.

History

Pebre is a staple of Chilean tables and barbecues, and almost every household has its own version, with more or less chili, coriander or onion. Its name is usually linked to the Spanish word for pepper. The salsa is made fresh and, in many homes, put on the table at nearly every meal alongside the bread basket.
