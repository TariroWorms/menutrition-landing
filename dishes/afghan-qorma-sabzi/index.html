Afghan qorma-e-sabzi (lamb and spinach stew)

Origin: Afghanistan

Qorma-e-sabzi is an Afghan stew of lamb simmered with a large amount of spinach, cilantro and dill. The sauce is dark green and mellow, finished with lemon. It is eaten with basmati rice or naan.

Recipe

4 servings100 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb in cubes
  • 500 g spinach
  • 100 g cilantro and dill
  • 2 onions (200 g)
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 30 ml oil
  • juice of 1 lemon, 1 tsp turmeric, salt

Method

  1. Brown the onions in oil.
  2. Add the lamb and sear.
  3. Add garlic, turmeric, salt and water to cover.
  4. Simmer 1 hour.
  5. Add the chopped greens.
  6. Cook 20 more minutes and add lemon.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the fat from the lamb and skim the surface of the stew.
  • Serve with brown rice.
  • Use more greens and less meat.

History

Qorma means a braised stew, a method found across Afghan and Persian cooking. Herb stews like this are a regular family dish.
