Qizha (Gaza black seed cake)

Origin: Palestine · Gaza

Qizha is a Gazan sweet made from a paste of ground black seed (nigella) mixed with semolina, sesame, oil and sugar, then baked until firm. It tastes deeply nutty and slightly peppery with a sweet finish, and is cut into small squares. It is a traditional treat for the cold season and special visits.

Recipe

12 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g semolina
  • 100 g ground black seed
  • 100 g sugar
  • 100 ml vegetable oil
  • 50 g sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 150 ml water

Method

  1. Mix semolina, ground black seed, sesame, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Stir in oil and water into a thick batter.
  3. Spread into an oiled tray and smooth the top.
  4. Score into squares and bake at 180 C for 25 minutes.
  5. Cool and cut along the scores.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by a third.
  • Replace half of the oil with yogurt.
  • Enjoy a small square with tea.

History

Black seed has long been valued in Palestinian homes as a flavoring and folk remedy, and qizha is the classic way of turning it into a sweet. It is a popular local dessert in Gaza.
