This tuna salad is a light, Mediterranean-style plate of flaked tuna over crisp lettuce with tomato, cucumber and olives, dressed simply with olive oil. It is fresh, juicy and briny, with plenty of crunch and enough protein to work as a full meal. It is served cold as a quick lunch or a light dinner.

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 160 g drained canned tuna
  • 1 small head lettuce, torn
  • 2 tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 cucumber, sliced
  • 40 g olives
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon, salt and pepper

Method

  1. Wash and dry the lettuce and spread it over two plates.
  2. Add the tomato wedges, cucumber slices and olives.
  3. Flake the tuna over the top.
  4. Whisk the olive oil with lemon juice, salt and pepper, and drizzle over just before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Measure the olive oil: 1 tbsp ≈ 120 kcal, so a spoon for two plates keeps the salad light.
  • Choose tuna in water rather than in oil.
  • Keep olives to a small handful — they add fat and salt quickly.

History

Salads of tuna with fresh vegetables and olives are eaten all around the Mediterranean and in cafés worldwide, in countless local variations.
