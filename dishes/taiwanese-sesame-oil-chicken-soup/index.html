Taiwanese sesame oil chicken soup (mayou ji)

A warming soup of chicken pieces cooked with plenty of ginger and toasted sesame oil in a light broth. It smells nutty and has a deep, gingery flavor. It is traditionally eaten in cold weather and by women after childbirth.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken pieces
  • 60 ml toasted sesame oil
  • 100 g ginger, sliced
  • 200 ml rice wine or water
  • 100 g mushrooms
  • 1 tsp salt

Method

  1. Heat the sesame oil gently and fry the ginger until fragrant and golden.
  2. Add the chicken and brown on all sides.
  3. Add the mushrooms and stir for a minute.
  4. Pour in the rice wine (or water) and another 600 ml water and bring to a boil.
  5. Simmer for 30 minutes and season with salt.

How to make it lighter

  • Skim the fat and use skinless chicken.
  • Replace the rice wine with water for an alcohol-free soup.
  • Add leafy greens and noodles for a full meal.

History

Sesame oil chicken is a traditional Taiwanese comfort dish, often prepared in winter. It is considered nourishing in Chinese food culture. Rice wine is traditional, but the alcohol largely cooks off or can be replaced with water.
