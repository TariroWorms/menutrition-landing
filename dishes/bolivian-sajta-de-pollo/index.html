Sajta de pollo (Bolivian chicken in yellow chile sauce)

Origin: Bolivia

Sajta de pollo is chicken cooked in a thick, bright yellow sauce of aji amarillo, onion and garlic. It is served with boiled potatoes, chuño or rice and has a warm, fruity heat. It is a typical Andean family lunch, especially in La Paz.

Recipe

4 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken pieces
  • 3 tbsp aji amarillo paste
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 500 g potatoes
  • 100 g peas
  • 500 ml water
  • 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp oregano, salt

Method

  1. Heat the oil and sauté the onion and garlic in a pot until soft.
  2. Stir in the aji paste and oregano and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Add the chicken pieces and brown briefly.
  4. Pour in water, add salt and simmer 35 minutes.
  5. Add potatoes and peas and cook until tender.
  6. Serve with rice or chuño.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove chicken skin to lower the fat.
  • Use less oil when sautéing.
  • Pair with extra vegetables.

History

Sajta comes from the Aymara tradition, where the yellow aji is central to many stews. The chicken version is a common everyday dish and a staple of Bolivian home cooking.
