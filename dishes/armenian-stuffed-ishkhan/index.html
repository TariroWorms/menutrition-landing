Stuffed Sevan trout (ltsonats ishkhan)

Origin: Armenia · Lake Sevan

Ltsonats ishkhan is trout filled with a fragrant stuffing of chopped walnuts, softened onion, tarragon, cilantro and tart pomegranate seeds, then baked. The nuts make the filling rich, while the pomegranate cuts through with bright, sour bursts. It is a festive fish dish, served whole and sliced at the table.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 4 trout (about 300 g each), cleaned
  • 100 g walnuts, chopped
  • 60 g pomegranate seeds
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • Small bunch tarragon and cilantro, chopped
  • 20 g butter
  • Salt and pepper, lemon

Method

  1. Soften the onion in the butter, then mix with walnuts, herbs, pomegranate, salt and pepper.
  2. Season the trout inside and out.
  3. Fill each fish with the stuffing and close with toothpicks.
  4. Lay on a lined tray and bake at 200 °C for 20-25 minutes.
  5. Check that the flesh flakes near the bone.
  6. Serve with lemon and extra pomegranate seeds.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the walnuts and add more herbs to cut calories.
  • Use a spoon of olive oil instead of butter for the onion.
  • Pair with a light green salad.

History

Walnuts, herbs and pomegranate are a classic combination in Armenian cooking, and using them to stuff fish is a well-known way to dress up Sevan trout for guests. Many families also add a little pomegranate molasses.
