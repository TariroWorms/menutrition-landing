Wallenbergare (Swedish veal patties)

Origin: Sweden

Wallenbergare are soft, delicate veal patties made with cream and egg yolks, coated in breadcrumbs and fried in butter. They are tender and mildly rich with a thin golden crust. They are served with mashed potatoes, green peas, lingonberry jam and browned butter.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced veal
  • 250 ml cream
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 60 g breadcrumbs
  • 40 g butter, plus extra for browning
  • 200 g green peas, lingonberry jam to serve
  • 500 g potatoes
  • salt, to taste
  • white pepper, to taste

Method

  1. Chill the minced veal well, then work it with salt and pepper in a processor.
  2. Add egg yolks and then gradually the cold cream until smooth and light.
  3. Shape into patties and coat in breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry in butter over medium heat for about 4 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Serve with mashed potatoes, peas and lingonberry jam.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter and shallow-fry in a non-stick pan.
  • Replace some of the cream with milk.
  • Serve with plenty of green vegetables.

History

The dish is named after the Wallenberg family and is considered a refined classic of Swedish cooking. Its smooth texture comes from a finely ground, well-chilled mixture.
