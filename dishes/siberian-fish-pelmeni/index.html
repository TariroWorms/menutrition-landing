Siberian fish pelmeni

Origin: Russia · Siberia

Fish pelmeni are small, thin-skinned dumplings filled with minced fish such as pike, salmon, whitefish or nelma, mixed with onion and a little butter. Once boiled they are juicy and delicate, lighter than the classic meat version. They are served with melted butter, sour cream or a little vinegar and black pepper.

Recipe

5 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • Dough: 400 g flour, 1 egg, 180 ml cold water, a pinch of salt
  • 300 g pike fillet
  • 200 g salmon fillet
  • 1 onion (100 g)
  • 30 g soft butter
  • salt, white pepper
  • to serve: sour cream, dill

Method

  1. Knead a firm dough from the flour, egg, water and salt and rest it for 30 minutes.
  2. Mince the fish with the onion, then mix in the butter, salt and pepper.
  3. Roll the dough thinly and cut into 5-6 cm circles.
  4. Put a teaspoon of filling on each, fold into a half-moon and join the ends into a ring.
  5. Boil in salted water for 5-6 minutes after they float.
  6. Serve with sour cream and dill.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only lean white fish such as pike or cod for fewer calories.
  • Serve with yogurt and herbs instead of butter.
  • Make the dough partly with wholemeal flour.

History

Siberia's great rivers are rich in fish, so local cooks have long filled pelmeni with fish as well as meat. Like other pelmeni, they were traditionally made in large batches and frozen outdoors in winter.
