🌶️ Penne all'arrabbiata

Penne all'arrabbiata is penne in a fiery tomato sauce cooked with garlic and dried chili, finished with chopped parsley. 'Arrabbiata' means 'angry' in Italian, a nod to the heat of the chili. The sauce is simple, tangy and spicy, and the dish is served hot as a first course.

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 360 g penne
  • 400 g canned peeled tomatoes, crushed
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1–2 dried chilies, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Fresh parsley, chopped
  • Salt

Method

  1. Cook the penne in salted water until al dente.
  2. Warm the oil with the garlic and chili over low heat for 2 minutes without browning.
  3. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt and simmer for 15 minutes until thickened.
  4. Toss the drained penne in the sauce for 1 minute.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the oil to 1 tbsp — 1 tbsp ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Add sautéed zucchini or peppers to the sauce for more volume.
  • Weigh 80 g dry pasta per person for a standard portion.

History

Arrabbiata is a Roman dish from the Lazio region and a classic of the city's trattorias. Its spare ingredient list makes it one of the most widely cooked pasta sauces in Italy today.
