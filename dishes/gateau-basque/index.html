🍰 Gâteau basque

Gâteau basque is a round, low cake from the French Basque Country with a rich buttery crust wrapped around a filling of vanilla pastry cream or black cherries — this version combines both. Made with ground almonds, butter and eggs, it is dense and crumbly outside and soft and custardy inside. It is served in slices at room temperature, often with coffee.

Recipe

8 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g almond flour
  • 150 g butter, softened
  • 150 g sugar
  • 2 eggs plus 1 yolk for glazing
  • 300 g pastry cream, chilled
  • 150 g pitted black cherries or cherry compote
  • 1 pinch of salt

Method

  1. Beat the butter with the sugar and salt, then beat in the eggs and fold in the almond flour to a soft dough. Chill for 1 hour.
  2. Press two-thirds of the dough into a buttered 22 cm tin, covering the base and sides.
  3. Spread the pastry cream over the base and dot the cherries on top.
  4. Roll out the remaining dough into a lid, lay it on top and seal the edges.
  5. Brush with the beaten yolk, score a criss-cross pattern and bake at 180 °C for 40–45 minutes until golden. Cool before slicing.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut into 12 thin slices instead of 8 — a smaller wedge satisfies because the cake is so rich.
  • Use more cherries and less pastry cream in the filling to lower the fat per slice.
  • Pair a slice with black coffee or tea rather than a sweet drink.

History

The cake is traditionally associated with the town of Cambo-les-Bains in the French Basque Country, where it has been baked for generations. The black-cherry filling links it to the cherries of Itxassou nearby. Today it is sold in bakeries across the Basque Country and southwest France.
