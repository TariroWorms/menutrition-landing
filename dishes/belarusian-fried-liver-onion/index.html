Smazhanaya pechan (fried liver with onion)

Origin: Belarus

Smazhanaya pechan is a quick everyday dish of thin slices of pork liver dusted in flour and fried with soft golden onion. A spoonful of sour cream and a little water turn the pan juices into a mild, creamy sauce. It is served with mashed potato or buckwheat and a pickled cucumber.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g pork liver
  • 2 large onions (250 g)
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 150 g sour cream
  • 2 tbsp sunflower oil
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Remove the membrane and veins and cut the liver into 1 cm slices; soak in milk or water for 20 minutes if you like.
  2. Fry the sliced onion in half the oil until soft and golden, then set aside.
  3. Dust the liver in flour and fry in the remaining oil for 2 minutes per side.
  4. Return the onion, add sour cream and 100 ml water and simmer for 3–4 minutes.
  5. Season with salt only at the end so the liver stays tender.

How to make it lighter

  • Liver is very rich in iron and vitamin A, so once a week is plenty.
  • Use light sour cream or yogurt stirred in off the heat.
  • Serve with buckwheat and a vegetable salad.

History

Liver was traditionally cooked fresh in Belarusian homes right after the pig slaughter, since it does not keep well. Frying it with onion and sour cream is a common East European way to soften its strong taste.
