Rajma masala is a hearty kidney bean curry cooked in a thick, spiced gravy of tomato, onion and fresh ginger, finished with garam masala. The masala is cooked down until it clings to the soft beans, giving a deep, warming flavour. It is eaten with rice or flatbread as a filling vegetarian main.

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g dried red kidney beans, soaked overnight
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 cm fresh ginger, grated
  • 3 tomatoes, puréed
  • 1½ tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Fresh coriander to garnish

Method

  1. Boil the soaked beans in fresh water for 45–60 minutes until tender.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the onions slowly until deep golden.
  3. Add the ginger and cook 1 minute, then add tomatoes, garam masala and salt.
  4. Cook the masala for 10 minutes until thick and glossy.
  5. Stir in the beans with a little cooking liquid, simmer 20 minutes and garnish with coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Cook the onions in 1 tbsp oil with a splash of water — 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Balance the plate: half beans, a quarter rice, a quarter vegetables.
  • Make a big batch; the beans are filling, so a standard portion keeps hunger away for longer.

History

Rajma masala is another name for the North Indian kidney bean curry, a staple of Punjabi home cooking. It is widely served in Indian homes, roadside dhabas and Indian restaurants abroad.
