Steak au poivre is a French dish of beef steak pressed into coarsely crushed black peppercorns, seared in butter and served with a pan sauce of brandy and cream. The pepper crust is hot and aromatic, the meat juicy and the sauce smooth and rich. It is a bistro classic, traditionally served with fries or potatoes and green vegetables.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 beef steaks, about 220 g each
  • 2 tbsp black peppercorns, coarsely crushed
  • 20 g butter
  • 40 ml brandy
  • 100 ml cream
  • Salt

Method

  1. Salt the steaks and press the crushed pepper firmly onto both sides.
  2. Sear in hot butter for 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare; rest on a plate.
  3. Off the heat, add the brandy to the pan and carefully flame or reduce it.
  4. Add the cream, simmer until slightly thick and pour over the steaks.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream and a splash of water for a lighter sauce.
  • Choose a lean cut such as sirloin and trim the fat.
  • Serve with green beans or salad instead of fries.

History

Steak au poivre is a classic of French bistro cooking; several Paris restaurants have been credited with inventing it, but its exact origin is uncertain.
