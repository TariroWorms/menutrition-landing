🍢 Cağ kebabı

Origin: Türkiye · Erzurum

Cağ kebabı is a lamb kebab from eastern Türkiye: thin slices of lamb marinated with onion and spices are stacked on a horizontal spit and turned slowly beside a wood fire. As the outside chars, the cook slices it off onto thin metal skewers (cağ), so every portion is juicy with crisp, smoky edges. It is served straight off the skewer.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g boneless lamb leg or shoulder, thinly sliced
  • 2 onions, grated
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp chilli flakes
  • Salt
  • Sliced onion and parsley to serve

Method

  1. Mix the lamb slices with grated onion juice, pepper, cumin, chilli and salt; marinate for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight.
  2. Stack the slices tightly on a long horizontal skewer, or thread them onto several flat skewers.
  3. Grill close to a hot charcoal fire, turning steadily, for 10–15 minutes.
  4. Slice off the charred outer layer onto small skewers and keep grilling the rest. Serve with sliced onion and parsley.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose lean leg meat and trim visible fat before marinating.
  • Fill half the plate with onion, parsley and tomato salad alongside the meat.
  • A 150 g portion of meat is a filling, high-protein main.

History

Cağ kebabı is a specialty of Erzurum and the surrounding Oltu area in eastern Anatolia, where it is cooked over wood. It has become known across Türkiye in recent decades, with dedicated cağ kebab restaurants in Istanbul and other cities.
