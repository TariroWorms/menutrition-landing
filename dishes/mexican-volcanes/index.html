Volcanes (crisp tortillas with melted cheese and meat)

Origin: Mexico
540kcal

Volcanes are corn tortillas toasted on the griddle until crisp, then covered with cheese that melts, bubbles and spills over the edges like lava. Chopped grilled meat, such as al pastor pork or beef, goes on top with onion, cilantro and salsa. They are eaten by hand at taquerías as a crunchy, cheesy cousin of the taco.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 200 g Oaxaca or Chihuahua cheese, grated
  • 300 g grilled beef or al pastor pork, chopped
  • 1/2 onion (60 g)
  • cilantro, salsa, lime

Method

  1. Toast the tortillas on a dry griddle until firm and crisp.
  2. Scatter each one generously with cheese.
  3. Leave on the griddle until the cheese melts and spills over the edges.
  4. Top with the hot chopped meat.
  5. Finish with onion, cilantro and salsa and eat right away.

How to make it lighter

  • Keep the tortillas dry-toasted rather than fried in oil.
  • Use a thin layer of cheese and more lean meat.
  • Add shredded lettuce and tomato on top.

History

Volcanes are a modern taquería snack found in Mexico City and in northern cities. The name simply describes the way the cheese erupts over the crisp tortilla.
