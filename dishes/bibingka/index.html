Bibingka (Filipino coconut rice cake)

Origin: Philippines

Bibingka is a soft, slightly chewy cake made from rice flour, coconut milk and eggs, baked in banana leaves with heat above and below. It has a smoky edge, a golden top and a fragrant coconut taste, and is often topped with salted egg, cheese and grated coconut. It is a Christmas-morning favorite.

Recipe

6 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g rice flour
  • 300 ml coconut milk
  • 3 eggs
  • 100 g sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 50 g butter
  • 1 salted egg
  • Banana leaves

Method

  1. Mix flour, sugar and baking powder.
  2. Whisk in coconut milk, eggs and melted butter.
  3. Pour into banana-leaf-lined pans.
  4. Bake at 190 C for 15 minutes, top with sliced salted egg and bake 10 minutes more.
  5. Brush with butter and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less sugar and add grated coconut.
  • Skip the butter topping.
  • It is rich, so share a slice.

History

Bibingka is traditionally sold near churches during the Simbang Gabi dawn masses before Christmas. It is cooked in clay pots with coals above and below.
