🍛 Shahi Paneer

Shahi paneer is a rich North Indian curry of soft paneer cubes in a smooth tomato sauce enriched with cream and ground cashews. Saffron and cardamom give it a fragrant, mildly sweet character, and the heat is gentle. It is served with rice or flatbread at festive meals.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g paneer, cubed
  • 3 tomatoes, puréed
  • 50 g cashews, soaked
  • 100 ml cream
  • Pinch of saffron in 2 tbsp warm water
  • 4 green cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt and chili powder

Method

  1. Blend the soaked cashews with a little water to a smooth paste.
  2. Heat oil, fry cardamom and ginger-garlic paste for a minute, then add the tomato purée and cook 10 minutes.
  3. Stir in the cashew paste, salt and chili and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Add the cream and saffron, then the paneer, and simmer gently 5 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half the cream and thin the sauce with water.
  • Reduce the cashews to 25 g; the sauce stays creamy.
  • Serve a smaller portion with plenty of vegetables on the side.

History

'Shahi' means 'royal', and the dish belongs to the Mughlai style of rich, nut- and cream-based cooking associated with the courts of North India. Today it is a standard on Indian restaurant menus and at celebrations.
