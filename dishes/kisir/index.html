🥗 Kısır

Origin: Turkey · Southeastern Anatolia

Kısır is a Turkish bulgur salad: fine bulgur softened with hot water, then kneaded with tomato paste, olive oil and lemon juice and tossed with chopped scallions, parsley and mint. Pomegranate seeds add sweet-sour pops. It is herby, tangy and lightly spicy, served at room temperature, often scooped into lettuce leaves.

Recipe

4 servings30 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g fine bulgur
  • 250 ml hot water
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 4 scallions, finely sliced
  • 1 bunch parsley and a handful of mint, chopped
  • Seeds of ½ pomegranate, salt, chilli flakes

Method

  1. Pour the hot water over the bulgur, cover and leave 15 minutes.
  2. Knead in the tomato paste, olive oil, lemon juice, salt and chilli until evenly red.
  3. Fold in the scallions, parsley and mint.
  4. Scatter with pomegranate seeds and serve at room temperature.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the olive oil and add more lemon juice: 1 tbsp olive oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Double the herbs and add diced cucumber to increase volume per portion.
  • Serve in lettuce leaves instead of with bread.

History

Kısır is closely linked to southeastern Turkey, where bulgur has been a staple grain for centuries. Today it is a fixture of Turkish home gatherings, especially women's afternoon get-togethers, and is eaten across the country.
