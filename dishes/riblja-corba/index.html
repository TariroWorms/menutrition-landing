Riblja čorba is a Serbian fish soup made with several kinds of river fish simmered with plenty of onion, tomato, sweet paprika and hot chili. The broth turns brick-red, light and peppery, with chunks of tender fish. It is ladled into bowls straight from the pot and is a classic of riverside taverns.

Recipe

4 servings60 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg mixed river fish (carp, catfish, pike), cleaned and cut
  • 3 onions, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped, or 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 tbsp sweet paprika
  • 1 small dried chili
  • 1.5 l water
  • 1 tsp salt, bay leaf

Method

  1. Simmer the onions in the water for 20 minutes until very soft.
  2. Add the tomato, paprika, chili, bay leaf and salt.
  3. Add the larger fish pieces first, then the smaller ones, and simmer gently 20–25 minutes without stirring.
  4. Shake the pot rather than stirring so the fish stays in pieces.
  5. Serve hot with chili on the side.

How to make it lighter

  • The soup is naturally light; keep any oil to a minimum.
  • Choose leaner fish such as pike over fatty carp.
  • Make it a full meal with a side salad.

History

The soup is closely tied to the fishing communities along the Danube and Sava, and fish-soup cooking contests are held in Serbia and neighbouring countries. It is related to the Hungarian halászlé.
