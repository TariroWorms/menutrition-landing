Mermez (Tunisian lamb and chickpea stew)

Origin: Tunisia

Mermez is a slow-cooked stew of lamb and chickpeas in a red sauce of tomato, paprika and garlic, often with a little harissa. The meat becomes very tender and the sauce is thick and warming. It is served with bread or couscous.

Recipe

6 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g lamb shoulder, in pieces
  • 200 g dried chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp harissa
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in olive oil with the onion.
  2. Stir in garlic, tomato paste, paprika and harissa.
  3. Add the drained chickpeas and cover with water.
  4. Simmer gently for 1.5 to 2 hours until the meat is tender.
  5. Adjust seasoning and let the sauce thicken.
  6. Serve hot with bread or couscous.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim visible fat from the lamb for a lighter dish.
  • Use less oil and more chickpeas for extra fibre.
  • Serve with a green salad instead of bread.

History

Mermez is a home-style dish found across Tunisia, often cooked for family gatherings and holidays. Recipes differ from family to family in the spices and the amount of chilli.
