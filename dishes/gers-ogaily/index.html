🍰 Gers ogaily

Gers ogaily is a Kuwaiti sponge cake flavoured with saffron, cardamom and rosewater and topped with sesame seeds. Made from eggs, sugar and flour beaten to a light batter, it bakes into a springy, golden, highly fragrant cake. It is cut into squares and served with tea, especially during Ramadan and family gatherings.

Recipe

10 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 180 g sugar
  • 180 g flour
  • A pinch of saffron soaked in 2 tbsp rosewater
  • 1 tsp ground cardamom
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds, a little oil for the tin

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs and sugar for 8–10 minutes until thick and pale.
  2. Fold in the saffron-rosewater and cardamom.
  3. Gently fold in the flour sifted with the baking powder.
  4. Pour into an oiled 22 cm tin, sprinkle with sesame and bake at 175 °C for 25–30 minutes until golden and springy.
  5. Cool and cut into squares.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut into small squares — the cake is fragrant enough that a small piece satisfies.
  • Reduce the sugar by about a quarter; the saffron and cardamom carry the flavour.
  • Serve with unsweetened tea or Arabic coffee.

History

Gers ogaily is a traditional cake of Kuwait and the wider Gulf, where saffron and cardamom have long flavoured sweets and drinks. It is still a home and bakery favourite at Gulf tea tables today.
