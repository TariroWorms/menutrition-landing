Kholodets (Russian jellied meat)

Origin: Russia

Kholodets is a cold dish of tender shredded meat set in a firm, clear jelly made by long simmering of pork trotters, shanks and beef. It tastes deeply meaty with plenty of garlic and black pepper. It is cut into slices and eaten cold with horseradish or mustard, especially at winter holidays.

What it is made of

Recipe

10 servings480 min

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork trotters and shank
  • 700 g beef shank
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • bay leaf, black peppercorns
  • salt

Method

  1. Soak the trotters for 2 hours, then cover all meat with cold water and bring to the boil; drain and rinse.
  2. Cover with fresh water and simmer very gently for 6 hours, skimming.
  3. Add onion, carrot, bay leaf and peppercorns for the last hour and salt well.
  4. Take out the meat, remove bones and shred it; strain the broth.
  5. Place meat and crushed garlic in moulds, pour over the broth and chill overnight until set.

How to make it lighter

  • Lift off the fat layer from the top before serving.
  • Use more beef and less pork for a leaner jelly.
  • Serve with a fresh vegetable salad rather than bread.

History

Jellied meat has been part of Russian winter feasts for centuries, as the cold weather made it easy to set without refrigeration. Today it is still a traditional dish for New Year and Orthodox Christmas tables.
