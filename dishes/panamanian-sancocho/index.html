Sancocho panameño (Panamanian chicken soup)

Origin: Panama

Panamanian sancocho is the country's favorite comfort soup: chicken pieces simmered with ñame, yuca, corn and plenty of culantro. The broth is light but full of flavor, with a fresh herbal taste, and the yam thickens it slightly. It is served with white rice on the side and is considered a remedy for hangovers and colds.

What it is made of

Recipe

6 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g chicken pieces
  • 300 g ñame (yam)
  • 200 g yuca
  • 2 ears corn
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 bunch culantro or cilantro
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • salt
  • white rice to serve

Method

  1. Cover chicken with water, add onion, garlic, oregano and salt, and simmer 25 minutes.
  2. Add ñame and yuca in chunks.
  3. Add corn pieces and cook 25 minutes more.
  4. Stir in culantro for the last 5 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with white rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove chicken skin for a leaner soup.
  • Add extra vegetables and skip the rice.
  • Skim the fat off the broth before serving.

History

Sancocho is found across Latin America, and Panama has its own style based on ñame and the sharp-scented culantro herb. It is often called a national dish and is made at family meals and after parties.
