🍛 Chana masala

Chana masala is a North Indian curry of chickpeas simmered in a thick, tangy sauce of onion, tomato and garlic, spiced with cumin and garam masala. The chickpeas stay whole but soften and soak up the sauce. It is hearty and warming, served with rice or flatbread, and is a staple of home cooking and roadside dhabas.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 x 400 g cans chickpeas, drained (about 500 g)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves and a 3 cm piece of ginger, grated
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 green chili, chopped
  • 2 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp ground coriander, 1/2 tsp turmeric
  • Salt, fresh coriander to serve

Method

  1. Heat the oil and fry the cumin seeds for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the onion and cook for 10 minutes until golden, then the garlic, ginger and chili for 1 minute.
  3. Add the ground spices and tomatoes and cook until the sauce thickens, about 8 minutes.
  4. Add the chickpeas and 200 ml water, simmer for 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas to thicken.
  5. Season with salt and finish with fresh coriander.

How to make it lighter

  • Cook the onions in 1 tbsp of oil with a splash of water to save about 120 kcal per pot.
  • Serve with a smaller portion of rice and a side of cucumber and onion salad.
  • Chickpeas are filling; a 300 g bowl on its own makes a satisfying meal.

History

Chickpea curries have long been part of Punjabi cooking, and chana masala, sometimes called chole, is today eaten all over India and Pakistan and in Indian restaurants worldwide.
