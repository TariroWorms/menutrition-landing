Njeguški pršut (Montenegrin prosciutto)

Origin: Montenegro · Njeguši

Njeguški pršut is a dry-cured, smoked ham from the mountain village of Njeguši near Kotor. The pork leg is salted, pressed, smoked over beech wood and aged in cool mountain air. The slices are deep red and savory, with a faint smokiness. It is served as a starter with local cheese, olives and bread.

Recipe

1 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g Njeguši pršut, thinly sliced
  • 100 g local cheese
  • 8 olives
  • 4 slices of bread
  • fresh figs or melon in season

Method

  1. Cut the ham as thinly as possible.
  2. Arrange on a board with the cheese.
  3. Add olives and bread.
  4. Add fruit if available.
  5. Serve at room temperature.

How to make it lighter

  • It is salty, so keep to a few slices.
  • Serve with melon or figs for a balanced plate.
  • Skip extra salty cheese on the same board.

History

Njeguši, the home village of Montenegro's former ruling family, is known for its cured meats and cheese. The mix of mountain air and Adriatic influences gives the ham its character.
