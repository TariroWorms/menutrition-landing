🥗 Horiatiki Salata (Greek Salad)

Horiatiki is the Greek village salad: chunky tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, green pepper and Kalamata olives, topped with a thick slab of feta, dried oregano and a pour of olive oil. There is no lettuce. It is juicy, salty and tangy, and is served as a side or light meal with grilled meat or fish, the dressing mopped up at the end.

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe tomatoes, in wedges
  • 1 cucumber, in thick slices
  • 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 green pepper, in rings
  • 12 Kalamata olives
  • 150 g feta, in one slab
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

  1. Cut the tomatoes, cucumber and pepper into large chunks and place in a shallow bowl.
  2. Scatter the onion and olives on top and season lightly with salt.
  3. Lay the slab of feta over the salad.
  4. Sprinkle with oregano, drizzle with olive oil and serve straight away.

How to make it lighter

  • Measure the olive oil rather than pouring freely; 1 tbsp oil ≈ 120 kcal.
  • Use half the feta and crumble it so it spreads further across the salad.
  • Add extra cucumber and tomato to make a bigger, more filling bowl for the same calories.

History

The name means 'village salad' in Greek, reflecting its roots in simple summer produce from rural Greece. Today it is a fixture of tavernas across Greece and Cyprus.
