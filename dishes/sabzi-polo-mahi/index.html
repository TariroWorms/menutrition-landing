🐟 Sabzi Polo ba Mahi

Sabzi polo ba mahi is a Persian dish of fragrant basmati rice steamed with a large quantity of chopped fresh herbs, mainly dill, parsley and coriander, served with pan-fried white fish. A little saffron is drizzled over the rice for colour and aroma. The rice is green, herby and fluffy, while the fish is crisp outside and flaky inside.

Recipe

4 servings75 min

Ingredients

  • 350 g basmati rice
  • 60 g dill, chopped
  • 60 g parsley, chopped
  • 40 g coriander, chopped
  • 4 white fish fillets (about 600 g)
  • 1 pinch saffron, steeped in 2 tbsp hot water
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • salt, turmeric

Method

  1. Rinse the rice, soak for 30 minutes, then parboil in salted water for 6 minutes and drain.
  2. Mix the rice with the chopped herbs.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pot, add the herbed rice, cover with a lid wrapped in a cloth and steam on low heat for 40 minutes.
  4. Season the fish with salt and turmeric and fry in the remaining oil until golden and cooked through.
  5. Drizzle the saffron water over the rice and serve with the fish.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the fish instead of frying it to use less oil.
  • Reduce the rice and keep the full amount of herbs for volume.
  • Serve with a plain salad of cucumber and tomato on the side.

History

Sabzi polo ba mahi is the traditional meal for Nowruz, the Persian New Year, where the fresh herbs are often said to symbolise spring and renewal. It is eaten across Iran and in Iranian communities abroad, especially on the eve or first day of the new year.
