Churrasco ecuatoriano (steak and eggs plate)

Origin: Ecuador

In Ecuador, churrasco is not a barbecue but a generous plate built around a thin beef steak cooked quickly in a pan with onion and tomato. Two fried eggs sit on top, and around it come white rice, French fries, ripe plantain, avocado and a simple salad. It is salty, juicy and very filling. It is a favourite lunch at casual restaurants.

Recipe

2 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 thin beef steaks (150 g each)
  • 4 eggs
  • 300 g cooked white rice
  • 300 g potatoes for fries
  • 1 onion and 1 tomato, sliced
  • 1 avocado
  • lettuce
  • oil, salt, cumin

Method

  1. Season the steaks with salt, cumin and a little garlic.
  2. Cut the potatoes into sticks and fry or oven-bake until golden.
  3. Sear the steaks 2 minutes per side in a hot pan, then add onion and tomato for 2 more minutes.
  4. Fry the eggs sunny side up.
  5. Plate the steak with eggs on top, rice, fries, avocado and salad.

How to make it lighter

  • Bake the fries in the oven instead of deep-frying.
  • Have one egg instead of two and a bigger salad.
  • Choose a lean cut such as sirloin or round.

History

The word churrasco is shared across South America, but each country uses it differently. The Ecuadorian plate became a standard of the country's lunch restaurants. It is especially associated with Quito.
