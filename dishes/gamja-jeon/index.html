Gamja jeon (potato pancake)

Origin: South Korea · Gangwon
216kcal

Gamja jeon is a pancake of freshly grated potato, held together only by its own starch and fried until the edges are lacy and crisp. The inside is soft and chewy with a clean potato taste. It is served hot with a soy dipping sauce and is a favorite on rainy days.

What it is made of

potatosaltoilgreen chili

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g potatoes, peeled
  • 1 green chili (15 g), sliced
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 4 tbsp oil (60 ml)
  • dipping sauce: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp vinegar

Method

  1. Grate the potatoes finely and let the starch settle in the bowl.
  2. Pour off the water and return the starch to the potato.
  3. Stir in salt and chili.
  4. Heat oil in a pan and spread the mixture thin.
  5. Fry 4 minutes per side until crisp and serve with the dip.

How to make it lighter

  • Use a nonstick pan and less oil.
  • Add grated carrot or zucchini for fibre.
  • Serve with a salad rather than extra rice.

History

Potatoes are a classic crop of the cool Gangwon highlands in the northeast, and many local dishes feature them. This pancake is among the best known of them and is an easy everyday snack.
