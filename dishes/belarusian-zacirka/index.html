Zacirka (milk soup with dough crumbs)

Origin: Belarus

Zacirka are tiny pellets of dough made by rubbing flour with a little egg or water between the palms, then dropping them into simmering milk. They swell into soft, chewy bits that slightly thicken the milk, giving a mild, creamy soup that tastes of childhood for many Belarusians. A savoury version is cooked in broth with fried salo and onion.

What it is made of

flourmilkeggbuttersalt

Recipe

4 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 150 g flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 l milk
  • 20 g butter
  • 1 tsp sugar (optional)
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Beat the egg with a pinch of salt and a spoon of water.
  2. Pour the egg into the flour and rub with your fingers until the mixture forms small crumbs.
  3. Shake the crumbs through a coarse sieve to remove excess flour.
  4. Bring the milk to a gentle boil with salt and sugar.
  5. Sprinkle in the crumbs, stirring, and cook on low heat for 8-10 minutes.
  6. Add the butter and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use semi-skimmed milk to reduce fat.
  • Make the crumbs with wholemeal flour for more fibre.
  • For a savoury, lighter version cook them in vegetable broth with herbs.

History

Zacirka is a simple peasant dish made from the most basic ingredients of a village household: flour, milk and eggs. It was a common breakfast and supper in rural Belarus and is still cooked at home, especially for children.
