The mushroom Swiss burger is a grilled beef patty topped with a mound of sautéed mushrooms and a slice of melted Swiss cheese, served in a soft bun. The mushrooms bring an earthy, savoury depth, while the nutty cheese melts over them. It is a long-standing favourite on American diner and burger-chain menus.

Recipe

2 servings25 min

Ingredients

  • 2 beef patties (150 g each)
  • 200 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 slices Swiss cheese (40 g)
  • 2 burger buns
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 garlic clove, chopped
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Sauté the mushrooms and garlic in the oil for 6–8 minutes until browned; season.
  2. Season the patties and grill or pan-fry 3–4 minutes per side.
  3. Top each patty with mushrooms and a cheese slice; cover for 1 minute to melt.
  4. Toast the buns lightly and assemble.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 5–10% fat beef for the patties.
  • Swap the bun for large lettuce leaves to cut the bread.
  • Pile on extra mushrooms — they add volume with few calories.

History

The mushroom Swiss burger is a long-established item on American burger menus and is still widely served across the United States.
