🍳 Çılbır (Turkish eggs)

Origin: Turkey · Ottoman cuisine

Çılbır is a Turkish dish of soft poached eggs set on a bed of garlicky yogurt and finished with melted butter infused with Aleppo pepper. The cool, tangy yogurt, runny yolks and warm red butter make a silky contrast, and fresh dill adds a bright note. It is eaten for breakfast or a light meal, often with sliced cucumbers and tomatoes alongside.

Recipe

2 servings15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 250 g thick yogurt
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • 30 g butter
  • 1 tsp Aleppo pepper flakes
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar for poaching
  • Chopped dill, salt

Method

  1. Mix the yogurt with the garlic and a pinch of salt and let it come to room temperature; spread it over two plates.
  2. Bring a pan of water to a gentle simmer with the vinegar and poach the eggs for 3 minutes.
  3. Lift out the eggs with a slotted spoon, drain and place them on the yogurt.
  4. Melt the butter until foaming, stir in the Aleppo pepper and take it off the heat.
  5. Spoon the red butter over the eggs and finish with dill.

How to make it lighter

  • Halve the butter — about 10 g per plate still gives the red drizzle its colour.
  • Use low-fat yogurt for a lighter base.
  • Serve with plenty of sliced cucumbers and tomatoes to fill the plate.

History

Poached eggs with yogurt are often said to go back to Ottoman times. Today çılbır is a common breakfast and home dish across Turkey and has become popular in cafés abroad as Turkish eggs.
