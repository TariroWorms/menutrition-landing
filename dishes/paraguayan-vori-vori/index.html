Vori vori (Paraguayan chicken soup with cornmeal-cheese dumplings)

Origin: Paraguay

Vori vori is a hearty chicken soup with small dumplings made from cornmeal and cheese, simmered in a broth with onion, tomato and bell pepper. The dumplings are soft and slightly cheesy and soak up the savory broth. It is a typical comfort food in Paraguay.

Recipe

6 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g chicken pieces
  • 150 g cornmeal
  • 100 g fresh cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1.5 L water, salt

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken with onion, pepper and tomato for 35 minutes.
  2. Mix cornmeal, grated cheese, egg and salt with a little warm broth.
  3. Roll the dough into small balls.
  4. Drop them into the simmering soup.
  5. Cook 15 minutes until the dumplings float and are tender.
  6. Serve hot with the chicken.

How to make it lighter

  • Remove chicken skin and skim fat.
  • Use a light cheese in the dumplings.
  • Add more vegetables to the broth.

History

The name comes from Guaraní and is said to refer to the round, plump shape of the dumplings. The soup reflects the shared use of corn and cheese in everyday Paraguayan cooking.
