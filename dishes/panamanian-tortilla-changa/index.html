Tortilla changa (Panamanian fresh-corn tortilla)

Origin: Panama

Tortilla changa is a thick, soft tortilla made from freshly ground young corn rather than dried masa. The dough is lightly sweet and browns in a pan or on a griddle. Panamanians eat it for breakfast beside eggs, cheese or fried meat.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 500 g fresh corn kernels
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tbsp oil for the pan

Method

  1. Grind the corn in a processor to a coarse paste.
  2. Mix with sugar, salt and melted butter.
  3. Shape into thick discs about 1.5 cm high.
  4. Heat a lightly oiled pan on medium heat.
  5. Cook 5 to 6 minutes per side until golden and set.

How to make it lighter

  • Use less butter and brush the pan with a little oil.
  • Pair with eggs and avocado for a balanced breakfast.
  • Skip the sugar if you like a more savory tortilla.

History

Corn tortillas are an old part of Central American cooking, and Panama has its own thicker, juicier version made with fresh kernels. It is a common sight in rural kitchens and roadside stalls.
