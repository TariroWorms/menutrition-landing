🧀 Conchiglie ai quattro formaggi

Conchiglie ai quattro formaggi is shell pasta coated in a sauce of four cheeses: gorgonzola, fontina, parmesan and mozzarella. The hollow shells catch the melted cheese, and a short spell in a hot oven gives the top a golden, bubbling crust. The result is rich, salty and creamy, usually served as a hearty main course.

Recipe

4 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 320 g conchiglie pasta
  • 100 g gorgonzola, in pieces
  • 100 g fontina, diced
  • 125 g mozzarella, diced
  • 50 g parmesan, grated
  • 150 ml pasta cooking water
  • Black pepper, salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cook the conchiglie in salted water 2 minutes short of the packet time and keep 150 ml of the cooking water.
  2. Melt the gorgonzola and fontina with the cooking water in a wide pan over low heat, stirring until smooth.
  3. Toss in the drained pasta and half of the parmesan and season with pepper.
  4. Spread in a baking dish, top with the mozzarella and remaining parmesan and bake for 10–12 minutes until bubbling and golden.

How to make it lighter

  • Serve a smaller portion of about 200 g next to a big green salad.
  • Use less of the strongest cheeses — gorgonzola and parmesan give plenty of flavour in small amounts.
  • Add steamed broccoli or spinach to the shells to bulk up the plate with fewer calories per bite.

History

Four-cheese pasta is a classic of Italian home and trattoria cooking, built on cheeses from the north of the country such as gorgonzola from Lombardy and fontina from the Aosta Valley. Today it is served across Italy and in Italian restaurants worldwide.
