Varză călită (Romanian braised cabbage)

Origin: Romania

Varză călită is cabbage shredded and cooked slowly in a little oil with onion and tomato until soft and sweet. In winter it is made with sauerkraut for a gentle sour note, and sometimes diced smoked sausage is added. It is eaten as a side with roast pork or sausages, or alone with polenta during fasting periods.

Recipe

4 servings50 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g white cabbage or sauerkraut, shredded
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 40 ml oil
  • 2 tomatoes or 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 100 g smoked sausage, diced (optional)

Method

  1. Heat the oil and soften the onion for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the optional sausage and brown it lightly.
  3. Stir in paprika, tomato and the cabbage.
  4. Add 100 ml water, cover and cook on low heat for 35 to 40 minutes, stirring now and then.
  5. Season with salt and pepper and serve warm.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the oil to a tablespoon or two and cook under a lid so the cabbage steams.
  • Leave out sausage for a vegan, low-fat side.
  • Rinse sauerkraut briefly if you want less sodium.

History

Cabbage has been a staple winter vegetable in Romania for centuries because it stores well and ferments easily. The word călită refers to cooking something gently in fat until soft. Variations are found across the Balkans and Central Europe.
