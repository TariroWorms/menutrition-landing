Dulce de leche ice cream (Helado de dulce de leche)

Origin: Argentina

Helado de dulce de leche is the best-loved flavour in Argentina's Italian-style ice cream shops. It is a milk and cream base churned with plenty of dulce de leche, giving a dense, smooth texture and a deep caramel-milk taste. Versions such as 'dulce de leche granizado' add chocolate flakes, and 'súper dulce de leche' adds ripples of extra caramel. Argentines buy it by the quarter-kilo or the kilo and eat it at any time of year.

Recipe

6 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 500 ml whole milk
  • 200 ml cream
  • 350 g dulce de leche
  • 40 g sugar
  • 1 pinch of salt

Method

  1. Warm the milk with the sugar and salt until dissolved.
  2. Whisk in the dulce de leche until smooth.
  3. Stir in the cream and chill the mixture for at least 4 hours.
  4. Churn in an ice-cream machine until thick.
  5. Freeze 2 hours more before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Replace the cream with extra milk for a lighter, icier texture.
  • Serve one small scoop of about 80 g in a cup rather than a cone.
  • Pair it with fresh fruit instead of extra toppings.

History

Italian immigrants brought gelato-making to Argentina, and local makers soon combined it with dulce de leche. Today ice cream parlours, or heladerías, are a fixture of every Argentine town.
