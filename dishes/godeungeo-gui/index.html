Godeungeo gui (Korean grilled mackerel)

Origin: South Korea

Godeungeo gui is a humble home dish of salted mackerel halves grilled until the skin blisters. The flesh is rich, oily and tender, with a clean sea flavour sharpened by salt. It usually comes with steamed rice, a few banchan and a squeeze of lemon. It is a staple of Korean lunch tables and set meals.

What it is made of

mackerelsaltoil

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 mackerel, about 400 g each, cleaned and halved
  • 2 tsp coarse salt (10 g)
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil (15 ml)
  • lemon wedges
  • grated radish to serve (80 g)

Method

  1. Pat the fish dry and score the skin in two or three places.
  2. Rub with salt on both sides and rest for 15 minutes.
  3. Dry again and brush the skin lightly with oil.
  4. Grill or pan-sear skin side down for 5 minutes, then flip for 4 more.
  5. Serve hot with lemon and grated radish.

How to make it lighter

  • Salt lightly and serve with lemon to keep sodium low.
  • Grill rather than fry to keep added fat minimal.
  • Serve with plenty of steamed vegetables instead of just rice.

History

Mackerel has long been among the most common and affordable fish in Korea, often salted to keep it through the day. Grilling it simply is the way most households have prepared it for generations.
