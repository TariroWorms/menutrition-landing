Pizza prosciutto e funghi is a classic tomato and mozzarella pizza topped with cooked ham and sliced mushrooms. The mushrooms turn tender and earthy in the oven, the ham adds a mild saltiness and the cheese ties everything together. It is a family favourite in Italian pizzerias, served whole.

Recipe

2 servings35 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g ready pizza dough (or homemade)
  • 150 g crushed tomatoes
  • 125 g mozzarella, torn
  • 80 g cooked ham (prosciutto cotto), sliced
  • 100 g mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, salt

Method

  1. Preheat the oven with a stone or tray inside to the maximum.
  2. Sauté the mushrooms in a dry pan with a pinch of salt for 3–4 minutes to drive off water.
  3. Stretch the dough by hand into a 30 cm round, leaving a slightly thicker rim.
  4. Spread the tomato, add the mozzarella, ham and mushrooms.
  5. Bake on the hottest setting of the oven (250–280 °C) on a preheated stone or tray for 7–10 minutes until the crust is blistered.
  6. Drizzle with the oil and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Add more mushrooms and less ham: they bring volume for very few calories.
  • Use less mozzarella and spread it thinly — cheese is the most calorie-dense topping.
  • Eat half a pizza with a large green salad instead of a whole pizza alone.

History

Ham and mushroom is one of the standard combinations on Italian pizzeria menus, known as a crowd-pleasing middle ground between plain margherita and loaded pizzas. No single origin is recorded.
