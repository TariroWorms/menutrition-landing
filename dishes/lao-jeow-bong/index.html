Jeow bong (Luang Prabang chili paste)

Origin: Laos · Luang Prabang
57kcal

Jeow bong is a dark, jammy chili paste made from dried chilies, shallot, garlic and galangal cooked slowly with palm sugar and fish sauce. Traditionally buffalo skin is stewed in as well, but a simpler version is made without it. It is sweet, smoky and moderately hot, and is eaten with sticky rice, vegetables and grilled meats.

What it is made of

chili peppershallotsgarlicgalangalfish saucepalm sugar

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 10 dried chilies
  • 4 shallots
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp galangal
  • 2 tbsp palm sugar
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 3 tbsp oil

Method

  1. Grill or dry-roast the chilies, shallots and garlic until fragrant.
  2. Pound them with galangal into a coarse paste.
  3. Warm the oil and fry the paste on low heat for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in palm sugar and fish sauce.
  5. Cook until dark and jammy, then cool and jar.

How to make it lighter

  • Use only a teaspoon per serving; it is concentrated.
  • Cut the oil to a minimum.
  • Eat with raw vegetables rather than a lot of rice.

History

The paste is a signature of Luang Prabang, where jars of it are sold in markets for travellers to take home. It keeps well and appears at almost every table in the region.
