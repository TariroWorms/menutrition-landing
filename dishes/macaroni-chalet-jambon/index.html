🧀 Macaroni chalet with ham

Macaroni chalet with ham is the hearty Alpine bake of short macaroni and diced potatoes in cream, studded with cubes of ham and covered with melted mountain cheese. The potatoes make it filling, the ham adds a salty, smoky note and the cheese forms a golden crust. It is a typical ski-resort dish, served piping hot in the dish it was baked in.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 300 g short macaroni
  • 300 g waxy potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 150 g cooked ham, diced
  • 2 onions, sliced into rings
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 200 ml cream
  • 150 g mountain cheese (Gruyère-style), grated
  • Salt, pepper and nutmeg

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water for 5 minutes, add the macaroni and cook together until both are tender; drain.
  2. Fry the onion rings in the oil until deep golden. Add the ham for the last 2 minutes.
  3. Warm the cream with salt, pepper and nutmeg and mix with the pasta and potatoes and ham.
  4. Layer in a baking dish with the cheese, finishing with cheese on top.
  5. Bake at 200 °C for 15 minutes until melted and golden, and scatter the onions over before serving.

How to make it lighter

  • Use lean ham and less cheese and cream for a lighter dish.
  • Keep the portion to about 250 g and add a green salad.
  • Measure the cream: half the amount still coats the pasta well.

History

This dish belongs to the Alpine tradition of macaroni baked with potatoes, cream and mountain cheese, best known in Switzerland as Älplermagronen, which herders are said to have made from the few supplies kept in mountain huts. Today it is served in Alpine chalets and ski restaurants in Switzerland and neighbouring France.
