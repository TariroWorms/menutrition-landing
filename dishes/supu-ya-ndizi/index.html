Supu ya ndizi (Tanzanian green banana soup)

Origin: Tanzania
245kcal

Supu ya ndizi is a thick soup in which unripe cooking bananas are simmered with beef or bones, onion, garlic, tomato and carrot until they almost melt into the broth. The taste is mild and comforting with a gentle starchiness. It is typically served as a family meal in the highlands of Tanzania, sometimes with a squeeze of lemon or a little chilli.

Recipe

4 servings45 min

Ingredients

  • 6 green bananas, peeled and cubed
  • 300 g beef with bones
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 carrot
  • 1.5 litres water
  • salt, pepper

Method

  1. Simmer the meat in water for 30 minutes and skim the surface.
  2. Add onion, garlic, tomato and carrot.
  3. Add the banana cubes.
  4. Cook 20 minutes until the bananas are very soft.
  5. Mash some of them to thicken the soup and season.

How to make it lighter

  • Peel bananas with oiled hands to avoid staining.
  • Use lean meat and skim off fat for a lighter soup.
  • Add spinach at the end for extra vitamins.

History

Cooking bananas are a staple crop in the Kilimanjaro and Kagera regions, so soups and stews made from them are common home dishes. This soup is also a traditional food for people recovering from illness.
