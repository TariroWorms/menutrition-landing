Hunan steamed cured meats (La wei he zheng)

Origin: China · Hunan

Smoked pork, chicken and fish are sliced and steamed together on a bed of fermented black beans, chilies and ginger. Their smoky, salty fats blend into one rich, aromatic platter. It is eaten with white rice and is a centrepiece of New Year tables.

Recipe

3 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 100 g smoked pork belly
  • 100 g smoked chicken
  • 100 g smoked fish
  • 1 tbsp fermented black beans
  • 2 dried red chilies
  • 15 g ginger, shredded
  • 1 tsp sugar

Method

  1. Rinse the smoked meats in warm water and slice.
  2. Mix black beans, chilies, ginger and sugar.
  3. Arrange the meat slices on a plate and cover with the mixture.
  4. Steam over high heat for 25 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Soak the meats in water first to remove salt.
  • Serve in small portions with plenty of greens.
  • Choose lean cuts of cured meat where possible.

History

Hunan households traditionally smoke meats over wood fires before the Lunar New Year. This dish makes use of that preserved bounty in one serving.
