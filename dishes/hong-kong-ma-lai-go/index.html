Hong Kong sponge cake (ma lai go)

Origin: Hong Kong

A tall, golden-brown steamed cake with a springy, honeycomb crumb and deep caramel sweetness. It is soft and moist, with a slightly chewy edge. Traditionally served in squares at dim sum teahouses.

Recipe

8 servings70 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 150 g brown sugar
  • 200 g flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 100 ml evaporated milk
  • 50 ml oil

Method

  1. Whisk eggs and sugar until pale and thick.
  2. Stir in milk and oil.
  3. Fold in flour and baking powder.
  4. Pour into a lined pan.
  5. Steam over medium heat for 35 minutes.

How to make it lighter

  • Reduce sugar by a quarter.
  • Replace half the flour with wholemeal.
  • Cut into smaller squares.

History

The name roughly means Malay cake and refers to the Southeast Asian influence on this Cantonese dim sum dessert. It is steamed rather than baked.
