🥤 Dates milkshake

This thick shake blends soft dates with cold milk, ice cream and crushed ice into a sweet, frosty drink with a caramel flavour. It is richer and more filling than a plain milkshake because of the fruit. Juice bars across the Gulf serve it in tall cups, and it is a favourite after iftar in Ramadan.

What it is made of

Recipe

2 servings5 min

Ingredients

  • 8 soft dates, pitted
  • 350 ml cold milk
  • 2 scoops (about 100 g) vanilla ice cream
  • 1 cup ice cubes

Method

  1. Soak firm dates in a little milk for 10 minutes.
  2. Blend the dates and milk until completely smooth.
  3. Add the ice cream and ice and blend until thick and frosty.
  4. Serve immediately in tall glasses.

How to make it lighter

  • Swap half the ice cream for more ice to keep it thick but lighter.
  • Use semi-skimmed milk.
  • Order the small size: a large juice-bar cup can hold well over 500 ml.

History

Date shakes are sold in juice bars throughout the Gulf states, where dates are the most familiar local fruit.
