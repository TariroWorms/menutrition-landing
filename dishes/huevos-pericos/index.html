Huevos pericos (Colombian scrambled eggs)

Origin: Colombia

Huevos pericos are Colombia's everyday scrambled eggs: tomato and spring onion are softened in a little butter or oil, then the beaten eggs are stirred in until just set. The vegetables give the eggs a fresh, slightly sweet flavor and a speckled look. They are eaten for breakfast with arepa, bread or rice and a cup of hot chocolate or coffee.

Recipe

2 servings10 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 ripe tomato, diced
  • 2 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 tsp butter or oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Heat the butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the spring onion and cook for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato and cook until soft, about 3 minutes.
  4. Beat the eggs with salt and pour them into the pan.
  5. Stir gently until the eggs are creamy and just set.
  6. Serve straight away with arepa.

How to make it lighter

  • Use one whole egg plus two whites per person for a lower-fat version.
  • Add spinach or peppers for more vegetables.
  • Serve with a small whole-grain arepa instead of white bread.

History

The name refers to the colors of the dish, said to recall a parakeet (perico). Pericos are found on breakfast tables across Colombia and also in neighboring Venezuela.
