Completo chileno

Origin: Chile · Santiago

The completo is the Chilean hot dog: a sausage in a soft bun piled with mashed avocado, chopped tomato and mayonnaise. The name means “complete”, and it is meant to be a full, generous topping. It is a favourite Chilean street snack and a common sight at fast-food counters.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 sausages (frankfurter style)
  • 4 long soft hot dog buns
  • 2 ripe avocados
  • 3 tomatoes, finely chopped
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • Salt
  • Optional: sauerkraut or a little chili sauce

Method

  1. Heat the sausages in simmering water for 5–6 minutes.
  2. Mash the avocado with a pinch of salt.
  3. Chop the tomatoes finely and drain off excess juice.
  4. Warm the buns and place a sausage in each.
  5. Top with plenty of avocado, chopped tomato and mayonnaise and serve at once.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose leaner sausages, such as chicken or turkey, to cut fat and keep protein.
  • Use more avocado and tomato and less mayonnaise: the toppings stay creamy but the calories fall.
  • Pick a smaller or wholegrain bun, and eat it with a side salad instead of fries.

History

The completo is a classic of Chilean street food and casual eating. Its trademark toppings of avocado, tomato and mayonnaise set it apart from the hot dog of other countries. It is a regular choice at sports events, markets and street stalls.
