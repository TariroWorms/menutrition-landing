Bob chorba (Bulgarian bean soup)

Origin: Bulgaria

Bob chorba is the classic Bulgarian bean soup, thick with white beans, onion, carrot, peppers and tomato, and scented with savory and mint. It is warming, earthy and mildly sweet. It is eaten with bread and often a dried chili on the side, and is a staple of fasting days.

What it is made of

white beansonioncarrotbell peppertomato

Recipe

4 servings90 min

Ingredients

  • 250 g dried white beans, soaked overnight
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried savory
  • fresh mint, salt

Method

  1. Boil the soaked beans in fresh water for 30 minutes and drain.
  2. Cover with 1.5 l fresh water and simmer until half tender.
  3. Sauté onion, carrot and pepper in oil.
  4. Stir in paprika, then add the vegetables and chopped tomatoes to the beans.
  5. Simmer until the beans are completely soft, about 40 minutes.
  6. Season with savory, salt and mint and serve.

How to make it lighter

  • Cook with little oil; the beans make it creamy anyway.
  • Mash a ladle of beans to thicken instead of using flour.
  • Pair with wholegrain bread for fiber.

History

Chorba, from the Persian word for soup, is a pillar of Bulgarian home cooking. Bean soup is especially tied to Christmas Eve and other fasting meals, when no meat is eaten.
