🍲 Youvetsi (Greek lamb and orzo bake)

Youvetsi is a Greek baked dish of lamb slow-cooked in tomato sauce with cinnamon, then finished in the oven with orzo pasta that absorbs the rich juices. It is served bubbling hot, generously sprinkled with grated cheese. The lamb is tender, the orzo soft and saucy, and the cinnamon gives the tomato a warm, rounded flavour.

Recipe

4 servings150 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g lamb shoulder, cut into large pieces
  • 300 g orzo
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes and 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 800 ml hot water, salt, pepper
  • 60 g grated hard cheese

Method

  1. Brown the lamb in the oil in a baking dish or pot, add the onion and cook until soft.
  2. Add the tomatoes, paste, cinnamon, salt, pepper and 400 ml water; cover and bake at 180 °C for 1 hour 15 minutes until the lamb is tender.
  3. Stir in the orzo and the remaining hot water and bake uncovered for 25–30 minutes, stirring once, until the orzo is soft.
  4. Rest 5 minutes and serve sprinkled with cheese.

How to make it lighter

  • Trim the lamb well or use leg instead of shoulder for less fat.
  • Use half the cheese — 1 tbsp grated hard cheese is about 20–25 kcal.
  • Serve a measured 350 g portion with a large green salad.

History

The name comes from the giouvetsi, the clay baking dish it was traditionally cooked in, a word Greek shares with the Turkish güveç. Youvetsi is a classic Sunday and holiday dish in Greece, and in the past families often took the pot to the neighbourhood bakery to be baked in its oven.
