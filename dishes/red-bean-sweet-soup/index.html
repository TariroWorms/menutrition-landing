Sweet red bean soup (hong dou sha)

Origin: China · Guangdong

A classic Cantonese dessert soup of soft red beans that break down into a thick, sandy puree. Dried tangerine peel gives it a citrus perfume that balances the sweetness. It can be served warm in winter or chilled in summer, often with a few tapioca pearls or lotus seeds.

What it is made of

red beandried tangerine peelrock sugar

Recipe

4 servings120 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g dried red beans
  • 1.5 l water
  • 1 piece dried tangerine peel
  • 60 g rock sugar
  • Pinch of salt
  • Tapioca pearls (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse the beans and soak them for 4 hours or overnight.
  2. Soften the tangerine peel in water and scrape off the white pith.
  3. Bring beans, peel and water to a boil, then lower the heat.
  4. Simmer for 60 to 90 minutes until the beans are soft and the soup thickens.
  5. Stir in the rock sugar and a pinch of salt until dissolved.
  6. Serve warm or chilled.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar by a third; the beans are naturally sweet.
  • The soup is high in fibre and almost fat free.
  • Use stevia or less rock sugar for a lower sugar version.

History

Red bean soups are a staple of Cantonese tong sui dessert houses. The dried tangerine peel, often aged for years, reflects the traditional link between sweets and soothing herbal-style ingredients.
