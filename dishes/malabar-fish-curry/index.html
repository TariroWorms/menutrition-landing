🐟 Malabar fish curry

Malabar fish curry is a tangy, spicy curry from the coast of northern Kerala in which firm fish is simmered in coconut milk soured with kokum or tamarind. Curry leaves, chili and turmeric give it colour and aroma, while the coconut keeps the sauce smooth and mellow. It is traditionally served with steamed rice.

Recipe

4 servings40 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g firm white fish, in chunks
  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 3 pieces kokum or 1 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 15 curry leaves
  • 2 green chilies, slit, and 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 onion, sliced, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp grated ginger
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric, salt
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil

Method

  1. Soak the kokum in 100 ml warm water for 10 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil, fry the curry leaves, onion, garlic, ginger and green chilies until soft.
  3. Stir in the turmeric and chili powder, then add the kokum with its water and salt.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk, bring to a gentle simmer and add the fish.
  5. Cook for 8–10 minutes without stirring hard, until the fish is just cooked. Serve with rice.

How to make it lighter

  • Use half coconut milk and half water for a lighter sauce.
  • Serve a measured cup of rice and fill the rest of the plate with vegetables.
  • Choose a lean white fish — the coconut milk already provides most of the fat.

History

The dish belongs to the Malabar coast of northern Kerala, where fishing, coconut palms and the spice trade have shaped the local cooking for centuries. Fish curry with rice remains an everyday meal there.
