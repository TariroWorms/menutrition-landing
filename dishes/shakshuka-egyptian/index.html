🍳 Egyptian Shakshuka

Egyptian shakshuka is a quick breakfast pan of eggs stirred into a spiced sauce of tomato, onion and green or red peppers until softly scrambled. Seasoned with cumin and chili, it is saucy, soft and slightly runny. It is eaten straight from the pan at breakfast, often alongside other morning dishes.

Recipe

2 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • ½ tsp chili flakes
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper

Method

  1. Heat the oil and soften the onion and pepper for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes, cumin and chili and cook 5–7 minutes until saucy.
  3. Beat the eggs lightly, pour into the pan and stir gently until just set.
  4. Season and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 1 tsp of oil instead of 1 tbsp — 1 tbsp oil is about 120 kcal.
  • Replace one egg with extra tomatoes and peppers for more volume.
  • Cook in a non-stick pan so very little oil is needed.

History

In Egypt shakshuka is often made with the eggs stirred through the sauce rather than poached whole, and it is a familiar home and café breakfast across the country.
