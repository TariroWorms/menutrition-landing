🦐 Grilled prawns (gamberi alla griglia)

Gamberi alla griglia are Italian grilled prawns, brushed with olive oil, garlic and parsley and cooked quickly over high heat until lightly charred and juicy. A squeeze of lemon at the table brightens the sweet shrimp flavour. They are served as a simple seafood main or antipasto, often with salad or grilled vegetables.

What it is made of

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g large shrimp, shell on or peeled
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt, pepper

Method

  1. Mix the olive oil, garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
  2. Toss the shrimp in the mixture and leave for 15 minutes.
  3. Grill over high heat for 2 minutes per side until pink and lightly charred.
  4. Serve with lemon wedges.

How to make it lighter

  • Brush on the oil instead of pouring it; 1 tbsp olive oil is about 120 kcal.
  • Pair with grilled vegetables or a big salad for a filling, light plate.
  • Use lemon and herbs generously for flavour without extra fat.

History

Simply grilled shellfish is a fixture of coastal cooking all along the Italian seaside, from the Adriatic to Sicily. The dish has no single place of origin and is found today on seafood restaurant menus throughout Italy.
