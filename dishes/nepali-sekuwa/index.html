Sekuwa (Nepali charcoal-grilled meat)

Origin: Nepal

Sekuwa is Nepal's beloved barbecue: chunks of meat marinated in spices and roasted over glowing charcoal. The result is smoky, juicy and warmly spiced with ginger, garlic, cumin and chilli. It is sold at roadside stalls and enjoyed with beaten rice, onion and lime.

Recipe

4 servings180 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g boneless goat, pork or chicken
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • salt and lime juice

Method

  1. Cut the meat into bite-size cubes.
  2. Mix with the oil, ginger-garlic paste, spices, salt and lime juice.
  3. Marinate for at least 2 hours, ideally overnight.
  4. Thread onto skewers.
  5. Grill over charcoal for 15-20 minutes, turning and basting with the marinade.
  6. Serve hot with sliced onion and lime.

How to make it lighter

  • Choose chicken breast or lean goat for fewer calories.
  • Use less oil in the marinade and baste lightly.
  • Serve with a large raw vegetable salad.

History

Grilling meat on skewers or open fires is an old tradition in the hills of Nepal. Today sekuwa is a popular street food and a staple of festive gatherings.
