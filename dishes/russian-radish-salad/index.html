Radish salad with sour cream (salat iz redki)

Origin: Russia

This simple salad is made from grated black or green radish softened with sour cream, a little onion and fresh dill. It is crunchy, peppery and cooling at the same time. It is eaten as a side with potatoes, meat or rye bread, mostly in winter.

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g black radish
  • 1 small onion
  • 100 g sour cream
  • small bunch of dill
  • salt

Method

  1. Peel the radish and grate it coarsely.
  2. Salt lightly and leave for 10 minutes, then squeeze out some juice to soften the bite.
  3. Chop the onion finely and the dill.
  4. Mix radish, onion and dill with the sour cream.
  5. Taste, adjust salt and serve right away.

How to make it lighter

  • Use 10% sour cream or yogurt to keep it light.
  • Add grated carrot or apple for a milder, sweeter taste.
  • Dress with sunflower oil for a vegan version.

History

Radish has long been a common winter vegetable in Russian peasant cooking because it stores well. Grated radish with sour cream or oil is a traditional everyday dish, especially during fasts when it is made with oil instead.
