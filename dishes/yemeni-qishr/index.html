Qishr (Yemeni coffee husk drink)

Origin: Yemen

Qishr is a light, golden infusion made from dried coffee husks rather than the beans. It is gently sweet and warming, with ginger and cardamom, and has a taste somewhere between tea and spiced fruit. It is served hot in small cups, often after meals.

What it is made of

coffee huskgingercardamomsugar

Recipe

4 servings20 min

Ingredients

  • 50 g dried coffee husks
  • 1 litre water
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 4 cardamom pods, crushed
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 tbsp sugar

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil in a pot.
  2. Add the husks, ginger, cardamom and cinnamon.
  3. Simmer on low heat for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in the sugar until dissolved.
  5. Strain into cups and serve hot.

How to make it lighter

  • Cut the sugar or use a little honey.
  • It has no milk, so it is light on calories.
  • Serve it after dinner instead of a sweet dessert.

History

Yemen is one of the oldest coffee-growing lands, and the husks left after processing beans were turned into this everyday drink. It is still popular in the highlands.
