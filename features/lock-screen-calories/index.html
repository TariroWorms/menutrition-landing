🔒Calories on your Lock Screen

How many calories are left today is right there on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island — no need to open the app.

How it works

  1. 1Log a meal — by photo, voice, text or barcode.🍽️
  2. 2Glance at your Lock Screen — calories left for today are already there.🔒
  3. 3On iPhones with the Dynamic Island, the remaining calories also stay at the top of the screen.🏝️

What you get

  • 👀One glance instead of opening the app — the number is always in front of you.
  • 🔄Updates as you log — every meal and drink you add changes what's left.
  • 💧Water too — see your water for the day alongside calories.
  • 💡Made from a user's request — Khalid from Abu Dhabi asked for it, and we built it.

Questions

Which iPhones show it in the Dynamic Island?

iPhone models that have the Dynamic Island. On other iPhones the remaining calories show on the Lock Screen.

Do I need to open the app for it to update?

No — as soon as you log a meal, the number on the Lock Screen updates.

Does it show water too?

Yes — alongside the calories left, you see how much water you have logged today.

Is it available on Android?

Me Nutrition is on iPhone today; the Android version is coming soon.

3 days free, then a subscription you can cancel anytime in App Store settings. iPhone now, Android coming soon.
